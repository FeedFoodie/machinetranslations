Around noon the next day, Seo Wanpyeong strolled through the main grounds of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult with a gloomy face.

Sunlight fell on his face. His skin was pale from the paranoia-induced insomnia and the assassination missions his martial arts required. Seo Wanpyeong frowned and shielded his eyes from the sun with his hand.

*What refreshing weather.*

Despite his expression, his mood was not bad. Ilmok was undoubtedly the reason his mood had improved recently.

Whenever gloomy thoughts surfaced, or he loathed himself for failing Master’s tasks, or a problem in the Dark Shadow Hall made him feel responsible, Seo Wanpyeong would visit his Youngest Junior Brother.

The Youngest Junior Brother was an amazing boy. He offered solutions Seo Wanpyeong could not easily find and always helped him understand when he misinterpreted Master’s words.

Recently, Seo Wanpyeong had even started thinking positively, entertaining the idea that Master might not hate him. He once felt pathetic for needing help from his much younger Junior Brother.

"What are you talking about, Senior Brother! This is nothing compared to the grace you've shown me. You let me witness your respected swordsmanship and even gave me a chance to duel!"

After hearing those words from his Youngest Junior Brother yesterday, such thoughts had completely vanished.

That was why Seo Wanpyeong was heading to the Wind Cliff Pavilion, where his Youngest Junior Brother was staying, again today. Even though he was not in a bad mood, he went willingly to duel his Youngest Junior Brother and teach him the sword.

While walking through the Demonic Cult’s main grounds, Seo Wanpyeong spotted a familiar man.

"Greetings, Eldest Senior Brother."

Wi Jinhak had been pacing like a puppy needing to relieve itself. He heard Seo Wanpyeong’s greeting and let out a hearty laugh.

"Uhahaha. It's been a while since we've met like this in broad daylight, Junior Brother."

His Senior Brother’s cheerful greeting made Seo Wanpyeong wonder if he was being mocked.

*Is he asking what a gloomy fellow who only seeks out dark places is doing wandering around in the daytime?*

He quickly shook his head to banish the negative thought. It could be his own misunderstanding.

*I should ask Youngest Junior Brother about this.*

With that thought, Seo Wanpyeong asked his Eldest Senior Brother, "By the way, is something troubling you?"

"A small problem has come up in the Red Dragon Brigade, and I'm wondering how to handle it. Hahaha."

At Wi Jinhak’s reply, Seo Wanpyeong nodded and said, "Is that so."

Just as Seo Wanpyeong belonged to the Dark Shadow Hall and handled the cult’s affairs, Eldest Senior Brother Wi Jinhak belonged to a military unit called the Red Dragon Brigade. The difference was that Seo Wanpyeong was a mid-level executive, while Wi Jinhak was the Commander of the Red Dragon Brigade.

"This is quite something. Leading the members to smash the skulls of those heathens is easy enough, but this matter is a bit complicated, so I'm troubled."

Watching his Eldest Senior Brother laugh heartily as he spoke, a thought occurred to Seo Wanpyeong.

"In that case, would you like to come with me?"

"Come with you? Where to?"

"I don't know what your troubles are, Senior Brother, but I have a feeling that child could give you some good advice."

"Oho. That sounds interesting!"

Without even asking who ‘that child’ was, Wi Jinhak laughed like a fool and followed his third Junior Brother.

{sep}

As usual, I was spending time with Seon'ah and Jin Hayeon.

"......"

The door to the Wind Cliff Pavilion opened, and two guests arrived. I recognized their faces and quickly performed a clasped hands greeting.

"Greetings, Eldest Senior Brother and Third Senior Brother."

Even as I greeted them, my mind raced.

*The Paranoid Senior Brother comes by all the time now, so that's one thing, but why is the Rage Group Leader Senior Brother here?*

The Paranoid Senior Brother kindly answered my unspoken question.

"It's good to see you, Youngest. Actually, I ran into Eldest Senior Brother on my way here. He mentioned he had something on his mind, so we came together."

"Is that what happened."

I replied reflexively, but I was inwardly dumbfounded.

*Seriously, why would you bring the Rage Group Leader Senior Brother here just because he has a problem?*

Eldest Senior Brother Wi Jinhak suffered from an anger control disorder because he practiced the Explosive Mara Sword Technique.

Rumors said he had reached a state close to the Transcendent Demon Realm, but the important thing was that he was not fully there yet. This meant one wrong word could trigger his anger control disorder.

The Paranoid Senior Brother, unaware of my thoughts, suddenly spoke to the Rage Group Leader Senior Brother.

"Eldest Senior Brother, you may have already heard, but our Youngest here is quite brilliant for his age. Perhaps he can solve your problem, so why don't you tell him about it?"

"Hahaha. Now that you mention it, I think I heard something like that from Master! In that case, I'll tell him."

After saying that, Eldest Senior Brother Wi Jinhak began to unload his troubles without asking for my opinion.

"Recently, the Vice Commander of our Red Dragon Brigade was recognized for his contributions and promoted to Commander of the Black Tiger Brigade, which had a vacancy. The problem is, instead of bringing in a Vice Commander from elsewhere, Master told me to choose one myself. From among the current group leaders of the Red Dragon Brigade."

Hearing it, it was certainly a headache of a problem.

*I have to promote only one person out of several. That's bound to cause a lot of problems.*

The problem seemed to be giving him quite a headache, because Eldest Senior Brother continued speaking at length.

"They each have their pros and cons, and since they're guys I've been with for a long time, it's even more troubling. All the current group leaders are men who've been with the Red Dragon Brigade since I first joined as a Vice Captain. One guy is skilled, but the problem is he slacks off too much, and another one is..."

Folding his fingers one by one, Eldest Senior Brother rattled off information about the group leaders. He spoke for a long time before finally finishing his explanation and asking, "That's the situation. In your opinion, Youngest, what do you think would be the best course of action?"

At Eldest Senior Brother’s question, I put on an expression of deep contemplation.

Third Senior Brother must have misunderstood my expression, because he said to me, "Speak your mind freely. Eldest Senior Brother may look fierce, but he's a great man who knows how to listen to others."

!!!

I flinched at Third Senior Brother’s words. He was fanning the flames.

*He just insulted the Rage Group Leader’s appearance to his face by saying he looks fierce!*

However, Eldest Senior Brother did not react as I did. He simply let out a hearty laugh.

"Uhahaha! There are quite a few friends who get scared when they see my face!"

I breathed an inward sigh of relief at his reaction.

*Phew. It seems he doesn't care much about comments on his appearance.*

However, I was not in the clear yet. I opened my mouth as cautiously as possible.

"I've thought about it hard, but honestly, I don't know either."

In truth, I had only pretended to think. I had planned to give this answer from the moment Eldest Senior Brother arrived.

*If I help him here, I'll just get more work. Am I crazy?*

It was the same in Korea. If you are good at your job, your workload increases. My life over the past two months had taught me this place was no different. I was already pestered by Master and Third Senior Brother and had no desire to add another burden.

The only problem was the fear that Eldest Senior Brother's rage might erupt.

To my surprise, he was not angry, which made my inner tension pointless. He wore a bitter expression. Actually, it was not Eldest Senior Brother, but Third Senior Brother.

*Why are you the one who's disappointed?*

Eldest Senior Brother, the one who had actually shared his problem, was not angry at all. He wore his characteristic vacant smile instead.

"Uhahaha. I guess it was a difficult problem for you too, Youngest Junior Brother."

For some reason, the disappointed Third Senior Brother even apologized to Eldest Senior Brother on my behalf.

"Ahem. I apologize, Eldest Senior Brother."

"Hahaha. What reason do you have to be sorry, Junior Brother? Hahaha. Second Senior Brother is vacant, so this kind of thing…"

Eldest Senior Brother, who was laughing heartily, turned his head to look around. His eyes met Seon'ah's.

"Oho. It's the little Young Lady of the Hyeokryeon Clan! I heard you've been visiting Youngest Junior Brother often recently."

"I'm not little."

Seon'ah replied in an uncharacteristically sulky tone.

"Ahahaha! It seems you're upset because this stupid Senior Brother took away your time to play with Youngest! Hahaha! What a cute Young Lady."

Perhaps her thoughts were read, or perhaps she was upset that Eldest Senior Brother kept taking up time. Seon'ah pouted her lips and whined, "First Young Lord is a fool."

The moment Seon'ah uttered those short words, a strange, inexplicable sensation washed over me.

"Ahahahaha!"

Eldest Senior Brother was bursting into his usual hearty, foolish laughter, but I felt a chill run down my spine.

Then, Third Senior Brother's urgent telepathy pierced my ears.

—Oh no! Junior Brother, get out of the way, now!

???

I looked at Third Senior Brother with a confused expression. Another telepathic message arrived.

—That's the reason Eldest Senior Brother hasn't reached the Transcendent Demon Realm yet! He no longer gets angry easily over anything else, but he cannot tolerate being called stupid. That's why even if Eldest Senior Brother calls himself stupid, you must never agree with him!

...

*What the hell is that.*

*It's okay for him to call himself stupid, but if someone else says it, he loses his mind? He's a complete lunatic.*

*Right. This place was a den of madmen.*

I reminded myself of that important fact.

"Ahahahaha! Did you just call me a fool?"

Eldest Senior Brother's rough laughter grew louder. At the same time, a violent wave of qi began to swirl around his body. It was directed at Seon'ah, the one who had provoked him.

Of course, Seon'ah was the precious daughter of the Hyeokryeon Clan, a prestigious family within the Demonic Cult. Harming her would cause problems even for Eldest Senior Brother, but if he considered such things before acting, he would not have an anger control disorder.

Eldest Senior Brother's laughter, laced with his inner arts, grew louder. The violent wave of qi raged toward Seon'ah.

Third Senior Brother and Jin Hayeon began to raise their inner arts to stop Eldest Senior Brother's rampage.

"Young Lady!!"

The Hyeokryeon Clan’s bodyguard, who had been waiting beyond the Wind Cliff Pavilion’s wall, rushed in.

"Eldest Senior Brother!! I've thought of it!!! The solution!!!"

I blurted out the first thing that came to mind to calm the situation.

Immediately, Eldest Senior Brother's gaze turned to me. He was still leaking a terrifying amount of qi.

My heart tightened, my chest grew heavy, and breathing became painful. I squeezed out what little inner arts I had and forced my voice out.

"Promoting the Team Three leader to Vice Commander is the best option!"

"The reason?"

Eldest Senior Brother’s eyes, now half-filled with madness, shot me a curt question as if his personality had completely changed.

The reason the Team Three leader should be promoted to Vice Commander? It was simple.

"You said he's the most meticulous at handling work, so he can take care of things for you when you're absent or busy, Eldest Senior Brother!"

He was the easiest person to delegate work to. Picking the most competent subordinate always makes life easier for the superior.

*Just like that damn Team Leader!!*

The solution came to me because it reminded me of the Team Leader from my time as Seo Jihun, the one who dumped work on me and left even at the moment of my death.

Of course, if the Team Three leader were competent in all areas, Eldest Senior Brother would not have needed to worry in the first place.

"You said the Team Three leader's martial skill is the lowest among the group leaders, but that doesn't matter! After all, you, Eldest Senior Brother, as the Commander, can more than make up for any lack of martial prowess. Therefore, the Vice Commander should be chosen as someone who can fill in your weaknesses, Eldest Senior Brother."

Eldest Senior Brother listened to my argument, unlike his usual raging self. When I finished speaking, he burst into a maniacal laugh.

"Hehahahahaha!!!"

Like a madman, no, befitting a madman, Eldest Senior Brother threw his head back and let out a heaven-shaking laugh. He then bizarrely lowered his head and looked at me with eyes full of madness.

GRIN.

"So, what you're saying is that because I'm stupid, I should promote the smart Team Three leader to Vice Commander."

As he spoke those words, the ground beneath Eldest Senior Brother's feet exploded. The madman with an anger control disorder charged at me, swinging a greatsword imbued with a massive, reddish aura.
