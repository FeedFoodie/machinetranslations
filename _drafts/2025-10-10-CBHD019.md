The Master must have found my opinion useful, because he ordered our return after breakfast. This meant the hellish movement arts training had begun again.

"Try to keep up."

Just like on the way to Wensu County, I alternated between riding in the carriage and running. After repeating this cycle, I finally returned to the Main Headquarters.

"Heok…"

I gasped, trying to catch my breath. The Master stepped leisurely from the carriage and spoke with a gentle smile.

"You’ve worked hard. Hohoho. It seems you’ve had enough movement arts training for today, so we shall skip the afternoon training with me."

The Master said it like he was doing me a great favor, but after all that hardship, was I supposed to be satisfied with such a reward?

"Hoo. Master. Then, when will I receive the Explosive Demonic Pill?"

The Explosive Demonic Pill was a miracle herb, the symbol of a miraculous encounter that always appears in martial arts novels. He had definitely promised to give it to me if I successfully completed this business trip.

*If I just take the miracle herbs, getting into the Demonic Academy will be much easier.*

If entering the Demonic Academy became easier, I wouldn't have to keep such a tight schedule. My dream and hope, a work-life balance, would finally arrive!

However, the Master shook his head. Just before the word ‘scammer’ escaped my lips, the old man added another sentence.

"Because your qi center has not yet grown enough, it is too soon to consume the Explosive Demonic Pill. I shall grant it to you when your qi center is a little more established and your acupoints have become stronger."

"Is that so…?" My tone was naturally skeptical.

*He’s not just trying to brush me off with words and pretend he forgot, is he?*

Having been burned so many times as Seo Jihun, suspicion was my first instinct.

*This isn't some crappy little company that doesn't keep its promises about performance bonuses, is it?*

The Heavenly Demon Divine Cult was massive enough to dwarf any conglomerate from the Republic of Korea. Still, recalling their slapdash way of handling things, it seemed entirely possible.

However, I couldn't run my mouth carelessly. Even if I ignored the evil old man, the religious fanatic Jin Hayeon would try to start a sword dance again.

A short while after the Master departed, leaving me with only a verbal promise, a knock echoed at the door.

KNOCK KNOCK.

The door to the Wind Cliff Pavilion slid open softly, and a familiar little kid’s face peeked in. The kid nervously surveyed the inside, but her face brightened when she saw me.

"Oppa~!"

Seon'ah ran into my arms with a pitter-patter. I patted her head and asked, "While I was gone, did you study and train hard?"

Seon'ah smiled brightly and chattered nonstop. She told me about the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult doctrines she had learned over the past two days. She also talked about how to kill people with the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique.

"……"

I wondered what the hell these crazy bastards were doing to a child, but since this was a heretic martial organization, it couldn't be helped. As I sighed internally, I realized my surroundings had gone quiet. Seon'ah had run out of things to brag about and was now looking up at me, her mouth closed.

"Our Seon'ah really worked hard to keep her promise with Oppa. Then, I should give Seon'ah a present too, right?"

I rummaged through my clothes and took out an item I had brought from Wensu County. It was a hairpin. The previous evening, while assessing the town's situation in Wensu County, I thought of the little kid waiting for me and bought one.

I stuck the hairpin into her long, reddish hair.

*Hmm. Am I doing this right?*

I had never used a hairpin before, so it felt a bit crooked, but Seon'ah looked purely happy with my gift.

As I watched Seon'ah's bright smile, a cold voice pierced my ears.

"Young Lady Seon'ah. Would you please return for today? The Young Lord has been busy with a tight schedule from yesterday until this morning and has not had enough time to study."

Seon'ah looked anxiously between me and Jin Hayeon. This was not a welcome situation. In this Demonic Cult full of psychopaths, my diary and this cute kid were the only two things keeping me sane. However, if I took Seon'ah's side, the two might needlessly come to resent each other.

"Instead of new material, I will conduct a personal review today. In its place, there is something I need Miss Jin to prepare separately."

"What should I prepare for you?"

"Preparations for a mock interview."

"A mock interview, you say?" Jin Hayeon asked, as if she had never heard the term. I had expected as much.

"Please prepare a mock interview based on the material Miss Jin has taught me over the past fortnight. Not only will it have the effect of practicing for the Demonic Academy entrance examination, but it will also allow me to check if there are any parts I have forgotten or missed."

The mock interview wasn't something I had just thought of on the spot. I had been thinking for a while that it would be good to do one around the two-week mark. These were things I learned during my long life as a student, but there are various hidden weapons methods in the world. Scientifically speaking, hidden weapons were ultimately the process of converting short-term memory into long-term memory, and the most important thing for turning short-term memory into long-term memory was, in the end, ‘repetition’. However, the effectiveness of that ‘repetition’ doesn't increase just by repeating something fifty times when you first learn it. The most efficient method was to recall it once in a while, just when you were about to forget. Review what you learned once on the same day, once the next day, once a week later, once a month later, once three months later, and once a year later. In that sense, a mock interview, or a practice test, was quite important. It allowed me to check which parts of my memory had become hazy and which parts I hadn't memorized, without needing to waste time rereading material that had already been converted to long-term memory. In other words, in addition to practicing for the test, it increases the efficiency of reviewing, and for this, the quality of the questions on the practice test was paramount.

"Please prepare questions at a level that would likely appear in the actual Demonic Academy entrance examination, and within the scope of what I have covered over the past fortnight, please prepare questions on every part, with as few omissions as possible."

At my instruction, Jin Hayeon looked uncharacteristically surprised. It was as if she hadn't expected me to think of such details.

"I plan to conduct a mock interview once every fortnight from now on. Oh, and since we're doing a mock interview, it would be good for Seon'ah to join as well. After all, Seon'ah is also studying things like the doctrines to enter the Demonic Academy, isn't she?"

After finishing my instructions to Jin Hayeon, I turned to look at Seon'ah.

"So, shall we check tomorrow just how well Seon'ah kept her promise?"

Seon'ah gulped, and her eyes trembled anxiously. She seemed to ponder for a moment, then suddenly pulled away from my embrace.

"I-I'll go study some more too."

With those words, Seon'ah left the Wind Cliff Pavilion in quick, short steps. The improperly secured hairpin wobbled as she went, catching my eye.

After that day, Ilmok's routine continued much the same, except for the mock interview. As instructed, Jin Hayeon prepared a brutally thorough set of questions, and Ilmok answered every single one perfectly.

"Wow! Oppa, how do you remember all that?"

Seon'ah, who took the mock interview with him, often looked at him with admiration in her bright, clear eyes. It wasn't that Seon'ah couldn't answer the questions. As the precious daughter of the renowned Hyeokryeon Clan, she had already read the book of doctrines several times.

However, because she was still young, Seon'ah's studying was closer to simple hidden weapons. Although she answered all of Jin Hayeon's questions, her answers were all short, single-sentence responses, at the level of reciting exactly what was written in the book of doctrines.

Ilmok, on the other hand, recited his answers with a fluency that 'silver-tongued' couldn't describe. When answering a question about a passage, he would cite related passages or use various analogies.

*…So he didn't bring up the mock interview just for Young Lady Seon'ah's sake.*

Naturally, Jin Hayeon began to see Ilmok in a slightly different light. He had truly understood and memorized everything he had learned over the past fortnight. Come to think of it, Ilmok had also been different in Wensu County.

*Perhaps, Young Lord Ilmok is truly a genius...*

In reality, he was just a middle-aged man accustomed to studying, but Jin Hayeon had no way of knowing that.

Regardless of Jin Hayeon's misunderstanding, Ilmok's daily life continued as usual. His external arts training with Jin Hayeon was on track, and he was slowly starting to build muscle. The same went for his martial arts training with the Heavenly Demon. After getting used to movement arts and movement arts during the trip, he now focused on executing the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword along with movement arts or movement arts.

For about a fortnight, he spent the rest of his time repeating qi circulation to increase his inner arts. His millet-sized qi center eventually grew to the size of a bean.

"At this rate, it seems it would be fine to start training in how to handle inner arts."

The Heavenly Demon's news made Ilmok's heart pound. Before teaching him, however, the Heavenly Demon first preached about the greatness of demonic art.

"Hohoho. There's no need to be surprised. The amount of inner arts currently accumulated in your qi center would take those hypocrites from the Central Plains at least two years to gather with their breathing techniques. Even with the divine techniques or ultimate arts the heathens of the Central Plains call their cultivation methods, it would take at least three months to gather this amount."

This meant an absorption speed three times faster than the arts of the Central Plains. Ilmok, however, understood this was just a play on words.

*But there are side effects, aren't there?*

Of course, Ilmok did not say this out loud.

"As expected! The demonic art of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult is truly the greatest in the world, Master."

"Hohoho. You understand very well."

The pleased Heavenly Demon laughed heartily and slowly drew the Heavenly Demon Sword from his side. He demonstrated the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw, the first form of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, and recited its secrets. After slowly finishing the draw and explanation, he sheathed his sword. He then began to draw it again while explaining the secrets in detail.

"As you grip the sword with your right hand, you must simultaneously draw qi up from your qi center and send it to the Qihai acupoint, and the flow of qi that begins from the Qihai acupoint…"

The Heavenly Demon finished his explanation in the time it took to slowly draw the sword once more. Ilmok asked, puzzled, "I-is that all?"

The secrets and the explanation were both shorter and less complicated than he expected.

"Hohoho. Demonic art emphasizes efficiency. Those hypocrites who call themselves the great orthodox sects, worried that someone might steal their martial arts, have written their secrets to be long and complicated with difficult, code-like words, but our demonic art is not like that. The flow of inner arts is the same. They deliberately circulate their inner arts in a complex and difficult way, but we do not utilize inner arts in such a manner."

"Is there a reason the hypocrites of the orthodox sects deliberately circulate their inner arts in such a difficult way?"

"Hohoho. It is because they are cowards. In their attempt to circulate inner arts while avoiding dangerous acupoints, their paths can't help but be complex, can they?"

"Ah…"

In other words, demonic arts didn't bother going around dangerous acupoints. They just charged straight through.

*Just like the crazy bastards they are...*

As Ilmok recalled the dangers of demonic art, the Heavenly Demon looked at him and said, "As you said, the secrets are simple, so you must have already memorized them all. Now, try to perform it yourself."

Ilmok took a few deep breaths to ease his tension and placed his hand on his sword's hilt.

"Since this is your first time, try to perform it slowly while recalling the secrets."

Even without the advice, Ilmok had intended to do just that. The Heavenly Demon said it passed through dangerous acupoints. Performing it recklessly could cripple him.

"Heup."

Ilmok took one last deep breath, held it, and slowly drew his sword. He reviewed the short secrets in his mind and circulated his inner arts accordingly.

ZIIIIING.

Surprisingly, even though he drew the sword slowly, a hum rang out as he completed the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw. It meant a great deal of power was imbued in the blade.

Fascinated, Ilmok looked at the humming blade and performed the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw several more times. His qi center was still only the size of a bean, so it emptied after just a few attempts.

"Assume the qi circulation posture. Let us begin again after you have refilled your qi center."

Following the instruction, Ilmok restored his inner arts through qi circulation. Then he performed the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw several more times. Once the secrets became familiar, he could perform the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw at his normal sword-swinging speed.

WIIIIING.

The sword let out an even clearer hum as it gained speed. Ilmok watched it, then furrowed his brow. He looked uncomfortable as he averted his gaze and began to look around.

"Is there a problem?" the Heavenly Demon asked.

Ilmok replied, "Well, my position seems a little strange."

"Position?"

Instead of answering, Ilmok took a step to his right.

"Huu. This is exactly the center of the Wind Cliff Pavilion. It felt uncomfortable being skewed to one side…"

Ilmok suddenly stopped talking as cold sweat ran down his back.

*Fuck.*

He realized the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword had begun.
