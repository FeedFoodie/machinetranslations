"Aaaah! O Lord of all demons!!"

"The Heavenly Demon has graced us with his presence!!"

The first residents to prostrate themselves and shout seemed to draw an even larger crowd. With each passing moment, more people gathered. At this rate, every single resident would flock to the narrow village entrance.

*I might not have known, but what is Master thinking?*

Could Master be an attention seeker?

*Then again, if he wasn't an attention seeker, he probably couldn't act as a heretic Cult Leader in the first place.*

While I was lost in these thoughts, Master advanced toward the villagers. His expression was more solemn than usual, and he exuded a strange pressure.

"Aaaah!"

"Behold the Heavenly Demon ! revered of all demons !!"

"The Heavenly Demon has come to save us!"

The residents who felt Master’s aura trembled as if they were on a Demonic Drug. Their faces contorted with emotion as they began speaking in tongues.

Master moved forward with excellent showmanship. He casually reached out to pat the heads of those prostrating nearby or lightly took the hands they had placed on the ground.

The reactions of those who received Master's 'blessing' were a sight to behold. Streaming tears was the bare minimum. Many collapsed with foam frothing at their mouths or went into convulsions while staring intently at the spot Master's hand had touched.

*What is this?*

I trotted behind Master and watched, feeling more scared than surprised. The scene was bizarre.

I was certain I had witnessed similar sights a few times back when I was Seo Jihun, not in person, but through the internet or Neotube. When a heretic Cult Leader pretended to shoot a palm blast, some heretic followers would tremble and collapse. When a heretic Cult Leader grabbed a follower's head and chanted a strange spell, the follower would convulse and foam at the mouth. It was the kind of scene I used to laugh off while watching it on the internet as Seo Jihun, thinking, ‘Why are those idiots like that?’

Seeing it in person, however, was more frightening than funny. I wondered why these people were so deeply engrossed in this heretic religion. They were ordinary commoners who had not even learned any demonic art.

Funnily enough, as I questioned their actions, I paradoxically began to understand Master's attitude.

*Is that why he insisted on coming in person instead of taking the carriage?*

They were religious fanatics, and in their devoted religion, Master was a god. A modern analogy would be the relationship between a superstar and crazed fans.

If a favorite entertainer just zipped past in a car after fans waited eagerly in line, they would feel empty, but what if that entertainer got out, shook everyone's hand, and took pictures with them? For existing fans, it was a display of showmanship that would make them fall completely head over heels. It was a method to turn fans into fanatics with just a little bit of effort.

I shuddered unknowingly at Master's trivial yet meticulous scheming.

{sep}

It took a full hour to reach our final destination, the Branch, as we passed the residents who had come to see Master. Once we arrived and looked back, the village entrance was not that far. We had spent an hour walking a distance that should have taken only fifteen minutes at a leisurely pace.

*They still haven't gone back.*

The sight of thousands of people prostrated on the ground, speaking in tongues all the way from the Demonic Cult Branch to the village entrance, was seared into my eyes.

A shout cut through the people’s babbling. "Behold the Heavenly Demon ! revered of all demons !! Wensu County Branch Manager Ma Gangil greets the Lord of all demons."

I turned my head and saw a burly man prostrating himself in front of the Branch building.

"Rise."

The man who rose at Master's command was a middle-aged man with a rugged face.

"Let's head inside first," Master said. "If this old man stays here any longer, it seems the common folk won't be able to get on with their livelihoods."

"I will obey the Heavenly Demon's command."

"Tell the branch members to soothe the residents and help them up after we've gone inside."

"As you command!"

Master gave his orders quickly and entered the building with the Branch Manager. Jin Hayeon and I followed them inside.

Branch Manager Ma Gangil guided us through the Branch. "You must be hungry after your long journey here, so I will prepare a meal first."

Master shook his head. "My youngest disciple trained on the way here and has sweated a lot. How about we wash up, rest a little, and then have our meal?"

At Master's words, the Branch Manager's gaze fixed on me.

The Branch Manager laughed cheerfully despite his rugged face and acted friendly toward me. "Ah, so you're the new disciple the Heavenly Demon took in? I've heard a lot about you. Hahaha."

"Then we shall begin the meal in about two hours. I will give orders to prepare warm water for bathing and purification, and in the meantime, I will guide the Heavenly Demon to a place where he can rest."

"Do so."

When their conversation ended, Jin Hayeon cautiously spoke to Master. "Then, until the bathwater is ready, I shall perform Body Purification for Young Lord Ilmok."

"You may."

Jin Hayeon and I headed to the room the Branch Manager showed us. I relaxed and received Jin Hayeon's Body Purification until the bathwater was ready.

"Haaah. I feel alive."

My body, exhausted from the brutally simple qi circulation and movement arts training, felt pleasantly languid after the Body Purification and the hot bath.

After I rested for some time, a martial artist from the Wensu County Branch whom I had never seen before guided us to the dining area. Seats were prepared there for Master, myself, and Branch Manager Ma Gangil.

"......"

After Master spoke and picked up the first bite of food with his chopsticks, our slightly late lunch began.

"By the way, how is the education of the Wensu County residents progressing?"

Just as I expected of an old-timer, Master relentlessly threw out work-related questions during the meal.

"Every day, my branch members and I go around the village looking after the residents. In the process, we are gradually teaching them the doctrines written in the Records of the Heavenly Demon. I would like to hand out the scriptures directly and have them study, but not many have learned enough characters to understand the Records of the Heavenly Demon, so we have no choice but to explain the doctrines verbally."

Master's questions and the Branch Manager's answers continued back and forth. Watching Branch Manager Ma Gangil, who was so tense answering questions that he could barely eat, was a pitiful sight.

NOM NOM.

Of course, I was famished from being worked hard since morning, so I was busy stuffing food into my mouth without a break.

When my hunger had somewhat subsided, Master's barrage of questions toward the Branch Manager finally ended. After finishing his assault, Master suddenly looked at me and asked, "Ilmok, what do you think?"

"...What do you mean?"

"Is there a way to change this place according to the system you mentioned last time?"

*He worked me hard since morning, and now he's trying to give me more work?*

I hid my dumbfounded feelings and replied, "I haven't observed enough yet, so nothing comes to mind right away."

"Hohoho. I suppose that's true. Then after the meal, have a look around the county with the Branch Manager."

"I will do so."

I figured I could just wander about the village like a tourist.

*Even if something comes to mind, I should just pretend I don't know and move on.*

I hated the idea of getting more work here.

Just as I lifted my chopsticks to finish the rest of the food, Master's voice pierced my ears. "If nothing suitable comes to mind even after looking around Wensu County, let's try visiting another place."

"Another place, you mean where...."

"Hohoho. There are plenty of places to go. Do you think there are only one or two villages that follow the doctrines of our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult?"

"......"

In other words, if I could not come up with a good idea, we would visit a different village each time. In that process, I would have to repeat what happened this morning every single time.

What's more, Xinjiang, where the Demonic Cult was based, was a ridiculously vast territory, far larger than North and South Korea combined.

*I'm screwed.*

It seemed that if I could not come up with a good idea by the end of the day, a journey comparable to a tour of the entire country would begin. A cross-country tour on foot, at that.

{sep}

After the suffocating meal with Master, I left the Branch with Jin Hayeon and Branch Manager Ma Gangil.

Branch Manager Ma Gangil forced a friendly-looking smile onto his rugged face. "Hahaha. It is an honor to be able to introduce Wensu County to you, Young Lord."

I was just some clueless young punk visiting someone else's workplace to inspect the site, but he showed no signs of displeasure at all.

As Branch Manager Ma Gangil guided me through Wensu County, I found it had the atmosphere of an ordinary village, unlike the frenzied mood when we first arrived.

*Then again, three hours have already passed since Master entered the Branch.*

Under the crimson sky where the sun was just beginning to set, villagers bustled about, busy with their livelihoods.

Branch Manager Ma Gangil, in contrast to his rugged face, affably approached a village elder. "Oh my. You're not feeling well, you should have told us."

"Aigoo. How could I burden people who are busy with important work with something like this?"

"Hahahaha. Helping all of you is the important work we're in charge of, Elder." He naturally took the bundle the stooped old man was carrying and continued the conversation.

Watching this, I cautiously asked the Elder, "Elder. Could I ask what you meant by 'important work' just now?"

The Elder's gaze turned toward Branch Manager Ma Gangil, a look that said, *Who is this young bachelor to be asking such a question?*

Branch Manager Ma Gangil, not flustered by my sudden interruption, smiled and said to the Elder, "Hahaha. Didn't you see him earlier? He is the Heavenly Demon's disciple."

As soon as he finished his answer, the Elder was startled and abruptly tried to kneel on the ground. I quickly lunged to catch the Elder, preventing myself from being bowed to by a man several times my age.

"You don't have to do that, it's alright."

"H-how could I not show respect to the Heavenly Demon's disciple?"

"It's fine. I've come out in secret right now, so it will be more troublesome if I attract people's attention."

My words made the Elder finally give up on bowing, though he still seemed somewhat restless. He looked uncomfortable, so I asked my question again to finish my business quickly and leave. "Elder, could you tell me what the 'important work' you mentioned earlier is?"

"Th-that would be the work of protecting us, wouldn't it?"

The story the Elder began to tell was simpler than I expected.

Originally, this Xinjiang region was mostly mountains and deserts, so there were quite a few mounted bandits and mountain bandits based in oases or somewhere in the mountain ranges. On top of that, there were criminals crossing over from other countries beyond the mountains. In an already harsh environment with many mountains and deserts making it hard to get by, such villains ran rampant, creating a hellish environment.

Amidst all that, the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult put down roots here in Xinjiang. The number of villains decreased sharply, and the world became a slightly more livable place for the common people.

"This is a story I heard from my great-grandfather when I was young, but he said that back then, they worried every single day that mounted bandits or mountain bandits might attack."

"...Pardon?"

I asked with a puzzled expression. Jin Hayeon, who was listening beside me, opened her mouth. "Our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult came to this place about two hundred years ago, Young Lord. Since then, we have gradually expanded our influence, and now we have completely put down our roots in the Billion Mountains."

Her explanation reminded me of what she had told me before.

*Ah, now that I think about it, she did say they were branded as traitors by the imperial family about two hundred years ago.*

It seemed they had crossed over from the Central Plains to Xinjiang back then and had been stealthily expanding the territory under their control.

"It's less frequent compared to the time my great-grandfather lived, but even now, mounted bandits and mountain bandits sometimes appear, and whenever that happens, the warriors of the Divine Cult protect us. A few years ago, my son ran into a group of mounted bandits while heading toward Ürümqi for business, but even then, the warriors of the Divine Cult saved my son."

At the Elder's praise, Branch Manager Ma Gangil added, perhaps to avoid taking credit for another's achievement, "The ones who saved your son back then were likely one of the martial corps on patrol, Young Lord Ilmok."

"To be honest, Wensu County is close to the Main Headquarters, so such villains rarely appear here. Therefore, we mainly focus on work that helps the villagers with their livelihood, like this."

After hearing all that, I could more or less grasp how things were run.

*Are the Branches in each region a kind of parish?*

They looked after the common people of each region, helped with miscellaneous tasks, and spread the doctrine. They also served as a sort of security force, suppressing outsiders who caused trouble or bandits who ran rampant.

I now understood why even the ordinary commoners had become religious fanatics like the demonic practitioners of the Main Headquarters. For nearly two hundred years, the cult had been helping them from the sidelines while subtly and continuously injecting its doctrines. I felt that even I would have been brainwashed.

However, there was another way to look at it.

*To think they've been doing things the same way for two hundred years, it makes the civil servant system of the Republic of Korea feel like a new piece of civilization by comparison.*

In many ways, I felt fed up.

Still, from my perspective, the situation was not so bad.

*At this rate, there'll be more than enough things to change, won't there?*

If it was running on such a one-dimensional system, there were many areas to tamper with.

Of course.

*I should only touch the bare minimum, so that as little work as possible falls on me.*

I had not the slightest intention of falling into work hell again.
