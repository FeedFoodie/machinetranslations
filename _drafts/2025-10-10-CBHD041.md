I was too disoriented to notice during the first attack, but I realized the truth while blocking the second. His technique looked similar to Third Senior Brother's at a glance, but it was subtly different. I knew because I had dueled Third Senior Brother dozens of times and watched him demonstrate the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art.

This man was not my Senior Brother on a rampage. He was a real assassin, here to kill me.

*Why me?*

The question surfaced, but I had no time to dwell on it. Survival was the priority.

SHK!

I retreated as he lunged, but I was too slow. His blade lightly cut my chest. I had nowhere left to run.

*Damn it.*

My back hit the wall, and the man lunged to finish me, swinging his sword. I gave up on my broken sword. I swung my right hand and threw the half-blade at him.

He twisted his body and easily dodged it.

I then imbued my left hand with inner arts and swung.

FWIP!

The other half of the blade, still in its scabbard, flew toward him like a hidden weapon.

"......"

The assassin, caught by surprise, swung his sword to deflect the scabbard.

WHOOSH!

I used my movement arts to their fullest and launched myself sideways, away from the wall.

"......"

A moment later, the wall where I had just stood was sliced apart. I thrust my right hand toward the assassin's back, mimicking a stab. I now held the scabbard I had somehow switched to my right hand.

It was a technique from the Soul-Reaping Flash, but performed with a scabbard instead of a sword. The grip was awkward without a hilt, but my survival instincts guided me to execute the most perfect strike of my life.

Maybe he was off-balance from swinging at the wall, or maybe my swordsmanship was better than he expected. Instead of a fluid counter, the assassin decisively rolled on the floor to evade my strike. It was the Lazy Donkey Roll. As expected of an assassin, he showed no wounded pride.

But I was not satisfied with just seeing him roll once.

SWOOOOSH!

I swung the scabbard at the rolling man. He rose from his roll and boldly met my scabbard with his sword.

SHK!

SHK!

His sword sliced off a piece of the scabbard with every clash. It was an absurd sight. Soon, the scabbard was half its original length, and the man had fully regained his posture.

As he lunged again, I threw the last piece of the scabbard at him.

SHK!

He futilely cut it down.

But I let out a sigh of relief.

"......"

"Young Lord!!"

"Don't tell me the Third Young Master has come to visit again!?"

Jin Hayeon and the two bodyguards from the Wind Cliff Pavilion had reached the room's entrance. My life was on the line, so the fight felt incredibly long, but only a few seconds had actually passed.

"It's not my Senior Brother!" I shouted.

"!!!"

Gwan Muyeol entered the room just as I finished.

"......"

The assassin abandoned his mission and threw himself toward the window.

"......"

A gruesome piercing sound followed.

"I'll give chase!" Jang Hoe shouted. He had apparently anticipated the escape and moved toward the window in advance.

After the assassin escaped, I took a deep breath to calm myself.

"......"

It was only a few seconds of fighting, nothing compared to my usual training, but my heart pounded and sweat poured from my body. Jin Hayeon approached without me noticing. RIIIIP! She ripped a piece of her clothing and pressed it against my wounds.

"Are you all right, Young Lord?"

She applied Golden Healing Medicine to the cuts on my forearm and chest, then wrapped them with a bandage. As I came to my senses, I scrunched up my face. The pain was not setting in.

*Damn it.*

The room was a complete mess. I felt an urge to clean it immediately. Sleep had fled, which would ruin my schedule for tomorrow. It was ridiculous. My life had just been on the line, yet these were the things I noticed, but the real reason for my sigh was not the mess or my schedule.

"Miss Jin."

"Yes, Young Lord."

"I'm sorry, but could you bring me a spare sword?"

"A sword?" she asked, her expression puzzled, which was unlike her.

I sighed deeply. "I think I need to train for a bit."

The sword paths I used against the assassin flickered in my mind. They were more perfect than ever, unconsciously executed while my life was on the line. I felt I would go crazy if I did not make them my own right now.

*And I was being so careful.*

That damn assassin had forced my Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword to advance.

{sep}

As expected, the cult was in an uproar by morning. Jang Hoe pursued the wounded assassin, but the man reportedly took his own life. Suicide does not hide everything, so a full-scale investigation began with the corpse.

Around noon, much earlier than usual, Master visited the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Is your body all right?"

"It wasn't a very serious injury, Master."

Master nodded, his expression grave. "I've come to tell you what we've figured out so far and to check on your condition." He then began to explain what they knew.

"The man who tried to assassinate you was named Chugak, from the Dark Shadow Hall. After investigating his activities and circumstances, it's being said that there's a high probability he was a spy for the Murim Alliance."

"The Murim Alliance, you say?"

"That's right. We are now investigating anyone who had even the slightest connection to him to see if there are other spies."

A Murim Alliance spy in the Demonic Cult was not unusual. The Demonic Cult had investigated the Murim Alliance to find who was behind Uncle Taehyun's assassination, so the reverse was certainly possible. Still, something did not make sense.

"Why on earth would the Murim Alliance target me?"

I was a disciple of the Heavenly Demon, but I was just a novice who had joined only six months ago. If they wanted to target Master's disciple, it made more sense to go after my Senior Brothers.

"As for the reason, I can think of two major possibilities."

"Not just one, but two?"

"That's right. First, it's because of the school. The school you proposed is already operating throughout Xinjiang, and it's widely known within the cult that it was your idea. Not only does it lead to the cult's development, but wouldn't they think it's better to kill a child with insufficient martial arts now, in advance?"

He meant cutting the weeds and pulling out the roots. I did not think the school was a big deal, but others apparently saw it as a groundbreaking change. While I pondered this, Master explained the second reason.

"And the other reason is that your existence is an obstacle to the Murim Alliance's propaganda."

"...Don't tell me it's because of that thing you mentioned last time?"

Master nodded at my question. The 'thing from last time' meant that. The story about Uncle Taehyun. Those bastards from the Murim Alliance killed Uncle Taehyun and then framed Master for it. They even spread a rumor that I, the waiter, had also died in the process, and yet, not only is that waiter perfectly alive, but he has also become a disciple of the Heavenly Demon. They could probably insist they were right, but it was a story that could certainly be an annoyance to them.

*So they thought they might as well kill the annoying guy while they were at it.*

The situation was troubling. I thought I was the only one targeting the Flying Dragon Hall Director, but the Murim Alliance already knew I existed and was trying to assassinate me.

{sep}

Not long after Master left to handle the remaining matters, the door to the Wind Cliff Pavilion was thrown open. "Oppa!!" Seon'ah ran in, her face pale, and fell with a THUD. However, as if that didn't matter, she quickly got up and ran to me, even though her face and arms were red and scraped from the floor. I was the one who almost died, but instead, this little kid clung to me looking like a drenched cat. It was a strange feeling. To have someone who cherished me this much. It was probably partly due to the side effects of the demonic art, though.

"Wh-who did it? How dare they target you, Oppa. I-I'll kill them all!"

"……"

Her way of showing concern was a bit off. Come to think of it, her eyes and hair seemed a little redder than when I first met her. Worried about her condition, I stroked Seon'ah's head.

"Ahem. He's already dead, Seon'ah, so you don't need to kill him. By the way, you waited until the promised time, didn't you?"

I meant it as a compliment, but her eyes grew redder.

"Everyone was hiding the fact that you were in danger, Oppa. When I get back, I won't let them get away with it."

"……"

*Is the Hyeokryeon Clan about to be torn to shreds?*

Well, that was not my business, so I kept my mouth shut.

Meanwhile, more visitors arrived after Seon'ah.

"Little Brother! Are you all right?!"

Eldest Senior Brother arrived with a shocked face just like Seon'ah's, but his expression was more grotesque than cute as he shouted.

"I'll submit a proposal for a Western Pacification to Master and the Guardian Hall! To think that my precious little brother was almost killed by a mere spy from the Murim Alliance! Does this make any sense?!"

"That's right!!" Seon'ah chimed in, and the two of them joined forces, yelling together.

"Little Brother is a talent who must do great things and lead the cult alongside me, Wi Jinhak! For such a talent to be so easily on the verge of death! This won't do. I will make sure to increase your bodyguard martial artists!"

"I'll protect you too, Oppa!"

Third Senior Brother, who had come with Eldest Senior Brother, looked completely dejected.

"I'm sorry for not being of any help at a time like this. This worthless Senior Brother was busy controlling his qi deviation until just a moment ago, not even knowing his Junior Brother was on the brink of death."

"What are you talking about? If it weren't for you, third Senior Brother, I'd be dead."

You could say that third Senior Brother's frequent night raids had served as a rehearsal. If I hadn't been prepared in advance, I would have gone straight to the afterlife without even waking from my dreams. Honestly, it was annoying how he startled me every time he showed up in the middle of the night, but it had turned out to be a blessing in disguise.

Third Senior Brother still looked gloomy despite my words, so I asked him a question.

"By the way, you were suffering from qi deviation?"

At my question, third Senior Brother gave a small nod.

It was a strange feeling.

*I had thought the assassin might be my Senior Brother suffering from qi deviation, but to think he was actually suffering from qi deviation for real.*

If he had come with the assassin and launched a pincer attack, the situation would have been mind-boggling.

"Hm?"

A questioning sound escaped my lips as my thoughts reached that point. The others looked at me, puzzled, but I ignored them, too focused on my sudden thought. Normally, I would have dismissed the thought, but I could not let it go.

I pondered various possibilities until I reached a single conclusion.

*Perhaps... he was not an assassin sent by the Murim Alliance.*
