Chapter 41: Assassination (3)

During the first attack, I was too disoriented to notice.
However, I realized it while blocking the second attack.
At a glance, it looked similar, but it was subtly different from my third Senior Brother's.
I knew because I had engaged in dozens of training duels with my third Senior Brother, and he had demonstrated the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art for me several times.
In other words, this guy wasn't my third Senior Brother on a rampage. He was a real assassin who had come to kill me.
*Why me?*
The question arose, but this was no time to dwell on such thoughts.
SHK!
For now, survival was the priority.
I retreated the moment he lunged, but I was a little too slow this time, and my chest was lightly cut.
And there was nowhere left to retreat.
*damn it.*
The moment my back hit the wall, the man lunged as if to finish me off, swinging his sword.
I decisively gave up on my broken sword.
I swung my right hand, which held the half-bladed sword, and threw it at him.
And in the instant he twisted his body to the side and easily dodged the sword.
This time, I imbued my left hand with inner arts and swung it.
FWIP!
The other half of the blade, still in its scabbard and which I hadn't been able to draw, flew toward him like a hidden weapon.
CLANG!
While the assassin, perhaps not having predicted this one, swung his sword to strike the half-blade away.
WHOOSH!
I used my movement arts to their fullest, launching myself sideways away from the dead-end wall.
SLASH!
Right after I moved, the wall my back had been against was sliced apart with a sharp sound.
At the same time, I thrust my right hand toward the back of the assassin who had just swung his sword at the wall, as if performing a stab.
In my right hand, I now held the scabbard I had somehow switched over.
A technique of the Soul-Reaping Flash, executed not with a sword, but with a scabbard.
Without a hilt, even gripping it was awkward, but perhaps due to my survival instincts, it was the most perfectly executed strike I had ever performed.
Perhaps it was because he had just swung his sword at the wall, or perhaps my swordsmanship was of a higher level than he expected.
Instead of his usual fluid counters, the assassin decisively rolled on the floor to evade my strike.
The technique of the Lazy Donkey Roll. However, as expected of an assassin, he showed no sign of his pride being wounded by such a move.
And I, too, had no intention of being satisfied just by seeing the Lazy Donkey Roll once.
SWOOOOSH!
I swung the scabbard again at the man who had rolled on the floor.
However, as he rose from his roll, he boldly swung his sword to meet my scabbard.
SHK!
SHK!
Each time his sword and my scabbard clashed, a piece of the scabbard was sliced off.
It was an utterly absurd sight.
Before I knew it, the scabbard's length had been reduced by half, and the man had regained his posture and stood up completely.
The moment he tried to lunge at me again, I decisively threw the last remaining piece of the scabbard at him.
SHK!
And though that final act of resistance was futilely cut down.
"Huu."
I, on the contrary, let out a sigh of relief.
"Young Lord!!"
"Don't tell me the Third Young Master has come to visit again!?"
It was because Jin Hayeon and the two bodyguard martial artists who resided at the Wind Cliff Pavilion had reached the entrance of the room.
Because my life was on the line, it felt like an incredibly long time had passed, but in reality, it had all happened in just a few seconds.
"It's not my Senior Brother!"
"!!!"
As soon as my shout fell, Gwan Muyeol entered the room.
CRACK!
The assassin, perhaps abandoning his mission, suddenly threw himself toward the window.
CRACK!
At the same time, the gruesome sound of something being pierced could be heard.
"I'll give chase!"
Jang Hoe's shout followed.
It seemed he had anticipated the man's escape and had moved toward the window in advance.
Right after the assassin escaped.
"Huu."
I once again calmed my mind and body with a deep breath.
It was only a few seconds of flinging my body around and swinging a sword. Compared to my usual training regimen, it was nothing, but my heart was pounding as if it would burst, and sweat poured from my entire body.
Just then, Jin Hayeon, who had approached without me noticing, ripped a piece of her clothing with a RIIIIP and began to press it against my body.
"Are you all right, Young Lord?"
She started applying Golden Healing Medicine to the cuts on my right forearm and chest from the assassin, then began to wrap them with a bandage.
Finally coming to my senses, I mercilessly scrunched up my face.
It wasn't because the pain from the wounds was belatedly setting in.
*Damn it.*
The room was a complete mess.
I felt like I needed to clean the room right away. On top of that, sleep had completely fled, so my schedule for tomorrow was bound to be affected.
It was ridiculous. My life had been on the line just moments ago, yet now, things like this caught my attention more.
However, the real reason I sighed was not because of the cleaning or tomorrow's schedule.
"Miss Jin."
"Yes, Young Lord."
"I'm sorry to ask, but could you bring me a spare sword?"
"A sword?"
She asked with a puzzled expression, which was unlike her.
And I, letting out a deep sigh, replied.
"I think I need to train with the sword for a bit."
The swordsmanship I had used against the assassin moments ago kept flickering before my eyes.
The sword paths, more perfect than ever, that I had unconsciously executed while my life hung by a thread.
I felt like I would go crazy if I didn't make them my own right now.
*And I was being so careful.*
Thanks to that damn assassin, it seemed the state of my Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword had advanced.
★★★
As was to be expected, the cult was in an uproar from the early morning.
Jang Hoe pursued the assassin he had wounded, but it was said the assassin chose to take his own life in the end.
Of course, suicide doesn't hide everything. A full-scale investigation began with the man's corpse.
And around noon, much earlier than usual, Master came to visit the Wind Cliff Pavilion.
"Is your body all right?"
"It wasn't a very serious injury, Master."
The Master, who nodded at my answer, wore a grave expression.
"For now, I've come to inform you of what we've figured out so far and to check on your condition."
With that, Master began to explain what they currently knew.
"The one who attempted to assassinate you was a man named Chugak from the Dark Shadow Hall. And as a result of investigating his activities and circumstances, it's being said that there's a high probability he was a spy for the Murim Alliance."
"The Murim Alliance, you say?"
"That's right. We are now investigating anyone who had even the slightest connection to him to see if there are other spies."
It was nothing unusual for the Demonic Cult to have a spy from the Murim Alliance.
After all, the Demonic Cult had dug up information on the Murim Alliance to identify the masterminds behind Uncle Taehyun's assassination, so the reverse was certainly possible.
However, there was still something I couldn't understand.
"Why on earth would the Murim Alliance target me?"
I may be a disciple of the Heavenly Demon, but I was just a novice who had joined the cult a mere six months ago.
If they were targeting me for being Master's disciple, it would make more sense for them to go after my Senior Brothers, not me.
"As for the reason, I can think of two major possibilities."
"Not just one, but two?"
"That's right. First, it's because of the school. The school you proposed is already operating throughout Xinjiang, and it's widely known within the cult that it was your idea. Not only does it lead to the cult's development, but wouldn't they think it's better to kill a child with insufficient martial arts now, in advance?"
He was talking about cutting the weeds and pulling out the roots.
I thought the school was no big deal, but perhaps others saw it as a groundbreaking change.
While I was pondering this, Master explained the second reason.
"And the other reason is that your existence is an obstacle to the Murim Alliance's propaganda."
"...Don't tell me it's because of that thing you mentioned last time?"
The Master nodded at my question.
The 'thing from last time' meant that. The story about Uncle Taehyun.
Those bastards from the Murim Alliance killed Uncle Taehyun and then framed Master for it.
They even spread a rumor that I, the waiter, had also died in the process.
And yet, not only is that waiter perfectly alive, but he has also become a disciple of the Heavenly Demon.
They could probably insist they were right, but it was a story that could certainly be an annoyance to them.
*So they thought they might as well kill the annoying guy while they were at it.*
It was a troubling situation in many ways.
I thought I was the only one targeting the Flying Dragon Hall Director, but it turns out the Murim Alliance was already aware of my existence and, on top of that, was even trying to assassinate me.
★★★
Not long after Master left to handle the remaining matters, the door to the Wind Cliff Pavilion was thrown open.
"Oppa!!"
Seon'ah, her face pale, came running in haste and fell to the floor with a THUD.
However, as if that didn't matter, she quickly got up and ran to me, even though her face and arms were red and scraped from the floor.
I was the one who almost died, but instead, this little kid clung to me looking like a drenched cat.
It was a strange feeling.
To have someone who cherished me this much. It was probably partly due to the side effects of the demonic art, though.
"Wh-who did it? How dare they target you, Oppa. I-I'll kill them all!"
"……"
Her way of showing concern seemed a bit... off.
Come to think of it, I had a feeling her eyes and hair had become a little redder compared to when I first saw her.
Somehow worried about the child's condition, I stroked Seon'ah's head and said.
"Ahem. He's already dead, so there's no need for you to kill him, Seon'ah. By the way, you managed to wait until the promised time, didn't you?"
I meant it as a compliment, but Seon'ah's eyes grew even redder.
"Everyone was hiding the fact that you were in danger, Oppa. When I get back, I won't let them get away with it."
"……"
Is the Hyeokryeon Clan about to be torn to shreds?
Well, that side of things wasn't my business, so I decided to just keep my mouth shut.
In the meantime, new visitors arrived one after another, following Seon'ah.
"Little Brother! Are you all right?!"
It was Eldest Senior Brother, who had arrived with a shocked face just like Seon'ah, but unlike her, he shouted with a face that was more grotesque than cute.
"I will submit a proposal for a Western Pacification to Master and the Guardian Hall! To think that my precious little brother was almost killed by a mere spy from the Murim Alliance! Does this make any sense?!"
"That's right!!"
When Seon'ah chimed in with Eldest Senior Brother's shout, the two of them suddenly joined forces and started yelling.
"Little Brother is a talent who must do great things and lead the cult alongside me, Wi Jinhak! For such a talent to be so easily on the verge of death! This won't do. I will make sure to increase your bodyguard martial artists!"
"I'll protect you too, Oppa!"
On the other hand, the third Senior Brother, who had come with Eldest Senior Brother, looked completely dejected.
"I'm sorry for not being of any help at a time like this. This worthless Senior Brother was busy controlling his qi deviation until just a moment ago, not even knowing his Junior Brother was on the brink of death."
"What are you talking about? If it weren't for you, third Senior Brother, I would have been as good as dead."
You could say that third Senior Brother's frequent night raids had served as a rehearsal.
If I hadn't been prepared in advance, I would have gone straight to the afterlife without even waking from my dreams.
Honestly, it was annoying how he startled me every time he showed up in the middle of the night, but it had turned out to be a blessing in disguise.
However, since third Senior Brother still looked gloomy despite my consolation, I casually threw him a question.
"By the way, you were suffering from qi deviation?"
At my question, third Senior Brother gave a small nod.
It was a strange feeling.
*I had thought the assassin might be my Senior Brother suffering from qi deviation, but to think he was actually suffering from qi deviation for real.*
If that man had come with the assassin and launched a pincer attack, it would have been a truly mind-boggling situation.
"Hm?"
As my thoughts reached that point, a questioning sound escaped my lips.
Those around me looked at me with puzzled expressions, but I was too focused on the thought that had suddenly occurred to me to pay them any mind.
Normally, it was a thought I would have dismissed, but for some reason, I couldn't let it go.
After pondering and considering various possibilities, I was able to reach a single conclusion.
*Perhaps... he wasn't an assassin sent by the Murim Alliance.*
