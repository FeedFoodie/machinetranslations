I was too disoriented to notice during the first attack. I only realized it while blocking the second. The technique looked similar to my third Senior Brother's at a glance, but it was subtly different. I knew because I had dueled him dozens of times and watched him demonstrate the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art.

This man wasn't my third Senior Brother on a rampage. He was a real assassin, here to kill me.

*Why me?*

The question surfaced, but I had no time to think about it. Survival was my only priority.

SHK!

I retreated as he lunged, but I was too slow. His blade lightly cut my chest. I had nowhere left to run.

*Damn it.*

My back hit the wall. The man lunged to finish me, swinging his sword. I gave up on my broken sword. I swung my right hand and threw the half-bladed sword at him. He easily twisted aside to dodge it. In that instant, I imbued my left hand with inner arts and swung.

FWIP!

The other half of the blade, still in its scabbard, flew toward him like a hidden weapon.

"......"

The assassin hadn't predicted this. He swung his sword to strike the scabbarded blade away.

WHOOSH!

I used my movement arts to their fullest and launched myself sideways, away from the wall.

"......"

A moment later, the wall where I had just been was sliced apart with a sharp sound. I thrust my right hand toward the assassin's back as he faced the wall. I now held the scabbard, having somehow switched it to my right hand. It was a technique from the Soul-Reaping Flash, but executed with a scabbard instead of a sword.

The grip was awkward without a hilt, but my survival instincts made it the most perfect strike I had ever performed. Maybe he was off-balance from striking the wall, or maybe my swordsmanship was better than he expected. Instead of his usual fluid counters, the assassin decisively rolled on the floor to evade my strike.

It was the Lazy Donkey Roll. As expected of an assassin, he showed no wounded pride. I had no intention of letting him off with just one roll.

SWOOOOSH!

I swung the scabbard again at the rolling man. He rose from his roll and boldly swung his sword to meet it.

SHK!

SHK!

Each time our weapons clashed, his sword sliced off a piece of my scabbard. The sight was utterly absurd. Soon, the scabbard was half its original length. The man had regained his posture and stood up completely. As he lunged at me again, I threw the last piece of the scabbard at him.

SHK!

He futilely cut down my final act of resistance.

"......"

But I sighed in relief.

"Young Lord!!"

"Don't tell me the Third Young Master has come to visit again!?"

Jin Hayeon and the two bodyguards from the Wind Cliff Pavilion had reached the room's entrance. My life had been on the line, so it felt like a long time had passed. In reality, it had only been a few seconds.

"It's not my Senior Brother!" I shouted.

"!!!"

Gwan Muyeol entered the room just as I finished.

"......"

The assassin abandoned his mission and threw himself toward the window.

"......"

A gruesome piercing sound followed.

"I'll give chase!" Jang Hoe shouted. He must have anticipated the escape and moved toward the window in advance.

The assassin escaped.

"......"

I took a deep breath to calm my mind and body. I had only fought for a few seconds. It was nothing compared to my usual training, but my heart pounded as if it would burst, and sweat poured from my body.

Jin Hayeon approached without me noticing.

RIIIIP!

She ripped a piece of her clothing and pressed it against my wounds.

"Are you all right, Young Lord?"

She applied Golden Healing Medicine to the cuts on my forearm and chest, then wrapped them with a bandage. I finally came to my senses and scrunched up my face. It wasn't because of the pain.

*Damn it.*

The room was a complete mess. I felt an immediate urge to clean it. Sleep had also fled, which would mess up my schedule for tomorrow. It was ridiculous. My life was just on the line, yet these things bothered me more, but the real reason I sighed wasn't the cleaning or my schedule.

"Miss Jin."

"Yes, Young Lord."

"I'm sorry to ask, but could you bring me a spare sword?"

"A sword?" she asked, her expression puzzled, which was unlike her.

I sighed deeply. "I think I need to train with the sword for a bit."

The swordsmanship I had just used against the assassin flickered in my mind. The sword paths were more perfect than ever, unconsciously executed while my life hung by a thread. I felt like I would go crazy if I didn't make them my own right now.

*And I was being so careful.*

Thanks to that damn assassin, it seemed my Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword had advanced.

{sep}

As expected, the cult was in an uproar the next morning. Jang Hoe pursued the wounded assassin, but the man reportedly took his own life. Suicide doesn't hide everything, so a full-scale investigation began with the corpse. Around noon, much earlier than usual, the Master visited the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Is your body all right?"

"It wasn't a very serious injury, Master."

The Master nodded, his expression grave. "For now, I've come to tell you what we've figured out and to check on your condition."

He then began to explain what they knew.

"The man who tried to assassinate you was named Chugak, from the Dark Shadow Hall. After investigating his activities, we believe there's a high probability he was a spy for the Murim Alliance."

"The Murim Alliance?"

"That's right. We're now investigating anyone connected to him to see if there are other spies."

It wasn't unusual for the Demonic Cult to have a Murim Alliance spy. The Demonic Cult had investigated the Murim Alliance to find the masterminds behind Uncle Taehyun's assassination, so the reverse was certainly possible. Still, I couldn't understand one thing.

"Why on earth would the Murim Alliance target me?"

I was a disciple of the Heavenly Demon, but I was just a novice who had joined six months ago. If they were targeting the Master's disciple, it made more sense to go after my Senior Brothers.

"I can think of two major reasons," the Master said.

"......"

"That's right. First, the school. The school you proposed is already operating throughout Xinjiang, and it's widely known that it was your idea. It contributes to the cult's development. They probably think it's better to kill a child with insufficient martial arts now, in advance."

He meant cutting the weeds and pulling out the roots. I thought the school was no big deal, but others must have seen it as a groundbreaking change. While I pondered this, the Master explained the second reason.

"The other reason is that your existence is an obstacle to the Murim Alliance's propaganda."

"...Don't tell me it's because of that thing you mentioned last time?"

The Master nodded. The 'thing from last time' was the story about Uncle Taehyun. The Murim Alliance bastards killed Uncle Taehyun and framed the Master. They even spread a rumor that I, the waiter, had also died.

Yet, not only was that waiter alive, but he had also become a disciple of the Heavenly Demon. They could insist they were right, but my existence was certainly an annoyance to them.

*So they thought they might as well kill the annoying guy while they were at it.*

The situation was troubling. I thought I was the only one targeting the Flying Dragon Hall Director, but the Murim Alliance already knew about me and was even trying to kill me.

{sep}

Soon after the Master left, the door to the Wind Cliff Pavilion was thrown open.

"Oppa!!"

Seon'ah ran in, her face pale. She fell to the floor with a THUD but quickly got back up and ran to me, her face and arms red and scraped.

I was the one who almost died, but she clung to me like a drenched cat. It was a strange feeling to have someone cherish me so much, though it was probably a side effect of the demonic art.

"Wh-who did it? How dare they target you, Oppa. I-I'll kill them all!"

"……"

Her way of showing concern seemed a bit off. Come to think of it, her eyes and hair looked a little redder than when I first saw her. Worried about her condition, I stroked Seon'ah's head.

"Ahem. He's already dead, so there's no need for you to kill him, Seon'ah. By the way, you managed to wait until the promised time, didn't you?"

I meant it as a compliment, but Seon'ah's eyes grew even redder.

"Everyone was hiding that you were in danger, Oppa. When I get back, I won't let them get away with it."

"……"

*Is the Hyeokryeon Clan about to be torn to shreds?*

Well, that wasn't my business, so I kept my mouth shut. More visitors arrived, following Seon'ah.

"Little Brother! Are you all right?!"

Eldest Senior Brother arrived with a shocked face just like Seon'ah's, but his expression was more grotesque than cute.

"I will submit a proposal for a Western Pacification to the Master and the Guardian Hall! To think my precious little brother was almost killed by a mere Murim Alliance spy! Does this make any sense?!"

"That's right!!" Seon'ah chimed in. The two of them joined forces and started yelling.

"Little Brother is a talent who must do great things and lead the cult with me, Wi Jinhak! For such a talent to be on the verge of death! This won't do. I'll make sure to increase your bodyguards!"

"I'll protect you too, Oppa!"

Third Senior Brother, who came with Eldest Senior Brother, looked completely dejected.

"I'm sorry I wasn't any help. This worthless Senior Brother was busy controlling his qi deviation, not even knowing his Junior Brother was on the brink of death."

"What are you talking about? If it weren't for you, third Senior Brother, I would've been as good as dead."

His frequent night raids had been like a rehearsal. If I hadn't been prepared, I would have died in my sleep. His nightly visits were annoying, but they had turned out to be a blessing in disguise.

Third Senior Brother still looked gloomy, so I asked him a question.

"By the way, you were suffering from qi deviation?"

He gave a small nod. It was a strange feeling.

*I thought the assassin might be my Senior Brother suffering from qi deviation, but he was actually suffering from it for real.*

If he had come with the assassin for a pincer attack, the situation would have been truly mind-boggling.

"Hm?"

A questioning sound escaped my lips. Everyone looked at me, puzzled, but I ignored them. I was too focused on the thought that had just occurred to me. Normally, I would have dismissed it, but I couldn't let it go.

After considering the possibilities, I reached a single conclusion.

*Perhaps... he wasn't an assassin sent by the Murim Alliance.*
