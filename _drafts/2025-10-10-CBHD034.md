The sun was already setting by the time we reached the Main Headquarters of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult after a two-day journey. Master summoned me as soon as I arrived. The official reason was a dinner to thank me for my hard work.

"Hohoho. What were your impressions from the trip?"

Just as I expected, Master was the type to discuss work during a company dinner. My stomach rumbled, but I put down my chopsticks and began to speak calmly.

"I was able to find a few areas for improvement, Master."

"oho. It seems there's more than just one or two."

Master looked at me with interest, so I explained what I had seen in Gaochang County. I told him how the Branch Manager went berserk from the side effects of his demonic art while teaching the children.

"...It seems the Branch Manager of Gaochang County must be punished."

"There's no need for that. The Branch Manager of Hejing County didn't go berserk, but it was obvious he was struggling."

"Hmm. Now that you mention it, others who inspected different counties told similar stories. They said the Branch Managers are having a hard time teaching the children in groups."

"Yes. If one or two people cause a problem, it's their fault, but if similar problems are occurring in multiple places, it means the system itself is flawed."

"Then, what is the solution you've come up with?"

"First, we need criteria for selecting the Principal. The Branch Manager doesn't have to be the one to teach the children. It's not a job where martial arts skill or age is important, is it?"

"That is true."

"Therefore, we should select the Principal based on the side effects of their demonic art. We should exclude those who've learned arts associated with anger, depression, or obsession. It would be better to have those closer to mania or apathy teach instead."

Master nodded, so I continued.

"Of course, that alone won't be enough to control the children. The teachers could be pushed to their mental limits, which could cause problems."

Everyone who learns a demonic art suffers from some form of mental illness. There was no telling what terrible things might happen if they were pushed too far. They needed a system that would work for everyone, a one-size-fits-all approach.

"Therefore, we need to add some leeway to the children's education. Children have limited attention spans, so it would be best to hold two sessions. Each session should have a forty-five-minute lesson followed by a fifteen-minute break."

This was a concept from modern education. Even in the modern era, elementary school students have forty-minute classes and ten-minute breaks, while high school students have fifty-minute classes and ten-minute breaks. This world just doesn't have the concept of a 'minute', so I divided the time appropriately.

"Additionally, they should learn one phrase from the Thousand Character Classic per lesson. That's four characters, taught twice. A total of only eight characters a day."

"Won't that be too little?"

"Even if they only learn eight characters a day, they can read the entire Thousand Character Classic in one hundred and twenty-five days. If we speed up the pace a little at the end, they can read it up to three times in a year."

I also discussed what to do if a child misbehaved. Just as I had advised the Gaochang County Branch Manager, we would send them home to be scolded by their parents instead of forcing them.

"Hmm. So you're trying to reduce the mental pressure on the teachers, even if just by a little."

"That's right."

Master nodded as if he understood, then asked, "Then, after about two hours of education, are the children sent back home?"

I shook my head. The solutions I had explained so far were the ones I devised after seeing the Branch Manager go berserk in Gaochang County. Thanks to the side effects of the damned Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, I had a few more ideas.

"After the two hours of education, it would be good to conduct basic martial arts and external arts training."

"!?"

For the first time, a look of surprise appeared on Master's face.

"Are you suggesting we teach demonic arts to the children of commoners?"

"Not demonic arts, Master, but basic martial arts and external arts. If children learn exercise and basic breathing techniques from a young age, it will help them greatly when they grow up and do physical labor. It can also increase the chance of discovering talented individuals."

"oho. So you're saying we teach them basic martial arts, then select the talented children to teach the demonic arts to."

"That's right. We'll teach them the Thousand Character Classic for a year and the Divine Cult's doctrines for another year, so we can observe them for two years. The Branch Managers in each region will teach for two years and report any children with outstanding talent to the cult."

The Demonic Cult's current method of nurturing talent was like an elite sports program. I was trying to turn it into a kind of recreational sports program. As the number of children training in martial arts increases, the probability of discovering a talented child will also rise.

"Hmm. Then, in addition to a simplified doctrine book, we'll also need to prepare basic martial arts and external arts to teach them."

"You don't need to worry about that. I've captured about twenty deserters who learned the military's martial arts."

This was why I had bothered to drag those bandits around after learning they were military deserters. Demonic arts were cheat-like martial arts that anyone could learn easily and advance in quickly, but they came with terrifying side effects. They were not something to be taught indiscriminately to children.

The military's martial arts, on the other hand, were likely well-organized, starting from a basic level designed for conscripts.

Furthermore, there was another benefit.

"If some of the Divine Cult's Branches lack a suitable Principal, it would be good to dispatch these men there."

They were all-weather talents when it came to teaching children, especially compared to demonic practitioners suffering from mental illness.

And finally.

"Children have limited attention spans. If we only focus on education and training, problems can arise. So, after about one hour of training, it would be good to have some playtime as a reward for following the lessons well."

As I spoke, I unfolded a booklet where I had drawn and organized the details. It described a ball made from an animal's bladder, coated with glue, and covered with straw rope or dried leaves. It also explained games played by kicking that ball or throwing it to hit each other. It was an explanation of soccer and dodgeball.

*The concept of a 'school' that I grasped after my modern memories mixed with the side effects of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was exactly this. Teaching children martial arts and exercise, on top of basic character education—in this case, the doctrines of the Demonic Cult, and finally, even providing appropriate games for them to play.*

*It's the only hope for dual-income couples raising children with boundless energy, who only fall asleep after running around all day until they're exhausted. The thing that was now starting to spread to foreign countries, including the United States. It was a Taekwondo academy.*

{sep}

After that, Ilmok and the Heavenly Demon continued their conversation over dinner. They discussed the details of the system Ilmok had proposed, such as what kind of reward to give a Branch Manager who produced many talented children, or what penalty to impose if they recommended children indiscriminately just to get a reward.

I finally finished that terrible time where I was so busy talking about work that I didn't know if food was going into my mouth or my nose.

After sending Ilmok away, the Heavenly Demon was lost in thought for a moment in his office. Just then, a visitor arrived.

"The Guardian Hall Director greets the Heavenly Demon."

The Guardian Hall was the group in charge of bodyguarding the Demonic Cult's key facilities and high-ranking figures. Its Director was also one of the top expert martial artists.

The Heavenly Demon stroked his beard and asked, "What brings you here at this late hour?"

"I heard a story from Gwan Muyeol and Jang Hoe, who served as Young Lord Ilmok's bodyguards on this trip, and came in a hurry."

"Hmm. I'm curious what story you heard."

The Guardian Hall Director explained how Ilmok had been attacked by the Gaochang County Branch Manager and how he had encountered the bandits.

"Furthermore, Young Lord Ilmok was also attacked by the First Young Lord when he went berserk before. For the sake of Young Lord Ilmok's safety, it seems dangerous to entrust his protection to just one attendant, Supreme Lord."

The Heavenly Demon nodded at the Guardian Hall Director's explanation.

*Indeed. That child, Ilmok, is leading a change, so it is necessary to prepare for danger.*

He had thought Ilmok was safe since it had only been about four months since he started learning the demonic arts, but incidents and accidents never seemed to cease around that child. It was like that with his first disciple, Wi Jinhak, and with the issue concerning the Hyeokryeon Clan. It seemed to be because that child was leading a change. After all, conflict was bound to arise when one brought about change.

"Then, who do you plan to assign?"

"How about assigning the two who accompanied him this time as his full-time bodyguards? They've already been together for four days, so wouldn't the Young Lord also be more comfortable with that?"

The Heavenly Demon nodded at the Guardian Hall Director's suggestion.

"Do so."

{sep}

After his meeting with the Heavenly Demon, the Guardian Hall Director returned not to his own quarters but to the Guardian Hall, claiming he had work to finish. There, as he was checking a few letters, he spoke.

"Come in."

Though there was no sign of anyone, a door opened cautiously and a figure entered the room. It was late, and the sky was steeped in darkness. A single candle on the Guardian Hall Director's desk illuminated only his immediate surroundings.

The Guardian Hall Director spoke to the man whose face was hidden in the shadows.

"Starting tomorrow, you will be assigned as the exclusive bodyguard for that Eighth Young Lord."

"I will obey the order."

The man in the shadows performed a clasped hands greeting. The Guardian Hall Director spoke again.

"From now on, you are to monitor the Young Lord's every move and report back. Determine whether he is a brat who can be left alone, or a future obstacle that must be dealt with. Also, if he must be eliminated, you will have to devise a way to do it without leaving a trail."

Eliminating him when that man left the Main Headquarters with Ilmok would have been one way. However, if that man were so directly involved, he risked exposing himself.

*Besides, if it's the Heavenly Demon, there's a high probability he has made separate preparations in addition to the two I assigned.*

He chose to observe for now because there might be a hidden bodyguard he hadn't identified. They had to find a way to eliminate him without exposing themselves.

It wasn't an impossible task. After all, they could now monitor his every move by placing a bodyguard this close to him, and they already had experience eliminating two others. There was the fourth Young Lord, whose outstanding talent led them to leak information and have him killed by those bastards from the orthodox sects of the Central Plains, and there was the fifth Young Lord, who they dealt with by disguising it as qi deviation because he had noticed their trail. The cover-up wasn't difficult, as the second brother Young Lord and the seventh Young Lord had died on their own from overexertion, without them having to do anything.

Recalling those memories, the Guardian Hall Director asked the man in the darkness, "Speaking of which, you've been with him for the past four days, so you must have a sense of him. What are your impressions of the Eighth Young Lord?"

The man in the darkness recalled the scene he had witnessed in Hejing County and answered.

"To put it nicely, he was skilled at improvisation and instigation."

He remembered the way Ilmok captured all the bandits alive, then claimed to have killed them all without a change in his expression. The man also frowned, recalling how Ilmok had instigated the villagers by claiming he was testing them, even though the man himself had kept his mouth shut for the inspection. He remembered how Ilmok had berated the Branch Manager in Gaochang County while being entertained, and how slick and evasive he had been with them.

"And to put it badly, he was like a con artist."

Instigation and improvisation were just nice words for it. He was at a level where lies automatically came out whenever he opened his mouth.
