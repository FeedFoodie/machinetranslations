The sun was setting when we reached the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's Main Headquarters after a two-day journey. The Master summoned me as soon as I arrived. The official reason was a dinner to thank me for my hard work.

"Hohoho. What were your impressions from the trip?"

*Just as I expected. The Master is the type to bring up work during a company dinner.*

My stomach rumbled. I put down my chopsticks and calmly began to speak.

"I found a few areas for improvement, Master."

"Oho. It seems there’s more than one or two."

The Master looked at me with interest. I explained what I had seen in Gaochang County, telling him how the Branch Manager went berserk from his demonic art’s side effects while teaching the children.

"...It seems the Branch Manager of Gaochang County must be punished."

"There’s no need for that. The Branch Manager of Hejing County didn’t go berserk, but it was obvious he was struggling."

"Hmm. Now that you mention it, others who went to inspect different counties told similar stories. They said the Branch Managers are having a hard time teaching the children in groups."

"Yes. If one or two people cause a problem, it’s their fault. However, if similar problems are occurring in multiple places, it means the system itself is flawed."

"Then, what is the solution you’ve come up with?"

"First, we need criteria for selecting the Principal who will teach. The Branch Manager doesn’t necessarily have to teach the children letters. It’s not a job where martial arts skill or age is important, is it?"

"That is true."

"Therefore, we should select the Principal based on the side effects of their demonic art. We should exclude those who have learned demonic arts associated with anger, depression, or obsession. It would be better to have those closer to mania or apathy teach instead."

The Master nodded, so I continued.

"Of course, that alone won’t be enough to completely control the children. Because of that, the teachers could be pushed to their mental limits, which could cause problems."

Since everyone who learns a demonic art suffers from some form of mental illness, there was no telling what terrible things might happen if they were pushed mentally. That was why a one-size-fits-all system had to guide them.

"Therefore, we need to add some leeway to the children’s education. Children have limited attention spans. It would be best to hold two sessions, each with a forty-five-minute lesson followed by a fifteen-minute break."

This was a concept from modern education. Even in the modern era, elementary school students have forty-minute classes and ten-minute breaks, while high school students have fifty-minute classes and ten-minute breaks. This world just lacked the concept of a ‘minute’, so I divided it appropriately into fifteen-minute units.

"Additionally, they should be taught one phrase from the Thousand Character Classic in one lesson. That is, four characters twice. A total of only eight characters a day."

"Won’t that be too little?"

"Even if they only learn eight characters a day, they can read through the Thousand Character Classic once in one hundred and twenty-five days. If we speed up the pace a little at the end, they can read it up to three times in a year."

I also discussed what to do if a child misbehaved. Just as I advised the Gaochang County Branch Manager, we would send them home to be scolded by their parents instead of forcing them.

"Hmm. So you’re trying to reduce the mental pressure on those who teach the children, even if just by a little."

"That’s right."

The Master nodded as if he understood, then asked, "Then, after about two hours of education, are the children sent back home?"

I shook my head. The solutions I had explained so far were ones I devised after seeing the Branch Manager go berserk in Gaochang County. Thanks to the side effects of the damned Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, I had a few more ideas.

"After the two hours of education are over, it would be good to conduct basic martial arts and external arts training."

"!?"

For the first time, a look of surprise appeared on the Master’s face.

"Are you suggesting we teach the demonic arts to the children of commoners?"

"Not demonic arts, but basic martial arts and external arts, Master. If children learn exercise and basic breathing techniques from a young age, it will be a great help when they grow up and do physical labor. Furthermore, it can increase the possibility of discovering talented individuals."

"Oho. So you’re saying we teach them basic martial arts, and then select the talented children to teach the demonic arts to."

"That’s right. We will teach them the Thousand Character Classic for a year, and then the doctrines of the Divine Cult for another year. We can observe the children for a total of two years. The Branch Managers in each region will teach for two years and select the children with outstanding talent to report to the cult."

To make a modern analogy, the Demonic Cult’s current method of nurturing talent was close to an elite sports program. I was trying to turn it into a kind of recreational sports program. As the number of children training in martial arts increases, the probability of discovering a talented child will also rise.

"Hmm. Then, in addition to a simplified doctrine book for the children to study, we will also need to prepare basic martial arts and external arts to teach them."

"You don’t need to worry about that. I’ve captured about twenty deserters who have learned the military’s martial arts."

This was why I had bothered to drag those bandits around after learning they were military deserters. The demonic arts were cheat-like martial arts that anyone could learn easily and advance in quickly, but they came with terrifying side effects. They were not something that could be taught indiscriminately to children.

On the other hand, the military’s martial arts were likely well-organized, starting from a basic level designed for conscripts.

Furthermore, there was another benefit.

"If some of the Divine Cult’s Branches lack someone suitable to be a Principal, it would be good to dispatch these men there."

They were all-weather talents for teaching children, especially compared to demonic practitioners suffering from mental illness.

And finally.

"Children have limited attention spans. If we only focus on education and training, problems can arise. So, after about one hour of training, it would be good to have some playtime at the end as a reward for following the lessons well."

As I spoke, I unfolded a booklet where I had already drawn and organized the details. It described a ball made from an animal’s bladder, coated with glue, and covered in straw rope or dried leaves. It also explained games played by kicking that ball, or throwing it to hit each other. It was an explanation of soccer and dodgeball.

*This is the concept of a ‘school’ I grasped after my modern memories mixed with the side effects of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.*

It involved teaching children martial arts and exercise, on top of basic character education, which in this case was the doctrines of the Demonic Cult. Finally, it even provided appropriate games for the children to play.

*It’s the only hope for dual-income couples raising children with boundless energy, who only fall asleep after running around all day until they’re exhausted. The thing that was now starting to spread to foreign countries, including the United States. It was a Taekwondo academy.*

{sep}

After that, Ilmok and the Heavenly Demon continued their conversation over dinner. They discussed the details of the system Ilmok had proposed, such as what reward to give a Branch Manager of a region that produced many talented children. Conversely, they also discussed what penalty to impose if a Branch Manager recommended children indiscriminately just to receive a reward.

I finally finished that terrible time where I was so busy talking about work that I didn't know if the food was going into my mouth or my nose.

After sending Ilmok away, the Heavenly Demon was lost in thought for a moment in his office. Just then, a visitor arrived.

"The Guardian Hall Director greets the Heavenly Demon."

The Guardian Hall was the group in charge of bodyguarding the Demonic Cult’s key facilities and high-ranking figures. Its Director was also one of the top drummers.

The Heavenly Demon stroked his beard and asked, "What brings you here at this late hour?"

"I heard a story from Gwan Muyeol and Jang Hoe, who served as Young Lord Ilmok’s bodyguards on this trip, and came in a hurry."

"Hmm. I’m curious what story you heard."

The Guardian Hall Director explained how Ilmok had been attacked by the Gaochang County Branch Manager and how he had encountered the bandits.

"Furthermore, Young Lord Ilmok was also attacked by the First Young Lord when he went berserk before. Therefore, for the sake of Young Lord Ilmok’s safety, it seems dangerous to entrust his protection to just one attendant. Supreme Lord."

The Heavenly Demon nodded at the Guardian Hall Director’s explanation.

*Indeed. That child, Ilmok, is leading a change, so it is necessary to prepare for danger.*

He had thought Ilmok was safe since it had only been about four months since Ilmok started learning the demonic arts, but incidents and accidents never seemed to cease around that child. It was like that with his first disciple, Wi Jinhak, and with the issue concerning the Hyeokryeon Clan. It seemed to be because that child was leading a change.

After all, conflict was bound to arise when one brought about change.

"Then, who do you plan to assign?"

"How about assigning the two who accompanied him this time as his full-time bodyguards? They’ve already been together for four days, so wouldn’t the Young Lord also be more comfortable with that?"

The Heavenly Demon nodded at the Guardian Hall Director’s suggestion.

"Do so."

{sep}

After finishing his meeting with the Heavenly Demon, the Guardian Hall Director did not return to his own quarters. He went to the Guardian Hall, saying he had remaining work to do. There, he was reading and checking a few letters.

"Come in."

The Guardian Hall Director spoke to the empty air, though there was no sign of anyone. A door opened cautiously and a figure entered the room.

It was late. The sky was steeped in darkness. A single aphrodisiac candle on the Guardian Hall Director’s desk only illuminated his immediate surroundings.

The Guardian Hall Director spoke to the man whose face was hidden in the shadows.

"Starting tomorrow, you will be assigned as the exclusive bodyguard for that Eighth Young Lord."

"I will obey the order."

The man in the shadows performed a clasped hands greeting. The Guardian Hall Director spoke again.

"Therefore, from now on, you are to monitor the Young Lord’s every move and report back. Determine whether he is a brat who can be left alone, or a future obstacle that must be dealt with. Also, if he must be eliminated, you will have to devise a way to do it without leaving a trail."

Eliminating him when that man left the Main Headquarters with Ilmok this time would have been one way. However, if that man were so directly involved, there was a risk that he himself would be exposed.

*Besides, if it’s the Heavenly Demon, there’s a high probability he has made separate preparations in addition to the two I assigned.*

He chose to observe for now because there was a possibility of a hidden bodyguard he hadn’t identified. They had to find a way to eliminate him without exposing themselves. It wasn’t an impossible task. They could now monitor his every move by placing a bodyguard this close to him.

And they already had experience eliminating two others.

There was the fourth Young Lord, whose outstanding talent led them to leak information and have him killed by those bastards from the orthodox sects of the Central Plains, and there was the fifth Young Lord, who they dealt with by disguising it as qi deviation because he had noticed their trail.

The cover-up wasn’t difficult, as the second brother Young Lord and the seventh Young Lord had died on their own from overexertion, without them having to do anything.

Recalling those memories, the Guardian Hall Director asked the man in the darkness, "Speaking of which, you’ve been with him for the past four days, so you must have gotten a sense of him. What are your impressions of the Eighth Young Lord?"

The man in the darkness recalled the scene he had witnessed in Hejing County and answered.

"To put it nicely, he was skilled at improvisation and instigation."

He remembered the way Ilmok captured all the bandits alive, then claimed to have killed them all without a change in his expression. The man also frowned, recalling how Ilmok had instigated the villagers by claiming he was testing them, even though the man himself had kept his mouth shut for the sake of the inspection. He remembered how Ilmok had berated the Branch Manager in Gaochang County while being entertained, and how slick and evasive Ilmok had been with them.

"And to put it badly, he was like a con artist."

Instigation and improvisation were just nice words for it. He was at a level where lies automatically came out whenever he opened his mouth.
