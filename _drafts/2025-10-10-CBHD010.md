Ilmok couldn't write certain concepts he knew as Seo Jihun in Chinese characters. These were ideas like Korean Neo-Confucianism, which he had arbitrarily linked to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's doctrines. Ilmok wrote these concepts in Hangul so only he could recognize them.

Jin Hayeon stared sharply, as if analyzing a code, but soon gave up. "Were you originally a scholar preparing for the state examinations, Young Lord?" she asked. "This kind of code seems like something a student would create."

"How could a lone orphan afford to prepare for the state examinations?" Ilmok replied. "The Elder who hired me as a waiter taught me to write. He told me to do greater things when I grew up. I created these codes to study more efficiently while learning from him."

Ilmok spat out the excuse he had prepared for this plan. He had thought of it in advance because using such characters risked arousing suspicion.

But Ilmok had deliberately shown her the Hangul for a reason beyond just taking notes.

{sep}

That night, Ilmok finished his routine. He had completed morning studies, external arts training, and martial arts training with the Heavenly Demon. The day ended with the chilling Body Purification and ritual bath from the aspiring psychopath.

"Young Lord, I've prepared a candle, ink, a brush, and a blank book in your room," Jin Hayeon said.

"Thank you. You should retire for the night too, Young Lady."

After a brief farewell with Jin Hayeon, I went to my room. Jin Hayeon's quarters were in another pavilion at the Wind Cliff Pavilion, so she was essentially next door.

"......"

I sighed lightly, gazing at the candlelight that lit the otherwise pitch-dark room. The day's final task waited for me before I could sleep. It was perhaps the most important task for my survival here.

The final task on my schedule was simple.

[Middle of the Hour of the Pig (21:00-22:00) - Review and Sleep]

It was originally review time for the Demonic Academy entrance, but its purpose had now changed. Entering the Demonic Academy was important, but if I only studied the Records of the Heavenly Demon, I risked becoming a heretic myself.

I sat at the candlelit table, picked up the brush, and began writing in the blank book.

— Day 1 of life in the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

My final, changed task was to keep a diary.

— The Heavenly Demon Divine Cult is a group of insane heretics.

The diary was a conversation with myself, a way to prevent becoming engrossed in their heretical ways.

— They've mixed Xunzi's Theory of Innate Evil with Maitreya. They're complete lunatics. The Heavenly Demon will save all living beings? Ugh. These crazy heretics.

If I were caught writing this, my neck would be on the line, but I was confident I wouldn't be caught.

— And that attendant? She's going to kill me in a year? This is why heretics are dangerous. Seo Jihun, get a grip. You must not be tainted.

Even if Jin Hayeon searched my room and found this diary, it wouldn't be a problem. The diary was written in Hangul. This was why I had deliberately shown Jin Hayeon the Hangul during our morning studies, calling it a 'code for studying'. If she saw this diary, she would just think it was part of my review. After all, my schedule said I would be reviewing at this time.

— That old geezer is a problem too. If the Transcendent Demon Realm is so difficult, he should've said so first! What am I supposed to do now that he only mentions the side effects after I've already learned the technique!

For a long while, I poured two days of built-up anger and distrust for the heretics into my diary.

"Hoo. Now I feel like I can live."

Feeling much more at ease, I closed the diary and went to bed.

{sep}

For the next few days, my daily routine spun like a hamster wheel, exactly as I had scheduled it. About eight days passed this way.

"Hohoho. Your progress is much faster than I thought," Master said.

"It's thanks to your teachings, Master."

Today, after I learned a new technique for the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, Master brought up an unexpected topic as I finished my training.

"Do you have any urgent plans this afternoon?" he asked.

"...As always, I was planning to review today's lesson and then go to sleep."

"Hohoho. In that case, postpone your review and come to my pavilion. Isn't it about time you greeted your Senior Brothers?"

I remembered Jin Hayeon telling me on the first day that I had Senior Brothers. She had said that every one of the Heavenly Demon's disciples had entered the Demonic Academy as valedictorian.

I had been too busy trying to save my own skin to pay it much mind, but Master's words sparked a question.

*Usually, when there's a new youngest disciple, isn't he introduced to his Senior Brothers first?*

I had woken up in a medieval martial arts fantasy world. Here, the saying 'ruler, master, and father are one' was taken seriously. A master and disciple were like father and son. If a new son appeared, it was only natural to inform the brothers.

*...Is it because they're heretics? Do they not follow Confucian doctrines?*

I wondered if my thoughts showed on my face.

"Hohoho. You seem curious why I'm only introducing you now," Master said, having read my thoughts.

I couldn't just reply, 'Yes, I am.'

"Ahem. I believed Master must have had his reasons."

The Heavenly Demon's will was always the truth. One wrong word, and Jin Hayeon, standing behind me, might perform a sword dance. Not to kill me, but a suicide show while crying, 'My education was insufficient, Lord Heavenly Demon!'

*...I thought psychopaths prioritize themselves and can't understand others' emotions, but she's a bit strange.*

She didn't prioritize herself. The Heavenly Demon was her top priority. It felt like she couldn't feel any emotion for anything unrelated to him. She was like a bizarre combination of a heretic and a psychopath.

"Hohoho. A commendable thing to say. As you said, there was a reason. All your Senior Brothers were away on missions I assigned them. I heard they all returned to the Main Headquarters today, so wouldn't it be good to have a meal and greet each other?"

Honestly, I wanted to refuse. I had only a year to prepare for the Demonic Academy entrance exam. Where would I find time to fool around? But as a follower of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, how could I dare refuse the Heavenly Demon's suggestion?

"I will do so."

I bowed my head.

*I want to clock in for overtime,* I thought.

I could guarantee this dinner was also overtime.

{sep}

Right after training with Master, I immediately received Body Purification and a bath from Jin Hayeon, which was not my usual routine. She couldn't speak during the Body Purification, so I waited until the bath, a service I had grown somewhat used to, to ask her a question.

"What do you know about my Senior Brothers?"

This was a very important matter. The dinner was soon. It was a dinner to 'welcome the newcomer', and I was that newcomer. As the youngest, I needed to know about my superiors. Of course, my position was too strange to be considered simply the 'youngest'. Even Jin Hayeon, who attended to me, was my subordinate.

This situation was quite familiar to me.

*Even a new Grade 7 civil servant outranks a Grade 9 civil servant.*

In military terms, I was a newly commissioned second lieutenant. The Vice Commissioners and regular soldiers were my subordinates in rank. A high-ranking newcomer still needed to get along with those of lower rank, if only to avoid a new second lieutenant ordering the first sergeant to do push-ups.

But this dinner wasn't with subordinates. It was with superiors. Even worse.

*A dinner with people who might kill me.*

It was obvious. There were many disciples but only one position of Heavenly Demon. I didn't know how long Master would live, but when he died, competition among the Senior Brothers was inevitable. This place was a gathering of psychopaths and a heretic cult. These people wouldn't hesitate to take each other's heads in a 'War for Supremacy for the Cult Leader position'.

Therefore, my most important task at dinner was simple.

*Show that I have no interest in the Cult Leader position.*

I had to appear harmless.

Someone might ask why I don't just align with the most powerful Senior Brother. That would be foolish. Aligning with someone can lead to rapid promotion if you choose well, but it also risks a catastrophic fall if you misstep. Conversely, if you don't align with anyone, your promotion might be slow, but the risk of falling is also reduced. It's what's called becoming a perpetual section chief.

In modern society, a perpetual section chief always had to fight anxiety.

*I don't know who will become the next Cult Leader, but the Cult Leader's Junior Brother probably won't get fired or kicked out.*

Master still seemed vigorous, so the balance of power could easily shift later. It wasn't that I hadn't considered aiming for the Heavenly Demon position myself.

*For now, I have no power, so not aligning with anyone is the safer option.*

I was already risking my life for an exam a year away. Competing with Senior Brothers who had already graduated as valedictorians would be suicide. In that sense, appealing my incompetence was a good strategy.

*The so-called 'youngest disciple hides his talent' strategy.*

While I was lost in thought, Jin Hayeon spoke in an indifferent tone as she applied soapstone to my body. "Currently, the Heavenly Demon has four disciples, including you, Young Lord Ilmok. They are the head disciple, Young Lord Wi Jinhak. The third disciple, Young Lord Seo Wanpyeong. The sixth disciple, Young Lord Jongri Chu, and finally, the eighth disciple, you."

"I'm the eighth disciple, so why are there only three Senior Brothers?"

She finished my arm, moved to my back, and continued applying soapstone as she answered.

"I heard the Second Young Lord died from qi deviation."

"......"

A chill ran down my spine, and I shivered. I felt her hand tickle my back.

"The Fourth Young Lord lost his life in a battle with the orthodox sects. The Heavenly Demon later avenged him, so his spirit was likely appeased. The Fifth Young Lord also died from qi deviation, and the Seventh Young Lord died in a duel he claimed would bring him enlightenment."

"Ahem. Ah, I understand," I said, hastily cutting her off.

Four of the eight disciples were dead. A fifty percent survival rate, and two of them had died from qi deviation.

*Where the hell did that crazy old man drag me into?*

For the first time in a while, resentment surged within me toward Master.

"Ahem. Th-then, what's the relationship between the remaining Senior Brothers? Do you know who Master trusts the most?"

"Wouldn't he trust Young Lord Wi Jinhak the most? He's been a disciple the longest."

I learned a little more about my Senior Brothers while Jin Hayeon finished my bath.

"You can wear this," she said, helping me into neat clothes different from my usual training attire.

I then left the Wind Cliff Pavilion. She guided me through the main grounds of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult and stopped before a huge pavilion. She showed an identification tag to the gatekeepers, who checked it, bowed lightly to me, and cleared the way.

I followed her down a huge corridor inside. She stopped at the largest door at the end.

"You may enter here," she said, bowing her head. It seemed she could not enter the room herself.

GULP.

I swallowed dryly, forced a calm expression onto my face, and pushed the door open. This was overtime with my future and life on the line. No, it was dinner time.
