A large man with a threatening aura stood before them. The Flame Dragon Commander Cheon Daewoong had arrived.

"From now on, anyone who moves without my permission dies. Don't even breathe," his voice boomed, infused with inner arts.

Behind Cheon Daewoong, seven elites from the Flame Dragon Brigade lined up. The Seven Flames were Cheon Daewoong's loyal limbs, known for their rough and cruel nature. On Cheon Daewoong's path to becoming commander, the Seven Flames had killed more people than the entire Flame Dragon Brigade combined.

Cheon Daewoong had received information and rushed here in pursuit. He brought only the Seven Flames, not the entire brigade. His blazing eyes scanned the inn's interior until they met Ji Han's. The moment he saw him, Ji Han shot up from his seat without realizing it.

"So you really were here." Cheon Daewoong's words made it clear someone had told him Ji Han was here. Originally, Cheon Daewoong should have gone to Shaanxi to pursue the Myriad Evils Mountain Hermit's corpse.

"How dare a mere hunting dog that just sniffs around pull a stunt like this?"

Ji Han's pupils wavered. Contempt and harassment he had habitually endured flooded back, freezing his body. Unpleasant emotions shook him.

"How dare a punk like you!" Cheon Daewoong's fury exploded. He couldn't believe Ji Han, of all people, had almost deceived him. The verbal abuse he had held back on his way here poured out. "Did your father teach you to betray the organization that raised you like this? Yeah, your father probably crawled on the ground sniffing shit, right? This monstrous bastard dares to deceive me?"

When Cheon Daewoong audaciously insulted his father, killing intent surged in Ji Han's eyes. Although his father had sold him to his Master at a young age, he did it so Ji Han could live well and not suffer in a poor family.

Then it happened. Surprisingly, the one who spoke calmly was not Geom Mugeuk, but the Flower Sword Supreme.

"......"

Ji Han stared at the woman across from him, surprised. It was the first time she had spoken to him.

"Let's eat together."

"......"

Surprised and bewildered, Ji Han sat down. Her single phrase instantly calmed his emotions, which had been on the verge of being consumed by anger and killing intent.

The inn fell silent as everyone stared at her. She exuded an elegant grace, the complete opposite of Cheon Daewoong's hostile atmosphere. The Flower Sword Supreme was the first to eat the pork in five-spice sauce.

Everyone held their breath. It seemed they could even hear the sound of her chewing. The Flower Sword Supreme closed her eyes for a moment, savoring the taste. Soon, her expression brightened.

"It's delicious."

Cheon Daewoong was dumbfounded. He had clearly told them to put down their chopsticks, yet she was eating as if to spite him? He should have burst out with curses, but the Flower Sword Supreme's mysterious beauty made him decide to wait and see how she would act.

Geom Mugeuk was delighted. "Ah, what a relief. I went through so much trouble to protect this pork in five-spice sauce. I was worried it wouldn't taste good."

He had truly protected that moment by running around and stopping all sorts of fights. The Flower Sword Supreme offered some to the Blood Heaven Blade Demon.

"Orabeoni, you should try some too."

The Blood Heaven Blade Demon also picked up his chopsticks and ate the pork in five-spice sauce. "Not bad."

A reaction like this was high praise from him.

"If you only eat noodle dishes at your age, it's bad for your health. You should eat a variety of things like this more often."

The Flower Sword Supreme's gaze turned to Ji Han again. "Why aren't you eating?"

Although he wasn't in the mood to eat, Ji Han picked up his chopsticks. "I will eat."

"Delicious food has a way of lifting one's spirits."

Ji Han realized she was trying to look after his feelings. He was inwardly surprised and moved. Ji Han ate the pork in five-spice sauce.

"It's delicious."

It was a lie. He was so distracted he couldn't taste it. Just then, Ji Han's gaze met Geom Mugeuk's. Seeing him, Ji Han realized he had momentarily forgotten whose subordinate he had become and who he was with. Freezing up at the sight of Cheon Daewoong was disrespectful to Geom Mugeuk.

"I'm sorry."

Geom Mugeuk smiled. "You saw it correctly."

"Pardon?"

"An idiot is definitely an idiot."

Ji Han had previously stopped Geom Mugeuk from killing Cheon Daewoong, saying it was better to have an idiot in the enemy camp. Now, that very idiot had come to find him.

Sensing he had been called an idiot, Cheon Daewoong unleashed his killing intent. "Now that you're done stuffing your faces, prepare to die."

SHWAAAAAA!

He didn't care if the people inside were hurt. However, the killing intent couldn't spread far before something blocked it. PWOONG, PWOONG, the killing intent was gently repelled.

Cheon Daewoong flinched in surprise. It felt as if an invisible barrier had enveloped him. This was the first time it had happened since he started learning martial arts.

Geom Mugeuk rose from his seat and walked toward him with a bright smile. "So you're my subordinate's former superior."

"Former superior?"

"Ji Han is my subordinate now."

"By whose authority?"

One would have expected him to say 'by my authority', but Geom Mugeuk looked at Ji Han. "By his own will."

He hadn't made him a subordinate. Ji Han had come asking to be taken in.

When his unleashed killing intent was blocked, Cheon Daewoong became more cautious. He decided to first flaunt the power backing him. "Are you confident you can handle the Evil King Sect?"

At the mention of the Evil King Sect, the watching customers turned pale. They had already guessed from his words and actions that Cheon Daewoong was not a good person, but the Evil King Sect? Their evil deeds were so infamous that everyone shuddered at the name.

"I can handle it, but the other customers might get indigestion."

TAK.

In an instant, Geom Mugeuk had his arm around Cheon Daewoong's shoulders. "Now, now, let's not do this here. Let's step outside for a moment."

The two had been standing apart, facing each other, but they were suddenly side by side. It seemed to everyone's eyes as if the intermediate steps had been skipped. Naturally, the most surprised person was Cheon Daewoong himself. The next most surprised were the Seven Flames standing beside him.

"Oh, your muscles are amazing! I happen to have a thing for muscular people."

Geom Mugeuk poked his bicep with a finger. Despite this, Cheon Daewoong didn't shake him off. Because of this, the watching Seven Flames instinctively knew Cheon Daewoong had been subdued. After all, he hated it when others touched his body.

The two men walked outside. The flustered Seven Flames followed them. Watching them, Ji Han shot to his feet.

"I'll go too!"

His leader had gone out to fight because of him, so he couldn't just sit there. As he rushed out, Tae Su also shot up and ran out.

"They're all so young."

At the Flower Sword Supreme's words, the Blood Heaven Blade Demon silently drank his liquor. The Flower Sword Supreme refilled his cup.

"Do you miss it?"

One would have expected her to say, 'Of course, I miss it'. The Flower Sword Supreme shook her head.

"I did before I met the Young Cult Leader. I regretted and missed the past. I was very angry with my life back then."

"And now?"

The Flower Sword Supreme smiled without a word. There was no need to answer. She was already showing her transformation, from her external appearance to her internal changes.

This time, the Flower Sword Supreme asked. "Orabeoni, do you have any regrets?"

After a moment of silence, the Blood Heaven Blade Demon finally brought out an emotion he hadn't shown for thirty years. "I regret it."

The Flower Sword Supreme broke the heavy silence. "Let's come here again. We can wait in line together."

The Blood Heaven Blade Demon looked at her with a surprised expression. "Next time, let's come just the two of us."

Staring at her quietly, the Blood Heaven Blade Demon silently nodded.

{sep}

Cheon Daewoong couldn't resist. He had no idea when his inner arts had been suppressed. The acupoints on his upper body were sealed, leaving him able to do nothing but walk.

Was it because the god of death had blinded his eyes? Instead of thinking 'He was a master!', he had a different regret.

*I was careless!*

He had been certain that the masters were the old man and the woman. Geom Mugeuk was too young to be a master. His actions were too frivolous, and he talked too much.

Cheon Daewoong glanced behind him. Fortunately, his subordinates followed closely. Yes, he would find an opportunity to eliminate this bastard. All the while, Geom Mugeuk kept talking.

"Everyone came here to eat delicious food, even waiting in line. Who are you and I to cause a scene in there?"

*What the hell is this crazy bastard talking about?* He was enraged that a hunting dog had bested him, and now a guy like this was handling him.

"By the way, why did you insult my subordinate's parents earlier? It was quite unpleasant to hear. I can tolerate most things, but I can't stand insults about parents."

"I wasn't insulting you, was I?"

"If you had, your head would be rolling around right now, kicked by the busy waiters' feet. Before that, your body would have been split in half. Ah, not that I would have done it. I'm the type who despises breaking things and killing people in taverns or inns. It's just that the two people with me are very fond of my father. The moment 'your father' came out of your mouth, your neck would've been lopped off, and your body sliced in two, is what I'm saying."

While he spoke, the two arrived at an empty lot behind the inn. As they stopped, the Seven Flames surrounded them with drawn swords. They couldn't attack recklessly, as Cheon Daewoong could be in danger.

Trusting in his subordinates' presence, Cheon Daewoong asked. "The treasure map, you have it, don't you?"

"That's right. We have it. Then let me ask you a question. Who told you we were at this inn?"

Cheon Daewoong didn't answer.

"......"

"Aaaaaargh!"

A scream erupted from Cheon Daewoong's mouth. With a light twist from Geom Mugeuk, one of his shoulders was crushed and broken.

"Oops, my apologies. I must have held on too tightly. I got nervous waiting for your answer. This time, I'll wait while holding this arm."

As Geom Mugeuk reached for his other arm, Cheon Daewoong shouted. "I don't know! I only know it was someone sent directly by the Sect Leader."

"If you don't know, you should at least describe his appearance."

"He was wearing a bamboo hat, so I couldn't see!"

Geom Mugeuk could guess. It was the same man who had brought the treasure map and told him to apply the Soul Tracking Incense. In any case, he had found out that the man was connected to the leader of the Evil King Sect.

"Since I broke your arm, I feel bad, so I'll give you a piece of information. The truth is, the treasure map you're looking for was intentionally leaked by the person who sent you."

"What nonsense is this?"

"You called my subordinate a hunting dog, but you're the real hunting dog who knows nothing. Compared to you, my subordinate is a man with a special talent that no one else in the world possesses."

Geom Mugeuk looked at a tree behind them. "Ji Han."

At his call, Ji Han revealed himself from behind the tree. "If you fought these guys here, how many are you confident you could take on?"

"Three of them."

The Seven Flames' expressions hardened. How dare a mere hunting dog claim he could handle three of them?

"With your talent, you could take on all seven of them with just a little more training."

Geom Mugeuk had accurately assessed the skills of both Ji Han and the Seven Flames. "I will show you the way later."

At the unexpected words, Ji Han's heart pounded. The Young Cult Leader was saying he would bestow his teachings upon him.

"No need to be too moved. Everyone else is cursing me for putting them through a hellish training regimen."

"If it's that kind of hell, I'll gladly go anytime."

Cheon Daewoong aimed for this moment when Geom Mugeuk was distracted by talking with Ji Han.

"......"

Simultaneously, the Seven Flames thrust their swords at Geom Mugeuk. The sound of tearing flesh was heard in an instant. Not a single swordsmanship missed its mark.

STAB! STAB! STAB! STAB! STABSTABSTAB!

The next moment, the Seven Flames' eyes widened at the scene before them. The person stabbed by the swords was Cheon Daewoong. The two men's positions had switched in an instant. It was as if they had been aiming for Cheon Daewoong all along.

Since they had all aimed for his vital points, Cheon Daewoong vomited a gush of blood and died instantly.

CRACK! CRACK! CRACK!

The necks of the Seven Flames, who surrounded Cheon Daewoong with their swords still embedded in him, began to droop. Geom Mugeuk had moved in a swift circle behind them, snapping their necks.

His movement was like a flash of lightning. The Seven Flames' necks were all broken before they could even pull their swords from Cheon Daewoong's body. It was over in a flash, the sound unable to keep up with the action.

CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! CRAAACK!

With Cheon Daewoong stabbed to death in the center, the surrounding Seven Flames all died standing, their necks broken. It was a bizarre scene for anyone who might discover it.

A thrill ran through Ji Han's entire body. He knew Geom Mugeuk was strong, but he never imagined he was this strong. Their skills were worlds apart, like heaven and earth.

The Young Cult Leader looked at him, his playful demeanor gone. He spoke with deep eyes. "Don't look back anymore. We move forward."

Ji Han performed a deep bow on the spot. "I will never be trapped by the past. Thank you."

Just then, a voice shouted from behind. "Me too!"

As Tae Su, who had just appeared, was about to say something, Geom Mugeuk raised a hand to stop him. Then, as if he knew what Tae Su was about to say, he spoke calmly.

"Make your decision after you've seen your father."

Tae Su realized his heart had been too hasty and let out a deep sigh. "Yes, I understand."

The three of them returned to the inn. When they returned safely, the customers and waiters beamed with joy. Some people even applauded.

Ji Han and Tae Su ate the pork in five-spice sauce and drank liquor with peace of mind. A special dish was even brought out from the kitchen.

"It's so delicious."

Only now did Ji Han truly taste the pork in five-spice sauce. Geom Mugeuk ran to the front of the kitchen and shouted into the service window.

"It's so delicious. Please continue to preserve this flavor without changing it! There are people who will come looking for it again someday!"

Watching him, the Blood Heaven Blade Demon and the Flower Sword Supreme looked at each other. They had just agreed to come again, just the two of them.

*It's like he can read minds.*

The Blood Heaven Blade Demon shook his head and held out his cup. The Flower Sword Supreme toasted with him. What their cups held was not liquor, but a promise.

The carriage ran and ran, finally arriving in Zhejiang province. On the way, they saw numerous martial artists flocking to Zhejiang province. There were masters and novices. Some came in groups, and some came alone. They gathered like moths to a flame.

When the carriage arrived at the foot of a mountain, Tae Su finally revealed the exact location of the Grave of Swords. "The Grave of Swords is here, on Mount Tianmu."

It was Mount Tianmu, known as the Eye of Heaven. "There are ponds on the eastern and western peaks of the mountain. When viewed from the sky, they look like eyes, which is why it's called Mount Tianmu."

Tae Su revealed the precise entrance to the Grave of Swords. "The entrance to the Grave of Swords is below the left eye."
