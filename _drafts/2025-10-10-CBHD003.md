I knew the meaning of the alias 'the Heavenly Demon'. In most martial arts novels, it symbolized overwhelming martial power. The character was often the protagonist or the final boss.

Above all, it meant the leader of the group commonly called 'the Demonic Cult'. The Demonic Cult went by many names depending on the story, such as the Divine Cult, the Ming Cult, the Sacred Flames Cult, or the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

In short, he was the ridiculously strong leader of a heretic religion.

*So, am I going to become the disciple of a heretic Cult Leader?*

It could also mean I might inherit the Cult Leader's position one day.

*Is this... a good thing?*

For someone like me, who just wanted a stable and peaceful life, the situation felt very ambiguous.

Images of heretic cult leaders from the modern news naturally came to mind.

*...It might not be so bad?*

Their countless crimes were obviously wrong, but as long as I didn't commit any crimes myself, I wondered if there could be an easier life.

I would just have to show up once a week on Sunday(?), spout some nonsense, and perform a little showmanship. I could shoot palm winds or make a believer channel a spirit(?).

In a martial arts world, I could actually shoot palm winds, so I wouldn't need fake tricks. I could genuinely shoot palm winds and fly through the sky with movement arts.

People in the modern world went crazy for fake shows despite their advanced science. Imagine how they would react here.

*Ah, is that why the Heavenly Demons in novels have such powerful martial arts?*

I pondered the connection between heretic religions and the Heavenly Demon's martial arts.

I then realized the heretic Cult Leader, my future Master, was gazing at me intently.

"I'm sorry," I said. "I was just so surprised by your unexpected alias that I was rude."

"hohoho. How could that be considered rude?"

The Master laughed gently and stroked his beard. He then said something unexpected. "More importantly, you are truly peculiar."

"...What do you mean?"

"I'm talking about your reaction. When you first awoke, you seemed a bit surprised but quickly calmed down. It would've been natural to be afraid or angry, yet you tried to talk logically instead. It's the same now. After hearing this old man's alias, you weren't afraid. You just seemed to be pondering something."

"Th-that's just because these events are so far beyond my expectations that they don't feel real yet."

"hohohoho. Don't worry, I didn't say it to find fault. It means you have a deep mind and a calm heart for your age. It seems this old man's eyes weren't mistaken. I'm very pleased."

First, he said he liked my body. The nuance was a bit strange, but he probably didn't mean it in *that*(?) way. It seemed this body was just exceptionally well-suited for learning martial arts.

Then, he tested my character with that strange threat. Now he was saying he liked my deep mind and calmness. The body was fifteen, but the mind inside was thirty-four, so it was only natural.

*Somehow, I feel like his rose-tinted glasses are getting thicker and thicker.*

I had a feeling it would become harder and harder to escape from this Heavenly Demon's grasp.

Still, the life of a heretic Cult Leader seemed quite livable, so it wasn't all bad. Building a heretic religion from scratch would be a different story. Becoming the leader of an established one promised a comfortable life.

Once my thoughts reached that point, I quickly spoke.

"Ah, speaking of which, I believe my introduction is overdue. My name is Ilmok."

Telling him my name after hearing his was basic courtesy.

"Hmm. Ilmok... I don't believe I've ever heard of the surname Il (一)."

"It's not really a surname. My parents used the character 'Il' for 'first son'. For my name, they used the character 'Mok' (木), one of the few Chinese characters they knew."

In an era of vast educational disparities, many people knew few or no characters, so my story wasn't unusual.

"Hoh. Then what happened to your parents? You called the inn owner 'someone like a parent', so he must not have been your real father."

"They passed away about a year ago. After that, he took me in. He gave me food and a place to sleep in exchange for work, and he also taught me characters."

"He even taught you characters..." the Heavenly Demon said, looking at me with pity.

"When we return to the cult, I'll have someone investigate that matter. You should at least know who assassinated the one who was like a parent to you, shouldn't you? If you wish, I'll give you the chance to take revenge yourself. If not, I'll send the cult's martial artists to avenge you."

I asked hesitantly, "...If you mean that assassin, didn't he already die at the inn?"

The Heavenly Demon replied with a gentle smile. "hohoho. That man was merely following orders. Who would say they've gotten revenge just by breaking the sword? You must punish the one who wielded it, mustn't you?"

"The ones who wielded the sword..."

"The ones who pursued this old man's trail, and we must find and kill everyone who ordered the massacre at that inn, mustn't we?"

"..."

I looked at the Heavenly Demon, who promised mass slaughter with a gentle expression.

*...Should I try to escape, even now?*

As expected, the Demonic Cult was the Demonic Cult.

{sep}

Escape was impossible.

The old man in the carriage with me was an incredible drummer, a kidnapper, and possibly my future master. The man driving the carriage was also a considerable drummer.

I later learned the carriage driver was the man who had dealt with the assassin from the Murim Alliance at the inn. If I foolishly tried to escape, I could end up just like him. Besides, a bodyguard accompanying the Heavenly Demon would not be weak.

The carriage carried me along for a while. I felt a strange mix of excitement and anxiety about becoming a heretic Cult Leader.

We finally arrived at the Main Headquarters of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. It seemed I had been unconscious for a long time.

As soon as the Heavenly Demon stepped out of the carriage, the two gatekeepers at the entrance dropped to the ground in a full prostration.

"We greet the Lord of Ten Thousand Demons!"

The reverent greetings did not end with the gatekeepers.

"We greet the Lord of Ten Thousand Demons!"

"Please grant us salvation!"

"O, Heavenly Demon!!"

Every person we met bowed with utmost reverence, lying flat on the ground as they offered prayers. The Heavenly Demon, the object of their prayers, accepted all their greetings with a gentle smile, completely used to it.

I followed behind the Heavenly Demon, witnessing all of this with a peculiar mix of emotions.

*This is the life of a heretic Cult Leader?*

How should I put it? It was a slightly burdensome sight, but...

*This is awesome, isn't it?*

It was a sight truly worthy of the phrase 'absolute power'.

The bowing people all looked at me trailing behind the Heavenly Demon with curiosity, but not a single one dared to question him. The atmosphere simply suggested that 'the great Heavenly Demon must have a reason for bringing that guy along'.

After walking through the valedictorian for a while and witnessing the Heavenly Demon's majesty, he stopped. He stood before a valedictorian with the words [the Wind Cliff Pavilion] written on it.

"You must be tired from traveling so far in a body that hasn't learned martial arts," he said. "Rest here for the day."

Moved by the Heavenly Demon's consideration, I made up my mind.

"Thank you, Elder. No, from now on, I'll call you Master."

Perhaps because of my sudden change in address, the Master stroked his beard and looked at me with interest.

"Have you finally made your decision?"

"Yes. As you said, Master, if this is the only option left, I believe doing my best here is the right way to live."

"hohohoho. You have thought well."

Facing the gently smiling Master, I spoke to seal the deal. "Then, though it's late, may I offer my bow now?"

People usually think of the Nine Bows when bowing to a master, and I thought the same. This was a misconception. The Nine Bows literally referred to the etiquette for nine different types of bows.

Among those nine, the bow performed for a superior in a master-disciple, parent-child, or lord-vassal relationship is called a kowtow.

I boldly knelt on the floor and performed a kowtow to the Heavenly Demon. The Master burst into hearty laughter.

"Hahahaha. It seems I've gained a very fine disciple in my later years."

After saying so, the Master told me to rise and added one last thing.

"I must handle the cult's major and minor affairs, so even though you're my disciple, this old man can't be with you twenty-four hours a day. Therefore, starting today, I'll assign a handmaiden to you. That child will help you and teach you what you need to know as a believer of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult."

His speech was quite long, as expected of the highest authority, but the content was not boring at all.

*A handmaiden...*

As a civil servant in the Republic of Korea, I had suffered from petty power trips. Now, I had a subordinate. I had no intention of abusing my power or harassing her, but the moment filled me with peculiar emotions.

*As expected, life is all about connections.*

And the best kind is a personal connection. Since the Cult Leader himself had become my master, there could be no better connection.

Not long after the busy Master left, a voice like rolling jade beads came from the entrance of the valedictorian.

"Young Lord Ilmok. I am Jin Hayeon. I have come at the behest of the Heavenly Demon to assist you with your chores."

Her voice was exciting just to hear, but I forced myself to speak calmly.

"You may come in."

I wasn't used to an authoritative tone yet, so I replied to the handmaiden politely.

The woman who opened the door and entered had snow-white skin with deep black hair and eyebrows, contrary to her name, which meant 'Red Lotus'. She looked like a doll.

*Damn...*

She was the most beautiful woman I had ever seen in my life.

She was the kind of beauty you would only see on TV, an appearance that had no connection to my real world. I had dated before in my thirty-four years and thought I had a decent immunity to women, but this was different.

A woman so stunning it would be intimidating to even talk to her, let alone date her, was to be my handmaiden?

*Master. Just what kind of life have you been living?*

If a mere disciple of the Cult Leader gets this, what kind of life does the Cult Leader himself lead? A rosy future replayed endlessly in my mind.

The movie in my head kept restarting no matter how many times I tried to pause it. Just as it began a scene where I contemplated the name of our second child, she spoke, her face calm.

"I heard from the Heavenly Demon that you turned fifteen this year, Young Lord."

She started by talking about age. Then again, age is an important matter between a man and a woman. My body was only fifteen, but my mind was in its mid-thirties. There shouldn't be any major problems in continuing our relationship(?) going forward.

"That is correct."

She responded to my confident reply. "In that case, you must enter the Demonic Academy next year when you turn sixteen."

"What kind of place is this Demonic Academy?"

"It's an educational institution for raising the demonic practitioners who will one day lead our Divine Cult. It selects the most outstanding children from the disciples and offspring of our believers. Everyone who turns sixteen enters it each year."

In short, it was an elite training center.

*Hmm... Since it's a place to learn martial arts, is it like a military academy?*

With that thought, I asked Jin Hayeon, "Then, as the Master's disciple, can I just get in?"

"Even as the Heavenly Demon's disciple, you must take the test and pass to be admitted."

"...If it's a place only the most outstanding can enter, haven't the others been preparing for a long time?"

"That is correct."

"...And I have to get in there in just one year?"

"That is correct."

She replied in an utterly business-like tone.

"Th-that seems like a slightly absurd condition, don't you think?"

"Because you only have one year, I am telling you to 'just pass'. Among all the disciples of the Heavenly Demon so far, not a single one has failed to enter as the valedictorian or graduate as the valedictorian."

Her cold reply made me realize one important fact. I wasn't the Master's only disciple. In other words, there was no guarantee I would become the Cult Leader.

A more important problem remained, so I asked her again. "Then, what happens if I fail to get into the Demonic Academy?"

"You will die."

It seemed being the disciple of a heretic Cult Leader wasn't such a comfortable position after all.
