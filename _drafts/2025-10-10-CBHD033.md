After coordinating with the former bandits, now our slaves, we headed for Hejing County.

Leading nearly twenty slaves into Hejing County would be too conspicuous. We decided to take the bandit leader as a hostage instead. The rest were ordered to camp some distance away from the county.

The bandits delayed us, so the sun had completely set by the time we reached Hejing County. Just as in Gaochang County, we stayed the night at an inn. The next morning, we began our inspection tour.

I wandered around the town for a while, dragging along the former bandit leader, whose face was full of resentment.

"......"

I stroked my beardless chin and came to a conclusion.

"It’s not much different from Gaochang County."

The Branch's martial artists helped the townspeople with their work, and the Branch Manager taught the local children, just like before. The children chattered nonstop, and the beleaguered Branch Manager struggled to continue the lesson.

The only fortunate thing was that the Branch Manager here was not consumed by rage.

Just as I concluded there was nothing else to investigate, a loud noise erupted from somewhere.

"Martial artists! Please, help me!!"

A middle-aged man shouted as he clung to the trouser leg of a young man in a Demonic Cult uniform. The young man, a martial artist from the Demonic Cult Branch, wore a troubled expression. Judging by the luggage in his hands, he was in the middle of helping with other work.

*Hmm. Are they short on manpower?*

It would be difficult for a single Branch's martial artists to help everyone in town. I knew they operated by taking turns with each household. Still, the labor of a trained martial artist was far greater than a child's help, so even with a rotation system, their work was ultimately more efficient.

*Tsk. He should just wait his turn. Or could it be that this Branch is already committing corruption by only helping those they're close to?*

I thought this, but the conversation that followed proved my assumption wrong.

"Please wait just a little longer. We've sent a message to the Cult, so a martial corps will be dispatched soon."

"What if those bastards run away in the meantime!"

"Please trust the Divine Cult's martial corps. No matter how far those bastards flee, they will surely chase them down and deliver divine punishment."

"I'm grateful that you help with other people's work and teach the children to read, but you still have to catch the bad guys! Isn't that why we give donations to the Divine Cult!"

It seemed his valuables had been stolen. I needed to understand the situation, so I approached them.

"Is there a problem?"

In response to my question, the man explained his unfair situation. The story was simple. He was a nomad who traveled across the Xinjiang region with his companions, selling milk and other animal byproducts. On his way to Hejing County, he encountered bandits.

He had fortunately escaped with his life, but all his animals were stolen, leaving him on the verge of becoming a beggar.

After hearing the whole story, I smacked the back of the head of the former bandit leader standing next to me.

PWAK!

"Kuhk..."

The bandit leader groaned, powerless with his acupoints sealed. The poor nomad and the Branch martial artist stared at me, their expressions asking what I was doing.

Meanwhile, I lifted the chin of the bandit leader, who clutched the back of his head.

"Doesn't this bastard's face look familiar?"

The nomad's eyes widened as he carefully examined the bandit leader's face.

"I-It's him!! He's definitely the leader of those horseback bandits from back then!!"

"!!!"

At that shout, everyone from the Branch martial artists to the onlookers stared back and forth between me and the bandit leader with shocked faces.

I turned my head and looked at Jin Hayeon, who held my identification plaque. The inspection of Hejing County was more or less finished. With everyone's eyes on me, I saw no need to hide my identity.

Jin Hayeon seemed to understand my intention. She took the identification plaque from her sleeve and held it forward.

"This is Young Lord Ilmok, the eighth disciple of the Exalted One."

"We greet the disciple of the Heavenly Demon!!"

When her introduction finished, the Branch martial artist prostrated himself completely. The nearby onlookers took the hint and began to kneel one by one.

"Rise."

I was not yet used to this kind of power, so I had the people rise and then spoke.

"As the eighth disciple of the Exalted One, I came here after hearing news of those who disturb our Divine Cult's territory."

"Th-Then, does that mean you've come after wiping out all the bandits who were harassing us!?"

"That is correct," I answered with great confidence.

My original purpose was the inspection, but there was no reason to explain that.

And above all.

*As expected, people with insufficient faith were beginning to appear.*

Just as I told Master, continuous kindness makes people see it as their right. The words the nomad shouted while clinging to the martial artist's leg were proof. Therefore, I needed to use this opportunity to correct their wavering faith.

*Though I don't know why I have to be the one to play that role...*

Just then, the nomad with little faith, who had heard my explanation, cautiously asked a question.

"B-But why is it only the leader?"

"I killed all the rest. It would have been a hassle to drag dozens of corpses all the way here, so I captured only this one as proof that we subjugated the bandits."

"!!!"

This time, Jin Hayeon, the two bodyguards, and the bandit leader beside me all stared at me with shocked faces.

I glared at the four of them and spoke with my eyes.

[Just play along, you idiots!]

It was like sending a telepathic message, a skill I could not yet use.

"Ahem. That is correct."

Still, they seemed to understand my intention. The two bodyguard martial artists coughed dryly, and Jin Hayeon’s expression returned to its usual stoicism.

Just then, an oblivious onlooker asked a sharp question.

"But haven't you been walking around town with that bandit leader since this morning? Why were you hiding that fact?"

Well, it was for the inspection. I also had another use for this bandit leader and did not intend to kill him, but if I explained all that now, my words would lose their power.

"It was to test your hearts!" I chose a response befitting the disciple of a heretic Cult Leader.

"I looked around the town to see how much you all believe in our Divine Cult, but frankly, I am quite disappointed!"

At my shout, the villagers, starting with the faithless nomad, fell to their knees again and began to cry out.

"Oh, Heavenly Demon!!"

"Please forgive us!!"

"We have committed a sin worthy of death, Young Lord!!"

As I scanned the earnestly confessing crowd, a wave of guilt washed over me, but I had already come this far. It seemed better to wrap things up quickly.

THUD.

First, I threw my wallet of emergency funds to the nomad who had lost his valuables. Then I shouted again.

"This is compensation for the property you had stolen by these bastards. If it is not enough, I will check at the Main Headquarters and provide additional payment."

"The Divine Cult strives to help and protect you. If you take our goodwill lightly, then we too cannot help you. I will overlook this matter this time, so I hope you will trust and follow our Divine Cult a little more."

"Aaaah!!"

"We will believe and follow you, Young Lord!"

"Long live, long live, long, long live!!"

"Please save us!!"

Perhaps they believed they had repented and been forgiven. The residents bowed to me as if I were the Master himself.

"Then I will drag this bastard to the Cult to conclude this matter. Everyone, please return to what you were doing."

With that, I turned without delay and headed for the inn where our carriage was parked. It was not just because acting like a heretic Cult Leader was burdensome.

*Damn it. I should have sent them somewhere far away.*

If I delayed, the bandits I had waiting outside the town might be discovered. According to what I just heard, a request for support had already been sent to the Main Headquarters. This meant that while we wasted time here, the Demonic Cult's martial corps could annihilate the bandits.

My lie being exposed was one problem. It would also be troublesome if the men I had other plans for died so pointlessly.

*Haa. To think I have to go back without receiving a reception.*

The reception from Gaochang County flickered in my mind. I glanced back. Jin Hayeon, the two bodyguard martial artists, and the bandit leader all looked at me with various expressions. Some looked impressed, others intrigued.

Among them, the bandit leader still wore a sour expression, as if he had chewed on shit. I smacked the back of his head again.

PWAK!

Because of this bastard, I had to go back without getting a reception.

{sep}

The carriage left Hejing County and headed for the place where we had arranged to meet the bandits. Their leader was our hostage, so the men were waiting quietly. We then headed with them toward the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult Main Headquarters.

Just in case, we sealed all the bandits' acupoints and blocked their inner arts. They rode their horses and followed us.

We loaded the bandit leader, our hostage, into the carriage with us. This ensured his men would not dare attempt a rescue.

I asked the bandit leader, Hang Geon, who still wore a sour expression.

"What are you so unhappy about?"

"…I'm being dragged along and treated like this, so of course I'm unhappy."

I replied with a dry laugh.

"Didn't you hear what was said back in town? They summoned our Cult's martial corps to kill you all. If you hadn't run into me, all of your heads would have been cut off within a few days, you Khitan."

"I'd rather die than be treated like this!!"

Listening to him, I started to get annoyed.

"I've been thinking this since yesterday, but you're a real idiot. It's a waste that those men risked their lives to save someone like you."

"If you're going to insult me like this, just kill me!"

"Is your life only about you? If you have followers, you need a sense of responsibility. Huh? It was the same yesterday during the raid. The so-called leader acted like he didn't want to be there, making your subordinates do all the work while you just watched. You're a pathetic bastard. I can see why you were framed."

"W-What did you just say!"

"Shut up and listen! You're a moron with nothing left but your pride. Wasn't that guy who came as the thousand man commander from a wealthy family? In a few years, he would have risen to a position where someone like you wouldn't even be in his sights. Was it so hard to endure for just a few years? So hard that because of you alone, all those men had to risk their lives and desert?"

"!!!"

Hang Geon's eyes wavered greatly. If he had answered, ‘I didn't order it! They deserted with me on their own accord!’ then to hell with my plans, I would have just killed him or kicked him out. Working with a piece of trash with no sense of responsibility was a shortcut to ruining everything.

"If it were me, I would have seen it as an opportunity. I would have swallowed my pride, flattered him, and grabbed onto that connection. Yes. To you, that young man probably seemed incompetent, but then, on the contrary, after flattering him a bit and getting close, couldn't you have given him some advice indirectly? You should have done that for the sake of the careers of the men who believe in and follow you. Isn't that right?"

"……"

Perhaps because I kept lashing out at him, the stubbornly defiant Hang Geon hung his head as if his spirit was broken. Seeing him lost in deep thought, my own agitation subsided a little.

After a moment, Hang Geon organized his thoughts and asked in a calm tone.

"So, are you planning to let us live?"

"I'll have to discuss it with Master first, but as long as you do the work I give you, I have no intention of harming you."

"I suppose I must survive somehow, even as a slave. For the sake of my brothers," Hang Geon said bitterly.

My emotions had calmed, so I smiled gently and replied.

"Don't worry. As long as you do your work well, you won't have to live like slaves. I may be young, but I am a disciple of the Exalted One. Shouldn't you try grabbing the right rope this time?"

"!!!"

Hang Geon looked startled. He suddenly slammed his head against the carriage floor in a deep bow, even with his arms tied.

"I will serve you as my Liege!"
