After coordinating with the former bandits, who were now our slaves, we departed for Hejing County. Leading nearly twenty slaves into the county would be too conspicuous, so we took the bandit leader as a hostage. The rest were ordered to camp some distance away from Hejing County.

The bandits had delayed us, so the sun had completely set by the time we reached Hejing County. We stayed the night at an inn, just as we had in Gaochang County, and began our inspection tour the next morning. I wandered around the town for a while, dragging the resentful former bandit leader along with me.

"......"

I stroked my beardless chin and reached a conclusion.

"It's not much different from Gaochang County."

The Branch's martial artists helped the townspeople with their work, and someone who looked like the Branch Manager was teaching children. The children chattered endlessly while the beleaguered Branch Manager struggled to continue the lesson. Fortunately, the Branch Manager here was not consumed by rage.

Just as I concluded there was nothing else to investigate, a loud noise erupted nearby.

"Martial artists! Please, help me!"

A middle-aged man shouted, clinging to the trouser leg of a young man in a Demonic Cult uniform. The young man, a martial artist from the Demonic Cult Branch, looked troubled. Judging by the luggage in his hands, he was in the middle of helping with other work.

*Hmm. Are they short on manpower?*

It would be difficult for the few martial artists at a single Branch to help everyone in town. I knew they operated by taking turns helping each household. Still, the labor of a trained martial artist was far greater than a child's, so their turn-based system was ultimately more efficient.

*Tsk. He should just wait his turn. Or could it be that this Branch is already corrupt, only helping those they're close to?*

I had such thoughts, but the conversation that followed proved my misunderstanding.

"Please wait just a little longer. We've sent a message to the Cult, so a martial corps will be dispatched soon."

"What if those bastards run away in the meantime!"

"Please trust the Divine Cult's martial corps. No matter how far those bastards flee, they'll surely chase them down and deliver divine punishment."

"I'm grateful that you help with work and teach the children, but you still have to catch the bad guys! Isn't that why we give donations to the Divine Cult!"

It seemed the man had been robbed. I felt the need to understand the situation better, so I approached them.

"Is there a problem?"

At my question, the man clinging to the martial artist's leg explained his unfair situation. The man was a nomad who traveled across the Xinjiang region with his companions, finding pastures for his goats and donkeys and selling milk in towns. He said he encountered bandits on his way to Hejing County.

He had fortunately escaped with his life, but the bandits stole all his animals, leaving him on the verge of becoming a beggar.

After hearing the whole story, I smacked the back of the head of the former bandit leader standing next to me.

PWAK!

"Kuhk..."

The bandit leader groaned, his acupoints sealed and powerless. The poor nomad and the Branch martial artist stared at me, their expressions asking what I was doing. I lifted the chin of the bandit leader, who was clutching the back of his head.

"Doesn't this bastard's face look familiar?"

The nomad's eyes widened as he carefully examined the bandit leader's face.

"I-It's him! He's definitely the leader of those horseback bandits from back then!"

"!!!"

At the shout, everyone from the Branch martial artists to the onlookers stared back and forth between me and the bandit leader, their faces shocked. I turned my head and looked at Jin Hayeon, who held my identification plaque.

The inspection of Hejing County was more or less finished. With everyone's eyes on me, I decided there was no need to hide my identity.

Jin Hayeon seemed to understand my intent. She took the identification plaque from her sleeve and held it forward.

"This is Young Lord Ilmok, the eighth disciple of the Exalted One."

"We greet the disciple of the Heavenly Demon!"

As soon as she finished, the Branch martial artist prostrated himself. The nearby onlookers, taking the hint, began to kneel one by one.

"Rise."

I was not yet used to this kind of power, so I had the people stand.

"As the eighth disciple of the Exalted One, I came here after hearing news of those who disturb our Divine Cult's territory."

"Th-Then, does that mean you've come after wiping out all the bandits who were harassing us!?"

"That is correct," I answered with great confidence. My original purpose was the inspection, but there was no reason to explain that.

And above all.

*As expected, people with insufficient faith were beginning to appear.*

Just as I told Master, when kindness continues, people start to see it as their right. The nomad's words were proof of that. It seemed necessary to use this opportunity to correct their wavering faith.

*Though I don't know why I have to be the one to play that role...*

Just then, the nomad, the man of little faith who had heard my explanation, cautiously asked a question.

"B-But why is it only the leader?"

"I killed all the rest. It would've been a hassle to drag dozens of corpses here, so I captured only this one as proof that we subjugated the bandits."

"!!!"

This time, Jin Hayeon, the two bodyguards, and the bandit leader beside me stared at me with shocked faces.

I glared at the four of them and spoke with my eyes.

[Just play along, you idiots!]

It was like sending a telepathic message, a skill I could not yet use.

"Ahem. That is correct."

The two bodyguard martial artists seemed to understand my intention and let out a dry cough. Jin Hayeon also returned to her usual stoic expression.

Just then, an oblivious onlooker asked a sharp question.

"But haven't you been walking around town with that bandit leader since this morning? Why were you hiding that fact?"

Well, it was for the inspection. Also, I had another use for this bandit leader and did not intend to kill him. However, explaining all that now would weaken the power of my words.

"It was to test your hearts!" I chose a response befitting the disciple of a heretic Cult Leader.

"I looked around the town to see how much you all believe in our Divine Cult, but frankly, I am quite disappointed!"

At my shout, the villagers, starting with the faithless nomad, fell to their knees again and began to cry out.

"Oh, Heavenly Demon!"

"Please forgive us!"

"We have committed a sin worthy of death, Young Lord!"

As I scanned the earnestly confessing people, a wave of guilt washed over me, but I had come too far. It seemed better to wrap things up quickly.

THUD.

I threw my wallet, which held my emergency funds, to the nomad.

"This is compensation for the property you had stolen. If it's not enough, I'll check at the Main Headquarters and provide additional payment."

I then shouted again.

"The Divine Cult strives to help and protect you. If you take our goodwill lightly, then we can't help you either. I'll overlook this matter this time, so I hope you'll trust and follow our Divine Cult a little more."

"Aaaah!"

"We will believe and follow you, Young Lord!"

"Long live, long live, long, long live!"

"Please save us!"

Perhaps because they believed they had been forgiven, the residents bowed to me as if I were Master.

"Then I'll drag this bastard to the Cult to conclude this matter. Everyone, please return to what you were doing."

With that, I turned without delay and headed for the inn where our carriage was parked. It was not just because acting like a heretic Cult Leader was burdensome.

*Damn it. I should have sent them somewhere far away.*

If I delayed, the bandits waiting outside town might be discovered. According to what I just heard, a request for support had already been sent to the Main Headquarters. While we were wasting time here, the Demonic Cult's martial corps could annihilate the bandits.

My lie being exposed was a problem, but it would also be troublesome for those men to die so pointlessly when I had other plans for them.

*Haa. To think I have to go back without receiving a reception.*

The reception from Gaochang County flickered in my mind. I glanced back and saw Jin Hayeon, the two bodyguards, and the bandit leader all looking at me with various expressions. Some looked impressed, others intrigued.

I once again smacked the back of the head of the bandit leader, who still wore a sour expression as if he had chewed on shit.

PWAK!

Because of this bastard, I had to leave without getting a reception.

{sep}

The carriage left Hejing County and headed for our meeting place with the bandits. The men were waiting quietly, perhaps because their leader was a hostage. We then traveled with them toward the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult Main Headquarters.

For safety, all the bandits had their acupoints sealed and their inner arts blocked as they followed us on their horses. The bandit leader, our hostage, was loaded into the carriage with us so his men would not dare attempt a rescue.

I asked the bandit leader, Hang Geon, who still looked sour.

"What are you so unhappy about?"

"…I'm being dragged along and treated like this, so of course I'm unhappy."

I gave a dry laugh.

"Didn't you hear what they said in town? They summoned our Cult's martial corps to kill you all. If you hadn't run into me, all of your heads would've been cut off within days, you Khitan."

"I'd rather die than be treated like this!"

Listening to him, I started to get annoyed.

"I've been thinking this since yesterday, but you're a real idiot. It's a waste that those men risked their lives to save someone like you."

"If you're going to insult me like this, just kill me!"

"Is your life only about you? If you have followers, you should have a sense of responsibility. Huh? It was the same yesterday. The so-called leader didn't look like he wanted to do it, yet you just made your subordinates work while you watched. You pathetic bastard. I can see why you were framed."

"W-What did you just say!"

"Shut up and listen! You're a moron with nothing but pride. Wasn't that thousand man commander from a wealthy family? In a few years, he would've risen to a position where you wouldn't even be in his sights. Was it so hard to endure for just a few years? So hard that all those men had to risk their lives and desert because of you?"

"!!!"

Hang Geon's eyes wavered greatly. If he had answered, 'I didn't order it! They deserted with me on their own accord!' then I would have killed him or kicked him out, to hell with my plans. Working with a piece of trash with no sense of responsibility was a shortcut to ruin.

"If it were me, I would've seen it as an opportunity. I would've swallowed my pride, flattered him, and grabbed onto that connection. Yes. To you, that young man probably seemed incompetent, but couldn't you have flattered him, gotten close, and then given him some indirect advice? You should have done that for the careers of the men who follow you. Isn't that right?"

"……"

Perhaps because I kept lashing out, the defiant Hang Geon hung his head, his spirit seemingly broken. Seeing him lost in thought, my own agitation subsided.

After a moment, Hang Geon organized his thoughts and asked in a calm tone.

"So, are you planning to let us live?"

"I'll have to discuss it with Master first, but as long as you do the work I give you, I have no intention of harming you."

"I suppose I must survive somehow, even as a slave. For the sake of my brothers."

At Hang Geon's bitter words, my emotions now calm, I smiled gently.

"Don't worry. As long as you do your work well, you won't have to live like slaves. I may be young, but I am a disciple of the Exalted One. Shouldn't you try grabbing the right rope this time?"

"!!!"

Hang Geon looked startled. Then, despite his tied arms, he suddenly slammed his head against the carriage floor in a deep bow.

"I will serve you as my Liege!"
