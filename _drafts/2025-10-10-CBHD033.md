Chapter 33: Manpower Reinforcement (2)

After we finished coordinating with the former bandits, now slaves, we headed for Hejing County.
Leading nearly twenty slaves into Hejing County would be too conspicuous, so we decided to take the bandit leader as a hostage.
This meant the rest of them were ordered to camp out some distance away from Hejing County.
Perhaps because the bandits delayed us a bit, by the time we reached Hejing County, the sun had already completely set.
Just as in Gaochang County, we stayed the night at an inn and began to tour Hejing County the next morning for the inspection.
I wandered around the town for a while with the former bandit leader, who was being dragged along with a face full of resentment.
"Hmm."
I stroked my beardless chin and came to a conclusion.
"It’s not much different from Gaochang County."
The Branch's martial artists helping the townspeople with their livelihoods was the same, and the sight of someone who appeared to be the Branch Manager teaching children was also similar.
The children chattered nonstop, and the Branch Manager, beleaguered by them, was struggling to somehow continue the lesson.
The only fortunate thing, I suppose, was that the Branch Manager here wasn't running wild, consumed by rage.
Just as I concluded there was nothing more of particular note to investigate...
A loud noise came from somewhere.
"Martial artists! Please, help me!!"
A middle-aged man was clinging to the trouser leg of a young man dressed in the Demonic Cult's martial arts uniform, shouting.
And the young man, who appeared to be a martial artist from the Demonic Cult Branch, wore a troubled expression.
Judging by the luggage in his hands, he seemed to be a martial artist who was in the middle of helping with other work.
*Hmm. Are they short on manpower?*
Indeed, it would be difficult for just the martial artists belonging to a mere Branch to help with all the livelihoods in the town. I knew they operated by taking turns helping each household.
Still, compared to the help from children, the labor of martial artists trained in martial arts would be far greater, so even if they took turns, the efficiency would ultimately be higher.
*Tsk. He should just wait his turn. Or could it be that this Branch is already committing corruption by only helping those they're close to?*
I was having such thoughts, but after listening to the ensuing conversation, I realized it was my misunderstanding.
"Please wait just a little longer. We've sent a message to the Cult, so a martial corps will be dispatched soon."
"What if those bastards run away in the meantime!"
"Please trust the Divine Cult's martial corps. No matter how far those bastards flee, they will surely be able to chase them down and deliver divine punishment."
"I'm grateful that you help with other people's work and teach the children to read, but you still have to catch the bad guys! Isn't that why we give donations to the Divine Cult!"
It seemed he had his valuables stolen or taken by someone.
Feeling the need to grasp the situation in more detail, I approached and asked them.
"Is there a problem?"
And at my question, the man clinging to the martial artist's trouser leg explained the situation, claiming it was unfair.
The situation was simple.
The man was a nomad who traveled back and forth across the Xinjiang region with his companions, finding pastures to feed his goats and donkeys, and visiting towns to sell milk and other animal byproducts. He said he encountered bandits on his way to Hejing County.
Fortunately, he escaped with his life intact, but he said all the animals he was leading were stolen, leaving him on the verge of becoming a beggar.
After hearing the whole story, I smacked the back of the head of the former bandit leader standing next to me.
PWAK!
"Kuhk..."
While the bandit leader, powerless with his acupoints sealed, let out a groan, the poor nomad and the Branch martial artist stared at me with expressions that asked what I was doing.
Meanwhile, I lifted the chin of the bandit leader, who was clutching the back of his head, and said.
"Doesn't this bastard's face look familiar?"
At my question, the nomad's eyes widened as he carefully examined the bandit leader's face.
"I-It's him!! He's definitely the leader of those horseback bandits from back then!!"
"!!!"
At that shout, everyone from the Branch martial artists to the onlookers nearby stared back and forth between me and the bandit leader with shocked faces.
Then I turned my head and looked at Jin Hayeon, who was holding my identification plaque.
The inspection of Hejing County was already more or less finished, and with everyone's eyes on me, I decided there was no need to hide my identity.
As if she understood my intention, Jin Hayeon took the identification plaque from her sleeve and held it forward.
"This is Young Lord Ilmok, the eighth disciple of the Exalted One."
"We greet the disciple of the Heavenly Demon!!"
As soon as her introduction finished, the Branch martial artist prostrated himself fully, and the onlookers nearby began to kneel one by one, taking the hint.
"Rise."
I wasn't used to this kind of power-tripping yet, so I had the people rise and then spoke.
"As the eighth disciple of the Exalted One, I came here after hearing news of those who disturb the territory of our Divine Cult."
"Th-Then, does that mean you've come after wiping out all the bandits who were harassing us!?"
"That is correct."
I answered with great confidence.
My original purpose was the inspection, but there was no reason to explain that in detail.
And above all.
*As expected, people with insufficient faith were beginning to appear.*
Just as I told Master, when kindness continues, people begin to see it as their right. The words that nomad shouted while clinging to the martial artist's trouser leg were proof of that.
Therefore, it seemed necessary to use this opportunity to correct their wavering faith.
*Though I don't know why I have to be the one to play that role...*
Just then, the man of little faith who had heard my entire explanation, the nomad, cautiously posed a question.
"B-But why is it only the leader?"
"I killed all the rest. It would have been a hassle to drag dozens of corpses all the way here, so I captured only this one as proof that we subjugated the bandits."
"!!!"
This time, Jin Hayeon, the two bodyguards, and the bandit leader who were beside me stared at me with shocked faces.
I glared at the four of them and spoke with my eyes.
[Just play along, you idiots!]
As if sending a telepathic message that I couldn't yet use due to my insufficient level of mastery.
"Ahem. That is correct."
Still, as if they understood my intention, the two bodyguard martial artists let out a dry cough, and Jin Hayeon also returned to her usual stoic expression.
Right then, an oblivious onlooker asked a sharp question.
"But haven't you been walking around town with that bandit leader since this morning? Why were you hiding that fact?"
Well, it was for the inspection. In addition, this bandit leader wasn't meant to be killed, as I had another use for him.
However, if I were to explain all that now, the power behind my words would inevitably be diluted.
"It was to test your hearts!"
I chose a response befitting the disciple of a heretic Cult Leader.
"I looked around the town to see how much you all believe in our Divine Cult, but frankly, I am quite disappointed!"
At my shout, the villagers, starting with the nomad who had the least faith, fell to their knees again and began to cry out.
"Oh, Heavenly Demon!!"
"Please forgive us!!"
"We have committed a sin worthy of death, Young Lord!!"
As I scanned over those earnestly confessing their sins, a wave of guilt washed over me, but I had already come this far. It seemed better to wrap things up quickly.
THUD.
"This is compensation for the property you had stolen by these bastards. If it is not enough, I will check at the Main Headquarters and provide additional payment."
First, I threw my wallet, which contained my emergency funds, to the nomad who said all his valuables had been stolen, and then shouted again.
"The Divine Cult strives to help and protect you. If you take our goodwill lightly, then we too cannot help you. I will overlook this matter this time, so I hope you will trust and follow our Divine Cult a little more."
"Aaaah!!"
"We will believe and follow you, Young Lord!"
"Long live, long live, long, long live!!"
"Please save us!!"
Was it because they believed they had repented and been forgiven? The residents bowed to me as if I were Master.
"Then I will drag this bastard to the Cult to conclude this matter, so everyone, please return to what you were doing."
Saying that, I turned without delay and headed toward the inn where the carriage was parked.
It wasn't just because acting like a heretic Cult Leader was burdensome.
*Damn it. I should have sent them somewhere far away.*
If I delayed, there was a risk that the bandits I had waiting outside the town would be discovered by others.
Especially since, according to what I just heard, a request for support had already been sent to the Main Headquarters.
This meant that while we were wasting time here, the bandits could be annihilated by the Demonic Cult's martial corps.
My lie being exposed was a problem, and it would be troublesome for those I had other plans for to die so pointlessly.
*Haa. To think I have to go back without receiving a reception.*
The reception I received in Gaochang County flickered before my eyes, and when I glanced back, Jin Hayeon, the two bodyguard martial artists, and the bandit leader were all looking at me with various expressions.
Some looked impressed, while others looked intrigued.
Among them, I once again smacked the back of the head of the bandit leader, who still wore a sour expression as if he'd chewed on shit.
PWAK!
Because of this bastard, I have to go back without getting a reception.
★★★
The carriage, having left Hejing County, headed for the place where we had arranged to meet the bandits.
Perhaps because their leader was being held hostage, the men were waiting quietly there, and we headed with them toward the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult Main Headquarters.
Just in case, all the bandits had their acupoints sealed and their inner arts blocked as they rode their horses and followed us.
And the bandit leader, who was essentially a hostage, was loaded into the carriage with us so that his men wouldn't even dare to attempt a rescue.
I asked the bandit leader, Hang Geon, who still wore a sour expression.
"What are you so unhappy about?"
"…I'm being dragged along and treated like this, so of course I'm unhappy."
I replied with a dry laugh.
"Didn't you hear what was said back in town? They summoned our Cult's martial corps to kill you all. If you hadn't run into me, all of your heads would have been cut off within a few days, you Khitan."
"I'd rather die than be treated like this!!"
Listening to him, I started to get annoyed.
"I've been thinking this since yesterday, but you're a real idiot. It's a waste that those men risked their lives to save someone like you."
"If you're going to insult me like this, just kill me!"
"Is your life only about you? If you have followers, you should have a sense of responsibility. Huh? It was the same yesterday during the banditry. The so-called leader was giving off vibes that he didn't want to do it, yet you just made your subordinates do the work and watched. You pathetic bastard. I can see why you were framed without even looking."
"W-What did you just say!"
"Shut up and listen! You're a moron with nothing left but your pride. Wasn't that guy who came as the thousand man commander from a wealthy family? In a few years, he would have risen to a position where someone like you wouldn't even be in his sights. Was it so hard to endure for just a few years? So hard that because of you alone, all those men had to risk their lives and desert?"
"!!!"
Hang Geon's eyes wavered greatly.
If he had answered here, ‘I didn't order it! They deserted with me on their own accord!’ then to hell with my plans, I would have just killed him or kicked him out.
Because working with a piece of trash with no sense of responsibility was a shortcut to ruining everything.
"If it were me, I would have seen it as an opportunity. I would have swallowed my pride, flattered him, and grabbed onto that connection. Yes. To you, that young man probably seemed Incompetence. But then, on the contrary, after flattering him a bit and getting close, couldn't you have given him some advice indirectly? You should have done that for the sake of the careers of the men who believe in and follow you. Isn't that right?"
"……"
Perhaps because I kept lashing out at him, Hang Geon, who had been stubbornly defiant, hung his head as if his spirit was broken.
Seeing him looking as if he were lost in deep thought, my own agitation subsided a little.
And after a moment.
As if he had organized all his thoughts, Hang Geon asked in a calm tone.
"So, are you planning to let us live?"
"I'll have to discuss it with Master first, but as long as you do the work I give you, I have no intention of harming you."
"I suppose I must survive somehow, even as a slave. For the sake of my brothers."
At Hang Geon's bitter last words, my emotions having calmed, I smiled gently and replied.
"Don't worry. As long as you do your work well, you won't have to live like slaves. I may be young, but I am a disciple of the Exalted One. Shouldn't you try grabbing the right rope this time?"
"!!!"
Hang Geon, who had a startled expression, suddenly slammed his head against the carriage floor in a deep bow, despite his arms being tied.
"I will serve you as my Liege!"
