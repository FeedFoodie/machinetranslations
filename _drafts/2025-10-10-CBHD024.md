Perhaps it was the threat to my life. The moment Eldest Senior Brother's qi focused on me, my body's concentration peaked in a desperate bid for survival. The world began to flow in slow motion.

I could faintly see Third Senior Brother's sword draw, a movement I normally couldn't follow. Even as the world moved slowly, Eldest Senior Brother’s figure filled my vision, approaching at a tremendous speed.

Third Senior Brother’s sword wounded Eldest Senior Brother’s forearm, but Eldest Senior Brother ignored the injury and charged straight at me.

The madman’s greatsword flew toward my face, threatening to split me in two. A massive red energy, far larger than the greatsword itself, enveloped the blade. It was a terrifying Burning Sun Finger Qi, completely incomparable to my own shallow inner arts.

I couldn't just die like this. The world moved in slow motion, but my body was also as slow as a slug.

*Move!! Move!!!*

Perhaps that desperate thought reached my body. My hand, already gripping my sword, finally unleashed the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw. I drew the blade based on my trained senses.

My Soul-Reaping Quick Draw met the path of Eldest Senior Brother’s greatsword perfectly. My sword’s energy was feeble compared to his, but it moved strangely upon impact, shifting the greatsword’s path to the side. I was attempting to divert the force using the principles of the Soul-Luring Sword, the seventh technique of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.

CLANG!!!

The difference in power must have been too great. My sword shattered, barely diverting the greatsword’s path.

But the brief moment the Soul-Luring Sword created was enough. As I unleashed the technique, I was already pulling my body back. The aftershock still sent me flying backward, but I had avoided instant death.

I survived by using the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw, the Soul-Luring Sword, and movement arts all at once. I had no idea how I managed it. My body just seemed to move on its own in a moment of extreme crisis.

As I flew through the air, I saw Third Senior Brother instantly close in on Eldest Senior Brother and swing his sword at his back.

But Eldest Senior Brother twisted aside as if he had eyes in his back. He narrowly avoided Third Senior Brother’s surprise attack, an attack said to leave no shadow behind.

Just then, Jin Hayeon closed in from the side. She thrust her twin palms at Eldest Senior Brother's flank.

Her hands were enveloped in a pure white cold qi that exuded a murderous aura. She had clearly been holding back when she sparred with me.

But the chilling cold qi of her Pure Hands Demonic Art was no match.

FWOOSH.

It could not stand against Eldest Senior Brother's Explosive Mara Sword Technique, which was near the Transcendent Demon Realm.

The Burning Sun Finger Qi on Eldest Senior Brother’s greatsword instantly melted her cold qi.

BOOM!!!

The immense force sent Jin Hayeon flying far into the distance.

THUD!

"Keuk..."

My own flight ended as I crashed into a wall, a choked breath escaping my lips.

If I hadn't used the Soul-Luring Sword to divert some of that force, my entire spine would have shattered.

But this was no time to let pain overwhelm my senses.

"Oppa!!"

Seon'ah shrieked and charged at Eldest Senior Brother. Her eyes were redder than usual, enraged that I had been attacked.

It was madness. Jin Hayeon, who was much stronger, had been sent flying in a single blow. Fortunately, Seon'ah was not cut in two.

"Young Lady!"

The bodyguard martial artist from the Hyeokryeon Clan rushed to the crazed Seon'ah. He struck her acupoints and knocked her unconscious.

WHOOSH!

Eldest Senior Brother’s greatsword, which had been aimed at Seon'ah, now turned toward the bodyguard.

"Gack..."

The bodyguard also failed to withstand a single exchange and was sent flying backward. He held Seon'ah in his arms, fulfilling his duty, but the situation was spiraling toward disaster.

With just three swings, I, Jin Hayeon, the bodyguard, and Seon'ah were all down. Only Third Senior Brother remained standing.

"......"

Third Senior Brother shouted. Contrary to his gloomy demeanor, he stood alone to block Eldest Senior Brother.

He didn't look like he could last long. Third Senior Brother’s martial arts specialized in surprise attacks, not direct fights. There was also a difference in their skill. He was barely holding on, like a lantern in the wind.

If I ran to save myself, I might survive.

*Everyone but me will die.*

The crazed Rage Group Leader might take out his anger on everyone else here.

*But is there any way for me to stop that insane monster...*

!!!

A plan to save everyone came to mind. I ran toward the main gate of the Wind Cliff Pavilion as if I were fleeing for my life.

Just before leaving the Wind Cliff Pavilion, I shouted with all my might at the Rage Group Leader, who was tormenting Third Senior Brother.

"Hey, you ignorant fucking blockhead! Is that head of yours just for decoration!"

With a 'screw it all' attitude, I bolted from the Wind Cliff Pavilion with all my strength.

"I’ll kill you!!!"

BOOM!!!

A massive explosion erupted. The monstrous Rage Group Leader shot into the air and flew toward me.

"Shibaaaaaaal!!!"

I felt the immense qi rapidly closing in from behind. I sprinted, screaming curses with all my might.

SWOOOSH!!

A chilling sensation ran down my spine. I felt a piece of that immense qi flying at me from behind and quickly threw my body to the side.

BOOM!!!

A red energy blast from Eldest Senior Brother struck the spot where I had just been, causing an explosion.

I tumbled to the ground, scrambled back to my feet, and started running again.

"Where are you aiming, you stupid bastard!!"

"You vermin!! I’ll tear you limb from limb!!!"

The completely crazed madman’s shouts grew closer as he fired one red energy blast after another at me.

How long did I run while provoking the monstrous Rage Group Leader? With my life on the line, it felt like an eternity. In reality, barely ten seconds had passed.

Finally, the murderous Rage Group Leader caught me.

"......"

He swung his greatsword at me again.

CLANG!!

A spear, a saber, and a sword flew in from nowhere and blocked the madman's greatsword.

Their faces were unfamiliar. They were martial artists of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult from the area.

This was to be expected. We were in a vast training ground at the Main Headquarters of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, a place filled with martial artists. Someone falling into qi deviation or going rampant from mental illness was an annual event here. In this den of madmen, it was only natural for others to step in and suppress a rampage.

This was exactly why I provoked Eldest Senior Brother and led him here. If we had waited for help at the Wind Cliff Pavilion, someone would have died. Instead, I lured him to a place where other martial artists were likely to be.

As the three martial artists blocked Eldest Senior Brother’s greatsword, Third Senior Brother arrived.

"Just buy time until Master arrives!" he shouted as he joined them.

With three reinforcements, it seemed they could hold out for a while, unlike when he faced Eldest Senior Brother alone.

"......"

"Gack..."

Or so I thought. Another martial artist couldn't hold on and was sent flying through a wall.

I could see other demonic practitioners approaching from all directions, but at this rate, the people here were going to die.

"Why are you tormenting innocent people, you blockhead!"

I provoked Eldest Senior Brother again.

"You bastarrrrd!!!"

Eldest Senior Brother ignored the three martial artists surrounding him and charged toward me. Perhaps he had surrendered his body to rage, or perhaps he was just thoughtless.

"......"

Thanks to his obvious and simple movement, the three blocked Eldest Senior Brother's attack much more easily.

CLANG!!

The attack was blocked right in front of my nose.

"Heek."

Startled, I took a few steps back. I watched them suppress the rampaging flame boar.

"Looks like you’re not just stupid, you’re blind too?"

I provoked Eldest Senior Brother again whenever his opponents seemed to be losing ground.

"I’ll kill you! I’ll kill you!! I will kill youuu!!!"

I drew Eldest Senior Brother's anger and attention several more times. Before I knew it, dozens of martial artists had surrounded the boar with anger management issues.

I watched Eldest Senior Brother’s frantic rampage. He swung his greatsword and shot flames everywhere while surrounded, like a one-man fireworks show.

"Everyone, stand back."

A familiar voice reached my ears.

The master of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult had finally arrived. He was Master of both myself and the rampaging flame boar.

The Master’s words seemed to hold power. The moment he spoke, everyone surrounding Eldest Senior Brother instantly retreated.

Eldest Senior Brother, consumed by rage, swung his greatsword at me as soon as the others disappeared.

"......"

The Master easily blocked the blow with a sword he seemed to draw from nowhere.

WOOOONG.

An ink-black qi began to leak from Master’s body as his sword locked with Eldest Senior Brother's. It was not as violent as Eldest Senior Brother's qi, but it carried an immense pressure that weighed down on the surroundings.

Crushed by that heavy qi, Eldest Senior Brother could not withstand it and was pressed to the ground. The Master’s intervention ended the rampage of the man with anger management issues.

{sep}

Eldest Senior Brother’s rampage injured a total of nine people. Miraculously, there were no fatalities. Six were seriously injured, and three had minor injuries. Considering Eldest Senior Brother's level, they said this was practically no damage at all.

I was counted among the seriously injured, so I was resting in the clinic.

My whole body was bruised and my muscles were in shock from blocking Eldest Senior Brother’s attack and hitting the wall. I also had internal injuries.

I had already received treatment, so I didn't need to stay, but the Wind Cliff Pavilion was half-destroyed, so I was staying at the clinic during repairs.

Jin Hayeon was by my side, her face expressionless. Her arms were wrapped in bandages and supported by splints.

"Young Lord. It’s time to begin your studies."

They said her arms had suffered several hairline fractures because she couldn't withstand the force of her clash with Eldest Senior Brother.

She said it was a relief her bones weren't shattered, so they would heal quickly. She was definitely not in her right mind.

"Both I and Miss Jin are injured. What do you mean, study?" I asked, dumbfounded.

As expected, she answered bluntly.

"Since you can’t train in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword or external arts, we must make progress on the law books. That will let us accelerate our other training after you recover, to make up for lost time."

...

*Just as I thought. She’s not in her right mind.*

Still, I couldn't scold the woman who fractured her arms charging at that insane monster just to save me.

No, there was a more pressing problem.

*My life in the Demonic Cult is screwed. Hah.*

I had blurted out whatever came to mind because my life was in danger. Now I had made an enemy of the Heavenly Demon's head disciple, a man with immense backing.

I had relentlessly called him stupid, the one thing Eldest Senior Brother hated so much it triggered his anger management issues.

"Then, let's begin the lesson. Since my arms are like this, could you please open the book, Young Lord."

I was half-losing my mind, caught between Jin Hayeon's insane nonsense and my worries about the future. Just then, a commotion started outside the clinic.
