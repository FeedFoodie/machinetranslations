Naturally, the gazes of those watching me turned questioning.
Fortunately, I had already prepared a suitable excuse.
"You’ve probably heard that I worked as a waiter at an inn before I became Master’s disciple."
"You heard this when you were a waiter?"
"Yes. The inn owner Elder who hired and raised me was both the owner and the cook. He was deeply knowledgeable about various teas and foods, and he sometimes taught me about cooking."
What way did they have to know if I was telling the truth or a lie, when I claimed a dead man taught me?
Third Senior Brother seemed to misunderstand my words, because he muttered in admiration.
"Heo. He must have been a truly remarkable cook. To think he considered not just food combinations, but also how they matched a person’s constitution and disposition. To think they killed such a cook just to frame Master."
Third Senior Brother suddenly expressed a cold fury toward the Murim Alliance.
This was an unexpected development, so I spoke with a face full of righteous anger.
"Ahem… The innkeeper was a truly great man. I can never forgive those bastards from the Murim Alliance for assassinating him."
I truly did vow revenge, but I felt the conversation would stray if this topic continued, so I quickly brought it back.
"Anyway, the important point is that it might be possible to induce Senior Brother’s qi deviation with just food or tea. So, we need to find the late-night snack Senior Brother ate that day and the people who served it."
"Then I should capture those who prepared the snack and order them to remake what they gave me that day. It seems you know a thing or two about this, Junior Brother."
I flinched involuntarily.
*I… don’t really know anything.*
I had only seen Lee Jiyeon Senior Official avoid certain foods. I didn’t have a comprehensive knowledge of foods that were bad for depression.
Curiosity had led me to search for it online once, but I didn’t remember all the details.
My sympathy for Lee Jiyeon Senior Official was one thing, but I wasn’t the one with depression.
"Ahem. They might subtly change the ingredients, Senior Brother."
"Hahaha. Don’t worry. I may be ignorant about food, but thanks to my poison training, my memory for taste, smell, and texture is quite exceptional."
The paranoid Senior Brother spoke proudly, as if this was one of his few strengths.
Making another excuse here seemed likely to trigger his paranoia again, so I had no choice but to nod.
"…Then I’ll leave it to you for now, Senior Brother."
I prayed that whatever trick they used involved something I knew.
"I will keep this man’s acupoints sealed for now, just in case."
With those words, Senior Brother vanished into the darkness.
"If you are innocent, you will be released soon. Please be patient, martial artist Gwan."
"I understand, Young Lord."
Martial artist Gwan sat with only his mute acupoint released. I cautiously approached his side.
There was a small chance martial artist Gwan might attempt a sudden suicide.
I looked down at martial artist Gwan with a mixture of pity and suspicion.
"Young Lord!!"
Suddenly, martial artist Gwan shouted in a startled voice. Puzzled, I followed his gaze.
Swoooosh!!
What I saw was the tip of a spear flying toward me at incredible speed.
{sep}
The moment Third Young Master Seo Wanpyeong appeared carrying Gwan Muyeol, Jang Hoe sensed that something was wrong.
*Damn it. They suspected us.*
He had heard that the Third Young Master was away from the Main Headquarters on a separate mission from the Heavenly Demon, yet here he was, appearing out of nowhere.
Listening to the subsequent conversation between the Eighth Young Master, the Third Young Master, and Gwan Muyeol, his suspicion turned into certainty.
So, Jang Hoe subtly inserted himself into their conversation.
"The circumstantial evidence leaves no room for doubt, does it not?"
If Gwan Muyeol took the blame and died here, the suspicion on him would lessen. It would also have the effect of making the Eighth Young Master, who thought he had the upper hand, let his guard down.
However, the Eighth Young Master, who always acted slick and deceptive, was surprisingly not a pushover.
"It’s unlikely he targeted me on his own, right?"
The Eighth Young Master chose interrogation over execution. Jang Hoe had no choice but to chime in, trying to build the case against Gwan Muyeol.
"Young Lord, perhaps he’s trying a trick to commit suicide by taking poison."
But damn it, it didn’t work on the Eighth Young Master.
Pushing further might cast suspicion on himself, so Jang Hoe decided to just watch the situation unfold.
If only that one topic hadn’t come up.
"Among foods and teas, not just poisons, there are things that can make a person sensitive or depressed, Senior Brother."
The moment those words left the Eighth Young Master’s mouth, Jang Hoe had to use all his strength to maintain his composure.
*How does he know that!?*
If he had let his guard down for even a second, his shocked expression would have been exposed.
What they had used on the Third Young Master this time, and what they had used to kill the Fifth Young Lord by faking a qi deviation, was not ‘poison’ but food.
It was a special food still unknown to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. In fact, it was almost unknown not just to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, but to the entire Central Plains.
Therefore, Jang Hoe forced himself to keep a straight face while his mind raced.
*Should I just wait?*
The Eighth Young Master had hypothesized it might be food instead of poison, but there was no guarantee he knew about ‘that food’. The probability was also very low.
The only problem was…
*If the Eighth Young Master recognizes it, there’s a risk that the Fifth Young Lord’s murder will also be revealed.*
If that happened, not just a low-ranking member like himself, but everyone up to the Guardian Hall Director could lose their lives.
After much deliberation, Jang Hoe made his decision.
*It’s certain now. That bastard is an obstacle to our grand plan, and now is the chance to kill him.*
The Third Young Master had left to capture the others, and Gwan Muyeol’s paralysis acupoints were sealed.
He just had to kill the bastard with a surprise attack and then deal with Jin Hayeon.
Of course, since it was an open assassination attempt, the aftermath of killing the Eighth Young Master would be significant.
*But it’s safer for me to take the fall alone than for that thing to be discovered by the Eighth Young Master.*
He judged that the latter had a much higher chance of taking down the higher-ups as well.
The deliberation was long, but the action was swift.
Pat!
Jang Hoe moved without warning, instantly closing the distance to the Eighth Young Master.
"Young Lord!"
Swoooosh!
By the time Gwan Muyeol realized what was happening and yelled, Jang Hoe’s spear was already hurtling toward Ilmok.
"!?"
Ilmok’s eyes, filled with shock and bewilderment, watched the spear.
Just before Jang Hoe’s spear could pierce Ilmok’s face.
Clang!!!
With a jarring screech, the spear’s trajectory shifted slightly.
Seogeok!
Thanks to that, the spearhead that should have pierced Ilmok’s face only grazed the skin on his cheekbone, leaving a shallow cut.
"Damn it."
Jang Hoe cursed and looked at the one who had interfered at the last moment.
The owner of the palm, imbued with a blueish chill, that had struck his spear shaft. Jin Hayeon.
{sep}
*I almost died.*
My heart pounded as if it would explode.
I had instinctively taken a step back at the last second, and Jin Hayeon had reacted dramatically to alter the spear’s path. Both things happened at once, allowing me to narrowly avoid a hole in my head.
*How did she know?*
I had no idea how Jin Hayeon reacted to Jang Hoe’s surprise attack. Perhaps it was possible because she had, as she vowed, never let her guard down.
But that wasn’t what was important right now.
"Get out of the way!"
"......"
Jin Hayeon had miraculously reacted and saved me, but the problem was the difference in their skill levels.
She fought with a cool expression, not even letting out a groan, let alone a kiai, but it was clear she was being pushed back with every clash.
Jin Hayeon was still young, so it was too much for her to block the seasoned Jang Hoe on her own.
*His target is me anyway. I should run and draw his attention like with Eldest Senior Brother last time.*
Just as I thought that and prepared to move.
"Release my acupoints, Young Lord!"
At the shout from Gwan Muyeol beside me, I instinctively reached out to the martial artist and yelled.
"Where and how do I release them!?"
In response to my question, Gwan Muyeol named several acupoints. With a what-the-hell attitude, I poured my ki into my fingers and poked each acupoint in succession.
"Keuk…"
It seemed my strength control was off, as martial artist Gwan let out a pained groan, but regardless, Gwan Muyeol’s paralysis acupoints were released, and he quickly stood up and drew his saber.
"......"
Just as an overwhelmed Jin Hayeon was pushed back about three meters while blocking Jang Hoe’s spear.
"Jang Hoe, you bastard!!"
Gwan Muyeol took Jin Hayeon’s place, rushing at Jang Hoe and swinging his saber.
{sep}
Clang! Clang!
Jang Hoe, using a spear with a much longer reach, blocked Gwan Muyeol’s advance with concise movements. Gwan Muyeol also used minimal movements to block or evade the spear, trying to close the distance.
However.
"Keuk."
The closer he tried to get, the more wounds accumulated on Gwan Muyeol’s body.
"You bastard. You were hiding your true skill!"
Gwan Muyeol shouted with a face full of rage, but Jang Hoe’s expression wasn’t very bright either.
It was getting harder and harder to kill Ilmok.
Even worse.
"I’m joining in."
Jin Hayeon, with blood trickling from the corner of her mouth as if she had suffered internal injuries, stepped in to help Gwan Muyeol.
As Gwan Muyeol and Jin Hayeon pressed him from the front and back, the movements of Jang Hoe’s spear grew increasingly unstable.
"......"
Gwan Muyeol, who had been calmly parrying or blocking Jang Hoe’s spear, seemed to spot an opportunity. He put immense force into a strike against Jang Hoe’s spear.
This was a characteristic of the Heaven-Shattering Soulless Blade, the saber art Gwan Muyeol had learned. It was a saber art that waited for an opportunity with minimal movement, then went for a single, decisive strike. Perhaps the side effects similar to Ilmok’s had arisen from such a martial art.
Gwan Muyeol’s surprise all-or-nothing move overpowered Jang Hoe, sending his spear flying high into the air.
Pat!
Jin Hayeon seized the opportunity, closing in and thrusting her left palm toward Jang Hoe’s heart.
But even with his arm raised, Jang Hoe twisted his upper body violently, narrowly dodging the blow.
The crisis, however, was not over.
Pat!
While Jin Hayeon drew Jang Hoe’s attention, Gwan Muyeol had also closed the distance and swung his saber in a wide arc from left to right, as if to slice Jang Hoe’s body in two.
In addition, Jin Hayeon, whose left palm had missed, now thrust her right palm, aiming for Jang Hoe’s legs.
Seogeok.
Peok!
The moment Gwan Muyeol’s saber cut Jang Hoe’s side and Jin Hayeon’s right palm froze his thigh.
Gwan Muyeol’s face showed an expression of being tricked.
While being hit by their attacks, Jang Hoe had thrown his spear at Ilmok.
It wasn’t a desperate, improvised throw.
The spear Jang Hoe threw literally tore through the air, flying toward Ilmok at an incredible speed, imbued with a sharp spear qi.
Twisting his body wasn’t to dodge Jin Hayeon’s attack in the first place. The same went for his spear being knocked away by Gwan Muyeol.
From the moment the fight dragged on, Jang Hoe had been planning to take Ilmok down with him.
Jin Hayeon, who always wore a cool expression, looked at Ilmok with a face mixed with despair.
"!?"
Then, a rare look of astonishment appeared on her face.
For some reason, despite the spear flying through space toward him, Ilmok’s face was perfectly calm.
It wasn’t that Ilmok had expected this to happen.
He had simply been immersed in martial arts from a certain point. To be precise, from the moment he was first ambushed by Jang Hoe.
His body, having faced a life-threatening crisis, had summoned an explosive level of concentration for the sake of survival.
Even as his heart pounded wildly, the world seemed to flow in slow motion.
The fight between the far superior martial artists Jang Hoe, Gwan Muyeol, and Jin Hayeon was crystal clear in Ilmok’s eyes.
He could see every flow of the martial arts they displayed, and enlightenment washed over him like a flood.
And at this very moment.
The existence of the ferocious spear flying to take his life became the detonator that would set off the enlightenment that had not just washed over him, but had reached a saturation point.
The inner arts, which had grown significantly after consuming the Explosive Demonic Pill, instantly emptied from his qi center.
It was a flow of immense inner arts that Ilmok had never handled directly before, but his acupoints, widened and cleansed by the Heavenly Demon meridian cleansing and purification, withstood the massive flow.
The inner arts, which raced through Ilmok’s entire body at incredible speed according to the principles of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, amplified repeatedly and gathered in the palm of Ilmok’s hand holding the sword.
Pat!!
The sword, drawn according to the principles of the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw, the first form of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword that he had practiced countless times—a sword-drawing technique that was both offense and defense—shimmered with a grayish aura.
It had only been half a year since he started learning the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.
That was the time it took for Ilmok to create sword qi.
