Naturally, everyone who looked at me grew doubtful. Fortunately, I had already thought of a suitable excuse.

"I'm sure you've heard that I worked as a waiter at an inn before I became Master's disciple."

"You mean you heard about this while working as a waiter?"

"Yes. The inn owner Elder who hired and raised me was both an innkeeper and a chef. He was deeply knowledgeable about various teas and foods, and he sometimes taught me about cooking."

How could they know if I was telling the truth or a lie when I claimed a dead man taught me?

Third Senior Brother seemed to misunderstand me, because he muttered in admiration.

"Heh. He must have been a truly remarkable chef. To think he considered not just food combinations, but also how they matched a person's constitution and disposition. To think they killed such a chef just to frame Master."

Third Senior Brother suddenly expressed a cold fury toward the Murim Alliance.

This was an unexpected turn. I put on a face filled with righteous anger.

"Ahem… The innkeeper in Chengdu was a truly great man. I can never forgive the bastards from the Murim Alliance who assassinated him."

I truly did vow revenge, but I felt the conversation would stray if this continued. I quickly brought the topic back.

"In any case, the important thing is that it might be possible to induce your qi deviation with just food or tea. So, we need to find the late-night snack you ate that day and the people who served it."

"Then I should just capture those who prepared the snack and order them to make it again. It seems you know a fair bit about this, Junior Brother."

I flinched without realizing it.

*I… don't know that much.*

I had only seen Lee Jiyeon Senior Official avoid certain foods. I didn't know every single food that was bad for depression. I had searched it online once out of curiosity, but I didn't remember all the details. Despite my sympathy for Lee Jiyeon Senior Official, I wasn't the one with depression.

"Ahem. They might subtly change the ingredients, Senior Brother."

"Hahaha. Don't worry. I may be ignorant about food, but thanks to my poison training, my memory for taste, smell, and texture is quite excellent."

The paranoid Senior Brother spoke proudly, as if it were one of his few strengths. I felt his paranoia would flare up again if I made another excuse, so I had no choice but to nod.

"…Then I'll leave it to you, Senior Brother."

I prayed they had used something I might recognize.

"I'll keep this man's acupoints sealed for now, just in case."

Senior Brother said this and then vanished into the darkness in an instant.

"If you're innocent, you'll be released soon. Please be patient, Martial Artist Gwan."

"I understand, Young Lord."

Martial artist Gwan sat with only his mute acupoint released. I cautiously approached him. There was a small chance Martial Artist Gwan might try to commit suicide. I looked down at him with a mix of pity and suspicion.

"Young Lord!!"

Suddenly, Martial Artist Gwan shouted in a startled voice. Confused, I followed his gaze.

*WHOOSH!!*

My eyes met the sight of a spearhead flying toward me at incredible speed.

{sep}

The moment Third Young Master Seo Wanpyeong appeared carrying Gwan Muyeol, Jang Hoe knew something was wrong.

*Damn it. They were suspecting us.*

He had heard that the Third Young Master was away from the Main Headquarters on a special mission from the Heavenly Demon, but here he was, appearing out of nowhere. The subsequent conversation between the Eighth Young Master, the Third Young Master, and Gwan Muyeol turned his suspicion into certainty.

So, Jang Hoe subtly inserted himself into their conversation.

"The circumstances leave no room for doubt, do they?"

If Gwan Muyeol took the blame and died here, the suspicion on Jang Hoe would lessen. It would also make the Eighth Young Master, who thought he had the upper hand, let his guard down.

However, the Eighth Young Master, who always acted like a slick con artist, was surprisingly not so easy.

"It's unlikely he targeted me on his own, right?"

The Eighth Young Master chose interrogation over execution. Jang Hoe had no choice but to chime in, trying to build the case against Gwan Muyeol.

"Young Lord, perhaps he's trying to kill himself by taking poison."

But damn it, it didn't work on the Eighth Young Master. Pushing further might cast suspicion on himself, so Jang Hoe decided to just watch the situation unfold.

If only that one topic hadn't come up.

"There are foods and teas, not just poisons, that can make a person sensitive or depressed, Senior Brother."

The moment those words left the Eighth Young Master's mouth, Jang Hoe had to use all his strength to keep his expression neutral.

*How does he know that!?*

If he had let his guard down for even a second, his shocked expression would have been exposed. What they had used on the Third Young Master, and what they had used to kill Young Lord Oh by faking a qi deviation, was food, not poison. It was a special food unknown to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. In fact, it was almost unknown throughout the Central Plains.

Jang Hoe forced himself to maintain a calm face while his mind raced.

*Should I just wait?*

The Eighth Young Master had hypothesized it might be food instead of poison, but there was no guarantee he knew about 'that food'. The probability was very low. The only problem was…

*If the Eighth Young Master finds out, there's a risk that Young Lord Oh's murder will also be exposed.*

If that happened, not just a low-level subordinate like himself, but even the Guardian Hall Director could lose their lives.

After much deliberation, Jang Hoe made his decision.

*It's certain now. That bastard is an obstacle to our grand plan, and now is the perfect chance to kill him.*

The Third Young Master was gone to capture the others, and Gwan Muyeol's paralysis acupoints were sealed. All he had to do was kill the bastard with a surprise attack and then deal with Jin Hayeon. Of course, since it was an open assassination attempt, there would be significant fallout even after killing the Eighth Young Master.

*But it's safer for me to take the fall alone than for that thing to be discovered by the Eighth Young Master.*

He judged that the chance of his superiors being wiped out was much lower this way.

His deliberation was long, but his action was swift.

"......"

Jang Hoe moved without warning, instantly closing the distance to the Eighth Young Master.

"Young Lord!"

*WHOOSH!*

By the time Gwan Muyeol realized what was happening and yelled, Jang Hoe's spear was already lunging toward Ilmok.

"!?"

Ilmok's eyes, filled with shock and bewilderment, watched the spear.

Just before Jang Hoe's spear pierced Ilmok's face.

*CHANG!!!*

A harsh scrape echoed as the spear's path twisted slightly.

*SCRAPE!*

Because of that, the spearhead that should have pierced Ilmok's face only grazed his cheekbone, leaving a shallow cut.

"Damn it."

Jang Hoe cursed and looked at the one who had interfered at the last moment. It was Jin Hayeon, the owner of the palm, shrouded in blue frost, that had struck his spear shaft.

{sep}

*I almost died.*

My heart pounded like it would explode. I had instinctively taken a step back at the last second. Jin Hayeon had reacted dramatically and diverted the spear's path. Both things happening together had allowed me to narrowly avoid getting a hole in my head.

*How did she know?*

I didn't know how Jin Hayeon had reacted to Jang Hoe's ambush. Maybe it was possible because, as she had sworn, she never let her guard down, but that wasn't what was important right now.

"Get out of my way!"

*KWANG!*

Jin Hayeon had miraculously reacted and saved me, but the problem was the difference in their skill. She fought with a cool expression, not even letting out a grunt of pain, but I could clearly see her being pushed back with every clash. Jin Hayeon was still young. It was too much for her to block the seasoned Jang Hoe alone.

*His target is me anyway. I should run and draw his attention like with Eldest Senior Brother last time.*

Just as I thought that and prepared to move.

"Release my acupoints, Young Lord!"

At Gwan Muyeol's shout, I instinctively reached out to the martial artist.

"Where and how do I release them!?"

Gwan Muyeol called out several acupoints in response to my question. I thought, *what the hell*, and poured a generous amount of qi into my fingers, striking each acupoint in succession.

"Keuk…"

I must have used too much force, as Martial Artist Gwan let out a pained groan, but his paralysis acupoints were released. Gwan Muyeol quickly stood up and drew his saber.

*KWANG!*

Just as Jin Hayeon, overwhelmed, was pushed back about three meters while blocking Jang Hoe's spear.

"Jang Hoe, you bastard!!"

Gwan Muyeol took Jin Hayeon's place, rushing at Jang Hoe and swinging his saber.

{sep}

*CHANG! CHANG!*

Jang Hoe, using a spear with a much longer reach, blocked Gwan Muyeol's approach with concise movements. Gwan Muyeol also used minimal movements to block or evade the spear while trying to get closer.

But.

"Keuk."

The closer he tried to get, the more wounds accumulated on Gwan Muyeol's body.

"You bastard. You were hiding your true skill!"

Gwan Muyeol shouted with a face full of rage, but Jang Hoe's expression wasn't bright either. It was getting harder and harder to kill Ilmok.

And what's more.

"I'm joining in."

Jin Hayeon, with blood trickling from her lips as if she had an internal injury, stepped up to help Gwan Muyeol. Jang Hoe's spear movements grew increasingly unstable as Gwan Muyeol and Jin Hayeon pressed him from the front and back.

*CHANG!*

Gwan Muyeol, who had been calmly parrying or blocking Jang Hoe's spear, seemed to spot an opportunity. He struck Jang Hoe's spear with immense force. It was a characteristic of the Heaven-Shattering Soulless Blade, the saber art Gwan Muyeol had learned. It was a style that waited for an opportunity with minimal movement, then went for a single, decisive strike. Perhaps that martial art had created a side effect similar to Ilmok's.

Gwan Muyeol's surprise all-or-nothing attack sent Jang Hoe's spear flying upward, overpowered.

"......"

Jin Hayeon seized the chance, lunging forward and thrusting her left palm toward Jang Hoe's heart, but Jang Hoe, even with his arm raised, twisted his upper body violently and narrowly dodged the blow.

However, the crisis was not over.

"......"

While Jin Hayeon drew Jang Hoe's attention, Gwan Muyeol had also closed the distance. He swung his saber in a wide arc from left to right, as if to slice Jang Hoe in two. In addition, Jin Hayeon, whose left palm had missed, now thrust her right palm toward Jang Hoe's leg.

*SCRAPE.*

*PEOK!*

Gwan Muyeol's saber cut Jang Hoe's side, and Jin Hayeon's right palm froze his thigh. At that moment, Gwan Muyeol's face showed an expression of being tricked.

While being hit by their attacks, Jang Hoe had thrown his spear at Ilmok. It wasn't a desperate, improvised throw. The spear Jang Hoe threw literally tore through the air, flying toward Ilmok at incredible speed, imbued with sharp spear qi.

He hadn't twisted his body to dodge Jin Hayeon's attack. His spear being knocked away by Gwan Muyeol was part of the plan too. From the moment the fight dragged on, Jang Hoe had planned to take Ilmok down with him.

Jin Hayeon, who always wore a cool expression, looked at Ilmok with a face mixed with despair.

"!?"

Then, a rare look of astonishment appeared on her face. For some reason, even as the spear flew through space toward him, Ilmok's face was perfectly calm.

Ilmok hadn't expected this to happen. He had simply been immersed in martial arts from a certain point. To be precise, from the moment Jang Hoe first ambushed him. His body, facing a threat to its life, had summoned explosive concentration for survival.

Even as his heart pounded wildly, the world seemed to move in slow motion. The fight between Jang Hoe, Gwan Muyeol, and Jin Hayeon, all far superior martial artists, was crystal clear in Ilmok's eyes. He could see every flow of their martial arts, and enlightenment washed over him like a flood.

And at this very moment.

The ferocious spear flying to take his life became the detonator that would ignite the enlightenment that had reached a saturation point. The inner arts, which had grown significantly after consuming the Explosive Demonic Pill, instantly rushed out of his qi center.

It was a flow of immense inner arts that Ilmok had never handled himself, but his acupoints, widened and cleansed by the Heavenly Demon meridian cleansing and purification, withstood the massive flow. The inner arts raced through Ilmok's entire body at incredible speed according to the principles of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, amplifying again and again, and gathered in the palm of Ilmok's sword-wielding hand.

*PAT!!*

The sword was drawn according to the principles of the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw, the first move of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, a sword-drawing technique that combined offense and defense, which he had practiced countless times. A gray energy shimmered on the blade.

It had only been half a year since he started learning the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.

That was how long it took for Ilmok to create sword qi.
