Jin Hayeon.

She was the eldest daughter of an ordinary family. Of course, "ordinary" was by the Demonic Cult's standards. Her parents, who had little talent for martial arts, were common martial artists from the Demonic Cult. They rarely visited the Main Headquarters and served as low-ranking martial artists at a Branch in a quiet village.

If anything about them was unusual, it was that her parents were quite good-looking, despite their average martial arts and insight. In a way, it was a miracle that Jin Hayeon was born to such parents.

Setting aside her captivating looks, her talent for martial arts and her insight were both exceptional. In her birth village, people called her a "genius," and all the villagers congratulated her parents. She was her parents' pride, the pride of their Branch, and a girl cherished by everyone in the village.

Fortunately, while her parents were ordinary individuals, they were excellent parents. That is, they were excellent by the standards of the Demonic Cult. From a young age, Jin Hayeon's parents disciplined their child according to the cult's doctrines.

She could have grown arrogant from too much love, but a child raised on the doctrine became a devout believer.

*When I grow up, I'll become a great person who contributes to the Divine Cult!*

As a child, she studied the Demonic Cult's scriptures with her parents daily. This was what she always said before falling asleep.

"Hohoho. I believe Hayeon can definitely do it."

"Of course! She's smart and takes such good care of her younger siblings. Where else would we find a child like our Hayeon!"

As the eldest daughter, Jin Hayeon often looked after her younger siblings when her parents handled Branch affairs. When her siblings napped, she did chores like cleaning or followed her parents to help struggling people in the village. After her parents finished their work, they taught her demonic art.

The demonic art her parents knew was not very powerful, as they were ordinary, low-ranking martial artists. Thanks to this, young Jin Hayeon never suffered from serious side effects.

By the time she turned fifteen, she had already surpassed her parents' achievements. At that young age, she told her parents her wish.

"I want to enter the Demonic Academy!"

Praised as a genius by everyone in her village, she was confident. However, she was not arrogant.

"After I complete my studies at the Demonic Academy, I want to help people who are struggling!"

Her words omitted the phrase "by killing the bad guys with demonic art," but her parents understood her meaning. This was a natural sentiment in the Demonic Cult.

The strong use force to punish the wicked who torment the weak. The Demonic Cult revered martial prowess for this very reason, even accepting the risk of insanity. They considered this a noble mindset. Her parents, both martial artists of the Demonic Cult, accepted her wish.

At sixteen, Jin Hayeon took the Demonic Academy's entrance exam and passed confidently. She scored nineteenth out of the twenty who passed.

It was the first shock of her life. Jin Hayeon, praised as a genius in her village, was now at the bottom of the ranks at the Demonic Academy. That day, she realized how small her world had been, but she did not give up.

*Right. Everyone but me is from the Main Headquarters! They just grew quickly because they learned good demonic art from the start!*

The Demonic Academy trained the future leaders of the Demonic Cult. It made all demonic arts available, except for the Heavenly Demon Divine Art and the ultimate techniques of the five great families. She believed she could catch up if she just learned a top-tier demonic art. The only problem was which one to choose.

While she pondered this, a young man her age named Guyang Mun approached her. Just as Guyang Mun had fallen for her looks on the first day, Jin Hayeon was also aware of Guyang Mun. He was the boy who had passed the entrance exam as the valedictorian.

Her impression of Guyang Mun was simple.

*A guy born into a prominent family who succeeded easily.*

She did not like him. In her village, many people struggled to live. The Branch's martial artists helped them, and she had followed her parents to help the village elders, but while she spent her time on that, Guyang Mun must have lived comfortably, only training and studying.

*According to the doctrine, I'm the one who acted righteously, so why…*

Whether he knew what Jin Hayeon was thinking or not, Guyang Mun sidled up to her while she trained alone.

"Ahem. Should I help you?" he asked casually.

"It's fine. I can do it alone."

Despite her sharp words, Guyang Mun kept finding excuses to talk to her. She focused on her education and agonized over which demonic art to learn.

"Ahem. You just do it like this. Easy, right? You try it."

Guyang Mun would always approach her and give advice as if it were nothing.

She grew angry. She was struggling with the Demonic Academy's classes, yet Guyang Mun breezed through them. As a devout believer, she studied the scriptures whenever her heart wavered, believing the truth was within them. The scriptures did not betray her belief.

*Truly, all humans are evil, and I was no different…*

She belatedly realized what she was feeling. It was envy and jealousy, emotions she had never felt in her village. She felt disgusted with herself. As expected, the answer was in the scriptures. All humans were inherently evil, and the one who judged and watched over that evil was the Heavenly Demon.

*Oh, Heavenly Demon. Please save me.*

While confessing her wicked heart to the Heavenly Demon, she had an epiphany.

*Yes. Since the human heart is ugly anyway, I'll just discard it.*

This was why she chose the Pure Hands Demonic Art. There was a risk of losing feelings like sympathy and compassion, but she did not care.

*Even without emotions, if I act according to the doctrine, that is the path to saving people.*

She decided to entrust judgment entirely to the doctrine and eliminate her emotions, rather than judge with a human's selfish, malicious heart. After she began learning the Pure Hands Demonic Art, her skills grew rapidly.

From nineteenth out of twenty, she moved from the lower class to the upper class soon after. Just as she thought, the other students had only grown quickly because they were from the Main Headquarters. She proved she had no reason to fall behind them.

Except for one person.

No matter how hard she tried, she could not become the valedictorian. That title always belonged to Guyang Mun.

"Ahem. If I don't work hard, you might catch up to me."

"At this rate, you can do it too, right?"

Guyang Mun acted like a friendly rival, offering bits of advice. Her Pure Hands Demonic Art had not yet matured, and she found his behavior incredibly annoying.

*I'm doing my best, but that guy is so relaxed. He's even looking out for me and is still better than me.*

But her grades were not what angered her most.

*Why do you keep helping me! Why!*

She wanted to become a great person who contributed to the Divine Cult. She had entered the Demonic Academy to help others, but Guyang Mun kept treating her like a helpless child.

And so, Jin Hayeon had to endure Guyang Mun throughout her time at the Demonic Academy. She trained in the Pure Hands Demonic Art while fighting the anger and jealousy his attitude provoked. She wanted to surpass Guyang Mun in skill and change his condescending manner, to prove she was a complete martial artist in her own right.

But in the end, she never once took the valedictorian title from Guyang Mun.

Therefore, after completing her studies, she went to the Bodyguard Hall. She wanted to be someone who helps others, not someone who receives help, just as she had aimed for since childhood. After two years of frustration, she also realized she lacked the talent to reach the highest echelons.

So, since she was going to help others anyway, she decided to serve and help the Heavenly Demon, rather than ordinary commoners.

When Jin Hayeon finished her explanation, Ilmok was momentarily speechless.

Jin Hayeon had not told him every single detail. She did not speak of her childhood or the jealousy she felt toward Guyang Mun. She only explained briefly why she disliked him.

"At first, he seemed to have achieved success easily because he was born into a prominent family, and I didn't like that."

"I hated his attitude of always trying to help me. It felt like he was treating me as an inferior."

However, Ilmok, with the mental age of a man in his mid-thirties, could grasp the general situation.

*Yangmun… I think you're screwed.*

Ilmok had noticed it before. Jin Hayeon was a woman with a strong sense of independence who preferred giving help to receiving it. In modern terms, it was like a rich man's son casually tossing expensive gifts at a woman struggling to succeed on her own. From Jin Hayeon's perspective, his attitude must have felt like mockery.

*If their circumstances had been similar, it wouldn't have been like this. Anyway, to think that guy was a direct descendant of the Guyang Clan.*

This was the part that surprised Ilmok most.

The Guyang Clan. Like the Hyeokryeon Clan, it was one of the five great demonic families known as the five pillars of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. Having spent over half a year in the Demonic Cult, Ilmok already knew of their existence.

It was just that he never expected it.

*Whenever he got involved with Jin Hayeon, he only did stupid things, so I just assumed he was from a branch family.*

Ilmok wondered if the Guyang Clan, one of the five great families, would let such disgraceful behavior slide. Well, according to Jin Hayeon, Guyang Mun was a direct descendant but not the eldest son, so he lived quite freely. He was supposedly the current Patriarch's Youngest son.

It was the story of a chaebol's youngest son falling for a beautiful, hardworking commoner, hitting on her, and getting brutally rejected.

*This is like a morning drama.*

From a third-person perspective, it was quite an amusing spectacle.

That night, Ilmok shared a room with Guyang Mun again. After a moment of hesitation, Ilmok finally spoke.

"Master Guyang."

"Yes, Young Lord!"

"Actually, I had a brief conversation with Miss Jin this morning."

"...What kind of conversation?"

"A conversation about you, Master Guyang."

"!! D-don't tell me you told Hayeon about my feelings!?"

As Guyang Mun was about to have a fit, Ilmok quickly added more.

"I just asked why she dislikes you so much, so don't worry!"

"...Hayeon dislikes me?"

Guyang Mun's face turned pale, as if he had suffered a severe internal injury.

"...Hasn't she always been particularly cold to you, Master Guyang?"

"I just thought she was being shy..."

"......"

Ilmok was at a loss for words. He sighed and then spoke.

"It seems there's a bit of a misunderstanding between you two, so I'm taking the liberty of telling you."

Ilmok relayed what he had heard from Jin Hayeon, editing it carefully so Guyang Mun would not suffer another internal injury.

"Don't try to help Miss Jin so much. She takes pride in pulling her own weight. The more you help, the more you might just hurt her feelings. This is especially true because you, Master Guyang, are a direct descendant of the Guyang Clan."

"...Then how am I supposed to convey my feelings to her without helping?"

*No, I mean, don't convey them.*

The words rose to Ilmok's throat, but he barely managed to hold them back.

"Don't help her. Just watch over her and acknowledge her. As a fellow martial artist."

Ilmok's advice continued, and Guyang Mun listened with a serious expression. Just as Ilmok sighed in relief, thinking Guyang Mun was finally listening, something happened.

*Thump.*

Having heard all of Ilmok's advice, Guyang Mun abruptly stood up and left the room.

"...Master Guyang?"

Feeling an inexplicable dread, Ilmok followed Guyang Mun. He stopped in his tracks and rubbed his face in exasperation.

"Hayeon!!"

The crazy bastard had run to Jin Hayeon's quarters in the middle of the night and was shouting her name.

"......"

"Do you have something to say?"

Guyang Mun shouted at Jin Hayeon, who opened the door with a cold expression.

"I heard from Young Lord Ilmok. About why you dislike me."

Ilmok, still rubbing his face, muttered to himself.

"Please. Oh, God."

He wanted to knock Guyang Mun out and drag him away, but he could only bow his head when Jin Hayeon's icy gaze turned toward him.

Regardless, Guyang Mun shouted with an uncharacteristically serious face.

"It was all your misunderstanding. I didn't try to help you because I thought you were pitiful or inferior to me!"

"...Then why did you do it?"

Looking at her face, which was even colder than usual, Guyang Mun declared confidently.

"Because I love you!"

Ilmok, who had been bowing, looked up with wide eyes. The word "crazy" escaped his lips. Sure, if you love someone, you can confess, but now was not the time.

Whether he knew what Ilmok was thinking or not, Guyang Mun's confession assault continued. He explained that he had fallen for her at first sight and had always approached her to help. He rambled on with his love story to clear up her misunderstanding.

For some reason, as his story went on, her perpetually pale face began to turn red.

"So, you've been doing all that because you like me?"

At her question, Guyang Mun thought his confession was working and delivered his final blow.

"Yes. It was all a misunderstanding! I can do anything for you. If my family offends you, I'm willing to leave my family for you!"

As soon as he finished speaking.

"Cough."

Jin Hayeon coughed up blood.

Her face had turned red not from embarrassment or love. It was a precursor to qi deviation.
