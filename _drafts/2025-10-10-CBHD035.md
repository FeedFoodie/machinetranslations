After the man in the darkness left, the Guardian Hall Director was lost in thought.

*Skilled at instigation...*

Some might take it negatively, but instigation was an important skill for leading a group. It was a very important ability, especially for someone who had to lead a religious group like the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

Of course, the bodyguard martial artist had added that he was like a con artist. The Guardian Hall Director did not think so.

*If he were truly a con artist, there's no way the Heavenly Demon would be fooled.*

The current Heavenly Demon was not naive enough to be deceived by a con artist. If he were such a man, he would not have ascended to his position, and they would have no reason to move so cautiously. The current Heavenly Demon's abilities were beyond doubt, and the faith in him within the Divine Cult was absolute.

That was why they were lying low. They were waiting for the time after the current Heavenly Demon's Ascension.

In that case, there was only one conclusion.

*His con artist-like appearance must be an act to deceive outsiders.*

Goosebumps rose on the Guardian Hall Director's arms as his thoughts reached that point. To have such a calculating mind at only fifteen.

*He is someone who must be dealt with.*

From Ilmok's perspective, it was an incredibly unfair misunderstanding.

The day after I returned to the Main Headquarters, my life returned to normal. Or rather, I wished it would.

Gwan Muyeol and Jang Hoe visited the Wind Cliff Pavilion early in the morning.

"Why have you come again?" I asked.

The two men answered in turn.

"It's because of a strict order from the Supreme Lord and the Guardian Hall Director," Gwan Muyeol said.

"Just in case, from now on, we will be serving as the Young Lord's dedicated bodyguards," Jang Hoe added.

Their answer reminded me of being chased by a rampaging Eldest Senior Brother. Indeed, it seemed bodyguards were necessary to survive in the Demonic Cult. However, I had a bad memory of these guys.

"Don't tell me you're planning to train me like last time? As for training, I'm already receiving plenty from Miss Jin and Master."

"That time, we only did so 'unavoidably' because of the Supreme Lord's orders," Jang Hoe replied, emphasizing the word 'unavoidably'.

Gwan Muyeol wore an expression as if it was not even worth answering. "We will simply stay by the Young Lord's side and protect you. We won't interfere in your affairs. That is, unless there's an order from the Supreme Lord."

"Hmm. I'll trust you for now."

I ignored the two bodyguard martial artists who took their positions in a corner of the Wind Cliff Pavilion. The morning lesson with Jin Hayeon began.

Around the time I finished lunch and my external arts training, Gwan Muyeol's voice sounded from the entrance, followed by a cheerful voice I had not heard in a while.

"Young Lord. A visitor has arrived."

"Oppa!!"

I ordered the door to be opened. As soon as Gwan Muyeol opened it, Seon'ah ran in and jumped into my arms.

*Is this what it feels like when your daughter runs out to greet you after a business trip?*

A comfortable smile naturally formed on my lips. "Hahaha. Did you miss me that much?"

For me, this moment was like welcome rain in a drought. I was tormented by demonic art and heretical doctrines, surrounded by people watching me and others itching to put me through the wringer. Time spent with a cute kid like this, who was happy just because I was there, was precious.

"So. Did you train hard while I was gone?"

At my question, Seon'ah looked up at me with her bright, clear eyes. She began to boast about what had happened over the past five days.

"Yes! I can even pierce rocks with the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique now! My aunt said that at this level, I can kill someone by stabbing them straight in the heart without having to avoid the ribs!"

"I-Is that so?"

I was not sure why her boasts were so bloodthirsty. Anyway, I spent a short while listening to Seon'ah's boasting.

"Young Lord. The First Young Lord has arrived."

"Hahaha. Little Brother! Are you in there?"

This time, Eldest Senior Brother came to visit the Wind Cliff Pavilion. I patted Seon'ah's head as her lips were about to pout, then greeted Eldest Senior Brother. I did not like having my break interrupted either, but it could not be helped.

"Greetings, Eldest Senior Brother, but what brings you here?" I asked, glancing at Hang Geon who stood beside Eldest Senior Brother. I wondered why that guy was with him.

"Hahaha. Master has entrusted me with the school matter that you proposed, Little Brother. He said that now that I've reached a certain level, I need to learn how to govern the cult in addition to my duties in the martial corps."

"Congratulations, Eldest Senior Brother. It seems Master expects that you are capable of becoming the Heavenly Demon. Hahaha."

"Uahahaha! Isn't this all thanks to you, Little Brother!" Whether my flattery worked or not, Eldest Senior Brother burst into hearty laughter.

"Anyway, I came because of that. Master told me to coordinate with this friend here and our Youngest Brother. We need to organize the basic martial arts and external arts to be taught to the children, and then compile a curriculum to be sent to each Branch."

"That is correct, Liege," Hang Geon said.

"fufufu. I heard from this fellow. He said he decided to serve you as his Liege, Little Brother? Since I will be your Liege, it seems this fellow will also become my loyal subordinate. Hahaha!"

Watching Hang Geon, whose attitude had changed to serve me politely, and Eldest Senior Brother, who rambled on nonstop, I felt my head start to spin.

However, the visitors did not end with those two.

"Young Lord. The Third Young Master has arrived."

Gwan Muyeol's voice called out in the same emotionless tone, as if he were a human doorbell. Soon after, Third Senior Brother entered the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Not expecting Eldest Senior Brother to be there, Third Senior Brother hurriedly offered him a clasped hands greeting.

"Greetings, Eldest Senior Brother."

"Hahaha. It seems you also have something to discuss with our Youngest, Third Brother!"

"That is correct."

After the light greeting, Third Senior Brother came straight to me.

"Youngest."

"Yes, Senior Brother."

"Thank you."

"Yes?" *What is this nonsense all of a sudden?*

"Second Senior Brother has recently made progress in his martial arts. Isn't this also thanks to you, our Youngest, for believing in him?"

"Ha. Hahaha. Th-that's just thanks to Senior Brother's own effort and talent, isn't it? What could I have possibly done?"

"No. Thanks to our Youngest, his negative thoughts have lessened recently. So isn't this also thanks to you, Ilmok?"

"Ahem. I can only congratulate you on your achievement, Senior Brother."

"Uahahahaha! Congratulations, Junior Brother! My, my, if I don't work harder, I might get overtaken by our Third Brother!"

Perhaps because Eldest Senior Brother suddenly joined the conversation, shouts of 'Congratulations!' erupted from all over the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Jin Hayeon had a stern expression and Jang Hoe had a faint smile.

"Congratulations!!" Hang Geon shouted loudly, flattering with all his might, as if he were a devotee of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

*What's with this guy now?* No matter how much his heart had changed, the guy who only cared about his pride had turned into a sycophant overnight.

"Congratulations," Seon'ah said, hiding behind me and only peeking her face out. She said it out of courtesy, but her expression was not very bright, perhaps because my time was being taken up.

Maybe because congratulations poured in from all sides, the pale Third Senior Brother, who looked like he had not seen much sun, blushed and squirmed.

"Hmph, hmph."

It was a somewhat grotesque sight. While I was laughing awkwardly, the human doorbell rang again.

"Young Lord. The Sixth Young Lord has arrived."

"!?"

This visitor was unexpected. Sixth Senior Brother, Jongri Chu, suffered from hallucinations as a price for learning the Frenzied Soul Sword Technique. He had no connection with me whatsoever. We had not even met since the group dinner last time.

However, I could not just turn him away.

"Greetings, Senior Brother."

As I offered a clasped hands greeting, Sixth Senior Brother immediately greeted the others as soon as he received mine. "Greetings, Eldest Senior Brother. Greetings, Third Senior Brother."

"Hahaha! Welcome, but what brings you here, Sixth Brother?" Eldest Senior Brother asked on my behalf, as if this were his own home.

"I heard that Eldest Senior Brother and Third Senior Brother often visit the Youngest here, so my curiosity was piqued and I decided to stop by today."

"Hahaha. Is that so! Ah, come to think of it, you probably haven't heard yet, Sixth Brother. Our Third Brother has made progress in his martial arts, so we were all congratulating him."

"Congratulations, Senior Brother!" Sixth Senior Brother hurriedly offered a clasped hands greeting to Third Senior Brother.

Just then, the voice of the human doorbell, Gwan Muyeol, was heard once again.

"Young Lord! Someone named Guyang Mun from the White Turtle Brigade has come to visit."

At this point, a thought occurred to me. *I wish everyone except Seon'ah would just get lost.*

"Young Lord! Hayeon! Are you inside~?" Guyang Mun's energetic voice came from beyond the door, unaware of the situation inside.

"Haaah. Let him in for now."

As I shouted with a sigh, the door soon opened and Guyang Mun entered, holding a small box.

"Hayeon! Look what I caught this time when I went out..." Guyang Mun had entered excitedly to show off a gift to the woman he loved, but he froze like a broken wooden doll. He hurriedly began to offer clasped hands greetings to everyone.

"Greetings, First Young Lord! Greetings, Third Young Master! Greetings, Sixth Young Lord!"

After finishing his greetings, Guyang Mun used his characteristic sociability and began to chat excitedly with the Senior Brothers.

"Congratulations, Third Young Master! I would be truly delighted if I ever get the chance to witness your Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art! Hahaha."

"Ahem. Hmph. W-well, there's no reason I can't show you."

I looked around at the madmen chattering away excitedly in someone else's home. I noticed Sixth Senior Brother smiling contentedly without joining the conversation.

"Are you alright, Senior Brother?"

"fufufu. This is much better than being alone in my quarters."

"Is that so?"

"When I'm alone, I tend to see things or hear voices, and my mind gets disturbed, but with eleven people gathered together like this, I don't have any hallucinations or hear any voices."

"......"

At Sixth Senior Brother's words, I was momentarily speechless. I scanned my surroundings and after confirming, I spoke cautiously to my Senior Brother.

"Uh, there are only nine people here right now, Senior Brother."

Even including Gwan Muyeol outside the door, there were only ten.

{sep}

Three Senior Brothers, Jin Hayeon, Guyang Mun, two bodyguard martial artists, Hang Geon, and Seon'ah were gathered. Including Ilmok himself, a total of ten people chattered away in the Wind Cliff Pavilion, and time flew by hectically.

The two bodyguard martial artists at least kept their mouths shut, but from Ilmok's perspective, who wanted a quiet rest, it was all the same.

That terrible time came to an end thanks to the human doorbell, Gwan Muyeol.

"Behold the Heavenly Demon! revered of all demons! We greet the lord of all demons!"

Two hours had passed in a flash. It was time for his training with the Heavenly Demon. With the Heavenly Demon's appearance, the visitors left like a receding tide. The Wind Cliff Pavilion was filled only with the sound of Ilmok's breathing.

"Let us begin the training."

That meant he was about to be worked like a dog.

Around the time Ilmok was being thoroughly worked over by the Heavenly Demon, Seon'ah had returned to the Hyeokryeon Clan.

"Haat!"

She practiced the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique alone in the training hall. Beyond the training hall wall, someone watched Seon'ah with a pleased expression.

*hohoho. Perhaps, among the descendants, my granddaughter might become the Patriarch of the family.*

The Demonic Cult revered martial prowess, so the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult was a place where even a woman could become a Patriarch, as long as she was the strongest in the family.

However, Hyeokryeon Cheongang did not know what thoughts were going through Seon'ah's mind as she trained.

"......"

Seon'ah pierced the area around the heart of a wooden training dummy with her fingernails. She looked at her blood-red nails and thought.

*I have to get stronger. Only then can I stay by Oppa's side.*

Seon'ah was young, but she was not a fool. The child knew that the people who took away her time with Ilmok were mostly of higher standing than herself and Ilmok. People like the First Young Lord, the Third Young Master, and the Heavenly Demon.

That is why the child thought.

*I have to become strong. Stronger than anyone else.*

She wanted to be so strong that no one would dare interfere with her time with Ilmok.

SHUK!

The fingernails Seon'ah swung drew a red arc, cleanly slicing the wooden dummy's neck in two.

"......"

As Seon'ah caught her breath, the red energy lingering in her eyes and hair had grown noticeably deeper.

In the dead of night, when everyone was asleep, even the moonlight was hidden by clouds. Only darkness painted the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

A whitish figure, obscured by the darkness, moved like a heat haze toward Ilmok's quarters. It was a bizarre sight.

The door opened and closed without a single sound. Mingling with the whitish figure, the door was suddenly closed as if it had never opened at all.

The shadow that had slipped into Ilmok's quarters under the cover of darkness looked down at the deeply sleeping Ilmok.
