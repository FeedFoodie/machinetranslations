*Shing...!*

Leo drew Bihar's dagger and silently gazed at the blade.

He examined the weapon closely before swinging it through the air.

*Swish...!*

A sharp sound cut through the empty space.

It was none other than a work of Dweno.

Even after thousands of years, the dagger had not forgotten its essence as a weapon.

Leo swung the blade a few more times before looking down at it again.

*It wasn't just my imagination.*

For the briefest moment earlier, Bihar's dagger had reacted to something.

At the same time, Leo had felt Bihar's presence, but now, as if nothing had happened, the dagger remained still.

As Leo wordlessly stared at the weapon, "Sir Leo, may I come in?"

*Knock, knock—*

"Come in."

*Clack—*

Chen Xia opened the door and entered.

Upon seeing Leo standing motionless in the middle of the room, she wore a curious expression.

"Aren't you going to change?"

Leo was still wearing Lunia's uniform.

"I had some things to think about."

Leo crossed his arms and answered indifferently.

His white Seiren uniform draped over his frame, and as he lowered his head slightly, his snow-white hair cascaded forward.

The hair, lengthened by magic for his disguise, glimmered like fresh snowfall.

Combined with his fair skin and his crimson eyes that seemed even more striking than usual, Chen Xia remarked, "I've always thought this, but Sir Leo is really quite a pretty boy. Dressed like this, you look rather elegant."

Saying so, she moved behind Leo and began braiding his hair.

"What are you doing?"

"I thought a braid would suit you."

At that, Leo shook his head.

His hair unraveled.

Opening a subspace with magic, Leo pulled out a pair of pants and put them on.

Tying the loose drawstrings of his baggy pants, he then took off the skirt and tossed it onto the bed.

Next, he removed the uniform top and threw it onto the bed as well.

Beneath the uniform, his toned muscles and defined abs were revealed.

Rolling his shoulders, Leo tilted his head side to side and circulated his aura.

*Crack! Crack! Crack, crack!*

The sound of bones shifting echoed through his body.

His physique began expanding.

Finally returning to his original build, Leo stretched and muttered, "Damn, that hurts."

Hearing his grumble, Chen Xia expressed admiration.

"I noticed it earlier, but it really is fascinating. Altering your skeleton itself with aura... Is that an aura technique you developed, Sir Leo?"

"You think I'd come up with something this pointless?"

"Then?"

"There's only one person among us who would create something like this."

"Sir Dweno?"

"Correct."

*"True beauty must transcend the passage of time! There is no need for illusions or magic to restore one's past self! And so, I created this technique, allowing the body to reverse time itself with aura!"*

"This whole thing started because of that old man's weird philosophy. The problem is that it's so hard to master that not just anyone can learn it."

"Hmm."

Using Dweno's aura technique, Leo had reverted his body to how it had been years ago.

By returning to a physique similar to Lunia's, he had been able to wear her uniform without issue.

Chen Xia, being skilled with her hands, had altered Eiran's clothes to fit herself.

"Can I learn that technique too?"

"Of course. You should be able to pick it up easily. Why?"

"I think it would be convenient in a lot of ways."

"Do you really need it? Even now, when you're with first-years, you look younger than them."

"So you're saying I look like a kid right now?"

Narrowing her eyes, Chen Xia jabbed a finger into Leo's side.

Leo replied blandly, "I meant you have a youthful face."

"What's the point of saying that to a teenager? Youthful face?"

To Chen Xia, who was often teased for looking the same age as Chelsea, it was not a welcome comment.

Leo flicked her forehead with a finger.

Rubbing her forehead, Chen Xia scoffed and turned away.

"Pouting?"

"Whatever."

Seeing her act so prim, Leo said, "Come here. I have something to discuss with you."

"With me?"

Chen Xia looked puzzled.

As Leo pulled a chair over and sat down, Chen Xia took a seat on the bed.

Leo showed her Bihar's dagger and said, "This is the dagger I gifted Bihar."

"Ah..."

Hearing it was an artifact used by an ancestor, Chen Xia's eyes widened slightly.

"And the moment I stepped into Eldon, this dagger reacted. Just for a second, I felt Bihar's presence. What do you make of that?"

Leo's question made Chen Xia pause in thought before she said, "Lady Bihar passed away due to her battle with Lady Velkia, right?"

"Yeah."

Under normal circumstances, the Xian Empire and House Ersar should have become bitter enemies, but given the nature of the Xian Empire, everything about them had remained hidden.

"...Do you think Lady Bihar held resentment toward Lady Velkia?"

"If it's the Bihar I knew, she wouldn't."

Leo's gaze darkened slightly.

"Instead, she would have felt regret."

"That, too, is a kind of lingering attachment."

Chen Xia closed her eyes and took a deep breath.

"If we assume that Lady Velkia is here, could it be that Sir Leo's spirit magic activated?"

"You think so too?"

"Yes."

Leo did not know much about spirit magic.

Of course, compared to ordinary summoners, he knew a fair amount, but since he had no ability as a spirit summoner, he had never studied it in depth.

That changed when Lysinas awakened her necromantic abilities.

Leo had begun to study spirit magic, and since Chen Xia could wield spirit magic, she had studied alongside him.

The reason was simple.

She wanted to be of even the slightest help to Leo.

"Since you've summoned Lady Luna before."

Leo nodded and raised the dagger.

"Are you going to try calling her now?"

"Yeah."

Leo nodded and channeled his spiritual energy.

*Vwoom...!*

Absorbing Leo's spiritual energy, Bihar's dagger began to hum in resonance.

*... If Bihar really does appear as a spirit, what should I say to her?*

Should he reprimand his disciple for never escaping the darkness?

Or should he praise her for dedicating her entire life to the world?

*Or... should I console her for blaming herself for failing to stop Velkia?*

Leo gazed at Bihar's dagger with a somber expression.

Moments later.

*Flash...!*

Bihar's dagger emitted a brilliant light.

As Leo released his grip, the dagger floated into the air.

A summoning circle, drawn with Leo's spiritual energy, formed around it.

The circle was quickly absorbed into the dagger.

And then.

*Thud...!*

Bihar's dagger fell lifelessly to the floor.

"......"

"......"

Leo and Chen Xia silently stared at the dagger.

"Nothing's happening?"

Chen Xia crouched beside the dagger and poked it with her finger.

There was no reaction.

Leo furrowed his brow.

"That's strange. I was sure it was Bihar's presence."

Chen Xia carefully picked up the dagger.

Still, there was no response.

"Sir Leo, here."

Chen Xia trotted over and handed the dagger to Leo.

Leo stared at it for a moment before saying, "That's yours now."

"Huh?"

"It belongs to your ancestor."

Leo said as he put on his shirt.

"You can use a dagger, right?"

"I can, but the killing intent is a bit too strong."

"Then train to control it."

"Yes."

Responding firmly, Chen Xia carefully secured Bihar's dagger.

Once he finished dressing, Leo asked, "Where are Lunia and Eiran?"

"They went to Eldon's council. Since they're big shots from Seiren, they said they needed to make an appearance."

"Do we not need to go?"

"Lunia said just the two of them would be fine. She asked us to gather intel around the city."

"Is that so?"

Leo, now wearing his coat, looked out the window.

Before he knew it, snow had begun to fall.

"Then let's get to work."

"Yes!"

Leo and Chen Xia left their lodging.

*Whooosh...!*

"Achoo!"

Late at night.

A piercingly cold wind howled through the streets.

Stepping out of the building, Lunia shivered and turned back.

The building she and Eiran had just exited was a palace, the very embodiment of extravagance.

Any other race might have been awed by its splendor, but elves valued elegance and simplicity.

For those who upheld tradition, such opulence would have been seen as distasteful.

And this building was the very heart of the Pureblood Society, which prided itself on tradition.

*They call other races' palaces vulgar and excessive, yet look at them!*

Lunia's expression darkened.

"Why did we have to waste time flattering Eldon's old geezers the moment we arrived?"

As Lunia grumbled, Eiran replied in a weary voice.

"It can't be helped. If we want to investigate the city freely, we need their cooperation."

"Ugh, I feel like I'm back in my second year of school!"

Lunia huffed.

"We'll have to attend that party too, won't we?"

"...Probably."

Lunia groaned and held her head.

*Boom...!*

Suddenly, a loud explosion rang out, and Lunia and Eiran's eyes widened.

Even amidst the snowstorm, flames burned brightly from a collapsed section of the city wall.

Their expressions hardened.

"An attack?"

"Could it be Tartaros?"

A ruthless, almost terroristic assault.

The first thing that came to mind was demonic forces.

Without hesitation, the two sprinted toward the site of the explosion.

And then...

"Hurry! Hurry!"

"Before the Pureblood Society bastards get here!"

Seeing figures looting a warehouse beyond the broken city wall, Lunia and Eiran's jaws dropped.

"E-Elves?"

Masked elves were ransacking the place.

Lunia reeled in disbelief.

*What kind of messed-up city is this?*

Elves stealing from elves? She had never even imagined such a thing.

"Who goes there?!"

"Are you Pureblood Society lapdogs?!"

Armed elves on lookout quickly surrounded Lunia and Eiran.

"Lunia, what do we do?"

"What else?"

Lunia's face hardened.

"We're not going down without a fight. Take them out!"

"Yes!"

{sep}

Leo and Chen Xia continued gathering information in the streets late into the night.

And they uncovered a few facts.

"This city is incredibly wealthy."

Chen Xia's words were accurate.

The elves walking the streets all looked affluent at a glance.

Despite the harsh cold, they wore only light clothing.

It wasn't just because they were mages.

They were dressed in mana cloth, a type of fabric woven with mana. Elsewhere, the highest-grade mana cloth was only used for the uniforms of hero academies, yet here, everyone on the streets wore clothes made from it.

That was why they could endure the cold in such thin garments.

In human society, even most nobles had difficulty obtaining such fine mana cloth.

Considering that Leo and his group weren't even in the wealthiest district, it was clear how rich this city was.

"Not to mention, it's incredibly indulgent. These elves are quite different from the ones I know."

Chen Xia looked around with a bewildered expression.

On the surface, the city seemed well-organized.

The streets were pristine.

Since the entire populace practiced Stellar Magic, the city was filled with magic-related shops.

The passersby all carried themselves like mages, yet, every elf was adorned with extravagant jewelry, flaunting their wealth.

Their faces bore the unmistakable arrogance of *privilege.*

And once night fell, the entire atmosphere shifted.

The streets, which had been filled with scholars pursuing magic by day, vanished without a trace.

Every shop had transformed into a tavern.

The entire city was drowning in indulgence and pleasure.

Leo realized something.

*This city is the same as Balharun at its core.*

It was dazzling on the outside but rotten within.

Leo furrowed his brow.

He felt an inexplicable sense of filth clinging to the air.

*If it were the current Velkia, she would have burned this city down the moment she saw it.*

Yet, the city remained untouched.

As Leo pondered, "Shall we investigate a bit more?"

"Yeah. Let's split up for now."

"Alright. We've been moving together the whole time, so let's separate and gather more intel."

During the day, they had approached pedestrians, asking if anything unusual had happened in the city.

Every elf had responded with the same smile, insisting that Eldon was peaceful, but what about at night?

*They might be looser after a few drinks.*

As Chen Xia walked through the streets, having parted ways with Leo, she suddenly froze.

*Boom...!*

Chen Xia instinctively turned toward the source of the sound. *An explosion?* 

"Such pretty black hair," a dry, emotionless voice remarked from behind her.

"...!" A chill ran down her spine.

*Rustle—*

A cold hand reached out and played with Chen Xia's hair, yet, she couldn't move an inch, because the hand that touched her had no warmth.

Chen Xia trembled as the fingers, which had been toying with her hair, grazed the tip of her pointed ear.

*Hiss—*

Leo's magic was dispelled, and the owner of the hand gripped Chen Xia's shoulder, and turned her around to face them.

With a face drained of all color, Chen Xia lifted her gaze to the figure standing beneath the snow-filled night sky.

"Bihar's heirs are still the same as ever," an elf whispered longingly, yet her eyes carried deep sorrow and an unspoken apology.

Chen Xia murmured the name of the most beautiful elf she had ever seen, "Vel... kia."
