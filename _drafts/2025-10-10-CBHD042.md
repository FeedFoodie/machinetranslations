Chapter 42: Assassination (4)

Although we had tidied up in a hurry since morning, the room still had sword marks left on the wall.
Eldest Senior Brother, Third Senior Brother, and I were sitting there in a circle. We had sent everyone else out.
"Is there a problem?"
Perhaps it was because of my grave expression. Eldest Senior Brother asked with a rather serious look on his face.
"What I'm about to tell you is just about a possibility. It's not a certainty."
Having started like that, I began to explain the deductions I had made a short while ago.
"Currently, the man named Chugak is known to be a spy from the Murim Alliance, but it's possible he's an assassin sent by someone within the Divine Cult who is targeting me."
"!!"
"What do you mean by that?"
"First, I should probably explain what happened when the assassin came. I was able to survive the assassin's attack thanks to Third Senior Brother. Because Third Senior Brother has visited me at night a few times, I had prepared a way to know if someone entered my room."
"...So that's why you said it was thanks to me."
Third Senior Brother nodded with a gloomy expression, as if he finally understood.
"Yes. And this was a secret only I knew. Since the two bodyguard martial artists and Miss Jin dislike Third Senior Brother's secret nightly visits, I prepared it so I could greet him calmly without being startled."
"I-I didn't realize you were being that considerate."
"Ahem. Anyway, the important thing is that I survived because I made those preparations. To put it another way, if I hadn't made those preparations, I would have died without even knowing an assassin had come."
"Hahaha! It's just as Youngest said, Junior Brother. You practically saved our Youngest!"
At Eldest Senior Brother's praise, the gloomy expression on Third Senior Brother's face eased slightly. However, that wasn't the important thing right now.
"The important point here is what would have happened if I hadn't been prepared. In other words, if I had died in vain."
"If you had died, Junior Brother?"
"Yes. If that had happened, who would be the first person to fall under suspicion?"
At my point, Eldest Senior Brother, who had been pondering for a moment, turned his gaze toward Third Senior Brother. And Third Senior Brother's face had also turned deathly pale.
Their deduction was correct, so I nodded.
"If I had died without even making a sound, my corpse would have been discovered in the morning by Miss Jin or the bodyguard martial artists. And Miss Jin and the bodyguard martial artists knew that Third Senior Brother often visited my quarters secretly in the dead of night."
"...But I was in my quarters at that time. Wasn't I even in a state of qi deviation?"
As if he were being framed as the real culprit, the pale-faced Third Senior Brother protested.
"That's the problem. Think about it, Senior Brother. I would have lost my life in the middle of the night, and at the time of my death, you were in a state of qi deviation. In that situation, if something like a blood-stained sword was left in your room, what would you think?"
"...I would have thought that I'd killed you, Junior Brother, without realizing it while I was in qi deviation."
For a moment, Third Senior Brother and Eldest Senior Brother wore somber expressions. Then, as if a thought had just occurred to him, Eldest Senior Brother asked in a puzzled tone.
"But why would a blood-stained sword suddenly be placed in Third's room?"
"For my theory to hold, the ones targeting me must have known that Third Senior Brother fell into qi deviation last night. In other words, someone close to Third Senior Brother leaked that information."
"!!!"
"If such a person exists, it wouldn't be difficult for them to take the sword after I was assassinated and secretly place it in your room while you were in qi deviation, Senior Brother."
Of course, there was a leap in logic here. The two Senior Brothers, surprised by my inadequate story, didn't seem to notice.
To kill me and frame him, they would just endlessly wait for Third Senior Brother's qi deviation, which could happen at any time?
And they would even know when that qi deviation would end, kill me, and then hide the sword near Third Senior Brother?
Well, they could just skip the blood-stained sword and frame him with circumstantial evidence alone, but that still felt a little insufficient.
There was one way that could make this possible.
*If the ones targeting me had a way to induce qi deviation or control its duration, all contradictions would be resolved.*
Although I didn't know if such a thing actually existed.
"In any case, if this theory is correct, it means there's a mole among those who know that Third Senior Brother visits me at night. And there must be at least one by Third Senior Brother's side as well."
Jin Hayeon, Gwan Muyeol, and Jang Hoe. At least one of these three was an underling of the person targeting me.
"Then shouldn't we report this to Master immediately!!"
Enraged by the mention of a mole, Eldest Senior Brother began to rise, radiating killing intent.
I had to quickly grab the hem of Eldest Senior Brother's pants to stop him.
"Didn't I say so at the beginning? This is just about a possibility. Since there's even evidence that he's a spy from the Murim Alliance, the chances are honestly slim. It's just a theory, there's no direct evidence."
"Evidence? If we interrogate those three, won't something come out?"
"......"
Honestly, even I, who came up with this theory, wasn't certain. It was just something I thought of while obsessively considering the 'what ifs', and perhaps because of that obsession, it was hard to just let it go.
Because of that, suspecting those I was close to was a very unpleasant option.
"Not only is it more likely to be untrue, but if we carelessly report this to Master without knowing who the mastermind is, there's a risk the mastermind will go into hiding. We must approach this matter cautiously. This is important not just for me, but for you as well, Eldest Senior Brother."
"???"
Toward Eldest Senior Brother's uncomprehending expression, I added a further explanation.
"If this wasn't the work of the Murim Alliance, and if they were trying to frame Third Senior Brother, why would they go through such a complicated plan to target me?"
"...Don't tell me, is it because I cherish you?"
I nodded at Eldest Senior Brother's question.
No matter how much I thought about it, that was the only reason to target me. To be precise.
"It's probably because they judged that we would be an obstacle to them becoming the next Heavenly Demon."
There is someone who intends to ascend to the position of the next Heavenly Demon, even if it means eliminating the other Heavenly Demon disciples.
Realizing this, Eldest Senior Brother's face looked as if it would explode at any moment from suppressed rage.
"To think that believers of the great Heavenly Demon Divine Cult would resort to such a petty trick!!"
The reason for his anger seemed to be because they had chosen a despicable method instead of fair competition.
Even as he seethed as if he would go berserk at any moment, a red glint shooting from his eyes, Eldest Senior Brother somehow held onto his sanity.
It seemed he had indeed entered the Transcendent Demon Realm.
Then he turned that murderous face to me and asked through clenched teeth.
"So, what do you want to do, Youngest? Do you have some kind of plan in mind?"
Facing that terrifying energy head-on, I answered in the calmest tone possible.
"First, as I've said, this is just a theory based on a slim possibility. It's not certain. However, I do have a plan in mind, just in case."

The Guardian Hall Director and two other men were meeting in the darkness.
"Tsk. To think a member of the Dark Shadow Hall couldn't even take care of a brat who's only been in the cult for half a year. What a shame."
At the words of the man on the right, the Guardian Hall Director spoke placatingly.
"Still, we succeeded in diverting suspicion to the Murim Alliance, did we not? It was truly fortunate that we had made preparations."
The plan to frame Third Young Master was something they had thought of long ago, based on information brought by the mole they had placed near Ilmok.
The plan had been delayed to prepare for contingencies.
In case the assassin failed. They needed time to create false evidence to pin the crime on the Murim Alliance.
However, the man on the right, seemingly unsatisfied with just that, looked at the man in the center and asked in an anxious tone.
"But, is it really safe? What will you do if that thing we used on Third Young Master is discovered?"
At that question, the man in the center clicked his tongue lightly and replied.
"Don't worry. It leaves no evidence behind after use."
Perhaps finding the whining man on the right unpleasant, the man in the center said to the Guardian Hall Director.
"The bodyguards will be replaced because of this incident, so we won't be able to use the one we planted."
"We can just prepare another one, so don't worry about it. More importantly, what are you planning to do now?"
At the Guardian Hall Director's question, the man in the center stroked his beard for a moment before answering.
"It wouldn't be a bad idea to use what you mentioned back then. Didn't you say that Eighth Young Lord has been suffering from the side effects of his demonic art recently?"
The fact that Ilmok had consulted a bodyguard martial artist about how to deal with the side effects had also already been relayed to them.
"You mean we should dispose of him by disguising it as qi deviation, just like we did with Fifth Young Lord?"
The man in the center nodded at the Guardian Hall Director's question.
It was a method that only worked on the side effects of certain demonic arts, but they had a way to stimulate those side effects to induce qi deviation.
Just as they had used it on Third Young Master this time.

The next morning.
Master visited the Wind Cliff Pavilion again.
"Behold the Heavenly Demon revered of all demons !"
"We greet the Lord of all demons!"
As the two bodyguards and Jin Hayeon, who were guarding the Wind Cliff Pavilion, paid their respects, Master accepted their greeting as usual and said.
"I have something to discuss with Ilmok, so leave us."
"As you command!"
After sending the three of them outside the Wind Cliff Pavilion, Master turned to me, who was holding a sword, and asked.
"If you overexert yourself, your wound might reopen."
"It was a shallow cut, so it's not a big problem. Maybe because I'm still young, I seem to be recovering quickly. Hahaha."
"Hohoho. You speak as if you've experienced being old."
"Ahem. It's something I often heard from Uncle Taehyun. He said that maybe because he's getting older, his body doesn't feel better even after a full night's sleep."
It was a moment where I almost revealed that I had been reincarnated without realizing it.
Fortunately, Master didn't seem to have any particular doubts about my words and moved on to the main topic.
"Over the past day, an investigation was conducted on those related to Chugak. Naturally, the first to be investigated was the Dark Shadow Hall Director, but nothing particularly suspicious was found about him. We are looking into others who were even slightly close to Chugak, but it seems highly likely that it was his crime alone."
Master spoke much like Eldest Senior Brother had. That is, the signs of his suppressed anger were palpable.
He seemed quite enraged by the fact that not only had a spy from the Murim Alliance infiltrated the cult, but that they had also targeted his youngest disciple.
"Furthermore, I plan to replace your bodyguards and attendant. You've already said you've mastered the scriptures, history books, and law books, so there's no need to receive lessons from Attendant Jin. I will assign you people with more experience as personal bodyguards."
Master said this, likely out of concern for my safety. It seemed he had dismissed the bodyguards for the purpose of this conversation.
And this was the conversation I had been waiting for.
"If my safety is the issue, wouldn't it be enough to just increase the number of bodyguards slightly? Is there really a need to cast them out?"
"...I know you've grown attached to them. However, you were in danger, so I cannot simply let this pass."
"But aren't I perfectly fine like this?"
"The fact that they didn't know until the assassin entered your quarters is already tantamount to failure."
"In any case, since I, the one they were guarding, am fine, it wasn't a failure, was it? So, I ask that you end this by simply assigning one or two additional people."
"...What is the reason you're defending them to this extent?"
At Master's question, I answered with the saddest expression I could muster.
"If they are punished for this incident, won't their future lives in the cult be difficult? How could they rise to a higher position while bearing the stigma of having failed as bodyguards?"
For a moment, Master watched me speak so sorrowfully, as if I were about to burst into tears. Then he stroked his beard and said.
"How commendable. I understand well your heart that cares for the believers."
"Thank you, Master!"
"However, this is the only time. You are the disciple of this old man, the sky of the cult. This means your life is not a private matter. Protecting those who have failed in an official duty out of sentiment could ruin things in the future."
"I will take it to heart! I will take it to heart!"
I bowed to the floor, showing Master the utmost respect.
There was nothing servile or special about it.
This was the normal way for believers to act before the Heavenly Demon.
Besides, I wasn't asking Master this because I truly felt sorry for the three of them.
*Alright, the first step is complete.*
The chances are low, but if my theory is true, at least one of those three is an enemy mole.
And isn't there a famous line from a certain movie? Keep your friends close, and your enemies closer.
My first plan. It was to become the bait myself.
