Master taught me the inner arts techniques and then left without a backward glance. He said he had other matters to attend to.

Alone in the valedictorian, I ate the dinner Jin Hayeon prepared and started my evening routine.

My routine during the Hour of the Dog, from 7 to 9 PM, was flexible. It was time after dinner to improve on any areas I lacked from that day. If my morning faith studies were insufficient, I would review the parts I missed. If my afternoon martial arts training was lacking, I would practice those arts further.

Therefore, today I decided to focus on martial arts.

I decided the duel was more urgent than the interview, given my progress. The interview only required memorizing about five hundred pages, so it didn't seem like a big problem. Of course, the more I trained in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, the faster its side effects would appear.

*First, I have to improve my skills somehow to a level where I can enter the Demonic Academy.*

Not learning it was simply not an option.

I swung my sword for another hour and performed qi circulation. Then, Jin Hayeon approached me.

"Young Lord, you must have accumulated fatigue in your muscles and acupoints from today's practice. I'll perform Body Purification for you before your bath."

Body Purification was like a modern massage or acupressure. It was performed not just with hands, but with inner arts. I had already trained for over four hours today, so my body was tired. The offer of a massage was truly welcome.

As I lay facedown on the wooden floor, she calmly began to press the muscles and acupoints of my body with her fingers. Her energy flowed into me through her fingertips.

The refreshing sensation made me groan unconsciously.

"Ughhh..."

It was not the refreshing feeling of relieved muscles, like an adult sinking into a hot bath and sighing, ‘Ah, that feels good’.

It was genuinely cold. The energy flowing from her fingers carried a deep chill.

I shivered. She withdrew her hands, evened her breathing, and spoke. She clearly had not reached Master's level of speaking while circulating her inner arts.

"The martial art I've learned contains yin qi, so you might feel a little cold, but rapidly cooling your heated muscles with this chill helps them recover. Please endure it for a little while, Young Lord."

After speaking, Jin Hayeon once again reached out and began pressing various points on my body. Goosebumps rose on my skin from the cold each time her hands touched me, and a question formed in my mind.

"By the way, what martial art are you learning, Miss Jin?"

A martial art that handled yin qi did not seem ordinary.

At my question, she withdrew her hands again. She sighed faintly and answered.

"It’s the Pure Hands Demonic Art, Young Lord."

"...Was the Pure Hands Demonic Art something just anyone could learn?"

The question came to me naturally. I was sure Master said it was one of the top demonic arts, second only to the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

"In the Divine Cult, all demonic arts except the Heavenly Demon Divine Art are available to any student who enters the Demonic Academy."

"Ah!"

Well, it was an institution for cultivating the elites who would lead the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, so that made sense.

*Hmm. Is that why Jin Hayeon, a handmaiden, is in charge of my education?*

An ordinary handmaiden preparing someone for the Demonic Cult's top educational institution. It did not make sense. Of course, the question of why someone who graduated from the Demonic Cult's best educational institution was working as a handmaiden still remained.

But a much bigger question struck me.

"Wait, the side effect of the Pure Hands Demonic Art is..."

My words trailed off. It was only natural, since the side effect was *that*.

An intensified libido...

As my thoughts reached that point, I accidentally swallowed hard.

*N-no way, was she maintaining that expressionless face all the time to suppress her libido?*

Jin Hayeon must have misunderstood my reaction. She frowned slightly and spoke.

"I apologize, Young Lord, but the side effects of the Pure Hands Demonic Art are different for men and women."

"Ahem. I-I see. Then, what are the side effects when a woman learns it?"

"When a woman, or a man born with strong yin qi, learns the Pure Hands Demonic Art, their reason develops more than their emotions. They become capable of much more cold-headed thinking."

I tilted my head at her answer.

*That's a side effect? It's too mild compared to other demonic arts.*

Finding it strange, I asked again.

"Are there any other side effects?"

"...I’ve heard it diminishes one's compassion and ability to empathize with others, but since it’s a martial art for punishing heathens, I don’t consider that a side effect."

"..."

So, it was a martial art that turned people into psychopaths.

I was speechless.

"From now on, please ask your questions after the Body Purification is finished."

With those words, Jin Hayeon reached out her hands again and resumed the Body Purification. A psychopath-in-training was now pressing all over my body with fingers imbued with inner arts.

"......"

Goosebumps erupted all over my body. I could not help but shudder.

Early the next morning, Ilmok woke up refreshed, with no muscle soreness. It was either thanks to the aspiring psychopath's cold Body Purification or because his body was truly blessed by the gods. Thanks to that, Ilmok could lightly perform qi circulation early in the morning before beginning his lessons.

When the lesson had progressed to a certain point, Jin Hayeon watched Ilmok listen intently to her explanation and felt a faint, internal admiration.

*As expected.*

*An awl in a sack, is it?*

They had only had lessons for two days, yesterday and today, but in that short time, she could already recognize his talent. It was surely his first time encountering the doctrine, yet he understood it fluently. He even asked frequent questions.

His questions were not from a lack of understanding. They were to confirm if his own understanding was correct. On top of that, he had shown physical talent and an aptitude for martial arts yesterday.

However, her admiration was not for Ilmok.

*As expected, the Supreme Lord's judgment is precise.*

She admired the Heavenly Demon, who had recognized such qualities in Ilmok.

As Jin Hayeon inwardly reinforced her faith in the Heavenly Demon, Ilmok suddenly raised his right hand.

"When are we scheduled to start studying the books of law and history?"

Jin Hayeon inwardly sighed at his question.

*His talent is as outstanding as the Supreme Lord predicted, but his laziness is the problem.*

Jin Hayeon thought Ilmok was lazy because of their squabble on the first day.

She was half right.

"Don't worry. With your insight, Young Lord, you'll understand the books of law and history without much difficulty, just like this book of doctrine."

He just was not lazy when his life was on the line.

"I'm not asking about the difficulty. I'm asking when we start."

Jin Hayeon found Ilmok's repeated question unexpected. She began to explain in detail.

"We're scheduled to begin those two after you've finished reading the Records of the Heavenly Demon once. The Records of the Heavenly Demon is the foundation of our Divine Cult, so the books of law and history are related to its doctrine. It'll be easier to understand them if you study the book of doctrine first."

"I see."

Ilmok thought he had received a sufficient explanation and nodded, but for some reason, her explanation continued.

"Even after you finish the book of doctrine, if you find a part you don't understand while studying law and history, it'll help to read this book again. As I just said, all studies in our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult are based on this book of doctrine."

Ilmok saw her gaze upon the book of doctrine with a face full of conviction, and a certain curiosity surged within him.

"Then, how many times have you read this book of doctrine, Miss Jin?"

"How could I possibly count every time I've read it? I reread this book of doctrine countless times when I was studying history and law. Even after that, whenever I had worries or doubts, reading this book brought me peace."

But why? Contrary to her words about finding peace, the more she praised the book of doctrine, the deeper the madness in her eyes became.

"Now, I can recite any passage from any chapter, without missing a single detail. Even so, I still find it more enjoyable to read the words directly than to just recall them in my head."

*...A psychopath and a religious fanatic? What is this hybrid?*

Ilmok witnessed the terrible thing born from the fusion of a heretic religion and demonic art. He belatedly realized one important fact.

Ilmok stared at the Records of the Heavenly Demon spread before him and swallowed dryly. This book of doctrine was dangerous. Not reading it as much as possible seemed beneficial for his mental health.

The only problem was...

*If I can't memorize this, my head will fly off in a year...*

Worse, if Ilmok could not memorize it all, he could not even start the other test subjects, the books of history and law. It was a true dilemma, like being caught in one of Zhuge Kongming's traps.

As Ilmok frantically racked his brain for a solution, an idea flashed in his mind.

*Right! I can use that.*

It was not perfect, but he had a useful idea. A problem still remained, however.

*I can't trust that psycho religious fanatic...*

She was a woman crazy not for the Young Lord she served, but for the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. In a way, she was both a handmaiden and a watcher. As Ilmok pondered how to hide ‘that’ from Jin Hayeon, he changed his thinking.

He decided to deceive her by showing it instead of hiding it.

"I'm sorry, but could you bring me some ink, a brush, and a blank book? Ah, and a thin brush, if possible."

Jin Hayeon was puzzled by the sudden instruction, but she accepted Ilmok's order. She was his handmaiden, after all.

*...His qualities are excellent, but I worry he might be lazy.*

Jin Hayeon had an ominous feeling that he was sending her away on an excuse to rest. She quickly erased the irreverent thought.

*He is the one chosen by the Heavenly Demon. Doubt is forbidden.*

She forcefully erased her ominous feeling and brought the blank book, ink, and thin brush. Ilmok opened the Records of the Heavenly Demon and began writing passages into the blank book.

Jin Hayeon looked down, wondering what on earth he was doing. She soon understood.

*So he's trying to copy the contents of the scripture.*

It seemed to be a form of review, so he would not forget what he had learned today. However, it was subtly different from the review she imagined.

He was not just mindlessly copying the scripture. He used the thin brush to edit the scripture's content. He omitted passages for emphasis and redundant explanations. Instead, he added the explanations Jin Hayeon herself had given during the lesson.

She probably did not understand, but Ilmok was creating his own textbook as he reviewed. He deliberately created a new one because he was told the scripture must never be damaged. Otherwise, he might have just scribbled her explanations directly onto the book of doctrine.

Just then, Jin Hayeon examined the content Ilmok was writing. She tilted her head and spoke.

"Excuse me, Young Lord. May I ask what this content means?"

"Ah, this?"

Ilmok smiled lightly at her question.

"It’s my own code. It’s hard to write long passages multiple times while learning, so I created a code that condenses various meanings."

What Ilmok intended to show her was none other than Hangul.
