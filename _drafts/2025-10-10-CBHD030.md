*An audit...*

As a former public official, I was somewhat familiar with the task. It was a job meant to prevent injustice, corruption, and unlawful acts. Ironically, it was also the best position for committing those very things.

*They used to make a killing that way back in the day.*

My work in the social welfare department was a world away from that, but stories were common. People slipped money to officials to get away with illegal building extensions. A company might get an outsourced project, like developing a program, and inflate the payment to an absurd degree compared to the final product's quality.

The official in charge was supposed to monitor these things, but many turned a blind eye for a backroom deal.

*It's still this bad, even with the internet. I can't imagine how it was in the old days.*

This era was so ancient it made the "back in my day" stories from my seniors seem modern. The probability of getting caught for injustice, corruption, or bribery was ridiculously low compared to my time as Seo Jihun.

*Should I try to make a haul this time?*

A vile smile started to form on my lips.

"What are you thinking about so intently?"

The Master’s voice broke me from my thoughts.

"Hahaha. I was just wondering if a young person like me might overstep and make a mistake."

"Hohoho. You’re more than capable, so don’t you worry."

A cold sweat ran down my back as I faced the Master’s benevolent smile.

*The chances of getting caught might be low, but I have no idea what would happen if I was discovered.*

He smiled benevolently, but the Master was the Cult Leader of a heretical religion full of psychopaths.

*And now that I think about it, there's not much to fleece anyway.*

There were plenty of ways to extort them. I could visit a Branch and threaten them a little, maybe mention submitting a negative report to the Heavenly Demon. They would surely offer up all sorts of things on their own.

*At best, it would be special food or liquor. Or maybe some gold and treasures.*

Gold and treasures. Those words would have made my eyes spin back when I was Seo Jihun.

*Do I... really need it?*

My position as a disciple of the Heavenly Demon already guaranteed me a personal attendant, three meals a day, and a place to sleep. The title alone was enough to live a life of leisure in the Demonic Cult. It made me understand why they say public officials must be paid well to prevent corruption.

Of course, being worked to the bone *because* I was a disciple was annoying.

*If I took the money and ran, the Demonic Cult would hunt me for the rest of my life.*

That future was too terrible to imagine. The very people who cherished me now, calling me ‘Young Lord’ or ‘Junior Brother,’ would turn into crazed fanatics and chase me with wild eyes.

"Since you put it that way, Master, I will do my best."

I concluded that I was locked into a job with nothing to gain.

*I should think of this as a vacation, not a business trip, and just laze around before coming back.*

It was a rotten mindset for an auditor.

{sep}

A few days later, I left the Main Headquarters of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult in a carriage. I was not heading to Wensu County, my last destination. The Master had designated a much more distant place this time. He said that as his disciple, I needed to understand the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult’s situation in greater detail.

He told me to see and feel it for myself, claiming the atmosphere would be quite different from Wensu County, which was close to the Main Headquarters.

*That damned old man.*

He was a master who seemed itching to work his disciple to death.

Jin Hayeon, riding in the carriage with me, was unaware of my thoughts. She spoke with a serious expression.

"It seems the Heavenly Demon truly believes that you, Young Lord Ilmok, will grow into someone great. Isn’t that why he entrusted you with inspecting a place so far away, to grasp the details of the Divine Cult?"

She always had a cold demeanor, but her head was bizarrely in the clouds when it came to the Master.

"Do you truly believe he sent me all the way to the distant Hwajeong because he trusts me?"

"Isn’t it obvious? If he didn’t trust you, Young Lord, wouldn’t the Heavenly Demon have accompanied you?"

She was right. Unlike last time, the Master was not with me on this long-distance trip.

*Right. Since the Master isn't here to watch me, I'll just think of it as a vacation and...*

Before I could finish the thought, the carriage suddenly stopped.

"Is something wrong?" I shouted outside.

The two bodyguards the Master had assigned for the trip answered in turn.

"Young Lord. From now on, you must get out of the carriage and follow it using your movement arts."

"This is a strict order from the Supreme Lord."

"..."

Now I understood. He did not trust me. He had calculated that he could work me hard even without being present. If I was the auditor meant to grasp the situation of each Branch, then those bodyguards were meant to watch me.

{sep}

"Haa. Haa."

I staggered, my breath catching in my throat.

"Giddy-up!"

The carriage running beside me stopped. With a now-familiar motion, I climbed back inside. During the last trip, I did not know how to use inner arts, so my ‘movement arts’ was just mimicking the motions while running. My speed was no match for the carriage.

Now, however, I could use inner arts to perform true movement arts and run at the same speed as the carriage. Of course, my inner arts were still shallow, so I could only run for about fifteen minutes at a time.

*Though this is only possible because it's a demonic art.*

The Master had said the inner arts in my qi center were equivalent to about five years of cultivation for a Central Plains heathen. It was an amount that would take five years to accumulate using the simple Tonap method of absorbing qi. I had gathered it all in just under four months.

I caught my breath for a moment, feeling the demonic art’s terrifying effectiveness.

"Young Lord. It is time to begin your qi circulation," Jin Hayeon said.

I sighed and sat in the lotus position. I had to perform qi circulation inside a swaying carriage moving at full speed. I had grown accustomed to it from my last trip, but I sighed for a simple reason.

*Once I recover my inner arts, I'll have to run again, won't I?*

I had already repeated this insane act four times. How many more times would I have to run before we reached our destination?

*I should probably stall for time.*

I continued my qi circulation as slowly as possible. I breathed in and out deeply, gathering my inner arts at the slowest pace I could manage. Even as I deliberately dragged it out, my small qi center filled up quickly.

*Let's hold out a little longer.*

I pretended I was not finished and took a few more deep breaths.

The bodyguard martial artists’ voices came from the front of the carriage.

"Young Lord. We are aware that your qi center is already full."

"If you keep this up, we will have no choice but to report it to the Heavenly Demon."

"Huuu."

I let out a deep sigh, got up, and stepped out of the carriage.

*I'll get my revenge, no matter what. I'm your Cult Leader's disciple, you bastards.*

{sep}

How long had those bodyguard martial artists run me ragged? By the time the sun was setting, we arrived at a town. It was not our final destination, Hwajeong.

The distance from the Billion Mountains, the Demonic Cult’s Main Headquarters, to Hejing County was an absurd length of over a thousand ri (400km). This meant it was impossible to arrive in a single day unless you were a master at the pinnacle of movement arts or rode a legendary thousand-ri steed.

We planned to rest in Gaochang County, about halfway between the Billion Mountains and Hejing County, before departing again. Naturally, Gaochang County also had a Branch of the Demonic Cult. We were scheduled to conduct an audit here as well, since it was on the way to Hejing County. The entire schedule and route had been planned for this purpose.

As the sun set, people all over town were finishing their work and heading home. We went not to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult’s Branch, but to the only inn in Gaochang County. The reason was simple.

The great Master had ordered us to hide our identities for the on-site audit. He said that if we announced our arrival, we would not be able to see their usual state.

*He's unnecessarily meticulous about things like this.*

While I grumbled inwardly about the Master, the carriage stopped again.

"......"

My body tensed, unknowingly preparing to use movement arts. I belatedly remembered we had stopped because we had reached our destination.

"Damn it."

A curse escaped my lips. I felt like one of Pavlov’s dogs. Thanks to the repeated cycle of rest and movement arts, my body was caked in sweat that had long since cooled into salt crystals. I dragged myself out of the carriage and entered the inn with Jin Hayeon and the two bodyguards.

No one greeted us. I looked around puzzled and saw a middle-aged man, who appeared to be the innkeeper, talking with a child.

"Sampyeong! Didn’t I tell you you’ll forget everything if you don’t study now!"

"Hmph. I don’t wanna study."

A child who did not want to study and a parent forcing him to. It was a familiar sight.

"Ahem! Do you know what kind of opportunity this is! You have to learn when the people from the Divine Cult are teaching you! This Daddy couldn’t even learn a few characters when I was young because there was no one to teach me!"

"But you’re living just fine without knowing how to read, Daddy."

"I’m doing this kind of hard work because I don’t know how to read! And because I don’t know how to read, I could only name my son Sampyeong! Because I only knew a few characters!"

The father was practically shouting as he scolded the child, but his earnest desire for his son to do well was palpable.

"So, when the people from the Divine Cult have given us this opportunity. Shouldn’t you, Sampyeong, be the one to seize it!"

At his words praising the Divine Cult, Jin Hayeon and the bodyguards beside me smiled with satisfaction.

"Truly, opening the school as Young Lord Ilmok suggested seems to be having a great effect in inspiring the faith of the believers."

"......"

The school was just something I had come up with to avoid extra work from the Master. It was a task that would be easy to pass on to someone else.

"Ahem."

Feeling awkward, I let out a loud, dry cough. The father who had been scolding his son turned his gaze toward us.

"Oh my! Welcome! I’m so sorry. I didn’t even realize I had customers."

"Hahaha. It’s quite all right. Could you perhaps prepare two rooms and some dinner for us?"

"Yes! I’ll get them ready right away."

"Hmm, and could you also prepare some bathwater?"

The innkeeper showed a cautious attitude at my question.

"Well... the bathwater is quite expensive, would that be all right?"

The Xinjiang region did not have much water. Minimal drinking water or enough to wash one’s face was one thing, but preparing enough water to submerge one’s entire body was bound to be costly. Drenched in sweat, however, I had no intention of giving up on a bath.

"Don’t worry, I have plenty of money."

I shook the wallet I had received from the Main Headquarters. The father’s face, which had been clouded with worry for his son, brightened up.

{sep}

Early the next morning, we had breakfast at the inn and left. Despite being worked like a dog yesterday, I felt fine aside from a little fatigue.

*This body is really, damnably sturdy. This must be why the Master took a liking to me. Damn it.*

As I strolled through the town with such thoughts, I saw people in clothes embroidered with the character for ‘demon’ (魔) doing odd jobs. The demonic practitioners of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult Branch were helping the townspeople.

"Hmm. It seems the demonic practitioners of the Branch helping the residents in place of the children is proceeding without a hitch."

After observing the scene like unrelated strangers, we headed straight for the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult Branch. A small, temporary space had been built next to it. That was the temporary school for the children. Since I could not observe a class while hiding my identity, I cautiously approached the building.

"Now. Listen and repeat after me! The sea is salty and the rivers plain!"

"The sea is salty and the rivers plain!"

"And what this means is~"

Through the paper window, I could hear the Principal and the children studying.

"The first character means sea, and the second character means salty, so it means the sea is salty, and the third character means river. The last means plain, so the river is mild! In other words, the sea is salty and the river is mild!"

"Principal! What’s a sea~?"

"What does the sea look like?"

"But why does the character for river (河) look like that?"

It was a gathering of young children, so they chattered endlessly the moment the Principal finished an explanation.

"Now! Quiet, quiet!"

"Principal! Is the sea really salty?"

"No way! How can water be salty?"

"Maybe they put salt in it!"

"Salt is so expensive, why would they put salt in it!"

"The sea must be really small then!"

"Quiet, quiet!! The sea is a hu~ge body of water! Much bigger than a river!"

The Principal’s much louder voice erupted, piercing through the children’s chatter. As expected, the children were oblivious.

"No way!!"

"If it’s much bigger than a river, how do they make it salty?"

"They must have a lot of salt!!"

"Stupid! My Daddy told me salt is super expensive!"

"Hmph! You’ve never even tasted salt! I have, you know~?"

For a moment, only the sound of children chattering echoed out. I wondered why the Principal’s voice had gone silent.

"You little brats!!!"

A roar infused with inner arts burst forth. At the same time, killing intent and demonic qi began to surge from inside the school.

Only then did I realize I had forgotten one very important problem. The demonic practitioners of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult learn demonic art. Naturally, the martial artists of the Branch would also be learning demonic art, and demonic practitioners suffer from mental illness as a side effect.

In other words, I had entrusted the education of young children to a bunch of madmen.
