The knights guarding the front of the knight order's training ground approached Catherine's carriage, then hesitated. This was because the first person to step out when the carriage door opened was not Catherine, but Dmitri.

The eldest son of the Bello family, which had produced outstanding scholars for generations. The most coveted groom, desired by all for his good family and striking looks. In terms of family alone, the marriage between Dmitri and Estella could be seen as the perfect match.

However, the problem was the severe disparity in their appearances.

Of course, Estella had lost a lot of weight recently and was very different from before, but based on looks alone, Dmitri was a better match for Catherine.

A picture-perfect match, at that.

That was why the knights' gazes toward Dmitri were anything but favorable. In the eyes of the knights, Dmitri was a shameless scoundrel who, despite being engaged to Lady Estella, was now making a move on Lady Catherine. It went without saying that Gai was at the center of these disapproving glares.

While the knights hesitated, Catherine stepped out of the carriage, escorted by Dmitri.

"Hello, am I coming by too often?"

"Not at all, my lady. We're grateful that you think of us so often."

Seeing Catherine's bright smile, like the morning sun, washed away the unpleasant feeling they had because of Dmitri. At the same time, Dmitri's eyes narrowed in displeasure. He was annoyed and hated that other men were looking at Catherine.

However, he was in no position to openly shield her, which only irritated him more.

When Estella jumped down from the carriage, Dmitri finally remembered that he hadn't escorted her.

"I was going to help you... You should have waited a moment."

He said it quite kindly, as many eyes were watching.

However, Estella only glanced at him before walking past, striding toward the training ground. Her blonde hair, tied tightly in a ponytail, swished back and forth like a horse's tail.

Watching Estella walk away without a backward glance as soon as she reached the Black Lily Knights' training ground, Dmitri let out a disbelieving scoff.

Dmitri stared blankly at Estella as she walked away, her tightly bound hair swishing, then sighed deeply and strode over to grab her wrist.

In that instant, Estella reflexively twisted his wrist and pressed down swiftly with her fingertips.

"......"

Caught by the sudden counterattack, Dmitri cried out before he even had time to be surprised. His twisted wrist hurt too much, regardless of his will.

"Estella, what is the meaning of this!"

At Dmitri's shout, not only Catherine but also the surrounding knights focused their attention on the two of them.

Estella stared silently for a few seconds at Dmitri, whose face was flushed red, then let go of his wrist.

"How could you suddenly grab and twist my wrist like that!"

"You're the one who grabbed me suddenly."

Estella replied indifferently, dusting off her palms as if something dirty had touched them.

Dmitri was so dumbfounded he was speechless. Who was the one who, until recently, had been fidgeting anxiously, wanting to be by his side even for a moment!

Can a person change this much all of a sudden?

Dumbfounded, Dmitri suddenly smirked as if he had realized something. His wrist, which Estella had grabbed and pressed, still throbbed, but he figured it just meant she was that angry.

It was surprising that she had such a sharp side to her. However, he wasn't one to fall for such a shallow trick.

"Hah, Estella. Have you changed your strategy to pretending to be indifferent now?"

What nonsense is this?

Estella's thoughts came to a halt for a moment, unable to comprehend what he was spouting. Dmitri looked at the speechless Estella with pity and forced a smile.

Sorry, but for now, this was the best consideration and kindness he could offer Estella.

"Estella. Just because you act like this doesn't mean I'll suddenly become interested in you."

"......"

Wow, Estella, just how ridiculously kind, no, stupid were you?

Not only were you trembling in fear, unable to say a peep while that bitch of a sister openly cheated with your fiancé, but you were treated like this on top of it all?

Estella felt like she was going crazy, both pitying and furious with the Estella of the past.

Being only too kind wasn't being kind.

It was being a fool.

"I understand how you feel, but my feelings are..."

"I get it, so stop."

Estella warned him politely, suppressing the urge to rip off Dmitri's lips as he spouted his nonsense.

However, to Dmitri, who believed the entire world revolved around him, even that looked like an attempt to get his attention.

Honestly, this popularity of mine.

"But don't worry. I'll keep our engagement as it is."

"*Is this fucking bastard crazy?*"

In the end, Yoon Seoyi's fury easily overcame Estella's kindness.

At the vulgar curse he had never heard before, Dmitri's eyes widened to the size of plums.

Estella took a bold step forward, toward the pale-faced Dmitri. Now, not even a handspan of distance separated the two.

"You..."

"What?"

"If you talk to me one more time."

She was a woman a full head shorter than him. However, Dmitri couldn't say a word in the face of Estella's blazing eyes as she looked up at him with an expression he had never seen before. It was an overwhelming feeling.

"You'll die," Estella said in a low voice.

"...What?"

"You'll die. So go play patty-cake with your pretty little thing and have fun. Just don't mess with me."

She wanted to curse him out even more, but she barely managed to shut her mouth due to Estella's instinctively kind nature.

He looked around in disbelief, but the others seemed too far away to have heard Estella's voice.

Estella looked at Dmitri, who was blinking as if in shock, let out a deep sigh, then spun around and walked toward the training ground.

Strength training was indeed the best way to relieve stress.

The Winterfall Forest was beautiful.

It was because of this very forest that he had returned here after wandering throughout the empire. Ciel slowly inhaled, then exhaled as if savoring it.

CLIP-CLOP, CLIP-CLOP, CLIP-CLOP.

Behind him, the horses his companions rode followed in rhythm, as if enjoying the leisurely pace.

"Captain, let's sleep under a roof tonight instead of on the ground. I'm at the point where I can't even remember what a bed looks like."

Ciel turned to look back at Bruno's grumbling voice and grinned. "Who was it that slept just fine last night, snoring away?"

Bruno, the tallest and largest of the group, also had the loudest snores. Riri was convinced that the reason monsters didn't attack them even when they slept in the middle of the forest was because of Bruno's damn snoring.

"Me? Where would you find someone who sleeps as quietly as I do?"

"Captain, should I just glue that bastard's mouth shut? I have some adhesive left over from fixing my boots. It sticks leather well, so I'm sure it'll stick that bastard's snout shut too."

As Bruno played innocent, Riri, who was riding alongside him, fumed. His already upturned eyes shot up even higher as he glared at Bruno. Facing Riri's murderous glare, Bruno subtly turned his head away.

"Adhesive is expensive. Save it."

"Captain!"

Bruno flared up at Ciel's added comment, but he just sighed deeply at the sound of the others snickering.

"No one's on my side. No one."

"Marquis Cardein's castle is just a little further. Let's impose on him for the night."

"I don't think they'll let us in at the marquis's castle..." Another member of the group, Airi, said in an indifferent voice. As always, his expressionless face was filled with boredom.

"Why not?"

When Ciel asked, puzzled as if he didn't understand, Airi slowly looked over the group before answering nonchalantly.

"Because we look like beggars."

"...You really don't know how to beat around the bush, do you."

Ciel said with a complicated expression, but Airi just shrugged his shoulders. Unlike his cute appearance, Airi was blunt and straightforward. Anyone who saw him for the first time would think he was a girl of about fifteen, based on his small and slight appearance.

Airi's dark brown hair, almost black, stuck out messily above his shoulders, and his tightly closed lips were adorable like a chick's beak. His large, clear eyes, reminiscent of a baby kitten's, further highlighted Airi's cute appearance. However, he was a proper twenty-two-year-old, and the words he hated most in the world were 'cute' and 'you look young'.

"Even if we look like beggars, don't you think they'd listen if Airi went and asked? If Airi goes and bats his eyes a few times, how could they not open the castle gates! So cute..."

Bruno, who had been chattering excitedly, felt a cold stare and shut his mouth.

"Captain, I'm telling you, let's just glue his snout shut. How does he make the same mistake every time? Is his head just for decoration? No, what kind of decoration looks like that. It's just a lump of dirt! A lump of dirt!"

Bruno couldn't say anything back to Riri's harsh nagging. This was because Airi's already pouting lips were sticking out a handspan further.

Haru and Kyle quietly rode their horses, listening to their companions' affectionate conversation. Bruno's lips were in danger of being sewn shut several times a day, Airi's pout grew longer by the day, and Riri's way of speaking grew rougher and rougher.

Listening to the sounds of his subordinates' chatter, Ciel urged his horse to go faster.

He too was extremely tired from the long journey and wanted to lie down somewhere warm as soon as possible.

In the distance, the magnificent gray castle of Marquis Cardein slowly came into view. From somewhere, the chirping of birds could be heard.

Ciel's lips curved into a gentle smile, feeling a premonition that something good was about to happen.
