As I expected, the task Master intended to give me was related to the last company dinner. It wasn't an order to just blindly create some system and reporting format, however.

"Your opinion was quite excellent, but you made it without a full grasp of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's actual situation. Therefore, let us first understand the state of our Divine Cult."

Master instructed me to first assess the situation in the regions the Divine Cult governed. In other words, he was telling me to go on a business trip.

My Master, whose kindness made me grind my teeth, thankfully gave me two days before I had to leave.

"Shouldn't you say your goodbyes to that child? She would likely be quite anxious if you suddenly disappeared."

After saying this, Master looked at me with an intrigued expression. I couldn't understand the intention behind his gaze. Regardless, thanks to Master's consideration, I was able to say goodbye to Seon'ah the next day.

"Can't you not go…?"

"Keuk..."

The little girl's pleading upward gaze made my heart ache. The saddest reason, however, wasn't her cuteness.

*I don't want to go either!!!*

The fact that I was being forced on this business trip made it doubly painful. Still, I couldn't exactly explain that I was being dragged along.

"Seon'ah. Oppa is a disciple of the most exalted one."

I couldn't curse Master in front of Jin Hayeon, who watched me with a cold gaze from the side.

"Because I’m a disciple of the great Heavenly Demon, I have to do various things too. So, even when Oppa is away, you have to study and train hard every day."

"Why…?"

"Because I'll probably get busier? It's nice playing with you, Seon'ah, but if you get smarter and your martial arts improve, we can work together instead of just playing, right? Then we can always be together, can't we?"

"Ah!"

Seon'ah's already large eyes widened as if she’d had an epiphany, and she nodded her head vigorously. Charmed by her cute appearance, I stroked her head and then held out my pinky finger to Seon'ah again.

"I'll buy you a present when I come back from this trip. So you study hard too, Seon'ah. Got it?"

"Yes!! I'll definitely become a great person and work with Oppa!"

After my brief farewell with Seon'ah, it was time for training with Master again.

"You have mastered all the forms of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword's techniques, so today, let us learn the movement arts and their forms."

As he spoke, Master strolled around the Wind Cliff Pavilion with light footwork. Surprisingly, deep footprints appeared wherever he walked.

"Follow these footprints and perform the movements this old man has demonstrated."

Following Master's guidance, I flitted about, twisting my body like a dancer for about two hours. By the time the movements had more or less settled into my body, the training session ended.

Then, early the next morning.

"Haaam."

Fully prepared, I let out a long yawn and headed somewhere with Jin Hayeon. She carried a bundle on her back that held books for me to study, along with ink and brushes. I followed her as we walked through the interior of the Demonic Cult for a while.

Before long, we arrived at a place where a large carriage was parked.

"hohoho. Welcome."

Master, who slept little in the mornings due to his age, had arrived first and was already aboard the carriage. Surprisingly, Master was also joining this business trip.

Once Jin Hayeon and I boarded, a man who served as both bodyguard and driver began to steer the carriage. As we passed the Divine Cult's valedictorian and entered the forest path, the lush green vegetation outside the carriage window captured my attention.

*Maybe because I'm with Master, it feels relaxed.*

It felt less like a business trip and more like a leisurely journey. I spent a good while that morning enjoying the forest scenery from inside the carriage.

"......"

The carriage suddenly stopped. Master looked at me and spoke.

"You may get out now."

"???"

There wasn't even a village in sight. Why was he telling me to get out? Though puzzled, I followed Master's order and exited, only to face a still more puzzling sight.

*Why am I the only one getting out?*

As if reading my expression, Master spoke from his seat in the carriage.

"From now on, you will run."

"!?"

Before I could say anything, the carriage started moving forward again.

*Crazy!?*

Startled, I quickly moved my feet and chased after the carriage. Strangely, it moved at a leisurely pace, as if matching my running speed. When I barely managed to reach its side, Master spoke.

"Ahem. Have you forgotten the movement arts this old man taught you yesterday? This is also part of your training. You must move according to the motions of the movement arts when you run, no matter when or in what situation. Until those movements are completely ingrained in your body."

Only then did I fully grasp the situation. The two days Master had given me weren't for saying goodbye to Seon'ah. He needed time to teach me the movements of the movement arts.

*No, maybe the reason he didn't call for me right after the dinner was also because of that.*

Master had a plan for everything. A plan to work me efficiently. Just then, Master's fiery command rang out.

"Ahem! Your form is wrong. Concentrate. In a real battle, do you think the enemy's sword will avoid you just because your stamina is low? Your form must not falter, no matter how difficult the situation."

It was a damn shitty start to a business trip.

{sep}

"Ahem. The angle of your waist is off."

"I apologize. It is because this humble girl's external arts education was lacking."

How long had I been running, listening to Master's nagging and Jin Hayeon's chiming in from the side? My mind was growing hazy.

"......"

The carriage running alongside me came to a stop.

"You may get on now."

"Heok. Heok."

Out of breath and ready to collapse, I staggered onto the carriage. Jin Hayeon offered me a gourd flask, and I drank the water.

"Regulate your breathing with qi circulation. It may be uncomfortable for now, but there is nothing better than qi circulation for recovering a tired body and stabilizing your breath."

At Master's command, which sounded stricter than usual, I sighed inwardly and sat cross-legged on the carriage floor.

"Seueup. Huu."

I took a deep breath, guiding the energy in the air deep into my lungs. I controlled that energy, circulating it through my acupoints according to the heart law of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, gradually transforming it into demonic qi.

*Crazy.*

I almost flinched. I wasn't sure if it was because I was short of breath from the exercise or because of the carriage's vibrations. The energy ran wilder than usual, and a strange pain came every time it passed through my acupoints.

Thinking about it now, what I was learning was a demonic art. Unlike the stable heart laws of the orthodox sects, it was an unstable heart law where the slightest mistake could lead to qi deviation. It was severe enough to cause mental illness even when practiced carefully, so how much worse could this be?

However, abruptly stopping the circulation would be even more dangerous. Feeling as if I were walking on thin ice, I carefully circulated my inner arts.

"Seueueup... Hoo… ."

I focused my mind, repeating my breaths as deeply and slowly as possible. After repeating the Minor Heavenly Cycle a few times, the rampaging demonic qi gradually stabilized. I seized the opportunity to store the energy in my qi center.

*Perhaps because I concentrated so hard, my energy increased more than usual.*

Confirming the noticeable increase in my energy, I took one last deep breath and opened my eyes. A cold sensation spread across my back as sweat dried. I wondered if it was from the intense running or a cold sweat from excessive concentration.

"hohoho. As expected, I knew you could do it, Ilmok."

Master's voice entered my ears.

"…Did you intentionally make me perform qi circulation under dangerous conditions?"

I asked back in an unintentionally blunt voice. The crazy old man smiled with satisfaction and stroked his beard.

"When living as a martial artist, situations where you must perform qi circulation while your body or mind is unstable often arise."

"…Is there really a need to practice that now?"

I asked if it wouldn't be better to do it after reaching a certain level, but Master shook his head.

"You must think of it the other way around. After becoming a martial artist, even a very trivial problem can become unmanageable. Between a small stream overflowing and a great river flooding, which do you think is more dangerous?"

"So, you're saying that because my inner arts are still shallow, you had me practice this dangerous energy circulation in advance?"

"hohoho. You understand this old man's meaning well. Also, I gave the order because your inner arts are not yet that great. If a problem had occurred, this old man could have taken immediate measures. If you had accumulated as much inner arts as the Sixth, even this old man would have found it difficult to guarantee your safety."

As expected, Master had a plan for everything.

*It would be great if he would just share that plan.*

Just as I had that regretful thought, the carriage suddenly stopped with a 'CLUNK'. I wore a puzzled expression. Master stared at me blankly for a moment before opening his mouth.

"What are you doing? Not getting out?"

"Have we arrived already?"

"You've rested enough now, so shouldn't you resume your movement arts training?"

"……"

Speechless, I looked back and forth between Master and Jin Hayeon for a moment. I sighed and stepped down from the carriage.

*Fuck.*

That word kept lingering on the tip of my tongue.

{sep}

Fortunately or unfortunately, the village was not far. By the time I was on my third round of movement arts training, the destination was visible in the distance.

"Let us end this training session when we arrive at that village."

Perhaps because the destination was in sight, I dashed toward the village like someone who had discovered an oasis in the desert. The funny thing was, just as Master intended, the movements of the movement arts had become completely ingrained in my body.

I wasn't sure if it was because I had run for my life while performing the movements, or if this body's talent was just absurdly outstanding. I no longer heard Master's nagging. Even when I ran with my mind zoned out, my body moved on its own according to the motions of the movement arts.

How long had I been running blindly, staring at the distant village? The small village gradually grew larger, and eventually, the carriage and I arrived at its outskirts.

"Giddy-up."

"......"

The carriage stopped. Master and Jin Hayeon, who had traveled comfortably inside, disembarked with calm steps. Drenched in sweat and half out of my mind, I entered the village with them.

"Hooray! Hooray! Long live!!"

Thunderous shouts from all around jolted my wandering mind back to its senses. I quickly looked around and saw people, who appeared to be villagers, all prostrating themselves on the ground and shouting.

"Oh, the Heavenly Demon!!"

"Please save us!!"

"Lead us not into evil!!"

Watching the scene gave me goosebumps.

*It's like they're really welcoming some kind of god, isn't it?*

Back at the Billion Mountains, the Main Headquarters of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, I had just accepted it as a heretic cult's base. However, I never expected such a fervent reaction from a village where ordinary people lived.

*Is this the power of a heretic?*

It was truly a terrifying sight.
