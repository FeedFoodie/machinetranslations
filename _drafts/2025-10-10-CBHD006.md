Master’s words were true. The moment I got involved with Master, my fate was sealed. I would either be chased by the Murim Alliance and killed by an advisor, or hunted by the imperial family and killed by an advisor.

The only option left was to survive as Master's disciple, but that was not comfortable either. I could still die in a year.

A renewed sense of urgency made me focus on the lesson. It was my first time learning the doctrines of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, but I quickly understood Jin Hayeon's explanation.

This was not because I was smart. I was simply used to studying.

*If I think of it in terms of Korean Neo-Confucianism, it's quite easy to understand.*

Connecting new information to something you already know is one of the best ways to learn. This is why instructors, professors, and teachers often use analogies. The human brain learns interconnected ideas more easily than completely separate concepts.

Of course, the doctrines of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult were not identical to Korean Neo-Confucianism. I had not memorized all the doctrines of Korean Neo-Confucianism either. I had studied national history, not theology.

It did not matter. I could understand the different parts by comparing them, thinking, *‘Oh, this part is different.’* For the parts completely unrelated to Korean Neo-Confucianism, I used other analogies or concepts to connect them.

{sep}

After morning studies and lunch, I began my external arts training as scheduled.

"We'll begin with the lower body," Jin Hayeon said with a blank expression. "It's a common saying, but the lower body is the foundation of all martial arts. It's common because it's important. This is an undeniable truth."

She then assumed the horse stance to demonstrate.

I might have an advantage in studying or preparing for exams, but she was clearly the expert in exercise and martial arts. I willingly imitated her movements.

*This is easier than I thought.*

I had experience going to the gym for health management in the modern world, though I often skipped it due to overtime fatigue or dealing with difficult complainants. In any case, holding the horse stance was not difficult. I did not feel off-balance, and my knees did not ache or my muscles strain.

As I held the stance comfortably, Jin Hayeon, who had been watching me, suddenly picked up a sandbag and placed it on my shoulders.

"......"

It became a little harder with the sandbag, but it still was not overwhelming. The external arts training continued.

"Please stand, Young Lord," Jin Hayeon instructed. "From now on, you'll hold the horse stance while I count to ten, then stand to rest briefly. We'll repeat this."

We repeated an action similar to squats.

"Now, you'll go around this training ground once. I'll demonstrate first."

It was like a punishment from my school days. We also did movements like the duck walk.

*Is this what you'd call a lunge?*

With that thought, I continued to punish my lower body with the sandbags on my shoulders.

GASP. GASP.

I thought it was nothing at first, but my breathing grew rough as the repetitions and sandbags piled up.

Watching me, Jin Hayeon suddenly asked an unexpected question. "Have you truly never practiced martial arts in your entire life?"

"That's right."

"Indeed. I think I understand why the Heavenly Demon chose you, Young Lord. I felt it last night while attending to your bath, but you have a truly outstanding physique."

As I expected, she had been analyzing my body during my bath last night. Cold sweat trickled down my spine as she spoke again.

"At least when it comes to external arts, it seems there will be no major problems with you entering the Demonic Academy in a year."

A sigh of relief escaped me. It felt like my life had been extended.

After that, she taught me various other external arts training methods. With each one, her praise emerged.

"Your muscular strength is hard to believe for someone who has never trained."

"It can truly be called a body blessed by the heavens, Young Lord."

But for some reason, the more her praise continued, the harder it was to tell if she was complimenting my body or praising the Heavenly Demon who found me.

"As expected, it seems the Heavenly Demon had a plan for everything."

"It seems the Heavenly Demon anticipated that you'd be more than capable of entering the Demonic Academy, Young Lord."

"The Heavenly Demon is the one who found you, Young Lord, while your talent was going to waste."

It seemed to be the latter.

*No wonder a person who seems so emotionless was pouring out compliments...*

{sep}

The external arts training lasted for two hours. Afterward, I simply washed the sweat from my face and ate the snack Jin Hayeon had brought.

*Hmm. They might not know the concept of protein, but I guess this is what you'd expect from a group of strategists?*

Surprisingly, the snacks were fruit and meat, not sweets. They seemed to know from experience that eating meat before or after exercise was helpful.

After the heavy snack, I opened the personal doctrine book I had organized in the morning and began to review. There was still about an hour until Master arrived.

I wondered if it would be better to schedule the external arts training right before Master's lesson and extend the morning study time, but exercising for four hours straight without a break seemed too difficult. On the other hand, if I trained early in the morning and then studied until my lesson...

*I'd be too exhausted to focus on the lesson.*

Studying while exhausted was inefficient. So, I chose to use this time to review instead of learning new material.

*Once I get used to the training, I'll have to adjust the schedule a bit.*

I rested my body and focused on reviewing the morning's material. Soon, I heard Jin Hayeon's voice from outside the door.

"I greet the Lord of All Demons."

I got up and opened the door. Master stood in the garden with a relaxed expression, while Jin Hayeon prostrated herself fully on the ground in a show of utmost respect.

"I greet you, Master."

I hurriedly bowed, following her lead.

"hohoho. That's enough. I hope this old man hasn't disturbed your studies."

"Not at all, Heavenly Demon," Jin Hayeon said, her forehead still touching the ground. "The Young Lord's insight is so outstanding that he has understood all of today's targeted material."

"Oho?" Master showed his interest at her words. "hohoho. I had some expectations, but it seems your insight is indeed quite outstanding."

Master laughed, pleased, and instructed Jin Hayeon and me to rise. As I tactfully got to my feet, Master spoke to me.

"If your insight is outstanding, then your understanding of martial arts should be outstanding as well. I'm truly looking forward to today's training. hohoho."

It was a strangely exciting moment. From the moment I realized this was a world where the murim actually existed, I had vowed not to get involved. It was a dangerous world where even ten lives would not be enough.

Paradoxically, however, I also wanted to try learning martial arts. Shooting palm wind, splitting boulders with a sword, running on water. The ability to physically do things that were impossible in the modern era piqued my curiosity. It was just that my life took precedence over my curiosity.

Now I had no choice. I had to learn martial arts, and since I had to, I felt a stir of interest.

Perhaps my eyes were shining, because Master laughed. "hohoho. First, there is something I must tell you before I teach you martial arts."

"Disciple. I'll listen attentively."

"Then I shall ask. Ilmok, do you know what the heathens of the Central Plains call the martial arts of our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult?"

I pondered the unexpected question for a moment, then answered on a hunch. "Don't they call it demonic art?"

"You're correct. Then, do you know why they call our martial arts demonic art?"

"Hmm. I believe it's just a stigma they attached to us to dishonor us."

"hohoho." Master laughed while stroking his beard, then shook his head. "That's about half-right. It's true that they tried to cast a stigma upon us, but there's another, greater reason why they call it demonic art."

"What is that?"

"It's because of the high risk of qi deviation. It's called demonic art because while the achievements are very fast, there are dangerous side effects."

Master finished his explanation and looked at me with a peaceful expression. Jin Hayeon, standing beside me, also wore her usual expressionless face. It seemed I was the only one who was flabbergasted.

"M-Master? Does that mean the demonic art I'm about to learn has dangerous side effects?"

"That is so," Master said, nodding with that same peaceful face.

Unable to hide my bewilderment, I asked the two of them, "W-why must I learn such dangerous martial arts?"

"hohoho. That's a very fundamental question I haven't heard in a long time."

"I apologize, Lord of All Demons. It's because this lowly one has not yet educated the Young Lord sufficiently."

As soon as Master finished speaking, Jin Hayeon suddenly began to prostrate herself, about to strike her head on the ground. A powerful wave of qi erupted from Master and forcibly stopped her.

"I'm not blaming you, so pay it no mind."

Master, who had momentarily exuded a terrifying aura, helped Jin Hayeon up. He then looked at me with a gentle expression.

*How am I supposed to live with this fear?*

What good was his gentle look now? At a single word, his servant tried to smash her head on the ground, and he unleashed a terrifying aura to stop her.

I swallowed hard against the creeping tension as Master asked me a question. "Have you perhaps heard for what reason our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult was created?"

I wracked my brain, recalling the material I had just reviewed. An ominous feeling that a huge disaster would occur if I got it wrong made my mind feel sharp and clear despite my fatigue.

"I heard it was to save the common people from villains like corrupt officials."

"hohoho. You know well. Then, think about it. The common people are suffering right now, so how can one leisurely learn martial arts? Even if one must endure side effects, isn't it right and proper to choose the path to become strong quickly?"

"......"

"That's why the heathens who call themselves the orthodox sects are hypocrites. How can those who claim to learn martial arts to protect the common people learn arts that take decades to become strong? They're nothing more than hypocrites who prioritize their own safety."

Listening to him, I felt my head spin.

*Somehow, that sounds plausible?*

Startled by the thought, I quickly shook my head. It seemed the diligent review had deepened my brainwashing, but I had no time for such thoughts. Master had asked another question.

"Do you perhaps remember when this old man first tested you, Ilmok?"

"I remember." It was a memory of almost dying. It would be stranger to forget it.

"At that time, this old man tested your character. The reason lies right here. As one is learning martial arts with side effects, only those with an upright character can overcome those side effects and achieve rapid progress."

"Ah...."

The thought that I was screwed came to me automatically.

*My character is upright?*
