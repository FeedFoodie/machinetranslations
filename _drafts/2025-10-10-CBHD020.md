That night, I sat in a corner of my room, scribbling in my diary by lamplight as usual.

Damn it. I kept using the side effect as an excuse with the old man, but it was already starting. I wrote down the day's frustrations for a moment, then stopped my brush and fell into thought.

*The handwriting… I don't like it.*

The Hangul characters I wrote with the brush looked crooked and uneven. I felt a sudden urge to tear the paper to shreds and start the diary again.

Damn side effect. No, when did I ever care about my handwriting? My writing had always been terrible, whether I used a brush or a pencil.

*I'm not writing for anyone else to see, so who cares what the handwriting looks like in my own diary! This handwriting is weird. I want to rip it all up right now.*

Angry, meaningless brushstrokes marked the very end of the diary entry. I vented my anger for a moment, wrestling with my own handwriting.

"......"

A thought suddenly struck me.

*Is there any reason to get stressed trying to ignore this side effect?*

Honestly, the side effect was currently just 'inducing irritation'. I could ignore it if I wanted to. That was why I kept writing in my diary, pushing past the annoyance.

However, it was going to get much more serious as my realm increased. If it was just irritation now, obsessive-compulsive symptoms would surely begin as my mastery of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword deepened.

I couldn't stop learning the demonic art now. I would die if I couldn't enter the Demonic Academy.

But separate from that, what was wrong with becoming a perfectionist? I only felt stressed because I saw it as a 'side effect'. What if I just surrendered to it and became a person who was perfect in everything?

"It might not be so bad?"

I finished my thought and nodded.

"That’s right. It’s only a problem because I think of it as a side effect. A positive mindset. Yep. Being positive is the best."

I muttered this to myself and tore up the diary. I tore out the parts I had written today, the parts with the handwriting I disliked.

Then, like an artisan crafting clothes with meticulous care, I poured my soul into the diary. I embedded each consonant and vowel one by one. I focused so intensely that beads of sweat formed on my forehead, even though I was only writing.

"Hoo. That was close," I muttered, hastily wiping the sweat from my forehead. If a drop had smudged the ink, I would have had to start all over again.

I averted the crisis and focused on the diary again with all my effort. I concentrated for a long time, losing track of the hours.

"......"

I finally finished writing everything in sharp, satisfactory handwriting and let out a deep sigh. My back and shoulders felt stiff from sitting tensed up for so long.

"Time to get some sleep."

For some reason, my mind felt much more at ease.

I led my weary body to the bed and lay down.

"......"

A sudden urge to urinate woke me in the middle of the night.

"Hah. A pit-style cremation room in the middle of the night is the worst."

I had been in this place for over a year, but I still couldn't get used to that. I complained to myself as I got up, then froze.

The night sky was dark. Moonlight peeked through the clouds and streamed through the paper-covered window, casting a faint light into the room, but in one spot, a person's shadow swallowed that faint light.

I hesitated, having discovered an unknown visitor in the dead of night.

The man’s face was shrouded in shadow. He looked at me, his eyes the only visible feature.

"So you saw me?"

"……"

It was the voice of Third Senior Brother Seo Wanpyeong.

*...He's finally come to kill me.*

A cold sweat trickled down my spine.

{sep}

I was speechless for a moment, but I quickly opened my mouth. There was a saying that you could survive a tiger’s den if you kept your wits about you. The tiger had invaded my home now, but doing something was better than nothing.

"What brings you here so late at night, Senior Brother?"

"It seems you’re not pleased with my visit."

At his negative tone, I tried my best to hide my surprise and answered calmly.

"How could that be? I’m just overjoyed that my most respected Senior Brother has come to visit. I was just curious why you came in the dead of night instead of during the day."

Lies spilled smoothly from my mouth, even as one part of my brain screamed about the threat to my life.

*I guess this is an occupational hazard, if you can call it that.*

My ingrained habit of dealing politely with difficult civil complainants had trained me well. I had reached a state where I could smile on the outside while cursing a storm on the inside.

My flattery seemed to work. Teeth appeared on Third Senior Brother's shadowy face. He was smiling. It was a twisted smile, but a smile nonetheless.

"It was to avoid the bodyguards."

"...I had bodyguards?"

*Is he talking about Jin Hayeon?*

I wondered about that, but Senior Brother's answer was unexpected.

"I’m not talking about your bodyguards. I’m talking about my bodyguards."

"...Are you saying you came here in the dead of night to avoid your own bodyguards, Senior Brother?"

*What kind of bullshit is this?*

I swallowed the thought as the crazy assassin Senior Brother resolved my confusion.

"That is correct. My bodyguards aren’t there to protect me. They’re there to protect those who I might kill."

"……"

*So, coming here while avoiding them means he came to kill me, right?*

The cold sweat running down my spine felt like a rainstorm.

*Should I scream?*

Jin Hayeon would come to save me, but my head would probably be chopped off before he arrived.

*If I draw my sword, can I hold out until Jin Hayeon gets here?*

The Soul-Reaping Quick Draw I learned today came to mind. It was a technique specialized for blocking a surprise attack the moment the sword is drawn. My sword was right next to my bed, just as I had been taught.

However.

*Can I really block that crazy assassin's attack?*

The gap between me, a new Demonic Initiate, and my Senior Brother, who was at the Ultimate Demon Realm, had to be unfathomable. My mind kept sounding negative warnings.

I racked my brain, trying to find a way to survive. For some reason, the crazy murderous bastard spoke again.

"It was always like that. It seems Master doesn’t trust me. Well, I suppose it’s because I’m too lacking to be a disciple of the Heavenly Demon. However, you were different."

"M-Me, you mean?"

"That’s right. I heard Master recently gave a new directive to all the Branches. It was about creating a school, I believe, and Master was very proud, saying the idea came from you."

"……"

So, Master praised me, and now my paranoid Senior Brother came to kill me out of jealousy.

*What kind of butterfly effect is this!!!!!*

It was so unfair I felt like I was going to lose my mind.

At that moment, the shadow of the psychopathic murderer moved. The shadow of his right arm shot forward.

*He's trying to kill me!*

I couldn't just accept death. I quickly moved my arm to grab the scabbard beside my bed, but his right hand thrust forward much faster than I could draw my sword.

But what was this? The paranoid murderer’s right hand wasn't holding a sword. It held only some torn and crumpled scraps of paper.

"???"

I hesitated, stopping my attempt to draw my sword at the sight of the paper. Senior Brother spoke.

"So, I came because I was curious. I wondered what the difference was between me and the youngest disciple that Master is so proud of. If I moved openly, the bodyguards might make a strange report to Master, so I came secretly. I cannot disappoint Master again."

"I-Is that so?"

"And when I came in, I found these papers on the floor. I wondered what the youngest disciple, the pride of our Master, had been writing before falling asleep, but it was a script I could not read."

Only then did I realize what the paper scraps were.

*The diary I tore up and crumpled!*

I was so focused on rewriting the diary that I had forgotten about the torn pages. My Soul-Reaping Heartless sword intent was still at the Demonic Initiate Realm, so the obsessive symptoms only appeared when I fixated on something. It wasn't at a level where I would pathologically clean, so I had just left them there.

"Can you tell me what is written here?"

I gave Senior Brother the same excuse I had given Jin Hayeon. It was a code I created for my studies.

"Studying the doctrine in such a way. It’s a method I never would have thought of. Indeed, you are completely different from someone like me. Then again, I suppose I’m the one who’s worthless."

I felt like if this continued, he would kill me just because he didn't like me, so I quickly spoke up.

"Worthless? What are you talking about, Senior Brother! How can you say such a thing, you who I respect second most!"

"You respect me second most?"

"That’s right. If we exclude the great Heavenly Demon, our Master, then I respect you, Third Senior Brother, the most!"

At my answer, his teeth were revealed from the shadows again. It was a somewhat crooked smile. However, Senior Brother quickly erased it and spoke in a gloomy voice.

"Respecting someone like me. You must be mocking me."

His tone was full of thorns, and I had a thought.

*Because of the side effect of the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art, his self-esteem is completely shattered.*

If I could boost his self-esteem, even a little, maybe he would leave without killing me. It seemed like a ridiculous idea, but it was better than doing nothing. Praise could even make a whale dance, after all!

"I heard you entered the Demonic Academy as valedictorian and graduated as valedictorian, Senior Brother. I’m just a nobody whose entrance isn’t even certain."

"A Junior Brother that Master has high hopes for could easily enter as valedictorian. Besides, Eldest Senior Brother and the sixth one were valedictorians too."

"I-I heard from Master! About how much he trusts you, Third Senior Brother!"

"Master said that?"

The perpetually negative Senior Brother finally reacted at the mention of Master.

"He told me in passing and said never to mention it to the other Senior Brothers. It seems Master doesn’t praise us directly so we don’t become arrogant. In fact, I’ve never received praise from Master while learning from him either."

I could immediately recall several times Master had praised me, but I said it confidently as if it had never happened.

"What you mentioned earlier, Senior Brother, was also the first time I’d heard of it. I was also worried that I might be too inadequate compared to Master and my Senior Brothers."

"A clever Junior Brother like you had such worries?"

"Me, clever? I’m always being scolded by Master, and while scolding me, he always told me to learn from you, Third Senior Brother."

"...Master said that?"

As he asked again, the teeth in his mouth repeatedly appeared and disappeared from view. The corners of his mouth were twitching. Convinced this was my chance, I didn't stop talking.

"That’s right, Senior Brother! Your Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art is truly the world’s number one assassination art. He said you could take care of the Chairman of the Murim Alliance if you set your mind to it, and that your existence alone puts Master’s mind at ease."

"Your towering height and handsome appearance are also endlessly enviable, Senior Brother. I really want to be like you when I grow up. Hahaha."

I flapped my gums for a long time, desperately searching for anything to praise as if my brain was on fire.

"Hahahaha. I truly never imagined that Master and Youngest Junior Brother thought of me that way. I always thought you were all ignoring me, thinking I was just some gloomy guy."

"Ahahaha. Th-That’s a misunderstanding, Senior Brother. How could I ever think such a thing?"

Third Senior Brother's expression finally brightened as he burst into laughter. He nodded and laughed in satisfaction, then suddenly tilted his head and asked a question.

"But, Youngest."

"Yes, Senior Brother."
