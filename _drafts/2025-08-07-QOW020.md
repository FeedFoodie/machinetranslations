What appeared in the green forest was a Howler, a giant, white-furred, wolf-type monster that traveled in large packs. Though their intelligence was low, their cooperative skills were excellent, allowing them to work together in battle. With powerful jaws, sharp teeth, and large claws, they were creatures that could easily tear a human to shreds.

AWOOOOO! AWOOOOONG!

As soon as they arrived in the forest, the cries of Howlers could be heard from all directions. It was the sound of them calling their comrades and arranging their ranks.

"They're Grade 3 monsters! Don't let your guard down! If even one gets past us, the people of the domain will be hurt!"

When Lucius shouted from the front of the formation, the knights expertly spread out into their positions.

"Stay by my side. Do not, under any circumstances, charge ahead. My priority is to eliminate the monsters. Protecting you, milady, comes after that."

"Don't worry. I can take care of myself," Estella replied, her sword held ready as she stared straight ahead at Lucius's low-voiced words. Lucius let out a small sigh as he watched Estella, who was burning with the will to fight.

*What on earth was the Marquis thinking?*

He had insisted that he absolutely could not take Estella on a monster hunt, but Marquis Cassian's stance had been firm.

> "Let Estella do as she wishes."

"They're coming. Get ready."

Estella nodded. This was, after all, her second monster hunt.

As expected of a highly trained group, the knights' movements were swift and systematic. Although the Howlers were monsters that moved as a pack under a leader's command, their coordination was no match for that of the Black Lily Knights.

The knights moved as if they were a single body.

The moment a Howler leaped to attack, they instantly formed a defensive wall with their massive shields, then mercilessly slashed at the creatures' legs and bellies through the gaps.

The knights herded the Howlers as if trapping fish in a net.

However, not all the Howlers could be contained. The ones the knights missed became the responsibility of the mercenary groups positioned behind them.

Ciel and the Valiant Squirrels cleanly dispatched the Howlers that came running toward their pre-assigned positions, then occupied a moderately open area and took care of the escaping Howlers.

All the mercenary groups did their part, but the Squirrel Mercenaries' movements were on another level. Their numbers were ridiculously small compared to the other groups, but they had no openings.

In particular, the performance of the mage, Airi, was nothing short of dazzling. It was rare for a mercenary group to even include a mage, whose services were worth a fortune, and even if one did, it was usually a low-level mage.

This was because even creating a fist-sized fireball above one's palm was enough to be recruited by the Imperial Palace or the Magic Tower for a high price.

"Airi's all excited again!"

Riri grinned, watching Airi run wild like a fish in water. Airi rarely ever smiled.

He didn't show his emotions outwardly, so in the three-plus years they had been together, the number of times Riri had seen him smile could be counted on one hand.

But now, Airi was smiling brightly, as if he was happy. That was because monster hunting was one of the things Airi enjoyed most.

VWOOOM!

An unsuspecting wolf leaped into the magic circle Airi had created. It had intended to swallow Airi in a single gulp. The Howler, drooling at the sight of the delicious-looking prey, only realized something was wrong when flames began to lick at its paws.

As the panicked creature spun in place, Airi's magic ring scanned its body like a scanner, leaving only a pile of blackened charcoal in its wake.

"I told you not to burn the fur, Airi," Kyle said with an indifferent expression, throwing a shuriken at a Howler rushing toward him.

While dealing with the Howlers, Ciel found himself extremely preoccupied with Estella, who was fighting both alongside and separately from the knights.

Her swordsmanship was a bit clumsy, but the way she swiftly rolled to dodge or counterattack was surprisingly masterful. She seemed like someone who instinctively knew how to use her body.

*Was it just that she hadn't learned swordsmanship? Was she a natural?*

"Captain, where are you looking!"

At Bruno's shout, Ciel tore his gaze away from Estella and swung his sword. The Howler's massive head tumbled to the ground. As it was three to four times larger than a normal wolf, its head was also huge.

It rolled quite a distance.

The head, now separated from its body, slowly closed its wide-open mouth and let out its last, thin breath.

"I told you not to damage the hide."

The disappointed voice of Kyle, the mercenary group's treasurer, was heard, but Ciel paid it no mind. Of course, a hide with the head attached would sell for several times more, but the fur itself was still traded at a fairly high price.

Ciel's gaze naturally found Estella again. She was in the middle of stabbing her sword into the side of a Howler that was lunging at a knight from behind. The knight who had narrowly escaped death immediately beheaded the beast and offered his thanks to Estella. The two of them then naturally teamed up, as if they had become a team, and took down one more Howler.

After their success, she grinned at her partner knight, which made her round face look even rounder.

"What, can't take your eyes off her? Aish, I got blood all over my hair!"

Riri approached and asked with a playful expression, then freaked out when he saw the blood in his hair.

"You got more on your face."

"My hair is more precious than my face."

Riri grabbed his blood-stained hair and rubbed it on his sleeve in frustration. It was no use, of course, and Riri despaired. All the while, Kyle was repeatedly nagging Airi not to burn the Howlers to death, but near Airi, there were already three Howlers that had been turned to piles of ash.

"So, have you fallen for her?"

Riri gave up on cleaning his hair and asked with a sly grin. While one side was in chaos fighting Howlers, the two of them were as calm as villagers out for a stroll.

"Aren't her movements strange?"

Ciel answered Riri's question with another question. Riri, who had been intent on teasing him, had no choice but to look toward Estella at Ciel's serious expression.

"She's pretty good."

He had thought she would be slow because she was a bit chubby, but she was surprisingly agile.

"Just like a squirrel."

At Ciel's words, spoken while he watched Estella, the corners of Riri's eyes, which had been downcast because of his hair, shot up in excitement.

*The captain is comparing a woman to a squirrel? He'd never even used that word for Airi, who had the cutest face in the world?*

Riri's mind raced with surging questions. Feeling a pleasant sense of excitement for the first time in a while, Riri couldn't help but whistle.

"A squirrel?"

"Just saying. Behind you."

At Ciel's casually tossed words, Riri immediately spun around and drew his bow at an approaching Howler.

THWACK!

The heavy, steel-tipped arrow pierced the beast's head in one shot. Despite it being a bow that even grown men would struggle to lift, Riri ran over as lightly as a feather and easily pulled the arrow from the beast's head with one hand.

Blood and brains clung thickly to the arrowhead, but he shook it off as if dusting a rug, then immediately changed direction and pierced another Howler's head.

"Yes, the earhole!"

When the arrow he just shot went straight through its earhole, Riri pumped his fist and shouted. Kyle, who was nagging Airi for incinerating yet another one, looked up and gave him a thumbs-up. Having minimized the damage to the hide, Riri now shouted "The nostril!" and fired his bow, but failed spectacularly. It hit the middle of the beast's cheek.

"Still better than having its head cut off, right?"

The remark was clearly aimed at Ciel, and Kyle once again gave a thumbs-up.

"They don't see me as their captain."

Ciel grumbled, then thrust his sword into the mouth of a Howler that had opened its maw wide as if to devour him, and pulled it back out. The tip of Ciel's sword pierced through the top of its head.

"Still made a hole! What's the squirrel lady up to?"

Riri grinned, teasing Ciel.

The total number of Howlers killed by the knights and mercenaries that day was fifty-seven. The knights killed thirty-two, and the mercenaries killed the remaining twenty-five, ten of which were taken down by the Squirrel Mercenaries. The mercenary group with the fewest members had killed the most monsters.

It was a major reversal for the Squirrel Mercenaries, who had been subtly looked down upon for their cute name and small numbers.

Everyone stared in amazement, but Ciel's party paid no mind to the others' gazes, as if this was a common occurrence. Jack and his men, who had been gleefully mocking the Squirrel Mercenaries before entering the forest, couldn't even turn their heads in their direction, their faces bright red.
