"Die? What do you mean? Are you saying the Master is going to kill me?"

The absurd situation surprised me, but she remained perfectly calm. Her beautiful voice, like rolling jade beads, flowed out once more.

"Why would the Heavenly Demon, the Lord of All Demons and our Savior, ever harm his disciple?"

But those jade beads held no emotion. They sounded only cold.

"However, a disciple chosen by the heavens cannot be Incompetent. I, who failed to assist that disciple properly, will lose my life."

She spoke of her own death without a hint of emotion. No, a madness dwelled deep within her seemingly emotionless eyes.

"But even if I die, the fault of having an Incompetent disciple won't be washed away."

I had forgotten. Before being my attendant, this woman with her beautiful voice and appearance was something else.

"If I'm going to die anyway, wouldn't it be better for me to kill Young Lord Ilmok and be branded a heretic? That would cleanse the Supreme Lord's error."

She was one of the religious fanatics, deeply engrossed in a heretic religion.

Her cold tone felt like a splash of cold water on my head. My flowery thoughts of becoming a Cult Leader and living a sweet life vanished, and my mind grew much calmer.

"Then we don't have time for this. First, what kind of test must I pass to enter the Demonic Academy?"

Ultimately, I had returned to being an examinee. I had been an examinee twice before, from my high school days to my time studying for the civil service exam. This was nothing special.

*Well, back then there wasn't a crazy penalty like getting my head chopped off for failing.* The important thing was that I had to pass the test. One of the most important things in an examinee's life was strategy. I had to create the most efficient plan possible, especially with a short time limit of one year.

However, she responded to my question with strange nonsense.

"You can't begin your studies today. The Heavenly Demon ordered the Young Lord to rest, and I must follow that command."

Then she shouldn't have threatened me with death in a year. It was absurd sophistry. If I were still a civil servant or a waiter, I might have been flustered and dragged along, but things were different now.

I might be on a one-year death clock, but I was still the disciple of the Cult Leader this woman worshipped as a god, and I knew the magic phrase a person in power used to corner a subordinate.

"So, if I rest today and happen to fail that test, can you take responsibility for it?"

It was the ultimate sentence that helped create a rigid civil service society obsessed with passing the buck.

*So, if something goes wrong, will you take responsibility?*

To think I would be the one saying the phrase that had tormented me so much.

She replied to my question with an emotionless face.

"Didn't I already tell you? If you fail, I'll lose my life."

She was trying to say she would take responsibility, but she was incredibly naive.

"Is this a problem that can be solved by just one person like you dying? This is a matter that will stain the great Master's achievements. Do you really think it can be resolved with the life of a single attendant? Is the Master's reputation worth so little to you?"

*Do you think this will be solved just by you losing your position?*

The moment the attack reached that point, no one could escape the argument of responsibility.

Just as I expected, her eyes trembled for a moment. She hadn't anticipated this line of attack.

"That's why I said I would risk being branded a heretic and kill the Young Lord."

She threatened me again, using my life as collateral. In the past, I might have been afraid. However, I had already died once and been tested by a far more dangerous hand seal Master than her. From my perspective, this was nothing.

"Hmph. If you put it that way, wouldn't it mean the Master's judgment was foolish? He assigned a heretic capable of murdering his disciple as that disciple's attendant."

"!!!"

Her eyes widened as if she had reached some sort of enlightenment, but that was only for a moment. She wore a broken expression, unsure whether to prioritize the Heavenly Demon's command for me to rest or to prioritize my education.

If I left her like this, she looked like she might fall into the qi deviation that martial artists often experience. I opened a way out for her.

"I don't mean I'll start studying and training today. I just want to know what kind of evaluation awaits so I can plan how to prepare. If I'm just sitting down and making a plan, couldn't that be considered a form of rest?"

She seemed to ponder for a moment before finally raising the white flag.

"The Young Lord's words seem correct."

Nodding, she began to explain the entrance evaluation for the Demonic Academy.

"The Demonic Academy entrance evaluation has two main stages. One is a martial arts assessment, where you'll duel an instructor from the Demonic Academy. The other is a faith assessment, which is an interview with an instructor."

For some reason, the explanation reminded me of the police civil service exam.

*So it's an interview and a physical fitness test.*

Should I be glad there was no preliminary written exam?

"Then I just need to prepare for the interview and the duel."

"That's correct, Young Lord."

"Can you tell me what the interview covers?"

"It's mainly questions about the doctrines in our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's scripture, the Records of the Heavenly Demon, and their interpretations. There are also questions about the laws and history of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult."

"So, I need to memorize the scripture, the law book, and the history book."

When Jin Hayeon shook her head, I asked again.

"Then is there something else I need to study besides those three?"

"That's not what I mean. The scripture isn't something you can master by simply memorizing it. Only by embracing its contents deep within your heart can you fully understand its passages."

As expected of a religious fanatic, she was spouting strange nonsense. However, as the disciple of a heretic Cult Leader, I couldn't exactly deny her words. It seemed best to just play along.

"I'll do so. In any case, can you tell me roughly the total volume of those three books?"

"It's approximately five hundred pages."

My head nodded on its own.

*The interview won't be that difficult, will it?*

Five hundred pages in one year. That was a thousand sides. From my perspective, having memorized seven subjects for the Grade 7 civil service exam, it was not a significant amount.

Since the interview part didn't seem to be a big problem, I asked her about the duel schedule.

"Then, what's the training plan to prepare for the duel?"

"The Heavenly Demon will fundamentally conduct the martial arts education. However, since the Heavenly Demon is busy, he plans to stop by once a day at 5 PM to teach you. At other times, I'll teach the Young Lord external arts or serve as your duel partner."

After a few more questions and answers, I nodded and gave her an instruction.

"I see. Then, could you bring me some ink and paper?"

She showed no particular question at my request, simply bowing her head with her characteristic blunt expression. Holding the ink she had ground and the paper, I began to scribble.

*First, the training time with the Master is fixed. I can't change that.*

It was like the classes I took as an examinee. My Master was the Cult Leader here, so his class was a top-tier instructor's lecture. I had to adjust to his schedule. I couldn't just skip his class for my own.

[5 PM ― Martial arts training with the Master]

After writing that in the middle of the white paper, I wrote a few more phrases above and below it.

[Myo Hour (5 AM–7 AM) ― Wake up and eat. qi circulation]
[Jin Hour, 9 AM ― Review and study for the interview]
[O Hour (11 AM–1 PM) ― Lunch and qi circulation]
[1 PM ― External arts training]
...
[Hae Hour and a half (9 PM–10 PM) ― Review and sleep]

I had written down a daily schedule.

Whether studying for the civil service exam or college entrance exams, all examinees heard the same thing. Instead of wasting time organizing your desk and making schedules, you should just open a book and start studying.

This was true to some extent. It was true for those with extremely weak wills who used it as a form of escapism and couldn't even begin to study.

However, it wasn't true for me. It wasn't that my willpower was particularly strong. If I didn't pass, I could die in a year. How could I possibly have a weak will in this situation?

I made a schedule not to strengthen my will, but to pursue 'efficiency'. To survive a year from now, I needed to create the most efficient daily routine possible. I also needed to get my body into a habit.

Humans are inherently lazy. Anyone would want to do nothing and just rest, except for a very small minority of workaholics or exercise addicts. That's why, while my will was still full and I was aware my life was in danger, I had to instill the habit in my body.

If you follow a schedule with set rules and times for more than a month, the human body naturally adjusts. Even when thoughts like *'Ah, what a pain,'* or *'I want to rest a bit today,'* pop up, your body will have already woken up and started preparing for activity.

However, Jin Hayeon frowned as she looked down at the schedule I had completed.

"Is there something you're dissatisfied with?"

"Unlike when you asked if I could take responsibility, it seems the Young Lord is thinking quite leisurely."

Saying so, she pointed to the beginning and end of the schedule.

"Does the Young Lord truly have the confidence to pass while sleeping for seven hours a day?"

"You're the one speaking foolishly. The evaluation isn't tomorrow, it's in a year. The human body needs sleep to recover, and so does the mind. Sleeping less to increase study time might be efficient for a day or two, but in the long run, it's far more efficient to study while getting enough rest."

In the past, there was a saying among examinees. 'Four pass, five fail,' or 'three pass, four fail.' Sleep four hours and you'll pass, sleep five and you'll fail. Or more extremely, sleep three hours and you'll pass, sleep four and you'll fail.

In conclusion, it was bullshit. At least, unless you were cramming the day before an exam because you hadn't caught up. If you had already covered all the material, you shouldn't reduce your sleep either. Your mind needs to be sharp to solve problems during the test.

Of course, a small minority of people are fine with little sleep, and because that small minority of mutants packaged their achievements as 'effort through reduced sleep,' it spread as if sleeping less and working hard was a virtue.

Aside from that small minority, foolishly cutting back on sleep would only cause your body and mind to gradually break down. The body would never adapt. That's not to say you needed to sleep an absurdly long time either.

For the human body to recover, the most appropriate amount of sleep was on average between six and eight hours, though it varies from person to person. There was a theory that the body recovers most between 10 PM and 2 AM. Above all, it was important to always sleep regularly. When the body gets used to the bedtime and wake-up time, it automatically begins to prepare, which helps you get a good night's sleep.

For these scientific reasons, I had set the sleeping time to be precisely from 10 PM to 5 AM.

That's right. Studying must be done scientifically.

However, it was difficult for a religious fanatic to accept such scientific reasoning.

"That's nothing more than an excuse. If you believe in the Lord of All Demons and focus entirely on the doctrines of the Divine Cult, you'll be able to achieve anything you desire."

It seemed she believed that if I just prayed to the Heavenly Demon and devoted myself without sleeping, I would pass through the Heavenly Demon's grace.

The corner of my mouth twisted up at her crazy, heretic-like words.

"If I fail because of that, can you take responsibility?"

"... ."

"No, according to you, if I just put in the eff~o~rt, I'll pass by the Master's grace. So if I fail, it would be the 'Master's fault,' wouldn't it?"

"... ."

Rendered speechless by the reappearance of the responsibility argument, she soon shot back with a cold expression.

"Then, if the Young Lord fails after being so leisurely, can you take responsibility?"

They say you learn bad things quickly. Jin Hayeon was now using the responsibility argument against me.

"Just as my life isn't enough to take full responsibility, the death of one Young Lord won't erase the stain left on the Heavenly Demon."

I almost burst out laughing at her attack. It was pathetic. How dare a novice who just learned the responsibility argument try to use that technique on me, someone who's been battered by it for five years.

The greatest counter to the responsibility argument.

"If you really don't want to do it my way, let's just quit. If you interfere with my plan and forcibly wake me when I should be sleeping, I'll just give up on everything. We can just smear shit on the Master's face and die together."

It was, in short, the 'not my problem' attitude.
