After discussing the mental ward for nearly two hours, Master left the Wind Cliff Pavilion to handle other matters.

The sky had already darkened. I received a bath and Body Purification from Jin Hayeon, then immediately passed out. The duel, Master's thinly veiled threats, and revising the proposal while suffering from side effects had exhausted my mind. I fell asleep as if knocked out, free from side effects for the first time in a while.

*Ah… I've been passing out like this for the past few days.*

The thought came to me the moment I opened my eyes. Jin Hayeon had knocked me out repeatedly while I was in the clinic.

Recalling this, I turned my head. Jin Hayeon's bedding was already put away. Bright sunlight streamed through the window.

*It feels like I just closed my eyes, but a whole day has passed.*

As I felt a sense of emptiness and folded my blanket, I heard footsteps outside.

*That's strange.*

Jin Hayeon would never make such loud footsteps. As I suspected, the person approaching was not Jin Hayeon.

"Oppa!!"

The door burst open, revealing a little girl with a mix of emotions on her face. Seon'ah kicked off her shoes and rushed into my arms.

I stroked the head of the little girl, who looked like a drenched cat.
"I'm sorry for making you worry."

Seon'ah looked up at me, her eyes trembling as if she were about to cry.
"Don't worry, I'm fine now."

"I'll kill all the bastards who are bothering you, Oppa!"

"Don't… what?"
What a harsh thing for a child to say.

*How in the world does the Hyeokryeon Clan raise their children?*
Then again, I was a fool for expecting anything from those heretic religious fanatics.

I sighed deeply and scolded the little girl.
"Hey! You can't say you'll kill everyone. You shouldn't think such dangerous thoughts."

"......"

"Now, now. Seon'ah, you're still young."

"There are bad guys targeting you, Oppa! And I'm learning martial arts to kill bad guys!"

Unbelievable! The little girl who followed me so obediently was now talking back, but she had a point. I was not surprised by her talk of killing people.

"That's why I said you can't. Many of the people after me are skilled fighters. If you try to kill them now, you could get killed instead. It's too dangerous."

Seon'ah had no reply to my words. She hung her head with a dejected look. Her pitiful appearance softened my heart, so I offered a carrot instead of a stick to soothe her.

"Still, it's good to see you're trying so hard, Seon'ah."

At my praise, the little girl slowly lifted her bowed head and looked at me. I grinned and added, "You must have been shocked to hear I was hurt, but you kept up with your training, right? You held back until today."

I had not seen Seon'ah once while I was recovering in the clinic. That must have meant she endured the urge to visit me and focused on her training.

*Well, it seems she couldn't hold back today.*
She came early in the morning instead of her usual afternoon time, so her patience must have reached its limit.

But for some reason, the little girl turned her head slightly, avoiding my gaze.

"???"

While I was feeling puzzled, Jin Hayeon entered through the door Seon'ah had left wide open, carrying a breakfast tray. She noticed Seon'ah by my side and spoke in a blunt tone.

"You're here again?"

"…Again?"

As I asked, Jin Hayeon placed the breakfast tray in front of me.
"She came to the clinic every day for the past few days. I just didn't wake you to let the Young Lord rest."

"I don't think I ever saw her during meals?"

"She said I'd be disturbing you while you were resting, so she kicked me out whenever I came during your meal times."
Seon'ah confessed with a pout.

It was cute, but I could not just let it slide. Spoiling a child just because she was cute was the fastest way to ruin her.

"Seon'ah? You have to keep your promises, right?"

"I-I am! I always finished all the training my aunt gave me before I came to visit!"

Before I could question Seon'ah's hasty excuse, Jin Hayeon surprisingly took the little girl's side.
"That is likely true."

Though she did add the word 'likely'. I glanced at Jin Hayeon, finding it strange that she would defend Seon'ah. Jin Hayeon added an explanation.

"The fact that 'that Hyeokryeon Clan' has not said anything to the Young Lord so far must mean there have been no problems with the Young Lady's training, correct?"

I could not help but nod at her reasoning. After all, those people had stormed in just because I played with the little girl for a bit. Their silence meant they had no reason to complain.

"Time is passing. Why don't you have your breakfast first, Young Lord."
Jin Hayeon urged me to eat, as if she could not afford to lose time from my morning studies.

However, her day did not go as planned. Just as I finished breakfast with the little girl who had arrived so early, two more guests came to the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Hahaha! Are you feeling better, Young Lord?"

"Youngest. How is your body?"

It was the combination of positive and negative, Guyang Mun and Third Senior Brother.

"I'm fine, thanks to the good rest I got at the clinic, but Master Guyang, what is that bundle in your hand?"
Was he carrying another bundle of gifts for Jin Hayeon?

Unfortunately, my guess was wrong. The madman had prepared something far more serious.

"Hahaha. Actually, I will also be staying here for a while starting today, Young Lord."

"…Master Guyang staying here? What do you mean?"

Third Senior Brother answered my question.
"After the last incident, Master was worried about your bodyguard situation, so I recommended Guyang. He's been saying he wanted to be your bodyguard for a few days, and I know I can trust him with the job. fufufu."

Third Senior Brother then glanced at Guyang Mun. Their eyes met, they shared a slight smile, and then quickly straightened their faces. It seemed the two had become close enough to call each other Hyung-nim and brother.

Third Senior Brother, now a Hyung-nim, seemed to have dragged Master into this to support his lovesick younger brother, appointing that man as my bodyguard, and I knew exactly why that man was so desperate to become my bodyguard.

"…Are you that worried about Miss Jin and I sleeping in the same room?"

"W-w-what are you talking about, Young Lord! I was just worried because I heard the Y-Young Lord was attacked again…"

"Does that mean you don't trust my bodyguard?"
At Jin Hayeon's cold question, Guyang Mun became flustered.

"N-no, that's not it…"

Perhaps feeling sorry for his fumbling younger brother, Third Senior Brother finally stepped forward.
"It's a misunderstanding, Miss Jin. I was the one who recommended Guyang. Even with you here, Miss Jin, how can one person handle the work of three bodyguards alone?"

Unable to direct her anger at Third Senior Brother, Jin Hayeon replied with her usual indifferent expression.
"I understand, Third Young Master."

And I had nothing more to say.

*If I say anything more here, Third Senior Brother might fall into his victim complex again.*
If he went on a rampage, claiming I was ignoring him, I might end up staring at the clinic ceiling again.

Besides, I felt a little sorry for him.

*It's understandable that a man would lose his mind if the woman he has a crush on is sleeping with another man.*
The only problem was that the other man was me.

{sep}

After a minor squabble, Guyang Mun ended up unpacking his belongings in my room. Around that time, Eldest Senior Brother, Hang Geon, and Sixth Senior Brother, who suffered from Johyeon disease, also visited, making the Wind Cliff Pavilion briefly noisy.

"The Young Lord has a lot of studying to catch up on today. Please, everyone, leave now."

Thanks to Jin Hayeon's cold dismissal, they all soon left the Wind Cliff Pavilion. All except Guyang Mun.

After that, the day was not much different from usual, except for the changed order of events. Since I had dealt with guests in the morning, I began my studies and external arts training around noon. Then, Master arrived right on time for a realistic duel as part of my training.

It meant I struggled against his terrifying killing intent and demonic qi, fighting desperately to survive. After Master left, I trained briefly on my own before receiving a bath and Body Purification.

This time, however, Guyang Mun did it. It was a strange feeling.

*He really is good at his job, isn't he?*
Receiving a bath and Body Purification from a man was quite uncomfortable, but his work was flawless. It made me wonder why a guy like this completely lost his mind whenever Jin Hayeon was involved.

And that night, as I ended up sharing a room with a man in the final stages of a one-sided love, I asked him.

"How did you fall for her so hard?"
Honestly, I was asking because I wondered if it was a side effect of his demonic art.

At my question, Guyang Mun quickly looked left and right. After confirming no one was around, he let out a sigh of relief. Then, with a nostalgic expression, he began to speak.

"The first time I met her was on the day we entered the Demonic Academy."

Thus began Guyang Mun's long, rambling story of his unrequited love. It started from their first meeting at the Demonic Academy and covered the various situations they faced while living and training together as peers.

"Hayeon was a passionate girl, unlike her cold and aloof appearance, Young Lord. She always trained the latest, and we competed fiercely for the valedictorian position."

"But she never tried to depend on anyone. She always tried to stand on her own. It was like… she looked strong on the outside, but if you looked closely, she seemed fragile, which made her feel even more pitiful."

"So I often offered to help, but she always refused in order to stand on her own. That image, like a noble crane, was beautiful."

I listened to his endless praise of Jin Hayeon for a long time. At some point, I started letting his words go in one ear and out the other.

It was not just because it was boring. It was because I had a pretty good idea of the situation.

*What? He just fell for her face at first sight, didn't he?*
His story began with him being stunned by her beautiful face when they first met. What followed was even more ridiculous.

He liked one thing Jin Hayeon did because it was pretty, then another because it was aloof, and another because she seemed diligent. He just liked everything she did. It felt like he decided he liked her first and came up with the reasons later.

"Ahem. That's enough. I understand your feelings well, Master Guyang."

"I still have much more to tell, Young Lord."

"I have things to do tomorrow, so I'll be turning in."

It seemed like more listening would just be a repeat of the same content, so I turned my back on him. I thought it was a side effect of his demonic art, but it was just a typical first-crush story that any man who has never dated would experience.

{sep}

The next morning, unlike the day before, I began my morning studies with Jin Hayeon after breakfast as usual. It was a review session, going over the doctrinal texts, history books, and law books I had already studied once.

As I focused on reading for a while, I glanced toward the door. After confirming it was securely closed, I spoke to Jin Hayeon.

"Miss Jin, may I ask you something?"

"Please ask."

"Why do you dislike Master Guyang so much?"
Perhaps because of what I had heard from Guyang Mun last night, I felt a bit curious. It was not that I particularly cared if Guyang Mun's love life succeeded or failed. It was just that…

*Come to think of it, it feels less like Jin Hayeon is uninterested in Guyang Mun and more like she dislikes him.*
It was not very noticeable because Jin Hayeon showed almost no emotional fluctuation. I had not noticed it before because I was not interested. I only realized it after yesterday's events.

"It is study time, Young Lord. Please ask questions related to your studies."

"I don't particularly want to ask either, but we'll be living together for a while, and it's uncomfortable for me when you two don't get along, you know?"

Did the words that the person she served was feeling uncomfortable because of her get through? The highly responsible woman, despite her lack of emotional expression, let out a faint sigh and opened her mouth.

"I disliked him from the very beginning."

It was the start of a story that would have made Guyang Mun weep tears of blood had he heard it.
