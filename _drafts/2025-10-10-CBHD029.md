The Eldest Senior Brother continued speaking with a foolish smile, completely ignoring my feelings.

"Of course, it’s still too early to talk about this since you haven't even completed the Demonic Academy, my Junior Brother, but I believe in you. I believe that you, Junior Brother, will soon become a fine demonic practitioner."

I had no desire to become one. A fine demonic practitioner, that is.

"You, you flatter me, Eldest Senior Brother. How could I possibly become your strategist?"

"...It seems I'm lacking as a leader for you to serve. Seeing how stubbornly you refuse, that is."

"!!!"

Cold sweat trickled down my back at the Eldest Senior Brother’s regretful murmur.

He claimed he could control his anger after entering the Transcendent Demon Realm. However, having already seen him rampage, it sounded like he would kill me if I refused again.

"W-What are you talking about! How could you be lacking, Eldest Senior Brother? I-I only said that because I'm the one who is lacking."

"Hahaha. If that’s the case, don’t you worry! I’m sure you're more than capable, Junior Brother. However, your words also seem right. I have yet to show you that I'm a Liege worthy of your service! Hahaha! Look forward to it! I'll become a man worthy of your service!"

"..."

*This crazy boar isn't listening to a word I'm saying.*

As if he had said all he needed to, he left the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"……"

I despaired, feeling my life in the Demonic Cult taking a sharp, twisted turn. Just then, Jin Hayeon poured oil on the fire.

"Congratulations, Young Lord. It seems the First Young Lord has properly recognized your abilities."

"...Is this something to be so happy about?"

"It is. If the First Young Lord becomes the next Cult Leader, wouldn't he place you in the position of one of the inquisitors or the Demonic Brain?"

"Haaah..." A deep sigh escaped me.

Jin Hayeon misunderstood my sigh and spouted more nonsense.

"Could it be that you're already devising a plan to lead the First Young Lord to the seat of the next Heavenly Demon?"

The question was ridiculous, but I could guess why she misunderstood. I had learned a fact about a month after joining the Demonic Cult. Just because someone was a Heavenly Demon disciple did not guarantee they would become the next Heavenly Demon.

Once the current Heavenly Demon achieved Ascension, a competition to select the next Cult Leader would begin within the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

The inquisitors chosen by the Ascended Heavenly Demon presided over this competition. There was only one minimum requirement to participate.

One had to win a duel against the inquisitors.

For generations, the Left and Right Emissaries were those who had mastered a specific demonic art passed down only to them. To defeat such individuals, one had to master a top-ranked demonic art to at least the Transcendent Demon Realm.

In other words, even a Heavenly Demon disciple could not aim for the position if they had not mastered a profound demonic art to the Transcendent Demon Realm.

Conversely, any outstanding drummer could aim for the position of the next Heavenly Demon, even if they were not a Heavenly Demon disciple.

The reason for this method was simple. To learn the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, the symbol of the Heavenly Demon, one first had to master a profound demonic art to the Transcendent Demon Realm.

It was the most powerful demonic art, but it also had the most vicious side effect. This tradition had arisen out of necessity.

The competition winner who began learning the art was called the ‘Cult Leader’. Only one who completely mastered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art and reached the Transcendent Demon Realm, like Master, was called ‘the Heavenly Demon’ in the truest sense.

When Jin Hayeon belatedly told me that story, I finally understood why the Demonic Cult was run so haphazardly.

From the moment a Heavenly Demon achieved Ascension, the Demonic Cult would enter a dark age lasting from a few years to several decades.

A fierce civil war would begin among the top drummers who had reached the Transcendent Demon Realm. They all fought to become the Cult Leader and learn the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

Even if one person won and became the Cult Leader, the side effect of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art would make them a madman.

If that leader failed to master the art and died from qi deviation, the competition for a new Cult Leader would start all over again.

In the end, they could only escape that terrible era when a very special person completely mastered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

Just like my Master.

A true Heavenly Demon like my Master would take power and briefly revive the Demonic Cult. Then the dark age would return. This cycle explained the state of the Demonic Cult's system.

In any case, the Eldest Senior Brother had finally reached the Transcendent Demon Realm with the Explosive Mara Sword Technique. He had at last earned the qualification to aim for the position of the next Heavenly Demon.

However, several drummers like Hyeokryeon Cheongang already existed in the Demonic Cult. This meant the Eldest Senior Brother was a latecomer.

Jin Hayeon had mistaken my sigh. She thought I despaired over the daunting task of being a latecomer's strategist, needing to devise a scheme to surpass those already qualified.

I was simply sad that my life as a jobless bum who just played and ate was slipping further away.

*As I thought, Seon'ah is the only one who understands my heart.*

Unlike Jin Hayeon, who endlessly congratulated me, Seon'ah was sighing right along with me.

After leaving the Wind Cliff Pavilion, Wi Jinhak visited a place familiar to Ilmok.

He entered the valedictorian with the characters for 'Hyeokryeon' written on it. In a pavilion in the deepest part of the place, Wi Jinhak met with a certain man alone.

"It has been a while, Patriarch."

"It has been a while, First Young Lord."

As they exchanged light greetings, Hyeokryeon Cheongang was inwardly quite surprised, though he did not show it.

"It seems you've achieved a great accomplishment recently."

"Hahaha. It was all thanks to my Youngest Junior Brother."

"Heo..."

An exclamation suppressing various emotions escaped Hyeokryeon Cheongang’s lips.

Wi Jinhak looked at the head of the Hyeokryeon Clan and spoke.

"Actually, I came here in person to apologize for the last incident. Although I was punished directly by Master, I thought it was right for me to come and see you myself."

"We decided not to make an issue of that matter, so you don't need to worry about it."

As Hyeokryeon Cheongang replied, various thoughts ran through his mind.

*It seems he truly has reached the Transcendent Demon Realm with the Explosive Mara Sword Technique.*

Wi Jinhak had changed so much in just one month. The man who previously would have been unable to contain his anger over an insult had instead come to apologize.

*And he even says it was thanks to that Eighth Young Lord.*

Naturally, Hyeokryeon Cheongang’s thoughts turned to Ilmok.

At first, he had only watched over him because of the Heavenly Demon’s order.

However, his granddaughter had changed after the Eighth Young Lord visited the clan.

It was a very positive change.

Since that day, his granddaughter had voluntarily immersed herself in martial arts. Before, she had only followed the clan's orders reluctantly. The change was a complete transformation.

He still disliked that she visited the Eighth Young Lord for two hours every day, but besides that, she devoted herself all day to martial arts training or studying at the clan.

Her talent was already outstanding, so her growth rate was astonishing.

Now, even the First Young Lord claimed he reached the Transcendent Demon Realm thanks to the Eighth Young Lord. Because of this, Hyeokryeon Cheongang’s thoughts about Ilmok had changed slightly for the better.

However, he still did not know why his granddaughter had changed so drastically, or the exact reason she had started to obsess over her training.

Late at night, three men gathered in the darkness for a meeting. They met in a corner of a massive valedictorian located in the Main Headquarters of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

As always, they gathered to discuss the major and minor affairs within the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

Today's topic, however, was inevitably about First Young Lord Wi Jinhak, who had emerged from the seclusion training cave a few days ago.

"You've all heard the story. That foolish First Young Lord has finally achieved complete mastery of the Explosive Mara Sword Technique and entered the Transcendent Demon Realm."

"...I had anticipated that risk, as there's still time before the Heavenly Demon’s Ascension, but I never imagined he'd succeed in reaching the Transcendent Demon Realm so suddenly."

Though their faces were hidden by the darkness, they all conversed in serious tones. Their main topics were predictions about Wi Jinhak’s future actions and how to deal with them.

"It's right to prepare, but there's no need to approach this too sensitively. That risks exposing our activities, and in the first place, he's just a greenhorn who has barely met the qualifications."

"However, as he is the great disciple of the Heavenly Demon, just having the qualifications is as good as securing an advantageous position. If the Heavenly Demon decides to support the great disciple before he leaves, it will inevitably become disadvantageous for us."

"Hmm. In the end, it seems we must pay closest attention to how the Heavenly Demon treats the First Young Lord."

Just as the discussion on this topic was wrapping up, the man on the right spoke cautiously.

"By the way, have you all heard that rumor?"

"A rumor? What rumor do you mean?"

"There's a story that the Eighth Young Lord played a significant role in the First Young Lord’s success in reaching the Transcendent Demon Realm."

"……"

A brief silence fell, as if the other two had not heard it.

Then the man on the left tilted his head and muttered.

"Wasn't it because of that Eighth Young Lord that the First Young Lord went on a rampage?"

"The rumor that the Eighth Young Lord provoked the First Young Lord, relentlessly spouting words he hates the most, has already spread widely throughout the cult, has it not?"

Rumors had inevitably spread about Ilmok relentlessly provoking the rampaging Wi Jinhak to draw his attention. Dozens of people had come to stop the rampaging Wi Jinhak at the time, and all of them had heard Ilmok taunting the First Young Lord.

"Heo. I had lost interest, assuming the Eighth Young Lord and the First Young Lord had become complete enemies."

"To think there was some other contact between them in the meantime."

"For now, a rumor is just a rumor. It seems we need to confirm if it's reliable information."

At the words of the man in the center, the men on the left and right nodded.

If the rumor was false, they would pay it no mind. They were not idle enough to be swayed by a false rumor and pay attention to the powerless Eighth Young Lord.

But what if the rumor was true?

The clever brat had changed the cult's system by speaking directly to the Heavenly Demon. Now, he had accomplished the incredible feat of giving an epiphany to the First Young Lord.

He might still be young, but he was a dangerous element who could have a major impact on their grand plan.

If such a brat sided with the First Young Lord, their plans could be ruined.

"If the rumor is true, then I'll place the Eighth Young Lord under surveillance," the man in the center declared, his tone laced with killing intent. "That way, we can seize any opportunity to dispose of him."

A few days later, after a day not much different from usual, it was time for my training with Master.

"Are the preparations for the Demonic Academy exam going well?" he asked.

The memory of a similar question flashed through my mind. As I hesitated, that damn Jin Hayeon answered for me.

"For his morning studies, he has already finished the Records of the Heavenly Demon, and the history books are also nearly complete. At this rate, we can likely reduce his morning study time and allow him to focus more on martial arts. Furthermore, his martial arts achievements are also improving rapidly."

"Hmm. It seems there won't be any major issues with him passing the entrance examination."

"That is correct. You also said you would bestow upon him the Explosive Demonic Pill in the future, so if we take that into account, his admission is a certainty. In my personal opinion, it seems possible for him to enter Class A right from the start."

The Demonic Academy selected only twenty trainees each year through an examination. Those twenty were divided into two classes based on their scores, Class A and Class B.

"Hohoho. To think he might enter Class A after preparing for only a year. He was truly born with excellent qualities."

"It can be said that it is thanks to the discerning eye of the Supreme Lord, who discovered Young Lord Ilmok."

I could not understand why a compliment about my talent concluded with the greatness of the Heavenly Demon, but there was a bigger problem.

*Wait, I think I've been through this before, haven't I?*

My ominous premonition was not wrong.

"Fortunately, it seems you have some time to spare, so I have a task for you, Ilmok."

"Ha, hahaha. B-But you never know, right? Just in case, I think it'd be right to step up preparations for the entrance exam."

"Do not worry. When you return after finishing this task, this old man will make time to personally oversee your training, just like last time."

He meant he would work me like a dog again.

Seeing the old geezer say it as if he were bestowing a reward, I had no choice but to give up.

"Ha. Hahaha. Thinking about it, I seem to have plenty of time. W-Why would a busy person like you, Master, need to set aside extra time? By the way, may I ask what the task is, Master?"

The Master must have taken my refusal of the reward as humility, because he smiled with satisfaction and replied.

"Just as you suggested last time, we have opened schools and started education at the Divine Cult's Branches located throughout Xinjiang. So, shouldn't you go and see with your own eyes whether each Branch is operating as you planned?"

In other words, he was telling me to go on another business trip.

Moreover, in the role of a field auditor.
