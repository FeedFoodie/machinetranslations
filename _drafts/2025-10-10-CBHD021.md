Oops.

I was so focused on the praise that I forgot to put down my sword.

"Th-that is..." I stammered. In that brief moment, my Senior Brother's cheerful face turned cold.

If this continued, he might accuse me of mockery and swing his sword. My mind went blank with a sense of dread, and I blurted out the first excuse I could think of.

"Th-there was a mosquito, Senior Brother!"

"A mosquito?"

"Yes. I took up my sword to catch the mosquito."

"Catching a mosquito with a sword?"

"Y-yes! It's training to cut small things accurately. Master told me to learn from you, third Senior Brother. He said your Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art is so precise it can slice a single hair fluttering in the sky."

"Hahaha. Did Master really say that?"

"Y-yes, he did, Senior Brother!"

My answer made a smile return to my Senior Brother's cold face. Even in the shadows, I could see his crescent-moon eyes and the glint of his teeth.

I inwardly sighed in relief.

"......"

A chilling sound brushed past my ear.

"..."

My body froze. I cautiously glanced to the side and saw a mosquito lying on the floor, sliced in two.

"Did you see that? I just demonstrated a strike from the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art for you. Reflect on Second Senior Brother's sword and perfect your own precise swordsmanship."

His self-esteem boosted, he spoke like an excellent Senior Brother.

However.

*What does he mean, see it clearly? I couldn't even see it, let alone imitate it.*

Even now, my heart is pounding.

*It's a good thing I didn't scream...*

I realized from that single sword strike just how wide the gap was between us. At my current level, I could not even follow his sword with my eyes, let alone block it.

The moment third Senior Brother decided to kill me, my head would be cut off before I even knew I was dead.

Perhaps my sense of danger had dulled from the hamster-wheel routine of daily life. I wondered just how many terrifying monsters existed in this Demonic Cult full of psychopaths.

As I was renewing my fading caution, Senior Brother offered his farewell.

"This was an enjoyable time, thanks to you, Junior Brother. I'll visit you often from now on."

"Uh, you're welcome to visit anytime, Senior Brother."

I gave that answer to survive. Senior Brother revealed his teeth in the moonlight and spoke his final words.

"Yes. It's late, so go to sleep now."

By the time I heard him say 'go to sleep now', Senior Brother had surprisingly vanished from the room.

*...Fuck. How am I supposed to sleep when my heart is still pounding like this.*

I did not dare say it out loud, thinking that crazy Senior Brother might still be hiding and watching me.

The next day around noon, after handling various affairs within the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, the Heavenly Demon went to the Black Cloud Pavilion where his third disciple, Seo Wanpyeong, stayed.

While he visited his youngest disciple, Ilmok, every day at 5 PM for training, he did not put as much effort into his other disciples. The Heavenly Demon had little free time, and in any case, there was nothing left for him to teach his first and third disciples.

After entering the Ultimate Demon Realm, one could only overcome their limits through their own enlightenment. Therefore, unlike with Ilmok, he would only visit them occasionally for a simple conversation.

"Greetings, Master."

"Yes. How are you finding the work of the Dark Shadow Hall?"

Like many older adults speaking with younger people, his idea of a 'light conversation' was usually about work.

"Have you made any progress with the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art?"

Or it was about training or studies. The Heavenly Demon was benevolent to his non-heathen subordinates, whether they were his disciples or believers of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, but he was undeniably an old-fashioned man.

After they conversed for a while, the Heavenly Demon stroked his beard and tilted his head slightly.

"Hmm. Wanpyeong. Has something good happened to you recently?"

It was the same Seo Wanpyeong as always, with a gloomy expression and deep shadows on his face. However, the Heavenly Demon had spent over twenty years with him and could tell.

He could tell that Seo Wanpyeong was in a rather good mood.

*The atmosphere is similar to when he was in a good mood during his time in the Demonic Initiate Realm, before he entered the Ultimate Demon Realm.*

Although a negative atmosphere had formed on his face from the long accumulation of negative thoughts, Seo Wanpyeong had been a disciple who knew how to smile normally when he was younger.

Seo Wanpyeong almost blurted out, "As expected!" at his Master's sharp eye.

*Master has been watching over me! Just as Youngest said.*

His shattered self-esteem, a result of his constant negative thinking, recovered just a little. This was especially true because it was the attention of the great Heavenly Demon, his Master.

Seo Wanpyeong felt his mood lift. He was about to mention his conversation with Youngest last night but quickly recalled an important fact.

*That's right! Youngest told me. He said Master is strict in front of each disciple but praises them behind their backs.*

If that was true, there was no need to tell Master about his conversation with Youngest. It would be like tattling that he and Youngest had figured out Master's method of discipline.

"Ahem. It's just that the weather is nice today, so I feel good for some reason, Master."

"Hoh. That is a very positive sign. Perhaps you are about to break through a wall with your Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art."

The Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art was a technique that caused its user to suffer from negative thoughts and paranoia the closer they got to the Ultimate Demon Realm. For the third disciple, who practiced such an art, to feel good just because the weather was nice was a great sign.

As his Master let out a hearty laugh for the first time in a long while, the corners of Seo Wanpyeong's mouth kept twitching.

*Master is proud of me!*

Feeling elated for the first time in a very long time, Seo Wanpyeong wondered how he could make his Master even happier. How could he make his Master even more proud of him?

The conversation he had with Ilmok last night naturally came to mind.

*That's right! Master said my Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art was outstanding enough to precisely cut even the smallest of objects!*

A plan to please his Master formed in his mind, and Seo Wanpyeong twisted his gloomy lips into a smile.

"Master!"

"Yes, my third."

Seo Wanpyeong uncharacteristically called out in a loud voice. Once his Master's gaze was on him, he acted.

"......"

He suddenly tore out a clump of his own hair. He then released the plucked hair, letting the strands scatter and drift on the wind.

"......"

Seo Wanpyeong drew and sheathed his sword without a sound or trace, followed by a belated cutting sound. In an instant, all the scattered hairs fell to the floor, chopped into dozens of pieces.

He possessed the skill to slice every thin strand of hair fluttering in the air. The movement was so fast and stealthy that an ordinary person could not even perceive the sword being drawn and sheathed.

Seo Wanpyeong, having demonstrated the ultimate technique of the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art, looked at his Master with an uncharacteristically confident expression, as if asking to be praised.

The Heavenly Demon watched the entire spectacle performed by his pitiful third disciple.

"..."

He looked at Seo Wanpyeong with an expression that clearly asked why on earth he was suddenly acting this way.

"..."

"..."

For a moment, the master and disciple exchanged silent, awkward glances.

"......"

"......"

Thinking his display was insufficient, Seo Wanpyeong once again tore out a clump of his hair, scattered it, and sliced it. He continued to pluck and slice his hair, as if determined to create a bald spot on his head.

"Ahem. Ah, that is very excellent skill."

The Heavenly Demon watched the crazy act in a daze for a while before muttering reluctantly.

"Hahaha. Thank you, Master! I will continue to strive forward."

Finally hearing his Master's praise, Seo Wanpyeong smiled brightly, forgetting the pain of pulling out his own hair.

"Y-yes. Continue to strive hard."

"Yes! I will definitely complete the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art and bring you the head of that bastard leader of the Murim Alliance!"

"Ahem. Yes. I will be counting on you."

The Heavenly Demon replied with a complicated expression, then quickly left the Black Cloud Pavilion, saying that urgent business had come up.

*It seems he hasn't broken through the wall to the Ultimate Demon Realm but has fallen into qi deviation.*

The Heavenly Demon suspected his poor third disciple had finally gone mad.

*It can't be helped. I have no choice but to increase the number of the third's bodyguards.*

This was not a measure to protect his third disciple. They were bodyguards meant to subdue the third disciple in case he went berserk.

After my unwelcome night guest, a very important concern arose. A problem I had intended to ignore resurfaced as a major worry.

*I need to prepare for this somehow.*

It was not a worry about the Demonic Academy entrance examination.

*If I don't, I might end up like that crazy Senior Brother.*

Third Senior Brother, Seo Wanpyeong, who had visited a few days ago, was not normal by anyone's standards. Of course, I wondered how many sane people could exist in this group of heretic religious fanatics.

*I've grown too used to it without realizing.*

I had become wary of myself for taking it all for granted. The side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword that I was learning resembled Third Senior Brother's condition in some ways.

There was a high risk of one's self-esteem being shattered.

I remembered a story from when I was Seo Jihun. It said that among famous and successful artists, Ghana businessmen, and film directors, there were many 'perfectionists'. Such people experience immense stress because they strive for perfection in everything they do.

Of course, that perfectionism and stress led them to create masterpieces or achieve complete mastery in business. The biggest problem arose when they set a goal of perfection that was nearly impossible to achieve. In such cases, perfectionists would feel like insects or trash for failing.

This meant that instead of becoming a successful perfectionist, I had a high chance of becoming a madman with shattered self-esteem, just like Third Senior Brother. To avoid that, I had to prepare something. I needed a way to not become a madman who seeks perfection by aiming for the impossible.

The only problem was...

*Why am I worrying about this in the dead of night?*

"Haaah. I'm really trash, Youngest. Master must not trust me. That's why he increased the number of bodyguards again."

At the gloomy voice of the Senior Brother who had given me this worry, I sighed deeply to myself before speaking.

"On the contrary, I think it's because he trusts you, Senior Brother. Didn't you vow to Master that you'd kill the Chairman of the Murim Alliance? Your martial arts are for assassination, so Master must have done this so you can train to infiltrate the Murim Alliance."

"Indeed! If I can't even break through the surveillance of a mere dozen people, how could I kill the Chairman hidden deep within the Murim Alliance! To think I couldn't understand Master's deep intentions and even misinterpreted his will. I truly am a worm."

I spoke to my Senior Brother, who had started digging his hole of despair again.

"What are you talking about, Senior Brother! People are naturally quick to notice things about others but slow when it comes to themselves. Isn't that how romance is, too?"

"I don't have any experience with romance. As expected, you must have been popular when you were young, Junior Brother."

Damn it. Was this a trap too?

"I-I just heard that somewhere, too, and people have their own areas of expertise. I might be good at understanding people's thoughts, but you, Senior Brother, are good at digging into people's flesh, right? Even if I trained until I was your age, I could never assassinate the Chairman."

"Haaah. Now that you mention it, you're right, Youngest. It's strange. My mind always calms down after talking with you. It feels like the demonic qi digging into my brain recedes. I'll come find you often when things get difficult."

I forced a smile at Third Senior Brother, who spoke as if he was counting only on me.

"Hahaha. You're welcome anytime, but could you perhaps visit during the day?"

At this rate, I felt like I was going to go crazy not from the side effect of the demonic art, but from lack of sleep.
