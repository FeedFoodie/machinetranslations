The escaped Howler was caught in front of a cave not far from the clearing where the monster corpses had been piled. The area was densely packed with centuries-old trees, and true to the name Azure Forest, the trees of the deep woods were suffused with a faint, bluish aura.

It was far from the refreshing feeling of ordinary trees, more like the desolate chill of a haunted house that clung to the skin like dampness.

The Howler that had barely escaped didn't get far before it lost its life. Traces of a fierce final struggle remained all over its body. Aside from one knight with a broken right arm, there were no major injuries.

"Alright, let's move out now!"

Lucius, having succeeded in the pursuit, shouted to his men in a loud voice.

Although some of the mercenary group had followed, it was the knights who caught the runaway. Lucius, who had been worried the knights might be outshone by the mercenaries, looked relieved.

And for good reason, as some mercenary groups possessed equipment far superior to that of the knights. It was already upsetting to see his men gaze at their weapons with envy. If they had been outdone in the monster hunt as well, Lucius's pride would have been deeply wounded.

However, the proud Black Lily Knights were doing their best even under unfavorable conditions. If he could, he would have armed his men from head to toe with the safest and strongest weapons in the empire.

"Wait a minute!"

Just as they were about to move the Howler's corpse, Estella's urgent cry was heard. Lucius, thinking another monster had appeared, reflexively drew his sword.

"......"

SHING. SHING.

The knights following Lucius also drew their swords here and there.

However, Estella, the one who had stopped the knights, was staring at the cave the Howler had tried to escape into, as if she were a tourist.

"What is it?"

The sun had already set, and danger lurked all around. They needed to get out of the forest as quickly as possible. Lucius felt like he would die of frustration watching Estella dawdle and stare at the cave when they should have been hurrying.

Whether she knew this or not, Estella stepped into the cave instead of answering.

"My lady!"

Lucius, startled, followed her and called out, but she picked something up from the cave entrance and abruptly held it out in front of him.

"It's Nebrion."

The light emanating from the Nebrion spread bluishly across Estella's palm.

"Pardon?"

"This cave is full of Nebrion."

Estella said with a wide smile. Her bright blue eyes, reflecting the light from the Nebrion, sparkled like the summer sea.

Estella knew that Nebrion was buried somewhere in a cave in the Azure Forest, but she hadn't expected to find it right away. She felt like she had hit the jackpot.

"What do you plan to do with that? It's a pretty but soft and useless magic stone. The mana it contains is also negligible."

Lucius explained patiently.

Nebrion wasn't a common magic stone. However, its color wasn't particularly beautiful either. Nebrion emitted a faint blue light, but not enough to illuminate its surroundings. Its hardness was too ambiguous to be used for anything. Not being exceptionally beautiful, it was also difficult to use for decoration. Nebrion was, in a word, one of the useless magic stones.

If it at least contained a lot of mana, mages would covet it, but that wasn't the case either.

"I have a use for it. I need to take some of this. Ciel!"

Estella, seeing that Lucius was unlikely to help, called out to Ciel, who was standing at a distance as if just watching. The monster hunt was over, so it was fine for him to do the work he was contracted for.

"Yes, ma'am!"

She had called for Ciel, but it was his subordinates who rushed forward. They looked incredibly excited for some reason, while their captain, following behind, seemed somewhat listless. Regardless, Estella, her eyes sparkling, gave the squirrels who had run up their first task.

"Take as much of the Nebrion from inside here as you can."

"Yes, ma'am!"

Riri answered cheerfully, and the others moved silently. Bruno whipped off his shirt, tied it up like a sack, and started filling it with Nebrion. Irene took off her hat and carefully placed them inside. Kyle used the cloak he was wearing, and Haru, having nowhere else to put them, held them blankly for a moment before starting to stuff them into his sleeves.

Riri, not having a suitable place to put them, looked around before approaching Ciel and holding out his hand.

"Captain, can I borrow your cloak?"

"No."

"Oh dear, the squirrel will be disappointed..."

"Take it."

Before Riri could finish his sentence, Ciel untied the cloak he was wearing. Riri took it excitedly and, like a child on a picnic, began to neatly stack the Nebrion from the cave floor into the cloak. Before long, they had split into teams and were lost in a competition to see who could gather the most.

They looked so innocent it was hard to believe these were the same people who had been brutally slaughtering monsters just a short while ago.

Estella was also diligently gathering Nebrion. With no cloak or hat, she began stuffing them into her pants pockets, which bulged out like a squirrel's cheeks storing acorns.

That damn squirrel!

Ciel shook his head as if to fix his own mind, which was surely malfunctioning, and reluctantly went to Estella's side.

He was thinking he would have to take back the cloak he had given to Riri.

A rumor that Estella had gone to subjugate monsters and came back with a load of useless magic stones spread like wildfire throughout the marquis's castle.

There was also talk that she had spent a large sum of money to contract a mercenary group just to bring back those worthless things.

The first thing people said upon hearing the news was, 'Of course she did.' Everyone was busy making a comment or two about Estella's reckless spending, saying that a person couldn't change so easily.

Catherine did not miss the opportunity.

"Marquis, Lady Catherine has arrived."

Cassian, who was sitting in his office reviewing a report on the monster subjugation, put down the document he was holding. Catherine, who used to visit almost daily, hadn't shown her face much lately.

Although it wasn't in his nature to seek her out first, the marquis was inwardly pleased to see her.

When he was buried under a mountain of work and worn out with fatigue, drinking the tea Catherine brought and listening to her chatter seemed to melt away the exhaustion that weighed down his entire body.

"Let her in."

He took off his glasses, gently pressed his eyelids, and then put them back on. His briefly blurred vision became clear again.

As always, Catherine walked in as gracefully as a butterfly, with a bright smile.

With her beautiful cherry-blossom-colored hair and dawn-like, light green eyes, Catherine was always as lovely as a spring fairy. She was a dependable daughter who always knew what to do, even though he hadn't been able to pay much attention to her, using his busy schedule as an excuse.

"What brings you here at this hour?"

His questioning voice was gruff, but his eyes, as he looked at his daughter, were full of affection.

Catherine knew this well, which was why she could always smile brightly even when the marquis treated her indifferently.

"I wanted to have tea with you, Father. It's been a while. Is now a good time? If you're too busy, I can come back later."

Instead of answering, the marquis took out his pocket watch to check the time. He thought it had only been a moment, but three hours had already passed since he sat at his desk. The marquis stood up to stretch his back. His back felt stiff.

He could almost hear Lucius's nagging, asking if he was going to stop exercising just because he had stepped back from the front lines.

"No, it's fine. Sit."

At the marquis's reply, Catherine's smile deepened.

In no time, the table in the office was colorfully adorned with the tea and snacks Catherine had brought. They were prepared by Catherine for the marquis, who enjoyed sweet foods.

"The chef took special care in making these. I told him I was going on a date with my father and asked him to make them extra delicious."

Catherine smiled sweetly and picked up a macaron, offering it to the marquis. He saw Raul, who was concerned about his health, frown slightly, but the marquis pretended not to notice. Raul, too, had a soft spot for Catherine.

The macaron was sweet, and the tea was fragrant.

Cassian felt the fatigue from the territory's many problems melt away in the leisurely atmosphere he hadn't experienced in a long time.

Catherine, who had been watching the marquis's expression carefully, began to speak when she saw his eyes soften.

"You must be very worried about the monsters lately, aren't you? Why do they keep appearing more and more frequently?"

"Don't worry. The knights are handling it well."

Although he said that, Cassian's expression, which had been bright for the first time in a while, grew heavy again. He had been having sleepless nights lately, worried about the residents of the territory who were suffering because of the monsters.

"It's chamomile tea. They say it helps with sleep."

"Thank you."

At his daughter's worried words, Cassian smiled again. There was a time when he had suspected that Catherine might not be his own flesh and blood. This was because Catherine had been born only eight months after he married her mother Marcella.

However, with the help of the family physician, Alexis, he had secretly conducted a paternity test at the temple, and the results confirmed that she was indeed his daughter.
