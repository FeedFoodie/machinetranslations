But I couldn't just confess and say, 'I'm actually a bad kid!'

I clung to my last hope and asked Master.

"Then, could you tell me what martial arts there are, and what their side effects are?"

"An excellent question. I was just about to explain that. First, there is the Explosive Mara Sword Technique. If you master it completely, it's a sword art with enough destructive power to obliterate a mountain peak."

"What is the side effect?"

"As your mastery increases, you'll become more prone to anger. You'll often find yourself seized by rage, losing all reason."

I nodded at Master's words.

*So it means I'll get an anger management disorder.*

This was likely a real anger management disorder, not the selective kind you often see.

*Then I should absolutely not learn this one.*

Unlike the selective kind, a genuine disorder doesn't pick its targets. It meant I'd ignore a stronger opponent or a dangerous trap, see red, and charge in to get myself killed.

"What other martial arts are there?"

"Hmm. How about the Pure Hands Demonic Art? If you master it completely, it's a terrifying ice art capable of instantly freezing an entire area with power to spare."

"What is the side effect?"

"Hmm. Unfortunately, a man must maintain his virginity to learn this art and accumulate yin qi. Also, perhaps because the yin qi builds up, his sexual desire grows stronger and stronger."

"What... what happens if you can't overcome that sexual desire...?"

"If you fail to overcome the desire even once, you'll become a lust-crazed demon. You'll violate women without rest until your yang qi is depleted, and when your yang qi runs out, you die."

"......"

While I stood speechless, Master kindly explained another martial art.

"Come to think of it, this martial art might suit you. It's called the Frenzied Soul Sword Technique. Its erratic movements and myriad transformations surpass the Twenty-Four Plum Blossom Sword Style of the Central Plains."

"...What is the side effect?"

I asked with no expectations. The Master stroked his beard with a placid expression and answered.

"They say that as your mastery increases, you begin to have auditory or visual hallucinations."

"......"

This time, it was schizophrenia.

{sep}

He introduced several other demonic arts after that. There was the Yellow Thunder Demonic Fist, the Wind Demon Asura Blade Technique, the Demonic Spirit Kick, and more.

Their side effects were just as varied. They included depression, mania, anorexia, and multiple personalities.

*I have to choose from options like these?*

I couldn't tell if I was choosing a martial art or a mental illness. Amidst all this, a question came to mind.

"I have a question, Master."

"Go on."

"Then, Master, have you learned all of these many demonic arts?"

He had listed more than ten martial arts. If Master had learned them all, he should already be suffering from more than ten different mental illnesses. He was literally a walking psychiatric ward. Considering his high level of attainment, he was a powder keg that could explode at any moment.

The Master seemed to sense my intent. He stroked his beard again and burst out laughing.

"Hohoho. Don't worry. The martial arts this old man has completely mastered are the Heavenly Demon Divine Art and the Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords. I learned the others only after mastering the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, so I could teach them."

"If you completely master the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, do the side effects of other demonic arts disappear?"

"The Heavenly Demon Divine Art is the most difficult and dangerous demonic art. Therefore, if you completely master it and enter the Transcendent Demon Realm, you can overcome the side effects of any demonic art. However, the Heavenly Demon Divine Art is permitted only to the Cult Leader of our Cult. You can't learn it unless you become the Cult Leader."

"Then, did you learn the Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords before you completely mastered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art?"

"That's correct. However, I can't pass down the Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords to you either."

I looked puzzled, so Master explained.

"The Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords is the ultimate technique of my main family, the Hyeokryeon Sword Family. Even though this old man has become the Heavenly Demon, how could I, an outsider, arbitrarily teach a sword art passed down only to the family's direct line?"

In the end, this meant I couldn't learn the two martial arts Master knew best. I felt a slight disappointment, but a more important issue remained, so I asked another question.

"But is completely mastering the Heavenly Demon Divine Art the only way to overcome a demonic art's side effects?"

If I couldn't become the Heavenly Demon, I'd have to live with a mental illness for the rest of my life. What could be more terrifying?

Fortunately, Master shook his head.

"A demonic art has three broad stages. First is the Demonic Initiate Realm, where one begins to learn. Second is the Ultimate Demon Realm, where the side effects peak. Third is the Transcendent Demon Realm, where one is free from the side effects. You'll start at the Demonic Initiate Realm, and the side effects will worsen as your mastery increases, but like all things, what fills must eventually empty. Once you reach the Ultimate Demon Realm, the side effects will lessen as your mastery grows higher."

The Master spoke as if escaping the side effects was easy, but I wasn't so easily fooled.

I've worked in the real world for years. Did he think I'd fall for such simple wordplay?

"What happens if someone in the Ultimate Demon Realm fails to gain enlightenment and is consumed by the side effects?"

You couldn't just sit still and pass from the Ultimate Demon Realm to the Transcendent Demon Realm. There was a clear condition, 'when your mastery becomes higher'. It meant you had to ascend to a higher realm through training and enlightenment while your mental illness was at its peak.

That was easier said than done. It seemed obvious that most would fail and end up as lunatics.

But in response to my question, Master just stroked his beard and spouted nonsense.

"Hohoho. That's why I chose you. You have outstanding insight and an upright character. Someone like you will surely reach the Transcendent Demon Realm."

"......"

I looked baffled at his irresponsible words. He seemed to feel awkward and changed the subject.

"Ahem. I've explained enough. Make your choice. Which demonic art will you learn?"

He was basically telling me to shut up and choose. Sadly, refusal was not an option.

So, which mental illness should I choose?

I pondered for a moment, then cautiously spoke.

{sep}

I had a few realizations as I listened to Master's explanation. The most peculiar part was the concept of a demonic art's realm.

*It doesn't seem to mean a realm like the Profound Realm or the Transcendent Realm, does it?*

It wasn't a concept like the orthodox sects' Profound Realm. Entering the Ultimate Demon Realm didn't mean you could freely handle enhanced qi, nor was the Transcendent Demon Realm a similar state of power.

Terms like Demonic Initiate Realm, Ultimate Demon Realm, and Transcendent Demon Realm only referred to the mastery level of a specific demonic art. It was just like how the Twenty-Four Plum Blossom Sword Style's mastery is described as third-star, seventh-star, or tenth-star.

In the end, it meant one could become a more outstanding drummer by learning a stronger demonic art and entering its Transcendent Demon Realm. The problem was that stronger arts had more severe side effects, which naturally made reaching the Transcendent Demon Realm harder.

The Master had completely mastered the Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords before learning the Heavenly Demon Divine Art. This suggested one could master a lower-level art, enter its Transcendent Demon Realm, and then learn a stronger one.

So, I asked cautiously.

"Master, of the demonic arts you mentioned, could you tell me which are stronger and more difficult, and which are weaker but easier?"

To my question, Master stroked his beard and replied.

"The power and difficulty of the arts this old man mentioned are all about the same. Excluding the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, every single one is considered among the best within the Divine Cult."

"......"

No, why do I have to learn the hardest one from the very beginning!

The cry lingered on my lips, but I calmed myself as much as possible and asked again.

"Since this disciple is starting to learn a demonic art late in life, I thought it might be better to start with something a little safer, if possible."

"Why would you want to waste time like that? If you have the talent for a more outstanding demonic art, learning a low-level one is just a waste of time. Someone like you should be able to learn it, so trust this old man and choose."

Damn it. I'm cornered.

If I suggest an easier demonic art now, it will mean I don't trust Master. As if to prove my prediction, the gazes of Master and Jin Hayeon grew colder.

I had brought up a needless topic and cornered myself. My mind started racing.

Survival and a peaceful life are the most important things to me. Naturally, the first demonic art that came to mind was the virgin martial art, the Pure Hands Demonic Art.

People have many negative preconceptions about virgin martial arts, but from my perspective, it didn't seem so bad. That is, if the virgin martial art also reduced sexual desire.

Becoming a eunuch might seem negative, but if you had no sexual desire to begin with, the sense of loss wouldn't be so great. You wouldn't need to impress the opposite sex or give anything up for dating or marriage. For a peaceful life, perhaps no martial art was more excellent than a virgin one.

But the Pure Hands Demonic Art was the exact opposite, so I had to give up on it. A virgin martial art where sexual desire gets stronger? How is that any different from an advisor?

Besides that, there were the arts that caused anger management disorder, schizophrenia, depression, mania, and so on. As a martial artist constantly facing life-threatening danger, I thought these were all illnesses that would make it easy to get my head chopped off.

Therefore, after much deliberation, I made my choice.

"I will learn the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, Master."

The heartless sword that reaps souls.

The name made it sound like the side effect was becoming an emotionless psychopath, but this demonic art was different.

"The Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, you say. As this old man explained, its side effect is obsessive-compulsive symptoms. You can't easily overlook even the most trivial things. Do you truly wish to learn the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword?"

The side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was obsessive-compulsive personality disorder. It was a symptom where you couldn't handle matters vaguely. A tiny flaw in a trivial issue would cause excessive stress until it was fixed.

Society often called people with such symptoms 'perfectionists'.

*As a martial artist, this art seems to have the highest survival rate.*

Always calculating risks and striving for perfection. I felt its survival rate would be overwhelmingly higher than the other demonic arts.

"Yes. I will learn the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword."

I answered firmly. The Master stroked his beard and gave me a peculiar look.

"Then, for today, let's first learn the forms of a few techniques of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. After that, I'll teach you the inner arts. Once you've perfected the forms and accumulated some inner arts, we'll learn how to imbue the techniques with that power."

"I understand."

"Then, I'll bring the swords, Lord the Heavenly Demon."

"Hohoho. This old man has a sword. Just bring one for Ilmok."

"I will obey your command."

Jin Hayeon showed her utmost respect, then left. She soon reappeared carrying a sheathed sword. I took the scabbard she handed me. It was heavier than I expected.

SHIING.

I grasped the hilt with my left hand and drew the sword, pausing unwittingly.

*Crazy.*

An incredibly sharp blade had emerged.

"A-are we starting with a real sword from the get-go?"

At my question, both Jin Hayeon and Master looked at me as if I were spouting nonsense.

"You're learning a martial art for killing people. If not with a real sword, what would you train with?"

"W-well... don't you usually start with a wooden sword? Since I'm not used to a sword yet, couldn't I accidentally cut myself or something?"

"I'm aware the hypocrites of the orthodox sects give wooden swords to their young disciples for such reasons. However, you're fighting with a sword to kill someone, yet you worry about hurting yourself. That's also hypocrisy. To resolve to kill an opponent means you accept that you could also die. How can you learn a martial art without at least that much resolve?"

The atmosphere seemed to ask if I was also a cowardly hypocrite from the orthodox sects.

"Hahaha. Y-you're right. Since this disciple is learning a martial art for the first time, I was a little mistaken because of rumors I heard in the Central Plains."

"Hohoho. Pay no mind to the nonsense spouted by the heathens of the jianghu."

"I will do so."

If I didn't, I had an ominous feeling that some great misfortune would befall me.
