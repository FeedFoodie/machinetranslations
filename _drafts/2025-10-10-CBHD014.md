"Did you just call this unruly behavior?"

Madam Cheonghwa asked, her viper-like eyes flashing. I felt a strange sensation as I looked at Madam Cheonghwa. Her face resembled the little girl, Seon'ah, but the mother and child had polar opposite auras.

*I guess the look in one's eyes is what's important, huh?*

I shook off the useless thought and spoke to Madam Cheonghwa. "Isn’t it enough that you barged in without permission? To scold another's servant right in front of her master, what is that if not unruly behavior?"

"Weren’t you the one who ignored the Divine Cult’s customs first, Young Lord?"

A sneer formed on my lips at her accusation. "If that's the case, shouldn’t you have come with proper etiquette to inform me of this custom? You, Madam Cheonghwa, accused my servant of acting like a bodyguard with the Heavenly Demon at her back. How is what you're doing now any different from acting like a bodyguard with the name of the Hyeokryeon Clan at your back?"

"……"

Madam Cheonghwa started to argue but clenched her teeth when I mentioned Master. She glared at me. I did not know what this custom Madam Cheonghwa spoke of was, but the justification was on my side the moment she acted unruly toward me, a disciple of the Heavenly Demon.

Perhaps realizing this, she bowed her head. "I was momentarily agitated and committed a discourtesy."

However, that was merely the minimum courtesy shown because many eyes were watching. Only her tone had become polite. Her gaze was still cold.

*I should probably let it go at this point.*

If I kept pushing Madam Cheonghwa after she had already bowed her head, the justification would shift to her side.

"I accept your apology. Now, could you explain that custom you mentioned? If it is truly my fault, then I, too, will apologize to you, Madam."

At my words, Madam Cheonghwa seemed to hesitate for a moment. She subtly turned her head to look at Seon'ah.

*Hm?*

It was a strange feeling. Affection and pity had momentarily settled in the woman’s incomparably venomous eyes.

*What is this?*

According to the information I heard from Seon'ah, she was a mother who was not even like a mother. Her mother did not give her a single ounce of affection, so Seon'ah had reached the point of calling the doll she hugged ‘Mom’.

"This is a difficult topic to discuss in front of Seon'ah, so could we speak separately?" Madam Cheonghwa asked. She had already turned her head back and was looking at me with that same cold gaze.

{sep}

In the end, Madam Cheonghwa and I went to an empty room in the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Jin Hayeon brought tea and came in with us, just in case. Madam Cheonghwa was also accompanied by one bodyguard.

"So, what is this custom that you can't even speak of in front of Seon'ah, the person concerned?" I asked.

Madam Cheonghwa took a sip of her tea and opened her mouth. "It is a long-standing custom of the Divine Cult to forbid contact with the female children of the Hyeokryeon Clan."

The custom was so absurd that I was momentarily speechless.

"…May I know the reason?"

"It is because of the demonic art that Seon'ah is learning."

"If it's a demonic art of the Hyeokryeon Clan, do you mean the Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords?"

"The Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords is a martial art learned by the boys of the Hyeokryeon Clan. The girls learn a demonic art called the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique."

"What is the side effect of this Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique that requires one to keep a distance from that young child?"

"The side effect is simple. Excessive obsession."

"……"

While I was at a loss for words, Madam Cheonghwa's explanation continued. "When they give their affection to something, they show symptoms of anxiety if the object of their affection is even slightly away from their side. It's also common for them to go mad if something happens to that object."

"So, you're saying you deliberately keep your distance to avoid becoming the target of that obsession?"

"That is correct. Usually, the easiest target to form an attachment to is another person. However, as long as one is human, it's impossible to be together forever. Furthermore, people are bound to get hurt or fall ill at some point. This is especially true for the believers of our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult."

I understood what she meant to some extent, but a question still remained. "Even if everyone avoids her like that, it's not like the side effect of the demonic art will disappear, is it?"

"That is why we avoid them. Until they choose something other than a person as their target. After a few years of everyone avoiding them, they naturally tend to form attachments to things like dolls, pillows, or accessories like hairpins."

As soon as I heard Madam Cheonghwa's explanation, I recalled the doll that Seon'ah carried around, the one she called ‘Mom’.

"Seon'ah began learning the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique last year. This is so she can form an attachment to an object, not a person, before she enters the Demonic Academy, and until a female child of the Hyeokryeon Clan develops symptoms of obsession for something else, it is a custom passed down from long ago for everyone in the Divine Cult to avoid that child."

Having finished her explanation, Madam Cheonghwa took a sip of tea and looked at me. Her gaze seemed to ask, *Now do you understand what you did wrong?*

However, that was an explanation that only those belonging to this insane heretic religion could understand.

*Fuck. Isn't this just child abuse?*

Honestly, even while I engaged in a battle of wills with Madam Cheonghwa, a corner of my mind held a certain anxiety. *What if there's a really serious custom? Did I just cause a huge incident?*

But after hearing the explanation, only displeasure filled the space in my mind that anxiety had occupied.

"…Seeing as you're deliberately telling this story where the child isn't present, it seems Seon'ah doesn't know about this custom yet?"

"If we tell her about the side effect beforehand, it will inevitably cause problems in getting her to form an attachment to an object instead of a person."

Madam Cheonghwa's attitude, which seemed to say it could not be helped, made my emotions boil. Was it because of pity for the poor child? Or perhaps it was the pain of shared suffering. I, too, was scheduled to suffer from a mental illness while learning a demonic art I never wanted, having been forcibly dragged here by Master.

On top of that, just like Seon'ah, my damn life was to be confined in this prison called the Wind Cliff Pavilion. I would dedicate myself only to training and studying under Jin Hayeon's watch.

However.

*At least, I was given a choice.*

That child had to live that way simply because she was born into the Hyeokryeon Clan.

Did she mistake my hardened expression for one of understanding? "Since it seems the Young Lord now understands, we will take our leave."

I watched the backs of Madam Cheonghwa and her bodyguard as she opened the door to leave. My thoughts were a tangled mess. I felt incomparably awful, but it was a long-standing tradition of this insane heretic group, so I did not know what to do.

Just as I was lost in such complex thoughts, Madam Cheonghwa spoke with a cold voice. "Let's go."

The child, who had shrunk back, turned her head and looked at me. I felt disgusted with myself for hesitating as I looked at that young child's desperate gaze.

"Wait a moment."

"Young Lord?" Jin Hayeon, who stood beside me, called out with a rare look of bewilderment as I abruptly rose and approached Seon'ah.

Regardless, I quickly went to Seon'ah, bent my knees, and met the little girl's eyes. "Seon'ah. Do you remember the promise we made earlier?"

"A promise…? Ah!" Seon'ah tilted her large head on her small body, then exclaimed as if she had remembered.

"Th-then, can I come tomorrow too?"

"Of course. We made a promise."

I had said it before Madam Cheonghwa barged in. I needed someone to talk with, so she should visit often. My feelings on that were still the same.

"If you keep working hard on your martial arts training and studies in the family, you'll be able to meet and play with Oppa."

"Eighth Young Master!!" Madam Cheonghwa, who was beside me, shouted venomously as soon as my words ended. "Are you truly intending to ignore the Cult's custom!!"

I, too, let out a furious shout. "Cut the crap about customs!"

That damn custom. Custom. As I kept hearing about it, I remembered those damn customs I experienced during my time as a civil servant, and my anger soared. Various absurd customs existed in the world of civil servants, created by that ridiculously conservative society. It started with things like ‘Probationary Rice Cakes’ and ‘Chief Appreciation Day,’ which were controversial even before I died.

And yet, here too, such a crazy custom was firmly in place. A custom that tormented a young child, no less.

"What is the reason the Cult teaches demonic arts in the first place! Do you teach it so people get consumed by its side effects? Doesn't it mean you're supposed to overcome the side effects? But not only did you create such a crazy custom because of the side effects, you think it makes sense to force that custom on the entire Divine Cult?"

At my question, the look in Madam Cheonghwa's eyes turned completely cold. She asked in a tone devoid of any emotion. "Your current actions. Can you take responsibility for them? I understand that the Young Lord must enter the Demonic Academy in a year, but what do you plan to do after you enter the Demonic Academy?"

She was asking what would happen after I entered the Demonic Academy, with Seon'ah having become obsessed with me. Seon'ah could fall into qi deviation due to the side effect of her demonic art, perhaps just for the reason that I had entered the Demonic Academy.

As I looked at Seon'ah while considering her question, a good idea suddenly came to mind. "I have a plan in mind, so do not worry. If a problem arises after I enter the Demonic Academy, I will take responsibility."

That night, two men were meeting in private in a grand hall located in the deepest part of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. The room was the office of the Heavenly Demon Hall, the residence of the Heavenly Demon, master of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

One was the Heavenly Demon, the master of the Demonic Cult, and the other was a man who appeared to be transitioning from middle to old age.

The Heavenly Demon heard the entire story from the man sitting across from him. He calmly stroked his beard and looked at the man instead of answering right away. Although they might have looked only a dozen or so years apart, that old man was the Heavenly Demon's great-grandnephew and the current Patriarch of the Hyeokryeon Clan.

The reason they did not look nearly forty years apart was that the Heavenly Demon, who had reached a lofty realm, had already experienced his body changing twice. However, that did not mean the Hyeokryeon Patriarch's martial arts were lacking.

The Hyeokryeon Patriarch was also an ‘old man’ who had successfully reached the Transcendent Demon Realm by completely mastering the Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords, the secret technique of the Hyeokryeon Clan. He had only become a little younger because he had undergone Body Reformation once.

This was one of the reasons why the Demonic Cult, full of madmen due to the aftereffects of demonic arts, could function at all. The leaders of the major factions within the Demonic Cult were mostly supreme masters who had learned powerful demonic arts to the level of the Transcendent Demon Realm.

With the Heavenly Demon at the forefront, the old masters who had succeeded in reaching the Transcendent Demon Realm suppressed the demonic practitioners with force, allowing the Demonic Cult to operate.

However, just because they had escaped the side effects of their demonic arts did not mean they could transcend and overlook matters that any ordinary person would get angry about. This was especially true for an act that ignored the customs of the Hyeokryeon Clan.

That custom of the Hyeokryeon Clan was not something the clan itself maintained because they liked it. It started with trivial problems, like being unable to focus on training or studies because of an excessive attachment to a parent or sibling. There were also countless cases where they went mad, unable to accept reality, when the person they were attached to died.

There were also frequent instances where outsiders or servants tried to exploit that side effect, approaching a daughter of the Hyeokryeon Clan to use her like a puppet. The current custom was created because of such experiences accumulated over generations.

Both Seon'ah's mother, Madam Cheonghwa, and her grandfather, the Patriarch who had come to confront the Heavenly Demon, were forcing themselves to ignore the child with hearts that felt like they were swallowing tears of blood. From the perspective of the prestigious Hyeokryeon Clan, the option of not teaching the child demonic arts never existed. Because that was their tradition.

The Heavenly Demon, who hailed from the Hyeokryeon Clan, knew this fact very well. In other words, the reason the Hyeokryeon Clan's head had come to see the Heavenly Demon was not to ask for his esteemed opinion. He had come to pay his final respects to the man who was both the Heavenly Demon and his great-granduncle before killing his disciple.

However, the words that came from the Heavenly Demon's mouth were beyond the Hyeokryeon Clan head's expectations.

"Just leave it be."

"…What do you mean by that?" The head of the Hyeokryeon Clan forced himself to swallow the words, *Do you mean that the disciple you just took in is more important than the Hyeokryeon Clan?*

However, instead of scolding him for his rudeness, the Heavenly Demon once again uttered strange words. "Aren't you curious? Whether that boy can break the shackles of the Hyeokryeon Clan?"

He was not saying this simply because the boy was his disciple. The Heavenly Demon was recalling the drinking party where the Senior Brother disciples had gathered. Ilmok, at such a young age, had conceived of a truly profound system that even he himself had not thought of. So, he naturally had expectations. He wondered if that boy would come up with some profound method this time as well.

"If he fails, what will you do?" the head of the Hyeokryeon Clan asked after a pause.

The Heavenly Demon replied, "At that time, you may deal with this old man's youngest disciple as the Hyeokryeon Clan wishes. In addition, I will take Seon'ah as my new youngest disciple. This old man will personally look after her at all times so her demonic art doesn't run rampant, so do not worry."

At the Heavenly Demon's answer, the head of the Hyeokryeon Clan showed a surprised expression for the first time since he had arrived. "Do you trust that youngest disciple so much?"

To that question, the Heavenly Demon simply stroked his beard with a ‘hoho’ laugh, without giving a particular answer.

*Trust, you say. Hohoho.*

It was more anticipation than trust.

*To already be preparing to make allies. Truly remarkable. Hohoho, and of all things, the Hyeokryeon Clan.*

If he succeeds in this, the Hyeokryeon Clan will show absolute support for Ilmok. Even after he, who was already over a hundred years old, leaves this world, and Ilmok can no longer be called ‘the Heavenly Demon's disciple’. The Heavenly Demon was anticipating that Ilmok might have calculated that far before acting.

*The wheels have already started turning, so all that's left is to wait for the results.*

Whether Ilmok will lose his life to the Hyeokryeon Clan in the future. Or whether he will gain the Hyeokryeon Clan, a great pillar of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, as an ally.

The Heavenly Demon truly looked forward to the outcome.
