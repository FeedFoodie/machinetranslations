Chapter 38: Obsession

Come to think of it, the situation was even more serious than what I had seen in the nightmare.
This was because the obsessive symptoms I was showing were not about my handwriting, of all things, but about martial arts itself.
Perfecting my handwriting?
The standard for ‘perfection’ might differ from person to person, but most people have an ideal script in their minds.
From my perspective as someone from the modern world, it would be one of the so-called Gulim, Batang, or Gungseo fonts that appear when creating a document.
Of course, it would take arduous effort to create a script as precise as one on a computer.
*Even so, it's a drop in the bucket compared to martial arts.*
In particular, the upper limit of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword I was learning was absurdly high.
The Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was composed of a mere twenty-two techniques. And of those, I had only learned fourteen so far.
However, the small number of techniques was due to the unique nature of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.
Each technique of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was nothing more than a ‘concept’.
For example, the first technique, the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw, was a sword art that integrated both offense and defense, allowing one to attack an enemy while drawing the sword or block a surprise attack.
And that concept itself was the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw.
So, whether I stepped back and swung horizontally while drawing my sword, or advanced forward and executed a thrust, it was still the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw.
The same was true for the thrusting techniques, the Soul-Reaping Flash and the Soul-Capturing Sword.
The Soul-Reaping Flash was a concise and fast thrust, but a thrust could be aimed at the opponent's head, their feet, or their heart.
They were all thrusts, but the detailed movements were completely different. The motion for thrusting at the head above could never be the same as the motion for thrusting at the feet on the ground.
In other words, even the same Soul-Reaping Flash could become dozens, even hundreds of different movements depending on its application.
Furthermore, the Soul-Capturing Sword, which started from the same stance as the Soul-Reaping Flash, was a thrust that utilized a feint, unlike the Soul-Reaping Flash. Its speed was slower than the Soul-Reaping Flash, but it was an artistry that exploited openings by changing the sword's path mid-way.
Thus, the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was not a martial art that forced a specific sword path, but one that taught concepts of thrusting, slashing, and defending, so each technique could be applied in dozens of different stances.
On top of that, the real problem was the goal pursued by the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.
The Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword is a sword art where all techniques flow together ceaselessly, gradually cornering the opponent and reaping their very soul.
I had to make it so that all fourteen techniques, each with dozens of possible applications, could connect seamlessly. And this had to be possible regardless of the order of the techniques.
Considering all that, the number of permutations exceeded tens of thousands.
And I was trying to make all of them ‘perfect’?
*This is the perfect way to go insane.*
Perhaps it was designed with this side effect to help one meet that absurdly high standard.
It induced the completion of that ridiculous sword art by making one obsessively pursue perfection. Forcibly, through mental illness.
However, the true intention of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was not the most important thing right now.
The problem was that I was learning it, and my proficiency had increased.
"Hoo."
My arms and legs were trembling, a testament to how I had been crazed with the sword for a few hours.
"Are you alright, Young Lord?"
Perhaps because I had finally stopped training, Jin Hayeon, who had been watching from the side, spoke to me.
"I think I should rest for today."
"That's a good idea. It's already past the Hour of the Pig (9-11 PM), so it would be best to stop and rest for tomorrow's sake."
After replying, Jin Hayeon prepared warm water for a bath.
After she served me refreshing Chugunggwa tea and attended to my hot bath, a wave of exhaustion washed over the body that had been tormented by obsession while swinging the sword.
My entire body was screaming at me to lie down on the bed and pass out immediately.
"……"
Entering the room, I hesitated, unable to move forward or back.
Like a dragonfly caught in a spider's web.
My body wanted to head for the bed, but my eyes were fixed on one spot and wouldn't move.
[You have to write in your diary before you sleep.]
[It's something you do every day, isn't it?]
[Don't you have to make your handwriting perfect?]
It was as if a voice was whispering such things from a corner of my mind.

The next day, early in the morning.
Jin Hayeon, who had brought the breakfast table, asked with her usual deadpan expression.
"Young Lord. Are you alright? You look very tired."
Just as she said, I felt like I was about to die from exhaustion. It seemed to be showing on my face.
"It's because I didn't get enough sleep last night."
I had tried my best to ignore the diary and go to sleep. In fact, I had succeeded in forcing my body onto the bed.
The only problem was.
"The thought of something I hadn't done kept popping into my head, so I couldn't fall asleep at all."
In the end, I gave up and was only able to sleep after I reluctantly wrote in my diary.
It was a terrible experience.
[This damn demonic art.]
[I'm dead tired!! Why am I writing in a diary at this hour with this exhausted body!]
[My arm is trembling, so the handwriting is weird!! I have to fix the handwriting!!]
[Just let me sleep, you crazy bastard!]
To think I was writing in my diary to curse the side effect of the demonic art, but the diary itself was being written because of the side effect of the demonic art.
It was the very definition of a contradiction. After wrestling with the diary while stuck in a dilemma, I was barely able to get some sleep.
And now, I had woken up without even having slept for two full hours.
Jin Hayeon, who had been listening quietly to my half-complaint, seemed to ponder something with a frown on her fine brow before bringing up something unexpected.
"How about asking Martial Artist Gwan?"
"Martial Artist Gwan, you say?"
Martial Artist Gwan referred to Gwan Muyeol, one of the two bodyguard martial artists recently assigned to me.
I just couldn't understand why that man was being brought up now.
To my question, Jin Hayeon replied with her usual bluntness.
"As far as I know, Martial Artist Gwan is also learning a demonic art with a similar type of side effect as you, Young Lord."
!!!
"His proficiency is also higher than yours, Young Lord, so wouldn't he know some way to control the side effect?"
"That's a truly excellent idea!"
It felt like a lifeline had been lowered from somewhere as the sky was falling.
I hurriedly summoned Gwan Muyeol to my room.
"Is there a problem?"
I briefly explained what happened last night and about the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword to Gwan Muyeol, who asked in a tone just as blunt as Jin Hayeon's, and then I questioned him.
"I heard from Miss Jin that you, Martial Artist Gwan, also suffer from a similar side effect. Is that true?"
"It's a little different, but I do suffer from obsessive-compulsive symptoms."
"Then, how do you overcome that side effect, Martial Artist Gwan?"
At my question, Gwan Muyeol frowned and asked back.
"Thanks to that side effect, you can heighten your achievements in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, so is there really a need to think so negatively about it?"
Just the kind of thinking you'd expect from madmen obsessed with martial arts.
In fact, as he said, the obsession wasn't necessarily a bad thing.
Perfectionism doesn't always bring only negative thoughts. While the obsession causes immense stress, the sense of accomplishment from handling a task 'perfectly' is also immense.
*It was like that yesterday when I was training with the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.*
When I felt something was wrong, I swung the sword obsessively, but it wasn't just difficult.
The sense of accomplishment I felt upon succeeding in correcting the flawed part was enormous. That's why I became even more immersed in the sword, and the problem was that the obsessive symptoms worsened.
And I didn't want to live like that. My dream was to live a comfortable life of leisure.
However, I couldn't reveal my dream here, so I decided to make a plausible excuse.
"However, shouldn't one take a long-term view of martial arts? If you stay up all night to raise your proficiency for a single day, it becomes difficult to even move the next. There's a need to know when to stop."
Perhaps finding my answer logical enough, Gwan Muyeol seemed to ponder for a moment before speaking.
"The simplest method is to create a conflicting obsession."
???
When I made a face showing I didn't understand, he added an explanation.
"I heard from Miss Jin that you, Young Lord, obsessively created and followed a daily schedule even before the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword began."
"……"
I thought it was natural as a student preparing for exams, but it was a moment I realized others might see it as an obsession.
"Therefore, if you truly become obsessed with that schedule, you should be able to stop yourself from training with the sword excessively. The sense of accomplishment from following the schedule and the sense of self-loathing from stopping your training will mix, and even if it feels a bit uncomfortable, you should be able to get by."
He spoke as if from personal experience.
*So it's to counter poison with poison, is it?*
Stopping an obsession with another obsession... it sounded absurd, but also plausible.
Above all, the man saying it was speaking from experience.
I had obtained some seemingly useful information, but I still had a question, so I asked Gwan Muyeol further.
"Then, what do you do when that's not possible? I mean, when you can't act on your obsession due to external factors."
As I asked, something came to mind, and I gave a specific example.
"Mm. For example, back when we went to Gaochang County and Hejing County. You couldn't train me as per Master's orders, right? Because I was unavoidably 'injured'."
As soon as I finished speaking, Gwan Muyeol's expression crumpled, as if recalling that time.
I thought this man was being difficult simply because of his personality, but now I saw it was likely because of his obsession.
To be precise, it was the self-loathing that came from not being able to perfectly carry out Master's orders. And it seemed he was like that because I was the cause of that self-loathing.
*Come to think of it, this isn't someone else's problem, is it?*
What if, while I was swinging my sword like a madman due to the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, someone intervened, tied my limbs, and stopped me from swinging it?
*...I don't know about now, but once my proficiency gets higher, I might foam at the mouth and have a seizure.*
When my thoughts reached that point, my perspective on Gwan Muyeol changed.
*He endured that well.*
If it were me, I probably would have dragged him out to train by force, or my eyes would have rolled back in my head and I would have caused some kind of incident.
I couldn't empathize at all before, but now that I was starting to suffer from obsession, I could relate.
It reminded me of stories I often heard in the modern world. Anecdotes about CEOs of large foreign corporations, known for being perfectionists, who acted like psychopaths toward their colleagues and subordinates.
Before, I just thought those CEOs were crazy bastards, but now that I was suffering from obsession, my thinking changed.
If they didn't, they probably couldn't stand it themselves.
However, that didn't mean I wanted to become one of those people. That's why I was even more curious about Gwan Muyeol's coping mechanism.
When things don't go his way, and he feels like he's going crazy from the self-loathing brought on by his obsession. How on earth does he cope?
Meeting my expectant gaze, Gwan Muyeol, his face still crumpled, pondered for a long while before carefully opening his mouth.
"When I can no longer bear the self-loathing, I punish myself."
For some reason, as Gwan Muyeol said this, a dazed expression appeared on his face, and a flush spread across his cheeks.
"!?"
Seeing that grotesque sight, a certain scene naturally formed in my mind.
Gwan Muyeol shouting, 'I'm a bad boy! Please punish me!' and the image of a woman holding a whip.
*damn it. The Demonic Cult is the Demonic Cult, after all.*
He seemed relatively normal, but a demonic cultist was a demonic cultist.
"And, recently… Ah. It's nothing."
Gwan Muyeol, who was about to say more, hesitated and then avoided answering.
"Ahem. There is a method I've been trying recently, but it's not a sure thing, so I will tell you about it later."
"...I understand."
Somehow, I felt like I was about to learn a grown man's secret I shouldn't know, so I gave up on digging further.

The last part wasn't very helpful, but I still gained something from my conversation with Gwan Muyeol.
*Enduring with a conflicting obsession.*
By having an obsession with my schedule, I could avoid training with the sword until my body breaks down.
It seemed like a fairly useful method.
Of course, this was a temporary measure, and I needed a fundamental solution. After all, situations could arise where I couldn't stick to my schedule. For example, if I had to go on another business trip.
*As for the other solution Gwan Muyeol told me about... let's save that as a last resort.*
It felt like I would be crossing a line that shouldn't be crossed. I didn't want to develop that kind of sexual preference.
Although there was a beautiful woman right beside me who seemed like she would gladly provide both verbal abuse and whipping if I asked.
In any case, after the morning lessons and external arts training with the woman who seemed to have an outstanding talent for verbal abuse and whipping were over.
As usual, visitors came to the Wind Cliff Pavilion during the break time.
Seon'ah, Eldest Senior Brother, and Hang Geon.
Following the usual trio, to my surprise, the third Senior Brother and Guyang Mun also came to the Wind Cliff Pavilion together.
"You were alive?"
"Huh? What do you mean by that, Young Lord?"
When I asked the question without thinking, Guyang Mun replied with a puzzled face.
"Ahem. It's nothing. More importantly, how did you two end up coming together?"
Feeling a pang of guilt, I subtly changed the subject, and the third Senior Brother answered with a gloomy laugh.
"fufu. After hearing your story, Junior Brother, I went to find Master Guyang and had a talk about martial arts, and I found out he's a very interesting Master."
"I-Is that so?"
"No matter what I said, he always had something positive to say. He doesn't offer excellent solutions like you, Junior Brother, but just listening to him helped calm my mind a little."
Following the third Senior Brother's explanation, Guyang Mun added with a hearty laugh.
"Hahaha. How could I possibly compare to Young Lord Ilmok? I'm in a position to receive advice from the Young Lord myself, aren't I? Hahaha."
"fufu. Come to think of it, we did talk about that."
The third Senior Brother laughed gloomily and nudged Guyang Mun's side with his elbow. Guyang Mun suddenly put on an awkward expression and began to take something out of his sleeve.
"Ahem. Hayeon! I couldn't give this to you last time because the Young Lords were here, but I just came back from a mission and..."
"From what I've heard, our Master Guyang obtained this item with great difficulty. Take a close look at it."
Guyang Mun hesitantly started making his move on the cold-faced Jin Hayeon, while the third Senior Brother provided covering fire from the side.
It was truly a bizarre sight.
*What is this? A combination of positivity and negativity?*
If those two were to become one, it would be the very picture of perfect bipolar disorder.
"If it's an item you obtained with such difficulty, it would be right for you to use it yourself, Master Guyang."
Of course, even with their combined efforts, there seemed to be no chance of conquering Jin Hayeon.
Indeed, she was a true master of verbal abuse and whipping.
