I realized the situation was far more serious than my nightmare suggested. My obsessive symptoms were not about my handwriting, but about martial arts itself.

Perfecting my handwriting? The standard for ‘perfection’ differed for everyone, but most people had an ideal script in mind. As someone from the modern world, my ideal was a font like Gulim, Batang, or Gungseo from a word processor. Of course, creating a script as precise as a computer's would take arduous effort.

*Even so, it's a drop in the bucket compared to martial arts.*

The upper limit of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword I was learning was absurdly high. The art consisted of only twenty-two techniques, and I had only learned fourteen so far. The small number of techniques was due to the unique nature of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. Each technique was merely a ‘concept’.

For example, the first technique, the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw, integrated both offense and defense. It allowed one to attack an enemy while drawing the sword or to block a surprise attack. That concept itself was the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw. So, whether I stepped back and swung horizontally or advanced with a thrust, it was still the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw.

The same applied to the thrusting techniques, the Soul-Reaping Flash and the Soul-Capturing Sword. The Soul-Reaping Flash was a concise, fast thrust, but it could be aimed at an opponent's head, feet, or heart. They were all thrusts, but the detailed movements were completely different. The motion for thrusting at the head could never be the same as thrusting at the feet.

This meant even the same Soul-Reaping Flash could become dozens, even hundreds, of different movements depending on its application. Furthermore, the Soul-Capturing Sword started from the same stance but was a thrust that used a feint. Its speed was slower than the Soul-Reaping Flash, but it was an artistry that exploited openings by changing the sword's path mid-way.

The Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword did not force a specific sword path. Instead, it taught concepts of thrusting, slashing, and defending, so each technique could be applied in dozens of different stances.

The real problem was the goal pursued by the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. It is a sword art where all techniques flow together ceaselessly, gradually cornering the opponent and reaping their very soul. I had to connect all fourteen techniques, each with dozens of applications, seamlessly. This had to be possible regardless of their order.

Considering all that, the number of permutations exceeded tens of thousands, and I was trying to make all of them ‘perfect’?

*This is the perfect way to go insane.*

Perhaps the art was designed with this side effect to help one meet that absurdly high standard. It forced the completion of that ridiculous sword art by making one obsessively pursue perfection through mental illness.

However, the true intention of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was not the most important thing right now. The problem was that I was learning it, and my proficiency had increased.

"......"

My arms and legs trembled, a testament to the hours I had spent crazed with the sword.

Jin Hayeon, who had been watching from the side, finally spoke now that I had stopped training. "Are you alright, Young Lord?"

"I think I should rest for today."

"That's a good idea. It's already past the Hour of the Pig (9-11 PM), so it would be best to stop and rest for tomorrow's sake."

Jin Hayeon replied and prepared warm water for a bath. After she served me refreshing Chugunggwa tea and attended to my hot bath, a wave of exhaustion washed over me. My body, tormented by obsession, screamed at me to lie down on the bed and pass out immediately.

"……"

I entered the room and hesitated, unable to move forward or back, like a dragonfly caught in a spider's web. My body wanted the bed, but my eyes were fixed on one spot.

[You have to write in your diary before you sleep.]
[It's something you do every day, isn't it?]
[Don't you have to make your handwriting perfect?]

A voice seemed to whisper these things from a corner of my mind.

{sep}

The next day, early in the morning, Jin Hayeon brought the breakfast table.

She asked with her usual deadpan expression, "Young Lord. Are you alright? You look very tired."

Just as she said, I felt like I was about to die from exhaustion, and it must have shown on my face.

"It's because I didn't get enough sleep last night."

I had tried my best to ignore the diary and go to sleep. I had even succeeded in forcing my body onto the bed. The only problem was that the thought of something I had not done kept popping into my head, so I could not fall asleep at all. In the end, I gave up and only managed to sleep after I reluctantly wrote in my diary.

It was a terrible experience.

[This damn demonic art.]
[I'm dead tired!! Why am I writing in a diary at this hour with this exhausted body!]
[My arm is trembling, so the handwriting is weird!! I have to fix the handwriting!!]
[Just let me sleep, you crazy bastard!]

I was writing in my diary to curse the side effect of the demonic art, but the diary itself was being written because of that same side effect. It was the very definition of a contradiction. After wrestling with the diary, I barely got any sleep. Now, I had woken up without even two full hours of rest.

Jin Hayeon listened quietly to my half-complaint. She seemed to ponder something with a frown on her fine brow before bringing up something unexpected.

"How about asking Martial Artist Gwan?"

"Martial Artist Gwan, you say?"

Martial Artist Gwan referred to Gwan Muyeol, one of the two bodyguard martial artists recently assigned to me. I could not understand why his name was coming up now.

Jin Hayeon replied with her usual bluntness. "As far as I know, Martial Artist Gwan is also learning a demonic art with a similar type of side effect as you, Young Lord."

!!!

"His proficiency is also higher than yours, Young Lord, so wouldn't he know some way to control the side effect?"

"That's a truly excellent idea!"

It felt like a lifeline lowered from a falling sky. I hurriedly summoned Gwan Muyeol to my room.

"Is there a problem?" Gwan Muyeol asked in a tone just as blunt as Jin Hayeon's.

I briefly explained what happened last night and about the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. Then I questioned him. "I heard from Miss Jin that you, Martial Artist Gwan, also suffer from a similar side effect. Is that true?"

"It's a little different, but I do suffer from obsessive-compulsive symptoms."

"Then, how do you overcome that side effect, Martial Artist Gwan?"

Gwan Muyeol frowned at my question and asked back, "Thanks to that side effect, you can heighten your achievements in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. Is there really a need to think so negatively about it?"

That was just the kind of thinking I would expect from madmen obsessed with martial arts. In fact, as he said, the obsession was not necessarily a bad thing. Perfectionism did not always bring only negative thoughts. While the obsession caused immense stress, the sense of accomplishment from handling a task 'perfectly' was also immense.

*It was like that yesterday when I was training with the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.*

When I felt something was wrong, I swung the sword obsessively. It was difficult, but the sense of accomplishment I felt upon correcting the flawed part was enormous. That was why I became even more immersed in the sword, which only worsened the obsessive symptoms.

I did not want to live like that. My dream was to live a comfortable life of leisure. However, I could not reveal my dream here, so I made a plausible excuse.

"However, shouldn't one take a long-term view of martial arts? If you stay up all night to raise your proficiency for a single day, it becomes difficult to even move the next. There's a need to know when to stop."

Gwan Muyeol seemed to find my answer logical enough. He pondered for a moment before speaking.

"The simplest method is to create a conflicting obsession."

???

I made a face that showed I did not understand, so he explained.

"I heard from Miss Jin that you, Young Lord, obsessively created and followed a daily schedule even before the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword began."

"……"

I thought it was natural for a student preparing for exams, but I realized then that others might see it as an obsession.

"Therefore, if you truly become obsessed with that schedule, you should be able to stop yourself from training with the sword excessively. The sense of accomplishment from following the schedule and the self-loathing from stopping your training will mix. Even if it feels a bit uncomfortable, you should be able to get by."

He spoke as if from personal experience.

*So it's to counter poison with poison, is it?*

Stopping an obsession with another obsession sounded absurd, but also plausible. Above all, the man saying it was speaking from experience. I had obtained some seemingly useful information, but I still had a question.

I asked Gwan Muyeol further. "Then, what do you do when that's not possible? I mean, when you can't act on your obsession due to external factors."

As I asked, I thought of a specific example. "Mm. For example, back when we went to Gaochang County and Hejing County. You couldn't train me as per the Master's orders, right? Because I was unavoidably 'injured'."

As soon as I finished speaking, Gwan Muyeol's expression crumpled as if recalling that time. I had thought the man was being difficult simply because of his personality, but now I saw it was likely because of his obsession. To be precise, it was the self-loathing from not being able to perfectly carry out the Master's orders. He was like that because I was the cause of that self-loathing.

*Come to think of it, this isn't someone else's problem, is it?*

What if someone intervened while I was swinging my sword like a madman? What if they tied my limbs and stopped me?

*...I don't know about now, but once my proficiency gets higher, I might foam at the mouth and have a seizure.*

When my thoughts reached that point, my perspective on Gwan Muyeol changed.

*He endured that well.*

If it were me, I probably would have dragged him out to train by force, or my eyes would have rolled back in my head and I would have caused some kind of incident. I could not empathize at all before, but now that I was starting to suffer from obsession, I could relate.

It reminded me of stories I often heard in the modern world about perfectionist CEOs of large foreign corporations who acted like psychopaths toward their colleagues and subordinates. Before, I just thought those CEOs were crazy bastards. Now that I was suffering from obsession, my thinking changed. They probably could not stand it themselves otherwise.

However, that did not mean I wanted to become one of those people. That was why I was even more curious about Gwan Muyeol's coping mechanism. How did he cope when things did not go his way, when he felt like he was going crazy from self-loathing?

Gwan Muyeol met my expectant gaze. His face was still crumpled as he pondered for a long while before carefully opening his mouth.

"When I can no longer bear the self-loathing, I punish myself."

As Gwan Muyeol said this, a dazed expression appeared on his face, and a flush spread across his cheeks.

"!?"

Seeing that grotesque sight, a certain scene naturally formed in my mind. Gwan Muyeol shouting, 'I'm a bad boy! Please punish me!' and the image of a woman holding a whip.

*Damn it. The Demonic Cult is the Demonic Cult, after all.*

He seemed relatively normal, but a demonic cultist was a demonic cultist.

"And, recently… Ah. It's nothing."

Gwan Muyeol started to say more, then hesitated and avoided answering.

"Ahem. There is a method I've been trying recently, but it's not a sure thing, so I will tell you about it later."

"...I understand."

Somehow, I felt like I was about to learn a grown man's secret I should not know, so I gave up on digging further.

{sep}

The last part was not very helpful, but I still gained something from my conversation with Gwan Muyeol.

*Enduring with a conflicting obsession.*

By having an obsession with my schedule, I could avoid training with the sword until my body broke down. It seemed like a fairly useful method. Of course, this was a temporary measure. I needed a fundamental solution for situations where I could not stick to my schedule, like another business trip.

*As for the other solution Gwan Muyeol told me about... let's save that as a last resort.*

It felt like I would be crossing a line that should not be crossed. I did not want to develop that kind of sexual preference, although there was a beautiful woman right beside me who seemed like she would gladly provide both verbal abuse and whipping if I asked.

In any case, the morning lessons and external arts training with the woman who seemed to have an outstanding talent for verbal abuse and whipping were over. As usual, visitors came to the Wind Cliff Pavilion during the break.

Seon'ah, Eldest Senior Brother, and Hang Geon. Following the usual trio, the third Senior Brother and Guyang Mun also came together, much to my surprise.

"You were alive?"

"Huh? What do you mean by that, Young Lord?" Guyang Mun replied with a puzzled face when I asked the question without thinking.

"Ahem. It's nothing. More importantly, how did you two end up coming together?" I felt a pang of guilt and subtly changed the subject.

The third Senior Brother answered with a gloomy laugh. "fufu. After hearing your story, Junior Brother, I went to find Master Guyang and had a talk about martial arts. I found out he's a very interesting Master."

"I-Is that so?"

"No matter what I said, he always had something positive to say. He doesn't offer excellent solutions like you, Junior Brother, but just listening to him helped calm my mind a little."

Following the third Senior Brother's explanation, Guyang Mun added with a hearty laugh. "Hahaha. How could I possibly compare to Young Lord Ilmok? I'm in a position to receive advice from the Young Lord myself, aren't I? Hahaha."

"fufu. Come to think of it, we did talk about that." The third Senior Brother laughed gloomily and nudged Guyang Mun's side with his elbow.

Guyang Mun suddenly put on an awkward expression and began to take something out of his sleeve. "Ahem. Hayeon! I couldn't give this to you last time because the Young Lords were here, but I just came back from a mission and..."

"From what I've heard, our Master Guyang obtained this item with great difficulty. Take a close look at it." The third Senior Brother provided covering fire from the side as Guyang Mun hesitantly made his move on the cold-faced Jin Hayeon.

It was truly a bizarre sight.

*What is this? A combination of positivity and negativity?*

If those two were to become one, it would be the very picture of perfect bipolar disorder.

"If it's an item you obtained with such difficulty, it would be right for you to use it yourself, Master Guyang."

Of course, even with their combined efforts, there seemed to be no chance of conquering Jin Hayeon. Indeed, she was a true master of verbal abuse and whipping.
