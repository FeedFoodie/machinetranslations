I had a new goal to assassinate the Flying Dragon Hall Director, but my daily life did not change. This was a relief. I was creating a compulsive routine, and any disruption would have caused inconvenient situations.

My routine continued as usual, but things changed for the frequent visitors of the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Jin Hayeon lived with me, along with two bodyguard martial artists. Seon'ah and Master also appeared at the same time every day, as if it were their home. Besides these five, other visitors changed daily.

Guyang Mun occasionally visited to report his absences. "Hahaha. The White Turtle Brigade has a mission, so it seems I won't be able to visit for a while, Young Lord." He spoke to me but kept glancing at Jin Hayeon.

"Is that so," I replied.

"Fufu. Travel safely, Master Guyang."

Third Senior Brother saw Guyang Mun off with a smile. He also frequently left his post for Dark Shadow Hall work, so perhaps the two had become close friends.

*...I can't bring myself to ask what kind of mission it is.*

The word 'assassination' flickered in my mind. I considered the martial arts Third Senior Brother learned and the nature of a group like the Dark Shadow Hall.

I imagined them clad entirely in black, their swords coated in charcoal to prevent reflections. They would move through the night or hide somewhere, waiting. After tense and precarious moments, they would swiftly strike down their target and disappear, just like in a movie.

The only problem was the disconnect I felt. The person who was so kind and cheerful to me was doing such things.

After Guyang Mun left, I casually asked Seon'ah a question. "Seon'ah, where do you want to go after you complete the Demonic Academy?"

I was curious about the dream of the little kid I had grown close to. My curiosity grew because I had recently finished the history books and started the law books. I now knew about all the different organizations within the Demonic Cult.

The only minor problem was that the Demonic Cult did not offer a wide range of majors.

Eldest Senior Brother seemed interested in our conversation and suddenly burst into hearty laughter. "Hahahaha! How about the Red Tiger Brigade that I lead? As the Young Lady of the Hyeokryeon Clan, your talent is outstanding, so you'd be most welcome! Hahahaha!"

The Demonic Cult's military forces had sixteen brigades. Their names mixed the colors red, blue, black, and white with the four celestial beasts: Dragon, Tiger, Turtle, and Sparrow. Examples included the Red Tiger Brigade, which Eldest Senior Brother led, and the White Turtle Brigade, which Guyang Mun belonged to.

Each brigade had about forty members, and their roles were subtly different. Some, like the White Turtle Brigade, went on expeditions for missions. Others protected the Main Headquarters by surrounding it.

The Main Headquarters, the Billion Mountains, bordered the region that the Central Plains people called the 'Outer Lands'. From my perspective, they were like border guards.

The Billion Mountains contained more than just the Main Headquarters. The Demonic Academy I had to enter was on another peak, and there was also a dangerous mountain called the Forgotten Ridge. So, the brigades also protected and monitored places like the Demonic Academy and the Forgotten Ridge within the Billion Mountains.

*...Though for the Forgotten Ridge, it's closer to surveillance than protection.*

I shuddered as I recalled Jin Hayeon's explanation of the Forgotten Ridge.

Seon'ah seemed burdened by Eldest Senior Brother's sales pitch. She grabbed my sleeve tightly, clung to me, and shook her head. "I-I don't want to join the Red Tiger Brigade because you have to leave the cult often."

"!!!"

Eldest Senior Brother's eyes widened in surprise at Seon'ah's refusal. Then he laughed loudly. "Hahahaha! What are you talking about! Traditionally, a martial artist must have various experiences to grow! As nice as it is here, you can't reach a high position by just staying cooped up in the cult!"

The man in his forties advising a girl in her early teens looked exactly like a condescending old man nagging.

Third Senior Brother, who had suddenly become gloomy, chimed in. "If you don't like going outside, then you probably wouldn't like our Dark Shadow Hall either...."

The Dark Shadow Hall, which Third Senior Brother belonged to, was a kind of intelligence agency. It felt like they also handled assassinations. Because of its nature, its members were often away from the cult.

No, more importantly.

*You shouldn't be recommending things like assassination or intelligence operations to a twelve-year-old!*

I felt like Seon'ah would pick up strange habits if left with those two, so I quickly intervened. "Hahaha. Eldest Senior Brother, you're scheduled to move to another place soon anyway, aren't you? And Third Senior Brother, the work of the Dark Shadow Hall seems too rough for the still-young Seon'ah."

The title of Red Tiger Commander was too low for Eldest Senior Brother, who had entered the Transcendent Demon Realm. I had heard that because he was tied down at the Main Headquarters handling school matters, the Red Tiger Brigade was also unable to go on missions. I figured he would be transferred soon.

"And Seon'ah, if you don't want to leave the cult, how about the Guardian Hall or the Inquisition?"

The Guardian Hall was a group of bodyguards protecting key figures and facilities in the Demonic Cult. The Inquisition executed or imprisoned apostates who broke the laws.

Other options included the five prestigious families like the Hyeokryeon Clan, the teahouse that managed food, and the Bodyguard Hall that Jin Hayeon belonged to. There was also the Council of Elders, though Seon'ah would never go there.

The Demonic Cult's Council of Elders was a peculiar place. From my modern perspective, it was like a gathering of honorary retirees who failed to become executives. It was a place for those who reached mid-level management but failed to enter the Transcendent Demon Realm with high-level demonic art. They couldn't become heads of their groups, and having reached a certain age, they handed their positions to the rising youth.

Before Seon'ah could answer my suggestion, Eldest Senior Brother spoke up. "Hmm. Members of the Guardian Hall and the Inquisition also leave the cult from time to time."

It was a sharp point, uncharacteristic of Eldest Senior Brother.

*Right, he's not a complete idiot.*

What Eldest Senior Brother said was true. If a guarded person had to leave the cult, the bodyguards would accompany them, just as Jang Hoe and Gwan Muyeol had traveled with me through Xinjiang. The Inquisition also sometimes left the Main Headquarters to catch escaped apostates.

But it seemed Eldest Senior Brother had a different reason for pointing that out.

*Tsk! Kids these days! They only try to avoid hard work! Back in my day~.*

That was the sentiment in Eldest Senior Brother's gaze as he looked at Seon'ah. I thought only Master was a condescending old man, but Eldest Senior Brother was just as bad. Then again, the older generation looking down on the younger generation is a human tradition with a long, proud history. They say even ancient artifacts have been found with inscriptions about the problems with 'kids these days'.

But Seon'ah replied to Eldest Senior Brother's point with sparkling eyes. "I'll go to the Guardian Hall!"

"You'll have to go outside a lot in the Guardian Hall too!"

"That's okay! As long as I'm going with Oppa," Seon'ah said, looking at me with those sparkling eyes.

"Uwahahaha! I see now! It's not that you hate leaving the cult, but that you hate being separated from our Youngest!! Kuhahaha!" Eldest Senior Brother finally realized Seon'ah's true feelings and burst out laughing.

Although she had declared it herself, Seon'ah seemed embarrassed now that Eldest Senior Brother had said it so bluntly. She pressed herself closer to me.

I stroked the cute little girl's head and glanced to the side. Gwan Muyeol stood there, staring into space with a blunt expression as if our chatter had nothing to do with him. I pictured Jang Hoe guarding the entrance of the Wind Cliff Pavilion like a human doorbell, and a thought occurred to me.

*Maybe it would be better to be guarded by Seon'ah than to be stuck with these gloomy men?*

Above all, Seon'ah felt like she was definitively 'on my side'. I thought I could truly relax with her.

The days passed quickly, spinning like a hamster wheel. More than half a year had passed since Ilmok was dragged to the heretic group known as the Demonic Cult. Ilmok had finally mastered the law books, following the scriptures and history books.

Of course, perfectly memorizing something after one read was impossible unless you were a genius. He needed to review constantly until the entrance examination.

Apart from that, however, Ilmok felt the need to slightly change his routine. He would reduce his morning study time and increase his training time.

He worried about the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, but over the past two months, Ilmok had realized something. He realized the obvious fact that the realm of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword and its side effect were proportional.

In other words.

*As long as my realm doesn't increase rapidly, the possibility of the side effect running rampant is low.*

Ilmok had become acutely aware of his side effect on two occasions. The first time he circulated his inner arts, and when he absorbed the Explosive Demonic Pill and underwent meridian cleansing. In short, it was when his realm had increased explosively.

As long as his realm did not rise drastically, the risk of going mad or falling into qi deviation was 'less'. Conversely, it meant that if his realm rose slowly, the side effect would seep in little by little without him noticing.

Despite knowing this, Ilmok chose to raise his realm, albeit slowly. It was unavoidable if he wanted to succeed in entering the Demonic Academy and getting revenge for Mr. Taehyun.

And so, Ilmok finished his scheduled routine and went to bed, just like any other day. Some time passed.

It was the new moon, the one night a month when the moon hides itself in darkness.

A darkish something took advantage of the unusually dark sky and carefully entered the Wind Cliff Pavilion. The being gave off no presence as it moved toward Ilmok's quarters like a shadow.

WHOOSH.

With the faint sound of the night breeze, the being stealthily opened the door and slipped inside.

The being entered the quarters and was greeted by the sight of Ilmok, calmly sitting up in his bed.

"Hwaaam. Third Senior Brother. You're here?" Ilmok greeted him with a huge stretch.

His third Senior Brother had been away on a mission for the Dark Shadow Hall and had returned today. Just in case, Ilmok had tied a thin thread to the door and looped it around his arm. If the door opened even slightly, he would feel the vibration.

He had prepared this because his third Senior Brother had startled him several times before. Waking up with a jolt always ruined his sleep and affected his schedule the next day. This was a desperate measure born from his obsession.

But for some reason, the shadow showed no reaction to Ilmok's greeting. It just stood there motionlessly, looking down at Ilmok for a moment.

The shadow seemed to contemplate something, then it moved.

PAT!

"!!!"

The shadow lunged in an instant. A sword blade coated in ink that reflected almost no light slid from the shadow's side.

His sleep completely gone, Ilmok instinctively rolled his body, narrowly dodging the strike.

"......"

No, he thought he had dodged it, but his forearm was shallowly cut.

*Damn it. Has he finally gone crazy?*

With that thought, Ilmok drew his sword. He had rolled not only to avoid the attack but also to grab the sword beside his bed.

The mad Senior Brother swung a second killing blow, even faster than Ilmok could draw his sword. Although the blade was coated in ink, making its trajectory barely visible, Ilmok's innate eyes could faintly read the sword's path.

CLANG!!

He barely blocked the killing strike with his half-drawn sword.

KREEEK.

"......"

He circulated his inner arts to the maximum, but his sword was sliced in two moments after they clashed.

As the mad assassin who cut his sword pressed forward, Ilmok realized a very important fact.

*This guy. He's not Third Senior Brother.*
