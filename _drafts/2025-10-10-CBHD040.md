Chapter 40: Assassination (2)

Although I had a new goal, the assassination of the Flying Dragon Hall Director, no major changes came to my daily life.
It was a true relief.
I was in the middle of creating a compulsive routine, so if my schedule were disrupted now, it might have led to all sorts of inconvenient situations.
However, just because my routine flowed on as usual didn't mean there were no changes for those who frequented the Wind Cliff Pavilion.
There was Jin Hayeon, who lived with me at the Wind Cliff Pavilion, and the two bodyguard martial artists. And then there were Seon'ah and Master, who appeared at the same time every day as if this were their home.
Aside from these five, the rest of the visitors changed from day to day.
"Hahaha. A mission has been assigned to the White Turtle Brigade, so it seems I won't be able to visit for a while, Young Lord."
"Is that so."
Guyang Mun would occasionally visit like this to report his absence to me.
Well, he was only saying the words to me. He was speaking while sneaking glances at Jin Hayeon.
"Fufu. Travel safely, Master Guyang."
Perhaps they had become close friends now, as my third Senior Brother, who saw Guyang Mun off with a smile, also frequently left his post due to work for the Dark Shadow Hall.
*...I can't bring myself to ask what kind of mission it is.*
Considering the nature of the martial arts my third Senior Brother was learning and the characteristics of a group like the Dark Shadow Hall, the word 'assassination' kept flickering in my mind.
Clad entirely in black, with charcoal applied to their swords to prevent light from reflecting, moving through the darkness of night or hiding somewhere, waiting.
After spending such tense and precarious moments, scenes worthy of a movie, where they swiftly strike down their target as it passes and then disappear, came to mind.
The only problem was this.
The thought that a person who was so kind and cheerful towards me was doing such things inevitably created a sense of disconnect.
In any case, after Guyang Mun finished his farewells for a temporary departure and left.
I casually asked Seon'ah, who was beside me.
"Seon'ah, where do you want to go after you complete the Demonic Academy?"
It was a curiosity about the dream of the little kid next door I had grown close to.
This curiosity arose because I had recently finished the history books and started learning the law books, so I now knew about all the different organizations within the Demonic Cult.
If there was a minor problem, it was that the range of available majors (?) within the Demonic Cult wasn't very wide.
And as if he had become interested in our conversation, Eldest Senior Brother suddenly burst into hearty laughter.
"Hahahaha! How about the Red Tiger Brigade that I lead? As the Young Lady of the Hyeokryeon Clan, your talent is outstanding, so you would be most welcome! Hahahaha!"
The Demonic Cult's military forces were composed of a total of sixteen brigades, named by mixing the colors red, blue, black, and white with the animals symbolizing the four celestial beasts: Dragon (龍), Tiger (虎), Turtle (龜), and Sparrow (雀).
Like the Red Tiger Brigade, which Eldest Senior Brother led, or the White Turtle Brigade, which Guyang Mun belonged to.
Each brigade usually consisted of about forty members, and the role of each brigade was subtly different.
Some brigades, like the White Turtle Brigade, would go on expeditions whenever a mission arose, while others served to protect the Main Headquarters by broadly surrounding it.
With the Main Headquarters, the Billion Mountains, as its base, it bordered the region that those in the Central Plains called the 'Outer Lands', so from my perspective, it felt like a kind of border guard.
In particular, within the Billion Mountains, besides the mountain where the huge valedictorian called the 'Main Headquarters' was located, there were various other areas.
The Demonic Academy I had to enter was also located on another peak of the Billion Mountains, and there was also a very dangerous mountain called the Forgotten Ridge.
In other words, they also performed the role of protecting and monitoring not only the Main Headquarters but also places like the Demonic Academy and the Forgotten Ridge that existed within the Billion Mountains.
*...Though for the Forgotten Ridge, it's closer to surveillance than protection.*
As I recalled Jin Hayeon's explanation of the Forgotten Ridge, I couldn't help but shudder.
Just then, as if burdened by Eldest Senior Brother's sales pitch, Seon'ah grabbed my sleeve tightly, clung to me, and shook her head.
"I-I don't want to join the Red Tiger Brigade because you have to leave the cult often."
"!!!"
Surprised by Seon'ah's refusal, Eldest Senior Brother's eyes widened before he laughed loudly and said.
"Hahahaha! What are you talking about! Traditionally, to grow as a martial artist, you must have various experiences! As nice as it is here, you can't reach a high position by just staying cooped up in the cult!"
The sight of a man in his forties giving advice to a kid in her early teens looked exactly like a condescending old man's nagging.
To add to that, my third Senior Brother, who had suddenly become gloomy, chimed in.
"If you don't like going outside, then you probably wouldn't like our Dark Shadow Hall either...."
The Dark Shadow Hall, which my third Senior Brother belonged to, was a kind of intelligence agency. Well, it felt like they also handled assassinations.
In any case, due to its nature, its members were often away from the cult.
No, more importantly.
*You shouldn't be recommending things like assassination or intelligence operations to a twelve-year-old!*
It felt like Seon'ah would pick up strange habits if left to those two, so I quickly intervened.
"Hahaha. Eldest Senior Brother, you're scheduled to move to another place soon anyway, aren't you? And the work of the Dark Shadow Hall seems too rough for the still-young Seon'ah to handle, Third Senior Brother."
In the first place, the title of the Red Tiger Commander was a bit too low for Eldest Senior Brother, who had entered the Transcendent Demon Realm.
I had heard that because Eldest Senior Brother was tied down at the Main Headquarters while handling matters related to the school, the Red Tiger Brigade was also unable to go on missions.
So, I thought he would probably be transferred somewhere else soon.
"And Seon'ah, if you don't want to leave the cult, how about going to the Guardian Hall or the Inquisition instead?"
The Guardian Hall was a group of bodyguards who protected key figures and facilities within the Demonic Cult. And the Inquisition was a group that executed or imprisoned and managed apostates who broke the laws.
Well, besides those, there were the five prestigious families within the Demonic Cult, like the Hyeokryeon Clan, the teahouse that managed the food within the Demonic Cult, the Bodyguard Hall that Jin Hayeon belonged to, and places like the Council of Elders, though Seon'ah would never go there.
The Demonic Cult's the Council of Elders was a rather peculiar place. From my modern perspective, it was something like a gathering of honorary retirees who failed to become executives.
It was a place where those who had risen to mid-level management but couldn't become the heads of their respective groups because they failed to enter the Transcendent Demon Realm with high-level demonic art gathered. However, because they had reached a certain age, they had handed over their mid-level positions to the rising youth.
Anyway, before Seon'ah could answer my suggestion, Eldest Senior Brother spoke up.
"Hmm. Members of the Guardian Hall and the Inquisition also leave the cult from time to time."
It was a sharp point, uncharacteristic of Eldest Senior Brother.
*Right, he's not a complete idiot.*
What Eldest Senior Brother said was true.
If a person being guarded had to leave the cult, the bodyguard martial artists would naturally accompany them, just as Jang Hoe and Gwan Muyeol had traveled with me through Xinjiang.
And those belonging to the Inquisition also sometimes had to leave the Main Headquarters to catch escaped apostates.
Well, it seemed Eldest Senior Brother had a different reason for pointing that out.
*Tsk! Kids these days! They only try to avoid hard work! Back in my day~.*
That was roughly the sentiment contained in Eldest Senior Brother's gaze as he looked at Seon'ah.
I thought only Master was a condescending old man, but Eldest Senior Brother was just as bad.
Then again, the older generation looking down on the younger generation is a human tradition with a long and proud history.
After all, they say that even ancient artifacts are sometimes discovered with inscriptions pointing out the problems with 'kids these days'.
But what do you know. In response to Eldest Senior Brother's point, Seon'ah replied with sparkling eyes.
"I'll go to the Guardian Hall!"
"You'll have to go outside a lot in the Guardian Hall too!"
"That's okay! As long as I'm going with Oppa."
Seon'ah said, looking at me with those sparkling eyes.
"Uwahahaha! I see now, it's not that you hate leaving the cult, but that you hate being separated from our Youngest!! Kuhahaha!"
Finally realizing Seon'ah's true feelings, Eldest Senior Brother burst out laughing.
Although she had declared it herself, Seon'ah seemed embarrassed now that Eldest Senior Brother had said it so bluntly, and she pressed herself closer to me.
As I stroked the cute little girl's head, I glanced to the side.
There stood Gwan Muyeol, staring into space with a blunt expression as if our chatter had nothing to do with him.
And when I pictured Jang Hoe, who was acting as a human doorbell while guarding the entrance of the Wind Cliff Pavilion, a thought occurred to me.
*Maybe it would be better to be guarded by Seon'ah than to be stuck with these gloomy men?*
Above all, Seon'ah felt like she was definitively 'on my side', so I thought I could truly relax.

The days, spinning like a hamster wheel, passed quickly.
More than half a year had passed since Ilmok was dragged to the heretic group known as the Demonic Cult, and Ilmok had finally managed to master the law books, following the scriptures and history books.
Of course, perfectly memorizing something after one read was impossible unless you were a genius. He needed to review constantly until the entrance examination.
However, apart from that, Ilmok felt the need to change his routine slightly.
He would reduce his morning study time a little and increase his training time a little.
He was worried about the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, but over the past two months, Ilmok had realized something.
He realized the very obvious fact that the realm of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword and its side effect were proportional.
In other words.
*As long as my realm doesn't increase rapidly, the possibility of the side effect running rampant is low.*
There were two situations where Ilmok had become acutely aware of his side effect. The first time he circulated his inner arts, and when he absorbed the Explosive Demonic Pill and underwent meridian cleansing and purification.
In short, it was when his realm had increased explosively.
As long as his realm didn't rise drastically, the risk of going mad from the side effect or falling into qi deviation was 'less'.
Conversely, it meant that if his realm rose slowly, the side effect would seep in little by little without him even noticing.
Despite knowing this fact, Ilmok chose to raise his realm, albeit slowly, for now.
It was unavoidable if he wanted to succeed in at least two things, entering the Demonic Academy and getting revenge for Mr. Taehyun.
And so, just like any other day, after Ilmok finished his scheduled routine and went to bed, how much time had passed?
On the new moon, once a month when the moon hides itself in the darkness.
Taking advantage of the unusually dark sky, a darkish something carefully entered the Wind Cliff Pavilion.
The being, which gave off no sign of its presence, moved toward Ilmok's quarters like a shadow.
WHOOSH.
In time with the sound of the faint night breeze, the being stealthily opened the door and slipped inside.
And what greeted the being upon entering the quarters was the sight of Ilmok, calmly sitting up in his bed.
"Hwaaam. Third Senior Brother. You're here?"
Ilmok greeted him with a huge stretch.
His third Senior Brother, who had been away from the cult on a mission for the Dark Shadow Hall a few days ago, had returned today.
So, just in case, Ilmok had tied a thin thread to the door and looped it around his arm.
So that if the door opened even slightly, he would feel the vibration on his arm.
This was because of his past experiences of being startled by his third Senior Brother several times. Having woken up with a jolt every time, he had prepared himself to wake up calmly like this.
If he woke up with a start, his sleep would completely vanish, affecting his schedule the next day. In a way, it was a desperate measure born out of his obsession.
But for some reason, the shadow showed no reaction to Ilmok's greeting.
It just stood there motionlessly, looking down at Ilmok for a moment.
The shadow, which seemed to be contemplating something, moved.
PAT!
"!!!"
The shadow lunged in an instant. And from the shadow's side, a sword blade coated in ink that reflected almost no light was drawn.
His sleep completely gone, Ilmok instinctively rolled his body, narrowly dodging the strike.
SLICE!
No, he thought he had dodged it, but his forearm was shallowly cut.
*Damn it. Has he finally gone crazy?*
With that thought, Ilmok drew his sword.
Rolling a moment ago was partly to avoid the surprise attack, but more so to grab the sword next to his bed.
And even faster than Ilmok could draw his sword, the mad Senior Brother swung his second killing blow at him.
Although its trajectory was barely visible because it was coated in ink, Ilmok's innate eyes could faintly read the sword's path.
CLANG!!
With the sword he hadn't fully drawn, he barely managed to block the killing strike.
KREEEK.
SLICE.
However, despite circulating his inner arts to the maximum, the sword was sliced in two not long after they clashed.
And as the mad assassin who had cut Ilmok's sword pressed forward.
Ilmok realized a very important fact.
*This guy. He's not my third Senior Brother.*
