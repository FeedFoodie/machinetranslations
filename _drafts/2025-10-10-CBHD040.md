Even with my new goal to assassinate the Flying Dragon Hall Director, my daily life remained unchanged. This was a relief. I was building a strict routine, and any disruption would have caused problems.

But while my routine continued, things changed for the visitors at the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Jin Hayeon lived with me, along with the two bodyguard martial artists. Seon'ah and Master also visited daily, as if it were their home. Besides these five, other visitors came and went.

"Hahaha. A mission has been assigned to the White Turtle Brigade, so it seems I won't be able to visit for a while, Young Lord."

"Is that so."

Guyang Mun occasionally visited to report his absence. He directed his words to me, but he kept sneaking glances at Jin Hayeon.

"Fufu. Travel safely, Master Guyang."

My third Senior Brother, who saw Guyang Mun off with a smile, also frequently left his post for Dark Shadow Hall business. Perhaps they had become close friends.

*...I can't bring myself to ask what kind of mission it is.*

Given the nature of my third Senior Brother's martial arts and the Dark Shadow Hall's reputation, the word 'assassination' kept coming to mind.

I imagined movie-like scenes. Assassins clad in black, their swords coated in charcoal to prevent reflections. They would move through the night or lie in wait. After tense moments, they would strike down their target and vanish.

The only problem was the disconnect I felt. It was hard to believe that someone so kind and cheerful to me could do such things.

After Guyang Mun left, I casually turned to Seon'ah.

"Seon'ah, where do you want to go after you complete the Demonic Academy?"

I was curious about the dream of this little kid I had grown close to.

My curiosity came from recently finishing the history books and starting the law books. I now knew about all the organizations within the Demonic Cult.

The only small problem was that the Demonic Cult didn't offer a wide range of majors (?).

Eldest Senior Brother, apparently interested in our conversation, suddenly burst out laughing. "Hahahaha! How about the Red Tiger Brigade that I lead? As the Young Lady of the Hyeokryeon Clan, your talent is outstanding. You'd be most welcome! Hahahaha!"

The Demonic Cult's military had sixteen brigades. Their names combined the colors red, blue, black, and white with the four celestial beasts: Dragon (龍), Tiger (虎), Turtle (龜), and Sparrow (雀).

Examples included Eldest Senior Brother's Red Tiger Brigade and Guyang Mun's White Turtle Brigade.

Each brigade had about forty members, and their roles were subtly different.

Some brigades, like the White Turtle Brigade, went on expeditions for missions. Others protected the Main Headquarters by surrounding it.

The Main Headquarters in the Billion Mountains bordered what the Central Plains called the 'Outer Lands'. From my perspective, they were like border guards.

The Billion Mountains contained more than just the mountain with the huge structure called the 'Main Headquarters'. There were various other areas.

The Demonic Academy I had to enter was on another peak in the Billion Mountains. There was also a very dangerous mountain called the Forgotten Ridge.

In other words, they protected and monitored not just the Main Headquarters, but also places like the Demonic Academy and the Forgotten Ridge within the Billion Mountains.

*...Though for the Forgotten Ridge, it's closer to surveillance than protection.*

I shuddered as I recalled Jin Hayeon's explanation of the Forgotten Ridge.

Seon'ah, seemingly burdened by Eldest Senior Brother's sales pitch, grabbed my sleeve tightly. She clung to me and shook her head. "I-I don't want to join the Red Tiger Brigade because you have to leave the cult often."

"!!!"

Eldest Senior Brother's eyes widened in surprise at Seon'ah's refusal. Then he laughed loudly. "Hahahaha! What are you talking about! Traditionally, a martial artist needs various experiences to grow! You can't reach a high position by just staying cooped up in the cult, as nice as it is here!"

A man in his forties advising a young teenager looked exactly like a condescending old man nagging.

My third Senior Brother, suddenly gloomy, chimed in. "If you don't like going outside, then you probably wouldn't like our Dark Shadow Hall either...."

My third Senior Brother's Dark Shadow Hall was a kind of intelligence agency. It seemed they also handled assassinations.

Because of its nature, its members were often away from the cult.

No, more importantly.

*You shouldn't be recommending things like assassination or intelligence operations to a twelve-year-old!*

I felt Seon'ah would pick up strange habits if left with those two, so I quickly intervened. "Hahaha. Eldest Senior Brother, aren't you scheduled to move soon anyway? And Third Senior Brother, the Dark Shadow Hall's work seems too rough for someone as young as Seon'ah."

The title of Red Tiger Commander was too low for Eldest Senior Brother, who had already entered the Transcendent Demon Realm.

I heard the Red Tiger Brigade couldn't go on missions because Eldest Senior Brother was tied down at the Main Headquarters with school matters.

So I figured he would be transferred somewhere else soon.

"And Seon'ah, if you don't want to leave the cult, how about the Guardian Hall or the Inquisition instead?"

The Guardian Hall protected key figures and facilities in the Demonic Cult. The Inquisition executed or imprisoned apostates who broke the laws.

Besides those, there were other places Seon'ah would never go, like the five prestigious families, the teahouse that managed food, Jin Hayeon's Bodyguard Hall, and the Council of Elders.

The Demonic Cult's Council of Elders was a peculiar place. From my modern perspective, it was like a gathering of honorary retirees who failed to become executives.

It was for those who reached mid-level management but failed to enter the Transcendent Demon Realm with high-level demonic art. They couldn't become heads of their groups. Having reached a certain age, they handed their positions to the rising youth.

Before Seon'ah could answer, Eldest Senior Brother spoke up. "Hmm. Members of the Guardian Hall and the Inquisition also leave the cult from time to time."

It was an uncharacteristically sharp point from Eldest Senior Brother.

*Right, he's not a complete idiot.*

What Eldest Senior Brother said was true.

If a guarded person left the cult, their bodyguards naturally followed, just as Jang Hoe and Gwan Muyeol had traveled with me through Xinjiang.

Members of the Inquisition also had to leave the Main Headquarters sometimes to catch escaped apostates.

But it seemed Eldest Senior Brother had a different reason for his comment.

*Tsk! Kids these days! They only try to avoid hard work! Back in my day~.*

That was roughly the sentiment in Eldest Senior Brother's gaze as he looked at Seon'ah.

I thought only Master was a condescending old man, but Eldest Senior Brother was just as bad.

Then again, the older generation looking down on the younger one is a proud and ancient human tradition.

They say even ancient artifacts have been found with inscriptions complaining about 'kids these days'.

But to my surprise, Seon'ah replied to Eldest Senior Brother's point with sparkling eyes. "I'll go to the Guardian Hall!"

"You'll have to go outside a lot in the Guardian Hall too!"

"That's okay! As long as I'm going with Oppa."

Seon'ah said this while looking at me with those sparkling eyes.

"Uwahahaha! I see now! It's not that you hate leaving the cult, but that you hate being separated from our Youngest! Kuhahaha!"

Eldest Senior Brother finally realized Seon'ah's true feelings and burst out laughing.

Though she had declared it herself, Seon'ah seemed embarrassed by Eldest Senior Brother's blunt words. She pressed closer to me.

I stroked the cute little girl's head and glanced to the side.

Gwan Muyeol stood there, staring into space with a blunt expression, as if our chatter had nothing to do with him.

I pictured Jang Hoe guarding the entrance of the Wind Cliff Pavilion like a human doorbell, and a thought occurred to me.

*Maybe it would be better to be guarded by Seon'ah than to be stuck with these gloomy men?*

*Above all, Seon'ah feels like she's definitively 'on my side'. I think I could truly relax with her.*

The days spun by like a hamster wheel.

More than half a year had passed since Ilmok was dragged to the Demonic Cult. He had finally mastered the law books, after finishing the scriptures and history books.

Of course, only a genius could perfectly memorize something after one read. Ilmok needed to review constantly until the entrance examination.

However, Ilmok felt he needed to change his routine slightly.

He decided to reduce his morning study time and increase his training time.

Ilmok worried about the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, but he had realized something over the past two months.

He realized the obvious fact that the realm of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was proportional to its side effect.

*As long as my realm doesn't increase rapidly, the possibility of the side effect running rampant is low.*

Ilmok had become acutely aware of the side effect on two occasions. The first time he circulated his inner arts, and when he absorbed the Explosive Demonic Pill for meridian cleansing.

In short, it happened when his realm increased explosively.

As long as his realm didn't rise drastically, the risk of going mad or falling into qi deviation was 'less'.

Conversely, if his realm rose slowly, the side effect would seep in little by little without him noticing.

Despite this, Ilmok chose to raise his realm for now, albeit slowly.

It was necessary if he wanted to enter the Demonic Academy and get revenge for Mr. Taehyun.

So, just like any other day, Ilmok finished his routine and went to bed. Some time passed.

It was the new moon, the one night a month when the moon hides in darkness.

A dark figure took advantage of the unusually dark sky and carefully entered the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

The being gave off no presence as it moved toward Ilmok's quarters like a shadow.

WHOOSH.

With the faint sound of the night breeze, the being stealthily opened the door and slipped inside.

What greeted the being was the sight of Ilmok, sitting calmly in his bed.

"Hwaaam. Third Senior Brother. You're here?"

Ilmok greeted him with a huge stretch.

His third Senior Brother had been away on a Dark Shadow Hall mission and returned today.

So, just in case, Ilmok had tied a thin thread from the door to his arm.

This way, he would feel the vibration if the door opened even slightly.

He had been startled by his third Senior Brother several times before. Waking with a jolt each time, he had prepared this method to wake up calmly.

Waking with a start would ruin his sleep and affect his next day's schedule. In a way, his obsession led to this desperate measure.

But for some reason, the shadow did not react to Ilmok's greeting.

It just stood there, looking down at Ilmok.

The shadow seemed to contemplate something, then it moved.

PAT!

"!!!"

The shadow lunged instantly. It drew an ink-coated sword blade that reflected almost no light.

All sleepiness vanished as Ilmok instinctively rolled, narrowly dodging the strike.

"......"

He thought he had dodged, but the blade cut his forearm shallowly.

*Damn it. Has he finally gone crazy?*

With that thought, Ilmok drew his sword.

He had rolled not just to avoid the attack, but also to grab the sword beside his bed.

Faster than Ilmok could draw his sword, the mad Senior Brother swung a second killing blow.

The ink coating made the trajectory nearly invisible, but Ilmok's innate eyes could faintly read the sword's path.

CLANG!!

He barely blocked the killing strike with his half-drawn sword.

KREEEK.

"......"

But even as he circulated his inner arts to the maximum, his sword was sliced in two moments after they clashed.

The mad assassin cut Ilmok's sword and pressed forward.

Then Ilmok realized a very important fact.

*This guy. He's not my third Senior Brother.*
