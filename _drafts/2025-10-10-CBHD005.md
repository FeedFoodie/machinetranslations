Assigning blame only worked on people with a sense of responsibility. It also worked on those who feared being fired or wanted a promotion. In other words, the tactic was useless against someone who had already decided to quit or given up on promotions.

That was just how the world worked. The person in a hurry always got dragged along.

Even in university group projects, the student desperate for a good grade shouldered the entire burden. Those who had given up on their grades were more likely to get a free ride.

Of course, I was the one who was truly in a hurry, not her.

Wasn't my life on the line? And to me, my life was the most important thing in the world.

On the other hand, Jin Hayeon was one of those crazy religious fanatics. She would throw her life away like a blade of grass for the Heavenly Demon and the cult.

Still, I could afford this bold gamble for two main reasons.

First, she was a severe religious fanatic who valued the ‘prestige of the Heavenly Demon’ more than her own life. This meant my passing the Demonic Academy entrance exam was more important to her than her own life.

And the second reason was that I was a disciple of the Heavenly Demon.

*She threatened to kill me, but that just means she can't touch me until I fail the Demonic Academy evaluation.*

Of course, the second reason was just a guess. It was not certain. If I was wrong, she might immediately advise me and force the matter through.

An ominous thought surfaced, and a cold sweat ran down my spine. I made sure never to let the tension show on my face. No matter what anyone said, this was a matter of life and death for me.

Entrusting my life completely to a crazy religious fanatic? I could never accept that.

I barely controlled my expression and met Jin Hayeon's gaze in a staring contest.

"Hoo. I'll proceed with the training according to the Young Lord's schedule."

In the end, she raised the white flag. The price, however, was that her gaze and tone grew even colder.

I spoke in a placating tone, as if I was doing her a great favor.

"Don't worry. This doesn't mean we'll stick to this schedule until the evaluation. I plan to make changes once a month. For example, if my interview preparation is lacking, we'll adjust the external arts training time. Conversely, if my duel preparation is insufficient, we'll adjust the study time. If both are insufficient, then we'll reduce other times."

I proposed it as a negotiation offer for her, but I had planned to do so from the beginning.

That was how exam preparation worked. Nothing ever went according to the initial plan. Just in studying alone, some subjects progressed faster than expected, while others were slower. Therefore, it was necessary to adjust things little by little to match the progress.

Jin Hayeon seemed to like my proposal and accepted it.

"Understood. In that case, I'd appreciate it if you would revise the schedule through ‘consultation’ with me after a month."

She emphasized the word ‘consultation’.

{sep}

After the grueling negotiation with Jin Hayeon, I finally began my rest period as Master commanded. Resting while a religious fanatic who had threatened to kill me in a year attended to me was a truly peculiar experience.

My ‘body’ was comfortable, at least.

"I'll prepare your meal."

"Isn't it too hot for you?"

From preparing meals to even fanning me, she took care of every possible service. However, for me, receiving the service was an ordeal.

Her every move made me nervous, whether she was serving a meal or fanning me. The ominous thought that she might be up to something strange kept popping into my head.

"Before you retire for the night, how about you first purify yourself with a bath?"

"I shall do that."

"Then, I'll prepare warm water."

She returned a short while later.

"The bathwater is ready, Young Lord."

I followed her to the bathhouse. Warm steam rose gently from the bath.

*Hoo. I'm tired, but at least the water is warm.*

My muscles were stiff from constant tension. I thought a bath might loosen them up, but as I started to take off my clothes, I realized something strange.

"What are you doing over there?"

For some reason, she stood there blankly, staring at me.

"I'm waiting to assist you with your bath."

"!!!"

My eyes widened at this unexpected development. I moved according to her instructions in a half-dazed state.

"Please remove your clothes and enter the bath. I'll apply the soap for you."

Maintaining the bare minimum of my sanity, I undressed to my undergarments and entered the tub. She carefully rubbed the soap on my body, lathering me up.

I felt the soap and her fingers as she carefully touched me, starting with my arms. I glimpsed her skin through her clothes, which had gotten wet while she washed me.

It was a situation that could have made blood rush somewhere, but the reality was completely different.

GULP.

I swallowed dryly without realizing it. It was not because of any irreverent thoughts. It was a swallow born of tension.

The assassin who declared she would kill me in a year watched my bare body with indifferent eyes. Every time she applied soap to my body, an image appeared in my mind.

*A poke here will paralyze your arm.*

*A poke here will kill you instantly, but I can't let you die so easily.*

For some reason, I imagined her uttering those words. Of course, I knew that would not happen until I failed the Demonic Academy entrance evaluation.

*Couldn't you at least relax your expression while you're at it...*

She examined my body and applied the soap with a cold face, like a graduate student performing an anatomy dissection.

{sep}

As planned, I slept at 22:00 and woke up between 05:00 and 07:00. I did some light stretches and made my bed. Since I had not learned any martial arts yet, there was no particular need to perform qi circulation.

So, I washed up, ate the breakfast Jin Hayeon had prepared, and immediately started my morning studies.

*It's a bit less efficient, but it's better than doing nothing.*

A person's brain isn't fully awake right after they wake up. It usually takes two to three hours for the brain to become fully activated.

That was why examinees were advised not to sleep in until 8 or 9 for a 10 a.m. test. They should wake up around 7, prepare slowly, and kill time with a light review right before the test.

Could a thirty-minute review on the morning of the exam really have a significant effect on material that required two or three years of study? The essential meaning was in the ‘process of activating the brain’.

I moved my body lightly to wake up my brain. Jin Hayeon brought a book, placed it on the desk, and spoke.

"Well then, from now on, I'll teach you about the doctrines of our the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult."

Finally, the first faith study session of my life had arrived. I almost swallowed without realizing it.

*I have to keep my wits about me.*

There was a saying that if you kept your wits about you, you could survive even if you were dragged into a tiger's den. However, this was the headquarters of a heretic religion.

Thinking of modern heretic religions made me realize how dangerous it was to study faith in a heretic cult. If I let my guard down even a little or lost my focus, I risked becoming the same kind of person as the woman teaching me.

Perhaps because of that tension, my still slightly groggy head felt like it was waking up sharply.

She perhaps mistook my tense appearance for a reverent posture. She nodded once, handed me the scripture, and began to speak.

"Young Lord, haven't you been curious? About why our Divine Cult specifically uses the name the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult?"

"The character for ‘demon’ certainly gives off an ominous feeling."

"There is a reason for this."

I nodded.

What heretic didn't have its reasons? The problem was that it was all sophistry.

She took my nod as a sign of active listening and continued her explanation.

"Originally, all humans are evil. The ancient Confucian scholar Xunzi said as much. In reality, countless corrupt officials oppress the poor common people. This isn't someone's fault, but the natural order of things."

*Well, I'll be. She's even bringing up Xunzi?*

"To protect the common people from the oppression of such individuals, martial power is ultimately necessary. To save the masses in the future, one needs the power to destroy the existing order. Those who made up the existing order will call this ‘demon’. Young Lord, have you perhaps heard of Maitreya?"

"I have."

"The Maitreya mentioned here is none other than the Heavenly Demon of our Divine Cult. However, the monks of Buddhism are distorting the truth to deceive the masses. How can one possibly reform inherently evil humans with mere sermons?"

*Wow. Now she's bringing in Buddhism and Maitreya?*

*As expected, a heretic is a heretic.*

Most modern heretics also spread their doctrines based on the Christian Bible. They just interpret the contents of the Bible as they please, create absurd rules, and deceive their believers.

"That is why the Heavenly Demon is necessary. A demon sent from heaven. A being is needed to tear down the existing order and to watch over the evil dwelling within human nature to prevent it from running rampant. That is right. The Heavenly Demon is a ‘demon’ because he is the one who will single-handedly bear the evil inherent in all of us."

*Wow. Now she's even bringing in Christian doctrine?*

Listening to her reminded me of the story of Jesus bearing the original sin of all humanity and dying for it.

Feeling like I would lose my mind if I just kept listening, I quickly spoke up.

"So, the doctrine of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult means that the Heavenly Demon will overthrow the existing order, like corrupt officials, and bring salvation to the masses?"

"You have understood perfectly. Our the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult began with the idea that all humans are equal."

Her previously emotionless eyes shone as she replied, as if moved by my immediate understanding.

However, my instincts were blaring warning bells. This group was not just a heretic religion. It was a group plotting treason.

At the same time, something popped into my head.

*This feels somewhat... similar to Korean Neo-Confucianism.*

It felt similar to the ideology of Korean Neo-Confucianism, as in the Korean Neo-Confucianism Peasant Movement, or the Korean Neo-Confucianism Peasant Revolution. That was not to say that Korean Neo-Confucianism was a heretic religion.

Their advocacy for human equality was similar to their representative ideology, Innaechon (Man is Heaven). It was also similar to how the founder, Choi Je-woo, came forward saying he would save the foolish common people. In addition, the point about trying to strike down and kill corrupt officials instead of just spreading sermons to save the masses was, needless to say, also similar.

"Then, did the imperial family not brand our the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult as a traitor for deceiving the world and deluding the people?"

"...That was over two hundred years ago. How did you know?"

"..."

*Uh, well. Because I memorized Korean history while studying for the civil service exam?*

However, that was not the important thing right now.

*I had a bad feeling about this, but they were already branded as traitors.*

The Heavenly Demon Divine Cult were not just heretics. They were heretics branded as traitors, and I had become the disciple of the leader of those traitors.

*...If I testify that someone threatened me with a knife, would they consider it for extenuating circumstances?*

The possibility did not seem very high.
