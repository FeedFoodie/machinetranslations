"We greet the Lord of All Demons!"

"We greet the Heavenly Demon!"

The clinic erupted into an uproar because Master had come to visit. The physicians working there all shouted praises for Master.

Soon after.

"......"

The door to my clinic room opened, and Master entered.

"That’s enough. Stay seated."

The Master quickly stopped Jin Hayeon, who had splints and bandages on both arms, as she tried to prostrate herself on the floor.

Then, Master looked at me and asked, "How’s your body?"

"There are no major problems, thanks to the prompt treatment."

"That’s a relief. I heard the story. They said your quick thinking helped minimize the damage when the First went berserk."

I smiled faintly at Master’s praise. It was not a smile of joy from receiving a compliment.

*I risked my life provoking a mad boar, so how could a word or two of praise be enough?*

"In that case, are you perhaps planning to give me some kind of reward?"

When I was young, I learned that humility was a virtue, but my experience in the working world taught me otherwise. If you just endure and work, believing you’ll be rewarded someday, you’ll only be forgotten. When you do something well, you have to demand a reward then and there to receive proper compensation.

"Young Lord! How can you say such a thing to the Supreme Lord!!"

Of course, Jin Hayeon, who was beside me, was uncharacteristically horrified, but that was none of my concern.

"Hmm. A reward, you say. Indeed, thanks to you, the Hyeokryeon Clan has agreed not to press the issue with the First any further, so a reward is certainly warranted."

"You went to the Hyeokryeon Clan as well?"

The Master nodded his head.

"The girl admitted that her own words caused the First to go berserk. In addition, the testimonies of the bodyguard and the girl were consistent. They said you provoked the First and led him away to save everyone there."

It seemed Master had been handling the aftermath of Eldest Senior Brother's rampage while I was bedridden.

"Then, what happened to Eldest Senior Brother?"

"For now, as punishment, I have confined him to the seclusion training cave, and to those who were injured this time, I have bestowed healing medicine and some money for their recovery, so it seems right to give a reward to you, who did the most."

Oho.

*I wonder what kind of reward he will give me.*

My expectations were quite high, since it was a reward from the Cult Leader of this massive heretic cult.

The Master stroked his beard, pondering for a moment, before he finally announced the reward he was bestowing upon me.

"This old man will personally look over your injuries once more with qi guidance and Body Purification. In addition, I will increase the time I spend overseeing your martial arts training for a while."

"……"

*What the hell is that? Isn't that just a way of saying you're going to work me harder?*

My facial muscles twisted on their own, but I fortunately managed to regain my composure before my expression completely contorted.

"The Supreme Lord’s grace is truly as vast as the heavens!"

Beside me, Jin Hayeon shouted this with a face that looked ready to burst into tears.

Indeed, from the perspective of religious fanatics, what could be more gracious than spending more time with the Cult Leader? Even for those who weren't religious fanatics, any martial artist would consider the chance to learn martial arts from someone like Master an opportunity they would pay a fortune for.

The only problem was.

*I don't need that crap!!*

I was neither a religious fanatic nor was I that crazy about martial arts yet.

However, Jin Hayeon’s reaction made me realize something. As a disciple of the heretic Cult Leader, this was a reward I was supposed to be extremely happy about.

*Damn it.*

"I can only thank you for your grace, Master."

I put on a moved expression, contrary to my inner thoughts, and felt the corners of my mouth about to twitch.

Fortunately, before my facial muscles could contort, Master brought up another topic.

"Hmph. The reason this old man came here was actually to discuss something else."

"Was there some kind of problem?"

"Not a problem, per se, but the First said something as he was entering the seclusion training cave. He said he wants to talk with you, Ilmok."

"?!"

"Normally, the rule is that no one can meet or converse with anyone in the seclusion training cave, but since he has just entered, a brief meeting before his punishment begins should be possible. So, I came to ask your opinion first."

This was unexpected news. That mad boar wanted to see me as soon as he came to his senses?

*Don't tell me, he wants to kill me?*

That was the first thought that came to mind, but it would be difficult for him to kill me while locked in the seclusion training cave. Instead, I thought it might be an opportunity.

If things continued as they were, Eldest Senior Brother and I risked becoming mortal enemies. The problem was that Eldest Senior Brother lost his mind and went on a sword rampage, but from his perspective, my repeated 'stupid' remarks must have soured his mood.

Wouldn't it be better to clear up the misunderstanding with Eldest Senior Brother now, in a safe situation while he's confined to the seclusion training cave?

*It's good if it works, and if it fails, I lose nothing.*

Having thought that far, I answered Master.

"Is the door to the seclusion training cave sturdy?"

Of course, my safety was the top priority.

{sep}

Ilmok and the Heavenly Demon entered the seclusion training cave.

"This is the place."

The Heavenly Demon, who had guided Ilmok, pointed to an iron door visible beyond the torchlight.

"This old man will wait here, so go and have your conversation."

He meant he would stay out of the conversation between disciples.

Ilmok hesitated for a moment, but then approached the iron door, thinking it should be safe enough.

"Eldest Senior Brother. Are you there?"

"So it’s you, Youngest Junior Brother."

Wi Jinhak’s voice came from beyond the iron door in response to Ilmok’s question.

"I heard you were looking for me."

"Hahahaha. I called you to apologize. It seems my training is still insufficient, and I couldn’t control my anger."

Wi Jinhak’s hearty laughter echoed through the cave, carried by the iron door. It was his usual foolish tone, completely different from when he had lost his mind.

At this point, Ilmok wondered if it wasn't a rage disorder but a split personality.

*Still, it seems we haven't become enemies.*

Ilmok sighed in relief inwardly. However, he felt a little empty just turning back now.

"I also came to apologize. It was unavoidable under the circumstances to prevent others from dying, but I’m truly sorry for saying the words that Eldest Senior Brother hates the most."

"Uhahahaha. Now that my head is clear, I understand too. I know why you did it, Junior Brother. It was just a problem caused by Second Senior Brother's stupid Qinghai head."

Wi Jinhak was once again setting a landmine and waiting.

Ilmok considered giving a suitable reply like, ‘Eldest Senior Brother, you are not stupid,’ just as he had placated Seo Wanpyeong when he visited in the middle of the night. However, he had a feeling that wouldn't be the right approach for Eldest Senior Brother.

After all, Eldest Senior Brother’s condition was a rage disorder, not paranoia. Seeing how he constantly called others stupid but had a fit when someone said it to him, it seemed to be a kind of inferiority complex. Of course, Ilmok was a former civil servant, not a psychiatrist, so he wasn't exactly an expert.

*The iron door is protecting me anyway, so it should be fine.*

Reminding himself that he was safe, Ilmok carefully opened his mouth.

"Eldest Senior Brother. Do you perhaps remember when I recommended the head of Team Three for the Vice Commander position?"

"...You did."

Hearing Wi Jinhak’s strangely calm tone, Ilmok swallowed once and continued speaking.

"I didn’t say that because I was looking down on you, Eldest Senior Brother. Please recall the old saying, ‘The more, the better.’ Han Sin told Emperor Gaozu of Han that he only had the talent to command a mere hundred thousand strategists. However, he said that for himself, the more strategists the better. Instead, Han Sin stated that Emperor Gaozu of Han, Yubang, had the talent to command generals like himself."

Of course, a few years after that incident, Han Sin was executed by Yubang. Well, it was part of the process of eliminating the founding contributors to strengthen imperial authority, but there's also a joke theory that he was killed because his feelings were hurt after that event.

"Furthermore, the most famous for his intellect in Shu was Zhuge Kongming, but the emperor of Shu was Yubi. As you can see, what’s important for a leader isn’t brains. It’s the charisma to draw people in and the ability to place talented individuals in the right positions, and you, Eldest Senior Brother, are the great Heavenly Demon’s first disciple and the future leader of the Divine Cult."

While Ilmok’s lengthy speech continued, Wi Jinhak did not utter a single word from beyond the iron door.

*Did I say something I shouldn't have?*

Ilmok trembled with a sense of foreboding, feeling that he had made the misunderstanding worse while trying to resolve it.

"Let’s go now."

The Heavenly Demon, who had approached him at some point, brought Ilmok back to his senses.

"......"

As a dejected Ilmok trudged away, his shoulders slumped with the thought that he had screwed up, the Heavenly Demon uttered something unexpected.

"You did very well."

"???"

*What on earth did I do well?*

Ilmok wanted to ask, but for some reason, the Heavenly Demon’s gaze was not on him, but on the iron door.

*He probably didn't say it with this intention, but it seems it was a truly opportune moment.*

The Heavenly Demon had noticed the change occurring inside the seclusion training cave, beyond the iron door.

*They say there is such a thing as a destiny bestowed by the heavens, and this child must be the heavenly fortune designated for our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.*

He had succeeded in something that couldn't be achieved just by aiming for it, so there was no other way to explain it.

"Since you’ve accomplished such a great thing, I shall grant you another reward."

"???"

"Hmm. However, for this matter, it seems more appropriate to entrust it to the First, rather than for this old man to step in personally."

Unable to comprehend any of this, Ilmok’s confusion only grew.

*Entrusting my reward to Eldest Senior Brother? Does he mean he's going to hand over my head?*

Meanwhile.

Inside the iron door. Within the dark cave, only the faint light of a single night-luminescent pearl spread softly. There, Wi Jinhak was deep in meditation, sitting in the full lotus position. To be precise, he was fighting deep within the world of his mental image against the demonic qi that had burrowed into his mind.

However, the structure of the fight was different from usual. Unlike before, when he would endure the rising anger while fiercely clashing with the demonic qi of the Explosive Mara Sword Technique, he was now merely observing.

[Kill that bastard right now!!]

[How dare he mock me by calling me stupid!!!]

Even as he heard the demonic qi’s rage ordering him to kill his Youngest Junior Brother, Wi Jinhak’s heart was perfectly calm. He had realized it thanks to his Youngest Junior Brother’s story. The fact that he didn’t need to care about being called stupid.

If it had been words of comfort, like saying he wasn't stupid, he might have become even angrier. However, it had made him understand that he, at least, had no need to care about being called stupid. As Master’s grand disciple, he was destined to become the leader.

Of course, there had been others who had said similar things before. Most of them had either died at his hands or he had regrettably let them go because Master appeared just before he could kill them.

Just as the Heavenly Demon thought, achieving enlightenment through advice required a stroke of heavenly luck. This was because the advice was only meaningful when it was the most fitting for a special time or situation where it could be effective. Because Wi Jinhak had already reached a state close to the Transcendent Demon Realm, and because he had already gone on a rampage the day before, releasing the anger that had been gradually accumulating. He had been in a state ready to listen to his Youngest Junior Brother’s advice. At that perfect moment, which even the Heavenly Demon, who had taught and watched over Wi Jinhak for decades, had not anticipated, Ilmok had coincidentally offered that advice.

[Kill!]

[Kill everyone who looks down on me!]

[Burn this whole irrational world to the ground!!]

Another self screamed in malice. The demonic qi pounded at his brain, coursing through his acupoints according to the principles of the Explosive Mara Sword Technique. However, unlike usual, Wi Jinhak simply accepted and observed it all, maintaining a mindset with a heart as clear as still water.

KWANG!!!

At some point, a blocked path in his mind burst open with a tremendous roar, unable to withstand the demonic qi’s assault. The demonic qi, which had been endlessly condensed due to the blocked acupoints, rushed through all the acupoints in his body. That immense power began to drive out the impurities blocking his acupoints, and not stopping there, it twisted his muscles and joints.

Even amidst the waves of intense pain, Wi Jinhak merely observed himself, forgetting the world and focusing only on his enlightenment.

At a certain moment, the contortions of his body stopped. The demonic qi that had raged as if to burn the world also calmed and returned to his qi center.

PAT!

Wi Jinhak, having fully assimilated his enlightenment, opened his eyes. It was the moment he overcame the final wall and entered the Transcendent Demon Realm.

"The smell is awful."

Frowning slightly at the smell of the impurities and filth expelled from his body, Wi Jinhak drew upon the internal energy of the Explosive Mara Sword Technique. The Burning Sun Finger Qi erupted from his hand.

It was a bizarre sight. Even as the flames burned away the impurities and filth clinging to his body, not a single burn appeared on his skin.

Now able to perfectly control the demonic qi of the Explosive Mara Sword Technique, Wi Jinhak scratched his chin and thought.

*Gathering talented people and placing them in the right positions, huh.*

Naturally, someone’s face came to mind.

"Youngest Junior Brother will have to grow up quickly. He’ll need to become my strategist in the future."
