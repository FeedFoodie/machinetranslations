A small child stood there. Her black hair had a slight reddish tint, as if dyed. Based on her height and build, she looked about eleven or twelve years old.

*Come to think of it, Ilmok's body is fifteen, so there's at most a three or four-year age difference.* But my original self, Seo Jihun, was thirty-four. *No, I lived another year as Ilmok, so am I thirty-five?*

In any case, our mental ages were so different that she did not feel like a peer. She just seemed like a cute niece, one from a rather wealthy family. Even at a glance, the material of her clothes looked quite fine by this world's standards.

*Well, Jin Hayeon called her Young Lady, so she's no ordinary kid.*

I looked closer and noticed her eyes, like her hair, had a slight reddish hue.

*How strange.*

I must have been staring too intently at the child's eyes. The little girl, Seon'ah, shyly lowered her head and muttered something. She hugged a small doll and fidgeted with her tiny fingers, her nervousness obvious.

"I, I was curious…" she said.

Her nervous state was both pitiable and cute. I found myself using a tone for addressing a child without realizing it.

"What were you curious about~?"

The little girl kept her head slightly bowed. She raised her right hand and pointed with a small finger. Her finger pointed right at me.

"Me?"

The little girl’s bowed head nodded slightly.

"Why were you curious about me~?"

"I, I heard the adults talking. Th-that the n-new disciple of the, the Heavenly Demon is very smart…"

I had a rough idea of what happened. It seemed Master and my Senior Brothers had spread stories about me after my work at the last company dinner. Those rumors had apparently spread far enough to reach this little kid.

A cold voice came from Jin Hayeon.

"Young Lord Ilmok is busy preparing for his entrance to the Demonic Academy. Please return now."

At the clear order to leave, the little girl’s shoulders tensed. She had been shy just from making eye contact.

*Even if it's because of the side effects of demonic art, isn't she being too harsh on a kid?*

I sighed deeply at Jin Hayeon's terrible personality and spoke.

"It’s fine. If she’s just going to watch, it won’t interfere with my studies, will it?"

"......"

Jin Hayeon tried to retort in a chilly tone, but I quickly turned to Seon'ah.

Surprised by my words, the little girl lifted her deeply bowed head. She looked at me with wide, rabbit-like eyes.

"......"

When she was cowering, I felt pity, but the moment I met those bright, innocent eyes, my heart felt struck.

*She's so cute!*

In modern terms, it felt like a cute stray cat had chosen me as its owner.

Naturally, another thought came to mind.

*What kind of place is this, anyway?*

It wasn't enough that Jin Hayeon had looks that could shame any entertainer. Now there was a little kid whose cuteness rivaled any child actor. It was a mystery why such talents were gathered in a heretic religion.

"You can promise me you’ll sit there and watch quietly, right~?"

The little girl looked up at me with her rabbit-like eyes and nodded vigorously. Her head was still disproportionately large for her body, and seeing it shake so forcefully was a rare sight.

I smiled without realizing it, approached the little girl, and held out my pinky finger.

"Promise this ahjussi, no, this Oppa."

"???"

I taught the confused child how to hook pinkies and stamp with the thumb.

"Here, you hook your pinky like this, and stamp with your thumb. If you break the promise, scary people will co~ome and get you later. Got it?"

"Yeees~!" she shouted brightly.

The cowering child was gone, replaced by a bright shout.

Jin Hayeon watched from the side and let out a deep sigh, her expression cold.

Regardless, I ended the conversation so she could not pressure the child.

"Since she said she’ll just watch quietly, how about we start studying now?"

Jin Hayeon looked between the child and me for a moment. She sighed again with a cold face and replied, "As you wish."

{sep}

Our daily routine proceeded as usual, now with a young spectator. As promised, Seon'ah kept her mouth shut and watched quietly.

After a two-hour lesson, Jin Hayeon announced, "We will take a short break for about five minutes."

"Haaa…"

A sigh escaped me unconsciously.

Studying for two hours straight was not easy, even for someone like me who was used to preparing for exams. It might have been different if I were studying alone, but I was attending a lesson.

*Is this why the 50-minute class, 10-minute break became the standard?*

I shook my head to clear the useless thoughts. I saw Jin Hayeon in front of me, quenching her thirst with tea. She had likely been talking for two hours straight.

"Haah."

Another sigh escaped me.

*It’s called a break, but there’s nothing to do.*

In the modern world, I would be on my phone, but there was no entertainment here. Even if I tried to do something alone, Jin Hayeon was a distraction. She was supposed to be serving and teaching me, but she felt more like a guard.

*At this rate, I’m going to develop a mental illness from frustration, not from the side effects of demonic art.*

As I thought that, I turned my head. My eyes met the little girl's. She was still sitting in the same spot, watching me.

"What have you been doing~?" I asked with a playful smile.

The blushing girl slightly lowered her head and answered, "I, I was l-listening with you."

"Oh. Did you understand everything that Unnie was saying?"

"Yeees…"

Her shy reply was cute, but a thought occurred to me.

*Eleven? Twelve? The content seemed a bit complex for a twelve-year-old. Ah, she looked like she came from a wealthy family, so maybe she already started studying?*

I thought that perhaps she was also studying for the Demonic Academy entrance examination in a few years.

"What’s your name, little sister? I’m Ilmok."

The child fidgeted with her doll and replied, "Seon, Seon'ah. Hyeokryeon Seon'ah."

*Hyeokryeon? I feel like I’ve heard that somewhere.*

I searched my memory for a moment and recalled it easily.

*Master’s name was Hyeokryeon Ilhwi.*

I then remembered that the Eighteen Blood Shadow Swords, the martial arts Master learned before the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, was the ultimate technique of the Hyeokryeon Clan.

From the context, the Hyeokryeon Clan seemed to be a powerful family within the Demonic Cult.

*So that’s why Jin Hayeon called her Young Lady.*

She was the daughter of a prestigious family and likely a relative of the Heavenly Demon Master.

*So should I keep using formal speech with her? No, I’m the disciple of the Heavenly Demon Master, so am I higher in rank?*

Our status within the Demonic Cult felt similar. If so, there would be no problem if I, the elder, spoke informally.

"Then, what’s the name of that doll you’re holding, Seon'ah?"

"M-Mom."

"Mom?" I asked, puzzled.

Seon'ah nodded. "Y-yes. Mom doesn’t hate Seon'ah or leave her alone like my mother does. S-so this mom is my real mom."

"……"

I felt like I had just heard some family drama I should not have.

I quickly changed the subject. "Ahem. I-I see. S-so, what do you usually do, Seon'ah?"

"It’s similar to you, Oppa. I study, and I train in martial arts…"

"You only study and train? You don’t get to play?"

"I-I’m just with Mom."

The 'Mom' she mentioned was probably the doll, not her real mother.

*Listening to her, she sounds a lot like me.*

A sudden wave of sympathy washed over me. I also spent all day tormented by studying and training, with no time to hang out with anyone and relieve stress. That company dinner was just an uncomfortable event where I worried about making a mistake.

Jin Hayeon stuck to me all day, a cold-blooded person who only engaged in professional conversation. Just as she had her doll, my only friend was my diary, where I wrote down my resentment towards the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult in Hangul every day.

"It’s the same for me."

Perhaps because of this strange kinship, talking with Seon'ah felt much more comfortable.

"Do you want to visit like this often? I needed someone to talk to, too."

"Really?" Seon'ah asked, smiling brightly and not avoiding my gaze.

"Of course."

Just then, Jin Hayeon cut in with a cold voice. "Young Lord. The promise you just made could be problematic…"

As soon as she spoke, the little girl’s spirits fell, and she looked dejected again.

"Seon'ah kept her promise this time, didn’t she? I’ll keep to all my study and training hours, so don’t worry."

The dejected little girl took that as confirmation and lifted her head to look at me.

"Then, are we doing that thing from before again?"

"That thing from before?" I asked, puzzled.

Seon'ah held out her small finger and wiggled it.

"Ah! The promise?"

"Yes! The promise."

Seeing the cute, brightly smiling girl, I felt some of my stress from being trapped in the Demonic Cult begin to fade. I smiled happily and hooked my finger with the child's, making a promise.

Jin Hayeon watched from the side and sighed deeply.

*I know my entrance to the Demonic Academy is important, but why is she being so blatant? If she was going to be like this, she should have stopped the company dinner!*

That dinner was a much more serious problem if it was about reducing my study and training time.

As if to answer my question, Jin Hayeon's telepathy pierced my ear.

[Young Lord. It is dangerous to get close to a girl from the Hyeokryeon Clan.]

"???"

*What is this nonsense?*

I made a confused face. She tried to send another telepathic message, but an intruder appeared before she could.

CREAK. THUD!

The main gate of the Wind Cliff Pavilion swung open without the courtesy of a knock. My gaze turned to the gate.

A beautiful woman in her early thirties stood there, scanning the pavilion's interior with cold eyes. Behind her, the faint figures of several men and women were visible.

The woman's eyes changed subtly when she spotted Seon'ah next to me.

Seon'ah's shoulders flinched as their eyes met. "M-Mother…" she murmured.

Only then did I notice that Seon'ah's face resembled the woman's. By the time I realized the woman was Seon'ah's mother, her expression had turned even more venomous as she glared at me.

"What do you think you’re doing right now?" she snapped, her tone as venomous as her expression.

But another woman here was just as cold.

"Lady Cheonghwa, what is the meaning of this rudeness?" Jin Hayeon said. "This is where Young Lord Ilmok, the disciple of the Supreme Lord, is studying."

At Jin Hayeon's cold reply, Lady Cheonghwa’s gaze passed over me and landed on her.

"You’re the one being rude. No matter how great Young Lord Ilmok is as the Supreme Lord’s disciple, he isn’t the Heavenly Demon. Are you saying the Supreme Lord’s disciple is above the great Hyeokryeon Clan? Furthermore! For a mere handmaiden to dare talk back to me, you must be trying to ride on the Supreme Lord's authority."

Her viciousness was on par with the mothers-in-law from dramas. It seemed a step above Jin Hayeon's, who was losing her emotions from the effects of demonic art.

While Jin Hayeon could not respond, Lady Cheonghwa snapped again.

"I can understand that the Eighth Young Master doesn’t know the cult’s customs, since he hasn’t been here long. However, you should be teaching him about the Demonic Cult. You aren’t doing your job, so you’ll have to pay the price for your failure!"

*Custom? What custom?*

That thought came first, but something else was more important.

"What kind of ruckus are you causing in someone else’s pavilion?"

Even though Jin Hayeon felt more like a guard than a handmaiden, she was still my subordinate.

*Seriously, there’s a limit to how much you can disrespect someone.*

Berating my handmaiden in front of me was the same as berating me. In a way, it was even more insulting.

I stepped between the two women. Lady Cheonghwa glared at me with venomous eyes.

In my past life, that kind of glare would have made me cautious. She was like the daughter-in-law of a chaebol family in modern terms.

But now, I scoffed.

*So what if you’re a chaebol’s daughter-in-law? I’m the favorite disciple of the heretic Cult Leader.*

It did not sound like something to be proud of, but within this heretic cult, it was serious clout.
