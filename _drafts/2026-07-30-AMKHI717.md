I returned to the practice room with Jeong Seongbin. He started nagging the moment we arrived, asking why I was here instead of the dorm, and only quieted down when I told him I just had something to say and would leave right after.

"I've caused a bit of trouble."

"Yeah, well, it's better that you're the one who caused it."

"I stuck my nose in MYTH's business."

"Or maybe not..."

Lee Cheonghyeon did a complete one-eighty. His swift change of attitude almost made me laugh, but I held it in since no one else was laughing.

"I'm going to explain it from beginning to end. Is that okay?"

"Just say whatever you need to."

Kang Kiyeon generously gave me the floor. Thanks to him, I could explain the situation comfortably, without feeling rushed.

The story was not very complicated. I had simply meddled, fanned the flames, and then worried the fire would not burn bright enough. After hearing it all, the guys just stared with their mouths agape.

"So, I was suffering from a guilty conscience and ended up with a nosebleed..."

"I get it..."

Park Joowoo must have been dumbfounded, because he only sighed and nodded half-heartedly. He even gave me an insincere pat on the back.

*Is this what a troublemaker feels like when they're praised for only breaking one of two windows?*

"It'd be weirder if you weren't stressed. It's not like this is completely unrelated to you," Lee Cheonghyeon said.

He was not the only one. They all understood my situation.

"So it's safe to say that incident from before won't be brought up again, right?"

"Incident? Ah, the photo... You don't have to worry. MYTH will be more careful about it than I am now."

At my reassurance, Kang Kiyeon sighed in relief. That must have been his biggest concern.

"So what happens now?" Choi Jeho asked. "If it's sensitive, we can talk about it later at the dorm."

"It's not that. It's just not something I can say for sure. It'll depend on how Senior Han Gawoon decides to act."

Lee Cheonghyeon was curious about my ideal outcome. I was about to say 'for them to get hit with a shit-ton of fines' but I toned it down.

"Won't I only feel better after seeing them pay a few billion won?"

"A few billion...?!"

Park Joowoo's face turned pale.

*Is it really that surprising?*

A few hundred million or a few billion won is only a lot of money to an ordinary person. To a company on MYTH's level, that amount is nothing. It might be more trivial than the receipts they submit for entertainment expenses after playing golf and going on trips. If we sued UtopiA right now, the penalty fees would start in the hundreds of millions.

"A company that's made a habit of doing bad things won't reorganize its processes to avoid making the same mistake. They only come to their senses after their hard-earned operating profits get pointlessly drained."

Only when a huge chunk of money vanishes will the higher-ups make a fuss and demand it never happen again. Only then will the company start to lay low.

Hanpyeong Industry was the same. Countless times, they scrambled to respond only after getting caught and sensing they were screwed. That is how regulations get established, even if the loopholes surface years later because nobody thought things through.

MYTH did not seem much different from Hanpyeong Industry. In that case, they needed to be crushed with a hefty fine. What can you do when those bastards are so arrogant they do not know how to be careful? Let them off the hook because it is their first time, and they will just come back as even bigger thugs.

"It's unrealistic, and it's just a wish, so I'm not getting my hopes up. I'll be satisfied just thinking of the fact that they can't affect us anymore as an achievement."

Realistically, it was not easy for Senior Han Gawoon, a well-known celebrity with time left on his contract, to step up. Telling someone to live honestly or sacrifice for the greater good is something you can only say when you have a way to help them. Unless I was going to get him a legal representative, I should not carelessly hold onto hope.

*If Senior Han Gawoon speaks up, we can hope for a major blow, but if not, all we can do is hope for supporting fire from somewhere else.*

I did not want to force him, saying he should cooperate if he has a conscience just because of what I went through. Even if I did not get the satisfying ending I wanted, maintaining this level of detachment was the right call as long as I was not a direct party.

"So we should just focus on our comeback, right?"

"Yeah. As expected of our leader. You get right to the point."

Perhaps because they had suffered so much since IDC, the guys' expressions relaxed at the mere words that they would not have to get involved with MYTH anymore. I also decided to stop looking at entertainment news for a while to recover my condition.

Focusing only on my main job, my mental state recovered quickly. I returned to the days of passionate practice at once.

A few days later, however, the situation reversed when Jeong Seongbin came over with his phone.

"Hyung, you have to see this..."

A familiar SNS page displayed an unfamiliar account. A long block of text sat below a black picture, the bottom cut off from view.

Hello. This is Han Gawoon.

My heart is heavy to be greeting you over such an unfortunate matter.

{sep}

The account owner was Senior Han Gawoon. The post began with a serious introduction and seemed endless no matter how far I scrolled. It detailed his conflict with the agency and how all of MYTH's idols were experiencing the same problems.

There were many things I had not known. If they acted like such assholes to a nationally famous group like Hellas, what could other idols have done? Just reading it was devastating.

What infuriated me more was that this much text had been written in a clear, concise, itemized format. I was reluctant to even call it a company. It deserved a nickname like 'den of devils'.

While mainly dealing with MYTH's coercion of Parthe, Senior Han Gawoon also masterfully worked in the issues that former employees had unanimously raised. He had perfectly understood what it meant to join forces with the whistleblowing employees.

He did not write the main text emotionally either. It was dry, leaving no room for criticism that he was fishing for sympathy.

Considering how often celebrities appealing to sincerity are accused of being emotional, it was a wise choice. People often make mistakes like this if there is no one around to guide them.

Had the statement been entrusted to someone else, this point would not have been new. It was surprising because he had clearly written it himself.

*A legal representative wouldn't add superfluous comments that could harm their client...*

Senior Han Gawoon's post was not just about his own hardships. While discussing the company's concept theft and favoritism through lobbying, he did not forget to apologize to the people who were harmed. He wrote without reservation that he was ashamed for not stepping up despite knowing about the wrongdoing, and that he was sorry for causing trouble to his colleagues.

Since MYTH had previously posted an apology, I thought he would pretend not to know. Seeing that he had even appointed a representative, he clearly intended to confront the company head-on. Telling a story bound to attract criticism in a post under his own name, however, not Parthe's or a law firm's, was impossible without extraordinary courage.

"...He's confronting MYTH, right?"

"...Looks like it."

The part about being blackmailed over the tampering issue was also brilliantly included.

≫ Mentioned a colleague from another company for the purpose of blackmail

Is this about Hellas and Kim Iwol?

Yes, it is about us!

He never specified Hellas or my name, yet anyone who had seen the news could infer it from the context. There was no need to describe what photo was used. Hellas's fans would naturally rise up after realizing their artist was being used by his former company even after transferring.

The fact that there was an issue between MYTH and me was clearly stated in the court ruling from the dispute between me and my parents. It included the part where my dad stalked my colleague and acted as a cyber wrecker selling me out, passing that information to a rival company to apply pressure.

He very cleverly used me, a person whose messy involvement with MYTH was legally confirmed. Then again, this would only make MYTH more desperate to hide my photo.

The previous exposé posts on community forums had also spread quite quickly. However, the speed at which a post made directly on SNS by a popular idol spread was on a different level. The fandoms of various idols, realizing their own idols were under pressure bordering on blackmail from their companies, instantly erupted.

≫ Who knows what else will come out if they dig deeper. These horror stories just keep coming;;

└ It's not a matryoshka doll, but a new whistleblower comes out every day. Is this even a company?

≫ It's not just that MXX are being assholes, but they used our boys who were trying to have a beautiful farewell for something like that XX Leave Hellas alone. It was already shitty enough that they got hate because of the survival show MC you guys lobbied for. How long are you going to keep ruining the future of kids who left the company?

≫ I saw a comment from a broadcast station on the very first exposé post. Are they going to dig it all up?

└ Seeing as they're accepting tips from former employees, it looks like they're going to get thoroughly exposed. I'm waiting.

≫ It feels like just yesterday that PRT's reputation plummeted after they caused a national outrage for their power abuse, but now seeing them become victims of power abuse feels XX weird. What even is society?

The news spread like wildfire. The desire for a satisfying resolution to power abuse, an issue the younger generation could sympathize with, combined with the familiar social issue of injustice, 'an idol I know', and the algorithm, creating a synergy.

Everyone began to watch MYTH's every move.

≫ MYTH to be Investigated by Police for Power Abuse Allegations

≫ The Shadow of the Idol Industry Without Improvement, Is This Okay?

≫ Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism to Launch Fact-Finding Investigation into Pop Culture and Arts Agencies

Truly, everyone. The situation had blown up to a scale incomparable to their past history of lobbying a few broadcast stations. The ideal scenario I had envisioned was unfolding without a single deviation.
