A crazy old man coerced me while spouting absurd nonsense. Normally, I would have dropped to my knees and begged for forgiveness.

I had already cast aside half my pride as a civil servant dealing with public complaints. I learned the hard way in my first year that protecting my pride only made things messier.

It was even more true here. In this world where martial artists ran wild, a misstep could get me crippled or killed, not just complicate things.

But for some reason, my knees would not bend so easily.

How should I put it? It just fucking sucked.

*Was it self-loathing for the life I'd led these past few years, casting my pride aside?*

*Or was it defiance, a refusal to kneel before the bastards who might have killed Taehyun, the man who had cared for me like a father for a year?*

*Or perhaps this young body was surrendering to a primal rage, contrary to all reason?*

Maybe it was a mix of all those complicated things. I did not know the reason. My body and mouth simply ignored the voice of reason screaming, *'Get on your knees and beg for your life right now!'*

"What a load of fucking shit."

The old man, who exuded an aura like Mount Tai, twitched an eyebrow at my insolent expression.

"...What did you just say?"

Despite his overwhelming presence, my mouth moved on its own.

"You kill a man who was like a father to me and tell me to become your disciple? If you're going to be crazy, at least do it gracefully, you senile old murderer!"

After I let loose a stream of curses at the old man, my heart felt a little lighter. At the same time, a sharp pain shot through my neck.

My vision blurred in an instant, and a thought occurred to me.

*So I'm getting stabbed to death again.*

*Is this really the end this time? Or will I experience reincarnation or spirit possession again?*

With that empty thought, I lost consciousness.

{sep}

"......"

A dull ache spread through my entire body. I groaned and opened my eyes to an unfamiliar ceiling.

*Damn it. Is this another spirit possession?*

But a voice from my side shattered my expectations.

"You're awake."

"!?"

Goosebumps instantly erupted all over my body. We had not spoken for long, but it was a voice I could never forget. It was the voice of the old man who had shoved a knife into my neck.

"H-How?"

My muttered word was loaded with meaning. The old man looking down at me asked, "What do you mean?"

"Why, why am I still alive?"

The moment I asked, a terrible thought flashed through my mind.

*He's not done taking his anger out on me, is he!? Maybe killing me with a single strike was too boring, so he planned to drag me around and slowly torture me to death!?*

As a horrifying future painted itself in my mind, the old man, for some reason, burst into hearty laughter.

"Hahahaha."

It was a strange feeling. The old man who had boasted an aura like Mount Tai was nowhere to be seen. He had the same face and voice, yet he exuded no ferocious energy. He appeared almost harmless.

Perhaps because of the old man's calm atmosphere, I managed to pull myself together and scan my surroundings.

*A carriage... I think?*

It seemed I was being transported in a carriage with the old man. Just then, the old man finished his laugh, stroked his long beard with a calm face, and opened his mouth.

"First, I believe we should clear up a misunderstanding."

"A misunderstanding? What kind of misunderstanding are you talking about?"

"Hmm. I don't know if you'll believe me or not, but we weren't the ones who murdered the owner of that inn."

"..."

My expression hardened unconsciously at his words. It seemed this young body of mine could not easily hide drastic emotional changes. I could tolerate humbling my own pride, but it was difficult to face my benefactor's enemy with a smile.

"Then who killed him?"

"It was the work of the Murim Alliance."

"...Are you trying to pin the blame on the Murim Alliance?"

The Murim Alliance was a coalition of orthodox sects. While most martial artists could be considered gangsters or thugs in modern terms, the Murim Alliance was the group that at least pretended to be the 'good guys'. Seeing him try to frame them, this old man had to be a demon or some villain with a grudge against the Murim Alliance.

"It is the opposite. The Alliance killed the inn owner and tried to frame me for it."

"Why would the Murim Alliance do such a thing?"

"Only then could they tarnish my name and unite their forces, could they not?"

Listening to him, I wondered if he was suffering from dementia. Who was this old man that the orthodox sects would unite just to frame him? However, recalling the old man's immense presence just before I passed out, it did not seem like complete nonsense.

"Then what was that cutting sound I heard from the kitchen?"

"That was the sound of me killing the Murim Alliance assassin who murdered the inn owner. In the first place, I had no plans to stop at that inn. I merely dropped by on a whim. For the owner of such an inn to serve me poison, as if he had prepared for it, makes no sense, does it not?"

Having said that, the old man's eyebrow twitched once as if he remembered something. He said in a calm tone, "Come to think of it, this must be quite an unfair situation for you and the inn owner. To have gone through such a thing simply because I stopped by that inn."

Seeing the old man with a genuinely apologetic look on his face felt strange. Back when I worked as a civil servant, if I apologized like that, the entitled complainants would only get more emboldened and try to abuse their power even more. In other words, this was the perfect moment to get angry at the old man or act high and mighty.

*Somehow, he's similar to us, yet completely different.*

Maybe it was because I had caught a glimpse of the old man's true power, but that is how it felt. Like the final act of mercy from a predator? My whole body sounded an alarm, warning me that if I took his kindness for weakness, I might get torn to shreds.

Therefore, instead of getting angry, I asked my question in a calm tone.

"Then why didn't you say so at the time? It feels like you just came up with a convenient excuse while I was unconscious."

At my question, the old man stroked his beard and said, "There are two main reasons. First, there was no time for a lengthy explanation."

When I gave him a look of incomprehension, the old man elaborated.

"The fact that an assassin poisoned the food at that inn meant the Alliance was on my tail. It wouldn't have been long before the Alliance's hunting dogs swarmed the place. Of course, I'm not afraid of the Alliance's hunting dogs, but it would've been difficult to fight while also protecting a promising disciple candidate I had found after so long."

"Then why did you force such a choice on me?"

"That is the second reason. In that urgent moment, I wanted to test you."

"???"

"No matter how talented a child is in martial arts, if his character is twisted, raising him will only bring disaster. Though it was due to a misunderstanding, I was pleased with your spirit, choosing to lose your life rather than become the disciple of an enemy. Hohoho."

"..."

So, he was pleased that I cursed him out and defied him? This was not some drama from ten years ago, was it? Was this a 'you're the first person to ever treat me like this' kind of thing?

But that absurdity was fleeting.

*If I had said I'd become his disciple, I would have been fucking dead, wouldn't I?*

Goosebumps broke out all over my body again. My spontaneous act of defiance had actually saved my life.

Just then, the old man, having finished his explanation, asked, "Can you understand a little better now?"

"...I can't trust you completely, but I think it makes sense."

It was not just because the old man's story was logical. The biggest reason was that this old man had kept me alive. He had saved and brought along a waiter who had cursed him out while refusing to become his disciple. That act, unheard of in this world, somehow made me feel that the old man was not someone who killed people indiscriminately.

As I was organizing these thoughts, I came to my senses and met the old man's steady gaze. His eyes were like a calm, deep lake. I could not dare to fathom its depths, yet it felt as if that lake was reflecting me entirely, reading all my inner thoughts.

While feeling this strange emotion, I opened my mouth with a glimmer of hope.

"However, I do not completely believe your words, Elder. Therefore, I can't bring myself to become your disciple. If you would rather let me off here, I'll return to the village and find out what happened at the inn."

Whether the old man's words were true or not, I did not want to get involved in the affairs of the murim. It was a harsh world where people dropped dead almost daily. All I wanted was a peaceful and stable work-life balance, working moderately and resting moderately.

However, the old man, who had been conversing gently until now, shook his head firmly this time.

"That's not possible."

"...May I ask why?"

"The reason is simple. Why do you think I brought you along when you have not yet agreed to become my disciple?"

"..."

*Well, wasn't it to forcibly kidnap me and work me to the bone as a disciple?*

I could not bring myself to say those words out loud. I was afraid of the old man, but also, it did not add up when I thought about it. If he was going to force me to be his disciple, there was no reason to treat me so kindly.

As I met the old man's eyes, which seemed to be waiting for me to find the answer myself, I soon reached my own conclusion.

"Is it perhaps to prevent me from being killed by the Murim Alliance, like Taehyun?"

"You're correct. If you remain alive as a witness, it will disrupt their plan to tarnish my name. They're the kind of people who would kill you as well and pin everything on me."

"I'll absolutely never say anything that slanders you, Elder. No, I'll keep my mouth shut about you and go into hiding. So, please, just let me..."

"The Murim Alliance's surveillance network is tighter than you think, and no matter how upright a person's mind tries to be, the body is not always so. If the Alliance's hunting dogs capture you, pull out all your fingernails and toenails, press a hot iron to your body, or flay your skin piece by piece while they torture you to confess the truth, do you think you could endure it?"

"..."

I could not bring myself to say yes. No matter how much I tried to deceive myself, it was impossible to withstand that much torture.

As if reading my mind, the old man drove the final nail into the coffin.

"The moment you became entangled with me, you were left with only two choices. Join me, or be chased by the Murim Alliance for the rest of your life until you are captured in a prison and die at the end of a torture."

"..."

I had stepped in shit, and not just any dog shit, I had stepped into a world completely filled with shit. No, on second thought, I was not the one who stepped in it. The moment that old man entered the inn, the entire world turned to shit.

However, perhaps because my options had been reduced to one, my thoughts became quite simple, separate from my feelings of injustice and anger.

*If there's no other choice, I have to follow the remaining one diligently, don't I?*

"Is the only choice left for me to become your disciple, Elder?"

"That is so."

"Then, may I be so bold as to ask for the esteemed name of the Elder who may become my Master?"

I had to find out if the rope I was grabbing was a lifeline or a rotten cord.

"Hohoho. My esteemed name, you say. Your tone seems to have changed."

"Ahem..."

"I was joking. My name is Hyeokryeon Ilhwi."

Hearing the old man's name, I almost tilted my head in confusion. If I pretended not to have heard it here, I might hurt the old man's feelings. An old man who spoke as if the entire Murim Alliance was after him must have considerable pride. He might be offended if I did not know his name. That was how most martial artists thought.

However, perhaps my acting was a bit lacking, as the old man smiled faintly and spoke again.

"Hohoho. It seems my name is less known in the Central Plains than my alias. In the Central Plains, they often call this old man the Heavenly Demon."

The Heavenly Demon.

That was the alias of the old man who had stained my world with shit.
