Ilmok's sword crashed against the spear Jang Hoe had thrown, creating a massive explosion of sound.

The three people watching the scene had nearly identical reactions. Shock filled their faces because they knew how long Ilmok had been learning martial arts. After the initial shock, however, their expressions diverged.

*Damn it...*

Jang Hoe, who had tried to take Ilmok down with him, felt a wave of despair. Witnessing such incredible talent made his failure to kill Ilmok an even greater regret.

Worry then clouded the faces of Jin Hayeon and Gwan Muyeol.

"Cough..."

Although Ilmok had blocked the incoming spear, the difference in skill was undeniable. He stumbled back about three meters and coughed up blood.

"Young Lord!"

"Damn it..."

Ilmok spat out a curse, his face contorted with rage as if his pride had been wounded.

*To reach the level of a sword qi master in just six months is enough to be called a genius.*

*Is his pride hurt because he almost died?*

While Gwan Muyeol and Jin Hayeon had these separate thoughts, Ilmok muttered with an expression of pure frustration.

"My level… it rose again..."

Then, he collapsed onto the ground.

"……"

"……"

An odd silence fell. The three who had been in the middle of a fierce battle stared blankly at Ilmok, who now lay unconscious on the ground.

{sep}

*This ceiling is familiar.*

That was my first thought when I opened my eyes.

But this wasn't the Wind Cliff Pavilion where I usually slept. It was the ceiling of the clinic, a place I had visited once before.

"Are you feeling any better?"

Jin Hayeon's voice reached my ears.

I turned my head and saw Jin Hayeon looking down at me with her usual deadpan expression. She was covered in bandages. Master was also by her side.

"To be attacked five times, escape death three times, and end up in the clinic twice in just six months. You truly have an eventful fate."

I couldn't help but smile bitterly at Master's words.

From Eldest Senior Brother's rampage to the Gaochang County Branch Manager going berserk while teaching children, the bandit attack, the last assassin, and now Jang Hoe. My days had been nothing short of chaotic, but that wasn't why I smiled.

*The old man conveniently left out what he did.*

The first and most dangerous attack I ever faced was when Master held a sword to my neck and forced me to become his disciple. That incident was the root of all this trouble.

*Honestly, compared to the pressure I felt from Master back then, the rest feels like nothing...*

He was the reason my nerves had become so steady.

"Still, it seems you're growing quickly because of it. People grow stronger through adversity, so don't worry too much."

"…Yes, Master."

I wondered if he somehow sensed me cursing him internally, but there was a more important issue at hand.

*Right. My level rose...*

The moment I remembered, my whole body started to itch.

Honestly, I couldn't remember exactly how I had created the sword qi. I could only vaguely recall the feeling. So now, my obsessive compulsion was kicking in.

*I have to find that feeling again!*

*I need to grab a sword!*

*Now's my only chance!*

As I fought a fierce battle inside my head, Master's voice snapped me back to reality.

"I'd like to order you to rest since you haven't fully recovered, but there's an urgent matter. Let's handle that first, then you can rest."

"An urgent matter?"

"I heard the whole story from Wanpyeong and Hayeon. Wanpyeong has finished the preparations, so shouldn't you check the late-night snack?"

"......"

The events from before I fainted came rushing back.

With Jin Hayeon's help, I sat up and followed Master out of the clinic. The sky outside was still dark.

"How long was I asleep?"

"Probably not even two hours, Young Lord."

I chatted briefly with Jin Hayeon to get my bearings as we walked. Soon, we arrived at the teahouse, the place responsible for the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's tea and refreshments, but the atmosphere was different from usual.

*Wow… this is like a scene from a historical drama.*

A torch burned in the center of the teahouse, surrounded by dozens of martial artists who stood guard with a grim air. In the middle, several men and women knelt like criminals, a scene reminiscent of an interrogation from a period drama.

Master strode through the tense scene, sat in the seat of honor, and commanded, "Begin."

I thought an interrogation was about to start, but what happened next was unexpected. Two of the kneeling people, a man and a woman, stood up and began to brew tea and prepare snacks.

"Ilmok, from now on, carefully watch the ingredients and the tea they handle."

I finally understood the situation. Supported by Jin Hayeon, I approached them and began to inspect the ingredients they were using.

As I watched them prepare the late-night snack, one thought dominated my mind.

*I think I'm screwed...*

Maybe my head wasn't working right because of my injuries, but I couldn't tell what was wrong. To my eyes, they were just making snacks and brewing tea with ordinary ingredients.

*Should I just point at someone and frame them? Or should I be honest and say I don't know?*

Something was definitely going on, especially with Jang Hoe's sudden attack. Maybe I could bluff my way through it.

Just as I had that thought, the tea and snacks were ready.

"Well?"

When Master's question hit my ears, it felt like an eternity passed. After much deliberation, I made my decision.

"I didn't find anything unusual."

I couldn't predict what trouble a baseless accusation might cause.

Master gave me a strange look, then turned to Third Senior Brother, who had been waiting by his side.

"Taste it."

"As you command."

Third Senior Brother glanced at me once before walking toward the prepared table.

"First, the appearance is similar to what was served that day, Master."

He took a bite of a snack, then lifted a teacup and took a sip.

"…The taste and aroma are completely different from what I had that day, Master."

I breathed an internal sigh of relief. Being honest had been the right choice.

Master's face hardened with anger.

"Are you daring to mock me right now?"

The two who had prepared the food immediately prostrated themselves and cried out.

"We're innocent!"

"We… we made it exactly the same as that day. The taste of food can feel different depending on a person's mood. Because you suspected us, the Third Young Master felt it was different!"

At their protest, killing intent flared on Third Senior Brother's already gloomy face.

"How dare you treat me like a fool in front of Master? The appearance and taste were similar to the snacks from that day, but something was missing. It was much sweeter and more flavorful that day, and the tea was the same. It seems like the same tea, but it was much stronger and more bitter back then."

Hearing Senior Brother's words, something suddenly occurred to me.

*Come to think of it, don't they say sensitive people can be at risk if they drink too much tea or soda? Could it be that instead of using 'that', they just brewed the tea much stronger to intensify the effect?*

It was a possibility, but it didn't feel like the whole story.

*Senior Brother said the snack was missing something too, so there must be more to it.*

The two culprits and Third Senior Brother continued to argue their innocence. I was considering whether to intervene when several men emerged from the darkness. One of them had a familiar face.

*Master's personal bodyguard.*

It was the man who drove the carriage when Master first kidnapped me, the best bodyguard in the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult and the Director of the Bodyguard Hall. They each held something out.

"We searched the teahouse and their quarters and found these things."

Master nodded at the Bodyguard Hall Director's report and ordered me forward.

"Come and check."

As I walked toward Master with Jin Hayeon's help, a thought crossed my mind.

*He's so thorough.*

It seemed he had quickly detained the suspects to prevent them from destroying evidence, then sent his direct subordinates to collect it.

I approached them and began to examine the various pieces of evidence they presented. Most were things I had never seen before, but one item mixed in among them was familiar.

*…Wow. I can't believe this actually exists in this era.*

It was dark brown, shaped like a bean, and gave off a strangely sweet and bitter aroma. It was a coffee bean.

{sep}

Like most people overworked in the Republic of Korea, civil servants often enjoyed coffee when they arrived at work or after lunch. I frequently drank coffee with my seniors, and Lee Jiyeon was no different.

But one day, she stopped drinking it completely.

"I'll buy coffee today."

"Senior Official Seo Jihun. Then I'll have chamomile tea instead of coffee, please."

"Hmm? Come to think of it, Senior Official Lee Jiyeon, you don't drink coffee these days?"

"Ah, yes… It just doesn't agree with me anymore."

I found it strange that someone who had been drinking it for months would suddenly say that. Later that afternoon, her colleague, Kim Changwan, explained the situation.

"I guess the stress of dealing with civil complaints was too much. She recently went to a psychiatrist and was diagnosed with depression and panic disorder."

The news was quite shocking, and I felt sorry for her, but I was also curious.

"But what does coffee have to do with depression?"

"I'm not exactly sure, but I heard that if you drink coffee when your depression is severe, your heart pounds and you become more sensitive. You also feel suffocated, like you're about to have a panic attack."

"......"

{sep}

I vaguely remembered searching online about caffeine's side effects and foods that were bad for depression. I didn't have depression back then, so I only looked it up once and moved on. My memory of the details was hazy.

Besides, there was no guarantee they had given Second Senior Brother coffee. I didn't even know if coffee existed in this era. I was worried I wouldn't recognize anything, but I couldn't believe they had actually used coffee.

The situation was absurd, but I had more pressing matters to attend to.

"Third Senior Brother. Was the missing taste and aroma similar to this?"

I held out a coffee bean to Third Senior Brother. He came closer, sniffed it, then popped a bean into his mouth.

"Yes! This is the taste and aroma that was missing!"

At Third Senior Brother's exclamation, I turned to Master and spoke.

"Master. This is an ingredient typically used to make a type of tea by pouring boiling water over it. The tea made from this sharpens the mind and improves concentration, but it also has side effects like causing insomnia and making a person's nerves edgy, which can induce feelings of depression."

"Is that what you learned from that inn owner?"

"Yes, it is."

I felt sorry for the late Taehyun, but I couldn't exactly say it was a memory from my past life, so I made a plausible excuse.

Master seemed convinced. He nodded and gave his orders.

"Imprison them immediately and interrogate them to find out who's behind this. Also, investigate their past activities to find any accomplices, and discover the source of these wicked beans."

"As you command!"

As the assembled men bowed their heads to Master's command, the accused, their guilt now proven, cried out in desperation.

"This is a setup, the Heavenly Demon!"

"Third Young Master! I've served you for over ten years, how could you do this! Besides, didn't I also eat the late-night snack with you that day!? I'm innocent!"

"Didn't you hear him! He said it amplifies feelings of depression! It's medicine for healthy people, but it's been revealed to be poison for me. How dare you run your mouth!"

The enraged Third Senior Brother was not fooled by their act. I watched for a moment as they were dragged away.

*I guess my job here is done?*

I sighed in relief.

Of course, there would be interrogations and investigations to uncover who was behind it all, but that wasn't my responsibility. It wasn't like I had any talent for that kind of work anyway. More importantly, I had no time for it right now.

"Master."

I had something far more urgent to do.

"Speak."

With Master's permission, I replied in a very serious tone.

"Then, this disciple will return to the Wind Cliff Pavilion to train."

The memory of creating sword qi had been flickering in my mind ever since I woke up. I was dying to swing my sword, and this strange interrogation was driving me crazy.

But Master just frowned as if he couldn't understand my words.

"Train?"

"That's right. I must immediately make the insight I just gained my own..."

Before I could finish my explanation, my vision suddenly went black.

{sep}

Ilmok collapsed.

"Young Lord?"

Jin Hayeon, who had been supporting Ilmok, muttered in confusion as he lost consciousness in the middle of his sentence.

The voice of the Heavenly Demon, a god in her eyes, brought her back to her senses.

"I struck his pressure point."

She realized the Heavenly Demon had knocked out his youngest disciple.

"Overwhelming talent is also a problem. To think he suffers from such side effects."

The Heavenly Demon looked down at the unconscious Ilmok with a pitying expression, then gave Jin Hayeon an order.

"Until the injuries he sustained today are fully healed, only wake him briefly for meals. After he eats, put him back to sleep by force. His body could be ruined by excessive training."

Supporting Ilmok, Jin Hayeon replied to the absolute command from the one who was like the heavens to her.

"I will obey your command."

Ilmok would not have to worry about insomnia for a while.
