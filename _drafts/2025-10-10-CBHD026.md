Master kept his promise. After the uncomfortable meeting with Eldest Senior Brother, Master dragged his busy self over to personally perform my Body Purification and qi guidance.

Perhaps because the Demonic Cult's greatest master performed the procedure himself, my body recovered quickly without any lingering effects. I left the clinic after just a few days. The Wind Cliff Pavilion was also fully repaired in that time.

Even after I returned to the Wind Cliff Pavilion, Master kept his promise yet again.

Meaning.

"Gasp. Gasp."

"Ahem. How do you expect to strike an enemy's opening when you're that slow?"

He worked me like a dog.

I felt it during his last visit. The man knew nothing of moderation. Then again, one couldn't become the leader of this insane group with only moderate effort. For Master, a life of haaaard work was simply natural.

*Let's take a break, pleeeease!!*

I was the type who would have been satisfied just reaching a level sufficient to enter the Demonic Academy. This wasn't to say Master kept me for all twelve sessions of the day. He was a busy man, the Cult Leader after all.

He trained me for roughly three sessions a day, but those three sessions amounted to six hours of work that left no part of my body unscathed.

After I endured about ten days of this hellish special training with Master, he finally declared my liberation.

"Hmm. It seems you've more or less got the basics down. Starting tomorrow, I'll drop by for just one session a day, like before."

My daily life returned to normal. In the mornings, I had history lessons with Jin Hayeon.

"Please turn to the next page, Young Lord."

Of course, the fractures throughout her bones couldn't have healed in just ten days. I suggested she rest until she recovered from her severe injuries, but she wouldn't listen.

"As the Young Lord's attendant, how can I rest comfortably after failing as a bodyguard?"

So she was saying she could only be at ease teaching lessons, even with a broken arm? It was certainly different when you mixed a psychopath with religious fanatics.

Psychopaths supposedly prioritize their own well-being, but for Jin Hayeon, the Demonic Cult came first. She would never give up a task assigned by the cult, even when injured.

However, she couldn't use her arm, so she was unable to attend to my other needs besides the lessons. Thus, another attendant began to frequent the Wind Cliff Pavilion to serve me during her recovery.

As my morning studies ended, a knock came from the entrance of the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Come in."

The moment I gave permission, the door opened. A man entered. He was about 180 centimeters tall, around college-aged. In modern terms, he looked like he had been a bit of a delinquent in school.

*Who's this guy?*

The attendant who had been frequenting the Wind Cliff Pavilion until now didn't look like that. No, a male attendant in the first place? What kind of bizarre nonsense was this?

Fortunately, the man resolved my confusion.

"Greetings, Young Lord Ilmok. I am Guyang Mun of the White Turtle Brigade."

But a question still remained.

"But what business does a martial artist from the White Turtle Brigade have here?"

I had no connection to this man. I had never seen him before, and I had no ties to the White Turtle Brigade.

The man hesitated at my question, then looked at Jin Hayeon beside me and answered.

"Actually, I had something to discuss with Hayeon. I heard she was staying at the Wind Cliff Pavilion and not coming out, so I came to find her here."

At his answer, I turned to Jin Hayeon with a puzzled expression. Jin Hayeon replied with her characteristic emotionless face.

"He was in my class at the Demonic Academy, Young Lord."

"......"

*Did this place also have a culture like the Korean military, shouting about love for country and classmates?*

"It seems you two were quite close at the Demonic Academy?" I asked.

The two gave conflicting answers at the same time.

"That’s right."

"We were just classmates."

At Jin Hayeon's dismissive answer, the young man named Guyang Mun flushed and exclaimed.

"Just classmates! Have you already forgotten the memories of us competing for the valedictorian spot!?"

Oho.

To think they had competed for the top spot among the Demonic Cult's greatest prodigies.

"So, which of you graduated from the Demonic Academy as the valedictorian?"

At my question, Jin Hayeon shot Guyang Mun a cold look. Guyang Mun rubbed his index finger under his nose, a subtle mix of pride and awkwardness.

*Did he come here just to brag?*

I hid my bewilderment and asked, "So, what is it you wanted to say to Miss Jin?"

Guyang Mun suddenly grew hesitant at my question, glancing between me and Jin Hayeon.

*Is it something he can't say in front of me?*

While I pondered this, the man subtly avoided Jin Hayeon's gaze and spoke.

"Ahem. I heard the story. That you were injured stopping the First Young Lord's rampage. See? You should've trained harder."

"...Are you trying to say I'm incompetent?"

The woman who graduated second because of him replied coldly to the valedictorian's words.

"I'm not saying you're Incompetence. I just don't understand why you're wasting your talent as an attendant. Don't you remember? We promised at the Demonic Academy that we'd accomplish great things together for the Divine Cult."

"I chose this path for that great work. I can be closest to the Lord of All Demons and his disciples here. If I improve my skills and build more experience, I might become the Supreme Lord's shadow in the future. As believers, what could be more glorious than that?"

Until now, I had a certain question about Jin Hayeon. Why was a graduate of the Demonic Academy, where only the Demonic Cult's elites gather, working as a mere attendant? And as I learned today, she was even the salutatorian.

Jin Hayeon's answer resolved that question.

*Hmm. Is her goal to become like that person who was always by Master's side?*

The man who dealt with the Murim Alliance assassin when Master kidnapped me. Come to think of it, that man wandered the murim alone with Master, so he must have handled all the miscellaneous tasks.

In other words, to become the Heavenly Demon's personal bodyguard, it seemed one had to be an attendant, a servant, and a martial artist all at once.

However, Guyang Mun couldn't seem to accept that answer.

"Th-that's just an excuse! You don't have the confidence to climb higher, so you're running away with that excuse, aren't you? Tossing aside the promise you made with me!"

Watching Guyang Mun shout petulantly with a red face, a thought came to mind.

*What was that called again...*

Ah, right! Tsundere.

At a glance, his face looked red with anger, but a closer look made it obvious. He averted his gaze whenever he met Jin Hayeon's eyes. He was practically dying of affection.

But, while you could call him a tsundere.

*Is he a kid?*

It was the kind of thing an elementary schooler would do, constantly picking fights with the girl he liked. No, I hear even elementary schoolers don't do that these days.

*I guess it's because he's an old-fashioned guy.*

Whether I was thinking such thoughts or not, Jin Hayeon spoke.

"Are you looking down on this job of serving the Supreme Lord's disciple right now?"

"I'm not looking down on it! It's the truth! Have you even been training properly since you became an attendant? You haven't had any real combat! You used to be on my level, but I bet you're no match for me now!"

The two argued fiercely.

*Must be nice.*

As a man in his mid-thirties, their bickering, like a college students' lovers' quarrel, just seemed cute. The one-sided love was a bit of a problem, though.

"And what's so great about being an attendant anyway! The people you serve are great, but the job itself is nothing special, right?"

"Can you take responsibility for those words?"

"Responsibility? What responsibility?"

"If you're so confident, why don't you try it yourself?"

"Hmph. You think I can't do it? Since your arm is like that, I'll just serve Young Lord Ilmok while you recover!"

Guyang Mun's face twitched as he shouted. It might have looked like anger at a glance, but I could see it.

*He's absolutely thrilled. Dying of happiness.*

He struggled to hide his smile at the thought of staying by Jin Hayeon's side all day under the pretext of serving me.

Perhaps to control his expression, the man fumbled in his sleeve, pulled something out, and held it toward Jin Hayeon. It was a small container filled with clearly expensive medicinal herbs.

"This is for you to get better quickly! D-don't misunderstand! I'm a busy member of the White Turtle Brigade, so I can't serve for long. I have no choice but to give you this!"

What a load of bullshit.

{sep}

Not long after, the attendant meant to serve in Jin Hayeon's place arrived.

"I will be serving the Young Lord, so you may leave."

Guyang Mun sent her away before I could even speak.

Being served by a man... come to think of it, it felt rather unpleasant.

However, Guyang Mun's skills weren't as bad as I expected. Perhaps it was because he had been active as the Youngest in the White Turtle Brigade martial corps after graduating from the Demonic Academy.

"Isn't the weather hot, Young Lord? I happen to have a talent for fanning, you see. Hahaha."

"Hahaha! I knew you'd be thirsty, so I brought some water in advance, Young Lord!"

He was quick-witted like a private in the army, bringing whatever was needed.

Of course, Master also questioned the situation when he visited in the afternoon.

"Hmm. Why are you here?"

"I'm just helping my classmate Hayeon for a bit, until the White Turtle Brigade sets out on its next mission."

Master accepted Guyang Mun's answer and moved on. Guyang Mun spoke perfectly fine, except when dealing with Jin Hayeon.

"Hohoho. It's truly a fine sight to see such harmony between classmates."

"H-harmony? Y-you're too kind! Ahahaha!"

He just lost his composure whenever the topic involved Jin Hayeon.

As for Jin Hayeon, the object of Guyang Mun's unrequited love.

"......"

She just stared at him with a cold face, like a wicked mother-in-law waiting for her daughter-in-law to make a single mistake.

*Seeing how he still likes her after being treated like that, that guy is definitely not in his right mind either.*

Then again, how many sane people could there be in this Demonic Cult?

I felt a little sorry for his one-sided crush, so I decided to keep him as a temporary attendant and just watch. It was definitely not because the spectacle was amusing.

"Hahaha! I've performed Body Purification for the White Turtle Brigade Seniors, so I've become an expert at acupressure, Young Lord!"

And it wasn't because he was a pushover who was surprisingly good at serving me.

And so, Guyang Mun served me in Jin Hayeon's place. I trained my martial arts by dueling with the occasionally visiting Third Senior Brother or with Guyang Mun.

A few days passed like this. I received teachings from Master and took morning lessons from Jin Hayeon. Then I realized one strange fact.

*Seon'ah isn't coming?*

The kid who used to visit almost every day hadn't shown her face for a full fifteen days.
