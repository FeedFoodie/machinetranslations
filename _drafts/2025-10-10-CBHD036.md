The world was stained entirely black and white. In a corner of that world, I swung something around. It was a strange situation where I watched myself, but I felt no unease. I simply stared blankly, observing what I was doing.

As I watched vacantly, I soon realized I was swinging a brush. I also realized the world was not made of black and white. I was the one staining the white world black.

"This isn't it!!"

The 'me' swinging the brush screamed furiously. He painted over his own writing to blacken it, then started writing anew.

*Wow... What a complete madman.*

The thought came to me as I watched my other self, but an unknown sense of foreboding arose with it. The sight was familiar. I had been repeating that very act every night recently.

After recognizing the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword had begun, I had pondered a solution. However, no immediate solution to completely resolve the side effect came to mind. That was why I chose the next best thing. If the obsession was going to come anyway, I would confine that obsession to a specific element.

In other words, I wrote in my journal every night, obsessively correcting my handwriting. I hoped that in exchange for making my handwriting perfect, no other obsessive symptoms would appear. So, the one throwing a fit over there was 'me'. To be precise, it was a version of me when the side effect had worsened.

Even now, the obsession came to me from time to time, but thanks to my compulsive fixation on my handwriting, its frequency was very low. That was why I had been somewhat relieved, but perhaps deep down in my heart, I was afraid I would become like that.

Once my thoughts reached that point, I naturally understood what was happening.

*Is this qi deviation?*

The thought came to me first, but I soon shook my head. I was not in the middle of training or practicing qi circulation. Recalling my faint memories, the last thing I remembered was going to bed.

Which meant.

*It's a stupid dream.*

The moment I realized this was all a nightmare, the image of me struggling with my writing and going crazy vanished. Darkness fell. The reason it was dark was simple. I still had my eyes closed.

"......"

I snapped my eyes open to drive away the unsettling nightmare, but my surroundings were strangely pitch black.

*Am I still dreaming? Or is it just an unusually dark night?*

As I thought this, the clouds obscuring the moon must have parted. Moonlight streamed through the paper window and into the room. The moonlight reflected off something small and faint hidden in the darkness, catching my eye.

I narrowed my eyes and glared, trying to identify the small, faint light. I realized its identity without much difficulty.

It was an eye. A person's slightly open eye, looking down at me from right in front of my face.

"Shit, you scared me!!!"

I was so startled that I swore and shot up. In response to my movement, the eye that had been right in front of my nose moved away. The terrifying sight of an eyeball floating in mid-air sent shivers down my spine.

"Sh-Shit."

*I thought the Demonic Cult was only full of madmen, but are there ghosts here too?*

Then, below the ghost's eye, the darkness split open, and a new, faint light leaked out. It was a person's teeth.

"Are you alright, Junior Brother?"

The voice from the ghost's mouth was quite familiar.

"Third Senior Brother?"

It was the voice of Third Senior Brother Seo Wanpyeong, the mad assassin who suffered from paranoia. He used to visit often in the dead of night, but he had not come at night since I asked him to come during the day.

Just then, I heard the two bodyguard martial artists rush to the entrance and shout.

"What is it, Young Lord!"

"Are you alright!?"

A step behind them, Jin Hayeeon's voice also called out.

"Young Lord!"

Hearing their voices, I barely regained my senses. I answered in the calmest tone I could manage.

"Don't worry. Third Senior Brother came to visit, and we were just having a conversation."

I answered that way, thinking of myself as a hostage. If I asked for help, Third Senior Brother would slice my throat faster than they could burst into the room.

However, Gwan Muyeol shouted accusingly from outside the door, as if unaware of my thoughts.

"Third Young Master. Sneaking in like this at such a late hour is an act of disrespect towards us!"

*Why are these so-called bodyguards provoking an assassin who came in the middle of the night?*

As cold sweat trickled down my back, Third Senior Brother replied in a gloomy tone.

"I just came because I suddenly had something to ask Youngest. I'll be more careful next time."

At Third Senior Brother's apology, the bodyguards and Jin Hayeon soon withdrew. A silence formed now that we were alone. I broke it, asking cautiously.

"But, what brings you here at this late hour?"

The words *'Why are you here again, you crazy bastard'* rose to my throat, but I came to my senses and did not make that mistake. I had already shouted 'Shit', but since I blurted it out in Korean without thinking, it seemed I could just barely get away with it.

"Because of what happened during the day."

"During the day, you say?" I asked back with a puzzled expression.

The Senior Brother in the darkness nodded and replied.

"During the day, I just thought it was a compliment and felt shy, but now that it's night and I've thought it over, I feel like everyone was making fun of me for being embarrassed. I wonder if they were all inwardly sneering, 'Is he proud of something so trivial?'"

"……"

They say people get more emotional at night. It seemed Third Senior Brother's paranoia became more abundant.

*He has... improved, hasn't he?*

The man who used to be gloomy all day was now only gloomy at night, so it was an improvement. Come to think of it, his stealth skills also seemed to have improved compared to the first time Senior Brother visited. Back then, I could vaguely see his form, even if only thanks to the moonlight.

But now, even with the moonlight shining, I could barely see his eyes, and even that was only possible because Senior Brother had opened his eyes right in front of my face.

While I was lost in such thoughts, Senior Brother brought up an unexpected topic.

"More importantly, are you alright, Junior Brother?"

"???"

"Actually, you were sleeping so soundly, Junior Brother, that I was going to wait until you woke up, but you were whimpering as if you were having a nightmare. So, I was watching you up close, debating whether to wake you, when you woke up on your own."

"Ah..."

So that was why his face was so close, and here I was, thinking he was contemplating *'how to kill me'*.

"Ahem. I did have a nightmare, but it's not a big problem."

"That is truly a relief."

"Yes, and you don't need to worry either, Senior Brother. Myself included, everyone said those things because we genuinely wanted to congratulate you."

"Hmm. If it's coming from you, Junior Brother, who is sharp enough to read even Master's inner thoughts, then it must be true."

As if he had come all this way just to hear those words, Senior Brother nodded and began to withdraw. However, just before leaving the room, he suddenly turned his head as if he remembered something.

"By the way, what was that you shouted at Second Senior Brother when you woke up just now? Sipa? Ssi-bal? It was the first time I've heard that word."

"……"

My mind started racing furiously, perhaps because I sensed my life was in danger.

"Th-that was an exclamation that was popular only in my hometown. I-it's a type of dialect. Yes. It means something like, 'Oh my!' or 'What a surprise!' Yes, that's right. Ha, hahaha."

"Oho. An exclamation. How interesting. Sipal. The sound of it really sticks to the tongue."

Third Senior Brother kept muttering 'Sipal' to himself, like a foreigner practicing Korean for the first time. Feeling uneasy, I quickly started talking.

"Ah! And come to think of it, Eldest Senior Brother and the others probably wouldn't, but how about you try talking with Guyang Mun?"

"You mean that energetic fellow from earlier today?"

"That's right. Didn't he say he wanted to experience your Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art, Senior Brother? Whether that guy was mocking Second Senior Brother or being sincere, you'd get a good measure of it if you sparred with him, wouldn't you? Ha. Hahaha."

"Oho. That is also a fine idea."

Senior Brother seemed to like the idea. He nodded and opened the door to leave.

*...Yangmun, I'm sorry.*

The romantically challenged man might just evolve into a virgin ghost.

The next day, fortunately or unfortunately, neither Third Senior Brother nor Guyang Mun came to the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Furthermore, Sixth Senior Brother did not visit either, perhaps because he had only come out of curiosity yesterday.

Thanks to the absence of those three, the Wind Cliff Pavilion was much quieter, but a part of me felt incredibly uneasy.

*Don't tell me one of them actually died?*

Ninety-nine times out of a hundred, it would have been Guyang Mun who died, but then I could not understand why Third Senior Brother had not come.

*Is he disposing of the body...*

I wanted to sink into such thoughts, but Eldest Senior Brother and Hang Geon were chattering nonstop, giving me no time for idle thoughts.

"Hahaha! So as I was saying!"

"Liege. Something like that happened. Hahaha."

The topic of conversation was the school-related duties they had taken on because of me. The only strange thing was why they came to visit almost every day to report to me like this. I could understand Hang Geon, but I was not Eldest Senior Brother's superior.

It seemed to be for the reason he mentioned last time. He wanted to be acknowledged by me and become my Liege.

*...This doesn't seem right.*

Not only did his method feel wrong, but I had no intention of serving Eldest Senior Brother as my Liege. In fact, once I entered the Demonic Academy, I planned to just slack off and live comfortably. I had no intention of serving anyone as my Liege.

"Hahaha. That was truly an excellent choice, Eldest Senior Brother."

Of course, I did not know what would happen if I revealed my inner thoughts to the group leader, so I just glossed it over with some flattery.

Eldest Senior Brother and Hang Geon finished their story, which was somewhere between a report and a boast, and left. With only the two bodyguard martial artists, Seon'ah, and Jin Hayeon remaining, I casually posed a question to Jin Hayeon.

"By the way, didn't you say Eldest Senior Brother also entered the Demonic Academy as the valedictorian?"

If a man with an inferiority complex about being stupid could get in as the valedictorian, I could not help but doubt the standards of the Demonic Academy. I even wondered if I really needed to study and train this hard.

"The First Young Lord did harbor an inferiority complex about his wisdom, but that doesn't mean he was that lacking. He was simply lacking for a disciple of the Supreme Lord."

So she was saying he had an average level of intelligence.

"Also, the Demonic Academy's entrance exam consists of an interview and a duel. From what I've heard, the First Young Lord barely passed the interview, but his duel results were so overwhelmingly superior that he became the valedictorian in the overall evaluation."

I nodded at her explanation, thinking it made sense.

*I don't know about the interview, but is there no maximum score for the duel?*

It seemed the stronger you were, the more extra points you got.

*Right, Eldest Senior Brother reached the Ultimate Demon Realm when he was just over twenty, and in his twenties, he massacred his way through the Central Plains as if it were his own living room, even earning the alias the Crazy Demon.*

And now, at only his early forties, he had achieved complete mastery of the Explosive Mara Sword Technique. His talent for martial arts was on an overwhelming level.

Just as I was digging into Eldest Senior Brother's admissions scandal, the shout of Jang Hoe rang out from the entrance of the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Behold the Heavenly Demon! revered of all demons!"

It meant it was time for Master to arrive. Naturally, Seon'ah, who had been sticking close to my side, got up and headed toward the entrance. It was time for her to leave.

"I greet the Lord of all demons."

"Hohoho. Go on safely now."

Master and Seon'ah exchanged greetings as if changing shifts. Master entered the Wind Cliff Pavilion and took out a small box from his sleeve.

"This is the Explosive Demonic Pill I promised you last time."

"!!!"

They were the miracle herbs that constantly appear in martial arts novels, but which I had only ever heard of and never seen in person. However, rather than being simply overjoyed, I approached cautiously.

"Master. Last time, didn't you say my skills were still lacking and that it was too early for me to consume the Explosive Demonic Pill?"

"Hohoho. You've built a decent foundation now, haven't you? Of course, your qi center is still small, so it will be difficult for it to accept the Explosive Demonic Pill. However, I thought it wouldn't be good to keep postponing your promised reward, so I will personally help you absorb the miracle herbs with my qi guidance."

At Master's explanation, I prostrated myself on the ground.

"Your grace is immeasurable!"

"Hahahaha. Excessive courtesy is not always a good thing, so rise. This old man is not personally providing qi guidance simply because I cherish you. You presented a good opinion regarding the school, but this is being done as a reward, so don't mind it."

Master rambled on, explaining the justification, but it was not particularly important. The important thing was that I was about to consume miracle herbs.

What are miracle herbs! If you were to compare martial arts to modern sports, they were a type of doping. The effects of the medicine!

Of course, the effects of the medicine were not unconditionally good. Depending on the discipline or a person's constitution, some medicines were not suitable. I had heard somewhere that there were 'designers' who adjusted the type and dosage of medicine to best suit a person.

In that sense, absorbing miracle herbs with Master's direct help was truly a miraculous encounter. After all, he was the top expert in this field.

"Take the Explosive Demonic Pill and sit in a cross-legged position."

I took the small pill from the medicine box Master held out and sat down cross-legged.

*Fufufu. Once I absorb this, my acceptance into the Demonic Academy is guaranteed.*

According to the information I heard from Jin Hayeon, the duel carried more weight than the interview. As long as my martial arts realm was high, I just needed to avoid failing the interview.

"After you swallow the Explosive Demonic Pill, do not open your mouth under any circumstances, and you must not faint. Do not lose track of the flow of true qi that this old man will guide."

Following Master's warning and instructions, I swallowed the Explosive Demonic Pill. Master's palm touched my back. The small pill began to melt, pouring out a massive amount of energy. An equally powerful energy from Master's palm penetrated my body.

I focused my mind, determined not to die in vain while consuming miracle herbs. Meanwhile, a feeling arose in a corner of my mind. A feeling that I had forgotten and overlooked something important.
