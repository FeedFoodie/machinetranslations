I saw a world stained entirely black and white. In a corner of that world, I watched myself swing something around. It was a strange situation, but I felt no unease. I simply stared blankly, observing my own actions.

As I watched, I soon realized I was swinging a brush.

I realized then that the world was not made of black and white. I was the one staining the white world black.

The 'me' swinging the brush screamed furiously, "This isn't it!!" He painted over his own writing, blackening it, then started anew.

*Wow... What a complete madman.*

That thought came to me as I watched my other self. An unknown sense of foreboding arose with it, because the sight was familiar. After all, I had been repeating that very act every night recently.

I had pondered a solution after recognizing the side effects of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword had begun. No immediate fix came to mind, so I chose the next best thing. If the obsession was going to come, I would confine it to a specific element.

I wrote in my journal every night, obsessively correcting my handwriting to make it perfect. I hoped that in exchange, no other obsessive symptoms would appear.

So, the one throwing a fit was 'me', or to be precise, a version of me with worsened side effects.

The obsession still came to me sometimes, but my compulsive fixation on my handwriting kept it infrequent. I had been somewhat relieved, but perhaps deep down, I was afraid I would become like that.

Once my thoughts reached that point, I understood what was happening.

*Is this qi deviation?*

That thought came first, but I soon shook my head. I was not training or practicing qi circulation. Recalling my faint memories, I remembered going to bed. That meant one thing.

*It's a stupid dream.*

The moment I realized it was a nightmare, the image of me struggling with my writing vanished. "Huu." Darkness fell.

The reason it was dark was simple. I still had my eyes closed.

"......"

I snapped my eyes open wide to drive away the unsettling nightmare, but my surroundings were strangely pitch black.

*Am I still dreaming? Or is it just an unusually dark night?*

As I thought this, the clouds must have parted. Aphrodisiac moonlight streamed through the paper window, reflecting off something small and faint in the darkness and catching my eye.

I narrowed my eyes, trying to identify the faint light. I realized its identity easily.

It was an eye.

A person's slightly open eye looked down at me from right in front of my face.

"Shit, you scared me!!!" I swore, startled, and shot up.

The eye in front of my nose moved away in response. The terrifying sight of an eyeball floating in mid-air sent shivers down my spine.

"Sh-Shit."

*I thought the Demonic Cult was only full of madmen, but are there ghosts here too?*

Then, the darkness below the ghost's eye split open. A new, aphrodisiac light leaked out. It was a person's teeth.

A familiar voice came from the ghost's mouth. "Are you alright, Junior Brother?"

"Third Senior Brother?"

It was Third Senior Brother Seo Wanpyeong, the mad assassin who suffered from paranoia. He used to visit often in the dead of night, but after I asked him to come during the day, he had not come at night since.

Just then, the two bodyguard martial artists rushed to the entrance and shouted, "What is it, Young Lord!"

"Are you alright!?"

A step behind them, Jin Hayeon also called out, "Young Lord!"

Hearing their voices, I regained my senses and answered as calmly as possible. "Don't worry. Third Senior Brother came to visit, and we were just having a conversation."

I answered that way, thinking of myself as a hostage. If I were to ask for help here, Third Senior Brother would slice my throat faster than they could burst into the room.

However, as if unaware of my thoughts, Gwan Muyeol shouted accusingly from outside the door. "Third Young Master. Sneaking in like this at such a late hour is an act of disrespect towards us!"

*Why are these so-called bodyguards provoking an assassin in the middle of the night?*

As cold sweat trickled down my back, Third Senior Brother replied gloomily, "I just came because I suddenly had something to ask Youngest. I'll be more careful next time."

At Third Senior Brother's apology, the bodyguards and Jin Hayeon soon withdrew.

I broke the silence that formed once we were alone and asked cautiously, "But what brings you here at this late hour?"

The words 'Why are you here again, you crazy bastard' rose to my throat, but I caught myself before I said them. I had already shouted 'Shit', but since I blurted it out in Korean without thinking, I figured I could just barely get away with it.

"Because of what happened during the day."

"During the day, you say?"

I asked back, puzzled. The Senior Brother in the darkness nodded and replied, "During the day, I just thought it was a compliment and felt shy, but now that it's night and I've thought it over, I feel like everyone was making fun of me for being embarrassed. I wonder if they were all inwardly sneering, ‘Is he proud of something so trivial?’"

"……"

They say people get more emotional at night. It seemed Third Senior Brother's paranoia became more abundant.

*He has... improved, hasn't he?*

The man who used to be gloomy all day was now only gloomy at night, so it was an improvement. Come to think of it, his stealth skills also seemed to have improved compared to the first time Senior Brother visited at night.

Back then, I could vaguely see his form, even if only thanks to the moonlight, but now, even with the moonlight shining, I could barely see his eyes, and even that was only possible because Senior Brother had opened his eyes right in front of my face.

While I was lost in thought, Senior Brother brought up an unexpected topic. "More importantly, are you alright, Junior Brother?"

"???"

"Actually, you were sleeping so soundly, Junior Brother, that I was going to wait until you woke up, but you were whimpering like you were having a nightmare. So, I was watching you up close, debating whether to wake you, when you woke up on your own."

"Ah..."

*So that's why his face was so close, and here I was, thinking he was contemplating 'how to kill me'.*

"Ahem. I did have a nightmare, but it's not a big problem."

"That is truly a relief."

"Yes, and you don't need to worry either, Senior Brother. Myself included, everyone said those things because we genuinely wanted to congratulate you."

"Hmm. If it's coming from you, Junior Brother, who is sharp enough to read even Master's inner thoughts, then it must be true."

As if he had come all this way just to hear those words, Senior Brother nodded and began to withdraw.

However, just before leaving, he suddenly turned his head as if he remembered something. "By the way, what was that you shouted at Second Senior Brother when you woke up just now? Sipa? Ssi-bal? I've never heard that word before."

"……"

Perhaps because I sensed my life was in danger, my mind started racing furiously.

"Th-that was an exclamation that was popular only in my hometown. I-it's a type of dialect. Yes. It means something like, 'Oh my!' or 'What a surprise!' Yes, that's right. Ha, hahaha."

"Oho. An exclamation. How interesting. Sipal. The sound of it really sticks to the tongue."

Saying that, Third Senior Brother kept muttering 'Sipal' to himself, like a foreigner practicing Korean for the first time.

Feeling uneasy just letting this be, I quickly started talking.

"Ah! And come to think of it, Eldest Senior Brother and the others probably wouldn't, but how about you try talking with Guyang Mun?"

"You mean that energetic fellow from earlier today?"

"That's right. Didn't he say he wanted to experience your Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art, Senior Brother? Whether that guy was mocking Second Senior Brother or being sincere, you'd get a good measure of it if you sparred with him, wouldn't you? Ha. Hahaha."

"Oho. That is also a fine idea."

Senior Brother, who seemed to like the idea, nodded and opened the door to leave.

*...Yangmun, I'm sorry.*

The romantically challenged man might just evolve into a virgin ghost.

The next day, fortunately or unfortunately, neither Third Senior Brother nor Guyang Mun came to the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Furthermore, perhaps because he had only come out of curiosity yesterday, Sixth Senior Brother did not visit either.

Thanks to the absence of those three, the Wind Cliff Pavilion was much quieter, but a part of me felt incredibly uneasy.

*Don't tell me one of them actually died?*

Ninety-nine times out of a hundred, it would have been Guyang Mun who died, but then I could not understand why Third Senior Brother had not come.

*Is he disposing of the body...*

I wanted to sink into contemplation, but I could not.

"Hahaha! So as I was saying!"

"Liege. Something like that happened. Hahaha."

Because Eldest Senior Brother and Hang Geon were chattering nonstop, I had no time to get lost in idle thoughts. The topic of conversation was the school-related duties they had taken on because of me.

The only strange thing was why they came to visit almost every day to report to me like this. I could understand Hang Geon, but I was not Eldest Senior Brother's superior. It seemed to be for the reason he mentioned last time, that he wanted to be acknowledged by me and become my Liege.

*...This doesn't seem right.*

Not only did his method feel wrong, but I had no intention of serving Eldest Senior Brother as my Liege. No, once I entered the Demonic Academy, I planned to just slack off and live comfortably. In other words, I had no intention of serving anyone as my Liege.

"Hahaha. That was truly an excellent choice, Eldest Senior Brother."

Of course, I did not know what would happen if I revealed my inner thoughts to the group leader, so I just glossed it over with some flattery.

After Eldest Senior Brother and Hang Geon finished their story, which was somewhere between a report and a boast, they left. With only the two bodyguard martial artists, Seon'ah, and Jin Hayeon remaining, I casually posed a question to Jin Hayeon.

"By the way, didn't you say Eldest Senior Brother also entered the Demonic Academy as the valedictorian?"

If a man who had an inferiority complex about being stupid could get in as the valedictorian, I could not help but doubt the standards of the Demonic Academy. I even wondered if I really needed to study and train this hard.

"The First Young Lord did harbor an inferiority complex about his wisdom, but that doesn't mean he was that lacking. He was simply lacking for a disciple of the Supreme Lord."

*So he has an average level of intelligence.*

"Also, the Demonic Academy's entrance exam has an interview and a duel. From what I've heard, the First Young Lord barely passed the interview, but his duel results were so overwhelmingly superior that he became the valedictorian in the overall evaluation."

I nodded at her explanation, thinking it made sense.

*I don't know about the interview, but is there no maximum score for the duel?*

It seemed the stronger you were, the more extra points you got.

*Right, Eldest Senior Brother reached the Ultimate Demon Realm when he was just over twenty, and in his twenties, he massacred his way through the Central Plains as if it were his own living room, even earning the alias the Crazy Demon.*

And now, at only his early forties, he had achieved complete mastery of the Explosive Mara Sword Technique, so his talent for martial arts was on an overwhelming level.

Just as I was digging into Eldest Senior Brother's admissions scandal, the shout of Jang Hoe, who was guarding the entrance of the Wind Cliff Pavilion, rang out.

"Behold the Heavenly Demon! revered of all demons!"

It meant it was time for Master to arrive.

Naturally, Seon'ah, who had been sticking close to my side, got up and headed toward the entrance. It was time for her to leave.

"I greet the Lord of all demons."

"Hohoho. Go on safely now."

Master and Seon'ah exchanged greetings as if changing shifts. Master entered the Wind Cliff Pavilion and took out a small box from his sleeve.

"This is the Explosive Demonic Pill I promised you last time."

"!!!"

The miracle herbs that constantly appear in martial arts novels, but which I had only ever heard of and never seen in person. However, rather than being simply overjoyed, I approached it cautiously.

"Master. Last time, didn't you say my skills were still lacking and that it was too early for me to consume the Explosive Demonic Pill?"

"Hohoho. You've built a decent foundation now, haven't you? Of course, your qi center is still small, so it'll be difficult for it to accept the Explosive Demonic Pill. However, I thought it wouldn't be good to keep postponing your promised reward, so I'll personally help you absorb the miracle herbs with my qi guidance."

At Master's explanation, I prostrated myself on the ground.

"Your grace is immeasurable!"

"Hahahaha. Excessive courtesy isn't always a good thing, so rise. This old man isn't personally providing qi guidance simply because I cherish you. You presented a good opinion regarding the school, and this is being done as a reward, so don't mind it."

Master rambled on, explaining the justification, but it was not particularly important. The important thing was that I was about to consume miracle herbs.

What are miracle herbs!

If you were to compare martial arts to modern sports, they were a type of doping.

The effects of the medicine!

Of course, the effects of the medicine were not unconditionally good. Depending on the discipline or a person's constitution, there were medicines that were not suitable. I had heard somewhere that there were 'designers' who adjusted the type and dosage of medicine to best suit a person.

In that sense, absorbing miracle herbs with Master's direct help was truly a miraculous encounter. After all, he was the top expert in this field.

"Take the Explosive Demonic Pill and sit in a cross-legged position."

Taking the small pill from the medicine box Master held out, I sat down cross-legged.

*Fufufu. Once I absorb this, my acceptance into the Demonic Academy is guaranteed.*

According to the information I heard from Jin Hayeon a moment ago, the duel carried more weight than the interview. As long as my martial arts realm was high, I just needed to avoid failing the interview, to put it bluntly.

"After you swallow the Explosive Demonic Pill, don't open your mouth under any circumstances, and you must not faint. Don't lose track of the flow of true qi that this old man will guide."

Following Master's warning and instructions, I swallowed the Explosive Demonic Pill in my hand, and Master's palm touched my back. The small pill I swallowed began to melt, pouring out a massive amount of energy, and an equally powerful energy from Master's palm on my back penetrated my body.

I could not afford to die in vain while consuming miracle herbs, so I focused my mind.

Meanwhile, a feeling arose in a corner of my mind.

A feeling that I had forgotten and overlooked something important.
