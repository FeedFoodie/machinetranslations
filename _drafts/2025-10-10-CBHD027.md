At first, I thought she avoided me because I was resting at the clinic. Then Master worked me to the bone, so I had no time to worry about it, but more than half a month passed, and she still hadn't visited.

*Is the Hyeokryeon Clan stopping her from coming?*

They already disliked her associating with me. It was possible they were using this incident as an excuse to block her.

*Tsk. What did the kid do to deserve this?*

Something felt wrong. Eldest Senior Brother's mental illness was the problem. What had that child done to be locked up at home for over half a month? The only issue was that getting too involved in another family's affairs was a bad idea.

"......"

After a moment of contemplation, I made a decision.

"I'll skip my review today. I have a place to visit, so please prepare a bath for me."

I decided to visit the Hyeokryeon Clan myself. I didn't plan to cause a scene or demand they send her back to the Wind Cliff Pavilion. I just wanted to visit as a guest, see the kid's face, and leave. That wouldn't be excessive interference.

Once ready, I headed to the Hyeokryeon Clan with Jin Hayeon and Guyang Mun. As I walked, Demonic Cult martial artists and workers who recognized me offered their greetings. After walking for about a quarter of an hour, we finally reached the Hyeokryeon Clan's entrance.

It felt strange. I had taken Seon'ah's visits for granted, so this was my first time visiting the Hyeokryeon Clan.

*So that kid walked this path every day to see me.*

While I was lost in thought, a Hyeokryeon Clan gatekeeper recognized me and offered a greeting.

"Greetings, Eighth Young Master. I'll inform those inside. Could you please wait a moment?"

"Very well."

The gatekeeper's tone was polite but utterly devoid of warmth. After a short wait, the gate opened and an old man emerged to greet us.

"I am Chief Steward Hyeokryeon Hak of the Hyeokryeon Clan. I'll escort you inside."

We followed the Chief Steward, a member of a collateral branch of the Hyeokryeon Clan, toward a pavilion inside the clan grounds.

"My lady. Young Lord Ilmok has come to visit."

"Let him enter."

From the Chief Steward's words and the voice within, I knew who was inside. Seon'ah's mother, Lady Cheonghwa, was waiting for me. The Chief Steward's demeanor suggested his role was done. Jin Hayeon and Guyang Mun also stood beside the Chief Steward, indicating they would wait at the door.

I felt like a public official about to meet a difficult complainant, but I strode confidently into the pavilion. After all, I was no longer a public official.

"It's been a while, Eighth Young Master."

"It has, Lady Cheonghwa."

As expected, Lady Cheonghwa greeted me with a polite but chilly tone. We exchanged simple, courteous nods.

"Please, sit."

I sat in the indicated chair. Lady Cheonghwa lifted a prepared teapot and poured tea into my cup. After pouring her own, she immediately asked a question.

"So, what brings you to our Hyeokryeon Clan in person?"

She got straight to the point before I could even drink my tea, so I didn't beat around the bush either.

"I haven't seen Seon'ah for a few days, so I came to see her myself."

"...You speak as if Seon'ah is some subordinate of yours, Young Lord."

"If Seon'ah is avoiding me of her own free will, I won't argue about it."

Lady Cheonghwa's gaze turned venomous at my response.

"Are you implying that our family is suppressing Seon'ah?"

"Weren't you?"

Lady Cheonghwa and I stared at each other for a while. Perhaps feeling the act was beneath a martial artist, Lady Cheonghwa lifted her steaming teacup, took a sip, and spoke.

"That may have been true before, but it's not now."

"Then are you saying Seon'ah isn't coming of her own free will?"

"That's correct."

I narrowed my eyes slightly at her confident reply. Honestly, I didn't believe her. Why would that child suddenly avoid me? The kid who visited almost every day, calling out ‘Oppa! Oppa!’. However, arguing ‘Don't lie to me!’ here would just be picking a fight.

"In that case, may I speak with Seon'ah for a moment?"

"...Young Lord Ilmok. Even if you're a disciple of the Supreme Lord, don't you think you're being too rude to our Hyeokryeon Clan?"

"I'm not saying I'll drag her away by force. I just want to exchange greetings as a guest and then leave."

I replied, then lifted the teacup and took a small sip.

*Sip. How did she drink this?*

The tea was still ridiculously hot. I couldn't show it during our battle of wills, so I forced a blank expression and met Lady Cheonghwa's gaze. We continued our pointless battle of wills for another moment.

A sudden voice put an end to it.

"Let him meet my granddaughter."

"??"

I heard a voice from a direction where I hadn't sensed a presence. I turned my head slightly. A man, somewhere between middle and old age, opened a different door and entered.

Lady Cheonghwa, who had been locked in a battle of wills with me, hurriedly stood to show her respects. The man stopped her with a gesture.

"That's enough. Do as Young Lord Ilmok wishes."

"But, Patriarch..."

Lady Cheonghwa's title for him made me realize who he was. He was the Patriarch of the Hyeokryeon Clan and her father-in-law. Jin Hayeon had told me he was Hyeokryeon Cheongang, one of the most renowned drummers in the Demonic Cult. Which meant he was someone I couldn't treat carelessly, even as a disciple of the Heavenly Demon.

"Greetings to the Patriarch of the Hyeokryeon Clan."

"This is our first time meeting in person. I've heard a lot about you from various sources, including the Supreme Lord."

I was curious what on earth he had heard, but Hyeokryeon Cheongang spoke before I could ask.

"Go and meet with Seon'ah. I'll have the Chief Steward guide you."

Before I could even reply, Hyeokryeon Cheongang spoke toward the door I had entered.

"Chief Steward. Guide the Eighth Young Master to Seon'ah."

He didn't shout, but I could feel his voice, imbued with inner arts, travel a great distance.

"You may go now."

It was a clear dismissal, so I willingly left the room. My purpose was to meet Seon'ah anyway, and I wanted to avoid a battle of wills with that old man. I could manage against someone like Lady Cheonghwa, but a battle of wills with that old man would be suicidal.

{sep}

A brief silence fell inside the pavilion after Ilmok left. Hyeokryeon Cheongang was the one who broke it.

"Don't mind it. We must keep our promise to the Supreme Lord, mustn't we?"

"...I understand, Patriarch."

"The promise with the Supreme Lord lasts until the Eighth Young Master enters the Demonic Academy. If a problem arises with the child before or even after he enters, we'll simply punish her then, according to that promise."

Hyeokryeon Cheongang said this, then turned his gaze toward the pavilion where his precious granddaughter stayed. It was the same pavilion where Ilmok was headed.

{sep}

"We are here, Young Lord."

The Chief Steward had guided me to Seon'ah's quarters. I approached the door and knocked lightly. Even a child's privacy should be respected.

"Seon'ah."

She seemed to recognize my voice, as I sensed movement from inside.

"D-d-don't come in."

"!!!"

*Could it be? Is Seon'ah really avoiding me?*

*I-is this the kind of pain daddies with teenage daughters feel?*

It was like having a daughter who used to follow you around saying, ‘I'm going to marry Daddy!’ but then distances herself after hitting puberty, yelling, ‘Ugh, seriously! I told you not to come in my room!’

It felt like my long-healed internal injuries were flaring up again. I forced myself to even my breathing.

*You never know. Maybe she's avoiding me because Lady Cheonghwa said something strange to her!*

I barely endured the internal damage and spoke again.

"Seon'ah. Can you at least tell me why you're avoiding Oppa?"

"B-because Oppa a-almost died because of me."

"Ah..."

I thought I vaguely understood why Seon'ah was avoiding me.

"Don't worry. It wasn't your fault, Seon'ah. It was Eldest Senior Brother's men..."

I almost said 'mental illness', but I realized people were listening and quickly corrected myself.

"...tal state. He was going through a hard time. It wasn't because of you."

"B-but I'm still scared. I'm scared that Oppa will get hurt or die because of me... S-so it's better if we just don't see each other."

I belatedly realized this was subtly different from what I had thought. I assumed she was avoiding me because she was afraid I would hate her for almost getting me killed, but now I saw she was afraid of 'me being in danger' because of her.

*...They did say a side effect of the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique was excessive obsession.*

It seemed the thought of a cherished person getting hurt or dying was more terrifying to her than being hated by them, but understanding the difference didn't give me a solution. After all, I was a public official, not a psychiatrist. So, I could only act based on my own experience.

"I'm coming in for a second."

I opened the door and pushed into her fortress before Seon'ah could answer. The room was dark. Seon'ah's hair was greasy and her skin was rough. She looked like she hadn't eaten or washed for days while suffering from nightmares. She looked like a wreck, far too haggard for her age.

I carefully approached the little girl. As I approached, Seon'ah averted her gaze like a wounded cat trapped in a dead-end alley.

"Hmm. You said it's better not to see me because you don't want to see me get hurt."

"......"

"But for me, not being able to see you is more painful."

"!!"

Seon'ah, who had been avoiding my gaze, flinched. I continued speaking.

"Eldest Senior Brother's rampage was like a natural disaster. In life, accidents sometimes happen when it's no one's fault."

No one is a perfect match for another. Even family members who've lived together for decades don't match perfectly. Everyone hurts someone with words or accidentally injures them while living with friends, lovers, or family. Despite that, we live on, making friends, family, and lovers. I was the same.

"You can't avoid people just because you're scared of that. No, you have to be with people precisely because such things can happen. You can't endure it alone."

People can't live alone. In my past life in Korea, there were people called 'shut-ins' who lived confined to their rooms, but even they weren't truly alone. Their bodies were cooped up, but they were communicating with someone, somewhere, through games or internet communities.

"This time, you rushed at the terrifying Eldest Senior Brother to save me, didn't you? I survived because you bought me time. If you avoid me and an accident happens, and you later hear that I died, would you be okay with that, Seon'ah?"

"N-no!"

The little girl finally cried out as if having a fit and looked at me. I smiled gently and stroked the wounded kitten's greasy hair.

"So, can you protect me next time something like this happens? If you're in danger, I'll protect you."

The little girl hesitated for a moment, then hunched her shoulders and nodded.

{sep}

The next afternoon, Seon'ah came to the Wind Cliff Pavilion at the usual time. Unlike yesterday, her hair and skin were clean.

"Hahahaha! A pleasure to meet you! I'm Guyang Mun, temporarily serving the Young Lord in Hayeon's place."

Seon'ah, meeting Guyang Mun for the first time, hid behind my legs at his hearty greeting and peeked out at him.

"Haha. You're a shy Young Lady."

He was a truly sociable guy, except when dealing with Jin Hayeon. As Guyang Mun kept trying to talk to Seon'ah, Jin Hayeon remarked icily.

"You seem comfortable serving the Young Lord. As if you think this is your break time."

"H-hmph! I have time because I've already prepared everything! Do you think doing things as rigidly as you do makes everything better?"

The two youths began their lovers' quarrel again. I leisurely watched the scene with Seon'ah. To think I could watch a youth drama in real time without the internet or a TV.

"Here. Ahh~"

I felt like I was watching a drama with my niece. I fed Seon'ah a snack and watched the two youths' quarrel for a while longer. Seon'ah watched with me, then tilted her head and spoke.

"But Oppa. Does that man like Hayeon Unnie?"

"......"

"......"

The innocent child's single remark made a chilly silence descend upon the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"W-w-what are you talking about!! W-w-who likes who!"

Guyang Mun shouted as if having a seizure, then furtively glanced at Jin Hayeon. He met Jin Hayeon's icy gaze, which looked at him like an insect. Guyang Mun suddenly dashed toward the Wind Cliff Pavilion's entrance.

"I-I just remembered something urgent, so I'll be leaving for today!!"

"......"

"......"

I looked around the once again silent Wind Cliff Pavilion, sighed lightly, and muttered.

"Now who's going to serve me..."
