"Now, I’ll teach you the first technique of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. Ilmok, what do you think is the most important technique for a swordsman?"

"…Wouldn’t it be a terrifying ultimate technique that can kill an opponent in a single strike?"

Master shook his head at my answer.

"For a swordsman, the most important technique is drawing the sword. In a surprise attack or an unexpected fight, if you can’t draw your sword, you’ll be defeated in vain, won’t you?"

"Come to think of it, that really seems to be the case."

I thought about the wuxia stories I had seen in novels and comics. The characters often had their swords drawn before a fight started, but in a real fight, an opponent would likely try to prevent you from drawing your sword.

Enemies who would wait for you to prepare your killing intent in a real battle would be extremely rare.

"Of course, learning fist techniques, palm techniques, or grappling arts is a basic skill for a swordsman, in case you can’t draw your sword. Even if you have drawn your sword, it might be more convenient to use grappling or palm techniques when an opponent closes in, but think about it. If someone’s mastery of fist or palm techniques is higher, who would call them a swordsman? They’d be called a pugilist."

"Your words are truly wise, Master."

I chimed in with the sycophantic spirit I had cultivated over many years of corporate life.

Master smiled slightly and concluded his explanation.

"In that sense, the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword is quite an excellent sword art. Its first technique, the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw, is a movement linked directly to drawing the sword."

Master finished his explanation. He stepped back with his right foot, bent both knees into a resilient stance, and simultaneously drew the sword at his side, aiming it forward.

"You take one step back and draw your sword to aim at the enemy. As you draw, you match the path of the opponent’s weapon and block their attack. That is the core of the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw."

It seemed to be a technique for transitioning to the offensive. You block the enemy’s attack as you draw the sword, then immediately take a resilient stance and aim at the enemy.

But no matter how grand its meaning was, the visible result was quite anticlimactic.

He just took one step back with his right foot while drawing the sword to aim at the enemy. That was all.

Master looked at me as if telling me to try it. I gripped the scabbard with my left hand and the sword hilt with my right, and tried to imitate the movement.

After trying it once, I realized it was not as easy as it looked.

The most awkward part was drawing the sword itself.

The blade was about 90 centimeters long, so it was bound to scrape against the scabbard mid-draw. Because of that, instead of coming out smoothly like Master’s, it felt like it was clattering out.

Seeing me awkwardly draw the sword, Master smiled gently.

"Do you now understand why drawing the sword is the most important thing? If you draw your sword like that, you’ll die without even getting to fight."

I did not know why he was spouting such malicious words with a smile.

However, contrary to his malicious words, Master kindly offered his teachings.

"When drawing the sword, the hand gripping it isn’t the only important thing. You must also twist the angle of the scabbard with your other hand, according to the angle of the emerging sword, and you need to twist your shoulders and body to match."

He did not just use words. He also corrected my posture by touching my body with his own hands.

After drawing the sword a few times under Master’s guidance, I slowly started to get the hang of it.

SHIING!

I drew the sword cleanly in one go without any clattering and took my stance.

Master nodded his head in satisfaction.

"It seems you have now taken the first step. Then, let’s add some variations. The enemy’s attacks won’t always come from the same direction. You must be able to draw your sword and defend in various situations."

Saying so, Master showed various demonstrations. It was the same simple act of drawing a sword, but the sword path he produced was different each time.

Sometimes he drew the sword to block an attack to the head, and sometimes he blocked one to the legs.

After that, he also rotated his body to the right and took a step back, drawing his sword to block an attack to his side.

"Now, this old man will swing a sword at you slowly, so try to draw your sword and block it."

As soon as he finished speaking, Master slowly extended a real sword toward me.

A sword radiating a chilling killing intent flew toward me. Even though its speed was slow, the sight made cold sweat run down my back.

"......"

Still, perhaps because the speed was slow, I managed to draw my sword and block it.

When I sheathed my sword again, Master thrusted his sword from a different angle.

SHIING.

"......"

It was a strange sensation.

Even though I had not practiced any of the other stances, I was naturally drawing my sword and blocking.

SHIING.

"......"

I understood the concept of twisting my shoulders and torso and using the hand holding the scabbard. Whenever I thought, *I should move like this,* my body would naturally move according to that thought and draw the sword.

"hohoho. As expected, this old man’s eyes were not mistaken."

Master burst into laughter, pleased with my talent.

"Then, I shall raise the difficulty."

SWOOOOSH!

He suddenly increased the speed of his sword swing.

*Gasp!*

A scream almost escaped my lips, but my body moved on its own.

SHIING.

"......"

SHIING.

"......"

After successfully blocking a couple of times, my startled heart calmed down. Master seemed to confirm that my gaze had become composed, and he increased the speed again.

*Phew.*

This time, however, I was not as surprised as before.

SHIING.

"......"

Like that, I blocked the sword coming from various angles at gradually increasing speeds dozens of times.

SWOOOOOSH.

SHIING.

"......"

"hohoho. This should be enough. It seems you have sufficiently learned the basics of the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw."

Master stepped back with a satisfied laugh.

"Phew."

At the same time, the tension left me, and I sighed without realizing it.

DRIP.

The sound of a sweat drop rolling down my head and hitting the floor entered my ears.

Before I knew it, sweat was pouring down like a waterfall. I had not felt it at all while my concentration was at its peak.

I stared at the hand holding the sword with half-dazed eyes.

*Is this really my body?*

The attacks Master unleashed at the end were at a speed that was truly difficult to follow with my eyes.

To be precise, it was a speed that the eyes of my past body, Seo Jihun, would not have been able to follow.

However, these young eyes could see Master’s sword path perfectly. Of course, considering the god-like movements he showed at the inn, he was probably holding back a lot, but it was definitely an incredible speed.

And yet, I perceived that sword path accurately. The moment I thought I had to block it, my body moved naturally, without a hint of hesitation.

*What is this, some kind of prodigy?*

If he had been born in the modern era, this was the kind of talent that could have aimed for an Olympic gold medal in fencing.

But here, he was just a disciple of a heretic Cult Leader.

{sep}

I do not know how long I practiced the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw over and over again.

As sweat dripped from me, Master’s voice entered my ears.

"Hmm. Come to think of it, so much time has passed. There’s about half an hour left, so for the remaining time, I shall teach you the inner arts method."

Saying so, Master approached me, ordered me to sit in the full-lotus position, and then adjusted my posture.

"The martial arts learned by the hypocrites of the Central Plains have their inner arts, sword arts, and movement arts all divided, taking a long time to learn. The martial arts of our Divine Cult, however, are mostly bundled into one. You could say it is thanks to the prophets who paved the way for us, intending to allow us to grow strong in a short time to punish the wicked. hohoho."

Master spoke with a tone full of pride, but my mind applied a suitable filter to grasp the true meaning.

*The martial arts of the orthodox sects have no side effects and use pure inner arts, which is why they can use different martial arts separately. On the other hand, demonic art is dangerous even if you learn just one, so isn’t it more likely they bundled similar ones together as much as possible?*

Sword arts, inner arts, movement arts, and footwork, and even the minimum fist techniques or grappling arts to supplement the sword arts.

If one were to learn at least five or six martial arts as demonic art, like the orthodox sects, it would mean contracting five or six different mental illnesses.

*…Perhaps the Demonic Cult of old was really like that.*

You could say the grace of the prophets Master mentioned was very deep. If not for them, I would have had to choose between five or six mental illnesses.

While I was sighing in relief inwardly at that thought, Master continued.

"From now on, I will tell you the acupoints through which inner arts flow according to the inner arts method of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword."

Master continued his explanation, touching various points on my body with his index finger.

"For now, just memorize the acupoints this old man has shown you. You can learn the remaining acupoints of the human body from Hayeon later."

As soon as Master finished speaking, Jin Hayeon bowed from the waist as if to say, ‘Leave it to me.’

After finishing his explanation, Master suddenly placed his palm on my back.

"Concentrate from now on. Normally, it’s important to get used to it by circulating the inner arts yourself, but since you started learning martial arts late, Ilmok, this old man will directly help your qi circulation."

As soon as he finished those words, something began to enter me through Master’s hand on my back.

It was truly a strange moment, a sensation I was feeling for the first time in my life.

That energy entered my body, settled near its center, and stopped moving.

"This place is the qi center. Now, from this moment, take a deep breath through your nose. Feel like you’re pulling it deep into your lungs."

Even while sending inner arts to my qi center, Master spoke very naturally.

Following Master’s instruction, I took a deep breath through my nose. The energy from Master that was settled in my qi center moved.

That energy approached the air I inhaled, pulled something out, and returned to the qi center with it.

Then, starting from the qi center, it began to circulate through my body.

"This is the Qihai acupoint."

Every time Master’s energy and the energy I inhaled reached a specific acupoint, Master would tell me its name.

Feeling the qi directly touch the points with my body like this, I was able to grasp their exact locations much more accurately than when he just pointed them out.

I do not know how long I focused on Master’s explanations and the movement of qi.

By the time I had repeated deep breaths dozens of times and succeeded in memorizing the entire flow of qi and all the acupoints, the energy from Master that had been in my qi center returned to his hand. Then Master withdrew his hand from my back.

However, I was now somewhat accustomed to ‘qi (氣)’ and had memorized the entire path of the inner arts method, so I was able to circulate it on my own.

"Haaaaa."

"Fuuu."

I circulated the inner arts method on my own for a while.

When a bean-sized bit of energy had completely settled in my qi center, where I had felt nothing before, I calmly exhaled and opened my eyes.

I glanced to the side and saw that the sand in the hourglass that marked the remaining time had all run out.

As I opened my eyes, Master smiled warmly and offered his congratulations.

"hohoho. Congratulations on your initiation into the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword."

"Thank you, Master."

"hohoho. Now that you have entered the Demonic Initiate Realm, you must strive and strive again until you reach the Transcendent Demon Realm."

"I will do so."

That was a given. I had no intention of living my whole life as a lunatic.

Master stroked his beard while watching my determined expression with satisfaction. Then he uttered some strange words.

"In that sense, your will in choosing the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword is truly admirable."

"???"

"The Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword is a martial art that slowly breaks down the opponent by reacting to their movements in any and all situations. Therefore, it is a martial art that requires one to strive for perfection to handle any situation. In a way, you could say that such a side effect arose because of the nature of the martial arts."

"Then, is the rate of achievement also faster than other martial arts?"

"As I said before, because you must strive for perfection, the rate of achievement is not particularly faster compared to other demonic arts, even with the help of the side effect."

Up to this point, I just felt a little disappointed. It meant that even with the help (?) of the side effect, the achievement rate was not explosively fast.

However, this was something I had braced myself for the moment I started learning demonic art, so it did not matter.

Until his next words came out.

"However, no matter how perfectly you prepare, you will eventually reach a limit with a rigid method. For the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword to surpass the Ultimate Demon Realm and reach the Transcendent Demon Realm, a change that breaks away from perfection is necessary. Because of the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, it is truly difficult to break away from the perfection one has created for oneself."

"……"

*So what he’s saying is, it’s damn hard to reach the Transcendent Demon Realm, right?*

"And yet, you have chosen that difficult path, so as your master, this old man is truly proud."

"……"

I looked at Master and Jin Hayeon, who were gazing at me with pleasure.

*You should have told me that before you started teaching, you crazy heretics!!*

It was a scream I could not possibly let out.
