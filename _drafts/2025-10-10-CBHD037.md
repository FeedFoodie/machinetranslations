Master's immense energy entered through my back and advanced toward my esophagus. From there, it gently enveloped the savage, spreading energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill. So far, everything seemed normal. The progression was not strange in any way.

However, I did not know then that this was only the beginning.

The heavy, immense energy wrapped around the large, savage energy. The two energies coexisted without mixing, growing even larger together. That massive energy then began to burrow into my acupoints. It pushed toward my weak and tiny acupoints, which could barely handle five years of inner arts.

"!!!"

Unexpected, excruciating pain made my eyes fly open. I almost screamed.

Even amid the filthy pain, a thought surfaced in my mind.

*So this is why he warned me not to pass out or open my mouth!*

Of course, Master was not just ignorantly shoving that massive energy into my acupoints. Like pulling a thread from a giant ball of yarn, Master used his own energy to guide the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy, stretching it out long as it moved.

*S-Something that big won't fit!!*

Even so, the energy was too immense for my acupoints to accept.

"Hup!!!"

I clenched my teeth hard enough to break them, enduring the agonizing pain. I could not check without a mirror, but my face must have been horribly contorted. All the while, guided by Master's energy, the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill rampaged through my acupoints, violating them as it went.

[Do not lose track of the flow of true qi that this old man is guiding.]

As I was about to lose my mind, I recalled Master's final warning and forced myself to concentrate on that energy. Funnily enough, once I focused my mind elsewhere, the pain began to feel a little less intense.

On top of that.

THUD!

I could somewhat grasp what was happening inside me.

*So my acupoints weren't narrow to begin with.*

The energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill was not tearing my acupoints apart. It was pushing out the impurities lodged throughout my acupoints as it coursed through them. In other words, the pain I felt was from those impurities being forcibly ripped away from my blocked acupoints.

Perhaps because the impurities had been torn away, a refreshing sensation began to spread from the areas where I had felt pain.

It was the worst.

*Guuh!!*

I still felt pain in the foremost acupoints where the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill was breaking through, but a refreshing pleasure rose from the areas it had already passed. The mix of pain and pleasure felt like I was about to cross a line that should not be crossed.

How long did I endure, clenching my teeth and fighting myself?

*I-It's over...*

The energy, which had coursed according to the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword's heart technique, passed the final acupoint and rushed into my qi center. All I had to do now was absorb it into my qi center, and it would be over.

That thought was fleeting.

*!?*

For some reason, the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill headed toward my acupoints again instead of returning to the qi center. It was as if it was going for another tour.

The thought 'I'm screwed' dominated my mind, but perhaps because the first opening had pushed out most of the impurities, the second round was not particularly painful.

Just as it was about to finish the second lap and return to the qi center.

*!?*

That crazy old man started directing the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill back to my acupoints again.

*Why again?*

No sooner had I harbored that question than the crazy old man personally showed me the reason.

*Why there!?*

The energies coursing through my acupoints, guided by Master, split into dozens of tiny fragments and spread out. They left the major meridians and headed for the minor meridians throughout my body.

A thought occurred to me.

*If you could have split it into smaller pieces, why didn't you just do that from the start, you crazy old man!!*

Now I saw it was not that he could not split it, but that he just did not. Of course, having watched the whole process, I knew the reason. It must have been to maintain a size large enough to forcefully push out the impurities blocking the acupoints in one go.

However, regardless of the reason, the fact that I had suffered terrible pain thanks to that crazy old man remained. This time, the dozens of finely split strands of energy presented me with a new kind of pain.

If the pain of breaking through impurities in the major meridians felt like being poked all over with something blunt, then the current pain was a terrible agony. It was like dozens of ants had gotten inside me and were biting me from within. It reached a point where I could not tell if I was absorbing miracle herbs or being tortured.

Just when I could not bear it any longer and felt like I was about to pass out, I wanted to let go of everything.

"Hoo. You've endured it well."

From behind me, the crazy old man's praise came with a deep breath.

*Endured well, my ass.*

I really thought I was going to die. Honestly, I felt like letting out a stream of curses.

"Hm?"

But once I came to my senses, a sensation I had never felt washed over me. How should I put it? It felt like a lifelong illness had been suddenly cured. My body was so light. I wondered if my physical condition had ever been this good. It was physically impossible, but I felt the illusion that I could actually fly.

"Hohoho. It seems quite fascinating to you."

"Master, is this the power of miracle herbs?"

I thought this must be why martial artists chased miracle herbs with fire in their eyes, but for some reason, Master shook his head.

"Your inner arts haven't increased that much. This is the effect of meridian cleansing and purification, rather than from absorbing miracle herbs."

"!?"

Just as Master said, when I focused on my qi center, my inner arts had not increased by much. At most, it was about ten years' worth. Since my original qi center could hold about five years' worth, it had tripled, which was a lot, but since he said the Explosive Demonic Pill contained half a gapja of energy, only about a third of it had been added to my qi center.

More important than that was the phrase meridian cleansing and purification.

Meridian cleansing and purification. I knew roughly what it was from occasionally reading wuxia novels on my commute. It was a term that often appeared in those novels, a Great Ritual performed on newborns by prestigious families or great sects. It was a major surgery where an elder used inner arts to treat a newborn's bones and acupoints, transforming them into a constitution optimized for learning martial arts.

"B-But, isn't meridian cleansing and purification only possible for young children?"

"Hohoho. It becomes more difficult with age, but it is not a Great Ritual that is only possible for young children."

Master began his detailed explanation.

"I did not intend to perform meridian cleansing and purification from the beginning. Even for this old man, attempting it on a fifteen-year-old boy is a gamble. Therefore, I intended to simply remove the impurities from your major meridians and finish."

"Then why…?"

"First, there were fewer impurities in your body, Ilmok, than this old man expected. It seems you didn't eat a rich diet when you were young. On top of that, you endured so well that we preserved more of the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy than expected. Conversely, your qi center was too small to contain that great energy, so I had no choice but to remove the impurities from your minor meridians as well."

I came from a poor family and later became an orphan, so I never ate well, and yet, that had turned out to be a blessing in disguise.

"Your body is now in a very clean state, just like a child's after receiving meridian cleansing and purification. Your immediate inner arts haven't increased much, but you will be able to grow much faster than others."

However, as I listened, a question came to mind.

"But, is it possible to perform meridian cleansing and purification with just twenty years' worth of inner arts?"

If it were possible with just that much, I thought most martial families would perform it on all their children.

At my question, Master shook his head.

"Hohoho. How could that be? To preserve as much of the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy as possible, this old man primarily used his own inner arts."

"!!!"

Thinking back, Master's inner arts had always enveloped the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill while clearing my acupoints. In other words, the energy that directly contacted the impurities was mostly Master's inner arts, not the pill's. For only a third of the pill's energy to remain meant Master must have used at least half a gapja of his own inner arts.

"Thank you, Master!"

I completely forgot the memory of Master torturing me moments ago and bowed deeply to him.

"Hohoho. Think nothing of it. The fact that you started martial arts late was the only regrettable point, and now that disadvantage has disappeared."

Despite having lost more than half a gapja of his inner arts, which are more precious than life to a martial artist, Master wore a satisfied expression. He seemed even more satisfied than me, the one who had actually received the meridian cleansing and purification.

{sep}

After finishing the absorption of the miracle herbs and the meridian cleansing and purification, I changed into a new martial arts uniform that Jin Hayeon had brought and began training. The uniform I had been wearing reeked of a rotten smell, thanks to the impurities expelled from all over my body.

And so, I swung my sword several times in front of the watching Master.

*This is insane.*

I was greatly surprised by the effects of the meridian cleansing and purification. A moment ago, it was just a feeling of my body becoming lighter, but now that I was moving my body and inner arts myself, the difference was like heaven and earth.

Because the acupoints narrowed by impurities were now expanded, my inner arts coursed through them without obstruction. It was like a one-lane road had been expanded into a four-lane highway. Furthermore, since even the minor meridians had been cleared, it was as if all the cars parked in alleyways had been towed away. There was no such thing as traffic congestion.

If there was one problem, it was that my acupoints had become too large compared to my amount of inner arts. They were spacious enough to handle fifteen years' worth of inner arts flowing at once, so the power and speed I could generate in an instant were much stronger and faster, but I could not maintain it for long.

However, that was truly a minor problem.

*Since my acupoints have widened anyway, the amount of energy I can absorb through qi circulation has also increased dramatically.*

In fact, after performing the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword a few times until my inner arts were depleted, I performed qi circulation, and my empty qi center filled up in an instant.

I wondered if the guys who received meridian cleansing and purification and learned martial arts from a young age had been training under these conditions all this time. The young masters from prestigious families probably learn martial arts like this from birth, so the gap would widen absurdly as they got older. Those guys probably become prodigies, get called by some alias like the Five Dragons and Two Phoenixes, and live in splendor.

[I, too, reached this level because I worked hard! You lot are lacking because you're lazy!]

I could not help but think of the arrogant prodigies from prestigious families who would spout such lines.

*As expected, even in the murim, you have to be born with a golden spoon in your mouth.*

Or, like me, you have to win the lottery.

"Fufufu."

Since the Cult Leader of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult became my Master, a lottery ticket this good must be rare.

How long did I train the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, high on the pill's power and the meridian cleansing and purification? Even movements that had previously felt somewhat awkward now connected freely. The power and speed were incomparable to before, so swinging the sword was incredibly enjoyable.

When my inner arts ran out, I would perform qi circulation. When my qi center was full, I would swing the sword again.

*No. I have to swing a little faster here.*

*Not enough! My control of pace is clumsy! I need to slow down a little at the end and transition smoothly to the next movement!*

Every time I swung the sword, I found something lacking. After recovering my inner arts, I would correct it.

"Hohoho. This old man will be on his way now."

"Take care, Master! Thank you so much!"

Master, who had been watching with pure satisfaction, left the Wind Cliff Pavilion as if it was time.

Even at the hour when the sun had hidden behind the mountains and darkness had fallen.

"......"

"......"

*Still not enough!*

Intoxicated by the sword, I relentlessly repeated the cycle of swinging my sword and performing qi circulation, seeking to draw a perfect sword path.

How long had I been lost in my mind, intoxicated by the sword?

"Haa. Haa."

By the time the sweet smell of exhaustion wafted from my mouth, the muscles all over my body were screaming, and the palm of my sword hand began to sting. Belatedly coming to my senses, I realized an important fact I had forgotten.

When you absorb miracle herbs, it is only natural for your martial arts achievements to increase. Thanks to also receiving meridian cleansing and purification, my proficiency in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was rising steeply.

A higher level in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword meant that the side effect had also become more severe. I realized that until now, I had been swinging the sword like a madman, intoxicated by that side effect. Thanks to that, I had achieved tremendous progress in just one day, but that was not the important part.

Naturally, the scene I saw in my dream last night came to mind, along with the thought that perhaps that nightmare had been a premonition.
