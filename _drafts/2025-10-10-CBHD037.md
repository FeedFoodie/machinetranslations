The Master's immense energy entered through my back and advanced toward my esophagus. From my esophagus, the Master's energy gently enveloped the savage, spreading energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill.

So far, nothing seemed wrong. The progression felt normal, but I didn't know then that this was only the beginning.

The heavy, immense energy enveloped the large, savage energy. The two energies coexisted without mixing, growing even larger. Then, that massive energy began to burrow into my acupoints. It targeted my weak, tiny acupoints, which could barely handle five years of inner arts.

"!!!"

My eyes flew open from the unexpected, excruciating pain. I almost screamed without realizing it.

Even through the filthy pain, a thought surfaced in my mind.

*So this is why he warned me not to pass out or open my mouth!*

Of course, the Master wasn't just ignorantly shoving the massive energy into my acupoints. He used his own energy to guide the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy, pulling it like a thread from a ball of yarn and stretching it long as it moved.

*S-Something that big won't fit!!*

Even so, the energy was too immense for my acupoints to accept.

"Hup!!!"

I clenched my teeth hard enough to break them, enduring the agonizing pain. With no mirror, I couldn't check, but I was sure my face was horribly contorted.

All the while, the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy rampaged through my acupoints. It violated them as it moved, guided by the Master's energy.

[Do not lose track of the flow of true qi that this old man is guiding.]

As I was about to lose my mind, I recalled the Master's final warning. I forced myself to concentrate on the energy's flow.

Funnily enough, the pain lessened once I focused my mind. On top of that...

THUD!

I could somewhat grasp what was happening inside me.

*So my acupoints weren't narrow to begin with.*

The Explosive Demonic Pill's energy wasn't tearing my acupoints apart. It was pushing out the impurities lodged inside them as it coursed through. In other words, I was feeling the pain of those impurities being forcibly ripped from my acupoints.

Perhaps because the impurities were torn away, a refreshing sensation soon spread from where I had felt pain.

It was the worst.

*Guuh!!*

I still felt pain where the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy broke through the foremost acupoints, but a refreshing pleasure rose from the areas it had already passed. The mix of pain and pleasure felt like I was about to cross a forbidden line.

I endured for a long time, clenching my teeth and fighting myself.

*I-It's over...*

The energy coursed according to the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword's heart technique. It passed the final acupoint and rushed into my qi center.

*All I have to do now is absorb it, and it'll be over.*

That thought was fleeting.

*!?*

For some reason, the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy didn't return to my qi center. It headed toward my acupoints again, as if for another tour.

The thought *I'm screwed* dominated my mind, but the second round wasn't as painful, perhaps because the first pass had pushed out most of the impurities.

Just as it finished the second lap and was about to return to the qi center...

*!?*

That crazy old man directed the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy back to my acupoints again.

*Why again?*

Before I could even process the question, the crazy old man showed me the reason.

*Why there!?*

Guided by the Master, the energy in my acupoints split into dozens of tiny fragments and spread out. They left the major meridians and headed for the minor meridians throughout my body.

A thought occurred to me.

*If you could've split it into smaller pieces, why didn't you just do that from the start, you crazy old man!!*

I realized he could have split it, he just chose not to. Having watched the process, I knew why. The energy had to be large enough to forcefully push out the impurities in one go.

But regardless of the reason, the fact remained that the crazy old man had made me suffer terrible pain.

This time, the dozens of finely split energy strands presented a new kind of pain. The pain from the major meridians felt like being poked with a blunt object. This new pain, coursing through my minor meridians, was a terrible agony, like dozens of ants biting me from within.

I couldn't tell if I was absorbing miracle herbs or being tortured. I couldn't bear it any longer. I felt like I was about to pass out and wanted to let go of everything.

"Hoo. You've endured it well."

The crazy old man's praise came from behind me, along with a deep breath.

*Endured well, my ass.*

I really thought I was going to die. Honestly, I wanted to let out a stream of curses.

"Hm?"

But as I came to my senses, a sensation I had never felt before washed over me.

How could I describe it? It felt like a lifelong illness had been suddenly cured. My body was so light, I wondered if my condition had ever been this good. It was physically impossible, but I felt the illusion that I could actually fly.

"Hohoho. It seems you find it quite fascinating."

"Master, is this the power of miracle herbs?"

I thought this must be why martial artists chased miracle herbs so desperately, but the Master shook his head.

"Your inner arts haven't increased that much. This isn't from absorbing the miracle herbs, but the effect of meridian cleansing and purification."

"!?"

Just as the Master said, my inner arts hadn't increased by much when I focused on my qi center. I had at most ten years' worth. Since my original qi center held about five years' worth, it had tripled in size, which was a lot, but the Explosive Demonic Pill supposedly contained half a gapja of energy, so only a third of it had actually been added to my qi center.

However, the phrase meridian cleansing and purification was more important.

meridian cleansing and purification.

I knew roughly what it was from reading wuxia novels on my commute.

It was a term from wuxia novels, a Great Ritual that prestigious families or great sects performed on newborns. An elder would use their inner arts to treat a baby's bones and acupoints. This major procedure transformed them, creating a constitution and acupoints optimized for learning martial arts.

"B-But, isn't meridian cleansing and purification only possible for young children?"

"Hohoho. It becomes more difficult with age, but it's not a Great Ritual that's only possible for young children."

The Master began to explain in detail.

"I didn't intend to perform meridian cleansing and purification from the start. Even for this old man, attempting it on a fifteen-year-old boy is a gamble. I only intended to remove the impurities from your major meridians and finish."

"Then why…?"

"First, Ilmok, your body had fewer impurities than this old man expected. It seems you didn't eat rich food or much meat when you were young. You also endured so well that we preserved more of the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy than expected, but your qi center was too small to contain that energy, so I had to remove the impurities from your minor meridians too."

I came from a poor family and later became an orphan, so I never ate well. That turned out to be a blessing in disguise.

"Your body is now in a very clean state, like a child's after meridian cleansing and purification. Your inner arts haven't increased much right now, but you'll be able to grow much faster than others."

As I listened, a question came to mind.

"But is it possible to perform meridian cleansing and purification with just twenty years of inner arts?"

If it were, I thought most martial families would perform it on all their children.

The Master shook his head at my question.

"Hohoho. How could that be? This old man primarily used his own inner arts to preserve as much of the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy as possible."

"!!!"

Thinking back, the Master's inner arts had always enveloped the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy while clearing my acupoints. That meant the Master's inner arts, not the pill's energy, directly contacted the impurities. For only a third of the pill's energy to remain meant the Master must have used at least half a gapja of his own inner arts.

"Thank you, Master!"

I completely forgot the memory of being tortured just moments ago and immediately bowed deeply to him.

"Hohoho. Think nothing of it. Your only regret was starting martial arts late, and now that disadvantage is gone."

The Master wore a satisfied expression, despite losing more than half a gapja of his inner arts, which are more precious than life to a martial artist. He seemed even more satisfied than I was, and I was the one who received the meridian cleansing and purification.

{sep}

After finishing the miracle herb absorption and meridian cleansing and purification, I began training. I changed into a new martial arts uniform Jin Hayeon had brought. The old one reeked of a rotten smell because the expelled impurities had soaked it.

I swung my sword several times in front of the watching Master.

*This is insane.*

I couldn't help but be greatly surprised by the effects of the meridian cleansing and purification.

Before, I only felt lighter, but now that I moved my body and inner arts myself, the difference was like heaven and earth. My inner arts coursed without obstruction because the impurities that narrowed my acupoints were gone. It was like a one-lane road had become a four-lane highway.

Furthermore, with my minor meridians cleared, it was as if all the parked cars in the alleyways had been towed away. There was no traffic congestion.

The only problem was that my acupoints were now too large for my amount of inner arts. They could handle fifteen years' worth of inner arts at once, so I could generate much more power and speed instantly, but I couldn't maintain it for long.

However, that was a truly minor problem.

*Since my acupoints have widened, the amount of energy I can absorb through qi circulation has also increased dramatically.*

In fact, after depleting my inner arts by performing the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword a few times, qi circulation filled my empty qi center in an instant.

*I wonder if the guys who got meridian cleansing and learned martial arts from a young age have been training like this all along. The young masters from prestigious families probably learn martial arts like this from birth. The gap must widen absurdly as they get older. They probably become prodigies, get called something like the Five Dragons and Two Phoenixes, and live in splendor.*

*[I, too, reached this level because I worked hard! You lot are lacking because you're lazy!]*

I couldn't help but imagine the arrogant prodigies from prestigious families spouting such lines.

*As expected, even in the murim, you have to be born with a golden spoon in your mouth. Or, like me, you have to win the lottery.*

"Fufufu."

Since the Cult Leader of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult became my Master, a lottery ticket this good had to be rare.

I trained the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword for a long time, high on the pill's power and the meridian cleansing and purification. Movements that once felt awkward now connected freely. The power and speed were incomparable to before, making swinging the sword incredibly enjoyable.

When my inner arts ran out, I performed qi circulation. When my qi center was full, I swung the sword again.

*No. I have to swing a little faster here.*

*Not enough! My control of pace is clumsy! I need to slow down at the end and transition smoothly to the next movement!*

Every time I swung the sword, I found a slight flaw. After recovering my inner arts, I would correct it.

"Hohoho. This old man will be on his way now."

"Take care, Master! Thank you so much!"

The Master had been watching with pure satisfaction. He decided it was time and left the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

The sun hid behind the mountains, and darkness fell, but I continued.

"......"

"......"

*Still not enough!*

Intoxicated by the sword, I relentlessly repeated the cycle of swinging and performing qi circulation, seeking a perfect sword path.

I was lost in my mind, intoxicated by the sword, for a long time.

"Haa. Haa."

The sweet smell of exhaustion wafted from my mouth. My muscles screamed, and the palm of my sword hand began to sting.

As I belatedly came to my senses, I realized an important fact I had forgotten. Absorbing miracle herbs naturally increases your martial arts achievements. Thanks to the meridian cleansing and purification, my proficiency in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword rose steeply.

A higher level in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword meant a more severe side effect.

I realized I had been swinging the sword like a madman, intoxicated by that side effect. I had achieved tremendous progress in just one day because of it, but that wasn't the important part.

Naturally, the scene from my dream last night came to mind. I thought that perhaps the nightmare had been a premonition.
