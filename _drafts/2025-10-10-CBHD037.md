Chapter 37: The Pill's Power (3)

The Master's immense energy, which had entered through my back, advanced toward my esophagus.
From my esophagus, it began to gently envelop the savage energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill that was spreading out.
So far, there was nothing particularly wrong, and the progression wasn't strange in any way.
However, I didn't know it at the time. I didn't know that this was just the beginning.
The heavy, immense energy enveloped the large, savage energy. The two energies, coexisting but not mixing, grew even larger.
That massive energy began to burrow into my acupoints.
Toward my weak and tiny acupoints, which could barely handle five years' worth of inner arts.
"!!!"
My eyes flew open from the unexpected, excruciating pain, and I almost screamed without realizing it.
Even amidst the filthy pain, a thought surfaced in a corner of my mind.
*So this is why he warned me not to pass out or open my mouth!*
Of course, Master wasn't just ignorantly shoving that entire massive energy into my acupoints.
As if pulling a thread from a giant ball of yarn, he was using his own energy to guide the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill, stretching it out long as it moved.
*S-Something that big won't fit!!*
Even so, the energy was too immense for my acupoints to accept.
"Hup!!!"
I clenched my teeth hard enough to break them to endure the agonizing pain. I couldn't check since there was no mirror, but I was probably making a horribly contorted face.
All the while, the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill was rampaging through my acupoints, violating them as it went, just as Master's energy guided it.
[Do not lose track of the flow of true qi that this old man is guiding.]
As I was about to lose my mind, I recalled Master's final warning and forced myself to concentrate on that energy.
Funnily enough, once I focused my mind elsewhere, the pain began to feel a little less intense.
On top of that.
THUD!
I could somewhat grasp what was happening inside me.
*So my acupoints weren't narrow to begin with.*
The energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill wasn't tearing my acupoints apart. To be precise, it was pushing out the impurities lodged throughout my acupoints as it coursed through them.
In other words, the pain I was feeling was the pain of those impurities that had been blocking my acupoints being forcibly ripped away.
Was it because the impurities had been torn away?
Funnily enough, after a little time passed, a refreshing sensation began to spread from the areas where I had felt pain.
It was the worst.
*Guuh!!*
I was still feeling pain in the foremost acupoints where the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill was breaking through, but a refreshing pleasure was rising from the rear areas it had already passed.
The mix of pain and pleasure brought a sensation like I was about to cross a line that shouldn't be crossed.
How long did I endure, clenching my teeth and fighting against myself?
*I-It's over...*
The energy, which had been coursing according to the heart technique of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, passed the final acupoint and was rushing into my qi center.
All I have to do now is absorb it into my qi center, and it'll be over.
That thought was fleeting.
*!?*
For some reason, instead of returning to the qi center, the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill was heading toward my acupoints again.
As if it was going to do another tour of my acupoints.
The thought 'I'm screwed' dominated my mind, but perhaps because most of the impurities had been pushed out during the first opening, the second round wasn't particularly painful.
And just as it was about to finish the second lap and return to the qi center.
*!?*
That crazy old man started directing the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill back to my acupoints again.
*Why again?*
No sooner had I harbored that question than the crazy old man personally showed me the reason.
*Why there!?*
The energies coursing through my acupoints, guided by Master, split into dozens of tiny fragments and spread out.
Leaving the major meridians, they headed for the minor meridians throughout my body.
A thought occurred to me.
*If you could have split it into smaller pieces, why didn't you just do that from the start, you crazy old man!!*
Now I see it wasn't that he couldn't split it into smaller pieces, he just didn't.
Of course, having watched the whole process, I knew the reason.
It must have been to maintain a size large enough to forcefully push out the impurities blocking the acupoints in one go.
However, regardless of the reason, the fact that I had suffered terrible pain thanks to that crazy old man remained unchanged.
And this time, the dozens of finely split strands of energy were presenting me with a new kind of pain.
If the pain of breaking through the impurities in the major meridians felt like being poked all over my body with something blunt.
Then the current pain of it coursing through the minor meridians of my entire body was a terrible agony, like dozens of ants had gotten inside me and were biting me from within.
It reached a point where I couldn't tell if I was absorbing miracle herbs or being subjected to torture.
Just when I couldn't bear it any longer and felt like I was really about to pass out and wanted to let go of everything.
"Hoo. You've endured it well."
From behind me, along with a deep breath, came the crazy old man's praise.
*Endured well, my ass.*
I really thought I was going to die.
Honestly, I felt like letting out a stream of curses.
"Hm?"
But once I came to my senses, a sensation I had never felt in my life washed over me.
How should I put it?
It felt like what you might feel if a lifelong illness was suddenly cured.
My body was so light. I wondered if my physical condition had ever been this good.
It was physically impossible, but it was enough to make me feel the illusion that I could actually fly.
"Hohoho. It seems quite fascinating to you."
"Is this the power of miracle herbs? Master."
I thought this must be why martial artists go crazy for miracle herbs with fire in their eyes, but for some reason, Master shook his head.
"Your inner arts haven't increased that much. Rather than being because you absorbed miracle herbs, it is the effect of meridian cleansing and purification."
"!?"
Just as Master said, when I focused on my qi center, my inner arts themselves hadn't increased by much.
At most, about ten years' worth of inner arts?
Since my original qi center could hold about five years' worth, it had increased threefold, which was a lot.
But since he said the Explosive Demonic Pill contained about half a gapja of energy, in reality, only about a third of it had been added to my qi center.
However, more important than that was the phrase meridian cleansing and purification.
meridian cleansing and purification.
I knew roughly what it was because I'd occasionally read wuxia novels on my way to and from work.
It was a term that often appeared in wuxia novels, a Great Ritual performed on newborn babies by prestigious families or great sects.
It was a term for a type of major surgery where an elder's inner arts were used to treat a newborn's bones and acupoints, transforming them into a constitution and acupoints optimized for learning martial arts.
"B-But, isn't meridian cleansing and purification only possible for young children?"
"Hohoho. It becomes more difficult to perform as one gets older, but it is not a Great Ritual that is only possible for young children."
The Master began his explanation in detail.
"I did not intend to perform meridian cleansing and purification from the beginning. Even for this old man, attempting meridian cleansing and purification on a fifteen-year-old boy is a gamble. Therefore, I intended to simply remove the impurities from your major meridians and finish."
"Then why…?"
"First, there were fewer impurities accumulated in your body, Ilmok, than this old man expected. It seems you didn't eat a rich diet of cooked food and meat when you were young. On top of that, because you endured so well, we were able to preserve more of the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy than expected. Conversely, your qi center was too small to contain that great energy, so I had no choice but to remove the impurities from your minor meridians as well."
I came from a poor family and was later reduced to an orphan, so I never ate well.
And yet, that had turned out to be a blessing in disguise.
"Your body is now in a very clean state, just like a child's after receiving meridian cleansing and purification. Your immediate inner arts haven't increased much, but you will be able to grow much faster than others."
However, as I listened to the explanation, a question came to mind.
"But, is it possible to perform meridian cleansing and purification with just twenty years' worth of inner arts?"
If it were possible with just that much, I thought most martial families would perform meridian cleansing and purification on all their children.
And at my question, Master shook his head.
"Hohoho. How could that be? To preserve as much of the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy as possible, this old man primarily used his own inner arts."
"!!!"
Thinking back, during the process of clearing my acupoints, Master's inner arts had always been enveloping the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill. In other words, the energy that directly contacted the impurities at the forefront was mostly Master's inner arts, not the energy of the Explosive Demonic Pill.
For only a third of the Explosive Demonic Pill's energy to remain even after all that meant Master must have used at least half a gapja of his own inner arts.
"Thank you, Master!"
The memory of being tortured by Master just a moment ago completely forgotten, I immediately bowed deeply to him.
"Hohoho. Think nothing of it. The fact that you started martial arts late was the only regrettable point, and now that disadvantage has disappeared."
Despite having lost more than half a gapja of his inner arts, which are more precious than life to a martial artist, Master wore a satisfied expression.
Even more so than me, the one who had actually received the meridian cleansing and purification.
★★★
After finishing the absorption of the miracle herbs and the meridian cleansing and purification.
I changed into a new martial arts uniform that Jin Hayeon had brought and began training.
Thanks to the impurities that had been blocking my acupoints all over my body being expelled, the martial arts uniform I had been wearing reeked of a rotten smell.
And so, I swung my sword several times in front of the watching Master.
*This is insane.*
I couldn't help but be greatly surprised by the effects of the meridian cleansing and purification.
A moment ago, it was just a feeling of my body becoming lighter, but now that I was moving my body and inner arts myself, the difference was like heaven and earth.
Because the acupoints that had been narrowed by impurities were now expanded, my inner arts coursed through them without obstruction.
It was like a one-lane road had been expanded into a four-lane highway.
Furthermore, since even the minor meridians had been cleared, it was as if all the cars parked in the alleyways had been towed away, so there was no such thing as traffic congestion.
If there was one problem, it was that my acupoints had become too large compared to the amount of inner arts I possessed.
They were spacious enough to handle fifteen years' worth of inner arts flowing through them all at once, so the power and speed I could generate in an instant were much stronger and faster, but I couldn't maintain it for long.
However, that was truly a minor problem.
*Since my acupoints have widened anyway, the amount of energy I can absorb through qi circulation has also increased dramatically.*
In fact, after performing the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword a few times until my inner arts were depleted and then performing qi circulation, my empty qi center filled up in an instant.
I wonder if the guys who received meridian cleansing and purification and learned martial arts from a young age have been training under these conditions all this time.
The young masters from prestigious families probably learn martial arts under these conditions from birth, so the gap would widen absurdly as they get older.
Those guys probably become a prodigy later on, get called by some alias like the Five Dragons and Two Phoenixes, and live in splendor.
[I, too, reached this level because I worked hard! You lot are lacking because you're lazy!]
I couldn't help but think of the arrogant prodigies from prestigious families who would spout such lines.
*As expected, even in the murim, you have to be born with a golden spoon in your mouth.*
Or, like me, you have to win the lottery.
"Fufufu."
Since the Cult Leader of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult became my Master, a lottery ticket this good must be rare.
How long did I train the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, high on the pill's power and the meridian cleansing and purification?
Even the movements that had previously felt somewhat awkward now connected freely.
Even the power and speed were incomparable to before, so swinging the sword was incredibly enjoyable.
When my inner arts ran out, I would perform qi circulation again, and when my qi center was full, I would swing the sword again.
*No. I have to swing a little faster here.*
*Not enough! My control of pace is clumsy! I need to slow down a little at the end and transition smoothly to the next movement!*
Every time I swung the sword, I found something that was even slightly lacking, and after recovering my inner arts, I would correct what was lacking.
"Hohoho. This old man will be on his way now."
"Take care, Master! Thank you so much!"
The Master, who had been watching with pure satisfaction, left the Wind Cliff Pavilion as if it was time.
Even at the hour when the sun had hidden itself behind the mountains and darkness had fallen.
SWISH!
SWISH!
*Still not enough!*
Intoxicated by the sword, I relentlessly repeated the cycle of swinging my sword and performing qi circulation, seeking to draw a perfect sword path.
How long had I been lost in my mind, intoxicated by the sword?
"Haa. Haa."
By the time the sweet smell of exhaustion wafted from my mouth, the muscles all over my body were screaming, and the palm of my sword hand began to sting.
Belatedly coming to my senses, I was able to realize an important fact I had forgotten.
When you absorb miracle herbs, it's only natural for your martial arts achievements to increase.
Thanks to even receiving meridian cleansing and purification, my proficiency in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword was rising steeply.
And a higher level in the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword meant that the side effect had also become more severe.
I realized that until now, I had been swinging the sword like a madman, intoxicated by the side effect.
Thanks to that, I had achieved tremendous progress in just one day, but that wasn't the important part.
Naturally, the scene I saw in my dream last night came to mind.
Along with the thought that perhaps that nightmare had been a premonition.
