Guyang Mun, who had attempted a grand escape, returned in the evening carrying a lavish dinner table.

"Why did you come back?" Jin Hayeon asked coldly.

Guyang Mun forced a hearty laugh. "Ha, hahaha! The work I forgot about finished faster than I thought. A-and you haven't even recovered yet, so I can't just abandon my duty of attending to the Young Lord! I-I'm a man with a strong sense of responsibility! Hahaha!"

After speaking, Guyang Mun seemed unable to meet Jin Hayeon’s eyes and hurriedly set the table for me.

"P-please, eat comfortably, Young Lord!"

"I-I will."

The situation felt like a scene from a comedy. I awkwardly moved my chopsticks and ate the food.

"A little while ago, Young Lady Seon'ah mentioned…" Jin Hayeon began.

"W-what story? I-I didn't hear anything," Guyang Mun interrupted, having another fit.

It didn't work on Jin Hayeon. "The story about you liking me. Is it true?"

"W-w-what are you talking about! M-me, like you? Ha. H-how absurd."

Guyang Mun shouted his denials as if having a seizure. I sighed as I ate.

"Sigh."

*I had a rough idea ever since he started acting like an elementary schooler, but that guy is definitely hopeless at romance.*

{sep}

After dinner, I briefly reviewed my day's training. Guyang Mun performed Body Purification on me and then bathed me.

"Miss Jin, I'm feeling a bit hungry. Could you perhaps bring me some snacks? My hands are indisposed, so please have a servant from the teahouse bring them with you."

When I suddenly gave Jin Hayeon the task, Guyang Mun, who was applying soapstone to my body, quickly exclaimed, "No, Young Lord! I'll go!"

"That's enough. Miss Jin, you go."

I forced the errand on Jin Hayeon. She left the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"......"

I sighed and looked at Guyang Mun. I sent her away so I could talk to this pitiful man while she was gone. I had no intention of meddling in someone else's love life, but I felt sorry for him. After Seon'ah dropped the bomb, it felt awkward to pretend I didn't know.

"Do you like Miss Jin that much?"

"W-w-what are you talking about!?" he yelled, having another fit.

I frowned. "That hurts."

While having his fit, Guyang Mun had scraped my skin with the soapstone. My skin didn't tear, but it turned red.

"I-I'm sorry."

"Anyway, Master Guyang's methods are not very good."

"M-my methods? What do you mean by that?"

"You did it just a moment ago, didn't you? What are you trying to accomplish by having a fit when asked if you like her?"

"B-but it's embarrassing. To say outright that I like her."

"Tsk, tsk, tsk."

As a romance senior who had been in three relationships in my thirty-four years, it was time to give some advice.

"People naturally feel favorable toward those who like them. Conversely, it's extremely rare for someone to feel favorable toward a person who constantly says they dislike them."

Of course, there are exceptions, like if the person following them around has a trash personality or some other major problem, but this was generally the case.

"It would be a different story if Miss Jin liked Master Guyang to begin with. In a situation where even diligent courting might not be enough, do you think she would like a Master Guyang who so openly says he dislikes her?"

"F-favors? Haven't I been diligently granting them?"

"When?"

"Well... giving her the medicine, and attending to the Young Lord in Hayeon's place..."

I asked back, not hiding my bewilderment. "...After speaking to her like that, do you really think those were favors?"

"...T-then what should I do? Should I just confess that I like her?" Guyang Mun finally seemed ready to listen. He stopped applying the soapstone and asked his rather foolish question.

"Listen carefully. A confession is not about letting the other person know your feelings."

"!? W-what do you mean? Isn't a confession literally a confession?"

"No. A confession is a question to finally confirm the feelings between two people."

"!!!"

Guyang Mun looked at me with a shocked expression.

*Then again, I was like that too when I had my first crush around high school.*

"Before that, you must build up feelings little by little by showing small favors to each other. Be careful, because showing an excessive favor from the start is no different from confessing right away."

"T-then."

Guyang Mun tried to ask something, but I cut him off. "Also! You must not show favors the way the Master has been doing until now. Show them little by little, but in a way the other person can feel that it's a favor. Not blatantly like, ‘I love you!’ but just enough to make her wonder, ‘Hmm? Is this man interested in me?’ Just enough to make the other person uncertain is appropriate."

"S-so."

"Just shut up and listen for now. When you've exchanged small favors and the feelings have matured to a certain point, that is the moment for a confession."

Guyang Mun looked as if he'd been struck by lightning during my long lecture. He muttered, "...I can't believe it. The Young Lord's teachings are much deeper and more detailed than the Seniors' advice."

"...You received advice from the Seniors?"

"Yes. In fact, the reason I returned a little while ago was because of the advice from the White Turtle Brigade Seniors. They told me that running away and abandoning my duties was not manly, so I should show ‘responsibility.’ They said I need to look like a dependable man for when I become a husband."

"......"

*So that's what happened. That's why he emphasized the word 'responsibility' earlier. To look like excellent husband material.*

*This isn't the 80s... Ah, this place is even older than the 80s, isn't it?*

An uncomfortable feeling crept in, wondering if my advice would even work in this era. Just then, Guyang Mun asked a question.

"But, you said to build things up by exchanging small favors, but what if the other person shows no reaction even after receiving my favor?"

"...Show a favor two or three more times. If there's still no reaction, or if she seems uncomfortable and displeased, give up."

"!!!. What do you mean! For me, it's only Hayeon, Young Lord!!"

Watching Guyang Mun have another fit, I thought, *He truly has the qualities of an excellent stalker. There's no tree that won't fall after ten strikes? It spread like a romantic notion because of one guy who succeeded, but the other ninety-nine who failed were stalkers. If he expected to be that one in a hundred, I wanted to recommend he buy a lottery ticket instead.*

And above all.

"Listen carefully. An unwanted favor is no different from harassment."

"W-what do you mean? How can a favor become harassment?"

"Master Guyang, think of any woman you dislike the most, one who makes you uncomfortable just by imagining being with her."

"???"

Master Guyang looked confused, but he soon replied, "I've thought of someone."

"Then, imagine that woman visiting Master Guyang every day, bringing unwanted gifts each time, and on top of that, trying to kiss Master Guyang. Would it be torment? Or would it be happiness?"

"!!!"

Guyang Mun wore a shocked expression, as if struck by lightning again. It seemed he finally understood what it meant to put himself in someone else's shoes, but that was only for a moment.

"T-then what should I do? I-it's really only Hayeon for me..."

Guyang Mun looked at me so desperately, as if he were about to burst into tears. A sigh escaped me automatically. I didn't know what he might do if I only used the stick, so I decided to offer a carrot as well.

"As I said before, one-sided courting is harassment. So, take a break for a while and then show your favor. Timing is also important in romance. When the other person is having a hard time or is lonely, the walls around their heart are weaker, making it easier to break through, and to aim for such a time, you must first remain as a friend, not a lover. I'm telling you again, don't show excessive favors or keep visiting and making the other person uncomfortable."

"Don't use a big technique forcefully, but keep them in check with light techniques while observing, then aim for the moment the opponent shows an opening! It's just like martial arts, Young Lord. Indeed, it seems the saying 'all rivers lead to the sea' wasn't said for nothing."

An empty laugh escaped me.

*To suddenly talk about martial arts in the middle of a conversation about romance.*

One way or another, this guy was a demonic cultist through and through.

"Thank you for your great teachings, Young Lord. This Guyang Mun, I will serve the Young Lord as my other master for the rest of my life!" Guyang Mun shouted, suddenly bowing deeply to me.

"I told you to attend to the Young Lord's bath. What are you doing prostrated on the floor?"

Just then, Jin Hayeon returned to the Wind Cliff Pavilion with a servant carrying refreshments. She stared coldly at Guyang Mun prostrated on the floor.

Somehow, I had a feeling Guyang Mun's love life was doomed.

{sep}

About half a month later, Guyang Mun left the Main Headquarters of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult with the White Turtle Brigade for a new mission.

As they left the Main Headquarters together, the Seniors of the White Turtle Brigade asked with mischievous smiles, "Mun-ah. What happened with that thing back then?" These were the same Seniors who had listened to Guyang Mun's romance problems and had grown close with him over the past year.

"Hahaha. It's going well." Guyang Mun gave an answer that would have made me shake my head. "And from now on, I won't have to ask the Seniors about that matter anymore. Hahaha."

At Guyang Mun's answer, the eyes of the White Turtle Brigade members widened.

"What?"

"Don't tell me, you've already bedded her?"

"B-bedded her! W-what a vulgar thing to say!"

When Guyang Mun shouted as if the suggestion was beneath him, the Seniors started to beat him up.

"Oh? Getting a little too comfortable, are we?"

"What did you say to your god-like Seniors? Vulgar?"

Amidst the half-playful beatings, Guyang Mun thought something else.

*That's right! I must follow the Master's advice. The Seniors' advice is no help at all!*

The master he referred to was not a martial arts master, but the romance master, Ilmok.

*He told me to show favors intermittently and steadily, and to look for an opening.*

He felt it was for the best. As a member of the White Turtle Brigade, he had to go outside often anyway. Guyang Mun thought that he could just get her a gift each time he returned from a mission.

*Last time I gave her the White Lotus Jade Ointment, so this time I should search the mountains and find some miracle herbs or the inner core of a spirit beast.*

Ilmok's advice to show small favors had long been erased from Guyang Mun's mind.

{sep}

"I didn't realize it would be this quiet with just one person gone."

After Guyang Mun left, the Wind Cliff Pavilion became calm again.

"Young Lord. I've brought some snacks."

While I was enjoying the tranquility, Jin Hayeon returned, carrying a plate herself. Surprisingly, her fractured arm had completely healed in just over a month.

*It did seem expensive. Just what kind of medicine was it?*

No matter how I thought about it, the medicine Guyang Mun had bought for her didn't seem ordinary. In any case, the Wind Cliff Pavilion had returned to its original state. Seon'ah visited while I ate snacks, and I spent my time leisurely, studying or dueling with either Jin Hayeon or Seon'ah.

Just as I was enjoying the long-awaited peaceful atmosphere, a loud voice boomed.

"Hahahaha. Is the Youngest Junior Brother here!"

One loud guy left, only for a new one to appear. As soon as she heard the voice, Seon'ah flinched and pressed herself close to my side. I thought she was scared, but her gaze and sharpened nails showed she had entered a combat stance. It seemed she had moved closer to protect me.

In the meantime, a familiar figure entered the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Greetings, Eldest Senior Brother."

"Hahaha. I've just finished my seclusion training as of today."

The Eldest Senior Brother, who had been confined to the seclusion training cave for a month as punishment, had come to the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

But why would he barge in here right after finishing his seclusion training?

*Don't tell me, he's here to kill me!?*

Recalling what happened in the seclusion training cave, a cold sweat ran down my back. Perhaps he had decided to kill me back then.

"There are many places to visit, but I came to see my most cherished younger brother first."

"???"

*Why is he suddenly thanking me?*

When I showed a puzzled reaction, the Eldest Senior Brother added, "That day, thanks to my younger brother's advice, I was able to overcome the final wall. Hahaha. The state of my Explosive Mara Sword Technique has entered the Transcendent Demon Realm."

"!!!"

While I was stunned, Jin Hayeon was the first to show the proper reaction. "Congratulations, First Young Lord."

Seon'ah and I followed her lead and offered our congratulations.

"Congratulations, Eldest Senior Brother. Does this mean you no longer fall into a rage?"

"Hahaha. That's right. If you don't believe me, would you like to test it out?"

"......"

Having already seen him rampage, I didn't have the courage to test him.

"Hahahaha. Don't worry. I no longer care about being called stupid. Just as my younger brother advised, if I am lacking, I just need to hire a smart subordinate!"

"T-truly wise words. Ha, hahaha."

"So, after entering the Transcendent Demon Realm, I thought about it carefully. Who should I bring in as my strategist to best fulfill that role."

"Y-you did?" I asked hesitantly, wondering why he was telling me this. For some reason, the Eldest Senior Brother looked completely thrilled.

"fufufu. So, do you know who I thought of?"

"Um... Did you think of the Team Three leader I advised you about?"

"Hahahaha. My younger brother is so humble! Hahahaha! Of course, I thought of my younger brother. Who could be better than you as a strategist!"

The story was so sudden that I couldn't help but show a dumbfounded reaction.

"Me?"

*I just want to complete the Demonic Academy and then live a comfortable life of leisure.*
