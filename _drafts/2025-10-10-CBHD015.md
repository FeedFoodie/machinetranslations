Late at night, around the time the Heavenly Demon met privately with the Hyeokryeon Clan's Patriarch, Ilmok was in his room. He had finished his daily routine and now worked on something by lamplight.

He was not writing in a diary.

Deep in his work, Ilmok furrowed his brow.

"Is someone talking bad about me?"

Ilmok muttered in annoyance and dug into his ear. He gave it a good, satisfying dig, but the feeling was not particularly refreshing.

"Hoo. It’s been a while, so I’ve completely lost the feel for it."

He sighed as he looked at the object in his hand.

*Anyway, the final deadline is before I enter the Demonic Academy.*

Thinking this, he shook off his impatience. After all, he had experience making one in his past life as Seo Jihun. The only problem was that it had been a very long time ago, and he had only done it once.

Ilmok stared at the bizarre, unidentifiable object in his hand.

*For now, I'll just have to think of this as practice.*

{sep}

Despite the murderous atmosphere Lady Cheonghwa exuded, nothing changed at the Wind Cliff Pavilion. As always, I prepared for the entrance exam while Jin Hayeon tormented me.

If there was just one change, it was the little kid who visited every day.

"Oppa~!"

She came around the time I finished my external arts training. I could not let her show up whenever she wanted, so we had agreed on this specific time each day.

Perhaps because the Hyeokryeon Clan gave permission, she now came with a stoic-looking man who seemed to be a bodyguard. That bodyguard, likely because of that damn custom, never spoke a word to Seon'ah. He just waited at the entrance of the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Hehehe."

Seon'ah seemed to have no concern for the bodyguard. She just ran over with a pitter-patter and grinned foolishly by my side. My whole body reeked of sweat from Jin Hayeon’s training, but the child approached as if she did not mind the scent at all.

Seeing the little kid's smile made my heart feel much more at ease.

*As expected, it was a good idea to confront them.*

The decision was not just for Seon'ah. In this prison-like place, talking with a little kid in the same boat was like welcome rain for me. People need to relieve stress to live. If you do not leave a small breathing hole, you are bound to go crazy.

In this place full of madmen, it was not like anyone would notice if I went crazy.

*Is that what makes it more dangerous?*

Since everyone was a madman, I wondered if I was letting things slide, thinking I was fine by comparison. In any case, it was a peaceful daily life. Part of that peace was listening to the little kid chatter about her day.

"What were you doing at home before you came today?"

"Today, I learned how to apply the tenth technique of the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique. Hehet. They said if I do this with my fingernails and extend them like this, I can easily poke a hole in a bad guy’s heart."

I smiled bitterly as Seon'ah demonstrated with various hand gestures.

*What are these crazy bastards teaching a child?*

Anyway, it was a peaceful atmosphere. By the Demonic Cult's standards.

{sep}

I was enjoying a peaceful afternoon and blowing off steam when the bodyguard’s voice echoed from beyond the main gate.

THUD.

"I greet the Lord of All Demons."

It seemed my training time with Master had arrived.

"Rise."

Master's voice came from beyond the gate. Soon after, the gate opened to reveal his figure.

"We greet the Lord of All Demons."

Jin Hayeon and Seon'ah immediately prostrated themselves in a show of utmost respect. I also hurriedly bowed low.

"Hohoho. Rise, and now that the time has come, the child of the Hyeokryeon Clan should return to her family home."

"I-I will obey your command."

Seon'ah replied nervously. She pushed herself up and started toward the gate with small steps, then glanced back. I had already risen, so I gave her a slight wave. Seon'ah, who had been tense from Master's appearance, smiled brightly, waved back, and left.

Master watched this from the side and suddenly brought up a strange topic.

"You seem to get along well with the girl from the Hyeokryeon Clan."

"It just sort of happened."

"Hohoho. It just sort of happened... Yes, you must have your reasons, so do your best."

???

I could not understand what he wanted me to do my best at.

*Surely, he didn't misunderstand it in *that* way, did he?*

Our bodies were only three years apart in age, but still, that was not it. It was the feeling you get when you see a cute puppy or kitten, or find your young nephew's silly antics adorable.

*Perhaps the old man's tastes lean that way...*

They say you only see what you know.

I stared at Master for a moment as if he were pathetic. When his gaze suddenly turned to me, I quickly averted my eyes. If he caught me looking at him like that, my head would be chopped off.

"Well then, let's slowly begin our training."

"Yes, Master."

{sep}

The Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword consisted of twenty-two techniques, divided into fourteen former and eight latter techniques. Nearly half a month after I began learning the sword art, I had mastered the forms of all fourteen former techniques.

After teaching me all of them, the perverted old man, no, Master, explained the true meaning of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword.

"The reason the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword is called that is because it is, quite literally, a sword art that snatches the soul from your opponent. Its significance lies in making the opponent lose their spirit through a continuous and naturally flowing sequence."

"In that it is a sword art that focuses on linkage and variation, it is similar to the Frenzied Soul Sword Technique, but its nature is completely different. While the Frenzied Soul Sword Technique takes the neck of a startled opponent through bizarre movements that defy their expectations, the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword slowly corners the opponent and takes the neck of one who has given up everything. The dazed face of someone who has given up everything looks as if their soul has departed, hence the name Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword."

Master explained not just the physical movements, but the meaning embedded within the entire sword art. After his explanation, I vaguely grasped the most important point of the fourteen techniques I had learned.

*The lower body and waist are important. It's about stably and gradually cornering the opponent without losing balance during strenuous movements.*

Thinking it over, I realized all the techniques of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword could be linked together ceaselessly, regardless of their order. As long as I did not lose my balance, that is.

I sheathed my sword, then drew it according to the principles of the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw. I tried performing the techniques I had learned in various ways, connecting the movements differently, sometimes reversing the order, sometimes skipping one.

At first, my posture was awkward, so I swung slowly to get a feel for it. Then I gradually increased my speed, putting strength into my arms and legs. Naturally, once I raised the speed past a certain point, I felt my balance starting to falter slightly.

*Is this the best I can do for now?*

I continued to link and perform each movement with just enough strength and speed to not lose my balance. Master's satisfied laughter reached my ears.

"Hohoho. It seems you've understood the core of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword with just this old man's brief explanation. Just as you said, Hayeon, Ilmok's insight is quite outstanding."

"How could that be due to this girl's eye? It is entirely thanks to the Heavenly Demon for finding Young Lord Ilmok and bringing him to the Divine Cult."

I was the one working my ass off swinging the sword, but for some reason, the two of them created a heartwarming atmosphere, patting each other on the back.

I felt like a small child showing off his talents for family elders on a holiday. Feeling awkward, I sheathed my sword and calmly regulated my breathing.

Master observed me and then remarked, "The latter techniques can only be learned once you've reached a state where you can at least produce sword qi, so for now, continue to refine the former techniques."

After saying that, Master approached me and placed his palm on my abdomen.

"Hmm. It seems you have been practicing qi circulation diligently."

Master gauged the inner arts gathered in my qi center, then suddenly threw a question at Jin Hayeon.

"How are Ilmok's doctrinal studies progressing?"

"He is learning very quickly. It’s hard to believe he has only just joined the cult. His speed is so fast that it makes me wonder if he has already learned the doctrines to some extent before."

She rattled off praise with a business-like tone and a cold expression. Then again, it would be hard for her to criticize me. Only half a month had passed, yet I had already studied nearly thirty percent of the scripture, the Records of the Heavenly Demon.

"At this rate, he should be able to master the scriptures, history books, and law books within half a year at the latest. After that, just reviewing them should be sufficient."

"Hohoho. His comprehension of his studies is high, and his progress with the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword is also fast. In other words, that means he has plenty of time to spare."

"So far, yes."

"Hohoho."

Master's gaze, accompanied by a gentle laugh, fixed on me. For some reason, I felt a sense of déjà vu.

*This is that, isn't it?*

When you finish your work quickly, they do not let you leave on time. They give you more work because you are good at it. That unique Korean work culture. The so-called dumping of work.

Remembering what happened at the company dinner, I quickly opened my mouth.

"Ah, it’s still too risky, Master. I’m only making fast progress with the scriptures because I’ve just started. As the material I need to study piles up, the time I have for review will inevitably become insufficient."

Master had clearly said at the dinner that he would 'give me work later'. Now, he was undoubtedly creating a pretext to give me additional tasks in a way that was all too familiar.

The memory of my Team Leader dumping work on me surfaced in my mind.

"Hahaha. Our Senior Official Seo Jihun is so good at his job. Since it looks like you have some time to spare, how about you handle this too, Senior Official Seo Jihun? It’s related work, so you should be able to finish it quickly."

I had worked like a madman all morning to protect my work-life balance and leave on time. I absolutely could not let the same thing happen again.

"It’s the same for the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. I may have learned the forms quickly, but aren't my inner arts still lacking? Therefore, I think it would be better to invest any remaining time into qi circulation."

As if I would let him get his way so easily.

"Hohoho. Now that I hear it, that makes sense."

The battle of wits began, with Master trying to dump work on me and me trying to avoid it.

"In that case, to help you fill that lack of inner arts, I’ll instruct my clinic to prepare an Explosive Demonic Pill to be bestowed upon you, Ilmok."

"...What is the Explosive Demonic Pill?"

"It’s one of the elixirs produced by our Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, an excellent miracle herb that explosively increases one's inner arts."

And with that, it ended in my defeat.

"Besides, you'll be with Hayeon anyway, so it won't be a major hindrance to your studies."

Master swiftly blocked off my last escape route.
