"The daughter of this house."

"Ah..."

"Estella Cardein."

It was then that Estella answered with a grin.

BWOOOO! BWOOOO! BWOOOO!

The sound of a horn suddenly tore through the silent darkness, shaking the air.

The two exchanged startled, urgent glances. As if by agreement, they simultaneously ran in different directions. A flurry of movement could be felt in the dark air.

The once-empty training ground was soon filled with knights and mercenaries. Besides the Squirrel Mercenaries, four other mercenary groups had also entered. Their sizes varied, but each group had at least twenty members.

The mercenary groups, eyeing each other warily, spotted the sparsely numbered Squirrel Mercenaries and snickered.

"If you hide behind us, we can toss you the stragglers. How about it? Pretty lady, interested? Wanna come stand by me?"

Jack, the leader of the Golden Scorpion Mercenaries, flirted with Riri, giving her a suggestive smile. He was a man with a patch of hair on the crown of his head styled into a sharp rooster's comb.

Snickers could be heard from the surroundings, but Ciel's party didn't even glance in their direction.

"Looks like you're scared. Why'd you bring a bunch of kids who can't even pull their weight? It's obvious there's only one person here who can do any heavy lifting."

He was clearly pointing at the large-framed Bruno. The comment was meant to be insulting, but for some reason, no one rose to the bait. Only Bruno, who was secretly pleased by the remark directed at him, lifted his chin proudly.

"It's hard to survive in this business with just a pretty face. Ah, or maybe you use your 'strength' in other ways? If it's that kind of strength, I've got plenty to spare! Kehkehkeh!"

At Jack's provocation, as he suggestively stroked his own crotch, Riri's patience finally ran out.

"See, Captain? He says I'm the only one here who looks strong. You should build up some muscle." Riri tapped Ciel's shoulder and grinned.

"I'll try."

Satisfied with Ciel's answer, Riri smiled and gracefully raised her middle finger at Jack.

"He says he'll try. So get lost, you water deer dick-looking bastard. Before I rip off that long-ass jaw of yours and use it as a shovel."

As the vulgar curse flew from the mouth of the seemingly demure Riri, Jack covered his jaw in shock, and his subordinates' shoulders shook with laughter.

BWOOO, BWOOOOO! BWOO! BWOO BWOO!

The sound of the horn was quite different from usual. Estella, who had nearly finished her preparations to move out, looked at Ria and asked.

"Is it a Grade 3?"

Estella, who had been learning the horn signals from Ria, asked with wide eyes. A Grade 3 was threatening, but it was a level that could be handled with proper preparation.

*What kind of Grade 3 monsters were there again...*

She tried to recall what she had memorized, but there were too many to guess which one might have appeared.

BWOOO, BWOO BWOOOOOO BWOO, BWOO, BWOO!

Listening to the changed horn signal, a strange light gleamed in Estella's eyes.

"Don't tell me it's the Azure Forest?"

"Yes. Do you really have to go? Can't you stay?"

The Azure Forest was on a completely different level from the Reusilin Forest where the Goliath Hopper had appeared. Because of the gloomy energy that flowed through the entire forest, people who went there would always fall terribly ill for a day or two.

Ria spoke with a worried expression, but Estella, looking even more excited than before, hugged her tightly.

"Don't worry. I'm a proper member of the knight order now."

"You're a reserve member."

"I'll be dropping the 'reserve' part soon. You said I was a sword genius."

Estella boasted, but Ria's worries didn't disappear.

*How nice would it be if she just learned flower arrangement or embroidery like other young ladies.*

Ria was happier than anyone about her lady's change, but whenever she saw Estella rushing headfirst into danger, she was always on edge with a sense of anxiety rising in her heart.

"Oh, right, my lady, you have to take these!" Ria handed two daggers to Estella, who was just about to leave.

Based on Yoon Seoyi's memories, she had reduced the inconvenient flaws of the daggers currently used by the knight order and maximized their strengths.

She had removed all unnecessary decorations to reduce the weight and lessen the strain on the wrist, shortened the overly long blade for a dagger, made it double-edged, and added fine serrations to the center of the spine.

The hilt was made thick enough to be handled comfortably even with gloves on, and a leather strap was wrapped around it multiple times to prevent slipping. The unnecessarily curved guard, shaped like the toe of a traditional sock, was also minimized. Everything was an optimal shape, neither too much nor too little.

It required several revisions, but Dolan followed Estella's instructions without complaint.

Dolan, who had initially regarded her with suspicion, now seemed to be secretly looking forward to what kind of project Estella would bring next. He even pestered her to give him one of the modified daggers.

"Ah! Thanks!"

Estella slid the daggers she received from Ria into the sheaths on her thighs. The sheaths, made of leather and steel, were engraved with Estella's name. These adjustable sheaths were Claire's creation.

Claire, who thought she would only ever make dresses, was particularly happy while making the sheaths. Something about her fingertips tingling?

In this way, Estella was in the process of completing her own crew.

After securely equipping both her longsword and daggers, she felt her courage double.

"I'm off!"

In truth, there was a reason Estella was pushing herself to join this hunt.

*Nebrion.*

It was because of the enhancement magic stones.

According to Estella Cardein's memories, large quantities of Nebrion were buried in the Azure Forest, where the monster had appeared today. No one yet knew that a massive amount of magic stones was buried in the Winterfall territory.

Only Estella, with her memories of the future, knew the approximate location, even if not the exact one. The corners of Estella's mouth lifted pleasantly with the thrill of being the only one who knew the winning lottery numbers.

*If I can get my hands on Nebrion, I can modify Winterfall's weapons to be much more powerful than they are now.*

If she succeeded, it would be a great help in protecting the territory.

Estella waved cheerfully at Ria, who stood with a worried expression. She felt that having someone waiting for you was a very pleasant thing.

When Estella subtly joined the group, Lucius's eyes widened.

His gaze seemed to ask why she was there, but Estella slyly avoided his eyes and stubbornly held her ground.

The knights who used the training hall with her every day weren't very surprised. Seeing Estella's swordsmanship improve day by day, they had guessed a day like this would eventually come. Besides, they had already seen enough of Estella's skills in the Reusilin Forest.

Lucius, too, only stared intently for a moment before saying nothing. It was a relief.

"Phew!"

Estella, who had thought she might be kicked out, let out a huge sigh of relief and straightened her back. She wanted to compensate for her short stature this way, but it was no use. She briefly considered asking Claire to make her platform boots, but the moment she imagined herself clumsily hopping through the forest in boots with thick soles, she gave up.

She didn't want to fall to her death from her boots just trying to look a little taller.

"Who in the world is that, that the captain can't take his eyes off her?" Riri asked Bruno with a fascinated expression, noticing Ciel glancing at Estella.

"I don't know. Did the Marquisate have a female knight?"

"Idiot, does she look like a knight to you?"

Bruno shook his head slightly as he watched Estella's back as she rode her horse ahead of them. She seemed too soft to be a knight. However, judging by her riding form and the aura she gave off, she didn't seem like an ordinary woman either.

"Does anyone else know anything?" Riri asked loudly, but since the other three knew nothing, there was no reply. Ciel was dumbfounded.

"Hey, Riri, I'm right here next to you?"

"It's more fun to ask someone other than the person involved."

"They said they don't know."

"Don't worry. I'll figure it out myself."

"Just ask me."

"Ehh, that's no fun. It's been a while since the captain's shown interest in a woman."

"It's not like that."

"Yeees, yeees. I understand."

Riri didn't look like she believed him at all. The corners of her mouth were already raised a handspan, as if she had found enough material to tease him for the next six months. Ciel opened his mouth to say something but then just shut it.

He knew that once Riri had decided to tease him, no excuse would work.

They were all supposedly his subordinates, but strangely, he always felt like he was the one being managed.

"Hey, do you know who that woman riding up front is?" 

Ciel watched Riri shout the question at Kyle, who was riding at the very back, and thought, *Am I really the captain?*
