Nearly two months had passed since I was dragged into this damn heretic cult. Because of that, I had already finished the scripture and doctrinal text, the Records of the Heavenly Demon. I was now studying history.

Anyone who has studied diligently, whether as a student, a job seeker, or for a civil service exam, would probably understand. They would know that the study method changes depending on the topic, even for the same subject.

The doctrinal text and scripture, the Records of the Heavenly Demon, required some understanding, but it also involved hidden weapons. To be precise, it was not about memorizing the scripture’s phrases. It was about knowing the interpretations the heretic cultists twisted for themselves. Understanding their insane way of thinking was just a bonus.

However, the history book I was studying and the law book I would soon learn were different. They also required understanding, but they demanded far more ‘simple hidden weapons’.

There was no need to worry, though.

*When it comes to simple hidden weapons, it's all about the civil service exam.*

I had studied Korean history and administrative law for the civil service exam, which was similar to their law book. Thanks to many years as a student, from school to exam preparation, I knew various hidden weapons methods. These ranged from the memory palace and visualization to mnemonic devices and the acronym-based method I often used in school.

Most high school students in the Republic of Korea have probably used the acronym-based hidden weapons method. For liberal arts students, a prime example was the mnemonic for the Joseon dynasty kings, starting with Tae-Jeong-Tae-Se-Mun-Dan-Se. For science students, it was the one for chemical symbols, like Kal-Kal-Na-Ma-Al-A-Cheol-Ni-Ju-Nap.

*Ah, did they say liberal arts and sciences are integrated now, so they're not divided anymore?*

I shook my head, realizing how much like an old man that thought made me sound. In any case, since I was already sick of hidden weapons, my ‘studies’ progressed smoothly.

So then, how was my martial arts training going?

*To think I'd ever have a body like this in my life.*

After being rolled around by Jin Hayeon for two months, muscles were slowly starting to show. It was truly strange. I had heard it takes at least half a year to feel the effects of exercise, yet it was already so noticeable.

*Is this... overwhelming talent?*

I thought I could understand why Master, the Heavenly Demon, had coveted my body so much. My inner arts had also improved quite a bit. Now, if I controlled my strength properly, I could perform martial arts for about the time it takes an incense stick to burn.

I had memorized all the forms, secrets, and inner arts applications of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. Recently, I had started a slightly different kind of training.

Just like right now.

"......"

I drew my sword with the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw technique, blocking Jin Hayeon’s palm strike. She delivered it with her usual emotionless gaze. A chilling coldness seeped from her palm where it met my sword.

My inner arts were still too weak to withstand that energy, so I used movement arts to step back and deflect her hand.

As if expecting my retreat, she stepped forward and thrust out her left hand. This time, instead of blocking her hand, I thrust my sword toward her heart.

*The sword is longer than the hand!*

Jin Hayeon seemed to know that too. She quickly twisted her attacking left hand and tried to grab my sword with her five fingers using grappling arts. If her hand, trained in ice arts, caught my sword, yin qi could invade through the blade, so I withdrew it.

However, I did not withdraw the sword completely. I bent my shoulder and elbow only halfway, pulling back just enough to avoid her grasp.

SWOOSH!

I launched another thrust toward a different direction. It was something I had realized after several duels with her. The sword’s advantage is its usefulness for both thrusts and slashes. While shorter than a spear, it has a longer range than a barehanded opponent. Against her, a practitioner of ice arts, quick hit-and-run thrusts were far more effective than powerful slashes.

SWOOSH!

"......"

A series of concise, fast thrusts unfolded. Jin Hayeon parried or dodged them with minimal movement, continuously looking for an opportunity to close in. I regulated the inner arts coursing through my body while thrusting my sword several times. My heightened concentration made sweat pour from my body despite the short time.

In the brief moment a drop of sweat fell from my bangs and obscured my right eye.

FWOOSH!

!!!

She had instantly moved into my blind spot and was already thrusting her right hand toward my abdomen.

CLANG!!

"Keuk…"

I barely blocked it, but my stance broke, and I was pushed back two large steps.

"Control your inner arts, Young Lord. There is a risk of internal injury."

I did not need her advice. I immediately sat cross-legged and began healing by qi circulation. She must have controlled her strength at the end, as I had not suffered a serious internal injury. Controlling my inner arts did not take long.

"......"

I finished the first aid, took a deep breath, and opened my eyes. Jin Hayeon spoke in a blunt tone.

"The Soul-Reaping Flash and the Soul-Capturing Sword have become much more natural, Young Lord."

She showed no concern for my injuries and began a review of the duel as soon as I opened my eyes. The Soul-Reaping Flash and the Soul-Capturing Sword were the fourth and eleventh techniques of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, the thrusts I had used relentlessly against her.

They were thrusts that aimed at completely different directions and unfolded in various ways, but in the end, I was only using two techniques. All the techniques of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword were like that. Just like the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw, there was a basic form and movement, and one had to apply it in various ways.

After opening the review with a compliment, she coldly laid out her criticisms, as expected of an aspiring psychopath.

"However, you're relying too much on just those two techniques. Although thrusts are a good match against my Pure Hands Demonic Art, your next move becomes obvious if you only repeat those two techniques. You need more variation."

"I'll be careful."

"Also, it's disappointing that you panicked and lost track of me when sweat obscured your vision. Young Lord, you were born with excellent eyes and reflexes, so you have a bad habit of judging based on your opponent's movements. You must always predict your opponent's next move."

"I… I see."

"Finally, you're too cautious. At the end, if you had swung your sword at my neck instead of blocking my strike, you might not have won, but you could have at least managed a draw."

"……"

She had an exceptional talent for tearing people down.

*...If Jin Hayeon had been the one to teach Third Senior Brother when he was young, he might have hanged himself long ago.*

She could truly be called a self-esteem murderer.

"I want to do it too!"

Just then, the kid, who was now much more cheerful, shouted while waving her hand brightly. This was supposed to be a break time that Seon'ah had promised to visit during. However, thanks to Jin Hayeon, who disapproved of me always taking breaks with Seon'ah, it had turned into something a little different.

A time for Seon'ah and me to study or train together, I suppose? We would take turns sparring like this, and sometimes we would review the morning's studies together with Seon'ah.

"Here I come!!"

Seon'ah shouted and rushed forward, using her movement arts with a ‘FWOOSH’. The little kid closed the distance in an instant. Her brandished fingernails were already imbued with a red energy. The reddish tint in Seon'ah's hair and eyes also seemed to be an effect of the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique she had learned.

A metallic ‘CLANG’ rang out as Seon'ah’s inner arts-imbued fingernails clashed with my sword. Dueling with Seon'ah was also a great help to me. Unlike Jin Hayeon, who utilized cold yin qi with concise movements, Seon'ah’s Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique boasted sharp and unpredictable techniques. I had to respond in a different way.

Besides, unlike Jin Hayeon, who was a much higher hand seal than me, Seon'ah wasn't that different from my level. Seon'ah had been learning the Blood Fox Crimson Claw Technique for over a year, so she had the upper hand in inner arts and technique.

CLANG!!

However, because of the three-year age gap during our prime growth period, my physical abilities were far superior.

*Seon'ah is also learning claw techniques, so thrusts that utilize distance would be useful, but...*

For a moment, I maintained my distance with light thrusts, just as I did when dueling Jin Hayeon. Recalling Jin Hayeon’s advice, I waited until Seon'ah grew accustomed to my thrusts, then suddenly changed my trajectory and attempted a horizontal slash.

CLANG!!

Seon'ah, who had been moving nimbly and jumping around, barely blocked the surprise slash. Unable to withstand the force behind my sword, she was sent flying backward.

TAP!

Well, she did land stably on the ground with cat-like agility.

"Hahaha. As expected, Junior Brother, you learn so quickly. To incorporate Miss Jin's advice in an instant."

A familiar voice reached my ears. I turned my head in confusion and saw Third Senior Brother standing there.

"Greetings, Third Senior Brother."

As I hurriedly stopped the duel and greeted Senior Brother Seo Wanpyeong, a strange thought occurred to me.

*The fact that he knows what Jin Hayeon advised me means he's been here for a while, doesn't it?*

He was a Senior Brother with a ridiculously faint presence. That same Senior Brother gave a twisted smile. His face looked gloomy, but I now knew that was the smile he made when he was genuinely happy.

"Fufufu. It is good to duel with various people, Junior Brother. Although Miss Jin and the Young Lady of the Hyeokryeon Clan use different martial arts, they both use hand-based martial arts. How about trying a duel with a sword-wielding opponent this time?"

"A sword… Are you perhaps saying that you will duel with me, Senior Brother?"

My Senior Brother nodded at my question.

"For my most respected Senior Brother to duel with me, it is nothing but an honor!"

At my shout, full of flattery, Third Senior Brother let out a hearty laugh that did not suit his face.

"Hahahaha! There's no need to call it an honor."

The Senior Brother was now in high spirits. Seon'ah was pouting, perhaps because her playtime with me had been stolen, and then there was Jin Hayeon, with her usual icy expression.

*...For some reason, I feel like the number of people keeps increasing.*

It won't increase any more than this, right? Such an ominous thought came to mind.

In the dead of night, three people held a meeting in a corner of the grand valedictorian at the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's Main Headquarters. They used the shadows cast by the faint, cloud-hidden moonlight as their masks. After exchanging various news from within the cult, the man on the left brought up Ilmok.

"Come to think of it, I've been hearing quite a few stories lately about the youngest disciple the Heavenly Demon recently took in."

"Hmm. I heard that the matter of the school the Heavenly Demon is pushing for also came from that youngest disciple."

"Tsk. The brat is causing unnecessary trouble."

The old man believed the uneducated masses should just stick to their livelihoods and offer up money or food. That was all they were good for. Bestowing knowledge upon them would only make them more difficult to lead.

"In that case, how about we get rid of that youngest disciple before he causes any more useless trouble?"

There were countless ways to kill him. They could disguise it as an accident, fake a qi deviation, or leak information to the heathens of the Central Plains to lure him into a trap.

However, the man sitting in the seat of honor shook his head at the suggestion from the man on the right.

"The Heavenly Demon's age has long since passed one hundred anyway. His day of Ascension is not far off, so there is no need to take such a risk."

The current Heavenly Demon had achieved complete mastery of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art and reached a high realm, maintaining a youthful appearance for his age, but he could not defy his natural lifespan. The history of murim proved this. The current demonic practitioner Hyeokryeon Ilhwi may have reached a realm rarely seen, but he was not the only one in murim history to have done so.

"At most, it will be about ten years, and in ten years, the only one who could be an obstacle to us taking the position of Cult Leader is the great disciple Wi Jinhak."

Seo Wanpyeong, who had reached the next highest realm after Wi Jinhak, could also be called an expert martial artist, but he was no match for those gathered here. From their perspective, Seo Wanpyeong was just a brat still floundering in the Ultimate Demon Realm.

On the other hand, Wi Jinhak had reached a state close to the Transcendent Demon Realm. There was a risk he might succeed in reaching it by the time of the Heavenly Demon's Ascension.

"Even if Wi Jinhak succeeds in reaching the Transcendent Demon Realm, there is likewise no need to worry. Wi Jinhak is dull-witted, so we can play with him as much as we want."

The men on either side nodded at the confident words of the man in the center.

The man on the left asked as if he had remembered something.

"But, what will you do if, in those ten years, the youngest brat doesn't take his own path but instead sides with Wi Jinhak? From the looks of it, he seems to be quite sharp."

At that question, white teeth peeked through the shadow covering the man in the center. The man revealed a sinister smile and said.

"At that time, we just need to take care of the youngest brat before he grows any stronger, even if it means taking a small risk."
