After the noisy break time ended and everyone left, Master visited the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Ilmok, I noticed last evening that your progress with the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword is advancing rapidly."

"It’s all thanks to you, Master," I replied, my emotions conflicted. Outwardly, I acted polite and grateful.

*This damn demonic art.*

My obsessive compulsion had worsened, which troubled me in many ways.

"Did you devote yourself to the sword today as well?"

"Well…" I pondered Master’s question for a moment before answering with some honesty. "My level rose so suddenly that the side effects have intensified. I’m holding back for a while."

I had said everything except for the curses.

"......"

I expected him to be disappointed, but for some reason, Master stroked his beard with shining eyes.

"I was about to bring that up myself. It’s truly commendable that you’re being cautious on your own."

"???"

"Unlike the martial arts of heathens, demonic art boasts rapid achievement. The level is beyond comparison. Do you know how long it takes heathens to become a pinnacle-level drummer who can freely handle sword qi or fist qi by circulating it through the Conception and Governing Vessels?"

The conversation shifted to an unexpected topic. I thought for a moment before answering, "…I’m not sure."

"It varies greatly depending on one’s talent or any miracle herbs they find. Those with some talent generally reach the pinnacle level in their middle age. In the vast Central Plains, only a few ‘geniuses’ who have also consumed miracle herbs can reach the pinnacle level in their twenties. Now, what do you think it’s like for demonic art?"

"Hmm… if orthodox martial arts take that long, wouldn’t demonic art take about ten years?"

"Hohohoho. You’re somewhat correct. It depends on talent, but it generally takes five to ten years to reach what the heathens call the pinnacle level."

"F-five years, you say?"

"Hohoho, and that’s just the average time. Your Eldest Senior Brother, Jinhak, took a mere three years. He could already handle sword qi freely by the time he entered the Demonic Academy."

Master spoke with pride, then stopped laughing and suddenly grew serious.

"However, this demonic art has one major problem. Ilmok, you must be feeling it as well, aren’t you?"

"Isn’t it the side effect that makes your mind go strange?"

"That’s correct. That’s why it’s very dangerous to get engrossed in gaining power. You should raise your achievements quickly, but it’s important to stop for a while when the side effects become unbearable. You must wait until you can control your mind to some extent."

After hearing the explanation, I understood how Demonic Cult martial artists practiced demonic art.

They used its strengths to rapidly increase their achievements. When their mental illness grew too severe, they stopped.

They would wait until they thought, *I think I can handle it now,* then devote themselves to training again to raise their level. They repeated this process, stopping whenever the mental illness became severe.

When they finally thought, *Ah! I really can’t go any further,* they seemed to settle in and stop progressing completely.

The martial artists and Branch Managers I saw on my business trip were people who had stopped at a certain level. That explained why their levels were lower than the much younger Jin Hayeon, despite practicing the fast-growing demonic art and reaching middle age.

They built their skills on a precarious tightrope. The moment they failed, they suffered qi deviation or went berserk. When that happened, nearby demonic practitioners would subdue them, just like with Eldest Senior Brother’s rampage.

My thoughts led me to a natural conclusion. Demonic art might be for those who lack talent but possess strong mental fortitude.

It offered fraudulent growth speed, allowing one to reach the pinnacle in ten years even without talent. Anyone could become a drummer with enough effort. For someone with a tenacious spirit but no talent, what martial art could be more perfect?

Conversely, the art would unleash a terrible disaster if practiced by someone with overflowing talent but the mental fortitude of a sunfish.

From that perspective…

*Damn it. That means it’s the worst possible match for me.*

My body boasted such absurd talent for martial arts that even Master coveted it. Master had even performed meridian cleansing and purification, making it the perfect body for practicing martial arts.

If I added the fast-growing demonic art to that, my level might rise every time I trained. The problem was my mental strength.

*Am I mentally strong?*

My goal in life was a work-life balance of working and playing moderately.

The word ‘doomed’ floated endlessly in my mind as Master’s voice pierced my ears.

"However, you don’t need to be too afraid of the side effects. Once you pass the Ultimate Demon Realm and enter the Transcendent Demon Realm, all problems will be solved. Besides, if you avoid training out of fear, what’s the point of learning the fast-achieving demonic art?"

*It’s not like I learned this demonic art because I wanted to.*

The words stuck in my throat, but I held them back with my last bit of patience.

"…I understand."

A sigh nearly escaped me. I would have to start training again. If my level rose again, how much would I suffer from the side effects?

As I worried, Master brought up another topic instead of starting the training.

"And today, there is one more thing I must tell you, Ilmok."

"I will listen with washed ears."

"On the day this old man first met you… I have found those who ordered my pursuit and the assassination of that inn’s owner."

"!!!"

I was shocked as Master listed the masterminds of the incident.

"First, the one who found my trail and reported it to the Murim Alliance is the Sober Beggar of the Beggars' Gang Eight Absolute Elders. Second, the one who ordered my pursuit, dispatched the Alliance's martial artists, and requested support from surrounding sects is the Murim Alliance's Head Strategist Gongson Hyeon. Lastly, the one who killed the inn's owner and ordered poison put in my tea and food is the Murim Alliance's Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong."

Just from their positions, I knew they were all big shots. The only title that seemed somewhat manageable was the Flying Dragon Hall Director.

But the Demonic Cult’s intelligence network was even more surprising.

"To find everything in such detail… the Divine Cult’s intelligence network is truly amazing, Master."

"Hohoho. Our Divine Cult’s intelligence network is formidable, but the Murim Alliance actually helped us discover this."

"???"

"Didn’t I tell you that day? The Murim Alliance would pin the entire blame for this incident on me, and they did just that. They claimed the Beggars' Gang caught my trail and that the Head Strategist ordered my capture."

"……"

I almost asked if they were crazy.

*Why would they reveal that with their own mouths?*

But after hearing Master’s explanation, I understood why those bastards did it.

"Those bastards spread a rumor that I was trapped in their encirclement and near death, but escaped by taking the inn owner and a waiter hostage. They claim they were forced to let me go to save the hostages. They also say that after I escaped, I brutally tore the inn owner and waiter to death."

They had spread the rumor to pin the blame for the inn incident on Master.

"So, the Divine Cult found the masterminds based on those rumors. We tracked who from the Beggars' Gang caught my trail and who the Murim Alliance’s on-site commander was. It wasn’t difficult for our Divine Cult to find that the Sober Beggar and the Flying Dragon Hall Director were the masterminds."

Master finished his explanation and asked me with a solemn expression, "So, you must now decide. Will you take revenge with your own hands? Or will you leave it to me?"

"……"

I thought for a moment. Honestly, I was tempted to leave all three of them to Master.

However…

*Something feels off.*

I was suspicious of his motive for asking me specifically. It felt like a test.

This might have been a side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword making me consider every possibility, but I couldn’t shake the uneasy feeling.

On top of that…

*No matter what, I have to get revenge for Mr. Taehyun with my own hands.*

He was the benefactor who took care of me when I woke up in the body of a fourteen-year-old orphan. Since I couldn’t repay his kindness, I felt I had to at least avenge him.

"I’ll take my revenge with my own hands."

"Hoh. Are you saying you’ll deal with all three of them yourself?"

I shook my head. "I meant the Flying Dragon Hall Director, who was directly involved in Mr. Taehyun’s death. The Sober Beggar and the Head Strategist had some influence, but I believe only the Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong was directly involved."

Besides direct involvement, there was the issue of realism. How could I possibly kill the Head Strategist of the Murim Alliance? I thought it would be impossible unless the Demonic Cult and the Murim Alliance waged an all-out war that wiped out the Murim Alliance.

But if I said I was giving up on the Head Strategist because he was too difficult to kill, Master would be furious. So, I included the Sober Beggar and said I would only take revenge on the one directly involved in the murder.

"Hmm. You’ll only take revenge on the one directly involved in the murder. A wise judgment."

Master seemed satisfied with my answer. He stroked his beard and nodded.

"Then, I’ll leave the matter of the Flying Dragon Hall Director to you. However, your skills are not yet a match for him. They say ten years is a short time for a gentleman’s revenge, so you must devote yourself to martial arts for that purpose."

"I will do so."

I replied with a clasped hands greeting, but I had another thought.

*Somehow, it feels like I have another thing to do.*

I had planned to take it easy after entering the Demonic Academy, but now the Flying Dragon Hall Director was another goal.

*Seriously, this is the last time.*

Once I kill the Flying Dragon Hall Director and finish my revenge for Mr. Taehyun, I’m really going to slack off and enjoy life.

That night, the Heavenly Demon was attending to his duties in the Heavenly Demon Hall. He put down the letter he was reading and turned his head toward the moon outside the window. He looked east, toward the Murim Alliance.

*Hmm. To take revenge on the Flying Dragon Hall Director himself.*

He recalled his youngest disciple’s bold vow.

His youngest disciple’s talent was certainly outstanding. Even though idle chat took up considerable time today, his achievement had risen again after just under two hours of training.

*Perhaps his sadness over the inn owner is why his expression wasn’t bright, even after his achievement rose.*

This was a misunderstanding, as the Heavenly Demon did not know Ilmok’s inner thoughts.

Regardless of Ilmok’s inner thoughts, the Heavenly Demon had another reason for recalling the vow.

*I wonder if he’ll succeed in his revenge while this old man is still alive.*

The Heavenly Demon knew his own fate. He couldn’t put an exact number on it, but he knew he had less than ten years remaining.

The Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong was not comparable to his first disciple, Wi Jinhak, but he was a more skilled drummer than his third disciple, Seo Wanpyeong. For Ilmok to kill him, he would have to at least break through the wall of the Ultimate Demon Realm and advance several more stages.

If the man's skills improved during that time, Ilmok would have to raise his level even higher.

There was an even bigger problem. The Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong was the right-hand man of the Murim Alliance Head Strategist, Gongson Hyeon. He ranked somewhere between fourth and fifth within the Murim Alliance.

His level was high, and he never traveled alone. Unless he was on a very important mission, he always stayed inside the Murim Alliance.

In fact, the easiest of the three to assassinate was the Sober Beggar of the Beggars' Gang Eight Absolute Elders.

For Ilmok to kill the man, he had to meet one of two conditions.

First, he had to reach a level where he could break through everything, bodyguards included, and kill the Flying Dragon Hall Director. This meant mastering the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword to the Transcendent Demon Realm.

Or, after surpassing the Ultimate Demon Realm and advancing several more stages, he would have to set a trap so elaborate that even the Murim Alliance Head Strategist would be fooled, then lure the Flying Dragon Hall Director into it and kill him.

Knowing all this, the Heavenly Demon had no real intention of entrusting the revenge to Ilmok. He had revealed the masterminds’ identities only as a courtesy to Ilmok, the person involved, and had simply asked for his thoughts.

In other words, Ilmok had obsessively overanalyzed the Heavenly Demon’s thoughts and made a hasty assumption.

Unaware of these circumstances, the Heavenly Demon looked at the moon and smiled gently.

*Hohoho. Yes. What does it matter after this old man is gone? They say ten years is a short time for a gentleman’s revenge.*

The Heavenly Demon simply found it admirable that his youngest disciple had vowed to kill such a big shot himself.
