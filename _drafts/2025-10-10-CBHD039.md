After the noisy break time passed and everyone left, Master visited the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Last evening, I noticed your progress with the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword is advancing rapidly, Ilmok."

"It's all thanks to you, Master."

A reply filled with contradictory emotions slipped out. Outwardly, I acted polite and grateful.

*This damn demonic art.*

My obsessive compulsion had worsened, making the situation troubling.

"Did you devote yourself to the sword today as well?"

"Well…" I considered Master's question for a moment, then answered with some honesty.

"My level has risen so suddenly that the side effects have intensified, so I'm holding back for a while."

I had said everything except for the curses.

"......"

I expected him to be disappointed. Instead, Master stroked his beard, his eyes shining.

"I was about to bring that up myself. It's truly commendable that you're being cautious on your own."

"???"

"Unlike the martial arts of heathens, demonic art boasts rapid achievement. The level is beyond comparison. Do you know how long it takes for heathens to become a pinnacle-level expert martial artist who can freely handle sword qi or fist qi by circulating it through the Conception and Governing Vessels?"

The conversation shifted to this unexpected topic. I thought for a moment before answering.

"…I'm not sure."

"It varies greatly depending on talent or miracle herbs from a miraculous encounter. Those with some talent can generally reach the pinnacle level in their middle age. In the vast Central Plains, only a few 'geniuses' who have also consumed miracle herbs can enter the pinnacle level in their twenties. Now, what do you think it's like for demonic art?"

"Hmm… If orthodox martial arts take that long, wouldn't demonic art take about ten years?"

"Hohohoho. You're somewhat correct. It depends on talent, but it generally takes five to ten years to reach what the heathens call the pinnacle level."

"F-five years, you say?"

"Hohoho, and that's just the average time. Your Eldest Senior Brother, Jinhak, took a mere three years. He could already handle sword qi freely by the time he entered the Demonic Academy."

He spoke with pride, then stopped laughing and his expression turned serious.

"However, there's one major problem with this demonic art. Ilmok, you must be feeling it as well, aren't you?"

"Isn't it the side effect that makes your mind go strange?"

"That's correct. That's why it's very dangerous to become engrossed in gaining power. You should raise your achievements quickly, but it's important to stop when the side effects become unbearable. You must wait until you can control your mind to some extent."

After hearing the explanation, I understood roughly how the Demonic Cult's martial artists practiced demonic art.

They used demonic art's strengths to rapidly increase their achievements, stopping only when the mental illness went too far. They would wait until they thought, 'I think I can handle it now,' then devote themselves to training again. They repeated this process, stopping whenever the mental illness became severe. When they reached a point where they thought, 'Ah! I really can't go any further,' they would settle in and stop progressing.

In other words, the martial artists and Branch Managers I saw on my business trip were people who had stopped at a certain level. That explained why their levels were lower than the much younger Jin Hayeon, despite practicing fast-growing demonic art and reaching middle age.

They built their skills on a precarious tightrope. The moment they failed, they suffered qi deviation or went berserk. When that happened, nearby demonic practitioners would subdue them, just like with Eldest Senior Brother's rampage.

Once my thoughts reached that point, an idea came to mind. Demonic art might be for those who lacked talent but possessed strong mental fortitude.

It offered fraudulent growth speed, allowing one to reach the pinnacle in ten years without talent. Anyone could become a expert martial artist with enough effort. For those with a tenacious spirit but no talent, what martial art could be more perfect?

Conversely, it was a martial art that would unleash a terrible disaster if practiced by someone with overflowing talent but the mental fortitude of a sunfish.

From that perspective...

*Damn it. That means it's the worst possible match for me.*

My body boasted absurd martial arts talent, so much that even Master coveted it. Master had even performed meridian cleansing and purification, making it the perfect vessel for martial arts. If I added fast-growing demonic art to that, my level might rise every time I trained. The problem was my mental strength.

*Am I mentally strong?*

My life goal was a work-life balance of moderate work and moderate play.

While the word 'doomed' floated endlessly in my mind, Master's voice pierced my ears.

"However, there's no need to be too afraid of the side effects. Once you pass the Ultimate Demon Realm and enter the Transcendent Demon Realm, all problems will be solved. Besides, if you avoid training out of fear, what's the point of learning the fast-achieving demonic art?"

*It's not like I learned this demonic art because I wanted to, you know?*

The words stuck in my throat, but I held them back with my last bit of patience.

"…I understand."

A sigh almost escaped me. I would have to start training again. If my level rose again, how much would I suffer from the side effects?

While I was lost in these worries, Master brought up another topic instead of starting the training.

"And today, there's one more thing I must tell you, Ilmok."

"I will listen with washed ears."

"Regarding the day this old man first met you. I've found those who ordered my pursuit and the assassination of that inn's owner."

"!!!"

I wore a shocked expression as Master listed the masterminds one by one.

"First, the Sober Beggar of the Beggars' Gang Eight Absolute Elders found my trail and reported it to the Murim Alliance. Second, the Murim Alliance's Head Strategist Gongson Hyeon ordered my pursuit, dispatched the Alliance's martial artists, and requested support from surrounding sects. Lastly, the Murim Alliance's Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong killed the inn's owner and ordered poison put in my food and tea."

Just hearing their positions, I knew they were all big shots. The only title that seemed somewhat manageable was the last one, the Flying Dragon Hall Director.

However, the Demonic Cult's intelligence network was even more surprising.

"To find everything in such detail. The Divine Cult's intelligence network is truly amazing, Master."

"Hohoho. While our Divine Cult's intelligence network is formidable, the Murim Alliance actually helped us discover this."

"???"

"Didn't I tell you that day? The Murim Alliance would pin the blame for this incident entirely on me, and they did just that. They claimed the Beggars' Gang caught my trail and that the Head Strategist ordered my capture."

"……"

I almost asked if they were crazy.

*Why would they reveal that with their own mouths?*

After hearing Master's explanation, I had a rough idea why those bastards did it.

"Those bastards spread a rumor that I was trapped in their encirclement and near death, but escaped by taking the inn owner and a waiter hostage. They claim they were forced to let me go to save the hostages. They also say that after I escaped, I brutally tore the inn owner and waiter to death."

They had spread the rumor to pin the blame for the inn incident on Master.

"Therefore, the Divine Cult found the masterminds based on their own rumors. We tracked who from the Beggars' Gang caught my trail and who the on-site commander was. It wasn't difficult for our Divine Cult to find that the Sober Beggar and the Flying Dragon Hall Director were the masterminds."

After finishing his explanation, Master asked me with a solemn expression.

"So, you must decide. Will you take revenge with your own hands? Or will you leave it to me?"

"……"

I was lost in thought. Honestly, I was tempted to leave all three of them to Master.

However.

*Something feels off.*

I was suspicious of his motive for asking me specifically. It felt like a test.

It might have just been the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword's side effects making me consider every possibility, but I couldn't shake the uneasy feeling.

On top of that...

*No matter what, I have to get revenge for Mr. Taehyun with my own hands.*

He was the benefactor who took care of me when I suddenly woke up in the body of a fourteen-year-old orphan. Since I couldn't repay his kindness, I felt I should at least avenge him.

"I'll take my revenge with my own hands."

"Hoh. Are you saying you'll deal with all three of them yourself?"

I shook my head at Master's question.

"I was referring to the Flying Dragon Hall Director, who was directly involved in Mr. Taehyun's death. The Sober Beggar and the Head Strategist may have had some influence, but I believe only the Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong was directly involved."

It was about direct involvement, but also realism. How could I possibly kill the Head Strategist of the Murim Alliance? I thought it would be impossible unless the Demonic Cult and the Murim Alliance waged an all-out war and wiped them out completely.

However, Master would likely be furious if I said I was giving up on the Head Strategist because he was too difficult. So, I included the Sober Beggar and said, 'I'll only take revenge on the one directly involved in the murder.'

"Hmm. You'll only take revenge on the one directly involved in the murder. A wise judgment."

Seemingly satisfied, Master stroked his beard and nodded.

"Then, I'll leave the matter of the Flying Dragon Hall Director to you. However, your skills are not yet a match for him. They say ten years is a short time for a gentleman's revenge, so you must devote yourself to martial arts for that purpose."

"I will do so."

I replied with a clasped hands greeting, but I had another thought.

*Somehow, it feels like I have another thing to do.*

I had planned to take it easy after entering the Demonic Academy, but now the Flying Dragon Hall Director had appeared as another goal.

*Seriously, this is the last time.*

Once I kill the Flying Dragon Hall Director and finish my revenge for Mr. Taehyun, I'm really going to slack off and enjoy life.

That night, the Heavenly Demon was in the Heavenly Demon Hall attending to his duties. He put down the letter he was reading and turned his head toward the moon outside the window. He looked east, toward the Murim Alliance.

*Hmm. To take revenge on the Flying Dragon Hall Director himself.*

He recalled his youngest disciple's bold vow.

His youngest disciple's talent was certainly outstanding. Even with considerable time spent on idle chat today, his achievement had risen again after just under two hours of training.

*Perhaps because of his sadness over the inn owner, his expression wasn't bright even after his achievement rose.*

This was a misunderstanding, as the Heavenly Demon did not know Ilmok's inner thoughts.

Regardless of Ilmok's inner thoughts, the Heavenly Demon recalled the vow for another reason.

*I wonder if he will succeed in his revenge while I'm still alive.*

The Heavenly Demon knew this himself. He couldn't put an exact number on it, but he knew he had less than ten years left.

The Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong was not comparable to his first disciple, Wi Jinhak, but he was a more skilled expert martial artist than his third disciple, Seo Wanpyeong. For Ilmok to kill him, he would have to at least break through the Ultimate Demon Realm and advance several more stages.

Furthermore, if the man's skills improved while Ilmok was reaching the Ultimate Demon Realm, Ilmok would have to raise his level even higher.

There was an even bigger problem. The Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong was the right-hand man of the Murim Alliance Head Strategist, Gongson Hyeon. Within the Murim Alliance, he ranked somewhere between fourth and fifth.

Not only was his level high, but he never traveled alone. Unless he was on a very important mission, he always stayed inside the Murim Alliance.

In fact, the easiest of the three to assassinate was the Sober Beggar of the Beggars' Gang Eight Absolute Elders.

For Ilmok to kill the man, he had to meet one of two conditions. He had to reach a level where he could break through everything, bodyguards included, and kill the Flying Dragon Hall Director. In other words, he had to master the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword to the Transcendent Demon Realm. Or, after surpassing the Ultimate Demon Realm and advancing several stages, he would have to set a trap so elaborate that even the Murim Alliance Head Strategist would be fooled, lure the Flying Dragon Hall Director into it, and kill him.

Knowing all this, the Heavenly Demon had no real intention of entrusting the revenge to Ilmok. He had merely revealed the masterminds' identities to Ilmok as a courtesy and asked for his thoughts.

In other words, Ilmok had obsessively overanalyzed the Heavenly Demon's inner thoughts and made a hasty assumption.

Unaware of these circumstances, the Heavenly Demon suddenly looked at the moon and smiled gently.

*Hohoho. Yes. What does it matter after I'm gone? They say ten years is a short time for a gentleman's revenge.*

The Heavenly Demon simply found it admirable that his youngest disciple had vowed to kill such a big shot himself.
