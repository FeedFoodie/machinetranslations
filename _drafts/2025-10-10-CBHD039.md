Chapter 39: Assassination (1)

When the noisy break time had passed and everyone had left, Master came to visit the Wind Cliff Pavilion.
"Last evening, I noticed that your progress with the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword seems to be advancing rapidly, Ilmok."
"It is all thanks to you, Master."
A reply mixed with contradictory emotions slipped out.
Of course, on the outside, I was acting polite and grateful.
*This damn demonic art.*
My obsessive compulsion had worsened, making the situation troubling in many ways.
"Did you devote yourself to the sword today as well?"
"Well..."
After pondering Master's question for a moment, I answered with some honesty.
"Perhaps because my level has risen so suddenly, the side effects have also intensified, so I'm holding back for a while."
It meant I had said everything except for the curses.
"Oho."
But for some reason...
I expected him to be disappointed, but for some reason, Master stroked his beard, his eyes shining.
"I was just about to bring that up myself. It is truly commendable that you are being cautious on your own."
"???"
"Unlike the martial arts practiced by heathens, demonic art boasts rapid achievement. The level is beyond comparison. Do you know how long it takes for heathens to become a pinnacle-level drummer who can freely handle sword qi or fist qi by circulating it through the Conception and Governing Vessels?"
As the conversation shifted to this unexpected topic, I thought for a moment before answering.
"…I'm not sure."
"It varies greatly depending on one's level of talent or the miracle herbs obtained through a miraculous encounter, but those with a certain degree of talent can generally reach the pinnacle level in their middle age. In the vast Central Plains, only a very small number of ‘geniuses’ who have also consumed miracle herbs can enter the pinnacle level in their twenties. Now, what do you think it's like for demonic art?"
"Hmm... If orthodox martial arts take that long, wouldn't demonic art take about ten years?"
"Hohohoho. You're somewhat correct. It depends on talent, but it generally takes between five to ten years to reach what the heathens call the pinnacle level."
"F-five years, you say?"
"Hohoho. And that's just the average time. In the case of your Eldest Senior Brother, Jinhak, it took him a mere three years. By the time he entered the Demonic Academy, he could already handle sword qi freely."
After saying so in a tone filled with pride, Master stopped laughing and suddenly returned to a serious expression.
"However, there is one major problem with this demonic art. Ilmok, you must be feeling it as well, are you not?"
"Isn't it the side effect that makes your mind go strange?"
"That is correct. That is why it is very dangerous to become engrossed in gaining power itself. It means that while you should raise your achievements quickly, it is important to stop for a while when you feel the side effects are becoming unbearable. At least until you can control your mind to some extent."
After hearing the explanation, I got a rough idea of how the martial artists of the Demonic Cult practice demonic art.
They take advantage of the strengths of demonic art to rapidly increase their achievements, and when their mental illness starts to go too far, they stop.
Then they watch and wait, and if they think, ‘I think I can handle it now,’ they devote themselves even more to training to raise their level.
They repeat this process of stopping when the mental illness becomes severe again.
Then, at the point where they think, ‘Ah! I really can't go any further,’ they seem to completely settle in and stop progressing.
In other words, the martial artists and Branch Managers of the Branch I saw when I was on my business trip were people who had stopped at a certain level. That's why, despite practicing the fast-growing demonic art and reaching middle age, their levels were lower than the much younger Jin Hayeon.
They build their skills through such a precarious tightrope walk, and the moment they fail, they suffer from qi deviation or go berserk.
And when they go berserk, it seemed that nearby demonic practitioners would step in to subdue them. Just like with Eldest Senior Brother's rampage last time.
Once my thoughts reached that point, a certain idea naturally came to mind.
The thought that demonic art might be a martial art for those who lack talent but possess strong mental fortitude.
A fraudulent growth speed that allows one to reach the pinnacle after ten years of training, even without talent in martial arts.
Anyone can become a drummer if they just put in the effort. For those who lack talent but have a tenacious spirit, what other martial art could be more perfect?
Conversely, it was a martial art that would unleash a terrible disaster if practiced by those with overflowing talent but the mental fortitude of a sunfish.
From that perspective.
*Damn it. That means it's the worst possible match for me.*
A body that boasts such absurd talent for martial arts that even Master covets it.
Moreover, since Master even performed meridian cleansing and purification, it had become the perfect body for practicing martial arts.
If the fast-growing demonic art was added to that, my level might rise every time I trained.
The problem was my mental strength.
*Am I mentally strong?*
Me, who made a work-life balance of working moderately and playing moderately my goal in life?
While the word ‘doomed’ endlessly floated in my mind, Master's voice pierced my ears.
"However, there is no need to be too afraid of the side effects. After all, once you pass the Ultimate Demon Realm and enter the Transcendent Demon Realm, all problems will be solved. Furthermore, if you avoid training out of fear, what would be the point of learning the fast-achieving demonic art?"
*It's not like I learned this demonic art because I wanted to, you know?*
The words were stuck in my throat, itching to come out, but I held them back with my last bit of patience.
"…I understand."
A sigh felt like it would escape me at any moment. Because I would have to start training again from now on.
If my level rises again from here, how much will I suffer from the side effects?
While I was lost in such worries, for some reason, Master brought up another topic instead of immediately starting the training.
"And today, there is one more thing I must tell you, Ilmok."
"I will listen with washed ears."
"The day this old man first met you. I have found those who ordered the pursuit of this old man and those who ordered the assassination of that inn's owner."
"!!!"
While I wore a shocked expression, Master listed the masterminds of that incident one by one.
"First, the one who found this old man's trail and reported it to the Murim Alliance is the Sober Beggar of the Beggars' Gang Eight Absolute Elders. Second, the one who ordered the pursuit of this old man, dispatched the Alliance's martial artists, and requested support from surrounding sects is the Murim Alliance's Head Strategist Gongson Hyeon. Lastly, the one who killed the inn's owner on site and ordered poison to be put in this old man's tea and food is the Murim Alliance's the Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong."
Just hearing their positions, they were all big shots. The only title that seemed somewhat manageable was the last one, the Flying Dragon Hall Director.
However, what was even more surprising was the intelligence network of the Demonic Cult.
"To find everything in such detail. The intelligence network of the Divine Cult is truly amazing, Master."
"Hohoho. While our Divine Cult's intelligence network is indeed formidable, this was actually discovered because of the Murim Alliance."
"???"
"Did I not tell you that day? That the Murim Alliance would pin the blame for this incident entirely on this old man. And indeed, the Murim Alliance did just that. They claimed that the Beggars' Gang caught this old man's trail, and that the Head Strategist ordered my capture."
"……"
I almost asked if they were crazy without realizing it.
*Why would they reveal that with their own mouths?*
However, after hearing Master's subsequent explanation, I had a rough idea of why those bastards did it.
"Those bastards spread a rumor that this old man was trapped in their encirclement and on the verge of death, but escaped by taking the inn owner and a waiter, who were ordinary commoners, hostage at a nearby inn and threatening them. They are spreading rumors that they were forced to let this old man go because they were trying to save the hostages, and that unfortunately, the inn owner and waiter who were taken hostage were brutally torn to death by this old man's hands after he escaped the encirclement."
It meant they had spread such a rumor to pin the blame for what happened at the inn on Master.
"Therefore, the Divine Cult found the masterminds based on the rumors they spread. Who from the Beggars' Gang caught this old man's trail, and who the on-site commander sent by the Murim Alliance was that day. Tracking them based on the rumors they themselves created, it was not a difficult task for our Divine Cult to find out that the Sober Beggar and the Flying Dragon Hall Director were the masterminds."
Having finished his explanation, Master asked me with a solemn expression.
"So, now you must decide. Will you take revenge with your own hands? Or will you leave it to this old man?"
"……"
I was lost in thought for a moment.
Honestly, the thought of just leaving all three of them to Master was strong.
However.
*Something feels off.*
How should I put it? I was suspicious of his motive for specifically asking me. It felt as if he was testing me.
It might have just been a possibility that came to mind from considering every what-if scenario due to the side effects of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, but I couldn't shake the uneasy feeling.
On top of that.
*No matter what, I have to get revenge for Mr. Taehyun with my own hands.*
He was the benefactor who took care of me when I suddenly woke up in the body of a fourteen-year-old orphan. Since I couldn't repay his kindness, I felt I should at least avenge him.
"I will take my revenge with my own hands."
"Hoh. Are you saying you will deal with all three of them yourself?"
I shook my head at Master's question.
"I was referring to the Flying Dragon Hall Director, who was directly involved in Mr. Taehyun's death. The Sober Beggar and the Head Strategist may have had some influence, but I believe the only one directly involved in Mr. Taehyun's death is the Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong."
There was the matter of direct involvement, but honestly, there was also the issue of realism.
By what means could I possibly kill the Head Strategist of the Murim Alliance?
I thought it would be impossible unless the Demonic Cult and the Murim Alliance waged an all-out war and completely wiped out the Murim Alliance.
However, if I answered that I was giving up on the Head Strategist because he was too difficult to kill, Master would likely be furious. So, I appropriately included the Sober Beggar and said, ‘I will only take revenge on the one who was directly involved in the murder.’
"Hmm. You will only take revenge on the one directly involved in the murder. A wise judgment."
Seemingly satisfied with my answer, Master stroked his beard and nodded.
"Then, I shall leave the matter of the Flying Dragon Hall Director to you. However, your skills are not yet a match for the Flying Dragon Hall Director. They say ten years is a short time for a gentleman's revenge, so you must devote yourself to martial arts for the sake of that revenge."
"I will do so."
I replied to Master with a clasped hands greeting, but on the other hand, I had another thought.
*Somehow, it feels like I have another thing to do.*
I was planning to stop and take it easy after entering the Demonic Academy, but now another goal called the Flying Dragon Hall Director has appeared.
*Seriously, this is the last time.*
Once I kill the Flying Dragon Hall Director and finish my revenge for Mr. Taehyun.
From then on, I'm really going to slack off and enjoy life.

That night.
In the Heavenly Demon Hall, the Heavenly Demon, who was attending to his duties, put down the letter he was reading and turned his head toward the moon hanging beyond the window.
It was to the east, where the Murim Alliance was located.
*Hmm. To take revenge on the Flying Dragon Hall Director himself.*
He was recalling the bold vow of his youngest disciple.
Certainly, his youngest disciple's talent was outstanding.
Even though a considerable amount of time was taken up by idle chat today, his achievement had risen again with just a little under two hours of training.
*Perhaps because of his sadness over the inn owner, his expression wasn't bright even after his achievement rose.*
It was a misunderstanding by the Heavenly Demon, who did not know Ilmok's inner thoughts.
Regardless of Ilmok's inner thoughts, there was another reason why the Heavenly Demon was recalling Ilmok's vow.
*I wonder if he will succeed in his revenge while this old man is still alive.*
The Heavenly Demon himself knew it. Although he couldn't express it in exact numbers, he knew that his own fate had less than ten years remaining.
And, the Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong was not comparable to his first disciple, Wi Jinhak, but he was a more skilled drummer than his third disciple, Seo Wanpyeong.
In other words, for Ilmok to kill him, he would have to ‘at least’ break through the wall of the Ultimate Demon Realm and advance several more stages.
Furthermore, if the man's skills improved in the time it took Ilmok to reach the Ultimate Demon Realm, Ilmok would have to raise his level even higher.
However, there was an even bigger problem.
The Flying Dragon Hall Director Jeongyeong was the right-hand man of the Murim Alliance Head Strategist, Gongson Hyeon.
If you were to rank him within the Murim Alliance, he would be somewhere between the fourth and fifth positions.
Not only was his level high, but he never traveled alone in the first place, and unless he was on a very important mission, he was always holed up in the Murim Alliance.
In fact, among the three, the easiest one to assassinate was the Sober Beggar of the Beggars' Gang Eight Absolute Elders.
In any case, for Ilmok to kill the man, he had to meet one of two conditions.
He had to be at a level where he could break through everything, bodyguard or not, and kill the Flying Dragon Hall Director. In other words, he had to at least master the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword to the level of the Transcendent Demon Realm.
Or, after surpassing the Ultimate Demon Realm and advancing several more stages, he would have to set a trap so elaborate that even the Murim Alliance Head Strategist would be fooled, lure the Flying Dragon Hall Director into it, and kill him.
Knowing all these facts, the Heavenly Demon had no particular intention of entrusting the revenge to Ilmok.
He had merely revealed the identities of the masterminds to Ilmok, the person involved, as a minimal courtesy and asked for his thoughts.
In other words, Ilmok had obsessively overanalyzed the Heavenly Demon's inner thoughts and simply made a hasty assumption.
However, unaware of these circumstances, the Heavenly Demon suddenly looked at the moon and smiled gently.
*Hohoho. Yes. What does it matter after this old man is gone? They say ten years is a short time for a gentleman's revenge.*
The Heavenly Demon simply found his youngest disciple, who had vowed to kill such a big shot himself, to be admirable.
