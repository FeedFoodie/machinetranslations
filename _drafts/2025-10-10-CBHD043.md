*Is it said that most of the world's pleasures happen at night?*
This quiet and holy night.
*I can't... sleep!*
I was spending the night with my eyes wide open. The reason was simple.

I could see an unrealistically beautiful face just by slightly turning my head.
*No. It's not because of her face!*
Although it was long past my bedtime, I shook my head violently.

*Jin Hayeon might be making a hand seal. The moment I fall asleep, she might stab me in the heart with an indifferent expression!*
Right. It was because of anxiety. The side effects of the demonic art and the added threat of assassination must have brought on insomnia.

*That must be why my heart is pounding like this. Yes.*
Even as I tossed and turned, brainwashing myself.
SLIGHTLY.
I couldn't help but unconsciously turn my head. I looked at the face of Jin Hayeon, who slept soundly across from me.

*Wow... How can a person look like that?*
Usually, her psychopathic attitude made my affection for her drop day by day, but just watching her lie still felt like looking at a doll. It was a doll crafted with care, stitch by stitch, by a master artisan.

I dazedly watched that doll illuminated by the moonlight.
"......"
Suddenly, the doll opened its eyes.

"Heek!?"
I let out a strange scream, feeling like a child caught doing something wrong.
However, Jin Hayeon ignored my reaction and opened her mouth. Her tone was characteristically calm.

"Are you unable to sleep, Young Lord?"
"Ahem. I, I'm a bit of a light sleeper. Since this is different from usual, I'm having trouble falling asleep."
Even I thought it was an excellent improvisation, but Jin Hayeon slightly furrowed her beautiful brows.

"For someone like that, I recall you slept quite well during the last expedition, though?"
This is why sharp children are so tiring.
Was it fatigue suddenly washing over me? Or was it her unique way of speaking, polite yet devoid of emotion? My heart, which had been racing since I got into bed, rapidly regained its composure.

"Ahem. I'm suddenly feeling sleepy. You should get some rest too, Miss Jin."
"I am already quite comfortable."
"I, I see."
Thanks to the sudden drain of energy, I fell into a deep sleep right then and there.

{sep}

"Hwaaam."
Fatigue washed over me the moment I opened my eyes, perhaps because my pounding heart kept me up so late. I stretched my stiff body and looked to the side. The bedding Jin Hayeon had used was already neatly put away.

"I've brought breakfast, Young Lord."
The door opened as if she had sensed me waking up. She entered the room carrying a tray of food.

*This makes us seem just like a married couple.*
I entertained the horrifying thought for a moment, then quickly shook my head.
Right, that's a trap. The moment I fall for that face, a terrible future where I suffocate day after day will unfold.

CHOMP CHOMP.
For a short while, I shoved the breakfast she had brought into my mouth with my tired body.
She opened her mouth around the time I had more or less finished my meal.

"Your rising time was about two hours later than usual, so after I clear the table, we will begin the morning studies immediately, Young Lord."
"……"
As expected, she was a woman with a talent for making one feel suffocated.

After spending the day as usual, through morning studies and lunch, visitors began to arrive one by one, just like any other day. However, it felt somewhat empty compared to usual.

"Oppa!"
Seon'ah, who always visited, was here again today. She pressed herself close to my side, rubbing her chubby cheeks against me and giggling, but almost no one else came. Eldest Senior Brother and Hang Geon didn't come, and neither did Sixth Senior Brother, who suffers from auditory and visual hallucinations. I knew that Third Senior Brother, who suffers from paranoia, had left on a mission.

Instead, Guyang Mun, who had returned to the Main Headquarters after completing his mission, was filling the empty space all by himself.
"Hahahaha! It seems rather quiet today for some reason, Young Lord!"
He was chattering on endlessly by himself.

I wonder how long I spent letting Guyang Mun's nonsense go in one ear and out the other, while playfully twisting cute Seon'ah's hair like a toy.
*It's still peaceful for now.*
A yawn escaped my lips unconsciously when it occurred to me that neither Jin Hayeon nor the two bodyguard martial artists were making any particular moves.

"Haam. Ah, my apologies, Master Guyang."
I quickly offered my apology since yawning while someone else was talking was rude, but Guyang Mun laughed heartily as if he didn't mind.

"Hahaha. It's alright, Young Lord! But it seems you're very tired."
"I didn't sleep well yesterday, so I suppose I'm a bit tired."
"Well, after what happened recently, it's understandable that you couldn't sleep."
"No, well, it's not necessarily because of that."
I tried to brush it off appropriately since people involved in that assassination incident were nearby.

However, Guyang Mun seemed unable to do so. He furtively glanced at Jin Hayeon before speaking.
"I heard the story. That you, Young Lord, spoke to the Heavenly Demon and asked for leniency for Hayeon. Purely as a Demonic Academy class!mate!, I am just offering my thanks on Hayeon's behalf. Hahaha!"

It seemed he had taken my advice in a strange way. I told him to show a moderate amount of goodwill, but he was spouting unnecessary words, needlessly attaching the word 'classmate' to everything.

"We're just classmates, so why are you thanking the Young Lord on my behalf?"
As the one-sided lover's quarrel was about to start again, my eyes and the eyes of the little kitten rolling around beside me lit up. It was our only source of entertainment.
However, I didn't know that this entertainment would come back to me like a bomb.

"Ahem. Still, if... if you're classmates, isn't that something you can do?"
"It's needless meddling. I'm reflecting on my own without you interfering. That's why, starting yesterday, I've been sleeping in the same room as the Young Lord, continuing my bodyguard duties even during his sleeping hours."

"!?"
As soon as Jin Hayeon finished speaking, Guyang Mun's eyes widened as if they would tear. He stared back and forth between Jin Hayeon and me with his jaw agape.
The little kid next to me also looked back and forth between Jin Hayeon and me, but her reaction was strangely different.

"Hmph. I want to play with Oppa at night too...."
The little girl pouted her lips slightly and muttered as if in envy.
Unlike that innocent child.

"Wh-wh-what. Wh-what are you talking about?"
Guyang Mun, who had momentarily broken down, was on the verge of having a seizure.
And unlike the child, Guyang Mun's impure imagination went one step further.

"C-come to think of it, a little while ago, the Young Lord said he was t-tired.... D-don't tell me!?"
As Guyang Mun continued to speak, Jin Hayeon's expression changed. She looked at him like he was an insect.
However, Guyang Mun, who had already lost his mind, failed to notice her gaze.

"Master! How could you do this to me, Master!"
"Ahem. Get a grip, Master Guyang, and why am I your master?"
"Didn't I say I would serve you as my master then! Don't tell me all the advice you gave me back then was a lie!?"

Somehow, it felt like Guyang Mun's romantic endeavors would be completely ruined if I left him like this. No, it already felt like they had crossed the river Styx, but I felt I should at least help them remain as 'classmates'.

"Get a grip. We were only in the same room for pro!tec!tion! Nothing happened."
"I-is that true?"
"It is."
I met Guyang Mun's suspicious eyes with my own, which were completely shameless.
Was it because of my incredibly confident gaze? Guyang Mun's constantly wavering eyes gradually found their stability.

And Guyang Mun, having cast aside his suspicion, seemed to ponder something for a moment. He suddenly opened his mouth in a determined tone.
"Young Lord."
"Speak."
"Please accept me as your bodyguard martial artist."

"What kind of bullsh... no, what nonsense is this? Aren't you part of the White Turtle Brigade, Master Guyang?"
"If you just give your permission, Young Lord, I will go and submit my withdrawal application immediately. If I, as your bodyguard, share a room with you, Young Lord, then there's no need for Hayeon to go out of her way to sleep with you, is there?"

The suspicion was gone, and his eyes were filled with madness. As expected, this guy was also a madman worthy of being a demonic cultist.
*I thought the side effect might be mania, but now I see it's obsession?*
However, the diagnosis of this madman wasn't what was important right now.

"Is it that easy to transfer your affiliation within the Cult? Get a grip and focus on your own work."
Sleeping in the same room with a crazy man who clearly has stalker tendencies. I absolutely hated the idea.
Was it because I had rejected him flatly?

GRAB.
This madman finally knelt down and began to cling to my trouser leg.
"P-please, I beg you, Young Lord! I don't care if you work me to the bone, please appoint me as your bodyguard martial artist!"
Watching his desperate appearance, a thought naturally came to mind.

*How pathetic, Guyang Mun.*
No matter how much they say pride doesn't matter in love, this was just too pathetic.

{sep}

Guyang Mun had been showing the depths of human patheticness. He belatedly witnessed Jin Hayeon's gaze looking down on him like an insect and ran out of the Wind Cliff Pavilion.
After time passed and the break was over, I spent my time as usual. I either had a duel with Jin Hayeon or trained with my Master.

Even more time passed, and Jin Hayeon was preparing to perform Body Purification on me.
"Perhaps the training was a bit excessive today, I'm feeling a little hungry."
I muttered, rubbing my stomach.

"I will bring a late-night snack from the teahouse."
As Jin Hayeon was preparing to leave the Wind Cliff Pavilion, Gwan Muyeol, who stood guard on one side of the pavilion, interjected.

"Miss Jin, please proceed with the Body Purification. I will go to the teahouse."
"It's alright, Martial Artist Gwan."
When Jin Hayeon refused, Gwan Muyeol replied in a serious tone.

"I'm the one who's alright. Besides, even if I leave my post, Jang Hoe will be here, and don't you have a lot to do, Miss Jin?"
In the end, Jin Hayeon had no choice but to nod at Gwan Muyeol's firm, yet not-quite-forced, insistence.

"Then I'll be in your care."
As soon as Jin Hayeon finished speaking, Gwan Muyeol left the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Jin Hayeon, with an emotionless face, turned and began to touch my body.
I felt the cold energy transmitting from Jin Hayeon's hands.

*What's this? Was that guy interested in Miss Jin too?*
Until now, neither Gwan Muyeol nor Jang Hoe had ever stepped forward to help Jin Hayeon while she attended to my various needs. It was strange that he was suddenly showing goodwill to Jin Hayeon, perhaps because of Guyang Mun's obnoxious behavior today.

*Hmm. If this goes well, I might have another interesting spectacle to watch.*
The first picture that came to my mind was a love triangle fight between Gwan Muyeol and Guyang Mun over Jin Hayeon, and knowing Jin Hayeon, she would surely look at the two of them as if they were insects.

{sep}

Meanwhile.
Gwan Muyeol, the victim of the strange fantasy I was imagining, moved quickly and arrived at the teahouse.
"What can I prepare for you?"
"I'd like some Longjing tea and a few simple snacks to go with it."
Gwan Muyeol replied in a blunt tone to the woman on duty at the teahouse.

The woman drew hot water from the stove, which had been kept on a low fire throughout her shift. She brewed the tea, then placed a few pre-prepared late-night refreshments on a plate and served them.
Gwan Muyeol then placed the cup, in which the tea leaves were just beginning to steep, and the snacks on a tray and stood up.

"You're not having it here?"
"I'm here on an errand."
At Gwan Muyeol's answer, the woman didn't ask any more questions and bowed her head.

"Take care on your way."
Gwan Muyeol nodded and left the teahouse. However, instead of heading straight for the Wind Cliff Pavilion, he ducked into a dark side alley along the way.
Gwan Muyeol scanned his surroundings with anxious eyes, then let out a sigh of relief. He rummaged inside his clothes and pulled out a small leather pouch.

SRRK.
He untied the string binding the leather pouch. Inside was a mushroom with a vibrant pattern that looked like a poisonous mushroom.
Gwan Muyeol then pinched off the tip of the mushroom with his thumb and forefinger. He infused it with his inner arts to turn it into powder and mixed it into the Longjing tea.
