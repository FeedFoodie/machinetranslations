People say that most of the world's pleasures happen at night. On this quiet and holy night, I was wide awake with my eyes open.

*I can't... sleep!*

The reason was simple. I only had to slightly turn my head to see an unrealistically beautiful face. I shook my head violently, even though it was long past my bedtime.

*No. It's not because of her face!*

*Jin Hayeon might be making a hand seal. The moment I fall asleep, she might stab me in the heart with an indifferent expression!*

Right. It was because of anxiety. The side effects of the demonic art and the added threat of assassination must have caused my insomnia. I tossed and turned, trying to brainwash myself.

*That must be why my heart is pounding like this. Yes.*

SLIGHTLY.

I couldn't stop myself from unconsciously turning my head. I looked at the face of Jin Hayeon, who slept soundly across from me.

*Wow... How can a person look like that?*

Her psychopathic attitude usually made my affection for her drop daily, but watching her lie still was like looking at a doll, one crafted stitch by stitch by a master artisan. I was dazedly watching that doll illuminated by the moonlight.

"......"

The doll suddenly opened its eyes.

"Heek!?"

I let out a strange, involuntary scream, feeling like a child caught doing something wrong. Jin Hayeon ignored my reaction and spoke in her characteristically calm tone.

"Are you unable to sleep, Young Lord?"

"Ahem. I-I'm a bit of a light sleeper. This is different from usual, so I'm having trouble falling asleep."

I thought it was an excellent improvisation, but Jin Hayeon slightly furrowed her beautiful brows.

"For a light sleeper, I recall you slept quite well during the last expedition, though?"

This was why sharp children were so tiring. Maybe fatigue suddenly washed over me, or maybe it was her polite yet emotionless way of speaking. My heart, which had been racing since I got into bed, rapidly calmed down.

"Ahem. I'm suddenly feeling sleepy. You should get some rest too, Miss Jin."

"I am already quite comfortable."

"I, I see."

The sudden energy drain made me fall into a deep sleep right then.

{sep}

"Hwaaam."

Fatigue washed over me the moment I opened my eyes, probably because my pounding heart kept me up so late. I stretched my stiff body and looked to the side. The bedding Jin Hayeon had used was already neatly put away.

"I've brought breakfast, Young Lord."

The door opened as if she had sensed me waking. She entered carrying a food tray.

*This makes us seem just like a married couple.*

I entertained the horrifying thought for a moment, then quickly shook my head. Right, that was a trap. The moment I fell for that face, a terrible future of daily suffocation would unfold.

CHOMP CHOMP.

I tiredly shoved the breakfast she brought into my mouth. When I had more or less finished, she spoke.

"Your rising time was about two hours later than usual. After I clear the table, we will begin morning studies immediately, Young Lord."

"……"

As expected, she had a talent for making one feel suffocated.

I spent the day as usual with morning studies and lunch. Afterward, visitors began to arrive one by one, just like any other day. It felt somewhat empty, though.

"Oppa!"

Seon'ah, a regular visitor, was here again. She pressed close to my side, giggling and rubbing her chubby cheeks against me, but almost no one else came.

Eldest Senior Brother and Hang Geon didn't come, nor did Sixth Senior Brother, who suffers from auditory and visual hallucinations. I knew that Third Senior Brother, who suffers from paranoia, had left on a mission.

Instead, Guyang Mun, who had returned to the Main Headquarters after his mission, filled the empty space all by himself. "Hahahaha! It seems rather quiet today for some reason, Young Lord!" He was chattering on endlessly by himself.

I spent some time letting Guyang Mun's nonsense go in one ear and out the other while playfully twisting cute Seon'ah's hair like a toy.

*It's still peaceful for now.*

I noticed that neither Jin Hayeon nor the two bodyguard martial artists were making any particular moves. A yawn escaped my lips unconsciously. "Haam. Ah, my apologies, Master Guyang."

Yawning while someone else was talking was rude, so I quickly apologized, but Guyang Mun laughed heartily as if he didn't mind. "Hahaha. It's alright, Young Lord! But it seems you're very tired."

"I didn't sleep well yesterday, so I suppose I'm a bit tired."

"Well, after what happened recently, it's understandable that you couldn't sleep."

"No, well, it's not necessarily because of that."

I tried to brush it off appropriately since people involved in the assassination were nearby. However, Guyang Mun seemed unable to let it go. He furtively glanced at Jin Hayeon before speaking.

"I heard the story. That you, Young Lord, spoke to the Heavenly Demon and asked for leniency for Hayeon. Purely as a Demonic Academy class!mate!, I am just offering my thanks on Hayeon's behalf. Hahaha!"

He had taken my advice in a strange way. I told him to show moderate goodwill, but he was spouting unnecessary words and needlessly attaching 'classmate' to everything.

"We're just classmates, so why are you thanking the Young Lord on my behalf?"

The one-sided lover's quarrel was about to start again. My eyes and the eyes of the little kitten rolling beside me lit up. It was our only source of entertainment.

However, I didn't know this entertainment would come back to me like a bomb.

"Ahem. Still, if... if you're classmates, isn't that something you can do?"

"It's needless meddling. I'm reflecting on my own without you interfering. That's why, starting yesterday, I've been sleeping in the same room as the Young Lord, continuing my bodyguard duties even during his sleeping hours."

"!?"

As soon as Jin Hayeon finished speaking, Guyang Mun's eyes widened as if they would tear. He stared back and forth between Jin Hayeon and me with his jaw agape.

The little kid next to me also looked back and forth between Jin Hayeon and me, but her reaction was strangely different. "Hmph. I want to play with Oppa at night too...." The little girl pouted her lips slightly and muttered in envy.

Unlike that innocent child, Guyang Mun had momentarily broken down and was on the verge of a seizure. "Wh-wh-what. Wh-what are you talking about?"

And unlike the child, Guyang Mun's impure imagination went one step further. "C-come to think of it, a little while ago, the Young Lord said he was t-tired.... D-don't tell me!?"

As Guyang Mun continued to speak, Jin Hayeon's expression changed. She looked at him like he was an insect. However, Guyang Mun had already lost his mind and failed to notice her gaze.

"Master! How could you do this to me, Master!"

"Ahem. Get a grip, Master Guyang, and why am I your master?"

"Didn't I say I would serve you as my master then! Don't tell me all the advice you gave me back then was a lie!?"

Somehow, I felt that if I left him like this, Guyang Mun's romantic endeavors would be completely ruined. No, they already felt like they had crossed the river Styx, but I should at least help them remain 'classmates'.

"Get a grip. We were only in the same room for pro!tec!tion! Nothing happened."

"I-is that true?"

"It is."

I met Guyang Mun's suspicious eyes with my own completely shameless ones. Maybe it was my incredibly confident gaze, but Guyang Mun's wavering eyes gradually stabilized.

After casting aside his suspicion, Guyang Mun seemed to ponder something for a moment. He then suddenly spoke with a determined tone.

"Young Lord."

"Speak."

"Please accept me as your bodyguard martial artist."

"What kind of bullsh... no, what nonsense is this? Aren't you part of the White Turtle Brigade, Master Guyang?"

"If you just give your permission, Young Lord, I'll go and submit my withdrawal application immediately. If I share a room with you as your bodyguard, then there's no need for Hayeon to go out of her way to sleep with you, is there?"

The suspicion was gone, replaced by madness in his eyes. As expected, this guy was a madman worthy of being a demonic cultist.

*I thought the side effect might be mania, but now I see it's obsession?*

However, diagnosing this madman wasn't important right now.

"Is it that easy to transfer your affiliation within the Cult? Get a grip and focus on your own work."

I absolutely hated the idea of sleeping in the same room with a crazy man who clearly had stalker tendencies.

Perhaps because I had rejected him so flatly, the madman finally knelt.

GRAB.

He began to cling to my trouser leg.

"P-please, I beg you, Young Lord! I don't care if you work me to the bone, please appoint me as your bodyguard martial artist!"

Watching his desperate appearance, a thought naturally came to mind.

*How pathetic, Guyang Mun.*

No matter how much people say pride doesn't matter in love, this was just too pathetic.

{sep}

Guyang Mun, who had shown the depths of human patheticness, belatedly saw Jin Hayeon's insect-like gaze and ran out of the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

Time passed and the break ended. I spent my time as usual, either dueling with Jin Hayeon or training with my Master. Even more time passed, and Jin Hayeon prepared to perform Body Purification on me.

"Perhaps the training was a bit excessive today. I'm feeling a little hungry," I muttered, rubbing my stomach.

"I will bring a late-night snack from the teahouse."

Jin Hayeon prepared to leave the Wind Cliff Pavilion, but Gwan Muyeol, who stood guard on one side of the pavilion, interjected.

"Miss Jin, please proceed with the Body Purification. I will go to the teahouse."

"It's alright, Martial Artist Gwan."

When Jin Hayeon refused, Gwan Muyeol replied in a serious tone. "I'm the one who's alright. Besides, Jang Hoe will be here even if I leave my post, and don't you have a lot to do, Miss Jin?"

In the end, Jin Hayeon had no choice but to nod at Gwan Muyeol's firm but not-quite-forced insistence. "Then I'll be in your care."

As soon as Jin Hayeon finished speaking, Gwan Muyeol left the Wind Cliff Pavilion. Jin Hayeon turned with an emotionless face and began to touch my body.

I felt the cold energy transmit from Jin Hayeon's hands and thought.

*What's this? Was that guy interested in Miss Jin too?*

Until now, neither Gwan Muyeol nor Jang Hoe had ever stepped forward to help Jin Hayeon with her various needs. His sudden goodwill toward Jin Hayeon was strange, though perhaps it was because of Guyang Mun's obnoxious behavior today.

*Hmm. If this goes well, I might have another interesting spectacle to watch.*

The first image that came to mind was a love triangle fight between Gwan Muyeol and Guyang Mun over Jin Hayeon, and knowing Jin Hayeon, she would surely look at both of them as if they were insects.

{sep}

Meanwhile, Gwan Muyeol, the victim of my strange fantasy, moved quickly and arrived at the teahouse.

"What can I prepare for you?" asked the woman on duty.

"I'd like some Longjing tea and a few simple snacks to go with it," Gwan Muyeol replied bluntly.

The woman drew hot water from a stove kept on a low fire. She brewed the tea, then placed a few pre-prepared late-night refreshments on a plate and served them.

Gwan Muyeol placed the cup of steeping tea and the snacks on a tray and stood up.

"You're not having it here?"

"I'm here on an errand."

At Gwan Muyeol's answer, the woman asked no more questions and bowed her head. "Take care on your way."

Gwan Muyeol nodded and left the teahouse. However, he didn't head straight for the Wind Cliff Pavilion. He ducked into a dark side alley along the way.

Gwan Muyeol scanned his surroundings with anxious eyes. He sighed in relief, then rummaged inside his clothes and pulled out a small leather pouch.

SRRK.

He untied the string binding the pouch. Inside was a mushroom with a vibrant, poisonous-looking pattern.

Gwan Muyeol pinched off the mushroom's tip with his thumb and forefinger. He infused it with his inner arts, turning it to powder, and mixed it into the Longjing tea.
