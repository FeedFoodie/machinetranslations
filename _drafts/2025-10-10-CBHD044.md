Gwan Muyeol’s pupils constricted, his body taut with tension. He stared intently as finely ground powder drifted from his fingertips and settled into the Longjing tea.

Just before the last of the powder mixed into the tea.

*Flick.*

A hand shot out from the darkness, its fingers aiming for Gwan Muyeol’s acupoints.

"!?"

Gwan Muyeol noticed the ambush too late and tried to swat the hand away, but he was a step behind. His focus on the Longjing tea and the poisonous mushroom had allowed his enemy to approach undetected.

*Tap! Tap!*

The man in black moved like the wind, striking the paralysis acupoint and then the mute acupoint in quick succession. Gwan Muyeol was completely subdued.

As Gwan Muyeol’s body went limp, the bag of mushrooms and the teacup of Longjing tea started to fall. The man in black moved swiftly, catching both items before they hit the ground.

With the evidence secured, the man in black hoisted the paralyzed Gwan Muyeol over his shoulder and melted back into the shadows.

{sep}

"Ughhh."

I let out a strange groan, a mix of blissful relief from Jin Hayeon’s Body Purification and a chill from the cold air.

*Thump.*

Suddenly, a man in black appeared, vaulting over the wall of the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Who goes there!" Jang Hoe shouted, drawing his spear. Behind me, Jin Hayeon, who had been performing the Body Purification, instantly assumed a starting stance, her cold energy flaring.

But when the man in black stepped out of the shadows, they both wore bizarre expressions.

"...Third Young Master. Would you care to explain the situation?"

The man who had just jumped the wall was Third Senior Brother Seo Wanpyeong, who was supposed to have left the Main Headquarters on a mission, but his sudden appearance wasn't the only reason for their strange looks.

"I’d have used the main gate, but my hands were full."

Absurdly, Third Senior Brother had Gwan Muyeol slung over his left shoulder, held in place with his left hand. His right hand balanced a tray holding snacks and a teacup.

*Wow... he didn't spill a single drop through all that.*

Despite leaping over a wall without using his hands for support, not a single drop of tea had spilled from the cup on Third Senior Brother’s tray. While Jang Hoe and Jin Hayeon looked dumbfounded, I was the only one calmly admiring Third Senior Brother’s movement arts.

I already knew Third Senior Brother hadn't left the Main Headquarters. I just hadn't expected him to show up looking like this.

I slowly pushed myself up from the prone position I had taken for the Body Purification and asked, "Did that man do something suspicious, Senior Brother?"

Third Senior Brother nodded and dropped Gwan Muyeol onto the ground. "After getting the refreshments from the teahouse, he didn't come straight back to the Wind Cliff Pavilion. He ducked into a secluded corner and secretly mixed this into the Longjing tea."

As he spoke, Senior Brother pulled a small leather pouch from his sleeve and tossed it to me.

*Fwip.*

I caught the pouch easily and untied the string sealing it. Inside was a brightly colored mushroom.

*Heh... anyone can see this is a poison mushroom.*

Jang Hoe and Jin Hayeon, who had moved closer to look inside the pouch with me, wore grim expressions.

"Then we should begin the interrogation."

"The circumstances leave no room for doubt, do they?" Jin Hayeon said.

I shook my head at Jang Hoe’s comment. "Do you really think he’d target me on his own?"

"Ah..."

Jang Hoe let out a sound of realization, but Jin Hayeon and Third Senior Brother just nodded as if they had expected it.

Third Senior Brother carefully approached Gwan Muyeol and released the mute acupoint. He kept the paralysis acupoint sealed and held a hand ready before the man’s mouth, just in case he tried to bite his tongue.

But that precaution was unnecessary. The man didn't choose suicide. Instead, he shouted.

"It’s a misunderstanding!!"

Incredibly, he was still pleading innocence.

"You call it a misunderstanding when you were putting this in the tea meant for me?" I scoffed, shaking the leather pouch.

Gwan Muyeol spoke with uncharacteristic urgency. "That tea wasn't for you, Young Lord! I-I was going to drink it myself!"

"That’s ridiculous..."

Before I could say more, he spouted another excuse. "Please, Young Lord, remember when we talked about the side effects of the demonic art! Didn't I mention I was trying something new recently?"

I did recall a conversation where Gwan Muyeol consulted me about suffering from side effects similar to my Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. He had blushed while admitting he resorted to self-harm when the obsessive compulsions became unbearable. He mentioned trying something else, but I hadn't pressed for details, assuming it would be unpleasant.

So that other method was eating poison?

"That sounds like a poor excuse you just made up. Do you really think eating poison will calm your compulsions?"

Gwan Muyeol looked at me with an expression of pure frustration. "Actually, that poison mushroom is a poison, but it's also not a poison!"

"Stop talking nonsense."

"Then let me drink the tea and you’ll see!"

"!?"

I couldn't help but look dumbfounded as Gwan Muyeol offered to drink the poisoned Longjing tea himself, but after the initial shock, I realized his suggestion made a certain kind of sense.

Jang Hoe, standing beside me, spoke with suspicion. "Young Lord, he might be trying to commit suicide by ingesting the poison."

"Jang Hoe!! What are you saying!! Are you suspecting me too!?"

While Gwan Muyeol and Jang Hoe argued, I turned to look at Third Senior Brother, who was still restraining Gwan Muyeol. He seemed to understand my unspoken question, nodding as he said, "I’ll keep a close watch on his condition. If he tries to kill himself, I’ll take immediate action."

The decision was made to let him drink the tea. It wasn't just because I believed Gwan Muyeol.

*The circumstances are suspicious, but they're almost too suspicious to make sense.*

These were the same people who had framed the Murim Alliance for the last assassination attempt. Would they really resort to such a simple and obvious method now? It was possible they were desperate, but something felt off.

While I organized my thoughts, Third Senior Brother prepared to administer an antidote at a moment's notice. He carefully tilted the teacup to Gwan Muyeol’s lips. We all watched Gwan Muyeol’s reaction after he took a sip of the Longjing tea laced with the mushroom powder.

Suddenly, Gwan Muyeol’s condition changed.

"Heehee. Hehet."

His usually stern and stoic face slackened. His eyes lost focus, and he began to giggle foolishly. He grinned bizarrely and mumbled strange noises like a religious fanatic speaking in tongues.

Then, just as suddenly, it stopped.

"……"

The mushroom’s effects seemed to wear off, and Gwan Muyeol’s face returned to its usual stoicism. No, it was slightly different. His face was flushed beet red, as if he might burst from the shame of having shown us such an unsightly side of himself. He tried to feign a dignified expression to hide his embarrassment, but it was completely ineffective.

Everyone has a side they want to hide. Perhaps we had just been forced to witness the one Gwan Muyeol wanted to hide most.

"Ahem. My apologies."

Still, I felt it was unfair to me as well. Witnessing such a pathetic display wasn't exactly pleasant for me either. "Why did you have to put that stuff in the Longjing tea I was going to drink?"

"...As I said, I was going to drink it myself," Gwan Muyeol answered, struggling to contain his shame.

"Weren't you getting my midnight snack?"

"My plan was to secretly drink the tea, then claim I spilled it by mistake and get another from the teahouse."

"At a time like this?"

"...It was because of a time like this."

"???"

Seeing our confused expressions, Gwan Muyeol sighed heavily. "I failed to protect you, Young Lord. Because of that, my compulsive symptoms have gotten worse than usual. I felt like I was about to be consumed by my inner demons and go mad, but I couldn't resort to self-harm while on duty as your bodyguard, so this was my only other option."

His explanation left me feeling troubled.

*These damn side effects are the problem.*

Since I also practiced a demonic art with similar side effects, his problem didn't feel entirely foreign.

I let out a soft sigh and asked him, "Does eating this mushroom help calm your compulsions?"

"...It’s a mushroom so poisonous that a single large bite is fatal, but if you take a small amount, grind it into a powder, and mix it with water or tea, it makes you feel good. It temporarily gets rid of the compulsions, so I use it in emergencies."

"Does anyone else know about this mushroom?"

"...How could someone who practices the martial arts of the Divine Cult admit to being defeated by side effects and relying on something like this?"

It seemed his shame wasn't just from us seeing him high on a drug. He appeared to be more ashamed of himself for relying on a poison mushroom, a sign of his failure to overcome the side effects. Having that weakness exposed to others must have deepened his sense of self-loathing.

Of course, no matter how you looked at it, relying on such a strange substance didn't paint a good picture. Though from Gwan Muyeol’s perspective, he was using medicine, not poison.

*A poison mushroom can be medicine...*

It was a strange thought. It was something I had heard often. What is poison to one can be medicine to another, and what is medicine to one can be poison.

As my thoughts tangled around demonic art side effects, mental illness, poison, and medicine...

"Hm?"

A memory buried deep in my mind surfaced.

"Senior Brother. On the night I was attacked, did you happen to have a midnight snack?"

"As you know, Junior Brother, I suffer from insomnia and eat a late meal almost every night."

Senior Brother’s answer turned my suspicion into certainty. Seeing my expression, Senior Brother seemed to guess what I was thinking.

"But there was no poison, Junior Brother. Do you think I wouldn't be able to detect something so simple?"

Seeing his paranoia flare up again, I quickly replied, "It wasn't poison."

"???"

"It wasn't poison, but there are things that are as good as a deadly poison to you, Senior Brother."

The memory that had surfaced was from my time as Seo Jihun, before I was reincarnated as Ilmok. It was from the day I was stabbed to death by a citizen. It was a memory related to Lee Jiyeon Senior Official, who had been arguing with that citizen.

Lee Jiyeon Senior Official, who had spent her early days at her new post crying every day, eventually began to suffer from depression. It got a little better after she completely embraced her black flames, but when her depression was severe, she avoided certain foods.

She said they had a negative effect on her depression.

Which meant...

"Senior Brother, even among foods and teas that aren't poison, some things can make a person irritable or depressed."

Foods that were bad for depression existed, and Third Senior Brother was a severely depressed patient suffering from extreme paranoia.

"...Things that aren't poison can make a person depressed?" Senior Brother tilted his head, unable to comprehend. The others also looked puzzled.

"Yes. Just as the poison mushroom became medicine for the martial artist, what is medicine for some can be poison for others, and aren't there ordinary teas or foods that are fine when eaten alone but cause problems when eaten together?"

This much was common knowledge even in this era, and everyone nodded in understanding.

"Also, aren't there foods and teas that make you feel relaxed and comfortable after eating them? Or things that are rumored to be good for stamina because they boost yang qi."

"Hmm. I've heard that whenever a food is rumored to be good for stamina, it disappears from the market," Third Senior Brother said, nodding in understanding. His frequent missions outside the Main Headquarters likely exposed him to such rumors. Jin Hayeon, however, frowned at the talk of stamina.

"Conversely, some foods, while not ‘poison’, can make a person irritable. For ordinary people, it might sharpen their mind and improve concentration, making it a good thing, but for someone who is already irritable and sharp, it's no different from poison."

"I see..."

Third Senior Brother seemed to be aware of his own irritability and depression, as he nodded in acceptance, but, true to his paranoid nature, he didn't just let it go.

"But, Junior Brother, how do you know such things?"
