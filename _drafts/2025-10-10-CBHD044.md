Gwan Muyeol's pupils constricted with extreme tension. He stared intently at the fine powder falling from his fingers into the Longjing tea.

Just before the powder completely mixed into the tea, a hand shot out from the darkness.

"......"

Fingers jabbed toward Gwan Muyeol's acupoints.

"!?"

Gwan Muyeol noticed the ambush too late. He tried to swat the hand away but was a step behind. His focus on the Longjing tea and the poisonous mushroom had left him vulnerable to the stealthed attacker.

*Papat!*

The man in black moved like the wind. He swiftly struck Gwan Muyeol's paralysis acupoint, then his mute acupoint, completely subduing him.

The bag of mushrooms and the teacup fell from Gwan Muyeol's paralyzed hands. The man in black moved quickly, catching both items before they hit the ground. He then hoisted the paralyzed Gwan Muyeol over his shoulder and vanished into the darkness.

{sep}

I groaned, a strange mix of lethargy from Jin Hayeon's Body Purification and a chill from the cold air.

"......"

Suddenly, a man in black vaulted over the wall of the Wind Cliff Pavilion.

"Who are you!" Jang Hoe shouted, drawing his spear in alarm.

Behind me, Jin Hayeon stopped the Body Purification and assumed a starting stance, her cold energy radiating outwards.

But when the man in black's face became visible in the dark, they both looked bewildered.

"...Third Young Master. Would you mind explaining the situation?"

The man who vaulted the wall was Third Senior Brother Seo Wanpyeong, who was supposed to be away from the Main Headquarters on a mission.

Their confusion likely stemmed from more than just his unexpected appearance.

"I wanted to use the front gate, but I didn't have a free hand," Third Senior Brother said. He was carrying Gwan Muyeol over his left shoulder with his left hand. In his right hand, he held a tray with a teacup and snacks.

*Wow... he didn't spill a single drop.*

He had vaulted the wall without using his hands, yet not a single drop of tea had spilled from the tray. While Jang Hoe and Jin Hayeon stared in disbelief, I calmly admired Third Senior Brother's movement arts.

I already knew Third Senior Brother hadn't left the Main Headquarters. I just hadn't expected him to appear like this.

I slowly rose from the prone position I had taken for the Body Purification. "Did he do something suspicious, Senior Brother?" I asked.

Third Senior Brother nodded and dropped Gwan Muyeol on the floor. "After getting the snacks from the teahouse, he didn't come straight back to the Wind Cliff Pavilion. He hid in a corner and secretly mixed this into the Longjing tea."

As he spoke, Senior Brother pulled a small leather pouch from his sleeve and tossed it to me.

"......"

I caught it easily and untied the string. Inside was a brightly colored mushroom.

*Wow... that's obviously a poisonous mushroom.*

Jang Hoe and Jin Hayeon came closer to look inside the pouch. Their expressions hardened.

"Well then, we should interrogate him," I said.

"Isn't it obvious from the situation?" Jang Hoe asked.

I shook my head. "It's unlikely he acted alone, don't you think?"

"Ah..." Jang Hoe gasped in realization. Jin Hayeon and Third Senior Brother, however, nodded as if they had already considered that.

Third Senior Brother carefully released Gwan Muyeol's mute acupoint. The paralysis acupoint remained sealed. He kept his hand ready by Gwan Muyeol's mouth, just in case he tried to bite his tongue.

But Gwan Muyeol didn't try to kill himself. Instead, he shouted, "It's a misunderstanding!"

He was absurdly pleading his innocence.

"You put this in the tea meant for me and now you're claiming it's a misunderstanding?" I scoffed, shaking the pouch with the mushroom.

Gwan Muyeol spoke with uncharacteristic urgency. "That tea wasn't for you, Young Lord! I was going to drink it myself!"

"That's ridiculous..."

Before I could say more, he rushed to explain. "Please, Young Lord, remember what we discussed about the side effects of the demonic art! Didn't I tell you I was trying something new recently?"

I did remember talking with Gwan Muyeol. He suffered from side effects similar to those from my Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword. He had blushed while admitting that he resorted to self-harm when the compulsions became too strong. He mentioned trying something else recently, but I hadn't asked for details, assuming it would be unpleasant.

*So his other method is eating poison?*

"It sounds like you're making excuses. Do you really think eating poison calms your compulsions?" I asked.

Gwan Muyeol looked extremely frustrated. "That mushroom is poisonous, but it's not a poison!"

"Stop talking nonsense."

"Then let me drink the tea and see for yourself!"

"!?"

I was stunned by Gwan Muyeol's offer to drink the poisoned Longjing tea. My shock faded quickly. It was a plausible way to prove his claim.

Just then, Jang Hoe spoke to me with suspicion in his eyes. "Young Lord, maybe he's just trying to kill himself by drinking the poison."

"Jang Hoe! What are you saying! You suspect me too?" Gwan Muyeol cried out.

While they argued, I turned to look at Third Senior Brother, who still held Gwan Muyeol down.

Senior Brother seemed to understand my unspoken question. He nodded and said, "I'll monitor his condition closely. If he tries to kill himself, I'll intervene immediately."

So, we decided to let him drink the tea.

It wasn't just because I trusted Gwan Muyeol's words.

*The situation is suspicious, but it's almost too suspicious to be true.*

These people framed the Murim Alliance for the last assassination attempt. Why would they use such a simple, obvious method now? It was possible they were desperate, but something felt inconsistent.

While I sorted through my thoughts, Third Senior Brother prepared an antidote and carefully tilted the teacup to Gwan Muyeol's lips. We watched as Gwan Muyeol took a sip of the Longjing tea laced with the mushroom powder.

Suddenly, his condition changed.

"Heeik. Hehet."

His usually stern, stoic face went slack. He started grinning foolishly with unfocused eyes.

He giggled strangely and babbled nonsense like a religious fanatic speaking in tongues. Then, just as suddenly, he stopped.

"……"

The mushroom's effect seemed to wear off, and Gwan Muyeol's usual stoic expression returned. No, it was slightly different. His face was bright red, as if he were about to explode from the shame of showing us such a pathetic side of himself. He tried to hide his embarrassment behind a stern mask, but it didn't work.

Everyone has a side they wish to hide. We had perhaps just witnessed the one Gwan Muyeol wanted to conceal most.

"Ahem. My apologies," I said, but I felt wronged, too. It wasn't pleasant for me to see that side of him either. "So why did you put it in the Longjing tea meant for me?"

"…As I said, it was for me to drink," Gwan Muyeol answered, struggling to contain his shame.

"Weren't you getting my midnight snack?"

"My plan was to drink the tea secretly. Then I'd claim I spilled it and go get another cup from the teahouse."

"Why now, of all times?"

"…It's precisely because of the timing."

"???"

Seeing our confused faces, Gwan Muyeol sighed deeply. "I failed to protect you, Young Lord. Because of that, my compulsions have gotten much worse. I felt like I was about to fall to my inner demons and go mad. I couldn't resort to self-harm while on duty as your bodyguard, so this was my only other option."

His explanation troubled me.

*These damn side effects are the real problem.*

Since I practiced a demonic art with similar side effects, his struggle felt personal.

I sighed lightly. "Does eating this mushroom help calm your compulsions?"

"…A single large bite of this mushroom is fatal, but if you take a small piece, grind it into a powder, and mix it with tea or water, it makes you feel good. I use it in emergencies because it makes the compulsions disappear for a short time."

"Does anyone else know about this mushroom?"

"…How could a practitioner of the Divine Cult's martial arts admit to being defeated by side effects and relying on something like this?"

His shame wasn't just about his strange behavior. He seemed ashamed of his weakness, of his reliance on the mushroom to overcome the side effects. Showing that weakness to us must have deepened his self-loathing.

Still, his reliance on such a strange substance was unsettling, even if he considered it medicine instead of poison.

*A poisonous mushroom can be medicine...*

The thought felt strange but familiar. I had heard it before. What is poison to one can be medicine to another, and vice versa. My thoughts tangled around demonic arts, side effects, mental illness, poison, and medicine.

"Hm?"

A memory surfaced from the back of my mind.

"Senior Brother, on the night of the ambush, did you happen to eat a midnight snack?"

"As you know, Junior Brother, I suffer from insomnia. I eat a midnight snack almost every night."

His answer turned my suspicion into certainty.

Senior Brother saw my expression and seemed to guess my thoughts. "But there was no poison, Junior Brother. Do you think I couldn't detect something so simple?"

Seeing his paranoia flare up again, I answered quickly. "It wasn't poison."

"???"

"It wasn't poison, but for you, Senior Brother, there's something that might as well be."

I recalled a memory from my past life as Seo Jihun, before I became Ilmok. It was from the day I was stabbed to death by a citizen. I remembered Lee Jiyeon Senior Official, who had been arguing with that person. Early in her career, she cried every day and eventually suffered from depression. It lessened after she became jaded, but when her depression was severe, she avoided certain foods. She said they made her depression worse.

That meant one thing.

"Senior Brother, some foods and teas that aren't poisonous can still make a person irritable or depressed."

Foods that worsen depression exist, and Third Senior Brother was a severely depressed patient suffering from extreme paranoia.

"…Foods that aren't poison can make someone depressed?" Senior Brother tilted his head, confused. The others looked just as puzzled.

"Yes. Just as the poisonous mushroom became medicine for Gwan Muyeol, what is medicine for one person can be poison for another, and aren't there ordinary foods and teas that are fine on their own but cause problems when eaten together?"

This was common knowledge, so they all nodded in agreement.

"Also, some foods and teas make you feel relaxed and comfortable, right? Others are rumored to be good for virility because they boost yang qi."

"Hmm. I've heard that among commoners, any food rumored to boost virility quickly becomes scarce." Since Third Senior Brother was often away on missions, he nodded in understanding. Jin Hayeon, however, frowned at the mention of virility.

"Conversely, some foods aren't 'poison' but can make a person more irritable. For a normal person, they might sharpen the mind and improve focus, but for someone who is already on edge, they're no different from poison."

"I see…" Third Senior Brother nodded in understanding, perhaps acknowledging his own sensitive and depressive state.

But, true to his paranoid nature, he didn't let it go.

"But Junior Brother, how do you know all this?"
