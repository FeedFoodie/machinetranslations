I opened the door, entered, and immediately performed a clasped hands greeting, bowing my head.

"Youngest disciple Ilmok greets Master and Senior Brothers."

When I slowly raised my head, the scene inside came into view. Appetizing food lay on luxurious dishes. The Master sat at the head of the table.

"Hohoho. Come in. Everyone, greet him. This is the youngest disciple, Ilmok, whom this old man took in his later years."

Following Master’s brief introduction, a middle-aged man sitting closest to him nodded.

"Hahahaha. It’s good to meet you, Junior Brother. I’m your Eldest Senior Brother, Wi Jinhak."

Eldest Senior Brother greeted me with a laugh that was either hearty or foolish. I couldn't be fooled by that smile, however.

*He definitely learned the Frenzied Soul Mara Sword Art.*

This was information I had heard from Jin Hayeon. The side effect of the Frenzied Soul Mara Sword Art was an anger control disorder. It wasn't a selective disorder, but a genuine one. He was the type of man who would slice my head off the moment his eyes rolled back, regardless of whether Master was next to him.

"It’s an honor to meet you, Eldest Senior Brother."

I tried to ignore the sweat trickling down my spine and offered my greeting with the utmost respect.

*…Honestly, it feels like I should be calling him Junior Uncle or something similar, rather than Senior Brother.*

Eldest Senior Brother Wi Jinhak was forty-five years old. Considering this body was fifteen, he was not at all in the age range to be called ‘hyung’.

*To think a man like that has the alias, the Crazy Demon...*

He had the air of a kind, slightly slow neighborhood uncle, the type you used to see in every town. However, Wi Jinhak’s alias was none other than the Crazy Demon.

About twenty years ago, when the Eldest Senior Brother's mastery of the Frenzied Soul Mara Sword Art reached the Ultimate Demon Realm. Unable to control his anger, Wi Jinhak had apparently gone on rampages and was subdued by Master several times. The Master was said to have given the Eldest Senior Brother this advice, 'If you cannot control your anger, do not torment innocent commoners. Release it on the wicked.'

Deeply moved by this advice, the Eldest Senior Brother would reportedly leave Xinjiang for the Central Plains whenever he felt the onset of qi deviation or the initial symptoms of his anger disorder. As a result of unleashing his fury on the martial artists and corrupt officials of the Central Plains, the number of martial artists, corrupt officials, and corrupt government soldiers killed by the Eldest Senior Brother alone was said to have easily surpassed a thousand.

Naturally, the Murim Alliance declared the Eldest Senior Brother a murim public enemy, and they dubbed him 'the Crazy Demon' after witnessing him rampage in a fury. Thanks to thoroughly venting his anger for nearly ten years, the Eldest Senior Brother was finally able to escape the Ultimate Demon Realm of the Frenzied Soul Mara Sword Art.

*...No matter how I think about it, it's amazing he didn't die.*

He was a remarkable man in many ways to have survived after rampaging dozens of times in the Central Plains and killing over a thousand people. If he had died in the process, I suppose it would have been considered a death from the side effects of a demonic art.

In any case, with time, he had now left the Ultimate Demon Realm and reached a state close to the Transcendent Demon Realm. What I felt was most important was the phrase, ‘close to the Transcendent Demon Realm’. In other words, he wasn't completely in the Transcendent Demon Realm yet.

*Which means if I provoke him and he snaps, that anger disorder could flare up.*

If I was fooled by that vacant smile and underestimated him, I would be as good as dead.

After I finished greeting the tense Eldest Senior Brother, the man sitting across from him spoke in a gloomy tone.

"I’m Seo Wanpyeong."

He was the third disciple, Seo Wanpyeong. I believe he was thirty-four. I performed a clasped hands greeting to the man, who exuded the somber aura of a world-weary assistant manager.

"A pleasure to meet you, Third Senior Brother. I’m Ilmok."

"Why are you greeting me again? Are you treating me like I’m dead?"

"……"

For a moment, I was speechless.

*Fuck...*

The martial art Seo Wanpyeong learned was the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art. True to its name, it was a demonic art that boasted a stealthy and swift sword. Its side effect was paranoia, a symptom where one perceives every situation negatively, and damn it all, Seo Wanpyeong, at thirty-four, had yet to completely escape the Ultimate Demon Realm.

*If I hadn't greeted him, he would've thrown a fit, asking if I was ignoring him.*

As I hesitated, unsure what to do, Master spoke.

"Third."

A voice imbued with immense energy flowed from Master’s lips.

"!!!"

Perhaps Master’s energy drove out the demonic qi that had seeped into Third Senior Brother’s mind. Third Senior Brother shook his head a few times and looked at me with slightly clearer eyes.

"Ahem. My apologies."

"Hahaha. It was my fault for not knowing the proper etiquette yet. Please don’t worry about it, Senior Brother."

His senses seemed to have returned for a moment, but I was extremely careful not to provoke him. If his paranoia flared up again, he was the type who might sneak in on a moonlit night to take my head. They said the Fourteen Shadowless Combat Art was a sword style specialized for assassination, after all.

After finishing the greeting that sent a chill down my neck, a young man sitting one seat further from Master greeted me.

"It’s a pleasure to meet you like this. I’m the sixth disciple, Jongri Chu."

"A pleasure to meet you, Sixth Senior Brother. I’m Ilmok."

Fortunately, Sixth Senior Brother didn't react to my greeting like Third Senior Brother had. Instead, he furrowed his brow, studied me for a moment, then suddenly turned his head to look beside me. Sixth Senior Brother Jongri Chu glanced back and forth between the empty space and me.

The martial art he learned was the Frenzied Soul Sword Technique, and its side effect was schizophrenia.

The Senior Brother, who seemed to be seeing something that wasn't there, soon turned back to me and smiled gently.

"Ah. My apologies, Junior Brother. I was just mistaken for a moment, so don’t mind me."

It was a relief, I suppose. Since he was still young at twenty-four, he hadn't fully entered the Ultimate Demon Realm yet. In other words, he only confused hallucinations with reality for brief moments. He hadn't completely succumbed to hallucinations or auditory illusions.

*...After I complete the Demonic Academy, I should avoid him as much as possible.*

He might be ‘relatively’ sane for now, but it would take a year to enter the Demonic Academy and another two years to complete its training. After three years, I suspected he would have entered the Ultimate Demon Realm. Or maybe Third Senior Brother would assassinate him in the meantime.

A sharp glance. Third Senior Brother Seo Wanpyeong was glaring darkly at Sixth Senior Brother. He seemed to think Sixth Senior Brother had interrupted my greeting, and that the reason was he was being looked down upon.

In response to Third Senior Brother’s dark gaze, Sixth Senior Brother looked in his direction.

"Hm?"

More precisely, he looked at the empty seat next to Third Senior Brother.

"Fourth Senior Brother. When did you arrive?"

"……"

Sixth Senior Brother was greeting the Fourth Senior Brother, who had supposedly died at the hands of the orthodox sects while on a mission. He suddenly shook his head a few times, and his eyes soon cleared.

"Ah. It seems I was mistaken again. I apologize."

"Uahahaha. It’s alright, Junior Brother. You must have missed the fourth brother you knew when you were young. Hahahaha."

At Sixth Senior Brother Jongri Chu’s apology, Eldest Senior Brother Wi Jinhak burst into that foolish laugh again.

*What a mess.*

The scene almost made me sigh, but I managed to endure it with superhuman patience. The moment I sighed, I might incur the wrath of one of the three Senior Brothers. None of them were of sound mind, after all.

"Ahem. It must be uncomfortable to keep standing, so go ahead and take a seat."

At Master’s intervention, I hesitantly walked over and sat in the empty seat next to Third Senior Brother. Fortunately, Sixth Senior Brother Jongri Chu didn't spout any more nonsense like, ‘That’s the Fourth Senior Brother’s seat,’ perhaps because he had come to his senses.

And so the pleasant dinner party began with my pitiful Senior Brothers, who were all mentally ill.

"Hahahaha. I’m truly delighted to be drinking with Master and my Junior Brothers like this after so long."

Surprisingly, Eldest Senior Brother, not Master, led the atmosphere. He poured drinks into Master’s cup and his Junior Brothers’ cups, then filled his own and initiated toasts. He led the mood with a constant stream of foolish laughter.

They enjoyed the food and drinks while making small talk for a while.

"I’m also pleased to be with you all like this. By the way, have you all taken care of the tasks I assigned while this old man was away in the Central Plains?"

At Master's words, I almost let out a sigh.

*Talking about work at a dinner party.*

As expected, a company dinner was definitely overtime, especially one with an old-fashioned, condescending boss.

"Hahahaha. Don’t worry, Master. There were no problems near Karamay where I went."

"As for the area near Pacho, a group of mounted bandits had recently appeared in the Tarim Basin, so this disciple took care of them before returning."

However, the Senior Brothers themselves didn't seem to have any complaints. Come to think of it, the Senior Brothers were old-fashioned people too, so they probably didn't feel uncomfortable talking about work at a dinner party.

I listened to Master and the three Senior Brothers discuss their work for a while. Suddenly, Master, who had been listening to their reports, looked at me.

"Hohoho. Come to think of it, I haven’t explained the situation to the Youngest."

"Please don’t mind me. Continue your conversation comfortably."

"Hahahaha! How could I do that, Junior Brother? After you complete the Demonic Academy, these will be tasks you must undertake as well."

"Jinhak is right."

Thanks to Eldest Senior Brother’s meddling, Master suddenly started talking to me about work too.

The Master’s explanation was brief. My kidnapping in the Central Plains was just a coincidence. Originally, Master had other business there and had to leave Xinjiang for a while. So, he had instructed the three Senior Brothers to travel through the regions in Xinjiang under the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult’s influence and check for any problems with commoners being harassed.

That was why the Senior Brothers were still away when I returned to the Main Headquarters with Master. Because Master had been tailed by the Murim Alliance and had kidnapped me as his disciple, he returned to the cult earlier than planned. That was why the Senior Brothers, who hadn't finished their tasks yet, returned a step behind him.

After he finished the general explanation, Master looked at me with a peculiar expression and asked.

"What do you think, our Youngest?"

"About what, sir?"

"I’m curious about your impression of what you’ve heard so far. Do you have any thoughts on the way our Divine Cult looks after the common people, or perhaps a good idea for improvement?"

The gazes of the Senior Brothers, who had been busy laughing and talking, turned to me at Master’s question.

I had a feeling that the moment had finally come. According to my plan, I had to feign Incompetence here.

However, simply answering, 'I don't know anything,' was not the right move. The one asking the question was Master, and I was the disciple of the Heavenly Demon. In other words, the standard for my Incompetence had to be 'disappointing for a disciple of the Heavenly Demon.' It would be a problem if I seemed less capable than an average person. That's why, on my way to this dinner, I had been pondering how I could appear reasonably intelligent, yet still give the impression of being lacking for a disciple of the Heavenly Demon.

The solution I came up with was to act like a naive new recruit, full of passion but little else. I planned to give off the impression of someone who works hard but achieves little, not quite Incompetent, but lacking that final two percent.

*Come to think of it, Master's question was about the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's way of handling its affairs, wasn't it?*

It just so happened to be the perfect topic for me. I was all too familiar with the most archaic and responsibility-shirking work system in the Republic of Korea.
