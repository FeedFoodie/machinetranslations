Chapter 31: The Audit (4)

I had my own excuse.
The only Branch Manager I had ever met was Branch Manager Ma Gangil of Wensu County.
And Branch Manager Ma Gangil's side effect was, at worst(?), mania.
I had figured that if the side effect was just constantly feeling good and laughing heartily, there would be no problem dealing with children.
However, on second thought, there was no way the Branch Managers of every Branch had learned the same demonic art as Ma Gangil.
On top of that.
*I had forgotten just how infuriating it is to deal with dozens of kids!*
It's a daily occurrence for even perfectly sane people to lose their minds from the stress of dealing with children, so I had completely forgotten that there was no way mental patients could endure that stress.
However, now was not the time to be regretting that.

BANG!

"Calm down!!"
I shouted as I hurriedly threw the door open, before the children could be massacred.
The eyes of the children gathered in the school turned to me. Naturally, the gaze of the Principal, who was also the Branch Manager spewing streams of demonic qi and killing intent, followed suit.
"How dare you interfere with the sacred event of the Divine Cult!"
Perhaps because he had already started to run rampant, the crazed Branch Manager suddenly yelled and charged at me, cutting through the children.
He streamed killing intent and demonic qi as he came.
The killing intent of a middle-aged demonic practitioner poured down on me, someone who had started learning demonic art less than four months ago.
Funnily enough, I felt no particular discomfort.
From Master to the group leader Eldest Senior Brother, I had experienced the killing intent of martial artists on a completely different level, so this felt like it didn't even register.
You could say my guts had grown bigger than my skills.

SWOOOSH!

The Branch Manager, who had closed the distance in an instant, drew the saber from his side and swung it.
Strangely, the movement was perfectly clear to my eyes.
Naturally, I had no intention of getting hit by an attack I could see.

CLANG!

In an instant, I drew my sword using the technique of the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw and simultaneously deflected the Branch Manager's first attack with the Soul-Luring Sword.
"Kuh."
However, perhaps due to the difference in our inner arts, my right arm, which held the sword, throbbed even though I had deflected the force.
I boldly retreated, using the principles of the Soul-Reaping Flash to check the Branch Manager's advance with a concise thrust.

CLANG!
CLANG!

I took a few steps back, parrying the saber swung by the Branch Manager who charged like a wild boar.
"Die!!"
The Branch Manager finally closed in and was swinging his saber at me, but I no longer needed to retreat.
I had passed through the narrow entrance of the school and emerged onto the wide street.
"How dare you!"
And then, Jin Hayeon and the two bodyguard martial artists launched their attacks at the Branch Manager who had followed me out of the school.
"Don't kill him!"
Even without my order, it seemed they had no intention of killing him outright, as the three of them swiftly subdued the Branch Manager by striking his acupoints instead.
"How dare you persecute me in a Branch of the Divine Cult! Do you bastards even think you can call yourselves martial artists after this!!"
The Branch Manager, unable to move after his paralysis acupoint was struck, screamed with venomous rage.
However, he had chosen the wrong person to scream at.
"You're the one who should be careful, Branch Manager! Do you even know who this is that you dared to swing your weapon at him!"
Jin Hayeon shouted, then suddenly reached into her sleeve and pulled out a token.
"This is Young Lord Ilmok, the eighth disciple of the august one."
The moment he confirmed the token, the Branch Manager's attitude completely changed.
"Hiiik..."
His face, which had been red with anger just moments before, was now deathly pale.
"I dared to swing my weapon at a disciple of the Supreme Lord, who is like the heavens themselves! Please, I beg you, kill me!!"
A heretic cultist, looking as if he might burst into tears at any moment, begging not for his life but for death.
I thought I had grown somewhat accustomed to the Demonic Cult, but I just can't get used to seeing things like that.
Just then, the children from the school appeared and stood between us and the Branch Manager.
"Don't bully our Principal!!"
"Why are you bullying our Principal!"
At the children's attitude, the Principal's face somehow grew even darker.
After watching the scene for a moment, I let out a sigh and said,
"Let's go inside the Branch for now. Release the Branch Manager's acupoints."

★★★

I entered the Branch with Jin Hayeon, the two bodyguard martial artists, and the Branch Manager of Gaochang County.
The Branch Manager's utterly deflated demeanor was just like that of a teacher who had been caught doing something wrong by an inspecting scholar.
"Teaching children is harder than you thought, isn't it?"
"No! It is because I am lacking. Please kill me for failing to properly handle even a simple task given by the Heavenly Demon!"
I'm not some executioner from the Joseon Dynasty, why does he keep asking me to kill him.
However, no matter how I tried to calm him down with roundabout words, he just kept begging for death, so I changed my approach.
"How are the classes being conducted?"
"Are you giving them separate break times?"
"About how many characters do you teach per day?"
As I bombarded him with questions about his teaching methods, the Branch Manager finally began to answer properly.
After grasping all the current teaching methods, I gave him a few instructions.
"First, if a child does not follow the Branch Manager's instructions during class, give them one warning. If they still don't listen, send them home."
"!? H-How can you tell me to ignore the Heavenly Demon's command and send a child home?"
"I'm not telling you to actually send them away. It's a type of threat. A threat to the children's parents. I checked last night, and all the parents were delighted that their children could learn to read. Now, what do you think the parents will do if they're told their child was kicked out of the school for not listening?"
In modern times, they might raise hell, saying something like, ‘Who are you to discriminate against my child?’ However, this place was different.
"Well, wouldn't they come crying and begging for just one more chance? Or the parents would give the child a severe scolding at home."
"Exactly. There's no need for the Branch Manager to give in to anger. You just need to do your job. Warn them that you'll send them home, and if that doesn't work, just send them home. Then, when the parents come asking for forgiveness, you can teach the child again, acting as if you're letting it slide just this once."
"Ah!!"
Only then did the Branch Manager let out a cry of admiration, as if he had finally understood my intention.
"As expected of a disciple of the great one! How could you possess such wisdom!!"
I gave a dismissive wave at the Branch Manager's useless flattery.
From my perspective, this wasn't a very satisfactory solution either. In the end, it was just passing the corporal punishment onto the parents.
It's just that.
*if these people, driven mad by demonic art, were to administer corporal punishment, they would likely kill or cripple the child, so I have no choice.*
It was the next-best option to avoid the worst-case scenario.
And of course, this measure alone wouldn't solve all the problems.
"Also, children have limited attention spans. Don't try to force them to learn too much. It will only make things harder for you, Branch Manager."
"Then, how much would be appropriate?"
"Plan on teaching just eight characters a day. You'll be teaching the Thousand Character Classic for a year anyway, and at that pace, you can finish one reading in just one hundred and twenty-five days."
The Thousand Character Classic was structured like poetry. It was composed of 250 verses, each with four characters, which combined to form the whole.
In other words, I was telling him to teach only two verses a day.
"You don't need to teach both verses at once, either. Teach one verse first, and once the children have some understanding and practice their hidden weapons, give them a short break. It's fine to let them run around and play during the break. Then, after they've settled down a bit, you can teach the second verse."
"I understand, Young Lord."
"There's no need to be impatient or get angry with the children. Do you understand?"
I emphasized it compulsively.
Even now, a chill ran down my spine.
The thought that children all over Xinjiang might be getting killed or crippled by demonic cultists, all because of the school I had casually proposed to Master simply because I was feeling lazy.
"It seems quite a bit of time has passed, so you may go now."
"As you command!!"
Despite the long-winded nagging, the Gaochang County Branch Manager somehow looked moved as he bowed to me and hurriedly left.
"Bring me a brush, ink, and paper."
Then, I gave the order to Jin Hayeon, who was waiting by my side.

★★★

As soon as Jin Hayeon brought the four treasures of the study, Ilmok began to write ceaselessly on the paper.
*The class schedule and times must be adjusted to the children's level.*
*The instructors must also be changed. Exclude those who have learned demonic arts related to anger or depression. Someone with mania like Branch Manager Ma Gangil, or someone lacking emotion like Jin Hayeon, would be less likely to harm the children.*
The problems and improvement plans he had noted while looking around the village and the Branch yesterday and today.
However, as he wrote down the contents, Ilmok's inner thoughts were not so comfortable.
It wasn't just because of the guilt of almost getting the children killed.
This should be enough. It's a hassle, isn't this much sufficient?
Such thoughts did come to mind.
[It's not perfect yet!]
[What will you do if a situation like this occurs? Shouldn't you prepare a little more?]
Those thoughts kept creeping up from a corner of his mind.
It was the moment the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword flared up.

SWISH.

Naturally, Ilmok ceaselessly sought another solution, his hand moving the brush endlessly without rest.
While Ilmok was so engrossed in his work.
Jin Hayeon, who was standing behind him and watching the scene, discreetly sent a telepathic message to the two bodyguard martial artists.
[It seems we will be a disturbance, so how about we withdraw?]
Thinking Jin Hayeon's words were right, the two bodyguard martial artists carefully opened the door and stepped outside.
And just in case, Jin Hayeon and the two bodyguard martial artists stood guard at the entrance.
Perhaps they grew bored of the endless waiting.
One of the two bodyguard martial artists, a man named Jang Hoe, asked Jin Hayeon.
"By the way, is it true that the Young Lord has been learning martial arts for less than four months?"
"That is correct."
"He didn't learn any martial arts before that?"
"This is the first time he has ever learned martial arts."
At Jin Hayeon's answer, a strange light appeared in Jang Hoe's eyes.
"Hah. To think that's his skill level after learning for only about four months."
It was for a short time, but Ilmok had succeeded in holding his own against the Branch Manager.
Of course, all three of them here were stronger than the Branch Manager. The Branch Manager was, at best, just a person in charge among the low-ranking martial artists, not comparable to them, who were graduates of the Demonic Academy.
However, he was still a man in his middle years who had trained in demonic art for decades. The mere fact that Ilmok had withstood such a Branch Manager's attack was a testament to his astonishing level of talent.
To Jang Hoe's reaction, Jin Hayeon replied in a tone filled with uncharacteristic pride.
"Young Lord Ilmok's talent for martial arts is surprising, but his strategic mind is even more magnificent."
"A strategic mind, you say."
"Even now, isn't he ceaselessly writing such a long document based on the brief audit he conducted this morning?"
At Jin Hayeon's praise, the other bodyguard martial artist with them, Gwan Muyeol, frowned and said,
"Hmph. Having many opinions isn't necessarily a good thing. The content must truly be helpful to the Divine Cult, wouldn't you agree?"
"Your problem is that you're too strict."
Jin Hayeon interjected into the conversation between Gwan Muyeol and Jang Hoe.
"I also tend to agree with Martial Artist Gwan's words. It's not necessarily good just because one offers many opinions. However, I believe it will be different with Young Lord Ilmok. Please, just watch."
For some reason, her face was filled with pride. Just like when she spoke of the Heavenly Demon.

★★★

By the time Ilmok finished his work, it was already time for the children to go home.
*This damn side effect.*
Thanks to the combination of his modern memories and the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, Ilmok had reached a state of endlessly pouring out opinions onto paper, from which he had just barely escaped.
However, just because the children had gone home didn't mean Ilmok returned to the inn where he had stayed the previous night.
Having already revealed his identity and dealt with the commotion, Ilmok confidently spent the day being entertained by the Branch Manager.
"Young Lord, is the food to your liking?"
"Oh my. Hahaha. It is to my liking, but the arm that blocked the Branch Manager's saber earlier is throbbing. I think I need to eat more meat for the muscle to heal."
"Eek..."
As Ilmok answered while rubbing his right arm, the pale-faced Branch Manager would rush off and procure meat from somewhere.
"My goodness. The neck that was almost sliced off feels chilly. I think it would be good to warm my body with some nice, hot water."
No sooner had Ilmok finished speaking than a bath of water, hot enough to be mistaken for a hot spring, would appear.
This wasn't voluntary hospitality from the Branch Manager, but forced hospitality exacted by Ilmok.
Watching this for a moment, the bodyguard martial artist Gwan Muyeol frowned and said,
"Young Lord. It is not right for a disciple of the Supreme Lord to seize upon a weakness and persecute a martial artist of the cult. If you continue this, I will have no choice but to report it to the Supreme Lord."
"Oh, is that so?"
But for some reason, at the bodyguard martial artist's criticism, the corner of Ilmok's mouth twisted up.
"Well, well. I suppose I did it without thinking, getting hurt after having to draw my sword and fight myself, even with a bodyguard martial artist right beside me."
"...That's because the Young Lord suddenly went in alone."
"Ah, then I'll just have to report that to Master as well. That I nearly died moving alone, even with bodyguard martial artists by my side. Hahahaha."
"……"
The two bodyguard martial artists, who were looking at Ilmok laughing as if he had caught their weakness, soon turned their heads to look at Jin Hayeon.
*Is that the man you told us to watch?*
At the question in their eyes, Jin Hayeon squeezed her eyes shut.
