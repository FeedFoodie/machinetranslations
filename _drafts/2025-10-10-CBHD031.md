I had an excuse. The only Branch Manager I had ever met was Ma Gangil of Wensu County. Branch Manager Ma Gangil’s side effect was, at worst(?), mania. I figured that if the side effect was just feeling good and laughing heartily, he would have no problem dealing with children.

But on second thought, there was no way every Branch Manager had learned the same demonic art as Ma Gangil.

On top of that.

*I had forgotten just how infuriating it is to deal with dozens of kids!*

Even sane people lose their minds from the stress of dealing with children. I had completely forgotten that mental patients could never endure that stress. However, now was not the time for regrets.

BANG!

"Calm down!" I shouted as I threw the door open before the children could be massacred.

The children gathered in the school turned their eyes to me. The Principal, who was also the Branch Manager, spewed demonic qi and killing intent as his gaze followed theirs.

"How dare you interfere with the sacred event of the Divine Cult!"

The crazed Branch Manager had already started to run rampant. He suddenly yelled and charged at me, cutting through the children. He streamed killing intent and demonic qi as he came.

The killing intent of a middle-aged demonic practitioner poured down on me, someone who had started learning demonic art less than four months ago. Funnily enough, I felt no particular discomfort. From the Master to the group leader Eldest Senior Brother, I had experienced the killing intent of martial artists on a completely different level, so this felt like it didn't even register. You could say my guts had grown bigger than my skills.

SWOOOSH!

The Branch Manager closed the distance instantly, drew the saber from his side, and swung it.

Strangely, I saw the movement perfectly clearly. I had no intention of getting hit by an attack I could see.

"......"

I instantly drew my sword with the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw technique and deflected the Branch Manager’s first attack with the Soul-Luring Sword.

"......"

Despite deflecting the force, my right arm throbbed from the difference in our inner arts. I boldly retreated, using the principles of the Soul-Reaping Flash to check the Branch Manager’s advance with a concise thrust.

"......"
"......"

I took a few steps back, parrying the saber swung by the Branch Manager who charged like a wild boar.

"Die!!"

The Branch Manager finally closed in and swung his saber at me, but I no longer needed to retreat. I had passed through the school’s narrow entrance and emerged onto the wide street.

"How dare you!"

Jin Hayeon and the two bodyguard martial artists then launched their attacks at the Branch Manager who had followed me out.

"Don’t kill him!"

Even without my order, they seemed to have no intention of killing him. The three of them swiftly subdued the Branch Manager by striking his acupoints.

"How dare you persecute me in a Branch of the Divine Cult! Do you bastards even think you can call yourselves martial artists after this!" the Branch Manager screamed with venomous rage, unable to move after his paralysis acupoint was struck.

However, he had chosen the wrong person to scream at.

"You’re the one who should be careful, Branch Manager! Do you even know who you just dared to swing your weapon at!" Jin Hayeon shouted. She suddenly reached into her sleeve and pulled out a token.

"This is Young Lord Ilmok, the eighth disciple of the august one."

The Branch Manager’s attitude completely changed the moment he confirmed the token.

"Hiiik..."

His face, red with anger just moments before, was now deathly pale.

"I dared to swing my weapon at a disciple of the Supreme Lord, who is like the heavens themselves! Please, I beg you, kill me!"

A heretic cultist, looking as if he might burst into tears, begged not for his life but for death. I thought I had grown somewhat accustomed to the Demonic Cult, but I just can’t get used to seeing things like that.

Just then, the children from the school appeared and stood between us and the Branch Manager.

"Don’t bully our Principal!"

"Why are you bullying our Principal!"

The children’s attitude made the Principal’s face grow even darker.

After watching the scene for a moment, I sighed and said, "Let’s go inside the Branch for now. Release the Branch Manager’s acupoints."

{sep}

I entered the Branch with Jin Hayeon, the two bodyguard martial artists, and the Branch Manager of Gaochang County. The Branch Manager’s utterly deflated demeanor was just like a teacher caught doing something wrong by an inspecting scholar.

"Teaching children is harder than you thought, isn’t it?"

"No! It’s because I’m lacking. Please kill me for failing to properly handle even a simple task given by the Heavenly Demon!"

I’m not some executioner from the Joseon Dynasty. Why does he keep asking me to kill him? No matter how I tried to calm him down, he just kept begging for death, so I changed my approach.

"How are the classes being conducted?"

"Are you giving them separate break times?"

"About how many characters do you teach per day?"

As I bombarded him with questions about his teaching methods, the Branch Manager finally began to answer properly. After grasping his current methods, I gave him a few instructions.

"First, if a child doesn’t follow your instructions during class, give them one warning. If they still don’t listen, send them home."

"!? H-How can you tell me to ignore the Heavenly Demon’s command and send a child home?"

"I’m not telling you to actually send them away. It’s a threat. A threat to the children’s parents. I checked last night, and all the parents were delighted that their children could learn to read. What do you think the parents will do if their child gets kicked out for not listening?"

In modern times, they might raise hell, saying something like, ‘Who are you to discriminate against my child?’ However, this place was different.

"Well, wouldn’t they come crying and begging for just one more chance? Or the parents would give the child a severe scolding at home."

"Exactly. There’s no need for you to give in to anger. You just need to do your job. Warn them you’ll send them home, and if that doesn’t work, just send them home. Then, when the parents come asking for forgiveness, you can teach the child again, acting as if you’re letting it slide just this once."

"......"

The Branch Manager let out a cry of admiration, as if he had finally understood my intention.

"As expected of a disciple of the great one! How could you possess such wisdom!"

I gave a dismissive wave at the Branch Manager’s useless flattery.

From my perspective, this wasn’t a very satisfactory solution. In the end, it just passed the corporal punishment onto the parents.

It’s just that.

*If these people, driven mad by demonic art, were to administer corporal punishment, they would likely kill or cripple the child, so I have no choice.*

It was the next-best option to avoid the worst-case scenario. Of course, this measure alone wouldn’t solve all the problems.

"Also, children have limited attention spans. Don’t try to force them to learn too much. It will only make things harder for you, Branch Manager."

"Then, how much would be appropriate?"

"Plan on teaching just eight characters a day. You’ll be teaching the Thousand Character Classic for a year anyway, and at that pace, you can finish one reading in just one hundred and twenty-five days."

The Thousand Character Classic was structured like poetry, composed of 250 four-character verses. In other words, I was telling him to teach only two verses a day.

"You don’t need to teach both verses at once, either. Teach one verse first. Once the children have some understanding and practice their hidden weapons, give them a short break. It’s fine to let them run around and play. Then, after they’ve settled down a bit, you can teach the second verse."

"I understand, Young Lord."

"There’s no need to be impatient or get angry with the children. Do you understand?" I emphasized compulsively.

A chill ran down my spine even now. The school I had casually proposed to the Master simply because I was feeling lazy might be getting children all over Xinjiang killed or crippled by demonic cultists.

"It seems quite a bit of time has passed, so you may go now."

"As you command!"

Despite the long-winded nagging, the Gaochang County Branch Manager looked moved as he bowed to me and hurriedly left.

"Bring me a brush, ink, and paper," I then ordered Jin Hayeon, who was waiting by my side.

{sep}

As soon as Jin Hayeon brought the four treasures of the study, Ilmok began to write ceaselessly on the paper.

*The class schedule and times must be adjusted to the children’s level.*

*The instructors must also be changed. Exclude those who have learned demonic arts related to anger or depression. Someone with mania like Branch Manager Ma Gangil, or someone lacking emotion like Jin Hayeon, would be less likely to harm the children.*

He noted the problems and improvement plans he had observed while looking around the village and the Branch yesterday and today.

However, as he wrote, Ilmok’s inner thoughts were not so comfortable. It wasn’t just because of the guilt of almost getting the children killed.

*This should be enough. It’s a hassle, isn’t this much sufficient?*

Such thoughts came to mind.

[It’s not perfect yet!]

[What will you do if a situation like this occurs? Shouldn’t you prepare a little more?]

Those thoughts kept creeping up from a corner of his mind. The side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword flared up.

"......"

Naturally, Ilmok ceaselessly sought another solution, his hand moving the brush endlessly without rest.

While Ilmok was so engrossed in his work, Jin Hayeon, who stood watching behind him, discreetly sent a telepathic message to the two bodyguard martial artists.

[It seems we will be a disturbance, so how about we withdraw?]

Thinking Jin Hayeon’s words were right, the two bodyguard martial artists carefully opened the door and stepped outside. Jin Hayeon and the two bodyguards then stood guard at the entrance.

Perhaps they grew bored of the endless waiting. One of the bodyguards, a man named Jang Hoe, asked Jin Hayeon a question.

"By the way, is it true that the Young Lord has been learning martial arts for less than four months?"

"That is correct."

"He didn’t learn any martial arts before that?"

"This is the first time he has ever learned martial arts."

A strange light appeared in Jang Hoe’s eyes at Jin Hayeon’s answer.

"Hah. To think that’s his skill level after learning for only about four months."

For a short time, Ilmok had succeeded in holding his own against the Branch Manager. Of course, all three of them here were stronger than the Branch Manager. The Branch Manager was, at best, just a person in charge among the low-ranking martial artists, not comparable to them, who were graduates of the Demonic Academy. However, he was still a man in his middle years who had trained in demonic art for decades. The mere fact that Ilmok had withstood such a Branch Manager's attack was a testament to his astonishing level of talent.

To Jang Hoe’s reaction, Jin Hayeon replied in a tone filled with uncharacteristic pride.

"Young Lord Ilmok’s talent for martial arts is surprising, but his strategic mind is even more magnificent."

"A strategic mind, you say."

"Even now, isn’t he ceaselessly writing such a long document based on the brief audit he conducted this morning?"

At Jin Hayeon’s praise, the other bodyguard martial artist, Gwan Muyeol, frowned and said, "Hmph. Having many opinions isn’t necessarily a good thing. The content must truly be helpful to the Divine Cult, wouldn’t you agree?"

"Your problem is that you’re too strict," Jin Hayeon interjected into the conversation between Gwan Muyeol and Jang Hoe. "I also tend to agree with Martial Artist Gwan’s words. It’s not necessarily good just because one offers many opinions. However, I believe it will be different with Young Lord Ilmok. Please, just watch."

For some reason, her face filled with pride, just like when she spoke of the Heavenly Demon.

{sep}

By the time Ilmok finished his work, the children had already gone home.

*This damn side effect.*

Thanks to the combination of his modern memories and the side effect of the Soul-Reaping Heartless Sword, Ilmok had just barely escaped a state of endlessly pouring opinions onto paper.

However, just because the children had gone home didn’t mean Ilmok returned to the inn. Having already revealed his identity and dealt with the commotion, Ilmok confidently spent the day being entertained by the Branch Manager.

"Young Lord, is the food to your liking?"

"Oh my. Hahaha. It is, but the arm that blocked your saber earlier is throbbing. I think I need to eat more meat for the muscle to heal."

"Eek..."

As Ilmok answered while rubbing his right arm, the pale-faced Branch Manager would rush off and procure meat from somewhere.

"My goodness. The neck that was almost sliced off feels chilly. I think it would be good to warm my body with some nice, hot water."

No sooner had Ilmok finished speaking than a bath of water, hot enough to be mistaken for a hot spring, would appear. This wasn’t voluntary hospitality from the Branch Manager, but forced hospitality exacted by Ilmok.

Watching this for a moment, the bodyguard martial artist Gwan Muyeol frowned and said, "Young Lord. It’s not right for a disciple of the Supreme Lord to seize upon a weakness and persecute a cult martial artist. If you continue this, I’ll have no choice but to report it to the Supreme Lord."

"Oh, is that so?"

But for some reason, the corner of Ilmok’s mouth twisted up at the bodyguard’s criticism.

"Well, well. I suppose I wasn’t thinking, getting hurt because I had to draw my sword and fight myself, even with a bodyguard martial artist right beside me."

"...That’s because the Young Lord suddenly went in alone."

"Ah, then I’ll just have to report that to the Master as well. That I nearly died moving alone, even with bodyguard martial artists by my side. Hahahaha."

"……"

The two bodyguard martial artists looked at Ilmok laughing as if he had caught their weakness, then turned their heads to look at Jin Hayeon.

*Is that the man you told us to watch?*

At the question in their eyes, Jin Hayeon squeezed her eyes shut.
