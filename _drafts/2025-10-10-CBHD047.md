While Ilmok was unconscious, various events unfolded at the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult Main Headquarters. The investigation continued into those involved with Third Young Master Seo Wanpyeong's late-night meal, as well as Jang Hoe, who had attacked Ilmok.

Gwan Muyeol's blade had sliced Jang Hoe's side. Jin Hayeon's palm technique had given his thigh such severe frostbite that it needed amputation. Despite these injuries, Jang Hoe had avoided immediate death.

As the interrogations and investigation proceeded, the evidence began to point toward a specific culprit.

However, the investigation ended in a rather anticlimactic way.

{sep}

Late at night, two old men met in their usual gathering place.

The old man on the right stared at the empty seat once occupied by the Guardian Hall Director, then questioned the man in the center.

"I didn't expect you to handle it yourself."

"It concerned the Guardian Hall Director, so I couldn't entrust it to anyone else."

The old man on the right hesitated at the central man's answer before asking, "But why disguise it as a suicide? Do you really think the Heavenly Demon will fall for such a simple trick?"

One of the two who prepared Seo Wanpyeong's late-night meal, besides Jang Hoe, belonged to the Guardian Hall. Furthermore, the investigation revealed that the Black Bean Demonic Tea found on that person came from the Guardian Hall Director.

And the coffee they called the Black Bean Demonic Tea was something the central old man had given to the Guardian Hall Director.

Naturally, if the investigation and interrogation of the Guardian Hall Director continued, the central old man would inevitably be implicated. So, the central old man decisively cut his ties.

He went and killed the Guardian Hall Director himself, staging it as a suicide by poison. He even forged a suicide note, perfectly mimicking the Guardian Hall Director's handwriting.

The central old man, who had orchestrated everything, replied nonchalantly.

"Of course the Heavenly Demon won't be fooled."

"!?"

Ignoring his companion's shock, the central old man continued with a stoic expression. "But what does it matter? The tail has been cut. It'll be difficult to find us now. The dead tell no tales, do they?"

"Ah…"

The old man on the right let out an exclamation filled with complex emotions, then quickly composed himself.

*…He's even more dangerous than I thought.*

Staging a suicide by poison meant there were no signs of a struggle. The Guardian Hall Director must have been caught off guard, but he was still one of the cult's top experts. To kill such a man without a fight and disguise it as suicide…

While the old man on the right struggled to suppress his shock and fear, the central old man spoke.

"However, this incident will have sharpened the Heavenly Demon's temper, so we should lay low for a while. We've also lost a valuable piece in the Guardian Hall, which will disrupt our grand plan in many ways."

"…I never thought one brat would cause all this trouble."

Both old men simultaneously thought of the Heavenly Demon's youngest disciple.

A brief silence fell before the old man on the right realized something strange and asked a question.

"By the way, do you know the source of that Black Bean Demonic Tea? You said no one would know of it, but if a former inn waiter recognizes it, I can't trust its origin."

At that question, the central old man's expression instantly turned cold. His anger wasn't directed at those who had supplied the Black Bean Demonic Tea.

*…Now that I think about it, the Black Bean Demonic Tea isn't something a mere waiter would know.*

It was an item that had just started trending in the distant Western Regions and was still virtually unknown in the Central Plains or here in Xinjiang. It was something only a handful of people in the entire Central Plains would know, so an innkeeper or a waiter couldn't possibly recognize it.

The problem was that pointing this out to the Heavenly Demon would be tantamount to confessing their own guilt.

*…Should I contact them and have them investigate the Eighth Young Lord's past?*

The old man pondered this for a moment before shaking his head. He couldn't show them any weakness. Being outmaneuvered by a fifteen-year-old brat and appearing incompetent at gathering information could become a liability later.

Besides, there was no need to investigate the Eighth Young Lord's past.

Having organized his thoughts, the central old man smiled coldly, the corners of his mouth lifting.

"Instead of worrying about the source of the Black Bean Demonic Tea, isn't it more important to decide how to deal with that brat?"

"Didn't you say we were going to lay low for a while?"

The central old man gave another cold smile at the question and turned his head to the west. "We don't have to move ourselves. For example, couldn't a mishap occur during training at the Demonic Academy?"

"……"

The old man on the right fell silent. He had never expected the central man's influence to extend as far as the Demonic Academy.

{sep}

A few days later, Ilmok woke up in the clinic. He ate the meal Jin Hayeon brought, as usual, and then sighed deeply.

Ilmok closed his eyes with the face of a prisoner awaiting his final sentence. Soon after, he opened them again with a puzzled look.

"What are you doing?"

Jin Hayeon frowned at Ilmok's question. "What do you mean?"

"Knocking me out. Aren't you going to knock me out?"

For the past few days, Jin Hayeon had forced Ilmok to sleep day and night, waking him only for meals and to relieve himself.

"According to the Demonic Doctor, your body has fully recovered."

"Haaah."

Ilmok sighed in relief without realizing it.

Jin Hayeon watched him for a moment before speaking in her usual blunt tone. "So, you can return to the Wind Cliff Pavilion now. Everything has been prepared."

Ilmok got up and left the clinic with her.

On the way back to the Wind Cliff Pavilion, Ilmok learned from Jin Hayeon what had happened while he was unconscious. She told him that during a large-scale investigation into the Guardian Hall, the Guardian Hall Director was found dead by suicide, along with a note.

When Ilmok arrived at the Wind Cliff Pavilion, he was surprised to find the Heavenly Demon waiting for him.

"I heard from the Demonic Doctor. Since your body has healed, isn't it time to start training?"

"……"

*He wants to work a patient who just got out of bed. What a relentless old man.*

As if reading my thoughts, my Master added, "Aren't you itching to move because of the side effects anyway? It'll be safer for this old man to watch over you than for you to train recklessly on your own."

"Ahem. I can only thank you for your consideration, Master."

I acted grateful and bowed, then drew my sword. Despite the vicious training schedule, it was true that my body was itching for a workout.

"......"

I took a deep breath to calm my mind and recalled the events of that night. I remembered the sight of Jang Hoe's thrown spear flying toward me. The sensation of the world slowing down. The flow of powerful inner arts, starting from my qi center and rushing through all my acupoints.

But…

*Vweeeeng.*

The sword I drew with the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw only let out a sharp ring. It failed to produce the same vivid sword qi as before.

*What is this? It feels like my head remembers it vaguely, but my body can't follow.*

No, it wasn't even properly fixed in my head. The great enlightenment came in a sudden burst, so the flow wasn't properly organized in my mind. On top of that, I was unconscious for days, so the entire realization had grown hazy.

*Damn it. Should I have started training right away back then?*

I tried the Soul-Reaping Quick Draw a few more times, but the result was still lacking.

My Master, who had been watching me, prescribed a drastic remedy.

"Hmm. It seems the enlightenment has faded with time."

"…It seems so."

"Then, perhaps recreating the same situation will bring back some of that realization."

"The same situation…?"

While I stood there looking puzzled, a tremendous wave of energy suddenly erupted from my Master. A terrifying killing intent began to crush my entire body. At the same time, his sword, already drawn, was closing in on my heart.

"Heek!?"

I shrieked involuntarily and swung my sword.

*Slice.*

To my dismay, my sword, which had no sword qi, was instantly cut in half.

My Master stopped his blade just as it touched my chest and spoke.

"It seems you grow stronger faster when your life is in danger, Ilmok. So, we'll train like this until you recover the enlightenment from that day."

"*Gulp*…"

I swallowed hard without thinking.

*I'm not some Super Saiyan from Dragon Ball! How could I possibly get stronger every time I'm about to die?!*

"Ha, hahaha. M-Master? N-no matter what, training and a r-real fight are different, aren't they?"

At my protest, my Master withdrew the sword from my chest and smiled brightly.

"Hohoho. The human body is simpler than you think. Even if your mind knows you won't die, won't your body react on its own? And more importantly."

His smile vanished. He once again unleashed an immense killing intent and wave of energy as he spoke.

"Can you truly be sure that you won't die?"

A chill ran down my spine, and the hair on my body stood on end. The feeling was even more intense than during Jang Hoe's surprise attack.

{sep}

"Heok. Heok."

Only about two hours had passed, but my exhausted body was drenched in sweat as if I'd been caught in the rain. Facing my Master's incredible killing intent and energy for two straight hours was not only physically draining, but the mental toll was also severe.

"Hahaha. See? You can do it."

But in return, sword qi had once again formed on my blade.

*Hwiiing.*

It was a mirage-like sword qi that would scatter the moment my concentration wavered. Thanks to my Master's threats, my focus was forcibly heightened, allowing me to create sword qi intermittently. Of course, maintaining that forced concentration doubled the mental exhaustion.

There was another, more subtle benefit as well.

*I'm so exhausted that the side effects are calming down a little.*

I didn't know if it was because I had managed to recapture some of my previous night's achievement or simply from exhaustion, but the other self that had been screaming in a corner of my mind had quieted down.

"That's enough training for today. Get some rest."

My Master, who had been treating me as if he would kill me for two hours, had returned to his gentle demeanor.

"Master. Before you go, could we talk for a moment?"

Despite my exhaustion, I forced myself to stop him. With the side effects subdued, there were a few things I needed to discuss beforehand.

"Speak."

"I heard a brief summary of the situation from Miss Jin, but something doesn't feel right."

My Master nodded at my point. "This old man is also aware of the problem. However, the Guardian Hall Director's suicide has cut off the trail of evidence. I can't very well interrogate and purge everyone in the Main Headquarters, can I?"

I had to accept my Master's explanation. Given the atmosphere and what I knew of his personality, he wasn't the type to let things end like this. He was probably continuing the investigation in secret, planning to catch the mastermind whole the moment they made a move.

"This old man will keep an eye on that matter, so you just focus on your training. The Demonic Academy entrance examination is less than half a year away."

As my Master said this and turned to leave, I had to quickly stop him.

"Master! There's something else I need to discuss."

In truth, I had only asked about the Guardian Hall Director because Jin Hayeon's story felt suspicious. My real topic was something else entirely.

"Do you remember the black beans we found as evidence?"

"How could I forget? To ordinary people, it's just tea, but to others, it's a poison that amplifies depression. For our Divine Cult, it's no different from a deadly poison."

"That's right, and conversely, like the poisonous mushrooms Gwan Muyeol ate, there must be things that are poisonous but can also be used as medicine."

"…What are you trying to say?"

My Master looked at me with eyes full of anticipation, as if he had already grasped some of what I was getting at.

To meet his expectations, and more importantly, to secure my own future safety, I explained the plan I had diligently formulated while in the clinic, during the times I wasn't unconscious.

"Like the black beans or the poisonous mushrooms, there must be other foods and medicinal herbs in the world that affect a person's mind. Therefore, I believe it would be right to create an institution to research them. In addition, we should train specialized physicians to treat and counsel those suffering from the side effects of demonic arts."

It was, in short, a proposal to establish a psychiatric ward for the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.
