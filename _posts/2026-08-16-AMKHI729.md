The members of spArk spent most of a day waiting for their housemates, each in his own idol-like way. Their days varied, from someone like me who writhed in boredom to a guy who splendidly enjoyed his independent time.

Among them, one person was particularly fussy, and that was Lee Cheonghyeon. Lee Cheonghyeon filmed last due to his schedule, and his restlessness went beyond 'securing screen time like a madman, as an idol should'. The guy truly could not stay still for a moment.

『Cheonghyeon: No way... Is today the day I have to be all alone?』

As soon as Lee Cheonghyeon woke up and entered the living room, he tried hard to deny the silence. He went into Jeong Seongbin's room and our room one by one, then even checked the laundry room and the veranda.

『Cheonghyeon: Did they really all leave? Without anyone here?』

Lee Cheonghyeon's face fell with disappointment after he scoured the dorm. He had been notified about this content long ago and must have known the five of us had finished filming, making it his turn, yet his reaction made it seem like he was the first one abandoned in the dorm.

I could not just say, 'You also left without a word when it was our turn!' The guy genuinely looked lonely. It was hard to tell whether he was that disappointed or whether he was deliberately exaggerating his acting.

The breakfast scene was even more sorrowful. Lee Cheonghyeon was already thin and had lost a lot of weight from his busy life, so he was not on a special diet even with the comeback approaching. When Kang Kiyeon was around, he would hold back out of consideration for his friend, but now that he was alone, he could eat whatever he wanted.

『Cheonghyeon: ...I have no appetite.』

He ordered soybean paste stew from a famous restaurant but could not manage more than a few spoonfuls. All he did was pick out a few pieces of tofu. He struggled for nearly an hour to eat a scoop of rice the size of a baby's fist, not even Choi Jeho's.

If a pork cutlet place served that little rice, they would get a one-star review for skimping.

『Cheonghyeon: Our full group comeback is just around the corner, so why do I have to eat alone? It's unfair.』

He grumbled, chewing on a single piece of zucchini for five minutes.

『Cheonghyeon: I want to hear Iwol-hyung's nagging. I wish Jeho-hyung would just sit in front of me, even if he doesn't say anything. I want to debate with Seongbin-hyung about which army stew place is the best. I want to mix Joowoo-hyung's leftover sauce into my rice and eat twice as much. I want to eat tteokbokki with Kiyeon...』

He burned all the calories he had painstakingly accumulated just by talking. He could have built the Great Wall with his complaints.

≫ The fact that he wants to hear Iwol's nagging must mean he's really lonely.

Meanwhile, people in the comments judged how miserable Lee Cheonghyeon was based on whether he wanted to hear Kim Iwol's nagging. The video had not been up for long, yet hundreds of Sparklers had already liked the comment in agreement.

*He's not the type to over-act just to create an image of a member-loving enthusiast, is he?*

Lee Cheonghyeon had no reason to be less than genuine in front of the iEuro cameras. Everyone knew he was crazy about spArk.

It was not easy to rarely sell songs to others and compose solely for spArk, or to take a flight at any hour after an overseas schedule just to not miss a team activity. Even with an overwhelming lack of practice time, he never showed it and handled all supplementary schedules like fan signs with a bright smile.

The fans knew about Lee Cheonghyeon's passion. They more than acknowledged his hard work, flashing across Europe and Asia. He was far from a situation where he would have to pretend to love the team after the company told him he seemed distant and awkward with the members.

Lee Cheonghyeon mentioned spArk a lot when he went abroad by himself. He would always wonder what everyone was doing, his eyes lighting up with the suspicion they might be having fun without him. It was a joke, but he never once said, 'I like being out here alone.' When asked the common question about the pros and cons of solo activities, the pros did not come easily to him.

After his meal, Lee Cheonghyeon went to the veranda with a lifeless expression. He looked down toward the apartment entrance, even though he knew the structure led directly up from the underground parking lot via an elevator. When he did not see a familiar car, he became visibly crestfallen, like flower tea left to dry on the rooftop.

Lee Cheonghyeon pulled over a small chair Park Joowoo had brought to the veranda and called Kang Kiyeon.

『Cheonghyeon: Kiyeon, when are you coming~?』

『Kiyeon: There's a part of the choreography I want to fix, so I think I'll be late.』

『Cheonghyeon: No, you should've fixed that earlier. What do you mean you're fixing it now? Cheonghyeon's head is going to overload...』

『Kiyeon: It won't. I've never seen your head overload in my life.』

『Cheonghyeon: It's been a while since I've received such an unwelcome compliment.』

Kang Kiyeon said that was enough as long as he knew it was a compliment, and then hung up. Lee Cheonghyeon was alone again.

The guy tried everything to chase away the boredom. Though he finally had a break from writing songs, he opened his composition program, fiddled with it, and then closed it. He pulled out an old file, touched it up a bit, and then closed it again. He seemed to be building a track, then deleted everything he had worked on and saved it in its original state.

He brought a few books to the living room and read them, alternating between the sofa and the floor. The book in his hand changed every ten minutes. One moment it was a textbook, the next a novel, and then non-fiction. There was even a children's book in between.

≫ Is this parallel reading?

Is the content even registering, Cheonghyeon-ah...?

I had heard of parallel reading before, a method of reading several books by alternating between them. People were amazed watching Lee Cheonghyeon read one book while another lay closed on his chest.

Lee Cheonghyeon is a smart guy. His brain was the kind where the contents would not get jumbled just because he jumped from one book to another. If even this was difficult for him, it would have been impossible to juggle being an idol, a university student, and a composer without his schedules getting tangled.

*The real problem is that Lee Cheonghyeon wasn't originally someone who couldn't focus on one thing.*

Lee Cheonghyeon's concentration was far superior to an average person's. The sight of him sitting in one spot and working for hours without getting up was familiar to all of spArk. Even when there was dance practice, he was the type to say, 'I'll finish what I'm doing by this time and join you right away!' He would explain his plan in advance and ask for understanding, so we all knew his usual style.

Even in the 'Study With Me' content, he did not get sidetracked until he finished his assigned portion. Finish one thing, then move on to the next. Lee Cheonghyeon always followed this simple rule.

But now he acted all restless, changing spots because he could not finish one book in a single sitting.

It was not like Lee Cheonghyeon. Even as I glared at the screen, Lee Cheonghyeon put down his book and called Jeong Seongbin.

『Cheonghyeon: Seongbin-hyung, are you going to be late?』

Jeong Seongbin was, of course, going to be late. If Kang Kiyeon was practicing, would Jeong Seongbin not be practicing somewhere too? It is a group comeback. If it were not for this filming, you, Lee Cheonghyeon, would have been summoned to the basement practice room immediately.

Unable to bear the silence, Lee Cheonghyeon also turned on the TV. He kept singing along to songs while reading, however, so he turned the TV off shortly after in an unfortunate incident.

『Cheonghyeon: How could they all leave the house, leaving their little brother behind? So irresponsible. What were they thinking, what if I impulsively committed a rebellious act like mixing up all the clothes while I was left alone and lonely in the dorm?』

If that is the extent of your rebellious act, I would just leave. If it were me, I would go out and have fun too.

The arrow then turned to Choi Jeho. With a few taps of his fingers, Lee Cheonghyeon's private message chat with Choi Jeho filled with long texts. The content was long but could be summarized in seven words: 'I'm bored, so come back quickly.'

Lee Cheonghyeon's text assault did not stop at private messages. The guy also ran to BubblePop. He pulled out old selfies taken with the members, reminisced about memories, and shared stories, holding an online talk show for a full hour. In the midst of it all, he diligently tried to coax Park Joowoo, saying he would type on the keyboard for him if he came.

I decorated the grand highlight.

『Iwol: Why are you so bored? Do you want to go to work?』

『Cheonghyeon: Yeah.』

『Iwol: You must be sick. Go get some sleep.』

『The call was disconnected』

This scene, titled 'You want to go to work? You must be crazy,' spread across all sorts of online communities. This short clip got more views than the video of Kim Iwol guarding the dorm alone.
