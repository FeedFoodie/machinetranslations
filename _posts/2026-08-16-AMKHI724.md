The screen turned completely monotone the moment the part's focus changed, as if a black-and-white filter had been applied. A man appeared with eyes so heavily made up that you could tell even without color. His hair was stylishly swept back, and he wore a striped suit with only the top button of his shirt undone. This was the very person who had made Baek Haewon sigh.

*Is this a movie star concept?*

It was not just his Hollywood actor-like style. Seeing props like an antique makeup mirror, a chair, and a script that celebrities from a bygone era might have used, Baek Haewon felt her deduction was correct.

Kim Iwol possessed a classic feel, both different from and similar to the look Jeong Seongbin had styled for him. If the Kim Iwol in Jeong Seongbin's photos was close to a star's daily life, the Kim Iwol in the music video was a living legend, breathing and shining in the present.

Above all, his face was unrivaled. When the camera was at eye level, the scene was black and white. The moment the composition shifted to a high angle and became colorful, Baek Haewon was left speechless.

It was not just the lights in the bulbs decorating the mirror, the black set with its red accents, or the shoes of the bustling people. When the gray filter lifted, his transparent, bright crimson hair was revealed, dazzlingly reflecting the lights. His deep, dark pupils shone under the illumination.

If there was a question asking what is brighter than 『Hideout』, redder than 『Bright』, and more transparent than 『Sprint』, Baek Haewon, who had only watched Kim Iwol for five years, would surely answer immediately that it was the Kim Iwol from 『Alone』.

*Weren't the concept photos ash gray, even though they weren't the black-and-white version?*

Baek Haewon clearly remembered the gray-haired Kim Iwol she had burned into her eyes when she pre-ordered the album. In the concept photos and photo cards released at the time, Kim Iwol had hair that was undeniably a light silver with an ashen tone. Baek Haewon's data, compiled from exclusively following spArk, could not be wrong, and it was not as if they would have just changed the hair color with Photoplat.

The truth behind this strange phenomenon was not hard to guess. They must have filmed two versions, whether by using a temporary spray or some other method, simply for the impact of this one scene. Kim Iwol was more than willing to endure that much trouble.

*No wonder he didn't do the comeback live at the same time as the music video release!*

How could he do this unless he was determined to make a huge impact? She had been tricked by Kim Iwol like this time and time again.

Even though all the necessary buttons were fastened, the prominent wrist bone on his exposed wrist kept stealing Baek Haewon's gaze. Whether he knew a fan's heart or not, Kim Iwol opened a translucent door and stared endlessly somewhere beyond the mirror, not at the script or the staff, like someone waiting for another to arrive.

The parade of breathtaking handsomeness continued even after the next member appeared. A prime example was Choi Jeho's face, his brow furrowed as he examined a watch with a loupe. A handsome, platinum-blonde man in a pure white work uniform could not look anything but beautiful while concentrating.

He had a great body and was meticulous with his self-care, yet he was no picky eater. At the same time, he was not without a gourmet's disposition. He had good intuition and talent, so whenever a dance practice video was released, at least 100 clips of his genius moments would be made. He also had a surprisingly serious side and knew how to quietly get his work done.

For one person to be endowed with all these attributes, he should just stay silent even if people called it cheating. Kim Iwol insisted that the Banana leaf incident negated all these advantages, but one could not deny the reality that Watchmaker Choi Jeho was so awesome that the stock for Hwang-kwon was already about to be halted from its explosive rise.

Park Joowoo on the lake was beyond words. When Park Joowoo first appeared, Baek Haewon thought he stood on a shallow puddle. Gentle ripples seemed to spread out in a circle from him, but since the water's surface did not move, it seemed to be an artificial creation using something solid. The artificial lake, which shone like a glass craft, was surrounded by well-tended plants and small flowers.

As the camera zoomed out, a symmetrically decorated garden and a vividly colored bridge filled the screen. The landscaping was perfect.

Park Joowoo's skills had always been outstanding. The Meboz was the pride of Sparkler. In particular, Park Joowoo was a talent praised in unison by his seniors and juniors in the entertainment industry. His vocal tone was still as cold and transparent as glass.

A fluid, flowing quality that was not just sharp had emerged, however. The new weapon Park Joowoo had acquired was powerful. It delivered catharsis even without fast electronic sounds or high-pitched belted notes.

When Lee Cheonghyeon's side profile was added to this, she could not take it anymore. Through a small window that seemed carved through a wooden wall, Lee Cheonghyeon watched the rain fall outside. The effect of raindrops flowing over his pupils ensured there was never a dull moment, even when his large, shining eyes filled the screen.

The room was filled with magazines, books, and game consoles. Lee Cheonghyeon showed no interest in any of it, though. He sat quietly on a beanbag with a bored expression or stood vacantly while looking outside. He did not even glance at the booklet he had been reading, which now lay face down on the floor.

His indifferent downward gaze and the jawline revealed by his lowered chin were perfect no matter how you screenshotted it. A melancholy handsome man was a coveted commodity in the idol scene that you could not get even if you wanted to. What Sparkler could resist when it was served up with Lee Cheonghyeon's face? At least, there seemed to be none among Baek Haewon's Sparkler friends.

*Where did he get this maturity?*

Lee Cheonghyeon, who was in charge of visuals for the "cold handsome man" group spArk, may have had the personality of the team's vitamin, but his face had never been warm. Kim Iwol might ramble about being a sun that creates a white night on its own, but Lee Cheonghyeon had never possessed any warmth.

His nicely upturned lips when he smiled and his crescent-moon eyes had neutralized his coldness, however. This time, Lee Cheonghyeon exuded a cold aura like dry ice that sublimates at room temperature. That was why the rain falling outside Lee Cheonghyeon's cabin did not seem like a summer rain at all.

Every member was wistful and melancholic. Not a single one of them looked energetic. She wondered why they had given up on natural light, but it turned out to be a grand plan to aim for a pathetic beauty. She should have known when the sky in the teaser was pale.

In the cross-edited group dance scene, the members appeared in knits and slacks styled to suit their individual tastes. They did not run around ceaselessly like in typical dance tracks. A calmer, yet far more organized and organic choreography filled its place. The members' backs were shown in slow motion as they ran toward a field.

The music video ended with the resonance of the instruments. The song ebbed from her earphones like a tide. The worries of her daily life had long since vanished.

It was music worth attending broadcast recordings for, despite the broadcasting stations' unchanging attitude of treating fans like dirt. That was why Baek Haewon, despite suffering every time she went, sought out the broadcast station again. She received a lunch box that felt like it would make one of her arms thicker, got a cup of black bean soy latte wishing for a healthy meeting, and took a proof shot.

Meanwhile, her admirable idols had even called a coffee truck and kindly put the reverse-support drinks into bottles. It was a thoughtful gesture for the fans who could not drink them right away because they had to rest or go to the bathroom in a space where outside drinks were not allowed during the waiting time. Because of their kindness, Baek Haewon wrote about 60 gushing posts about spArk on her private account, which was only for her closest friends.

The live performance she heard in person from the audience seats was even better than the recorded version. She could instantly tell what the light and refreshing feeling, the cool atmosphere, and the lingering effect of the effector that had impressed her when she first heard the song were all designed for.

*The sense of space is insane.*

She had learned many metaphors while being a fan of Kim Iwol. This was the limit of the commentary that could come from Baek Haewon's head, however, as the only professional terms she had learned from her long K-idol fandom life were slang like "damping."

*I can't even properly say how good it is. Iwol-ah, can you please open a live lecture on music terminology? I've already watched all your WebCell lectures...*

Still, it was great to see Kim Iwol with light red hair. She thought it would be similar to light pink, but the feeling was subtly different. Our boy has cherry hair now! Not the dark red California Cherry, but a transparent, glossy cherry!

Above all, it was fun to look up reactions. After only hearing about other groups getting criticized, wondering when her group would have a comeback, or hearing that her group was on a decline, there was nothing more enjoyable than seeing social media filled with news of her favorite's activities.
