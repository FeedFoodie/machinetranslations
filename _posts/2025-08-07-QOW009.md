"I surprised you, calling so suddenly, didn't I?" Catherine asked as she gently pushed a cup of deeply brewed tea toward Gai.

The sight of her hands, so delicate they seemed painted, made Gai's heart pound as if it would burst. As her fingertips brushed against the teacup, he noticed the faint, maddeningly lovely flower dye on her nails. He had always thought her a goddess from afar, but seeing her this close, she was the very picture of a fairy.

"Ah, not at all, milady," Gai replied. "If you call, I'll come running anytime."

He had worried his voice might tremble, but to his relief, it emerged in its usual low, deep tone. He felt a flicker of pride for sounding so calm, especially since he had often been told he had a nice voice.

"How could I carelessly summon someone as busy as you?" Catherine said, offering a shy smile.

She looked like a single blooming flower, and Gai prayed this moment was not a dream. In that instant, he felt he could die for her.

"I'd come running from the middle of enemy lines if you called, milady."

"Oh my! Your words alone are more than enough," she said with a light laugh. "Hoho."

"Please, milady, speak comfortably with me."

"You're a precious person who works day and night for our marquisate. I couldn't possibly do that."

Gai fell silent, unsure how to respond.

"I'm doing this for my own peace of mind," she added gently, "so please don't worry about it. Alright?"

Her smile, as she lightly covered her mouth, was lovely. She was so beautiful, with a heart as fine as silk. He found himself madly envious of whoever would one day become the perfect Lady Catherine's husband.

Catherine eased his tension with talk of trivial things like the weather, desserts, and the opera. Only then did she belatedly arrive at her main point.

"Oh, by the way," she said, her tone casual as if the thought had just occurred to her, "I heard you went downtown with my sister?"

"Yes, that's right."

The smile vanished from his face, his expression hardening in an instant. Though Catherine feigned a sip of tea with her gaze lowered, she did not miss the way his brow furrowed at the mention of Estella's name.

"Where did you go?"

"We went to Lily's Dress Shop."

"Ah... and you didn't stop anywhere else?"

"No, but... may I ask why you're asking?" Gai trailed off, belatedly noticing the strange persistence in her tone. He realized with a jolt that this very moment was an opportunity, a chance to create something special between them.

"My sister is so naive," Catherine sighed. "I worry she might be swayed by a bad person. Ah, really, if she was going to a dress shop, she should have gone with me."

"We only went to the dress shop," Gai assured her, his voice low and his gaze tender. "Please don't worry."

"Thank you. Since you're in charge of my sister's escort, I'll be counting on you. Please stay by her side and watch over her. Make sure she doesn't meet any strange people and get hurt."

"Of course, milady," he promised. "Don't worry. I'll do my best."

"The tea is especially fragrant today, isn't it?" Catherine changed the subject, smiling brightly as she brought the teacup to her lips once more.

That single smile, tossed out like a charitable offering, made Gai feel as if he had gained the world.

The day Estella lost another ten kilograms was the same day Dolan's exercise equipment began to arrive at the castle, one piece after another. Dying of curiosity about the purpose of the strange items, Dolan had sped up his work, finishing five days ahead of schedule. He only realized afterward that he had been played right into Estella's hands, a fact he found rather amusing.

Three wagons in total arrived, each loaded with the equipment. While the number of items was not large, their construction of solid iron and sturdy wood necessitated the use of separate carts.

The blacksmith's workers began unloading the equipment and carrying it into the prepared training hall. Most of them were from Dolan's own forge, their curiosity about the true nature of the items they had helped create compelling them to volunteer for the delivery.

The knights paused their training, their attention drawn to the workers carrying strange-looking contraptions into the warehouse. They watched, wondering what was going on.

"What is all that?" the knight O'Nell asked, his brow furrowed as he watched the chunks of iron disappear into the warehouse.

"I heard Lady Estella is building a training hall," replied Denian, a newly minted knight. His expression was one of pure curiosity. As interested in physical training as anyone, he was extremely eager to see what kind of hall the lady was creating.

O'Nell, like the other knights, had not paid much attention to Estella at first, but after seeing her circle the training grounds every single day without fail, and watching her body grow more agile by the day, his curiosity had been naturally piqued.

The workers carried the equipment into the converted warehouse without a break. Located next to the training grounds, the building was quite spacious.

*This must be over a thousand square feet, right?*

When it was empty, Estella had worried if all the equipment would fit, but now that everything was installed, there was room to spare. She realized she could even order a few more pieces later.

When she first opened her eyes in this world, everything had felt hopeless. Now, with her own private training room and proper equipment, it felt like a rather successful life.

*But this is just the beginning.*

The old Seoyi would have been satisfied with this alone, but she was not just Yoon Seoyi anymore. She was also Estella, the woman who had been thoroughly used and abandoned for being too kind. The two egos, once disconnected, were rapidly merging, and it felt as if a hidden part of her personality was finally revealing itself.

An expression of pure satisfaction spread across Estella's face as she surveyed the scene, even clapping her hands together. It was time to truly begin her transformation.

"Alright, shall we get started?" she murmured, a wide smile on her face as she lifted the nearest dumbbell. She had to move diligently, if only to shed this soft, slime-like flesh.

Estella vigorously lifted the dumbbell, and the blacksmiths watched with wide eyes, anticipating what would happen next. Their interest quickly faded when she simply repeated the motion of lifting and lowering the chunk of iron instead of throwing it. They had abandoned their work and come all this way expecting something amazing, making this a real disappointment. To them, she was just a noble lady making a strangely happy face while lifting an odd-looking lump of iron.

Only Dolan watched her with shining eyes. He knew noble ladies. Raised in luxury, they wore only pretty things and adorned themselves with precious items, acting as if their arms would fall off if they lifted anything heavier than a flower. They never exited a carriage without an escort, revered like delicate flowers or fragile porcelain.

However, Estella was different. She deftly picked up the cast-iron dumbbells he had made, one in each hand. At five kilograms each, they were not impossibly heavy, but it was still a considerable weight for a noble lady to lift in one hand.

Engrossed in trying out the various pieces of equipment, Estella suddenly felt a gaze from behind and turned. The Knight Commander, Lucius, and the blacksmith, Dolan, stood just outside the wide-open door, staring blankly inside. More precisely, they were staring at her. Thrilled to finally be doing proper strength training, she had been excitedly trying everything.

*Oops.*

She stopped and approached the two men, wiping the beads of sweat from her brow with the back of her hand.

"Sir Lucius. Dolan."

Estella's voice was slightly breathless. Startled as if waking from a sleep, both men answered politely.

"Your Grace."

"Yes, Your Grace."

Lucius was inwardly surprised as he watched her approach with long, unhesitating strides. The coolly upturned corners of her mouth and her confident gaze made it clear this was not the timid Estella of the past. She had lost so much weight that even her walk was different, making the change all the more dramatic. He wondered what on earth could have changed the young lady so much.

"Would you like to come in and have a look?" she asked.

"Yes, of course."

Lucius accepted her hearty invitation and quickly stepped inside, his curiosity already piqued. Dolan followed, entering hesitantly as he gauged the situation.

Lucius already knew from the Marquis that Estella intended to build a training hall, but knowing about it and seeing it in person were two very different things. He considered himself an expert in physical training, yet most of the objects before him were things he had never seen in his life. While he could guess the use of some at a glance, many others left him completely baffled.

"Are you curious about how to use them?" Estella asked, seeming to read his mind.

"Pardon? Ah, yes," Lucius replied. "I'd be grateful if you would show me."
