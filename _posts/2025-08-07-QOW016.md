"Haah, Catherine. What am I to do with you?" Dmitri sighed, raising a hand to brush away her tears. 

Catherine flinched from his touch, turning to wipe them away herself.

A flash of anger tightened Dmitri's jaw. He could not understand how Estella failed to see the sincerity in a girl as kind as Catherine, and what was she sulking about now, anyway? For the past three months, she had refused to even see him.

"So where is Estella, then?" he asked.

Dmitri was determined to see her today, if only for Catherine's sake. He could already imagine his father's reaction if he learned that he had not laid eyes on his own fiancée for three consecutive months.

"Ah... My sister is just in the city for a bit..."

"Catherine?"

Catherine was scrambling for an excuse, desperate to prevent a meeting between Dmitri and Estella, when the one voice she least wanted to hear echoed from behind her. At the sound of it, she squeezed her eyes shut.

*Of all times! Worthless bitch.*

Nevertheless, Catherine possessed a natural talent for concealing her emotions. The corners of her mouth, which had trembled only a moment before, lifted as brightly as the tail feathers of a canary. It was a smile so lovely that one could not help but smile in return. With practice, her acting had improved to the point where her expressions perfectly mirrored her thoughts, a fact that made her incredibly proud. 

*I'm someone who tries so hard.*

"Oh my, Sister! I thought you went downtown?" Catherine exclaimed, her expression one of complete ignorance, and her voice laced with what sounded like genuine delight at the chance encounter.

Had Estella not known her sister's true self, had she not known what the future held, she might have paused to reflect on her own deep misunderstanding.

"No. I was on my way to the training grounds and heard you were going too." Estella glanced at the carriage, from which a sweet scent wafted, and explained herself. A chance visit to the kitchen had revealed Catherine's plan to take snacks to the knights. The ploy was so obvious that Estella had come out purely to interfere.

*But I never expected to run into such an unwelcome face.*

Her childish desire to tease Catherine soured instantly to regret as she looked upon the two faces she despised most.

*To think I forgot that today was the day.*

She knew Dmitri had visited the marquisate to see her, but she had no desire to meet him and had sent him away with one excuse after another. More than anything, she was all too aware of the relationship between him and Catherine. The old Estella might have tolerated it, but she no longer wanted to endure their detestable act. The memory of their wedding day, of her hiding in a corner to stifle her sobs, surfaced with a sudden, sharp clarity that made her shudder.

Estella stared blankly at the two of them standing side-by-side like a pair of cockroaches before whipping her head away. Just looking at them felt as if it would inflict her with every eye disease imaginable, from astigmatism and myopia to strabismus, styes, glaucoma, and cataracts. Her eyesight was precious.

"Estella?"

The woman he saw after three months was a completely different person. Gone was the body that looked as if it could roll faster than it could walk. She was half her former size, her face now distinctly different. In her newly chiseled jawline, he thought he could even see a hint of the girl she had once been. Her blue eyes, once buried beneath puffy eyelids, now seemed twice as large, and her blunt nose had become considerably sharper. The belly fat that had strained the seams of her dresses was gone, revealing a neckline that was no longer lost in her chin.

Seeing the dazed look on Dmitri's face, Catherine quickly linked her arm through Estella's, pressing snugly against her side. Though Estella had lost a great deal of weight, the difference in their builds was still significant when compared to the willowy Catherine. The stark contrast between them snapped Dmitri, who had been staring as if bewitched, back to his senses.

*She's still just Estella.*

Deep within his heart lay a profound disdain for her. He hated the way she mumbled, chewing on her words until they were incomprehensible. He despised her gluttony and her complete lack of interest in anything besides eating. Even more detestable was her insistence on acting like his fiancée without knowing her place. The more he thought about it, the more he realized she was a woman composed of everything he disliked.

Then why did his gaze keep drifting to her?

Puzzled for a moment, Dmitri decided it was just an old habit. It was the pity that lingered in a corner of his heart, a hint of guilt, and a meager sense of courtesy toward an old friend.

"Ah, Dmitri." Estella finally turned to him, her voice reluctant as she spoke his name. Her childhood memories were filled with him and him alone. He was the kind boy who had offered her a handkerchief when she cried for her mother, the brilliant child who, despite being her age, seemed to know everything. Dmitri had been the only certainty in her uncertain future.

When she realized he had become unable to take his eyes off Catherine, it had felt as if her world was collapsing, but the kind, foolish Estella had acted oblivious, afraid her petty jealousy would make them uncomfortable. Now, she stared at the man who was once the sole purpose of her existence with cold, vacant eyes.

Even her voice was different. Dmitri was momentarily taken aback but quickly regained his composure.

*She's still just Estella.*

He stared at her, his habitual, vague disdain punctuating the thought. "Today's our designated meeting day. I came last month, and the month before, but you weren't around."

"I know," Estella remarked nonchalantly, glancing at the girl beside her. "And you met with Catherine before you left, didn't you?"

Dmitri's brows furrowed at her dismissive attitude. She seemed to have no interest in him whatsoever. The surprise was so great he felt he might fall over, but he held himself in check for Catherine's sake. The tenderhearted Catherine would surely be hurt by even his smallest reaction. Steeling himself once more, he focused on Estella.

"You've lost a lot of weight," he began, clicking his tongue as he took in her training trousers. "It's nice to see you trying to change, but what in the world are you wearing?" It looked like an attempt to stand out, but choosing an outfit like that only made the contrast with Catherine more obvious. He sighed, his gaze shifting between Catherine, dressed as splendidly as a violet, and Estella, who looked like a clumsy foal.

"Thanks for the compliment. You two have a nice time. I'm a bit busy."

Dmitri was slightly flustered that she paid his criticism no mind, but he refused to let it show. She was not even looking at him anymore.

"Catherine, since I'm going that way anyway, I'll deliver the snacks. You must be tired."

"What? T-That's..." Catherine's large eyes went wide, her expression one of utter helplessness. Estella was disgusted by her act of being the most harmless creature in the world, but she endured it. It was not yet time to fully reveal her hand.

"You need to entertain our guest," Estella continued. "Dmitri came all this way, so please take care of him for me."

"But I prepared each and every one of these for the knights myself..."

The kitchen staff had toiled all night, yet Catherine spoke as if she had made everything herself. Estella snorted in derision.

"Then should I stay with Dmitri? I don't mind."

"What?" Catherine flinched as Estella spoke with a bright smile.

She hated the idea of Estella taking the snacks she had 'prepared' to the knights, but she hated the thought of Estella spending time alone with Dmitri even more. Of course, it was not as if Dmitri would fall for Estella just because they spent some time together.

Catherine's deliberation did not last long. If she disliked both options, she simply had to choose neither.

"Why don't we all go together instead?" she suggested. "How about you come along too, Dmitri? It'll be a chance to see an old friend." She was referring to Gai, whom she had recently invited for tea.

Dmitri pretended to think it over, humming for a moment before finally nodding. "A fine idea. We can go together, then."

In truth, he and Gai were hardly what one would call close. He could not stand how Gai, who came from a mere family of knights, acted so arrogant and aloof, relying solely on his skill with a sword. His own dignified standing prevented him from openly revealing his disdain, so he simply exchanged greetings just enough to avoid awkwardness whenever they met. Hearing that man's name from Catherine's lips soured his mood and made him even more determined not to let her go alone, yet even so, he found himself sneaking glances at Estella, who was not even looking in his direction.
