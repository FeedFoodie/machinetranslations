"Good morning, Mr. Iwol."

The manager extended his hand. His face looked familiar, but his attire felt entirely strange. The manager, who normally loved sweatshirts and jeans, transformed his image completely with a cable-knit sweater.

Even while shaking his hand, I could not wake up from the feeling of a dream. Reality stared right back at me. Even during the brief moment I squeezed and released his hand, bewilderment gripped me.

"Is Manager-nim really the person who's becoming my friend today?" I asked again, unable to believe it.

Even that awkward smile belonged to the real Manager Daeyeon. He suggested we sit down and talk, so I awkwardly took a seat.

"You two are the new friends who'll spend a one-day outing together and get close."

PD-nim gave his official confirmation. I could not even guess what was happening.

"No, but he's our manager! He's someone who's shared thick and thin with spArk for years!"

Was I about to become a rude idol who was not even close to his own manager? Would I really hear the broadcast narration say, 'Mr. Iwol, try getting along with your manager from now on'?

I desperately tried to clarify. My vision went dark anyway. They told me to dress nicely, so today must be my funeral. No wonder they handed me a white shirt.

"I came here without knowing my partner, but Manager-nim..."

"I applied directly, requesting to appear with Mr. Iwol."

The manager flashed a pleasant smile. To me, it looked like the final kindness of the Grim Reaper.

The manager took the risk of exposing his face publicly and mustered the courage to borrow a broadcast show's power, all just to improve his relationship with me.

"Manager-nim... Are we not close...?" I asked in a trembling voice.

*I can hear the bell tolling for my idol career. I'm sorry for staying up late without listening to the managers. I deeply reflect on my actions. I won't forget the grace of the managers until the very end...*

{sep}

The filming proceeded by fulfilling the wishlist items we wrote during the pre-interview, one by one. I had mentioned wanting to have tea time to talk with someone I met for the first time.

Because of that, Manager Daeyeon and I had to drink beverages at a table in the middle of a vast cafe in Han Gawoon.

"There are a lot of desserts, so which one would you like? Mont Blanc? Cake? I saw tarts on the way in too. However, Manager-nim, you don't really like super sweet things, right?"

"Your memory is really good. I eat other things well, too."

"It's not often that the two of us get to eat delicious food together, so you shouldn't just order anything, but something you like. How about ordering a combination like one refreshing option and one with cream cheese?"

We ordered snacks too. Since I could not eat much while promoting, the manager requested a dessert other than tiramisu or Earl Grey cake, even though he knew the idol sitting across from him would not touch it anyway.

Even during our first meeting, the manager was just as attentive. He checked what the members could not eat in advance and asked if there was anything else to watch out for.

Seeing that made me resolve to keep him at this company for the rest of his life. Old memories bubbled up one after another.

"Since you came out to become friends like this... can I take it to mean you don't plan to resign in the near future?"

"You ask me whenever you're about to forget, so are you really that worried about it?"

"Do I seem like I'm acting? Should I call the guys and verify how sincere I am about the managers' long-term service? Looking up at the sky, I've never spoken a single word of exaggeration in front of you, Manager-nim."

The manager tried to gloss over the situation, saying that would not be necessary. Since I was the manager's good idol in charge, I let it slide as he wished.

"Did you drive here? If I'd known, we could've come together!"

"I wanted to make a surprise appearance. You didn't notice at all?"

"I really had no idea. How could you fool me so completely? Is our relationship only worth this much?"

"Right? I thought Mr. Iwol would notice, but I guess our relationship really is only worth this much."

I suffered a critical blow from that response. I usually kept track of which manager accompanied each member's schedule, so I could not refute his point about why I did not know this time.

More than that, if I had known my partner was Manager Daeyeon, I would not have spent time chatting cozily in a cafe. It became a coffee chat with the location shifted from the company building to the outside world.

Besides, sitting in a cafe with Manager Daeyeon tanked my right to speak vertically. I had a history of actively pushing to visit a cafe with the manager, only to cause a major accident.

Right after the accident, the manager did not say much. He supported me completely so I could focus on rest and recovery.

He scolded me severely after I completely healed. Ever since then, I barely mentioned the letter 'c' in cafe. I only cautiously added chamomile tea whenever Jeong Seongbin asked what everyone wanted to drink as a group.

Past days of ordering while barely keeping my shoulders straight flashed through my head.

*Is this the first time I've come to a cafe alone with Manager-nim since that day...?!*

I swallowed my tears inside. Fortunately, Manager Daeyeon responded to the conversation with a bright smile.

If he had said something like, "Something like that happened before, haha. You were really cheeky, you know?", I would not have known what to do with myself and would have bowed my head to the ground first.

"This is the first time I've seen Manager-nim dressed like this. You usually wear sweatshirts. The knit sweater suits you well!"

"Really? I prepared it so we could hang out dressed similarly."

"Did you prepare it to match on purpose?"

"I asked Stylist Seungha in advance what Mr. Iwol would be wearing today. However, just because the clothes are similar doesn't mean my visuals become like a celebrity. How embarrassing."

I thought I just needed to gratefully accept the clothes given to me and wear them without wrinkles, but I was arrogant. I had no choice but to kneel before the manager's thorough preparation.

This was no time to scold the other guys to stay sharp. I needed to pull myself together first.

"Hold on. What else did I write down besides the cafe?"

"Didn't you write places you want to go play or something?"

"As if I would write something like that. Manager-nim drives a lot as it is, so what if it turned out to be another long distance?"

Just as I was worrying, we received notice to fulfill the manager's wishlist. At the manager's instruction to get in the car, I sat in the passenger seat as if spellbound.

It was a useless thought, but the vehicle provided as product placement was spacious and nice inside. Far from driving a car like this myself, I ended up making the manager drive again.

This was no time to praise Jeong Seongbin for passing his written exam. I should have opened a study book next to him.

I should not have brushed off the advice around me that if you do not get a driver's license at twenty, you never will. Since I could not create a non-existent license now, I wanted to escape out the window.

"Where are we going?"

"A good place to talk with Mr. Iwol."

"You really have a knack for making people's hearts flutter."

The course chosen by the manager, who requested the meeting, had atmosphere. The vast botanical garden offered a great setting for strolling leisurely in the sunlight.

Unlike me, who picked a cafe just to chatter, his choice reflected the sense of a mature adult.

"It's been a while since we walked quietly outdoors, right?"

"Especially while the sun is up. It's thanks to you, Manager-nim."

Where should we go so I can take a once-in-a-lifetime photo for the manager? As I quickly scanned our surroundings, the manager called out to me.

"Mr. Iwol."

"Yes!"

"How long are you going to keep calling me Manager-nim?"

"Huh?"

What else would I call Manager-nim if not Manager-nim?

The manager kindly offered a supplementary explanation as I stood there blinking.

"Only Mr. Iwol calls me Manager-nim."

"Ah."

In spArk, I was the only one who did not call the managers hyung. This was true even though Manager Chanyoung was the only one with a difference in how long we had known each other since trainee days. I even addressed Dong Euion, who was younger than me, with '-ssi' and used formal speech.

Perhaps that was why Manager Daeyeon always called me 'Mr. Iwol'. Manager Chanyoung called us by name because he had watched us since our trainee days, and Dong Euion called us hyung since he was younger than spArk, but a situation had arisen where only Manager Daeyeon attached '-ssi'.

"If that's comfortable, it's fine, but I wondered if you still find me difficult to be around."

*So that's why he applied to come out with me.*

It turned out to be fate that Manager Daeyeon, of all managers, and I, of all members, ended up appearing together. Cold sweat ran down my back.

'spArk Iwol, distant relationship with manager... "Let me become close now"' No. I could even write the article on his behalf as soon as recording ended.

"Is it because I find the managers uncomfortable? I, of course, act out of respect and love..."

"I know Mr. Iwol takes good care of the staff."

The manager let out a hearty laugh. Teasing me seemed quite entertaining to him. Now that I gave the manager a laugh like this, could Kim Iwol, who built a wall with titles, be forgiven even a little for his mistake?

"Looking back, my sin is grave..."

"Hahaha!"

The manager's laughter rang out in a remarkably refreshing way. Seeing him dressed up stylishly to bury me in a grave made me wonder if this was the spot for my resting place.

For a moment, I imagined calling the managers hyung. Chanyoung-hyung? Daeyeon...hyung? Euion...?

It felt as though I was doing something that violated Confucian values. How could he do this without knowing what I might do if I stopped politely calling him Manager-nim? Do I look like someone with such flawless moral character?

"Even if you don't completely change how you address me... I'd like to hear you call me hyung at least once before filming ends."

The manager hammered the nail into my death sentence. I had a mere four hours remaining. A hellish time limit had arrived.
