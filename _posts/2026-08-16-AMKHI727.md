The 'spArk Waits for the Members' series was specially scheduled for twice-a-week uploads to reduce lulls and provide quick teasers. Thanks to this schedule, we could check in on each other's solo skills every three to four days.

First up was Choi Jeho. I worried less about him writhing in boredom than about him not providing enough footage. I was so concerned we would get nothing usable that I did not even nag him with things like 'Don't do XX'. We had to film *something* to get any usable clips, right?

"We're filming your daily life, so don't just lie there. If you think of something, do it. Got it?"

"Yeah."

"Are you going to answer that half-heartedly?"

"How am I supposed to answer to sound sincere?"

I barely resisted hanging him from the veranda. It would be horrifying if the self-produced content started with our center member hanging from a laundry rack, catching the autumn breeze. Choi Jeho should be grateful I care so much about his image.

I grew so anxious that I called him in the middle of the shoot.

*Our immature Emperor might be bored and just lying around on his phone.*

I took time from my break to call him.

[You called. What is it?]

...That was his opening line. I barely resisted the urge to scream at him to turn off the camera.

What was even more infuriating was that this was Choi Jeho's version of being mindful of the cameras. Normally, Choi Jeho would have ended it with a single word, "Why?" I was so moved by the sincerity of him making his sentence nine times longer that I almost cried tears of blood.

After a nerve-wracking day at work, I came home. Just as I had expected, Choi Jeho was perfectly dry and peaceful, while I was the only one drenched in sweat. Lee Cheonghyeon, who had gotten home first, ate pear stew with a face just as carefree as Choi Jeho's.

"What's this?"

"Jeho-hyung made it!"

"Want some too?"

*The guy who can't even properly flip a kimchi pancake made a stew?*

Half-doubting, I took a bite. It was absurdly delicious. He must have made enough for six people, because plenty was left even after Lee Cheonghyeon and I each had a glass.

What kind of day Choi Jeho had spent was revealed soon after.

The video showed him waking up leisurely around 11 AM. Perhaps because I had filmed first, the second person up was not too surprised by the sudden solo mission. Unlike me, who had crawled from my room like a ghost wrapped in a blanket, Choi Jeho made a grand entrance, vigorously ruffling his hair.

Then he sat on the sofa and spaced out for about five minutes. A helpful caption read '~Contemplating how to spend the day~', but his head looked completely empty.

A moment later, Choi Jeho got up and did something horribly unimaginable. He took out all the leftovers from the fridge, added gochujang and sesame oil, and mixed it all into a hearty bibimbap. He showed no sense of duty as an idol with a comeback on the horizon.

≫ The Eldest Hyungs

I almost fainted when I saw a comment on the timeline that said, 'Same ingredients, different visuals'. How could he clean out the fridge and end up with such a farmhand's bibimbap? I struggled to contain my bewilderment.

After filling his stomach, Choi Jeho laid a mat in the living room and started exercising. He pushed the table aside to make space, yet the living room still looked packed with just one Choi Jeho using his whole body. He moved less like a person and more like a storage cabinet.

*And to think I've been keeping something like that by my side, calling him a colleague.*

The one saving grace was that Choi Jeho had the commendable habit of showering right after sweating. Sparklers have a long and storied history of loving a drenched Choi Jeho. As long as the fan service was on point, it would be fine even if Choi Jeho did a windmill.

After roughly drying his hair with a towel, Choi Jeho neatly wiped the water droplets clinging to his jawline for the camera. Then he chose his next task. 'Tightening the dining table bolts'.

The plot was completely unpredictable. My humble self struggled just to keep up.

He shook the table a couple of times, decided it needed a fix, and went to get his tools.

『Jeho: Someone told me how to do this before...』

*Doesn't that just mean he doesn't remember anything?*

Muttering meaningless words, Choi Jeho squeezed his body under the dining table. Then, somehow, he managed to tighten the bolts. After confirming the tabletop no longer wobbled, Choi Jeho emotionlessly gathered his tools and put them away.

He also called his mother. I gave him bonus points for this part, since it was a scene I had not shown.

『Jeho: That thing with honey in a pear, how do you make it?』

『Jeho's Mom: Baesuk?』

Apparently, his mother often made it when his older sister or younger brother caught a cold as kids. The story also revealed that since Choi Jeho never caught colds, he always got to eat the baesuk by sheer luck.

Having received the recipe, Choi Jeho went grocery shopping and peeled the pears himself. His skill befitted the person who peeled fruit during family holidays. When he got the kimchi pancake recipe, he could not even tell pancake mix from flour. His skills had improved, perhaps thanks to his longer experience living alone and cooking on variety shows and in self-produced content.

It was not that such a small change surprised me because I had underestimated Choi Jeho. Choi Jeho had truly made a huge leap forward. The primitive man who used to just poke holes when asked to draw on a pumpkin was gone. He was replaced by a sensitive man who knew how to neatly cut the top off a pear and scoop out the inside. It was a magnificent sight that would surely stop Choi Jeyoung-noonim from beating her chest in shame over Choi Jeho disgracing the family.

『Jeho: I should've just said I'd make some quince tea.』

The fans went wild over this one line he muttered while turning on the gas stove.

≫ Reasons why the line 'I should've just said I'd make some quince tea' is so good

You can see Choi's position in the dorm + He regrets making more work for himself because he got a little lazy, but he still diligently went out and picked all the ingredients himself + The gap between his huge build and the fact he's doing this to take care of his members' throat health before a comeback is just... so much.

I did not know if it was a point worth loving that much, but everyone seemed to love it anyway. The heartwarming ending where we all deliciously shared the finished baesuk after it had cooled was impressive.

{sep}

Perhaps because Choi Jeho's episode was particularly quiet, the next upload, Jeong Seongbin's episode, was quite lively.

For starters, there was a wake-up song. It was a different start from Choi Jeho, who relied on other people's alarms and woke up whenever his eyes opened after his phone unexpectedly disappeared. Upbeat music filled the audio from the very beginning of the video. The scene of him sitting on his bed and loosening up with some light stretching was good enough to be a Metube vlog.

His meal was fresh, too. Yogurt sprinkled with nuts and a salad were the epitome of an idol's meal. When he added cherry tomatoes cut in half, it created a simple yet excellent presentation.

He even showed the sincerity to film insert shots. It was rude to even compare him to someone who just mixed everything in the fridge, glossed over the visuals with gochujang, and then stuffed it all in his mouth.

『Seongbin: How did the hyungs spend half a day alone? They must have been bored.』

『Seongbin: I think Joowoo and Kiyeon will adapt quickly, but I'm worried about Cheonghyeon...!』

He even mentioned the members from time to time.

『Seongbin: Yes, I'm just trying to confirm the date for our live broadcast.』

『Seongbin: Including the one that was confirmed today, how many variety shows do we have scheduled as a full group?』

He also showed himself working, pulling out a variety of scenes. His skill at creating scenes in an observational variety show was outstanding. You could feel his so-called 'broadcast experience'.

He also got a ton of phone calls. The scenes of Jeong Seongbin receiving calls were edited into a quick montage, and his relationship with each caller was different.

One was a high school classmate, one was a cousin, one was a hyung from his old vocal academy, and another was a college classmate. I was well aware that Jeong Seongbin had a lot of people around him, but I was so used to it that I never really felt its scale. Seeing it like this, his social network was incredible.

He also mentioned that I had cleaned out the storage, creating a great connection by organizing his own belongings. To make viewers perceive multiple videos as a single series, continuity was essential. Jeong Seongbin did a great job highlighting this point. He was a clever kid, anyway.

『Seongbin: These are the earphones Iwol-hyung gave me as a gift. One side doesn't work anymore, but it feels like a waste to throw them away...』

*Except I wish he would throw those away.*

My heart ached seeing that part, so I ordered a headset for him online. Back then, I bought him earphones because I did not have money, but surely I could afford to buy our leader a headset now. I even screenshotted the self-produced content and the payment confirmation and sent it straight to Jeong Seongbin.

Me [Let's let the earphones go]

spArk's Eternal Leader [Hyung] [You know I have both a headset and earphones!]

Me [You have them and you still do this?]

It was not just the earphones I gifted him. In Jeong Seongbin's drawer was a book cover Choi Jeho had bought him because his 'little brother' was reading books, but it did not fit Jeong Seongbin's e-reader. There was also an album that was not his taste, which Park Joowoo had gifted him during their trainee days. He was just like that, a guy who knew how to cherish his colleagues' sincerity.

『Seongbin: Wow, a picture from our trainee days!』

An ancient relic also emerged. All the photos they had occasionally taken in the practice room were printed out and pasted in an album. The dedication was incredible.

『Seongbin: This is from when we all went to see the sunrise. I was still a high school student back then. What a memory.』

The passage of time hit me squarely, and the shock left me reeling.

*My god, back then, Jeong Seongbin was only nineteen.*

A wave of guilt washed over me for entrusting the team's leadership to a high school senior, pulling a 'I believe in you!'. Even if I went back to that time, however, I would probably make the same choice. Blame this version of me.

Jeong Seongbin and Park Joowoo started their careers at nineteen, while Lee Cheonghyeon and Kang Kiyeon started at eighteen. I knew this fact well, but the more I thought about it, the more surprising it was.

*How did they manage such a grueling schedule at that age, without proper meals or enough sleep? And what about the other idols who debut even younger than them?*

Maybe it is because I am getting older, but my heart aches whenever I see young kids going through hardships.

Jeong Seongbin posted a photo in the group chat that was taken on his way home from work. Everyone reacted to their fresh-faced appearances with a flurry of emoticons.

I remembered sending a reply too.

*What did I send? With my emotions all stirred up, I'm sure it was...*

Iwol-hyung [Didn't I tell you to take it under the brightest streetlight? Everyone's face is in shadow and you can't see them. What are we going to do about this unfortunate situation? I know everyone is doing well now, but let's use this as a lesson to strive even harder in our selfie-taking skills so this doesn't happen again... See More]

...It was not.

*I must have known that Jeong Seongbin was filming his self-produced content, so why on earth did I do that? It was as if I'd been possessed by an evil spirit.*

I desperately hoped the Sparklers would not misunderstand me based on that text alone.
