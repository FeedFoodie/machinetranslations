The arms of the two men holding the barbells trembled like aspen leaves in a storm. Estella had stoked their competitive spirits, but even she was inwardly shocked they had managed to go this far. It was an understandable reaction, considering the barbells each man lifted weighed nearly one hundred kilograms.

*These guys are truly amazing! If they're trained properly, they could be incredible!*

Like a master who had discovered a prodigious talent, Estella felt a thrill of excitement. She wanted to see just how far their limits went, but a glance at their current state told her they would be finished after just two or three more reps.

*This is when I should offer a carrot, right?*

"The team that successfully completes a thousand reps gets meat and alcohol on me!" Estella announced in a loud, clear voice.

"Waaaaaah!"

"One more!"

"One more!"

"Keep it up, Commander!"

"Chief! Don't you lose! This is a matter of pride for blacksmiths and dwarves!"

The fierce cheering from both teams erupted once more, and in the midst of it all, Catherine's presence was completely forgotten.

"It looks like you're all busy, so I'll come back later," she said, her gentle farewell scattering faintly into the air. "Keep up the good work, everyone."

Her words were drowned out by the monstrous roar. Catherine forced a smile as she left the area, her steps hurrying to escape the smell of sweat and iron and the eardrum-shattering noise as quickly as possible.

*The smell of sweat is going to numb my nose. I feel like I'm going to throw up.*

This was not a place that suited her. The path that had been so easy to enter took twice as long to exit, for the knights and blacksmiths kept pushing forward, eager to get a closer look at the contest.

{sep}

"My lady, you've returned?"

"Shall I prepare a bath for you, my lady?"

When Catherine returned to her quarters, her personal maids Marie and Natalie approached with bright smiles. To work in such a position within the marquis's household meant they had overcome fierce competition, and they couldn't help but carry a high sense of pride in their work. They, too, did not doubt for a moment that Catherine was the true heir to the marquis's family.

"No, it's alright. I just want to rest a bit," Catherine told them in a gentle voice. "Both of you can leave early and rest today."

"But..."

"I'm fine. I just want to be alone for a bit," Catherine said kindly, wrapping her arms around their shoulders. "So, leaving now would be helping me. Understand?"

Marie and Natalie hesitated, but they couldn't wipe the smiles from their faces at the joy of their duties ending early.

"Oh, right. These are cookies I brought from the kitchen for you. Take them and eat them."

Catherine even handed the two a basket full of cookies, its linen cloth cover unable to contain the sweet scent that wafted from within.

"My lady... thank you."

"We must be truly blessed to be able to serve someone as wonderful as you, my lady."

"Thank you for saying that. I'm the one who's happy to have met you both," Catherine replied. "Now, hurry along. I'm going to lie down for a bit."

"Yes, my lady."

"Then please rest well. Thank you."

Catherine smiled and waved until the two had left the room. She knew that as soon as they were gone, they would boastfully chatter to the other maids about what had just happened. That was precisely why she had designated those two as her personal maids. They were the ones who could spread her calculated good deeds the fastest, the ones who could elevate her status.

"Annoying bitches," she muttered as soon as the door closed. "If I tell you to go, you should go quickly. Why so much chatter."

Harsh words spilled from her mouth, a coarse stream that didn't suit her angelic face at all.

Lucille, already used to it, showed no sign of surprise. She was actually relieved that the tirade was milder than she had braced herself for.

SLAP!

As Lucille approached to attend to her, Catherine's hand whipped out, and the impact sent Lucille's head snapping to the side. She was hit so hard that she was completely dazed. This, too, was not the first time it had happened, so Lucille meekly bowed her head.

"I'm sorry, my lady. It was my fault."

"What were you doing while Estella was causing such a scene! Why didn't you report it properly!"

Even though she herself had known nothing, Catherine blamed everything on Lucille. She firmly believed that everything that happened in the knight order was under her jurisdiction. When she first found out Estella was building something like a physical training room, she had simply thought, *What could she possibly accomplish?* She hadn't paid it much mind.

Recalling what had just happened, Catherine's anger flared to the top of her head again.

SLAP!

Once more, Lucille's cheek swelled red.

 Tears welled in Catherine's own eyes, as if she were the one who had been hit. She opened her stinging palm and shouted at Lucille, "Go get something for a compress. My palm is killing me."

"Yes, my lady," Lucille replied, bowing her head deeply.

{sep}

The barbell lift ended with the victory of Knight Commander Lucius. Dolan, after forty-five grueling reps, finally collapsed, unable to continue.

"Waaaaaah!"

"Hooray for Commander Lucius!"

"We won!"

The knights cheered as if they had won a great war. It was a rare bit of entertainment for them, men who had been overworked lately due to the frequent appearance of demonic beasts. 

Lucius knew his subordinates were exhausted in mind and body, which was what made him try even harder. Of course, he also couldn't suffer the humiliation of losing to a blacksmith in front of his men. His arms ached as if they would fall off, but he laughed loudly like a child.

Although Dolan lost, he wasn't too disappointed. He too had done his best, and it was a huge accomplishment to have performed so well against the estate's knight commander.

"You two are really amazing," Estella said, her voice full of sincere encouragement as she praised the two exhausted men. "I didn't know you could go this far. That's incredible skill." She turned to the crowd. "As promised, I will bestow alcohol and meat upon the winning knight team! Of course, this is my personal money, so there's no need to worry about the estate's budget."

"Waaaaaah!"

"Hooray for Lady Estella!"

The knights shouted with excitement. 

The blacksmiths, though disappointed, knew their chief had done his best to the very end, so they clapped and congratulated him. They were incredibly proud that their chief had competed so evenly against a nobleman.

"Although you lost," Estella continued, turning to Dolan's team, "thanks to Dolan and all of you, we now have this excellent physical training room, and to think you finished the work five days early. I'm truly impressed by your skills."

At Estella's continued praise, Dolan grinned shyly. The faces of the blacksmiths who came with him were also overflowing with pride. 

Estella wasn't just any noble, but the daughter of the marquis, the lord of the estate. It was extremely rare to meet such a high-ranking noble in person, and now they were hearing her praise their work right before their eyes. This was a great story to brag about for a long, long time.

"This is the promised payment," Estella said, personally handing a pouch to Dolan in front of everyone. She gave his shoulder a friendly pat. "I've added a bonus since you finished earlier than the deadline. Use this to have a drink together on your way back. Thank you again for your hard work and for finishing it so well."

"Thank you, my lady," Dolan said, his voice thick with gratitude. "In the future, whatever you entrust to us, we will do our best."

"To receive such a promise from the best craftsman! I'm the one who should be more grateful!"

"Waaaaaah! Hooray for Chief Dolan!"

"Hooray!"

"Hooray for Lady Estella!"

Cheers of 'Hooray!' erupted from the blacksmiths' side. Like an athlete who had won a gold medal at the Olympics, Dolan raised the pouch he received from Estella high in the air. He shook it wildly and smiled so brightly that all his teeth showed.

{sep}

The marquis had come with the thought of just taking a look, since it was the first thing Estella had undertaken with any motivation. He stopped in his tracks, startled by the tremendous cheers that seemed to shake the training grounds. The sound was coming from the direction of Estella's new physical training room.

"Go find out what's happening, Tom," Raul ordered.

"Yes, I understand!"

Tom, who served as Raul's aide and was also his nephew, quickly ran toward the source of the noise. 

A moment later, Tom returned, panting for breath.

"Lady Estella..."

He was out of breath from running, but he reported everything he had seen and heard without omission. As if he couldn't believe it himself, he added the words 'It's true!' several times in between his gasps.

Throughout Tom's report, both the marquis and Raul wore expressions of disbelief, when another round of cheers erupted, Estella's name mixed clearly within it. 

The marquis turned back with a faint smile.

"Aren't you going to go see her?" Raul asked, hoping the marquis would go and praise the lady for her rare accomplishment.

"This is entirely Estella's moment," the marquis said, his tone still gruff. "I can't intrude."

Nevertheless, Raul could clearly feel that his master's steps were light for the first time in a while.

*My lady! It seems the young lady is finally starting to regain her old self!*

Raul quickly wiped away the tears welling in his eyes and diligently followed his master.
