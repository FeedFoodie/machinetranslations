Dolan’s amber eyes, the color of gold coins, widened and sparkled as he peeked into the slightly open pouch.

“I’ve heard there’s no one better than you among those who work with iron,” Estella said. “So you must make these exactly as designed.”

A small smile curled Dolan’s lips at the blatant praise.

*Well, it’s not a lie.*

Seeing her now, Lady Estella seemed like a rather good person. The rumors circulating through town must have been false after all.

“What on earth are these things used for?” he asked, his curiosity piqued. “The dumbbells you mentioned won’t take long, but to also create a barbell and a wooden sled… that will take at least a fortnight.”

While the prospect of creating new items was exciting, he was incredibly curious about their purpose. The barbell and wooden sled were meant for Estella to pull while running, a replacement for a tire to build her strength. Just thinking about training with that equipment again made her body itch with anticipation.

“I’d like them made as quickly as possible,” Estella stated. “I’ll pay a bonus for every day you finish ahead of the fortnight. However, if you make them poorly, I’ll send them right back. As for what they’re used for, I’ll tell you on the day they’re delivered to the marquis’s estate.”

“Yes, I understand.”

With business being slow lately, the prospect of such a large income made Dolan answer with a rare enthusiasm. A fortnight was a tight schedule, but his long-dormant creative desire now blazed like a furnace.

After leaving the blacksmith’s, Estella, who was expected to return directly to the marquis’s estate, instead had the carriage turn toward Lily’s Dress Shop. When the carriage stopped before its entrance, Gai’s face wrinkled in a subtle frown, as if he had expected this all along.

*The territory is in such a difficult situation right now… Tsk, tsk!*

Though Catherine also had dresses tailored, he found only Estella’s actions disagreeable.

As Gai moved to escort her, Estella stopped and turned, holding up her palm. “We’ll go in by ourselves. You can stay here. There’s no need to follow us into the dress shop.”

Gai glanced at her chubby palm with an indifferent expression before bowing politely. “Yes, my lady. I will wait here.”

“Thank you. Ria, let’s go.”

Estella left Gai standing outside and entered the shop with only Ria. Inside, Claire, who had been contacted beforehand, was waiting to greet them warmly.

“My lady, this way.”

Instead of leading them into the main part of the shop, Claire opened a door on the left side of the lobby. Without a backward glance, Estella and Ria followed her through it. A short walk down a long corridor brought them to another door, which opened onto a different city alleyway. Claire handed them two black hoods she had prepared.

“Thank you,” Estella said, offering a gentle smile. “I’ll be counting on you in the future as well.”

“Yes! Don’t you worry,” Claire beamed.

Ria silently hurried after Estella as the two of them quickly navigated the alleyways, soon arriving at the Commerce Management Office. It was an important institution that granted and protected exclusive rights for technologies or products invented within the empire, with a branch in every imperial territory. While not as grand as the offices in the capital or other large cities, it was still a building established by imperial law, and its interior was quite luxurious.

CLICK-CLACK.

As the two figures with hoods pulled low crossed the lobby, people glanced at them briefly but did not stare. This was a place frequented by all classes, from mercenaries and merchant guild masters to nobles in disguise.

Following behind Estella, Ria wondered what business her lady could possibly have here. Estella had only told her to follow without giving a reason, so Ria suppressed her curiosity and quietly trailed after her. Since she began working at the marquis’s estate, Ria had never really been anywhere other than the bank. She had no family, nowhere to go, and no one to meet. Since her clothes and food were provided, she had no particular reason to spend money.

“Whoa…” Ria let out an involuntary gasp of awe upon entering the building.

Estella smiled, finding her reaction cute. “Is it that amazing?”

Embarrassed, Ria’s face flushed as red as a ripe plum. “Yes. It’s such a wonderful place. Thank you so much for bringing me here, my lady,” she said with sincerity.

The marquis’s castle was also very grand, but it was old, with many worn and broken parts. This building, however, was newly constructed, and everything her eyes landed on gleamed brightly.

“Not at all,” Estella replied. “Thank you for coming with me.”

Ria carefully clasped her hands together and followed Estella without making a sound.

At the lobby’s information desk, a clerk asked in a business-like tone, “How may I help you?” Her hair was twisted up in a perfect bun, as if a single stray strand would be a fatal offense, and a dove brooch, the symbol of the Commerce Management Office, was pinned neatly to her chest.

“I’m here to apply for a patent,” Estella replied, her tone just as professional as the clerk’s.

Ria was inwardly amazed by this confident side of her lady she had never seen before.

*My lady is so cool.*

“Please proceed to Room 301 at the end of the third-floor hallway.”

Without a verbal reply, Estella gave a slight nod and went straight up to the room.

It was about an hour later when they returned to the dress shop. The patent application process had been much simpler than expected. Though the verification would have been a hassle, Estella had decided to use an alias for the application. She figured that if wealth accumulated under her own name, it would only be a matter of time before word reached Catherine, who had her people planted everywhere.

“Are you going to keep crying?” Estella asked, unable to watch any longer. After finishing the patent registration, Ria had been crying nonstop. Estella offered her a handkerchief.

“Why… are you doing this… for me?” Ria’s voice was thick with emotion. “It’s more than I deserve.”

She accepted the handkerchief but could not bring herself to wipe her tears on something so pretty and fine. Instead, she used the back of her hand. The question came because, when applying for the patent, Estella had arranged for ten percent of the sales profits to go to Ria.

“It’s only natural. You’re the one who made Miraclium.”

“But I only did what you told me to do, my lady.”

Estella shook her head. She took the handkerchief back and gently wiped Ria’s tears herself. “It wouldn’t have succeeded without you. So you are more than qualified.”

“My lady…”

“Let’s get rich together. Alright?”

Fresh tears began to stream down Ria’s face. She had not received a single coin yet, but she already felt rich. The simple fact that her lady was being so considerate was overwhelming and deeply moving.

“But you can’t tell anyone,” Estella added. “It’s a secret. You know?”

“Yes. I’ll absolutely keep it a secret.”

“Good. We’re going to be a fantastic team from now on.”

At Estella’s playful expression, Ria smiled brightly through her tears. The lady who had saved her life was now trying to change it.

As soon as he returned from the outing, Gai was summoned by Catherine. He now sat in a chair at her tea table, his expression flushed with excitement.

*I’ve been invited by Lady Catherine!*

When Lucille told him that Lady Catherine was looking for him, he thought he had misheard. He was so surprised and delighted that his legs felt weak, and he nearly missed a step several times on his way to her drawing room. If not for his supernatural reflexes, he might have taken an unsightly tumble down the stairs.

Catherine’s drawing room had a lovely atmosphere that matched its owner’s cute and delicate image. There were curtains and a tablecloth of hand-knitted lace, porcelain dolls and ornaments placed here and there, and furniture engraved with intricate carvings. Not a single item was placed carelessly, and nothing felt out of place. Gai straightened his back and sat upright, trying to calm his rapidly pounding heart.

Catherine appeared about thirty minutes later.

“I’m so sorry. I’m terribly late, aren’t I?” she said, her voice as beautiful as a skylark’s. “My conversation with my father went on longer than I expected…”

The moment he heard her, Gai shot up from his seat. Catherine Cardien was walking toward him with a smile as radiant as a spring day. Approaching in a dress that fanned out like a flower bud, she was like a single, perfect blossom.

“Not at all. It is an honor to be summoned, my lady,” Gai said with sincerity as he showed his respects.

As she drew closer, a faint floral scent filled the air around them. Gai’s ears turned red, a blush that spread all the way to his thick sideburns. He had always secretly admired the beautiful Catherine, and now he wondered if he was dreaming.

Lucille skillfully filled the teacups for the two of them as they sat across from each other. The gentle aroma of the tea subtly colored the air between them.
