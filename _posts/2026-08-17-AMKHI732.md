On the screen, Choi Jeho said, 『Should I say he helps carry the burden for others when they're struggling, even without being asked? In that sense, he's very mature.』

The brilliant statement perfectly encapsulated Jeong Seongbin's strengths.

"Why has he gotten so honest?"

First came the drinking, then the money problems. After confessing his sore spots to the members once, Choi Jeho no longer hesitated with his words.

He looked much more comfortable too.

"Isn't it because he's more psychologically stable?"

"If he gets any more stable, he'll blurt out the dorm's passcode."

Fortunately, Choi Jeho did not reveal the front door's passcode. The chat window, however, became a sea of tears.

"Seongbin, you must have had a hard time too."

"Not at all. Getting advice from Jeho-hyung was a new and nice experience! He's kind of..."

"He has this ability to make the worries I'm holding onto feel incredibly insignificant."

"He takes deep problems and doesn't treat them so deeply, right?"

"Yes. So it makes me think, 'Ah, I was racking my brain over a completely useless problem, wasn't I?'"

"That's a talent in itself."

Park Joowoo expressed his gratitude to his absent friend. He mentioned that Jeong Seongbin had been with him at every major turning point, helping him make a decision.

『If it weren't for Seongbin, I would've never even thought about college...』

『Speaking of which. When are you guys graduating? Hasn't it been a while since you enrolled?』

『Let's move on...』

The topic changed so naturally that the talk of graduation vanished in a blink. Lee Cheonghyeon laughed, slapping the innocent blanket.

Park Joowoo praised him, saying it was amazing that Cheonghyeon could get good grades while promoting, then covered him with the blanket and gave him a hug.

『I'm most grateful that Seongbin-hyung is our leader.』

Lee Cheonghyeon was grateful for the very existence of their leader, Jeong Seongbin. It was a sentiment all of spArk surely shared.

『It must be really hard promoting with extreme members like us, who are either overflowing with sensitivity or have none at all, even if you scrape the bottom of the barrel.』

『Is it that bad?』

『We know each other's personalities well now and have matured, so we have a framework in place, but think back to our trainee days. Jeho-hyung, are you confident you could lead the trainee version of spArk? I'm always amazed by Seongbin-hyung's generous heart and tolerance.』

『...』

『I... might have collapsed...』

The members were serious, but Jeong Seongbin just laughed carefreely.

"They were all so diligent since their trainee days, they're just saying that for no reason."

*Are they finally recognizing my hardships!*

I thought he was moved by that, but he was not. How could a person be so suited to being a superior?

It was admirable. If UA did one thing right in the past, it must have been choosing Jeong Seongbin as the leader.

The next person to be praised was Jeong Seongbin's favorite(?) younger brother, Kang Kiyeon. As soon as the target of praise was decided, Lee Cheonghyeon shouted as if he had been waiting.

『He did well with his solo activities.』

『Is that... a compliment...?』

『If I say he did well, it's a compliment!』

The reply came out as fast as lightning.

『Does it seem insincere? But I felt it while working on the album together this time. Kang-geon has a desire for improvement. Because his desire to do well is so strong, he grows with each promotion.』

『...Kiyeon has that kind of ambition. That's probably why even if we're both sitting on the floor, Iwol-hyung tells me, 'Joowoo, get up,' but tells Kiyeon, 'Rest for just 3 minutes and then get up'...』

『That's because he's softer on the younger ones.』

"Seongbin, do you also think I treat you, Joowoo, Cheonghyeon, and Kiyeon differently?"

"You do tend to go a little easier on the youngest ones, don't you?"

"You're not just saying it feels that way, you're stating it as a fact?"

"It's not a bad thing! I can't be harsh with Cheonghyeon and Kiyeon either."

"You're not harsh with me or Choi Jeho either..."

*From what I've observed so far, the only person Jeong Seongbin treats harshly is Mr. Jeong Seongjun.*

I buried that thought deep inside, afraid I would receive the same harsh treatment if I said it.

『I also think his hard work is his strong point. I told him that myself.』

『You two talked about that...?』

『It's amazing. You guys didn't get along at all in the past, how did you end up clicking so well?』

Park Joowoo praised Kang Kiyeon's loyalty. He said that Kang Kiyeon always puts his friends and colleagues first.

『In our team, we each have areas we're stubborn about. Kiyeon is also firm in his opinions... but if there's a difference, it's that Kiyeon always seems to give in at the end. It's not just because he's considerate, but because he cherishes the other person...』

『True, Kang-geon doesn't just yield to anyone.』

『Is that why he doesn't give in to me.』

『What are you talking about? He gives in to you a lot! Do you know how many times I've seen you two in a meeting?!』

『That's him giving in?』

『It's not that Kiyeon isn't stubborn at all... It just means he's relatively less so with the members.』

A lively conversation, just as animated as the live broadcast, opened up in the dorm.

"Seongbin, what do you think?"

"I think Joowoo is right, don't you?"

"If Kiyeon heard that compliment, would he like it or feel awkward?"

"I'll cast my vote for 'he'd be embarrassed.'"

Finally, it was my turn. When I suggested we stop watching the live and disperse, Jeong Seongbin snickered.

"You can go on ahead, hyung! I'll finish watching and then wash up."

"You seem to be enjoying this?"

The one left with an awkward aftertaste was not Kang Kiyeon, but me. I could not escape to my room and was held captive on the sofa.

『Now we have to praise Iwol-hyung.』

『Right...?』

『But the purpose of our content is to praise the absent member without them knowing.』

Lee Cheonghyeon looked directly at the camera. I was just monitoring when our eyes suddenly met.

The kid pointed his finger and said, 『Iwol-hyung, turn off the broadcast.』

『Heut...!』

I had just sat back down, only to be rejected. Park Joowoo at least covered his mouth to laugh.

The Sparklers in the chat did not hide their laughter. If the fans are enjoying it, what is a little public embarrassment, I suppose.

"Cheonghyeon's kicking me out, so I guess I have to go."

"Really?"

"He asked me to protect the content's identity, so I have to respect that."

"But I've already seen my part."

"They didn't tell you to leave, so you're fine. I'll get myself kicked out now."

I waved to Jeong Seongbin, went into my room, and lay down. The sound from the speaker carried in from outside, but I was not particularly curious about the content.

There was a time I was busy writing down everything they said. There was another time I watched with my eyes peeled, worried they might say something strange on a broadcast.

Even when they said they would do fine on their own, I could not feel at ease and would fret, feeling like I had to watch over them. However...

"It's strange how people change."

A dry laugh escaped me. It did not feel bad.

Most companies, depending on the job, became busier as the year-end approached. Summarizing the year's performance and establishing plans for the next year was a crucial task.

For Won Chaehee, a slave to her job, November was no different. While handling her main duties, she also had to document everything she had done during the fourth quarter and the entire year before December arrived.

*What's the point of working my ass off and packaging it nicely to report? They don't even read it.*

They never gave incentives when sales rose, but they were quick to hound you if growth indicators were conservative or declined. A happy, recognized future did not exist.

If they praised an employee, that employee might ask the next year, "I did well last year, so will you raise my salary this year?" causing them to clutch the back of their necks and collapse. This whole process led to one of two outcomes: 'averting a crisis' or 'getting disciplined'.

Won Chaehee was in no position to complain, however. Beyond the partition, Yeo Hana compiled and organized her own, her team members', and the team's fourth-quarter and annual performance reports as the price for her promotion.

The saying that people these days do not want promotions was true. Won Chaehee wanted to stay in her current position for the rest of her life.

The exhausting, repetitive days made her desperately long for rest. She needed time in her bed to recharge her energy for this harsh life.

After attending a public broadcast recording and receiving a ton of food, she wanted to give up on her real life for a while. She felt she would have nothing more to wish for if she could just follow spArk's schedule and eat the food they gave out.

Her real life had interfered with her fangirling for years, damn it. This fateful cycle seemed destined to continue unless Won Chaehee herself quit her job before spArk retired.

Things might have been different if her fangirl life was only joyful, allowing her to forget her arduous social life completely. The fan life of a fansite master running a large account was never smooth, however.

Deep fangirling was a continuous struggle.

*I'm too busy getting off work to even go see spArk leaving, but they mistake me for a sasaeng and raise hell demanding an explanation. I upload one concert video and they raise hell telling me to take it down. Then they send a private message asking if I have any other videos, raising hell to secretly get data behind the scenes.*

*And is that the end of it? Interference also flies in from across the sea. Kim Iwol goes to the trouble of dyeing his hair ash gray, but the concert organizers blast blue lighting, so I have to color-correct the photos to show his hair color as close to real life as possible. Then they raise hell, saying Korean fans make him excessively pale. They demand his skin color back, even taking photos from foreign media outlets, deliberately editing his face to be more yellow, and pasting them on while raising hell. They claim Iwol is their favorite but can't even be bothered to learn his name, not even writing the five letters of 'Yiwol' correctly and raising hell...*

*The takedown of the IDC Season 1 broadcast is another thing. The videos are set to private because the cast, led by some bastard who does drugs, causes all sorts of trouble, yet there's a lot of talk about it overseas. They claim Korean netizens oppress celebrities by forcing excessively high moral standards on them. Damn it, so are we just supposed to let criminals and power-abusers keep their faces out there? They sure have strong stomachs.*

*Try spending as much money as the Korean fan clubs before you run your mouth.*

In the eyes of Won Chaehee, who lived coldly without turning on the boiler in winter and endured the summer with just a fan, pouring all her saved money into fangirling, a love that was all talk was nothing but frivolous.

She once posted that she would not accept opinions from people who do not stream, and was stoned from all sides for it. Won Chaehee had already been through too many storms to be hurt by such things, however.

Nevertheless, Won Chaehee did not quit her fangirl life. spArk made the corners of Won Chaehee's mouth lift faster than her company and outsiders could wear her out.
