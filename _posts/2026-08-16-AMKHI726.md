Even after befriending the Mini Three Musketeers, Kim Iwol still checked on his members every so often. He was like a parent calling every hour to ask when their daughter would be home, right after Baek Haewon had started college.

Yet he was a good cook on his own. He made rapid progress after looking up recipes. He found ingredients quickly and prepped them into uniform sizes in no time. The meticulously worn apron was conspicuous, befitting his neat nature of rarely spilling anything on his clothes.

Some people say fans love their idols regardless of their cooking skills, but I hope they realize that a fan's heart can grow cold, separate from their love for a favorite, if they discover their idol in his mid-twenties cannot even cook a single pack of ramen. The boom for self-sufficient idols in the idol scene did not happen for nothing.

Kim Iwol did not rest while the rice cooked. After pressing the cook button, he sat right down at the dining table and immersed himself in online shopping. When he opened his notepad app, a long list of things to buy appeared.

『Iwol: Why are there so many things we're out of...』

Kim Iwol muttered, checking each item and adding the necessary ones to his cart. The purchase units started with sets or boxes, perhaps because it was a large family.

『Iwol: You guys should stand here and look at the state of this storage room. Isn't there too much junk? Goodness, with all these toiletries, how are we out of shampoo?』

『Iwol: There's a whole box of unopened soap right here, so why are you asking me to buy more? Jeho, are you watching this?』

Listening to the nagging felt like my ears would bleed. It was identical, not a single word different, to what my mom used to say whenever she rummaged through things on the veranda. Why would God put the soul of a meticulous household manager into the body of a handsome young man? It was a mystery.

『Iwol: Oh right, we had this?』

Kim Iwol's voice rose a tone as he rummaged through the storage room. He held an object familiar even to Baek Haewon. Waving a white board in front of the manggaetteok figurine, Kim Iwol spoke.

『Iwol: Manggae, do you know what this is? It's called a foam board. A long time ago, we used to write our names on these, wear them on our heads, and appear on Visible Radio.』

He spoke as if telling a fairy tale. The corners of his mouth turned up high, a clear sign of his delight. Like a pro idol dropping tidbits for fans at every opportunity, Kim Iwol poured out stories like an endless spring.

『Iwol: A long time ago, our team made and wore headbands with our names on them, right? But Kiyeon was so worried about his hair getting flattened. Even wearing a beanie the wrong way can make your hairstyle look really bad. So I always carried a tail comb. There's this mini tail comb with a foldable handle...』

『Iwol: Our old dorm didn't have a pantry like this. So we used to put things we didn't use in a small built-in closet... Cheonghyeon put his school uniform in here. This precious thing! Speaking of uniforms, it reminds me, Kiyeon always used to write his name on his uniform labels, you know? On his shirts and pants, all of them. Since he went to the same school as Seongbin, he was worried their clothes might get mixed up, but he couldn't ask his hyung to mark his clothes for him, so he just wrote his name on his own things to tell them apart. It was so fresh, really.』

『Iwol: This box is for Jeho's clothes. I keep a close eye on his closet, and if I see something #FF6600 that he doesn't wear often, I periodically collect it and put it in here, you see? Then I fill the space with new clothes, but I don't know where he keeps buying such strange clothes from. The proportion of orange never decreases. I don't know if I'm the weird one, or if he is.』

『Iwol: The materials for making moru dolls are still here. The trend is probably over by now, right? It looks easy, but it's surprisingly difficult when you try to make them yourself. If you keep twisting and untwisting them, the wire core pokes out... It's a memory. I only made them for the Sparklers, but should I make one for each of the guys too? No. Get a grip, Kim Iwol! At this rate, I won't even finish cleaning before the sun sets.』

"The sun is shining brightly outside, what do you mean, stop!"

Baek Haewon interjected, but her words did not reach the Kim Iwol on the screen. Kim Iwol stopped his storytelling and got up when the rice cooker announced the cooking was complete.

≫ Can't we sell that rice cooker to a junkyard or something

≫ It has no sense

└ Why are you so angry lol

└ How can I not be angry right now?

Unlike the disappointed Baek Haewon, Kim Iwol was serene. He squeezed the ketchup with a steady hand, making my heart flutter as I thought he was drawing a picture, but the bright red word 'Alone', which seemed to protest his solitude, only reminded me of my favorite's tendency to hold a grudge. I only connected it to the title promotion later.

It was quite cute how he moved the figurines with his large hands before even eating his own meal. He took food-shaped magnets from the refrigerator and even set a table for them.

『Iwol: We do all this to live, so we have to eat our meals.』

He placed a mini cola in front of the 'kids', but for himself, he only got water to drink.

Still, Kim Iwol looked happy. He had to talk to his mini friends while eating and search for magnets too. It seemed he would not have a moment to be bored.

*I wonder if Cheonghyeon and Kiyeon ever thought their hyung would play with his friends this well...*

Stop taking the members away from Kim Iwol's side. The kid is getting weird because there is no one around. At this rate, he is going to become best friends with inanimate objects.

Kim Iwol did not stay still for a single moment. He did the dishes as soon as he finished eating, and before the water in the sink could even dry, he was already in front of the storage room. Kim Iwol plopped down and sent a message to the members.

Me [You know how we kept our group practice outfits even though the knees are stretched out because it felt like a waste to throw them away?] [Should we just throw them out now?]

spArk's Eternal Vocal (Joowoo) [Throw them away...?] [ㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠ]

spArk's Eternal Soul (Cheonghyeon) [We leave this hyung alone and he goes crazy throwing out our belongings] [Stop doing nonsense and just relax!]

spArk's Eternal Symbol (Jeho) [You can throw mine away]

spArk's Eternal Soul (Cheonghyeon) [So heartless, Jeho-hyung...]

He had to get permission to throw things away and repack the items he put back into storage. He was busy going back and forth.

"When did he change their contact names?"

The names were all so similar. She would not have been able to tell who was who if there had not been subtitles next to them.

Baek Haewon remembered the event spArk held for Kim Iwol's birthday during their concert. Was there any Sparkler who did not know about it?

For one, Baek Haewon, who was at the venue, had cried with tears streaming down her face. All the Kim Iwol fans Baek Haewon knew had cried. The anxiety that Kim Iwol might not return to spArk had only recently been resolved, so the emotional turmoil was unavoidable.

*Is that why they gave him a name like that? Why didn't Kim Iwol tell us this fun story? It's not like his nimble fingers wouldn't have typed it out...*

Baek Haewon trusted her idol too much. She trusted him so much she could not even conceive that the great Kim Iwol might have changed the contact names himself and kept quiet out of embarrassment.

In the middle of it all, he also got a call from someone.

『Iwol: Yes, Senior-nim.』

『???: What are you doing?』

『Iwol: Guarding the dorm.』

『???: Do thieves break into your dorm?』

It was a certain actor with whom Sparklers felt a sense of inner closeness.

『Senior Jahan: Woo Yunjae said he'll buy us a meal. Want to come out?』

『Iwol: Did Senior Yunjae agree to that?』

『Senior Jahan: I haven't asked him yet.』

『Iwol: I shouldn't have let you two get close.』

『Senior Jahan: What was that?』

The call ended with Kim Iwol saying he could not go out because of the filming.

It would be great if Kim Iwol started his own personal Metube channel to film a talk show. Then Woo Yunjae and Gu Jahan could appear and share lots of Kim Iwol episodes. Baek Haewon held on to a small hope, believing her wish would one day come true.

There was a reason why Baek Haewon kept getting her hopes up.

『Iwol: ...Shall I play a game?』

The 'Kim Iwol playing games' that the Sparklers had been so eager to see had finally appeared in this self-produced content.

*He brings us everything like this if we just wait, so isn't it okay to be a little greedy?*

She sometimes got lost in thought over her own shamelessness, yet she ultimately enjoyed the reality of it. Baek Haewon loved this fangirl life so much.

『Iwol: As a person... no, as a character, Arthur, I thought you'd at least pull the weeds for me, but I guess you have to do your own work, right? You're a good friend who fosters your companion's independence.』

『Iwol: If fishing is this hard, what do people eat during the off-season? Do they have to just stock up on what they harvested in the fall and eat only that? You have to guarantee a minimum catch to survive the winter.』

『Iwol: This village isn't suitable for farming. The plains aren't wide enough. Historically, a breadbasket region is a wide, flat land with no mountains and nearby water, where you can harvest rice, but this town has the sea below and mountains above... Is what I'm supposed to be doing here farming? Or is it land reclamation?』

『Iwol: So you're telling me what I just caught was trash? Not a metaphor, I literally caught actual trash. I paid money to buy and use bait, and I ended up with a loss, right? But the fact that I don't know how many more pieces of trash I'll catch from that sea in the future is terrifying.』

『Iwol: If I had known I'd be catching this much ocean trash, I wouldn't have brought a fishing rod, I would have brought a pay-as-you-throw garbage bag. I'd post on the town bulletin board that I'm going 'Polo-gging'. Do you think this sea will get clean by relying solely on an individual picking up trash?』

Was he this talkative because he was filming, or was Kim Iwol just a chatterbox? The members had said Iwol-hyung was always talkative, so it was probably the latter. Baek Haein's claim that the high schooler Kim Iwol was moderately quiet seemed like a total lie, no matter how she looked at it. As if I would ever believe a word that bastard says again.

Even while catching nothing but trash day and night, Kim Iwol did not stop fishing. He did his best with the sole intention of showing a fish to the cat figurine.

『Iwol: A fish!』

The moment he caught an anchovy after painstaking effort, Kim Iwol let out a cry of triumph, his face filled with ecstasy. He looked happier than anyone in the world.

Even after that, Kim Iwol laughed and cried over a single game. He yelled at people to stop littering, cooked and ate another meal, and even delivered the members' laundry. The video ended with him waving and greeting the camera alongside the members who had returned home. After watching the long video, Baek Haewon left a short review on social media.

*Right, Iwol is a total workaholic. Let the Sparklers have some fun...*
