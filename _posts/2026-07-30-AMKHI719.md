MYTH's poking into other companies and tormenting of former employees ground to a halt as bigger problems piled up.

UtopiA found peace, and so did I. After I told Polo my stance on Han Gawoon, every last worry vanished. Naturally, the nosebleeds and dizziness did not return either. Thankfully, we could wait for our comeback day in a stable atmosphere.

*It's just a shame that no matter what album we release, it won't become a hot topic...!*

The entertainment news filled with stories about MYTH day after day. At least spArk had a significant difference in weight class, but most of the teams that had their comebacks first had to swallow a bitter pill. We had barely managed to announce our news, so we could not afford to be smug.

The music video itself pleased me, however. We could show a completely opposite side from our previous work, 『MooNStruck IOve』.

The previous music video actively used experimental directing and intense focal points. Our new title track, 『Alone』, primarily featured serene, vast landscapes and simple styling instead.

The sets also moved from the 『MooNStruck IOve』 filming location, which was filled with silver flowers and a mercury-like liquid, to comfortable rest areas, well-decorated workshops, and glass greenhouses. It was much more human, and I liked it.

The colors were more diverse too. Having escaped the metallic-tasting palette, spArk received clothes with heavily washed-out colors and a background of piled-up autumn leaves. It was not flashy, but anything more varied than the black and silver combination counted as an improvement.

There was a reason we did not use vibrant colors like in 『Hideout』 or 『Sprint』.

For this album, spArk took on the role of lukewarm handsome men, neither boys, youths, nor men, waiting for someone with faces that looked full of stories.

They transformed from pure-hearted, heartbroken men who could not accept their breakup and fell into despair, into wistful men who were 20% tragic, 60% calm, and 20% numb. They kept their eyes fixed beyond the window, thinking, *You'll come back someday...!*

I specifically requested to lower the saturation to maximize the song's impression. 『Alone』 was a tropical house track, a genre spArk had never pushed as a title song before.

House-style songs truly shine in the summer. Their low temperature gives them a refreshing yet cool feeling, so they pair very well with images of a sun-drenched blue sea or a beach on a midsummer night at sunset. Search 'tropical house' on MeTube right now and you could find dozens of playlists with such pictures.

So why did we bring this song out in November?

My will was entirely involved in this. I did not want to lose the refreshing feel, and I needed to convey a sense of wistfulness, but I also wanted to preserve the rhythm befitting a dance group's title track and the members' personal colors. Mixing all those intentions resulted in this.

"It should be refreshing, but not cheerful," I explained. "Moist, but not damp. It should feel cold enough for your breath to fog, but not like a severe winter experience. I want it to have a chill that feels like you're pulling cold air into your lungs, making your chest feel wide open."

"Sir, could you please just order something from the menu?"

"Don't you have a custom service? I'm hoping for a freeze-dried tropical house."

"I hate that I think I know what you mean."

And then Lee Cheonghyeon actually came back with a low-temperature, high-humidity house track that had a moist, refreshing feel. It was a song like the wind that touches your skin when you step outside on an autumn morning. I swallowed tears of emotion in the face of his great talent.

Since we took the energy out of the song, the styling had to match. So I opened up the color palette and the 'Dye Sheet by Promotion' that we back up for every comeback, and picked out low-saturation colors that did not overlap.

Iwol - Ash gray

Jeho - Platinum blond (as much white tone as possible)

Seongbin - Ash brown (He has had brown hair many times before. I would like a significant difference to distinguish it.)

Joowoo - Ash blue

Cheonghyeon - cheonghyeon (navy-cyan) color

Kiyeon - Rouge gray (close to light gray)

★ All members must have high brightness and low saturation (except Cheonghyeon)★

※ Color chips attached separately on the reference page

I wrote this in the proposal and submitted it, and I even got a confirmation text from Jukyung-nim.

Team Leader Min Jukyung [Iwol-ah]

[About wanting Cheonghyeon's hair to be cheonghyeon color]

[That wasn't a typo, you really mean the navy-cyan color, right?]

[You want to lower the saturation and match the brightness with the other guys, but only to an extent that doesn't ruin the impression of his signature color]

Me [Yes, that's right!]

[I've always wanted to dye Cheonghyeon's hair cheonghyeon color at least once, and this is the perfect chance]

Team Leader Min Jukyung [Got it...]

Me [Are you tired of me being like this?]

Team Leader Min Jukyung [No, I like that you're consistent]

I wanted to joke around more, but I held back so I would not waste a busy professional's time. I also resisted the urge to dye Choi Jeho's hair pumpkin-colored. *I feel like a proper adult.*

After spending two days and one night in and out of the salon, we ended up with hair colors like mold on cheese. They came out so well that a blue cheese brand could have packaged and sold them on the spot.

The well-dyed members of spArk were wrapped up prettily like a bouquet of flowers and put on an emotional performance of waiting for a missed loved one. Perhaps worried about what they would have to give up this time, they started preparing before anyone even told them to practice acting, playing a collection of drama clips with explosive emotional scenes at the dorm.

It is embarrassing, but I have my own history with this kind of concept. This body of mine survived the first monthly evaluation as a trainee by singing a song full of loneliness and emptiness. Still, I watched the drama clips diligently next to Kang Kiyeon, afraid I might get banned from talking to the manager.

Returning with a much more mature atmosphere after being the midsummer heartbroken man in denial, our image changed so much you could call it a transformation. Even compared to our 『Memory』 era, which had a somewhat similar outward impression, more than two years had passed, letting us present a much more mature feeling.

The mood of reminiscing about the past hinted at several things. The lyrics clearly depicted bygone memories, and the music video portrayed the traces of someone.

We had never pushed this kind of material as our main concept, but I expected spArk to pull it off well. The 5-member spArk often pushed it during their 'frozen-to-death lawn fairy' days.

It is an ambiguous concept for a rookie idol group to take on. It does not leave a visually impactful impression, and since it suits lyrical songs well, it is difficult for a dance group to pull it off stylishly. It was a style that would get buried immediately if the song and styling were even slightly old-fashioned.

*We debuted with a concept like this and ended up in that state, so we didn't even dare to try it again until we were a few years into our career.*

It went beyond not daring to try it. I had completely discarded it in my mind. It was not for nothing that we released dazzlingly colorful music videos with flashing lights on screen until our third year. We were desperate too.

Now that the conditions were right, however, I thought it was an idea worth pushing. It was worth trying if we had the experience to pull off melancholic acting, a trendy melody, a storyboard that could create sensational visuals, and listeners who would pay attention even to a minor title track.

Seeing the results of 『Memory』 and 『Scene』, I gained the confidence that we would not completely flop. The issue of needing to supplement our vocals as the performance became less intense was resolved as half the members prepared for their solos.

Accessibility to the general public, who prefer more emotional codes compared to the idol scene, also opened up. With all this, there was no way I could not take on the challenge.

And, this is a petty truth I had kept buried deep inside... If before I had not considered it because I thought the time was not right, after I ran away from home and came back, a big part of it was that I was reluctant to touch anything that carried traces of the 5-member spArk.

It is true that we deliberately released strong concepts one after another to refresh the perspective of the fandom and the industry. 『Sprint』 and 『Flare』 contained the intention to show that 'spArk is completely intact!'. That is why we danced until our legs nearly broke. There was a reason we spent a ton of money on the music video for 『Stand by』 to have a lavish year-end wrap-up, and then returned as a youth band the next year.

At the same time, I also had the thought, *I'm going to work hard as part of spArk now, so I kind of want to... say goodbye to the 5-member spArk?* If I had once felt uncomfortable, as if I were in a place I should not be, then being dragged back to the dorm had left me attached to the 6-member spArk. It sounds cheesy saying it like this, but it is the truth, so what can I do.

But now, having been forgiven for my sins and resolved to stay with spArk, the remnants of the 5-member group posed no threat to Kim Iwol, whose job security was guaranteed. I now had the guts to tell someone to sit down and talk first even if they told me to leave, so overlapping our current selves with the 5-member spArk era, something I had been reluctant to touch, became embarrassingly easy.

The spArk guys must also be happier now, being a good example of how to use muted colors, than during their 'personal color-ignoring, icicle-weed fairy' days when their faces looked mugwort-colored. If they say they are not... I will have to lock them in a room for eight hours for a little chat.

Anyway, it was nice that decorating ourselves in a new style opened up new things we could try. With the colors washed out, the guys looked kind of like flowers made from naturally dyed paper.

I once told Lee Cheonghyeon that his beauty was like a hydrangea, and I was deeply moved when he became a 3D bouquet of hydrangeas made from hanji. *I'm worried a hanji craft museum might come and snatch him away.*

As I was rewatching the rough cut, full of worries, my phone rang. The screen, which had brightened once then dimmed, could not handle the flood of messages and practically spewed fire. I paused the video because of the incessant vibrations.

The culprit behind the countless notifications was the group chat that included the managers. The last messages were all just 'ㄲ's. When I scrolled up to where my name was tagged, I saw the sentence, 'Read up to here.'

It was a sign that the rule of always tagging the relevant person when sharing new matters had taken root.

Manager Euion [Sharing the menu and banner drafts for the reverse tribute coffee truck for our first broadcast! (* ...spArk _Iwol-hyung ...spArk _Jeho-hyung ...spArk _Seongbin-hyung ...spArk _Joowoo-hyung ...spArk _Cheonghyeon-hyung ...spArk _ Kiyeon-hyung)]

[I'll give you the details at the meeting later!]

It was news I had been waiting for anyway. I immediately checked the image.
