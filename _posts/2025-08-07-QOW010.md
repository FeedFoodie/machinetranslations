Lucius, whose gaze had been fixed on the strange equipment, gave a delayed nod. Estella's expression, however, overflowed with a confidence so profound she looked like someone who had just acquired a priceless jewel.

"How do you use this?" Lucius asked, approaching a peculiar piece of equipment that was too narrow for a bed yet lacked the backrest of a chair.

"This is a bench press."

"A what?"

A dizzying wave of confusion washed over Lucius as he watched Estella use such an unfamiliar term so casually. She seemed like a stranger, not the Estella he knew, and he found himself studying her face intently. Her confidence was absolute, leaving no room to doubt her words.

"You lie here," she explained, demonstrating the motion herself, "and lift a barbell or dumbbell above your chest. You repeat it slowly, gradually increasing the weight. It strengthens your chest, shoulders, arms, and even your abs."

Her demonstration was precise. "It's best to lower the barbell until it lightly touches your chest. You have to be careful though, don't let the weight crush your chest or neck if you can't handle it. When you lift, you need to exhale and straighten your elbows. Lift it straight up, right in line with your shoulders!"

The movement looked simple, but it clearly required a great deal of precision.

"May I try?"

"Of course," Estella readily agreed, then turned to Dolan, who was standing nearby. "Would you like to try too? As it happens, there are two benches."

Based on height alone, Lucius seemed to have the clear advantage. Dolan, however, had dwarven blood and possessed incredible muscles that were plain to see.

"Oh, how could I possibly dare compete with a knight..."

"Why not?" Estella countered. "This is a good chance to see whose strength is greater, a knight's or a blacksmith's."

At her sly provocation, the two men's gazes locked. Though the one looking down and the one looking up were both smiling, Estella easily spotted a competitive fire blazing in their eyes, hot as a forge's furnace.

"I'm fine with it," Lucius said, "but it might be difficult for Dolan. Besides, his arms and legs are short..."

Knight Commander Lucius was the first to rise to the bait. He was an upright man who seemed unshakable against any temptation, yet his competitive spirit flared instantly. It was surely that same spirit that had propelled him to the rank of Knight Commander at such a young age.

Estella fanned the flames with the practiced skill of a seasoned trainer. "Is that really so, Dolan?"

"What nonsense," Dolan shot back, not yielding an inch. "I've been swinging a hammer for thirty years. My hammer is ten times heavier than most swords."

Deciding to raise the stakes, Estella shouted with deliberate force. "Alright! It's a showdown between Knight Commander Lucius and the blacksmith Dolan!"

At her cry, the knights and blacksmiths who had been losing interest scrambled back, gathering in front of the physical training room. The entrance, however, was small for such a large crowd, and the shorter blacksmiths began to clamor, unable to see.

"Come on in and watch," Estella invited.

At her words, they rushed inside, scrambling for the best view with the fervor of young ladies who had just received free opera tickets. Soon, two half-moon-shaped crowds had formed around Lucius and Dolan. Estella positioned herself confidently at the head of the two men lying on the benches, taking on the air of a referee.

"The one who lifts the most repetitions wins," she announced. "To ensure fairness, one of the blacksmiths will count for Commander Lucius, and one of the knights will count Dolan's lifts!"

It was a full-fledged showdown.

{sep}

Catherine, having heard that Estella was up to something, was heading toward the physical training room in her carriage. A massive roar from the training grounds suddenly made her ears perk up.

"WAAAAAAAAAAH!"

"WAAAAAAH!"

The closer the carriage drew, the louder the roar became. It sounded as if a major event was taking place, yet Catherine had heard nothing about it.

"What is that noise?" she asked.

Lucille, who was just as clueless, simply shook her head in bewilderment. Then, as if a thought struck her, she quickly offered an answer. "Perhaps it's a sparring training day?"

"Sparring isn't until next week."

Catherine was more interested in the knight order's schedule than anyone. The knights were the first group she needed to win over to build her power within the marquisate, so she made a point of personally attending every one of their events, large or small. If she could not go, she sent words of encouragement through Lucille. Her mere appearance was enough to make the knights cheer like soldiers on the brink of defeat seeing the arrival of reinforcements. Each time it happened, Catherine never doubted that she would soon be the master of the marquisate. The knights loyal to her father would one day become her people.

*But there's an event I don't know about?*

As the carriage neared the training grounds, the roar grew louder, and Catherine's anxiety swelled with it.

She had her carriage stop boldly near the grounds and was quite bewildered when no one came out to greet her. Normally, they would have rushed to escort her the moment the carriage stopped, but today, there was no one. Instead, the unidentifiable roar continued, so mercilessly loud it made her eardrums ache.

When the coachman could bear it no longer, he quickly dismounted to escort her himself. Catherine reluctantly stepped down from the carriage with a displeased expression, though she did not forget to offer the coachman a bright smile and a word of thanks. The corners of her forced smile trembled minutely.

"Her Ladyship has arrived, yet where is everyone!" Lucille exclaimed, reading the displeasure on Catherine's face and growing angry on her behalf.

Just then, three or four low-ranking knights who had belatedly spotted the carriage came rushing over.

"It's alright," Catherine said, stopping Lucille before turning to the knights with her question. "Where has everyone gone?" 

She had to show how understanding she was, all while maintaining the dignified and elegant demeanor befitting the heir to the marquisate. She was absolutely not the sort of vulgar person who would fly into a rage at her subordinates.

"They are gathered in the physical training room."

"The physical training room?" A bad feeling crept over her. "Don't tell me... you mean that place my sister made?"

As expected, the name she hated to hear grated on her nerves.

"......"

As the knights answered, they kept stealing glances toward the source of the roars, their eagerness to return perfectly clear.

*So everyone is with my sister?*

Catherine wanted to scream, but forced herself to hold it in. She could have built a stupid physical training room for them anytime.

"Oh, is that so?" she said. "I'd like to go see it too. Would you guide me?"

Though she was furious, she knew that the moment she appeared, all the attention focused on her sister would instantly shift to her. With that knowledge, Catherine's steps became as light as flower petals. Estella, who was merely born the eldest daughter and was incompetent, lazy, and even ugly, could never defeat her. Not her, who had worked with tear-inducing diligence.

Just as Catherine had predicted, the knights who spotted her scrambled to move aside as she appeared before the physical training room. The faces of those standing close enough to touch looked enraptured, as if they were gazing upon a living goddess.

"We greet the Jewel of Cardien, Lady Catherine."

At the loud declaration, the anxiety that had been churning in her mind vanished completely. Catherine turned toward the voice and offered a bright smile in return, which was met with a wave of admiring gasps. As always, revealing her presence before so many people was a thrilling experience.

*This is how it should be. This is right.*

Savoring the familiar gazes that clung to her like magnets, she walked forward step by step. The knights blocking her path parted smoothly to either side, like the Red Sea.

At the spot where the human sea ended, Catherine stopped and finally saw Estella.

Her sister stood there in unsightly trousers like a stablehand, wearing no makeup and not even properly dressed. She was positioned between two men who were frantically repeating some useless motion, looking as if she were their judge.

*What is that! It's horrible! It's vulgar!*

Catherine suppressed the urge to scream, forcing a smile as she stared at Estella. With her arrival, the surrounding roar began to die down. Now, everyone in the physical training room was aware of her presence. While the crowd stood frozen, only two people paid her no mind. The two men continued their repetitive movements, lifting and lowering something from the start.

"Sister..."

"What are you doing! Keep counting! The team that can't count accurately loses!"

Catherine opened her mouth, ready to offer a greeting in her nightingale-like voice, but Estella's shriek cut through the air. Catherine snapped her mouth shut in surprise as the two people standing on either side of Estella resumed their loud count.

"Forty-one!"

"Forty-three!"
