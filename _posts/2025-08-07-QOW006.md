"Goodness, you made all this?" Estella asked, her voice filled with surprise as she stared at the watermelon-sized jar of ash. It shimmered with a faint blue light, a perfect result that had once taken her a month of repeated failures to achieve. Ria had managed it in a single day.

"Your face!" Estella's surprise turned to alarm when she finally noticed the dark circles under Ria's eyes. "Don't tell me you were up all night."

"...Yes, but it's alright. I'm not tired at all."

Ria's calm reply only made a pang of guilt strike Estella.

"You could have taken your time," she said, her voice firm. "Don't ever do this again."

"But it was the first task you ever gave me, my lady."

Ria had always been like this, never knowing how to pace herself. She was the furthest thing from lazy, impeccably diligent and so quiet that in her past life, Estella had never even known she existed. By the time she learned this girl had mourned for her until the very end, there had been no time left. Now, miraculously returned in another's body, Estella understood. God must have granted her this second chance to teach her to cherish the small connections she had once overlooked. She resolved to treasure everything before her, selectively of course.

"Ria. Go get some more sleep."

"No, it's fine. I want to accompany you when you go to exercise. Please let me stay by your side. I'm really not tired at all."

Staring at Ria, who insisted she was fine with her kind eyes wide, Estella finally relented with a small smile and a nod.

"Then let's do this. You'll go to bed early tonight. How about that?"

"......"

Ria's shy, bashful smile was her answer. Estella brushed a speck of ash from the girl's hair and took a deep breath as if to clear the air.

"Alright then, shall we try the Miraclium tea that Ria made?"

*Miraclium.*

Ria silently savored the name Estella had given it. Such a cool name made her feel as if she had accomplished something truly amazing, and joy swelled within her. She watched intently as Estella spooned the ash into a teapot and slowly brewed it. Ria had never heard of making tea from ash, let alone imagined it could smell so fragrant.

POUR!

The liquid that filled the teacup was tinged with the same blue as the ash. It looked like a piece of the sky, filtered until not a single cloud remained, captured in porcelain. Ria marveled that such clear water could be drawn from simple ash.

Estella slowly sipped the tea, its faint minty aroma filling her senses. A cool, refreshing sensation spread through her mouth, clearing her head.

Miraclium.

The common weed, known as gall grass, had a secret. When turned into blue ash and consumed as tea, it purified the dulling effects of punyangcho, a key ingredient in weight-gaining medicine, and restored the body to its original state. This effect had only been discovered when Estella was over forty in her past life, sparking a Miraclium craze that swept the empire.

It was the so-called miracle diet tea.

Its price had later skyrocketed to astronomical heights, yet it remained so rare that most could never obtain it. Estella savored the precious liquid before looking at Ria.

"From now on, instead of lemon water, could you bring me this tea, chilled, after my workout?"

"Yes, my lady!"

Ria's reply was immediate and bright. Her amber eyes, shining in a genuine smile, sparkled like stars, overjoyed to have found a way to be useful to her lady.

"What are you looking at so intently?"

Vice-Commander Gai approached Commander Lucius on the training ground's watchtower. Lucius stood perfectly still, his gaze fixed on something below.

"Ah... it's Lady Estella."

Gai's lips twisted into a sneer, his expression filled with scorn rather than anticipation. He did not view Estella favorably, a sentiment shared by the entire knight order. The opinion that 'It would have been better if Lady Catherine were the eldest daughter' was common gossip, and Gai was a firm believer.

"What do you see?" Lucius asked, his gaze never wavering. It was the stare of a predator observing its prey, a sharpness honed by long years of war against demonic beasts.

"She's just been running for much longer than usual," Gai replied with a shrug. He assumed this was just another of Estella's unreasonable attempts to lose weight, even if this one was lasting a little longer than the others.

"Is that all?"

"Pardon? What do you mean, Commander?"

"Look again. Watch the way Lady Estella is running."

Gai reluctantly looked down again, not understanding what meaning could possibly be found in a simple run, but as Estella completed her fifth lap, his expression hardened with disbelief.

"Do you see it now?" Lucius asked quietly.

"...But how," Gai whispered, his gaze wavering.

Despite five full laps around the training ground, Estella's breathing was perfectly steady.

And there was more.

"She's strategically managing her stamina and maintaining a constant pace," Gai mumbled, admiration seeping into his absentminded tone.

It was a method he recognized, starting slowly, accelerating at the midpoint, then controlling her speed at the end to stabilize her energy. It was remarkably similar to the movements soldiers used to maintain stamina during a battlefield retreat. Only then did Gai turn to Lucius with a dazed expression.

"That's right," Lucius said, a smile of interest touching his lips.

"That's not just simple exercise. It's a basic tactical drill for training soldiers."

"Oh my goodness! My lady, I think we'll have to take in the dress again!"

Claire, who had come to the castle with the altered dress, exclaimed as she saw the loose gap between the fabric and Estella's body. She even clapped her hands, looking genuinely delighted.

"Oh, really?"

Estella feigned ignorance, though she could feel her body changing day by day. It was inevitable, given she spent nearly every morning exercising until the sun was high in the sky. Moreover, the Miraclium Ria had made so perfectly was shockingly effective, burning fat at a rate two or three times faster than exercise alone. Her facial lines seemed different every time she woke up. The 'miracle potion' had certainly earned its name.

Seeing Estella's gentle smile, Claire began to fuss over her.

"Yes! Your jawline is sharper, and your arms and legs are so much slimmer. You've lost a lot around your waist, too! And your skin looks much better. The pimple on your chin is gone!"

Claire was as happy about the daily transformation as if it were her own. Listening to the tailor, Estella examined her reflection from various angles. Just as Claire said, she looked to have lost a lot of weight since first opening her eyes here. Without a scale she couldn't be sure, but it felt like at least twenty kilograms were gone.

Yet, despite the considerable loss, her body still felt sluggish and overweight.

*I have to get back to my old body as soon as possible.*

Being slim and pretty was a secondary concern. She couldn't do anything in this state. To prevent her father's suspicious death and the family's downfall, she needed strength, and there was a limit to what she could accomplish in a body that felt like a soggy dumpling. Her muscles itched to move with their former nimbleness, and a sharp frustration flared up several times a day when her body refused to obey.

*I wish I had a gym...*

She was tired of just running laps and wanted to do some proper strength training. The thought sparked another. As a noble lady, she surely had the funds to build one. Estella stopped adjusting her clothes and hurried into the dressing room. If her memory served her, this version of Estella owned a great deal of jewelry.

"Just as I thought!"

Sure enough, the room was filled with enough dazzling jewels to shame a jewelry shop. The designs, however, were all more suited for a dignified old matron than a young woman just past twenty. She wondered if this, too, was the result of her 'lovely' little sister's influence. The dressing room was a testament to the rumors of a foolish duchess with closets bursting with ill-fitting dresses and hoarded jewels, filled with nothing but tacky and valuable items.

"I can just sell these!"

She was unsure of the value of jewels here, but she felt confident she could make enough to create simple exercise equipment like dumbbells and barbells.
