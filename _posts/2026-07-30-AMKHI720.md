A while ago, spArk held a 'collaboration cafe play' at their dorm for Kang Kiyeon.

For various reasons, they were not filming for iEuro, so they only took photos of the collaboration cafe play, with no video recording.

After the exciting event, the quick-fingered members of spArk spread the story of celebrating Kang Kiyeon's solo far and wide through BubblePop, live streams, and their official accounts. Since spArk had never dropped spoilers without eventually releasing the content, Sparklers found it hard to accept that no self-produced content was coming.

≫ What do you mean only Sparklers can't see Kiyeon's collaboration cafe play...? Guys, is this all our friendship amounts to...?

≫ It's so funny how Sparklers got all excited to get the 'col-ca-nol' content crumbs and then got sad. Actually, it's not funny, it's sad...…

≫ I get why they didn't release it, so I'm not upset or anything, but Sparklers have been spoiled up until now, so we can't accept this situation. This is all spArk's fault.

≫ I mean, why would you film such fun content of you guys pulling photocards and having otaku talks by yourselves? It makes me jealous.

≫ You don't have to give us the content, just tell us the stories ㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠㅠ

The understanding Sparklers refrained from posting hurtful comments in public, like saying our discipline had slipped or that we had become lifeless like frozen pollocks. Talk of disappointment was bound to come up in fan-only spaces, however. I prepared the 'spArk Reverse Tribute Cafe Play', an event that allowed unlimited use of our IP.

In other words, I booked a coffee truck to send to the music shows during our first comeback week.

I had been thinking about it since planning the collaboration cafe play, so it did not take long to prepare. If we could not show them the self-produced content, we had to provide something else.

The last fan event prize was a raffle for a hellish t-shirt you could not wear in public without ruining your social life, so this time I arranged a proper event that could fill Sparklers with pride. To make up for it, I added a touch of the collaboration cafe play's flavor to ensure the fans would not feel left out.

Had the Kim Iwol from my assistant manager days been in charge of naming, he would have probably come up with names like 'Manager, I'll Have an Americano' or 'Manager, I'm Taking Herbal Medicine, so I'll Have a Peach Iced Tea'. For dessert, he would have sold something like 'Break-When-You-Want-to-Break-the-Company Approval Chocolate Cookies'.

The idol Kim Iwol, his exorcism complete, created a normal menu. It also helped that I had learned the grammar of naming while coming up with drink names for Kang Kiyeon.

The final menu featured a black bean soy latte to wish for a healthy meeting, a calamansi juice that wakes you up just by looking at it, a chocolate smoothie that looks sweet but is made bittersweet with dark chocolate, a blue lemonade that might be the secret to a clear voice, a matcha latte that is freshly made and delicious despite its appearance, and a taro milk tea full of chewy and cute boba.

I typed and retyped with trembling hands, afraid the names would be rejected for being too long, but the effort was worth it. For reference, the drinks matched each member's hair color.

You need tea foods with tea. I learned that from borrowing and reading Kang Kiyeon's comic books. Where I had previously filled dessert boxes mainly with trendy snacks, this time I packed them full of classic finger foods.

You are supposed to eat an afternoon tea set from the bottom tier up, but I could not make the snack box three-tiered. As a next-best option, I placed a special order for the components so they could be eaten one by one in a clockwise direction. Naturally, the instruction card included with the reverse tribute items also became endlessly long.

*I need to learn to be more concise when necessary. This is a problem.*

"We can get this along with the lunch boxes, right?"

As I lamented my rambling habit, Jeong Seongbin checked the pickup times for the lunch boxes and snacks.

I kept telling them I put sandwiches in the snack box. Choi Jeho asked whether that could even be considered a meal, however. I became afraid we would become idols who starve their fans, so I ordered extra lunch boxes.

*Being idols who starve their fans is more humiliating than being lifeless 'frozen pollock' idols. That's a title I never want to have for the rest of my life.*

After the coffee truck issue, the next topic on the agenda was self-produced content to film by the end of the year.

This was the main agenda for the day. The screen filled with things we had jotted down, suggestions from the planning team, recent trending topics, and items mentioned in the fan community.

"Shall we start by picking out the ones that fit the album concept?" Min Jukyung directed.

Lasers shot from everyone's eyes. Their determination to pick a good topic was intense.

"Is the person who proposed 'A Day in the Life of ○○ Waiting for the Members' here?"

"That's me...!" one of the staff members raised their hand. "I wanted to do something that connects with the music video concept while maintaining the fresh atmosphere that self-produced content should have. I also thought it was a topic that wouldn't be too burdensome to film in the middle of promotions since it doesn't have high costs."

"The main person will keep changing, but we can't release the same item for six weeks in a row. Would it be better to upload other videos and insert these in between?"

"We could do it in pairs, or cut them into shorter segments like sub-content and upload them outside of the regular schedule. Right now, attention is trending towards being scattered by news from other companies, so we need to proactively do whatever we can to keep interest focused on us."

A fierce discussion ensued. We each checked our schedules, trying our best to find a day when only one member would be left behind.

"What if there's just no way for only one person to be left behind?"

"Then everyone but one person packs their bags and leaves for a while," Min Jukyung said firmly.

At Min Jukyung's words, all of spArk became prospective runaways. I had never been this excited for a shoot before.

"What do you think about filming a pajama live stream?"

"Pajamas?"

"I think it would look pretty if we set a calm and cozy mood, matching the tone to their hair colors. Would a live stream be better than a recording?"

"If they're wearing pajamas and doing an active segment, a recording would be better. However, if it's mainly them lying down and talking, the best topics for real-time communication are taking questions or talking about recent events, and we just filmed a Q&A for our self-produced content recently."

"Ah, so you're saying a recorded Q&A would be redundant?"

Once promotions started, we would have to do a few live streams anyway. The pajama live stream was adopted as a filming idea, with the opinion that a pajama concept would be fine for one or two of them.

"How about the spArk members split into two teams of three for the shoot?"

The question came just as I was wondering what kind of pajamas I should wear.

"Not as a full group?"

*Not the full group for a group comeback!*

It was not that there would be no full-group live streams, since all six of us would definitely be at the comeback live after the MV release and the final broadcast live. Still, I was a little surprised, because I had naturally pictured all six of us together.

"Iwol, your condition isn't great, is it? Your schedule isn't just the live stream, and I'm not worried about dead air with three of you on screen. Let's do it this way so you can take care of your health."

*So it was because of me. Everyone must have been quite worried about my nosebleed. My internal conflict over Han Gawoon has been resolved, so I'm a happy Kim Iwol now, though.*

It was gratifying to know they trusted us not to have dead air even if we were not a full group. The past, where I had worked so hard to make spArk's members spout comments automatically, flashed before my eyes.

"If you're worried about overlapping content, we should create different combinations. We'll look at the members' schedules, create a plan, and share it with the managers."

Thank goodness, the name 'Cheong-Je-Gi' would not appear on the spArk Metube channel again. Had that damn unit name come up again, I might have lost my temper and hung Choi Jeho from the dorm veranda.

"It would be good to include a segment that utilizes the advantages of a three-person broadcast, besides just a communication-focused stream. We should aim to reverse any potential disappointment about it not being the full group, making them think, 'Oh, so this is the kind of content we get to see because it's not the full group!'" I said, suppressing my anger.

Tying Choi Jeho to the veranda was one thing, but work still had to be done.

"Like a 'Praise the Absent Member' segment. Rolling papers or 'first/current impression' talks are popular because people want to hear stories about their favorite members from others' perspectives. If we only say negative things, it'll feel like we're just talking behind their backs and be similar to the voice-modulated content, so wouldn't it be nice to have a heartwarming time instead?"

Perhaps everyone was worried about spArk becoming snipers who target their own teammates. The 'Highly Advanced Roundabout Compliments Disguised as Front-stabbing' segment was approved smoothly.

"Now, shall we look at the ideas the members submitted?" Min Jukyung said.

The screen changed. The column where our submitted ideas were entered greatly enlarged.

"I was reviewing this before the meeting and was shocked. It's almost December, but there's a suggestion about swimming."

"That's the thing Jeho-hyung promised during the Q&A broadcast, right?" Lee Cheonghyeon had a good memory.

I had half-forgotten about it and only just realized. My brain probably wanted to erase the memory of watching that broadcast.

"Should we go film on location?"

"Should we just get it over with when we film the next music video?"

Unaware of my inner turmoil, the staff members deliberated on how to make Choi Jeho's empty promise a reality.

*I wish they would stop this discriminatory treatment against the Center Emperor. Someone should try asking me what kind of self-produced content I want to film. I'd say, without taking a breath, that we should film an internet lecture commemorating Labor Day becoming a public holiday. They wouldn't listen to my opinion if I said that, yet they only listen to Choi Jeho. It's so unfair.*

"Who wrote down 'insubordination'...?" Jeong Seongbin muttered, staring at the sheet.

It was so hard having more than one or two audacious guys on the team. Meanwhile, my brain tried to convince my ego, saying it might be okay when I thought about the guys suffering under a talkative hyung and a quiet hyung.

*The only 'insubordination time' we had was briefly on IDC, and even that broadcast was taken down...I wonder if it'll be okay.*

"Me."

An unpleasant voice reached my ears just as I was casting a spell on myself to view my colleague's opinion as positively as possible. The culprit was Choi Jeho.

"While we were doing the Q&A, I thought about it, and it seemed like life in the dorms would be stifling for the members who only have hyungs."

*This bastard again. Of all people, a convincing reason had to come out of his mouth!*

Unable to ignore the moved looks in the eyes of Jeong Seongbin and the other two members, spArk ended up deciding to hold a grand festival of insubordination led by the second-oldest hyung. It seemed like it was going to be a fruitful promotion period.
