Staring into the mirror, Estella’s memories poured into Seoyi’s mind like a waterfall. The torrent of a lifetime rushed in all at once, yet it was not overwhelming. Instead, each memory etched itself into her mind as simply as a seal stamped upon a contract, a fleeting moment that felt as if a stubborn blockage had finally been cleared away.

"I've... become Estella."

From a childhood spent enduring all sorts of scorn to an execution at forty-nine under a false accusation, the memories were as vivid as if she had lived them only yesterday. The emotions, the pain, and the anger of that time felt so completely her own that she could not possibly think of them as belonging to someone else. In that moment, she understood just how desperately Estella had pleaded for another to live in her stead.

The foolishly kind Estella had given her last meal to a starving old woman just before her execution. After the old woman had devoured the warm soup and soft bread, she had asked Estella for her wish.

> "If only I could go back to the past, I would."

It was a wish Estella knew was futile, but she had been just that desperate.

When a miracle granted her another chance, she doubted her own ability to see it through. She was terrified that if she lived again, she would only repeat the foolishness of trusting Catherine until the very moment of her death. In the end, Estella chose the soul of Yoon Seoyi, a soul from which she felt a powerful energy. The god in the guise of the old woman gladly granted this final wish.

> [I have heard your prayer.]

The old woman’s benevolent smile and her hazy, dreamlike voice remained vivid in her mind.

"Then what about me?"

Seoyi had been satisfied with her life.

*To climb to that position, I worked to the point of death... yet this is my reward? To live the life of someone with an unattractive body and a weak spirit, a woman ultimately abandoned by the family she believed had loved her?*

Rage boiled inside her.

"My only sin was living diligently," Seoyi trembled, her fists clenched tight. "You can't do this to me. Even if you're a god, this isn't right."

Then a sudden realization struck her.

*Couldn't I be the one to change it?*

If she, who had died in an explosion, was now living in a different time and a different world, then this was a chance to remake not only Estella’s life but her own as well. With the vivid memories of both Yoon Seoyi and Estella at her disposal, she could definitely change the future.

Having organized her thoughts, Seoyi slowly raised her head to meet the round face in the mirror. As her mind settled, her seething heart calmed. *First, let's do what I can.*

Unlike the formidable Yoon Seoyi, who had been difficult to even approach, Estella had a truly round and gentle impression. Her body was so overweight that her neck was invisible, which made her hands and feet seem small by comparison. A closer look, however, revealed sparkling blue eyes, bright forsythia-blonde hair, and fair skin that were all quite presentable.

*Is this an unscratched lottery ticket?*

As Seoyi carefully examined the face in the mirror, she heard a soft knock, knock.

"Come in."

The response flowed from her mouth so naturally that it startled her for a moment. Then she remembered again that she was now Estella. She was both Yoon Seoyi and Estella Cardein. Two complete personalities, not leaning toward either side, were vividly alive within her, making her feel like an actress with a dual role.

The door creaked open and a woman as pretty as a fairy entered the room with a bright smile. Seoyi gasped without realizing it at the sight of such unrealistic beauty.

"Sister, did you sleep well? You're up early today, aren't you?"

The sweetly speaking girl was her half-sister, Catherine. She had cherry-blossom pink hair, light green eyes reminiscent of the dawn, and a petite frame a handspan shorter than her own. Her waist was so slender it looked as if it could not be grasped in one handful.

Catherine’s eyes quickly scanned Estella’s body, and the corners of her mouth lifted in a fresh smile.

*How could I not have known?* That sharp glint in her eyes was a look of confirming her own superiority.

"Yes. I woke up early."

While Estella was still in her nightgown, Catherine was perfectly dressed. Her well-brushed hair flowed with a soft sheen, and her light, barely-there makeup only enhanced her pure beauty.

Catherine clasped her hands as if in prayer and smiled as brightly as a primrose.

"Perfect! After our morning tea, let's go for a walk together! The roses in the garden are in full bloom. I think it would be lovely to pick a bouquet and put them in your room. What do you think?"

Her voice was like the sound of jade beads rolling on a silver tray. Listening to her speak, Seoyi imagined jade beads she had never actually seen.

Catherine placed the tray she was carrying on the table and smiled brightly. A familiar scent had tickled Seoyi’s nose the moment she entered. It was the faint scent of lavender mixed with the soft smell of milk. Every morning, Catherine personally made lavender milk tea for Estella and brought it to her sister’s room with sweet cookies.

She had been a good sister who sincerely loved and cherished her older sister, even going out of her way to do a maid’s job. Or so Estella had thought.

Estella stared blankly at the warmly heated milk tea, then raised the corners of her mouth toward Catherine. For years, she had deliciously drunk the milk tea Catherine made, never knowing it contained a weight-gaining drug. She had been moved every single time by her lovely sister’s kind heart.

"Alright. Let's do that."

When Estella answered in a calm tone, Catherine tilted her head as if something was strange. Even the simple gesture was breathtakingly lovely, a sight to behold even for another woman. The nickname ‘Jewel of Cardein’ was not for nothing.

"......"

"Um, it's nothing. You just seem a little different today, sister."

"I do?"

Estella flinched, feeling a pang of guilt, but she skillfully hid her emotions just as Yoon Seoyi would have. She could feel all of Estella’s anger, but she cooled the boiling emotions with Yoon Seoyi’s composure.

"Somehow... you seem calmer, I guess?"

"Ah... I think I might have a bit of a cold. I don't have much energy."

"Really? Oh no! Should we postpone the walk then? I'll tell Father and have him call for the doctor! Please have some tea while you wait!"

Catherine scurried out of the room. Her steps were so light she did not look like she was running but like a butterfly fluttering away. Catherine, who walked with a dancer’s grace, even had tiny feet.

"Oh dear. I think my sister is sick," she had the meticulousness to shout as if for everyone to hear, even after leaving the room. "I must tell Father and quickly summon Doctor Alexis."

Catherine was adored by everyone for her kind heart as much as for her beautiful appearance. As if to prove it, the maids in the hallway muttered in moved voices.

"My! How can Lady Catherine be so kind?"

"I know, right. Lady Catherine should have been born the eldest daughter."

"Hey, be quiet. What if Lady Estella hears you?"

"How could she hear? Her ears are probably too fat for her to hear anything."

"Hehe, true. Oh, by the way, did you see it? Lady Estella's new dress?"

"Oh. The pink one with all the lace?"

"Ugh! Do you think she really bought it thinking it would suit her?"

"Quiet! The kind Lady Catherine personally called the designer from the Lily Dress Shop to have it made for her."

"Still, for her size, it should have been a bit more..."

"Lady Catherine grants everything Lady Estella wants. She must have been stubborn, so there was no choice."

"She'll probably wear that dress to the upcoming banquet, right?"

"Oh, I can't watch. What do I do? I'm so embarrassed for her."

"Shh! Be quiet. Everyone's keeping it hush-hush. Let's pretend we don't know, for Lady Catherine's sake."

Estella pressed herself against the door, listening as the conversation receded. There was no way they thought she could not hear them when they were talking so loudly. The naive and timid Estella would have pretended not to hear and simply endured it. Over time, the insolent maids must have grown bolder, facing no repercussions for speaking so carelessly in front of the eldest lady.

"I didn't want to wear that dress," Estella muttered, recalling the memory.

She had only worn that horribly frilly lace dress because she had not wanted to waste Catherine’s efforts. That dress, which made her already clumsy body look even more massive.

"I will never wear something like that again."

Estella felt sick just thinking of herself in that dress. She had fled the banquet hall, feeling she would suffocate and die from the countless gazes of pity. Then she had tripped on the lace and fallen. The sound of people snickering as they watched her limp away in a torn dress was still vivid. Catherine had run over with a shocked face and, in front of everyone, draped her own shawl over Estella’s shoulders.

Her face was angelic, her eyes filled with tears as if she herself were in pain.

*Sister, are you okay? Are you hurt? Let's go home together.*

*Catherine, you should stay at the banquet longer.*

*No. A banquet without you isn't fun. If you're going home, I'm going too. I'm happiest when I'm with you, sister.*

*Ah.... my angelic Catherine. My beloved sister.*

*In this life, I will expose that detestable mask of yours piece by piece.*

Estella shoved the tray Catherine had left on the table, knocking it to the floor.

"......"

The beautiful teacup hit the floor and shattered into pieces.

*The tables have turned now.*

The sweet milk tea spread in a milky stain on the carpet, filling the room with a sickly sweet smell. It was then that the door burst open. Catherine, her face flushed from running, approached in surprise.

For a split second, her eyes twisted in annoyance. She quickly reverted, however, to the image of the good Catherine, full of worry for her sister. Her wide eyes were clear and beautiful, as if they held not a single trace of falsehood. It was so convincing that one might wonder if the twisted expression from a moment ago had been a figment of the imagination.

"Gasp! Sister, what happened? Are you hurt? Is your hand okay? Goodness! You must have been so startled."

As Catherine took Estella’s thick hand, her own looked comparatively more delicate. To think this was all an act. If this were a scene from a drama, her acting skills were more than enough to win a grand prize.

Estella, who had been staring at Catherine with an indifferent gaze, finally lowered her head.

"I'm sorry, Catherine," she said. "My hand slipped, and you went to all the trouble of making it."

*I know how to act too, you amateur.*

Estella wore a sullen expression as she held Catherine’s hand.
