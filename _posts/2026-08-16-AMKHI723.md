Baek Haewon was a stuck job seeker, her days as precarious as a lantern in the wind. Her university, which had taken her tuition every semester, was difficult with its prospective graduates. The job market claimed to want newcomers but had far too many demands.

*When will they ban the phrase 'salary to be determined by company policy'...*

They should not have accepted applications from students graduating in February if this was how it would be. They tagged posts with both 'newcomer' and 'experienced' to attract applicants, yet every job posting was arrogant. As a powerless applicant, Baek Haewon had nowhere to vent her frustrations.

spArk soothed Baek Haewon's weary heart. spArk had taken a commemorative photo for their 5th anniversary under a banner that read 'We'll ignite when the cold winds blow', and they seemed to have meant it. They returned with a new album as soon as the cool autumn breeze began to blow.

≫ The temperature drops and spArk shows up.

Am I just supposed to enjoy this?

Bewildered, she even posted on social media. When Miheon-nim asked if she had finished her resume, she almost started a keyboard war, which was unbecoming of a mature adult.

The blurred photocard info and album cover design suggested it would not be to Baek Haewon's taste. Baek Haewon's heart only pounded when a song's BPM exceeded 140, and based on the pre-order posts and concept photo colors, the probability of this album being energetic seemed to be zero. Seriously, what happened to the talk about how all of spArk were 'winter cool tones' and needed vivid colors to brighten their complexions?

She liked that every member had changed their hair color. The world had countless colors, but there was a limit to what could be achieved with dye (+ colors that suit my idol). Within those limited options, spArk perfectly used the colors that suited them and adjusted their makeup to pull off the ones that seemed like a slight miss.

She almost fainted when the high-definition photo of Kim Iwol with ash-gray hair was released. Fair skin and light hair could sometimes blur a person's features, but that was no concern for Kim Iwol.

His long, dense eyelashes defined his eyes, and his distinct features and refreshing mouth created a face with no dull spots. She lost track of time staring at the subtle harmony between the gray shadows on his face and the shimmering, ash-colored hair that fluttered above it.

*Having an oldest member who believes the nation prospers when a handsome man dyes his hair is truly a blessing.*

Baek Haewon recalled Kim Iwol's collection of memorable quotes, which she had seen so many times she could not forget them. Every line was a gem, ready to pop out like an answering machine. Perhaps because his original intentions had not changed, the hair spArk's members had hidden under hoodies now shone like a bouquet of hydrangeas on a rainy day.

The highlight medley overturned her prediction of a slow, ballad-style song. The light melody and simple composition combined with lively instruments to create a remarkably cheerful sound. It had a decent rhythm, so they probably would not just stand still and sing.

*No wonder the visuals were so polished.*

The visuals were quite flashy for a lyrical performance. As a 6th-year Sparkler, Baek Haewon did not immediately post 'This is too boring? Not my style, I'm disappointed ㅠ'. Instead, she calmly waited for spArk's new concept.

She could not wait in peace. A few days before spArk's comeback, a huge issue made the idol scene buzz. This damn company was always a hindrance whenever spArk tried to do anything.

It did not just end with mentions in online communities. Related content appeared on social media and Metube, completely unrelated to idols. Then, one or two news articles multiplied like mushrooms after rain.

As she watched the situation unfold, Baek Haewon's feelings went from 'That house is burning nicely' to...

*They're just trying to have one damn comeback, and all this bullshit is happening.*

…This. Her boys' names kept coming up in another household's fight. No fan would be happy to see their idol dragged into something unpleasant.

Baek Haein, once a master of saving PDFs for legal action but now living her real life, also heard the news. She asked whether Kim Iwol's group had another problem. Baek Haewon replied that her classmate had not done anything wrong and was just suffering from yellow dust from a neighboring country.

≫ It was already infuriating that some crazy company spread a pathetic rumor about making an active idol act as a stooge to poach a senior group from a rival company on the day of a major accident, leaving all the other members behind, but now you're telling me that was just a media play stunt to discipline the guys who were trying to use their wits to escape from under them????

You devil bastards, I hope you all go to hell.

≫ I endured you copying our team's performance. I endured you dissing them on variety shows. I endured you siding with Rekka, hiring paparazzi, and creating a cyberbullying mess, but now that we're digging, there's more??

You don't deserve to call yourselves a company and make money. Take down your sign, you XX.

Baek Haewon, who would not post that her boys' song seemed boring but would not hold back her fury either, saw her cyber-banner spread like wildfire. Even Jjae-i, who rarely reacted to anything but official posts or candid photos, shared Baek Haewon's post. If not for the news of an ongoing audit, Baek Haewon might have collapsed from a stress-induced illness.

Please let MYTH be ruined. Let Parthe fend for themselves. She prayed earnestly and vowed to pour her remaining energy into enjoying spArk's comeback.

{sep}

Fortunately, Baek Haewon's anger did not last long. spArk's music video was released shortly after, and Baek Haewon's attention returned completely to them.

The emotional visuals and the cool energy of the music went well together. The cinematography was excellent, with an intro that captured a cloudy sky and a vast wilderness. It was perfect for a cool autumn day!

spArk looked like they had many stories to tell, which was moving. When Jeong Seongbin opened the song with an intro close to a hum in his fresh, beautiful voice, Baek Haewon felt the temperature in her eardrums drop. His light-colored hair, a vague shade of brown, seemed to flutter with his voice.

Jeong Seongbin's hair was not the only thing swaying. The hems of their clothes and the leaves of the plants in the glass greenhouse all moved slowly.

*Even without slow motion, everyone probably has an image-correction filter applied to Jeong Seongbin, making him play at 0.5x speed on his own...*

She had seen many poor souls become Sparklers after being captured by Jeong Seongbin that way. There was no escape from the leader's malicious self-appeal. He bewitched people with his respectable, neat appearance, secured them with his upright character and mature demeanor, and then revealed his secret true nature at the point of no return.

Last month, she jokingly sent a chat during Kim Iwol and Jeong Seongbin's live stream. 'What do you think about Seongbin using false advertising to attract fans?' Kim Iwol answered that he would take responsibility for eradicating all exaggerated advertising, and it startled her so badly she turned off the broadcast. Since then, Baek Haewon had not sent a single chat during their live streams.

Kang Kiyeon appeared, unzipping his rash guard, and was no ordinary sight. A water droplet trickled down his exposed neck. Behind him were old photos of surfers, a line of surfboards, a vintage black-and-white TV, and informational signs. This exotic space was a surf shop.

On screen, Kang Kiyeon roughly dried his wet hair with a towel. He leaned against a table and gazed at the cloud-filled sea. The towel slung over his shoulder, the soda can he popped open and lowered from his lips, his slouched posture, and his languid expression all exuded an ease rarely seen in a 23-year-old K-Pop male idol, for whom aegyo is basic and playful charm is a must.

The kid released one solo album and gained an adult sexiness. They say true sexiness is not overt, but something that makes people swallow hard. Baek Haewon feared seeing her own lecherous smile on the dark screen if it turned off, so she could not pause the video and just connected her phone to the charger.

But who said things ever go as planned in love?

"Kim Iwol, you crazy bastard… … "

Even if no such saying existed, it would not have mattered to Baek Haewon.

The moment Kim Iwol appeared, she slammed the pause button. She did not dare touch the screen again until the set time passed and the screen went dark.
