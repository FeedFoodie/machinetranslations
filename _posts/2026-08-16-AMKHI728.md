Following Choi Jeho's cooking show and Jeong Seongbin's interpersonal skills show, Park Joowoo's segment presented a daily life documentary of an artist. Park Joowoo woke to sunlight filtering past his blackout curtains. He lingered in his blankets before struggling to get up.

『Joowoo: Seongbin-ah… Do I… have to get up…?』

Park Joowoo's voice was so pitiful that a passerby would have told him to go back to sleep. The Jeong Seongbin who would have let him sleep more was already gone, however.

『Joowoo: He's gone…』

Park Joowoo despaired, burying his face in his pillow. Only the back of his head remained on camera.

『Joowoo: Honestly, I think time should pass twice as slowly in the morning…』

Wrapped tightly in his cotton blanket, his tormented figure resembled a caterpillar that had fallen from a tree. After barely accepting that morning had come, Park Joowoo struggled out of bed into an afternoon as soft and fluffy as a dandelion seed.

Park Joowoo offset the time wasted in bed by drastically shortening his meal prep. He finished cooking by simply mixing egg wash and soft tofu, then steaming them. There was no seasoning.

The show broadcast him scooping up half a spoonful of the steamed tofu and egg, blowing to cool it, and chewing slowly and thoroughly. The scene played with only soft BGM and no subtitles. This strangely calming scene received a quietly positive reaction among Sparklers. As for what kind of positive reaction…

≫ Every element of this segment is peaceful

…It was popular for the same healing feeling as watching a small animal graze on grass. Some things are not stimulating, but you find yourself drawn to them the more you watch.

└ Except for Park Joowoo's shoulders

It seemed some parts were a bit stimulating after all.

After his meal, Park Joowoo did the dishes and cleaned the sink until it was spotless. He wiped away every last drop of water, put on work gloves, and headed to the veranda.

Living in the same house, I knew immediately what Park Joowoo was about to do.

*Repotting, probably.*

Repotting his plants and caring for his plant friends on the veranda was Park Joowoo's long-standing leisure activity on his days off. As expected, Park Joowoo meticulously laid calendar paper on the veranda and sat down. He wiped the dust from his trolley, then brought out and opened a 5kg bag of soil from storage.

The calendar paper was a collection of pages torn from past months. Back in our trainee days, Jeong Seongbin started collecting large sheets of paper for his friend, who would spend thirty minutes cleaning the veranda after repotting a single plant. This became an unspoken rule in our dorm, so we still neatly collect the torn-off pages of the wall calendar. Even when I cleaned the storage by myself, I did not throw them away.

Park Joowoo sometimes recharges his energy in his own space. We mostly did not disturb his time with his companion plants unless he called for us or looked like he needed a hand, but today, we got a glimpse into Park Joowoo's special hobby.

『Joowoo: You don't want to leave the pot you've grown attached to, do you…? I get it, since I also like living where I'm used to, but think of it as part of growing up and bear with it. I prepared a bigger one for you instead. I hope you like it…』

『Joowoo: They say there's a special class on how to make your own ceramic pots. I want to go… Then I could make them in a shape I like, right?』

『Joowoo: I want to bring home a new friend… but the fact that there's no room means you're all growing that well, so I've decided to think of it as a good thing. I won't scold you even if you don't bloom many flowers, so please take care of yourselves…』

The Park Joowoo on the observation camera was more talkative than I thought. I assumed he just watered them quietly, but he constantly interacted with his friends.

He was not just talking about raising plants. The show also revealed behind-the-scenes footage of him secretly hiding and whispering scary things.

The poison spewed by the human 'mallang-i' was potent. It was no exaggeration to call it a one-man talk show. His speaking skills had improved considerably after he became the owner of a Metube channel.

Still, his fundamental nature had not changed. After a large-scale cleaning operation where he even wiped the window frame crevices and tile grout, Park Joowoo spent the afternoon making a small hand-held cushion. He cut it from old clothes that were ready to be thrown away. He said he had learned to sew from Manager Chanyoung-nim in the meantime.

Park Joowoo washed up cleanly after his hard work and changed into soft pajamas. His cozy and 'greenery' segment came to a beautiful conclusion, leaving behind a cute stress ball (cushion-type) and a clean dorm.

Kang Kiyeon had a more active day than Park Joowoo. He moved swiftly out of bed, came into the living room, and woke himself up with leisurely stretches and simple exercises.

He chose granola cereal and milk for breakfast. It was much crunchier than the steamed tofu and egg. He downed everything, including the milk, less than twenty minutes after waking up.

After his meal, Kang Kiyeon zipped around the dorm, taking care of small tasks. He went to the shoe rack and changed the laces on his sneakers to a different color. He put his outerwear in the styler, then replaced the moisture absorbers in each shoe with new ones. While changing his own, he changed them for all the members as well.

Then he did a light bodyweight workout. He meticulously took care of his legs and ankles, perhaps because I had pecked and nagged him like a woodpecker for several years. I felt the satisfaction of all my nagging. The Sparklers also loved the artistic lines of his back muscles and forearms visible through his clothes.
