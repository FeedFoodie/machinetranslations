When idols at their peak share a dorm, days can pass without them seeing each other. Everyone is just that busy.

Work was not the only reason. They had workouts, treatments, and personal training sessions. A mountain of other tasks existed outside their group schedules. To handle it all, they could not afford to relax at the dorm, even with a moment to spare.

It was not that they left and never returned. They were in and out so often it was a wonder the threshold had not worn down. Some dropped by for clothes, others for a quick shower before leaving again. They used any gap in their schedules to stop by the dorm for sleep, which kept the place in constant chaos.

Today, however, was quiet. My alarm went off, and I opened my eyes to an empty bed. Not a sound came from our manager-nim or the members outside.

I sensed an ominous presence and shot up from my bed. A camera was installed in a corner of the ceiling. In the past, spArk only appeared on camera fully dolled up, but ever since our contract renewal, we keep showing our 100% real, bare faces.

*It's a disaster. I hear other groups get full makeup, pretend to be asleep, and then film their wake-up scenes for 'daily life' documentaries. Why do we have to film in a 'deserted island' style even when we're in the middle of the city?*

*I didn't expect them to install the cameras the day after our meeting. Did they figure this was the only way to catch me off guard?*

If so, it was a perfect success.

*I guess the staff and I have gotten really close. They can read my mind completely.*

I pulled the blanket over my head like a cloak and went to the living room. No one was there. I was truly alone. Only a MISSION card and a microphone sat forlornly on the table to greet me. Like a true professional, I put on the microphone first, then opened the card.

Iwol's Day: Waiting for the Members at the Dorm

"I have to be alone all day... ...!"

Using informal speech in front of the camera gave me goosebumps. Unable to leave, I was forced to mic myself up and generate my own screen time. The curtain had risen on this 'proactive, self-produced content'.

{sep}

After waking up and washing my face, I plopped onto the sofa and picked up my phone. I originally had an afternoon meeting, but a quick check showed it was postponed until tomorrow. It seemed I really was stuck at the dorm all day, waiting for the others to get off work.

"I was supposed to practice the challenge with Kiyeon... ..."

Kang Kiyeon, who I was supposed to watch other idols' challenge videos with this morning, was not in his room. With the camera rolling, I could not just think, *Where did Kiyeon go?* A monologue with a clear subject and content was essential. I called him, and he picked up right away.

[Yes, Hyung.]

"Where'd you go?"

[The practice room.]

"How could you leave without me? Are you trying to master the challenge on your own so you can trap me in the endless cycle of late-night practices when I fall behind?"

[I don't leave you alone during practice, Hyung.]

"That just means you *are* going to make me practice late at night, doesn't it?"

The call ended with me fuming.

After a sigh, I took over the living room and scrolled through recent trending challenges. Normally, I would be with another member, copying gestures or chatting about how we would split the parts. Since I was alone today, I just watched while stretching.

"If the difficulty of these challenges keeps rising, how is a survival-type idol like me supposed to make it? Don't educational channels offer online courses on 'Survival in the Great Challenge Era'?"

"Kiyeon should be the one filming a challenge like this. Where is he practicing that he's not churning out a cover video for us? That's a breach of his duty to be diligent."

"The outfits are pretty. It felt like we hadn't seen custom-made outfits for a while... ... Still, custom-made outfits have their own flavor. There's a vibe you just can't get from reformed clothes."

... ... I guess I did not just stick to stretching. Running my mouth has become a habit.

*They'll probably just fast-forward through a scene like this anyway.*

It probably would not seem too dragged out once edited. Since this was my daily routine, I spent my usual amount of time on monitoring. After watching the videos, I put on spArk's music video playlist and wiped the floor with a dry mop. A cleaning service came periodically, but there was nothing wrong with a group of singers having an extra clean home.

My and Choi Jeho's room only needed a quick dusting. Neither of us had many possessions, so things never piled up. Choi Jeho had a habit of dropping his bag, but not just anywhere. He always put it in the same spot, so I had given him permission to do as he pleased with his 'bag-tossing zone'. Thanks to that, the room always looked the same.

Jeong Seongbin and Park Joowoo's room needed no work. It was perfect. If anything, I had to be careful not to disrupt its order. I aired it out, even setting a timer for the fifteen-minute duration Park Joowoo prefers. I grabbed a new deodorizer from the storage closet and replaced the old one in their wardrobe. With that, my unauthorized visit to the main vocalists' room was complete.

*Hold on a moment. If Park Joowoo were left alone for a day, he would definitely deep-clean the entire dorm. So, is it okay for me to steal my younger member's 'Sparkling Clean' content?*

That was the most useless worry in the world. A dorm with only Park Joowoo would look like a professional disinfection company had swept through. Please do not compare me, just fiddling with a dry mop, to a human sterilizer.

"This room is the problem. This room."

I clicked my tongue and headed for the youngest members' room. It felt chaotic even before I opened the door. A doormat covered in brightly smiling, rainbow-colored flowers assaulted my vision, as if asking whether I was truly prepared to enter.

*There are cameras in every room, so they must have expected me to come in.*

The other guys must have known about today's shoot in advance. Trusting they had put away anything that should not be seen, I opened the door.

Then again, I suddenly got annoyed.

*We're idols. Isn't it a problem to have things in the dorm that shouldn't be seen? If I find even one thing, I won't let them off the hook.*

With grim determination, I opened the door. The aura within immediately overwhelmed me, and I shut it again. I needed a deep breath.

"Is this a room or a pawn shop?"

I carefully pushed the door open again. A charming space that could have passed for a Hongdae knick-knack shop greeted me.

It had not been like this before. I had not been out of their room for months. Just yesterday, it was only a cluttered room, but some black magic had turned it into a puppy paradise. I was about to call it a total mess, but the cute dolls had done nothing wrong, so I softened my phrasing.

"Am I supposed to dust off the dolls? Why are they all out?"

Besides the dolls, there was no room to step. An old laptop covered in colorful stickers, an old-fashioned phone, and toys from fan signs cluttered the space.

*The electronics... ... are they from Lee Cheonghyeon's collection?*

As I inspected the scene more closely, I discovered a letter in a pink envelope among the clutter.

"Until the younger members get back... ... play with these guys... ...?"

That was ridiculous. Was I really at a point in my career where I should be playing house with dolls?

Still, out of respect for their sincere effort to help me get screen time, I decided to hold an audition for my 'housemates for a day'. I selected one cute Mini creature, small enough to sit on the dining table and made of a material that would not get dirty easily. I also picked a feature phone, though I had no idea where they got it or why they kept it. I returned the other 'friends' to their original spots as best I could remember. My mission, after all, was just to dust the floor.

It was a Manggaetteok Mini creature. I gave it the temporary name Manggae. For Manggae and for Ponpon, the feature phone, I also brought an imported friend from my room. I introduced the healthiest-looking one from the 'Tired Cat' series I received as a gift while traveling.

"You three get along while this ahjussi cleans, alright?"

I was about to line them up on the sunny windowsill but worried Kang Kiyeon's Mini creature might fade. I placed them in the shade instead. After I finished dusting, I took them to the living room and arranged them in a row on the table.

After the long cleaning session, all I had were three silent, thumb-sized friends, five dust-covered mop sheets, and a clean dorm.

The chore was over quickly. Had I prepared a segment in advance, it would have been different, but being thrown in front of the camera without warning left my body moving like a machine while my mind was in chaos.

"Is no one coming back, even for lunch?"

On top of that, I had to voice every single thought. During a live broadcast, at least the Sparklers are there to watch. This, however, was my own one-man show with no writer, no PD-nim, and no audience. It was incredibly awkward for someone like me, who had never done solo promotions and filmed most content with the other members.

Even if I was the star, having only one person on screen could be boring. For a little fun, I decided to call Jeong Seongbin.

[Hyung! Are you having fun?]

"I'm so bored. I have to eat alone, right?"

[It's a chance to eat whatever you want!]

Jeong Seongbin's voice was relentlessly cheerful. It was a little sad that he did not understand my plight.

If I told them how I ate jjajangmyeon alone the day I moved into my studio apartment and how awful it tasted, maybe one of them would return out of pity. Or maybe not pity, but annoyance, just to chew me out. I did not want to get scolded on camera, so I held my tongue.

"What are you going to eat?"

[Poke with Joowoo.]

"So you're saying you're going to eat lunch with a friend while leaving me alone?"

[Don't you have friends at the dorm? Look carefully. Cheonghyeon and Kiyeon said they made thorough preparations.]

*Would Manggae and Ponpon eat poke with me?*

The call with our leader ended, leaving me with only emotional scars. In an instant, I was alone again.

The refrigerator held plenty of ingredients to make a meal. Once our comeback started, we would be too busy to eat at the dorm anyway. Aiming to clear out the fridge, I scraped together all the awkwardly leftover items. I laid out vegetables, eggs, low-sodium ham, and the ketchup Lee Cheonghyeon bought overseas. A meal idea came to mind.

"I should make some omurice."

As I was about to get rice from the veranda, I made eye contact with someone. Next to Manggae and Ponpon, the cat figurine holding a subway strap looked back at me, as if on its way home from work.

I put the rice cooker down and moved my precious friends-for-a-day to the dining table.

"You can watch me cook from here."

An innovative cooking show, where I was the only one wearing a microphone, was about to begin.
