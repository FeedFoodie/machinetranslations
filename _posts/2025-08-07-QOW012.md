That night, a noisy drinking party erupted in the dining hall adjacent to the training room. It was a simple affair with no fancy lights, decorations, or beautiful ladies, but for the first time in a while, the knights were having a joyful and spirited time. The tables groaned under the weight of whole roasted pork and high-quality wine.

"Hahahaha! And that's when the commander lifted the barbell one more time!"

"Whoa, now that was a sight."

"But that blacksmith, he's something else, isn't he?"

"Heard he's got dwarf blood in him."

"That'd explain the strength. How old do you think he is? If he's a half-dwarf, he'd age slower than us."

"Eighty this year, I overheard."

"What? Then it hardly feels like a real win, does it?"

"What're you on about? A win's a win!"

The knights chattered on, their conversation buzzing with the day's events. Many marveled at Dolan's incredible power, astonished that an ordinary blacksmith could possess strength comparable to their commander. Several knights even declared they wanted to compete against Dolan next.

But for all the talk of Dolan, the true subject of the night was Estella.

"The young lady seems to have really made up her mind this time, doesn't she?"

"I'll say. I never knew she had it in her."

"She looked a lot thinner today, too."

"She's out there every day, isn't she? This morning, she ran ten laps around the training grounds without a single stop."

"It's a huge improvement. She's really different this time."

The image they once held of her, the pathetic eldest daughter always in Catherine's shadow, was beginning to crumble. The ugly recluse who shunned everyone and never socialized, the vain woman who cared only for luxury goods while ignoring the state of the domain, all of it seemed to be a ghost of the past after seeing her today.

"Anyway, I saw Lady Estella in a whole new light today."

"Me too!"

"Did you see her lift that dumbbell with one hand? It must've been pretty heavy for a woman."

"She even showed me the right way to lift it. When I tried it her way, I could really feel the muscles right here tighten up."

Once one person broke the ice, the others were all eager to share a word or two about the exercise techniques they had learned from Estella.

"But where did she learn all that?"

"I asked Dolan. He said the young lady designed all of this equipment herself."

"Was she always this brilliant?"

"You didn't know? They used to call her a prodigy when she was young. She just suddenly started gaining weight one day and became like this."

"Really? Well, it's a relief she's getting back to her old self."

The knights' cheerful conversation carried on late into the night. The marquis, well aware of how exhausted they were from the successive monster appearances, provided them with additional wine and meat, and the dining hall once again filled with their joyous cheers.

That night marked a dramatic shift in the knights' attitude toward Estella. Many who supported Catherine still did not look upon her favorably, but no one dared to mock her openly anymore.

Deceive the heavens to cross the sea, borrow a knife to kill another, lure the tiger down the mountain, feint in the east and attack in the west, the best stratagem is to flee.

A controlled diet, the consumption of Miraclium, and consistent exercise worked in concert, and Estella rapidly began to recover her original physical condition. While still on the heavier side, the breathtaking clumsiness of her past was gone.

Estella opened the physical training room for the knights to use freely. Gradually, the number of knights using it before and after their swordsmanship practice increased. Even the initial skeptics, hearing the glowing reviews from their comrades, began to think, *Should I give it a try?*

But as the room's popularity surged, getting a machine became as difficult as plucking a star from the sky, especially during the hours Estella was there herself. Part of it was her gradually returning beauty as she lost weight, but the main draw was that learning directly from her was far more effective than fumbling through it alone.

Today, Estella was on a machine of her own design, a re-creation of a rowing machine adapted for the era. She secured her feet on the footplates, leaned back, and pulled the handle with all her might. With each pull, an iron weight attached to a small waterwheel would rise. The resistance could be adjusted by the number of iron weights.

At first, the knights had stared at the strangely wheeled contraption, wondering how to use it. Now, they watched in admiration as Estella worked, sweat dripping from her brow.

"That exercise must build strength throughout the entire body."

"You can even adjust the weight of the iron plates, so it must be very effective for building strength."

"To think she created something like that."

Focused on her workout, Estella was deaf to their murmurs of praise. She was simply thrilled to feel her body fat burning away day by day, proud of the firmness returning to her muscles. She barely had any clothes left in her dressing room that fit.

*How much time has passed?*

After a long session, she finally stood, wiping the sweat from her neck with a draped towel. The workout clothes Claire had made were so comfortable that wearing a dress now felt like a chore.

The moment she stood, the knights who had been waiting nearby approached her cautiously.

"I'm done. Anyone who wants to use it can go ahead. Oh, and I asked Dolan to make one more of each machine. They should be here around next week."

"WOOOOAH!"

"Thank you, my lady!"

It was exactly what they had been thinking, that the number of machines was just a little insufficient.

Estella beamed, a proud smile on her face as she watched them rejoice as if they had received a grand gift. When Ria, who was on standby, handed her a waterskin filled with cool Miraclium, Estella opened the lid and chugged it down. It was a sight unbecoming of a noble lady, yet no one looked at her with disdain. In fact, a growing number of knights were finding this side of her rather cool.

BWOOO BWOOO BWOOOOO!

The sound of a distant horn instantly blanketed the room, which had been filled with laughter and cheers, in a taut tension.

A monster had appeared.

BWOOOO BWOOOOOO BWOOOO.

The sharp notes cut through the friendly atmosphere. The knights, sweating from their exercises, all stopped what they were doing, their heads snapping up like meerkats.

"What's going on?"

"It's a monster," a knight answered Estella, his voice tense as he read the signal. "Seems to have appeared in the nearby forest." He ran outside without another word.

In less than five minutes, every knight from the training room had rushed out, armed themselves, and fallen into formation on the training grounds. It was a testament to the diligence of their usual training.

*A monster?*

She had received countless disaster alerts in her life for heatwaves, heavy rain, blizzards, earthquakes, and even fine dust, but never one so intense. Her heart pounded. A tension that was different from fear tightened its grip on her chest.

*To think I'd live to see a disaster alert for a monster appearance.*

The coexistence of her own memories with those of the original Estella Cardein was likely why she was not completely flustered. After a moment's thought, Estella tidied herself up and ran straight for the training grounds.

Outside, the air was chaotic with the sound of the horn, which a signal officer blew continuously in varying lengths and pitches. It was a serious matter that a monster had appeared so close, but the sight of the knights lined up in perfect rows and columns melted her worries away.

The marquis, Lucius, looked down upon the assembled knights with a stern expression. His voice thundered across the training grounds where a hundred knights stood ready.

"Every time we are victorious, countless lives will be saved. We cannot surrender our families to mere monsters! Do not let a single one escape, and slaughter them to the last!"

{sep}

Estella watched the knights ride out at full speed, kicking up clouds of dust. "It seems a monster has appeared nearby," she muttered with worry.

"I think it's the forest right outside the castle walls," Ria said.

"How do you know that?" Estella asked, very curious. All she had heard was the irregular blast of the horn.

"I started to understand the meaning after hearing it so often," Ria replied as if it were nothing remarkable.

"What? You understand the horn signals?"

It was like understanding Morse code, and this was coming from a maid who had never received a proper education, let alone any formal training, a girl who could not even read.
