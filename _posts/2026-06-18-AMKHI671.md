『Iwol: I'm last, right? Is Joowoo going to read my questions?』

『Joowoo: I will. Let's start with a light question. Q. Are you currently reading any webtoons or novels, and what are your favorite genres?』

『Iwol: I first got into webnovels as a trainee and started reading manga after our debut. I don't think I follow them like a hobby yet. I used to read mostly self-help or theory books, but lately, I've been borrowing and reading the books Cheonghyeon bought one by one!』

『Seongbin: Could you introduce one book you read a long time ago and one you've read recently?』

『Iwol: The one that comes to mind right now is Georges Sorel's 『Reflections on Violence』...? It's a book that really makes you think. As for Cheonghyeon's books, I found the 『The Rainbow Fish』

series interesting.』

『Seongbin: You mean the big picture book, right?』

『Iwol: Yeah, the one with the sparkly scales. Cheonghyeon's reading level is so high that the only thing I could handle was the children's book.』

『Joowoo: I saw that one too. The cover was cute...』

『Joowoo: Question number two. Q. Between Senior Yuur, Mr. Mo Yunguk, and Seongbin when he's smiling and speaking firmly, who do you think is the scariest?』

『Iwol: Isn't it obviously Seongbin?』

『Seongbin: Hyung?』

『Iwol: There's a premise, isn't there? 'Smiling and speaking firmly'. That's an emergency. It means I've already caused trouble.』

『Joowoo: But what if senior Yuur sends you a message saying, 'Iwol, let's meet in the conference room for a minute'...?』

『Iwol: ...』

『Joowoo: What if Mr. Mo Yunguk summons you...?』

『Iwol: ...』

『Iwol: Still, Seongbin is the scariest.』

『Seongbin: What?』

『Joowoo: Before Seongbin gets truly scary, I'll move on to the next question...』

『Seongbin: Wait a minute.』

『Joowoo: Q. There are many anecdotes about you lying down in front of the members or staff. We want to know in detail how you lie down.』

『Joowoo: ...』

『Joowoo: Did I just add fuel to the fire with Seongbin?』

『Seongbin: Joowoo...!』

『Iwol: I'll demonstrate this myself. First, you stand tall in the center of a wide space to show your strong will. With a grim expression, you make eye contact with the person you're trying to persuade, hinting that they're about to witness something terrible. Then you sit on the floor, stretch out your legs, and...』

『Seongbin: For the sake of our idol dignity! Let's stop here!』

『Iwol: After you press your back flat against the floor! You slam your heels and both arms down hard on the ground!』

『Seongbin: Please turn off the cameras for a moment!』

『Joowoo: Give it up, Seongbin...』

『Joowoo: We'll take a short break so Seongbin doesn't explode... Q. Hyung, do you plan to continue composing and acting?』

『Iwol: I'm studying composition consistently, but I don't have any plans for acting. I want to focus on spArk's activities this year! If I were to continue acting, I think I'd need more acting classes. I still have a lot to improve on.』

『Seongbin: Is there any role you'd like to try?』

『Iwol: I've already played various roles, so I don't think there's a specific part I'm coveting. We've filmed so many skits for our own content, too. I've even played a department head. Still, if I had to choose one, I wonder what it would be like to play a timid or introverted character, the complete opposite of my personality. I think it would be different.』

『Joowoo: A timid and introverted hyung...?』

『Seongbin: I can't imagine it!』

『Iwol: Right? Me neither.』

『Joowoo: This is another work-related question. Q. How do you determine if a sudden idea isn't just late-night sentimentality, but a genuinely good idea?』

『Iwol: I check it in two ways! First, I organize the idea and then read it during the day. Before bed, when I wake up, and while I'm working, I constantly check in my head if the idea is good. When I look at what I've written, I get a sense of whether it's a concept that can be developed, or if it's better to switch to a better one. Then I show the filtered material to the members or the company and ask for their opinion as a second check!』

『Seongbin: There hasn't been a single case where we said it was bad. That's probably because you spend a lot of time on planning and initial review, hyung.』

『Joowoo: Lastly, here's a heartwarming question. Q. If you were to express your current happiness in one line?』

『Iwol: That's a philosophical question. I'm not great at these, but, hmm... I'll say, 'I find myself waiting for tomorrow every day.' When a day is so enjoyable, you get excited about what might happen the next day.』

『Seongbin: That's a wonderful expression.』

『Iwol: But if you, Seongbin, call my name while smiling firmly, I probably wouldn't be waiting for tomorrow every day.』

『Seongbin: Yes, thank you for your hard work answering the questions!』

『Joowoo: Pfft...』

『Iwol: That's it for the individual questions, but there are group questions, right?』

『Seongbin: Yes. We received questions for all of spArk, so we thought we'd answer those as well!』

『Joowoo: Should we go in reverse order this time...?』

『Seongbin: Sounds good!』

『Joowoo: This is a question for Iwol. Q. Do you have any plans to film a daily vlog when the members travel or go to their family homes during a break?』

『Iwol: It might be difficult at our family homes since our families are non-celebrities, but I'd love to film one when we go on a trip! Not just short clips, but a full, long one. To do that, it would be better to go somewhere with few tourists, right? I think we could get a lot of fun scenes even if we just get a place and hang out inside by ourselves.』

『Joowoo: Even with just the six of us, all sorts of things happen. Really...』

『Seongbin: It's quite eventful. There are many scenes we were sad we couldn't show the fans, so let's definitely try filming one next time.』

『Seongbin: I guess it's my turn to ask Joowoo a question? Q. If you had to pick one funny incident from the group chat recently?』

『Joowoo: This one... there's something I've been dying to say. I really wanted to tell Sparkler about this...』

『Iwol: What is it?』

『Joowoo: You know how we always check the group chat to see who's coming home last? Even if we share our schedules on a calendar, things can happen and cause delays. As a family, we need to keep track of when everyone gets home.』

『Seongbin: That's right.』

『Joowoo: I think it was two days ago. It was my day to come home late, and Iwol asked, 'Who's coming home late today?'』

『Iwol: Ah.』

『Joowoo: In response, Jeho kept typing 'Buu' 'Buu'... and Iwol got angry, asking who he thought was there to watch him practice his aegyo...』

『(Leader Seongbin can't lift his head)』

『(The short Iwol, lost in thought)』

『Iwol: It seems 'ㅈ' and 'ㅂ' are next to each other, so it was a typo. Jeho sent it with great effort despite his bad eyesight... I was being thoughtless. I apologized. Still, I wonder why he, so unlike him, just called him by his first name without adding the family name.』

『Seongbin: You even gave Jeho a hard time about that!』

『Iwol: I'm just saying that fellow had his share of the blame.』

『Joowoo: Things like this happen every day...』

『Iwol: I'm about to ask Seongbin a question, but before that, there was a request for our leader.』

『Seongbin: A request? Ah, the ring sizes? Since you asked me to bring them as the leader, I've measured based on our ring fingers. You were a size 20, right, hyung?』

『Iwol: Yeah.』

『Seongbin: Jeho's is the thickest at size 22.』

『Iwol: Why are his fingers so thick? Is it because of his knuckles?』

『Seongbin: Yes. So there's a little space left after he puts it on. Joowoo is a size 20, I'm a perfect size 18, Cheonghyeon is a 16, and Kiyeon is a 15.』

『Joowoo: Cheonghyeon has big hands, but his fingers are slender...』

『Iwol: Now that we've submitted Sparkler's homework, shall we move on to the question? Q. As the leader, what behaviors have you seen from the members that make you want to take care of them?』

『Seongbin: I think everyone has at least one. For Iwol, it's him sleeping late. It's gotten better recently, but you used to go to bed very late.』

『Iwol: That's why you scolded me so much.』

『Seongbin: Jeho keeps falling asleep on the sofa with his glasses on. So sometimes I take them off for him...』

『Joowoo: I take them off for him sometimes too. I'm worried the frames will get bent...』

『Seongbin: For our Joowoo, it's when he stays on the veranda for a long time.』

『Joowoo: Huh?』

『Seongbin: You do that when you have a lot on your mind. So I don't make it obvious at the time, but when you come out from the veranda or enter your room, I'll ask, 'How are you feeling?'』

『Joowoo: So that's what it was... I was wondering how you always managed to talk to me whenever I was feeling down.』

『Seongbin: We're friends, so of course I know that much. For Cheonghyeon, I try to take extra care of him right before he finishes working on a song. In Kiyeon's case... you know how we sometimes go to the hospital for ankle or joint management, even if we're not sick? He feels really sorry about missing practice then, so I try to talk to him more often on purpose to lighten the mood and make sure he doesn't feel burdened.』

『Iwol: This is why I ask, how would this household even function without our leader?』

『Seongbin: Haha, you say that, but I know you'd all do your own jobs just fine!』

『Iwol: This is the last common question. I'll ask this on behalf of everyone. Q. To me, what are spArk and Sparkler?』

『Seongbin: To me, spArk is... my family to feed.』

『Iwol: So reliable.』

『Seongbin: I have this thought that I have to take responsibility for them no matter what, and Sparkler is my first love. I believe any singer who has met their fans would understand!』

『Joowoo: To me... spArk is my family. Truly, and Sparkler are friends I'll have for life.』

『Iwol: All the good expressions have been used, so I feel pressured.』

『Seongbin: You've left behind so many famous quotes, hyung.』

『Iwol: I'll say spArk are my creditors. The trust the kids have shown me is so great. I think it will take a lot of time to repay that trust.』

『Iwol: And Sparkler is my destination. Because they always allow me to come to this place. As long as Sparkler is there, I will definitely return to spArk. So please, always be with us.』

『Iwol: This concludes the Vocals' Q&A! Sparkler, did you enjoy it?』

『Seongbin: The Q&A with Jeho and the youngest members is also prepared. So please enjoy it to your heart's content!』

『Joowoo: Then we'll be going now, bye...!』
