The apartment complex that served as my destination was famous for housing quite a few celebrities. Manager Daeyeon, who gave me a ride, or rather, Daeyeon-hyung, set the navigation and asked whether Mr. Gu Jahan had moved.

Stepping out of the elevator with both hands full, I rang the doorbell. Mr. Han Gawoon greeted me in comfortable clothes.

"Pardon the intrusion."

"Welcome. As for the boxes... you can just put them anywhere."

"They're not my luggage. They're housewarming gifts."

"Hmm?"

Mr. Han Gawoon wore a completely bewildered expression. He looked so gentle and mild.

Seeing him make a face like that made me feel like he would be eaten alive in the harsh world out there. Up close, a genuinely warm, handsome guy like him looked like a creature from another dimension.

"You invited me over. I couldn't come empty-handed!"

"To call it an invitation, the place is a bit of a mess."

Mr. Han Gawoon's reply was no exaggeration. Moving boxes packed the living room, and most of them remained unopened.

"I had to move in a hurry."

Mr. Han Gawoon originally lived in the group dorms. As the rift with MYTH deepened, however, he must have had to look for a new place to stay.

Was it a relief that despite suddenly becoming homeless, he possessed the financial means to move straight into an apartment like this? It looked visibly better than the spArk dorms.

*If the guys ever end their dorm life and move out on their own, they can easily get a place like this each.*

Since none of them seemed to have any intention of leaving anytime soon, however, imagining it was meaningless.

I carefully placed the housewarming gifts I ordered online next to the sofa so they would not be in the way.

The apartment remained in a completely raw state. The sofa had not even been unpacked from its plastic, the wall clock rested on the floor because there was nowhere to hang it, and clothes piled like a mountain inside huge plastic bags.

The only organized area was the kitchen. In contrast to the desolate living room, which resembled an abandoned city, the kitchen overflowed with signs of life. Delivery food packed the kitchen island.

"Is anyone else coming besides me?" I asked just in case.

What if it turned out to be me against Parthe, a one-versus-eight fight?

While I felt thoroughly tense, Mr. Han Gawoon spoke in a subdued voice.

"No. It's just that it's my first time inviting someone over..."

Mr. Han Gawoon seemed a bit embarrassed. I tried my best to comfort him by saying that living in a dorm rarely gave anyone a reason to invite guests over.

"I didn't want to treat you stingily, but it ended up like this while trying to prepare."

"It's more than generous! Where else would I get a spread like this?"

"I saw that etiquette says to serve things on plates, but mine haven't been delivered yet. I'm sorry about that."

Stingy or not, so much food covered the island that I could have asked to pack the leftovers. Instead of making useless remarks, the good junior Kim Iwol cheerfully sat on a chair and mentioned that the sushi looked truly delicious.

I did not directly ask why he called me here. I did not want to needlessly agitate a person who was already distracted and troubled.

Instead, I ate the food appreciatively. I praised him and said he really knew how to pick good restaurants. To prove my words were genuine, while Mr. Han Gawoon ate a single roll of sushi, I cleared out sushi, rice noodles, and pork cutlet all by myself.

"Did you stick to a strict diet during promotions?"

"Yes, but now I'm free for a while. How strictly do you usually manage your diet, sunbae-nim?"

"I haven't done it in a while. I don't really gain weight. My constitution wasn't originally like that, though..."

Mr. Han Gawoon ran both hands through his hair. Beneath his hands, a bitter smile showed.

"It just turns out that way when I get sensitive."

"......"

"Now that I'm on my own, I realize it. I think my mind was uncomfortable all this time. I lived without really noticing it."

Changes in appetite due to stress are a common symptom among modern people.

The only difference is whether one overeats or loses the desire to eat altogether. Mr. Han Gawoon appeared to be the latter.

*It is just tragic that his mind finally relaxing and allowing him to put food in his mouth results in a single roll of sushi.*

Mr. Han Gawoon did not seem to need sympathy or comfort, so I quietly waited for his next words.

Mr. Han Gawoon moistened his throat with steaming warm water and continued.

"Our side seems like it will be settled somehow."

"Settled somehow..."

"The team will disband. Since there's no one with aligned goals, I don't think we'll move to another company to carry on our previous legacy. Everyone will probably scatter."

"Are you ending group promotions altogether?"

Quite a few idols resume activities elsewhere even after fighting with their agency. Parthe, however, did not seem to have any intention of continuing entertainment activities under a different name or agency.

"The older members agreed to cooperate under that condition. Even if we had decided to move to another agency, it wouldn't have been smooth sailing, but more importantly, no member wanted to keep the team together like that anyway. So our goal was set to an amicable contract termination with MYTH. That also marks the end of Parthe's promotions."

"Don't tell me the person who proposed that condition was..."

"Can't you guess?"

Mr. Han Gawoon offered a resigned smile.

*Are you saying Song Minil stepped forward and said everyone should go their own way once the contract issue is settled?*

It was a behind-the-scenes story that felt suffocating just to hear. Since they had held a joint press conference, I thought they had shown a one-team spirit for once, but I was mistaken.

They had merely wrapped members who were still individually fragmented with something like the plastic band around a slice of cake.

I thought after overcoming a crisis together, they would have a healthy discussion about what to do next. Who could have imagined they would deliberately pull off a 'it was awful working together, let's never meet again'?

Besides, should words like that not come out of Mr. Han Gawoon's mouth at the very least?

Parthe was not entirely a victim in their relationship with MYTH. While they suffered because of the company, they also enjoyed conveniences backed by it. In that process, the biggest problem Parthe committed was probably 'not thinking for themselves'.

Parthe did not ponder whether they were acting wrong or being used. Even as they became adults and their influence grew, they did not mature mentally. They never realized they had become adults responsible for their own decisions.

The world is not easy. Unless you try to protect your own life, no one guarantees your stability. Nevertheless, drunk on short-term success, Parthe acted indifferently whether the company committed atrocities or not.

Because that attitude became a habit, they chose to handle things with minimal effort even when they became a candle flickering in the wind.

The price of being soaked in laziness will return as the punishment of fans turning their backs on them forever. Fans do not love idols who abandon their team.

They will only realize how delusionary it was to think they could live in luxury like before without the title of Parthe after nothing can be reversed.

"You look surprised."

"Yes, it's unexpected."

"I understand. spArk didn't seem like that. The atmosphere is quite different, isn't it?"

*It certainly is.*

When Jeong Seongbin suggested filing a lawsuit against UA, he spoke on the premise that we would naturally move together in the future as well. If we were currently faced with a conflict with UtopiA and had to leave the company, the six of us would find a way to continue activities somehow.

What was natural for us, however, did not seem to be natural for them.

You could not do anything with people whose motivation had completely died like that. Inspiring drive internally must have been out of the question.

I could only feel that Mr. Han Gawoon was impressive for conceding what was needed in such dire circumstances to achieve even this much unity.

*Even though he himself doesn't seem to want to quit singing altogether.*

If he did not care about what happened to his throat, he would not intentionally make sure to drink warm water in weather that was not particularly cold, yet every time the steam rising from his cup subsided, Mr. Han Gawoon kept filling it with warm water from the purifier.

Perhaps organizing the kitchen was not just to entertain a guest, but to establish an environment where he could drink warm water.

"I'm thankful they cooperated even this much. I didn't expect anything. As long as we reach the conclusion that not everything that happened was Parthe's fault, I'm satisfied."

"You must have gone through a lot of trouble persuading them."

"Can that even be called persuading...!"

"Pardon?"

Just what kind of terrifying thing had he done? I asked whether he had done something that should not be reported to the media, but Mr. Han Gawoon merely smiled.

Then, saying he had also ordered fruit, he handed me a fruit box fresh from the refrigerator as I finished my meal. Peeling off the inner skin of a pre-trimmed orange, Mr. Han Gawoon spoke.

"There was something else I wanted to talk about today."

"......"

"If possible, I wanted to call the other members... at least Minil-hyung, and I thought I should, but it didn't work out. I'm sorry about that in advance."

The moment I heard his preamble tinged with regret, I knew what Mr. Han Gawoon was trying to say. I replied with a smile.

"It's not something you should apologize for, sunbae-nim."

"No. Even before the members not being able to come..."

"Yes, I mean even including those 'before' things, there's no need for you to apologize, sunbae-nim."

Mr. Han Gawoon's expression turned subtle. He looked like he could not quite grasp what I wanted to say.

"Did you look down on us when we greeted you, sunbae-nim? Did you ever demean me to ruin my image, or exploit my circumstances? So why should you apologize on their behalf just because the actual person didn't come? Just because you're in the same team as Minil sunbae-nim?"

"......"

Mr. Han Gawoon averted his eyes. I swallowed down a bitter sigh that threatened to escape.

*I know this will happen.*

I vaguely anticipated that he would want to apologize for the damage spArk or I suffered.

Was there any need, however, for someone who did nothing wrong to apologize on behalf of others? It might be different if Song Minil would feel grateful to Mr. Han Gawoon for this. Since there was zero chance of that, I did not want to see Mr. Han Gawoon bow his head.

"If there's something you really want to say to me, sunbae-nim, I hope it's a thank you. I dare say I can claim a bit of stake in this matter."

"That goes without saying..."

"However, even with this, I only gave a slight push. You did everything, sunbae-nim. Deciding to leave the company, gathering members who were hard to convince, and bringing them to the public sphere, it's all your credit. I only want praise for my contribution."

In the process of healing large and small wounds, I had received enough apologies. Now that I knew scars caused by people could fade, even if I encountered situations where I did not get to hear words of reflection in the future, I could move past them just fine.

Rather than that, I preferred hearing something good.

"This is a secret, but if I do something that pricks my conscience or get stressed for various reasons, my body falls ill. My nose starts bleeding like crazy. Maybe that's why I don't want to hear grand expressions of gratitude when I didn't do much."

"......"

"Later, when everything is wrapped up well, invite me over again then. Just tell me a word like, 'Thanks to you, I didn't have to take all the blame alone and managed to resolve it somehow.' I'll buy the next meal. I ate so much today that I might have to buy twice, though."

Mr. Han Gawoon did not answer.

I asked, looking toward the living room.

"When are you going to unpack?"

"The boxes?"

"When are you going to organize all this alone? If you're going to unpack, let's open them together. If you bought this place, well... I'll even hammer some nails into the wall for you. So you can hang the clock."

Mr. Han Gawoon's eyes widened. He looked at me, wordlessly asking why I would help with that.

For someone like me whose skin had grown thick as steel plates through years of experience as an office worker and an idol, a look like that had zero effect.

"I have to earn my meal. I'm not the type to eat and run. Instead, if it gets late, order jjajangmyeon for me."

"Jjajangmyeon?"

"I'm telling you this in case you didn't know, sunbae-nim, but on moving days, eating jjajangmyeon is an absolute requirement."

Only then did Mr. Han Gawoon sluggishly get up from his seat. He found two box cutters and handed one to me.

Soon, the wall clock, which a fan had custom-made and gifted to match Parthe's album concept, hung in a prominent spot in the living room.
