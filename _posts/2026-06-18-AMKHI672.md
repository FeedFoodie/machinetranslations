『Cheonghyeon: I record melodies from inspiration as they come, but I fundamentally build the foundation from the project proposal and references. 『Kiyeon: There are also times when you expand on a melody you've already made, right?』

『Cheonghyeon: Yup, but only when it matches the plan and tone. Since we're creating a work for the team, the image the group needs to show comes first. It has to.』

『Kiyeon: I'll move on to the next question. What did you think when you first saw Iwol's contact name?』
