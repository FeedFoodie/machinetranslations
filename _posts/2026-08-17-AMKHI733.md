*That last live was really good.*

Won Chaehee loved content where they all wore pajamas and had a sincere conversation in a comfortable atmosphere. Once you reached an age where just watching a noisy video drained your energy, a tension level suitable for a meal companion was just right.

As an idol fan who lived and died by group dynamics, nothing was more interesting than hearing members praise another member behind his back.

Since Won Chaehee could not watch it at the scheduled time, she turned on the replay late at night and brought out convenience store popcorn. Chewing the salty and sweet popcorn dusted with seaweed powder, she enjoyed tidbits even sweeter than the popcorn itself.

From the moment Lee Cheonghyeon threatened to turn off the live stream, saying he was going to praise Kim Iwol, she smiled fondly, her cheekbones aching.

『Iwol-hyung, turn off the broadcast.』

『Hehe… !』

『What're you trying to say?』

Like Park Joowoo, Won Chaehee buried her face in a large cushion and watched the members' Kim Iwol praise show.

『I ran into Chaejun-hyung at the company a while ago, right? The moment he saw my face, he was like, "Hey, Cheonghyeon-ah. Why does Iwol get so talkative whenever you guys come up?" That's what he said!』

『Uh… I can just imagine what it was like without even seeing it.』

『Me too.』

Won Chaehee could also roughly picture it. Kim Iwol often talked about his members at fan signs, during pre-recording rehearsals, or on BubblePop.

While he only talked about his colleagues in their early debut days, he now endlessly shared stories about his members even while talking about himself.

It seemed Lee Cheonghyeon had decided to call the Hellas members 'hyung' anyway. Kim Iwol's form of address remained fixed on 'sunbaenim'.

Seeing Yuur or Polo speak casually to Kim Iwol in recent videos, it appeared that even though the seniors and juniors had gotten closer, the Confucianism-steeped Kim Iwol still respectfully addressed his seniors.

『Among Iwol-hyung's famous quotes, the ones related to visuals, self-management, and attitude get a lot of the spotlight, right?』

『Yeah.』

『So people who only know him superficially or have seen him in passing on variety shows often feel that 'Kim Iwol is very particular about his professional side, manners, and how he appears to others'. Because that side of him is shown often, but we know that hyung reads our… very deep inner thoughts. I hope more people get to know that side of him.』

『So on the outside, it seems like hyung pays a lot of attention to external factors, but… in reality, his personality is one that looks into and takes care of one's inner self much more?』

『As expected of Joowoo-hyung. You immediately catch what I'm trying to say.』

Lee Cheonghyeon's comment was so good that Won Chaehee nearly teared up in the middle of the night. That was right, Kim Iwol was not just the visuals patrol.

If Kim Iwol's head was truly filled with nothing but faces, he would not have spent a full ten minutes in front of Hellas talking about how sincere Lee Cheonghyeon was about the team. She only knew because Polo had shared the story on the radio.

『He's a hyung who recognizes the strengths and hearts of others. That's why I like Iwol-hyung.』

Won Chaehee immediately understood why Kim Iwol's biggest fans were soaking their tissues while watching the live in real-time. She felt filled with emotion.

Choi Jeho praised Kim Iwol's drive. He explained that it was a little different from being stubborn.

『I told Kang Kiyeon this once, but he doesn't compromise. If he thinks something has to be done, he does it no matter what.』

『Iwol-hyung is kind of like a bulldozer.』

『The fact that I get it right away means it's an accurate description…』

Park Joowoo took his time to think. After much deliberation, he praised Kim Iwol's 'gentleness'.

『That's the image that comes to mind when I think of Iwol-hyung. The way he waits for you to grow, no matter how long it takes, gives you his trust until the end, and when the results come out, he gives all the credit to the person who put in the effort… I think it might be because hyung is gentle.』

『He's so generous to others. Right, Joowoo-hyung?』

『Because Iwol-hyung is affectionate.』

The smiling face of Park Joowoo, the scene of the three lying side-by-side under a blanket, the way they gathered for a selfie, and the three-person selfie uploaded to the official SNS account were all perfect. She had sent them such unsparing applause just last week.

And then, the long-awaited today arrived.

Tonight, the trio of little flames who will help you get a good night's sleep are coming to see Sparkler!

The stars of the praise from the last live stream announced a three-person broadcast. Attached to the notice was a photo with handwritten messages from each member.

We'll be waiting for bedtime! See you later!

Let's lie down together and talk comfortably. See you soon.

Each message revealed their individual personalities. She could feel a difference in temperature just from the text of the refreshing leader and the mature youngest member.

Her bias, the eldest in today's broadcast and the eldest in spArk even when it was not a three-person broadcast, did not disappoint his fan's expectations this time either.

I know you won't fall asleep right away just because you lie down. Let's get ready for bed together starting at 9!

Even the three stars drawn next to the exclamation mark made her suspect they were matched to the number of participants. Since the stars were in order of size, was it not worth being suspicious?

The announcement even called them 'little flames' because they were the 'short line', so it did not seem impossible. Out of consideration for Kang Kiyeon, she did not post her theory anywhere.

The moment she read the notice, Won Chaehee's heart clocked out for the day. After her body arrived home, no one could stop Won Chaehee. She changed into her pajamas and dived straight into bed.

『Sparkler, hello!』

After waiting for nothing but the alarm, Kim Iwol's face was worth the excited wait. His neck and hands, whiter than his light gray pajamas, were dazzling.

*You invited us to sleep with you. How are your fans supposed to sleep when you show up looking like this?*

Every time Kim Iwol moved his head, his ash-gray hair fluttered. Despite his busy schedule, Kim Iwol had dyed his hair to match the monotone Kim Iwol from the music video as soon as the first week of music show promotions ended.

*His hair is going to break off.* While screaming that, Won Chaehee shed tears of joy internally.

Even though they wore the same pajama design in different colors, they exuded quite different atmospheres. Kim Iwol looked like a chaebol male lead from an OTT exclusive drama, while Jeong Seongbin looked like the potential love interest from a rom-com web drama.

Kang Kiyeon appeared remarkably gentle on screen. It was not just because he was sandwiched between his hyungs. He had never been outshone in terms of impression, even when all six members of spArk stood side by side.

After analyzing the cause for a while, Won Chaehee quickly found the reason with her years of observation skills.

*His sleeves are long, aren't they?*

Kang Kiyeon's pajama sleeves were unusually long, covering the back of his hands. She had never heard that Kang Kiyeon had short arms.

It seemed they had either intentionally given him a larger size or a different detailed pajama to create a distinction. His bangs, combined with the light color, transformed Kang Kiyeon into a very cozy and fluffy mood.

Jeong Seongbin asked, 『We're your sleeping buddies today. How do you like it, Sparkler?』 His soft voice and gentle tone were perfect for this time of night. It seemed like it would be incredibly easy to fall asleep.

The problem was that her mind snapped awake every time Kim Iwol smiled. With the addition of the silver team bracelet, the man looked like a plaster statue. Every single shadow that fell across his skin was breathtaking.

Moreover, there was the tone that came out whenever Kim Iwol put on his hosting voice. 『The previous batters came out as the 'tall line'. What do you think about this situation?』

The leisureliness that seeped through while he threw playful comments with an adult's smile. Won Chaehee really loved this voice.

That was why she wished Kim Iwol would just start a talk channel on Metube, but Kim Iwol rarely did any solo activities.

*The members are taking turns releasing solos, so he'll probably release something…*

The current active state was much better than the silence after the contract renewal. Won Chaehee resolved not to wish for more.

As a fan who had rejoiced at her idol's contract renewal only to see the group disband after releasing one album, she had become accustomed to putting up defense mechanisms.

『What can I do? The members' legs are so long that I became the shortest.』

『Don't worry. I won't leave you alone, Kiyeon. We'll be together as the 'short line'.』

『This feels even weirder.』

『Isn't that just your imagination? I like this combination!』

On one side, Kim Iwol, and on the other, Jeong Seongbin, ganged up on Kang Kiyeon in the middle. Kang Kiyeon's shoulders, which could never look narrow, seemed particularly shrunken today.

Lee Cheonghyeon had been so full of spirit next to his hyungs. Even within the same maknae line, it was this different depending on which hyung was around.

The fact that Kim Iwol was in the 'short line' gave her a lot to think about. A 183cm man was considered on the shorter side among his peers, and yet, as the team's eldest, his authority was solid, and he maintained his natural stamina and aura.

The fact that he was still a head or two taller when mixed in with staff and managers stimulated something in Won Chaehee. The high-resolution photo of Kim Iwol in a double coat that Jeong Seongbin had taken had been Won Chaehee's phone background since the day it was uploaded, which said it all.

『I'm sure Kiyeon felt the same, but I was also inwardly accepting the reality that I wouldn't be able to escape the 'short line'. I was already taller than my dad when I debuted.』

『Hyung, why're you lumping me in with you?』

『Kiyeon, did you think you could become part of the 'tall line' in our team?』

『…』

Kang Kiyeon shut his mouth at Jeong Seongbin's attack, which was filled with pure innocence. The damage seemed considerable. Kim Iwol chided Jeong Seongbin, asking why he was crushing Kiyeon's spirit.

『Still, I didn't know Iwol-hyung would end up on our side.』

『Honestly, I didn't expect it either.』

『Didn't Lee Cheonghyeon grow scarily fast?』

『He did, and Joowoo grew quietly. More than anything, who would have expected Choi Jeho to grow even taller?』

『Isn't Jeho-hyung going to hit 190cm next year?』

『Wow… Someone tie his legs up.』

The younger members burst into laughter at Kim Iwol, who sounded genuinely fed up. Won Chaehee imagined a future where Choi Jeho hit 190cm.

She was not too worried. If that happened, Kim Iwol would surely make everyone but Choi Jeho wear platform shoes to match their heights.

Thinking about it that way, she was not worried that Choi Jeho towering over everyone else would ruin the formation's balance. The talk was spicy from the very beginning.
