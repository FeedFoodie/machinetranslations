Kang Kiyeon chose 'his uncomplaining steadfastness in doing what needs to be done' as Choi Jeho's praise-worthy trait, while Jeong Seongbin picked 'his seriousness in listening to any concern.'

『I've almost never seen Jeho-hyung talk back when given a task. Even if he has complaints, he usually does what he's told first before complaining.』

『He's pretty calm. Though it's not like he never complains.』

『Jeho-hyung definitely has that trait.』

『While Jeho-hyung is the type to immediately act when an idea pops into his head, when he has to do something with others, he tends to wait for the other person to share their opinion first. He mostly accommodates them.』

『It's like that when he creates choreography with me too. If I ask, 'How about something like this?', he's the style to quietly get up and try dancing it himself.』

『The reason so many people misunderstand is because his image on 'Deserted Rest Island' was so strong. He was stirring everything up over there.』

『If anything, the one who's a man of action in group work is Iwol-hyung. He hands us a schedule and is already way ahead of us. He's even running.』

『Kiyeon is right. When Iwol-hyung hears about a problem, the words 'What should I do for you first?' or 'Should I do it like this?' come right out of his mouth. Then he grabs his phone before I can even stop him.』

『You can basically consider things handled the moment word reaches Iwol-hyung.』

Even in Won Chaehee's eyes, a steady, grounded person like Choi Jeho was rare. Choi Jeho did what he was told without holding back, nor did he bottle things up until someone had to ask why he stayed silent.

His great stamina also kept emotional drain from exhausting his energy.

*Kim Iwol is also considered a steady type by fans, but... among the members, it feels fifty-fifty, doesn't it?*

It seemed hard for Kim Iwol, who complained as if the world were ending whenever someone in the group wore an outfit that did not match their personal color, to be seen as 100% steady.

While he was reliable like a professional when working, Won Chaehee, who knew his passion stemmed from the premise that 'one slip-up could ruin spArk,' could see just how desperate Kim Iwol truly was.

『Talking about someone who isn't here definitely feels different. How about Joowoo?』 Kim Iwol asked.

Jeong Seongbin fell into deep thought.

『I want to give him a unique compliment, but I wonder what would be good...』

『He increased his barbell weight?』

『It's certainly a fresh topic for a compliment toward Joowoo-hyung.』

Listening to the conversation, Won Chaehee thought of Park Joowoo, who had recently grown into an adult man, not that he had seemed like a young boy before.

Until recently, Sparklers affectionately called Park Joowoo a Chinchilla. Slowly, Sparklers might need to prepare to change Park Joowoo's nickname to Capybara.

*Then again, Kim Iwol might veto Capybara, complaining that its eyes are too small compared to our Joowoo.*

Searching for capybaras on the internet, Won Chaehee became certain this furry animal would not pass Kim Iwol's inspection. A Chinchilla with revealed biceps would likely be the lowest compromise Kim Iwol could make.

『Actually, I was moved by Joowoo recently.』

『What was it?』 Jeong Seongbin showed interest in Kim Iwol's scoop.

『The jjolmyeon from the place we often order delivery from is a bit spicy, right? When there's no schedule right after promotions end, Cheonghyeon and Kiyeon sometimes eat spicy cup ramen, but Joowoo was never interested.』

『Because Joowoo can't eat spicy food.』

『Right? But do you remember the cheat day we had before this 『Alone』 comeback? I ordered jjolmyeon back then too, and Joowoo asked me if it was good.』

『You mean Joowoo-hyung actually showed interest?』

『He even tried some.』

『Really?』

『He tried it even knowing it might not be to his taste, right? It's not easy to change after becoming an adult, so seeing him try things he hadn't done before or food he rarely ate makes me proud. A guy who never did that even put an ice cube in his water. I guess it feels even more admirable because Joowoo's palate was so firm.』

『Joowoo is indeed trying to experience various things. Though sometimes he brings out something so unexpected that it surprises us.』

『I'm going with Joowoo-hyung's casual fashion styling skills.』

『Joowoo does dress well.』 Kim Iwol nodded. Even he, picky as he was, seemed to acknowledge Kang Kiyeon's comment.

『I'm the type who relies on items. If I belong to the category of styling through layering clothes or matching props, Joowoo-hyung feels like someone who cleverly uses basic items.』

『Kiyeon, you dress well in a way that suits you.』

『My outfit choices are entirely exclusive to me. If I put my taste directly on Jeho-hyung or Seongbin-hyung, it doesn't suit them or loses its shape, but as for Joowoo-hyung, if you took off what he's wearing and put it on anyone, it works most of the time.』

『It has great versatility then. Joowoo does make a big impact with simple combinations.』

Kim Iwol and Kang Kiyeon's conversation heated up over Park Joowoo's casual fashion style.

Jeong Seongbin put on a textbook smile of an attentive listener, stepped a hand's breadth back from the two, and simply nodded.

Park Joowoo's fashion sense became globally famous through photos taken during 『SYNDROME』 promotions. Fans also loved Park Joowoo's indifferent yet heart-fluttering style.

*However, Sparklers like your current pajama look just as much as your red carpet photos.*

Sparklers did not go crazy for no reason over sleepwear that had almost no distinct features other than color-matching their hair, churning out countless GIFs with pale filters over them.

Jeong Seongbin, a man with down-swept hair, sharp visuals, and right-angled shoulders, only needed to lie down in silk pajamas to catch the lighting well. There was absolutely no reason for him to stay distant just because he could not join the discussion of fashion nerds.

They raged if music show outfits were even slightly clumsy, acting as if their rankings would plummet tomorrow, yet they were lenient toward a set of pajamas.

In that sense, pajamas instantly climbed to the list of the second coolest outfits of the year, considering they provided moments where Kim Iwol pressed down Kang Kiyeon's collar with the back of his hand under the guise of reaching for an iPad when Kiyeon failed to cover his chest while lying face down, or Jeong Seongbin repeatedly pulled back up the sleeves that slid down to his elbows whenever he stretched his arms upward.

The fact that its type was the exact opposite of the indisputable number one, the 『MooNStruck lOve』 MV outfit, gave much food for thought... but fans were inherently complex beings by nature, so it could not be helped. Idol stans were just like that.

*I wish you wouldn't age, but I also want to see you mature. I hope you become top-tier and win first place like it's second nature. However, I want my concert seat to be easy to get. I want you to be handsome bare-faced, but come out with heavy hair, makeup, and styling. I hope the agency brings out luxurious outfits and you sweep up all the brand ambassadorships, but don't plaster yourselves in luxury brands and post flex selfies. Because it's annoying as hell...*

One must endure this contradiction to survive as a K-pop idol fan in the Republic of Korea.

While the members bought him time, Jeong Seongbin finished his long deliberation.

『Joowoo... handles the middle role in the team really well. Even though we're both in the middle line, I'm the leader, and since my personality is weak against older members, I tend to listen to the hyungs' opinions a bit more. Then Joowoo fills in the parts I miss by taking care of the younger ones.』

『Joowoo really likes the younger ones too. Is it influenced by siblings? Joowoo doesn't have younger siblings, and Seongbin, you didn't have older siblings, right?』

『Maybe. Since Joowoo is standing in places I didn't think to pay attention to, I'm extremely grateful as a leader.』

『It's definitely not that I look down on Joowoo-hyung, but among the hyungs, he's the only one who feels like a friend. Quietly approaching and asking, "Kiyeon-ah, how are you feeling?"... It's a sight that often appears in our self-produced content, so Sparklers probably know what I mean.』

Kang Kiyeon grinned. The atmosphere was heartwarming.

『What would happen if we don't praise Lee Cheonghyeon after all this?』

『Are you asking because you don't know? Wouldn't all three of us get called in front of Cheonghyeon to kneel and beg?』

The expressions of the three became identically unified as if staged. It seemed Lee Cheonghyeon's anger was indeed scary.

『Still, when it came time to praise Cheonghyeon, there was something I really wanted to say. May I go first?』

Jeong Seongbin asked Kim Iwol for permission.

Seeing the older member nod, Jeong Seongbin fiddled with the corner of a pillow and spoke.

『It's the same for this 『Alone』 comeback. While promoting with good songs, I often think about how grateful I am to consistently make comebacks with such wonderful tracks, and Cheonghyeon contributes so much to that.』

『That's true.』

『Once you achieve good results, you might settle, but Cheonghyeon always wants to try something new and seriously hopes the members will like it, which gives me the motivation to sing really hard whenever a new song comes out. It's thanks to Cheonghyeon that I do my best every time.』

Kang Kiyeon, who had been listening silently to Jeong Seongbin, brought up an unexpected compliment.

『The fact that he takes on the textbook demeanor of the youngest member?』

『Super cute Kiyeon, you're also faithfully showing spArk's youngest member moments.』

『Not the image-making you did with a nickname, hyung! The younger ones worry a bit, thinking, 'Should I give reactions showing I'm listening closely to the hyungs?', 'Should I approach the hyungs more affectionately?' But then they can't actually cling to the hyungs, whereas Lee Cheonghyeon does the behaviors expected of the 'youngest'... so I feel like I'm getting a free ride.』

『You were worrying about stuff like that?』

Kim Iwol, looking at Kang Kiyeon with wide eyes, patted his own shoulder.

At the command to lean affectionately against him for three minutes, Kang Kiyeon rested his face with a reluctant expression.

Although what Kang Kiyeon meant was not physical leaning, the visual became heartwarming nonetheless.

『As the eldest, the part I want to praise Cheonghyeon for the most is how much he treasures the team. Everyone cherishes and loves spArk, but among us, Cheonghyeon seems to express that feeling the most actively,』 Kim Iwol said with a smile.

『Even if I control your actions, I hardly touch upon what thoughts you hold or decisions you make, right? Not to mention Jeho. Seongbin is the leader too, but rather than forcing 'Do it like this!', he's the type to listen to the other person's voice more.』

『That's true. The hyungs generally have that tendency.』

『When that happens, a team gathered with strong individual personalities can end up dividing, but Cheonghyeon grounds us when we might otherwise play individually. He's always active, saying things like, 'Let's do this all six together!' or 'Let's do that as a full group!'』

『Cheonghyeon really is like that. Even off-camera or during non-promotional periods, he always wants to leave memories as a group.』

『Giving up one's own time to constantly try to make gatherings happen, especially for someone as busy as Cheonghyeon, must be incredibly hard. I think it's something all members should be grateful to Cheonghyeon for.』

Led by Kim Iwol, the three waved at the screen and offered words of gratitude to Lee Cheonghyeon.

Then, a comment with an artist badge popped up.

≫ Thanks for the touching compliments

They're worth 219 points~

『Cheonghyeon-ah, now that your compliments are over, turn off the live broadcast and go to sleep.』

『Hahaha!』

Kim Iwol's response was cold.

Jeong Seongbin laughed brightly.

By Won Chaehee's standards, it was a packed broadcast more than worthy of being inducted into the live stream hall of fame that every Sparkler had to watch.
