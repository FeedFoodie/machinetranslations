"The daughter of this house."

"Ah..."

"Estella Cardein."

A grin spread across Estella's face as she gave her answer.

BWOOOO! BWOOOO! BWOOOO!

The sudden blare of a horn tore through the silent darkness, shaking the very air. Startled, the two exchanged urgent glances before they simultaneously broke in opposite directions as if by silent agreement. A flurry of movement stirred the dark air.

Soon, the once empty training ground filled with knights and mercenaries. Four other mercenary groups had entered alongside the Squirrel Mercenaries. Their numbers varied, but each group boasted at least twenty members.

The mercenaries eyed one another warily until their gazes fell upon the sparsely numbered Squirrel Mercenaries. Snickers rippled through their ranks.

"If you hide behind us, we can toss you the stragglers," a man with a sharp rooster's comb of hair on his crown called out. It was Jack, the leader of the Golden Scorpion Mercenaries, and he flashed Riri a suggestive smile. "How about it, pretty lady? Interested? Wanna come stand by me?"

More snickers echoed from the surrounding men, but Ciel's party did not even spare them a glance.

"Looks like you're scared," Jack pressed on. "Why'd you bring a bunch of kids who can't even pull their weight? It's obvious there's only one person here who can do any heavy lifting."

His words were clearly aimed at the large-framed Bruno. The comment was meant as an insult, yet for some reason, no one rose to the bait. Only Bruno, secretly pleased by the remark, lifted his chin with pride.

"It's hard to survive in this business with just a pretty face. Ah, or maybe you use your 'strength' in other ways?" Jack suggestively stroked his own crotch. "If it's that kind of strength, I've got plenty to spare! Kehkehkeh!"

At his vulgar provocation, Riri's patience finally snapped.

"See, Captain? He says I'm the only one here who looks strong," Riri said, tapping Ciel's shoulder with a grin. "You should build up some muscle."

"I'll try."

Satisfied with his answer, Riri smiled and gracefully raised her middle finger at Jack.

"He says he'll try. So get lost, you water deer dick-looking bastard. Before I rip off that long-ass jaw of yours and use it as a shovel."

The vulgar curse flew from the mouth of the seemingly demure woman. Jack covered his jaw in shock while his subordinates' shoulders shook with suppressed laughter.

BWOOO, BWOOOOO! BWOO! BWOO BWOO!

The horn's call was quite different from usual. Estella, who had nearly finished her preparations, turned to Ria.

"Is it a Grade 3?" she asked with wide eyes, having been learning the horn signals from her. A Grade 3 was threatening, but it was a level they could handle with proper preparation.

*What kind of Grade 3 monsters were there again...*

She tried to recall the list she had memorized, but there were far too many to guess which one might have appeared.

BWOOO, BWOO BWOOOOOO BWOO, BWOO, BWOO!

As she listened to the changed signal, a strange light gleamed in Estella's eyes.

"Don't tell me it's the Azure Forest."

"Yes," Ria replied, her expression fraught with worry. "Do you really have to go? Can't you stay?"

The Azure Forest was on a completely different level from the Reusilin Forest where the Goliath Hopper had appeared. A gloomy energy flowed through the entire wood, and those who entered would always fall terribly ill for a day or two.

Despite Ria's concern, Estella looked even more excited than before and hugged her tightly.

"Don't worry. I'm a proper member of the knight order now."

"You're a reserve member."

"I'll be dropping the 'reserve' part soon," Estella boasted. "You said I was a sword genius."

Her words did nothing to ease Ria's worries.

*How nice would it be if she just learned flower arrangement or embroidery like other young ladies.*

Ria was happier than anyone about her lady's change, but her heart was always on edge with anxiety whenever she saw Estella rushing headfirst into danger.

"Oh, right, my lady, you have to take these!" Ria called out, handing two daggers to Estella just as she was about to leave.

The daggers were a new design. Based on Yoon Seoyi's memories, Estella had reduced their inconvenient flaws and maximized their strengths. She had removed all unnecessary decorations to reduce the weight and lessen the strain on the wrist. The blade, overly long for a dagger, was shortened and made double-edged, with fine serrations added to the center of its spine. The hilt was thick enough to be handled comfortably even with gloves on, wrapped multiple times in a leather strap to prevent slipping. Even the guard, once unnecessarily curved like the toe of a traditional sock, was minimized. Everything about them was an optimal shape, neither too much nor too little.

It had required several revisions, but Dolan had followed Estella's instructions without complaint. The blacksmith, who had initially regarded her with suspicion, now seemed to secretly look forward to whatever project she would bring him next. He had even pestered her to give him one of the modified daggers.

"Ah! Thanks!"

Estella took the daggers from Ria and slid them into the sheaths on her thighs. The sheaths, made of leather and steel, were engraved with her name. They were Claire's creation, adjustable to fit perfectly. Claire, who had thought she would only ever make dresses, had been particularly happy while making them, saying something about her fingertips tingling.

In this way, Estella was slowly completing her own crew.

She felt her courage double after securely equipping both her longsword and the new daggers.

"I'm off!"

In truth, there was a specific reason Estella was pushing herself to join this hunt.

*Nebrion.*

It was because of the enhancement magic stones. According to Estella Cardein's memories, large quantities of Nebrion were buried in the Azure Forest, the very place the monster had appeared today. No one yet knew that a massive amount of magic stones was buried within the Winterfall territory.

Only Estella, with her memories of the future, knew the approximate location. The corners of her mouth lifted with the pleasant thrill of being the only one who knew the winning lottery numbers.

*If I can get my hands on Nebrion, I can modify Winterfall's weapons to be much more powerful than they are now.*

If she succeeded, it would be a great help in protecting the territory.

Estella waved cheerfully at Ria, who still stood with a worried expression. Having someone waiting for you, she thought, was a very pleasant thing.

{sep}

Lucius's eyes widened when Estella subtly joined the group of knights. His gaze seemed to ask what she was doing there, but Estella slyly avoided his eyes and stubbornly held her ground.

The knights who shared the training hall with her every day were not very surprised. They had watched her swordsmanship improve day by day and had guessed a moment like this would eventually come. Besides, they had already witnessed her skills in the Reusilin Forest.

Lucius himself only stared intently for a moment before saying nothing more. It was a relief.

"Phew!"

Estella let out a huge sigh, certain she was about to be kicked out, and straightened her back. She hoped to compensate for her short stature this way, but it was no use. She briefly considered asking Claire to make her platform boots, but the image of herself clumsily hopping through the forest in boots with thick soles made her give up the idea. She had no desire to fall to her death from her own footwear just to look a little taller.

"Who in the world is that?" Riri asked Bruno, her expression fascinated as she noticed Ciel glancing at Estella. "The captain can't take his eyes off her."

"I don't know. Did the Marquisate have a female knight?"

"Idiot, does she look like a knight to you?"

Bruno shook his head slightly, his eyes on Estella's back as she rode her horse ahead of them. She seemed too soft to be a knight, but judging by her riding form and the aura she gave off, she was no ordinary woman either.

"Does anyone else know anything?" Riri asked loudly to the others. When no reply came from the three who knew nothing, Ciel looked dumbfounded.

"Hey, Riri, I'm right here next to you."

"It's more fun to ask someone other than the person involved."

"They said they don't know."

"Don't worry," Riri said with a sly grin. "I'll figure it out myself."

"Just ask me."

"Ehh, that's no fun. It's been a while since the captain's shown interest in a woman."

"It's not like that."

"Yeees, yeees. I understand."

Riri did not look like she believed him at all. The corners of her mouth were already raised a handspan, as if she had found enough material to tease him for the next six months. Ciel opened his mouth to argue but then just shut it again. He knew that once Riri had decided to tease him, no excuse would work.

They were all supposedly his subordinates, but strangely, he always felt like he was the one being managed.

"Hey! Do you know who that woman riding up front is?" Riri shouted at Kyle, who was riding at the very back.

Watching the scene, Ciel thought, *Am I really the captain?*
