The blessings of a promotion period overflowed. A new picture and a new episode were enough to make Baek Haewon laugh and cry. Fans were simple people, grateful for such small things. Why Baek Haewon's former biases had to cause such a ruckus and make people angry was a mystery that would never be solved.

In the first week, a behind-the-scenes video of their comeback preparations was uploaded. It mainly showed the recording process, the music video shoot, and the planning for the reverse-support coffee truck event. The 'most-watched scene' marker sat right at the moment Lee Cheonghyeon walked out with wet hair after receiving a shampoo for his new dye job.

*Their tastes are so transparent.*

This week's content was uploaded with the title 'Iwol Waiting for His Members'. Baek Haewon let the strange video title slide, since it was hardly a new thing.

After the new 5th-anniversary intro ended, their familiar dorm appeared. An alarm rang from a room with a closed door as soon as it turned 7 a.m. The screen then switched to a bed where a person lay completely covered by a blanket.

*It's Iwol.*

Choi Jeho did not wrap himself up in a blanket like that when he slept. Thanks to years of observing spArk, Baek Haewon could identify members by their sleeping positions alone, without seeing a single strand of hair.

Kim Iwol turned off the alarm and did not move for about three seconds. Then he shot up. His hair was a bird's nest, a sure sign he had just woken up. His eyes widened when he spotted the camera.

『Iwol: Oh, my goodness...』

Kim Iwol's expression instantly changed to resemble a famous internet meme of a sighing old man.

Was it okay for a 26-year-old man, an idol at that, to look so grief-stricken?

He fidgeted anxiously, then chose to entrust his body to the blanket. He emerged wearing it like a ghost, but he was so tall that his shins remained uncovered, which tugged at Baek Haewon's heartstrings.

『Iwol: Is there really no one here?』

Kim Iwol looked around the living room and discovered a MISSION card on the table. He habitually clipped on his microphone, then slowly unfolded the card.

Inside the 'A Day of Iwol Waiting for His Members in the Dorm' card, a very cute phrase was written.

『Iwol: I have to be alone all day...!』

And Kim Iwol was even cuter. It was a statement that would make any non-Sparkler friend tell her to get a grip, but Baek Haewon would not back down.

Just as the video title suggested, Kim Iwol really was left alone to wait for his members. The day for Kim Iwol, the temporary head of a one-person household, began with washing up. Horrified by his bird's nest hair, Kim Iwol washed his hair and his face.

『Iwol: Since we dyed our hair this time, when we wash it, we use a color-depositing shampoo... Pfft.』

Baek Haewon's bias was talkative even while washing his hair. He did his best to deliver the single piece of information that he used color-depositing shampoo, even in a situation where water could get into his mouth. It felt like water was splashing into Baek Haewon's own ears. The ASMR was incredibly realistic.

When Kim Iwol's jade-like face, clean and glistening with water, appeared in the mirror, she was so moved she was at a loss for words. Kim Iwol was so lucky. The face he saw after washing up was his own.

Watching Kim Iwol dry his hair with a hairdryer, Baek Haewon fell into deep contemplation.

*Is it just my rose-colored glasses that make even the way he squints from the wind seem so appealing? But if I know I belong to the nation of 'juice-squeezers', isn't that okay? Jjae-i-nim will definitely clip that scene, put it in slow motion, and upload it. So that makes me normal, not weird, right?*

Baek Haewon knew, logically, that her way of thinking was over the top. She could not stop, however.

A fan's heart was always like that. If their bias said they liked gat-kimchi, a fan would make a fuss, wondering, 'Do they like water kimchi too? Do they know any good gat-kimchi restaurants?' If one were to assign blame between the fan and the idol, the idol who turned a perfectly normal person into this state was clearly more responsible.

Kim Iwol sat quietly on the sofa and first tried to figure out his members' whereabouts. Although it was pixelated, a quick glance showed every slot packed with schedules.

Kim Iwol, who had said he was supposed to practice a challenge with Kang Kiyeon, immediately called his younger brother. He asked where he was. As soon as he heard Kang Kiyeon was at the practice room, he launched into a galaxy of complaints, whining about how he could possibly not take him along.

The scene of the team's reliable eldest hyung whining that he was bored without his younger brothers was a rare commodity. When a person showed a side different from their usual self, many people felt a thrill from the gap.

Kim Iwol had a particularly large number of dualities. He would shower people with over-the-top praise with his intelligent-looking face. Despite his appearance suggesting a lack of athleticism, he was good at every kind of sport. This included being so talkative while having the face and attitude of a gentle, handsome young man.

That was why Baek Haewon often wondered if Choi Jeho's assessment of Kim Iwol as a walkie-talkie that never turns off was correct. Kim Iwol always prayed for his friend to become a decent human being as soon as possible, but Sparklers had been amazed by Choi Jeho's brilliant insight more than once or twice.

Having accepted that he was doomed to be alone all day, Kim Iwol immediately adapted to reality. A time-lapse at 5x speed showed his back as he absentmindedly scrolled through challenges. During that time, his posture never faltered, except for when he occasionally stretched. It was amazing that he could sit so upright while encountering such exciting songs and addictive dances.

『Iwol: Don't educational channels offer online courses on 'Survival in the Great Challenge Era'?』

『Iwol: Kiyeon should be the one filming this kind of challenge. Where did he go to practice that he's not rolling out a cover video for us?』

And yet, he was still talking a lot. Baek Haewon's fingers moved as fast as her bias's mouth. She paused the video and posted a sharp remark on social media.

You need to be the one rolling out something like 'The Standard for Main-d Setting to Become a Physically and Mentally Healthy Idol'. Don't pass the buck, grab a mic, quickly.

Despite Baek Haewon's proactive demand, Kim Iwol did not aim for the position of an idol instructor in the K-pop scene. Instead, he focused on walking around the living room, wiping the dust off the floor with a cleaning tool. It was as if he had a daily schedule. He did not waste a single moment.

While Baek Haewon posted her warning on social media, Kim Iwol diligently cleaned his own room and the room of his vocalist younger brothers.

『Iwol: It's not like I don't buy them bags, and I make them replace them whenever they get holes, so I don't know why the bags they carry around are always in this state. It's strange.』

『Iwol: I've never seen dust accumulate in this room. So admirable. I should give them a Cleaning Fairy Award.』

He did not become the online course instructor she asked for, but he did get a job as the dorm's docent and gave explanations. Because the instructor was handsome, everything was perfectly understandable.

The nice instructor's demeanor changed as he moved toward the youngest members' room. Kim Iwol clicked his tongue, opened the door, and could not hide his shock at the scene before him.

『Iwol: Is this a room or a pawnshop?』

On the bed were dolls and electronic devices. The desk and shelves were lined with figurines. On the floor, a few particularly cute toys were lined up, facing forward.

Kim Iwol found it incredibly chaotic, but to Baek Haewon, it was just the level of a typical social media friend's room. It was only because Kim Iwol had come from a minimalist room and a sterile chamber. Had he done a Sparkler room tour just once, he would not have thought anything of it. Baek Haewon's own room was overflowing with keychains.

『Iwol: Until my brothers get here... should I play with these guys...?』

A cheer of delight escaped her at the youngest members' nice assist. There was nothing more enjoyable to watch than a solo broadcast of a giant handsome man with small, cute things. Others would obviously see Baek Haewon as a weirdo, but their opinions did not matter at all.

『Iwol: The manggaetteok[^1] friend is... Manggae, and you, from now on, are Ponponi.』

Baek Haewon wavered for a moment at the gentle comment.

*If people around me ask, 'Haewon, you like watching a guy who names a figurine Manggae?' I think I'd become too self-conscious.*

『Iwol: The three of you should get along while this ahjussi cleans. Got it?』

The Kim Iwol who then lined up the figurines in the shade, the Kim Iwol who knelt before the trash can to detach and sort the tattered non-woven fabric from the mop…

He looked like a very strange person. She could not bring herself to deny it. The fact that the world would see her, who loved even this side of Kim Iwol, as an even stranger person made Baek Haewon sad. Love was this cruel.
