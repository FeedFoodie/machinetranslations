Tucked in between those like a trap card were contents of a completely different nature. The star could be one person, two people, or all three.

Kim Iwol, an internet addict, Park Joowoo, who loved anything his members did, and Lee Cheonghyeon, who could not hold back when seeing something fun, often brought short-form videos they shot among themselves without warning.

『Even you, Kiyeon...』

Kim Iwol had been right to praise ceaselessly that Jeong Seongbin was the only material suitable to lead this team. A spArk without Jeong Seongbin as leader was now unimaginable.
