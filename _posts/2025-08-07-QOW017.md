As Catherine's carriage drew to a halt, the knights guarding the training ground moved to approach, only to falter when the door swung open. The first person to emerge was not Catherine, but Dmitri.

He was the eldest son of the Bello family, a house that had produced outstanding scholars for generations. With his good family and striking looks, he was the most coveted groom in the land. In terms of family alone, the marriage between Dmitri and Estella could be seen as the perfect match. The glaring problem, however, was the severe disparity in their appearances.

While Estella had recently lost a great deal of weight and looked quite different, Dmitri's looks made him a far better match for Catherine. They were a picture-perfect pair, at that.

This perception fueled the knights' unfavorable gazes. In their eyes, Dmitri was a shameless scoundrel who, despite his engagement to Lady Estella, was now making advances on Lady Catherine. It was no surprise that Gai stood at the very center of these disapproving glares.

While the knights were still hesitating, Dmitri escorted Catherine from the carriage.

"Hello," she said, her bright smile like the morning sun. "Am I coming by too often?"

"Not at all, my lady. We're grateful that you think of us so often."

Her smile washed away the unpleasantness his presence had caused. As the men looked at her, Dmitri's eyes narrowed. The sight of other men gazing at Catherine annoyed him, a sharp hatred coiling in his gut. His irritation only grew, knowing he was in no position to openly shield her from their admiration.

It was only when Estella jumped down from the carriage unassisted that Dmitri remembered he had not escorted her.

"I was going to help you," he said, his voice laced with a kindness meant for the many eyes watching them. "You should have waited a moment."

Estella merely glanced at him before striding past, heading directly for the training ground. Her blonde hair, tied tightly in a ponytail, swished back and forth like a horse's tail.

Dmitri let out a disbelieving scoff as he watched her walk away without a backward glance. He stared blankly for a moment, then sighed deeply and strode after her, his hand shooting out to grab her wrist.

The instant his fingers closed around her, Estella reacted. In a fluid, reflexive motion, she twisted his wrist and pressed down swiftly with her fingertips.

"......"

A cry tore from Dmitri's throat before he could even register his surprise. The pain was sharp and absolute, overriding his will.

"Estella, what is the meaning of this!" he shouted.

At his shout, not only Catherine but all the surrounding knights turned their attention to them. Estella stared silently at his flushed face for a few seconds before releasing him.

"How could you suddenly grab and twist my wrist like that!"

"You're the one who grabbed me suddenly," she replied with indifference, dusting off her palms as if she had touched something dirty.

Dmitri was so dumbfounded he was speechless. *Who was the one who, until recently, had been fidgeting anxiously, wanting to be by my side even for a moment!*

*Can a person change this much all of a sudden?*

A smirk suddenly touched his lips as if he had realized something. His wrist still throbbed where she had pressed it, but he figured it just meant she was that angry. It was surprising she had such a sharp side to her, but he was not one to fall for such a shallow trick.

"Hah, Estella. Have you changed your strategy to pretending to be indifferent now?"

*What nonsense is this?*

Estella's thoughts stalled, unable to comprehend the words he was spouting. Seeing her speechless, Dmitri looked at her with pity and forced a smile. It was, for now, the best consideration and kindness he could offer her.

"Estella. Just because you act like this doesn't mean I'll suddenly become interested in you."

"......"

*Wow, Estella, just how ridiculously kind, no, stupid were you? Not only were you trembling in fear, unable to say a peep while that bitch of a sister openly cheated with your fiancé, but you were treated like this on top of it all?*

A mix of pity and fury for the Estella of the past made her feel like she was going crazy.

Being only too kind was not being kind.

It was being a fool.

"I understand how you feel, but my feelings are..."

"I get it, so stop," Estella warned, her voice polite as she suppressed the urge to rip the nonsensical words from his lips.

To Dmitri, however, who believed the entire world revolved around him, even this looked like another attempt to get his attention.

*Honestly, this popularity of mine.*

"But don't worry," he continued. "I'll keep our engagement as it is."

"*Is this fucking bastard crazy?*"

In that moment, Yoon Seoyi's fury easily overcame Estella's kindness.

Dmitri's eyes widened to the size of plums at the vulgar curse he had never heard before.

Estella took a bold step forward, closing the distance until not even a handspan separated them.

"You..."

"What?"

"If you talk to me one more time."

She was a full head shorter than him, yet Dmitri could not say a word. He was trapped by her blazing eyes and an expression he had never seen before, an overwhelming feeling that stole his breath.

"You'll die," Estella said, her voice low.

"...What?"

"You'll die. So go play patty-cake with your pretty little thing and have fun. Just don't mess with me."

She wanted to curse him more, but Estella's instinctively kind nature barely managed to shut her mouth.

He glanced around in disbelief, but the others seemed too far away to have heard her.

After one last look at Dmitri, who was blinking as if in shock, Estella let out a deep sigh. She spun on her heel and walked toward the training ground.

Strength training was indeed the best way to relieve stress.

The Winterfall Forest was beautiful. It was this very forest that had called him back after years of wandering the empire. Ciel drew in a slow breath, then exhaled as if savoring the very air.

CLIP-CLOP, CLIP-CLOP, CLIP-CLOP.

Behind him, the horses of his companions followed in a steady rhythm, their hooves seeming to enjoy the leisurely pace.

"Captain, let's sleep under a roof tonight instead of on the ground," came Bruno's grumbling voice. "I'm at the point where I can't even remember what a bed looks like."

Ciel turned in his saddle to look back at him and grinned. "Who was it that slept just fine last night, snoring away?"

Bruno, the tallest and largest of the group, also had the loudest snores. Riri was convinced the monsters left them alone in the middle of the forest purely because of Bruno's damn snoring.

"Me?" Bruno feigned innocence. "Where would you find someone who sleeps as quietly as I do?"

"Captain, should I just glue that bastard's mouth shut?" Riri fumed, riding alongside him. "I have some adhesive left over from fixing my boots. It sticks leather well, so I'm sure it'll stick that bastard's snout shut too."

His already upturned eyes shot even higher as he glared. Faced with Riri's murderous expression, Bruno subtly turned his head away.

"Adhesive is expensive," Ciel added. "Save it."

"Captain!"

Bruno flared up at the comment, but the sound of the others snickering made him sigh deeply.

"No one's on my side. No one."

"Marquis Cardein's castle is just a little further," Ciel announced. "Let's impose on him for the night."

"I don't think they'll let us in at the marquis's castle," another member, Airi, said in an indifferent voice. As always, his expressionless face was filled with boredom.

"Why not?" Ciel asked, puzzled.

Airi slowly looked over their ragged group before answering nonchalantly.

"Because we look like beggars."

"...You really don't know how to beat around the bush, do you," Ciel said with a complicated expression, but Airi just shrugged.

Unlike his cute appearance, Airi was blunt and straightforward. Anyone seeing him for the first time would mistake him for a girl of about fifteen, with his small, slight frame, his dark brown hair sticking out messily above his shoulders, and his tightly closed lips that were as adorable as a chick's beak. His large, clear eyes, reminiscent of a baby kitten's, only highlighted this cute appearance. He was, however, a proper twenty-two-year-old, and the words he hated most in the world were 'cute' and 'you look young'.

"Even if we look like beggars, don't you think they'd listen if Airi went and asked?" Bruno chattered excitedly. "If Airi goes and bats his eyes a few times, how could they not open the castle gates! So cute..."

He trailed off, feeling a cold stare, and promptly shut his mouth.

"Captain, I'm telling you, let's just glue his snout shut," Riri insisted. "How does he make the same mistake every time? Is his head just for decoration? No, what kind of decoration looks like that. It's just a lump of dirt! A lump of dirt!"

Bruno couldn't say a word in his own defense against Riri's harsh nagging. Airi's already pouting lips were now sticking out a handspan further.

Haru and Kyle rode on quietly, listening to their companions' affectionate bickering. It was a familiar scene. Bruno's lips were in danger of being sewn shut several times a day, Airi's pout grew longer by the day, and Riri's way of speaking grew rougher and rougher.

Listening to the familiar sounds of his subordinates, Ciel urged his horse to go faster. He too was bone-tired from the long journey and yearned to lie down somewhere warm.

In the distance, the magnificent gray castle of Marquis Cardein slowly came into view. The chirping of birds drifted from somewhere on the breeze, and Ciel's lips  curved into a gentle smile. He had a premonition that something good was about to happen.
