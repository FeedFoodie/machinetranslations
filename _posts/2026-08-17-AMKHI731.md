"Did something like that happen?" Kim Iwol asked.

Jeong Seongbin replied carefully. "I think it was when you were gone for a bit, hyung."

He then added, "Jeho-hyung had a really hard time back then."

He remembered that time two years ago. The late autumn air, just as November began, was still vivid.

The immense shock made it difficult to think clearly. Back then, spArk's most urgent task was to keep fans from noticing Kim Iwol's absence. Everyone worked desperately. They also tried to act as if everything was normal.

Everyone did their best in their roles. They struggled to hide their turmoil from the fans. While no one had it easy, one person found each day particularly draining. That person was Choi Jeho.

The day Kim Iwol's room became empty, Choi Jeho was a little out of it. Lee Cheonghyeon had snapped at him, leaving him holding nothing but a shopping bag full of clothes.

He did not scream that it was impossible or run around with messy hair like a drama character. On the outside, he was the usual Choi Jeho. He worked out according to his routine and ate his meals just the same. If someone spoke to him, he answered normally.

His roommate, Park Joowoo, stuck a little closer to Choi Jeho's side. Deeply considerate, Park Joowoo took care of Choi Jeho in the busy Jeong Seongbin's place, looking after the member who had lost his friend of the same age.

*He's not just anyone, he's his friend. How devastated must he be...*

What would he feel if Park Joowoo suddenly disappeared one day? He had never imagined someone like that, a person who was like, or perhaps even more than, the hyungs he could mentally rely on and the younger members who gave him their full support, vanishing overnight, but that had become a reality for Choi Jeho.

More than a month passed with no progress. Winter arrived while they suppressed their complicated feelings and continued their schedules. It was the first December since their debut without Kim Iwol. The end of the year felt unfamiliar. When he lay down to sleep, he could not sleep, no matter how tired he was.

Jeong Seongbin, who had quietly left his room, found Choi Jeho sitting silently at the dining table. Choi Jeho stared out the window with his chin propped on his hand and asked why he was out.

"I can't sleep... How about you, hyung?"

"Just because."

A long silence followed. It felt like a lot of time had passed, but he remembered checking the clock and seeing that not even ten minutes had gone by.

"That bastard. Fucking... running off in the middle of winter."

Tossing out that one remark, Choi Jeho stood up.

"Go to sleep."

With that short farewell, his bedroom door closed.

Jeong Seongbin still could not forget the sight of Choi Jeho's back as he walked away that day.

{sep}

With the outdoor performance in Paju, the major schedules ended. The group reorganized to fit the current situation. They created new choreography formations for the remaining songs and re-recorded them with redistributed parts.

The five of them also did a live stream. People asked about Kim Iwol's well-being. It was natural. Kim Iwol was part of spArk too. Everyone was worried, thinking he could not show his face because he was sick.

Jeong Seongbin lied to the genuinely worried fans. "Hyung is resting well."

As he said those words, Jeong Seongbin worried not about Kim Iwol or the viewers, but about Choi Jeho. He was the hyung who likely got the most questions about Kim Iwol at fan signs and on the communication app because they were the same age.

After the live stream ended, the members split into two cars to go home. Choi Jeho and Jeong Seongbin were in different cars. After some thought, Jeong Seongbin sent a text to Choi Jeho.

Me [Hyung] [Do you want to go for a run before we head in?]

A short wait later, a reply came.

Jeho-hyung [Sure]

He changed into comfortable running clothes and went to a nearby park. Not a single person was there. It was a time when the area was deserted. The winter wind snapped his eyes open, though they had been about to close from fatigue.

They ran two silent laps around the track. White puffs of breath escaped Jeong Seongbin's lips, a sign that he was getting winded.

Choi Jeho's expression, however, did not change. The pace he had matched to Jeong Seongbin's did not seem to affect him at all. They stopped running around the time the tips of their noses turned red.

"Are you upset because of Kim Iwol?" Choi Jeho asked. He handed a water bottle to his younger brother, who was sitting on the bench.

Jeong Seongbin shook his head. "I asked you to come out because I wanted to talk about you, hyung."

"Is there anything to talk about me?"

"You looked like you had a lot on your mind."

Instead of arguing, Choi Jeho sat down next to Jeong Seongbin. He also wet his throat before closing the cap on his water bottle.

"It's not something you need to worry about."

"Do members have to only talk about serious things...!"

As he spoke, Jeong Seongbin felt a strange emotion. Perhaps it was because the days when he could not even suggest going to a basketball court or a PC bang with Choi Jeho were over.

Jeong Seongbin waited until Choi Jeho felt like opening up. Even if he could not pinpoint a person's true feelings like Kim Iwol could, he wanted to be someone Choi Jeho could talk to comfortably.

From beside him came the sound of a bottle cap opening again, followed by the sound of water being swallowed. The bare tree branches swayed back and forth whenever the wind blew.

"...The accident keeps appearing in my dreams."

Choi Jeho spoke the distressing words with an indifferent expression.

"Sometimes a light falls from above my head, other times a car drives onto the sidewalk, flashes go off..."

"......"

"I was fine when it actually happened, but it's been like this ever since Kim Iwol left."

It just was not obvious, but a big change had occurred in Choi Jeho. His daily life had become a little more burdensome. He had never paid close attention to such things, so he just endured it until problems started to surface.

When the person least likely to be shaken wavers, those around them tend to be more easily unsettled.

It was the same in Choi Jeho's case. His manager and other close acquaintances cautiously worried that he might have developed trauma.

Choi Jeho had witnessed his friend coming out of the operating room with his shattered head stitched up. He had watched from right in front as Kim Iwol self-harmed from extreme emotional instability. He had carried a collapsed person on his back, pushing through persistent cameras as he ran to the car. The video of the structure falling on his head during the outdoor performance and the CCTV footage of the member's car accident were on the news day after day, even if he did not want to see them.

Choi Jeho was not the direct victim, but he was always by his side. Kim Iwol and Choi Jeho were so close that it was difficult to distinguish whether the recent events had happened to someone else or to himself.

That became a poison. Choi Jeho received an opinion that he might have post-traumatic stress disorder.

Once he understood the situation, Jeong Seongbin immediately took action. He decided to switch rooms and become Choi Jeho's roommate.

It was not as if Jeong Seongbin was perfectly fine either. With the hyung he relied on gone, a huge hole had opened in Jeong Seongbin's sense of stability. He could not fill that void himself.

Because he could understand the emptiness that came with such a void, he felt he could help Choi Jeho, who was feeling the same loneliness.

One thing was the other members' dispositions. Park Joowoo and Lee Cheonghyeon hated being lonely, and Kang Kiyeon was the type who felt comfortable with someone nearby to consult. While he wondered if they should return to the bunk bed days for the first time in a while, Kang Kiyeon volunteered to take a room by himself.

"It's not often we get a chance to have a single room in our dorm... I'll take this opportunity to practice being more independent."

Behind his affable words was a concession for his colleagues. In place of Lee Cheonghyeon, who was greatly shaken by Kim Iwol leaving the team, or Park Joowoo, who hated being alone, Kang Kiyeon readily moved rooms.

Sharing a room, Jeong Seongbin and Choi Jeho talked about many things. They made silly jokes and discussed work. During that time, Choi Jeho started waking up less often at night.

There were ups and downs in the process of getting better. Choi Jeho, who had been holding up well with a healthy body even if his mind was less so, broke down during CPR. The feeling of a silent body with no heartbeat, even when he pressed his ear against it, and the sensation of bones he knew were breaking under his hands, brought Choi Jeho to his knees. Death had been too close to his life. Even after the ambulance arrived, Choi Jeho could not easily compose himself.

Choi Jeho said the sensation lingered in his hands for a long time. That was why Jeong Seongbin did not call Choi Jeho to the hospital room. He waited for Choi Jeho's daily life to become distant from fear, so he could be a step away from Kim Iwol.

After much effort, Choi Jeho was able to come back.

{sep}

"It took a long time for him to get better. I guess because it was a problem that had been building up for a while, it didn't get better in just a few days."

Jeong Seongbin smiled. I could not.

I had my suspicions. Not long after I returned to the dorm, I had woken up in a daze and my eyes met Choi Jeho's. He was standing by my bed, looking down at me.

"What are you doing?"

"...Did I scare you?"

"Wouldn't I be? What are you doing in someone else's room?"

"It's nothing."

Then he just turned and went back to his room. I had already heard that Choi Jeho could not bring himself to visit the hospital room. Apart from his noncommittal answer, it was not hard to guess the reason.

*He must have been worried I'd go into cardiac arrest again.*

I also trembled with similar anxiety after my sister's car accident. A scarred mind suffers from aftereffects for a long time until it recovers. Human effort could not solve that. It needed time and stability.

I was not ignorant of that feeling, so I deliberately used the same room to show him I was fucking fine. By sleeping within arm's reach, I reduced his sense of duty to stand there like a village guardian post, watching to see if I would die in my sleep. I put up with the cringiness because what Choi Jeho was dealing with was not simple anxiety, but an illness.

After that, Choi Jeho's tension level dropped in a gentle curve. He stopped standing by someone else's bedside at dawn, but I could not say I knew the full extent of Choi Jeho's struggle just from that. Only Jeong Seongbin would know what he was feeling when he told me it was enough that I was alive.

"And you didn't tell me it was that bad for Choi Jeho."

"How could I tell you, hyung? It might have sounded like I was blaming you."

"Don't I bear some of the responsibility?"

It would not be an exaggeration to say I hold a large share, not just some. Jeong Seongbin just looked at me, smiling silently, so I did not add anything more.

"You said it wasn't obvious on the outside, but you managed to handle it well."

"We haven't been on the same team for just a day or two."

"I knew him for five years and still didn't notice he was annoyed with me, and I paid a huge price for it."

"Is that why you two fought?"

"I told you, I was the one who got beaten up."

"There you go again."

Jeong Seongbin gave Kim Iwol a half-joking, half-serious look.

"I received a lot of help from Jeho-hyung, too. I'm telling you, I was really at a loss back then."

"What did Choi Jeho do for you?"

"If I was down, he'd just come up and pat my shoulder. You know Jeho-hyung's style. Sometimes even a short conversation can be encouraging."

"Why does he have conversations with you, but doesn't use human language with me?"

"Ahaha!"

This time, Jeong Seongbin did not glare at me. It seemed he realized I was not actually picking on Choi Jeho, but just grumbling for the sake of it.
