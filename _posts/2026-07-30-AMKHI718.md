The story of the fire at MYTH ignited a firestorm among entertainment industry insiders. After Parthe's group press conference, even old, dormant rumors were dredged up, putting spArk on the chopping block as well.

Because of that, calls flooded in, even from people unrelated to the incident. We had a hard time explaining that we were not deeply involved.

Explaining ourselves did not completely separate us from the issue either. Lee Cheonghyeon, who had been watching the situation unfold, even put me through an interrogation.

"I'm asking this half-doubting, half-certain. Hyung, did you have a hand in timing the staggered exposés?"

"I only made the suggestion. I didn't know they'd execute it so perfectly that everything would fall into place. Senior Han Gawoon... he's a talent too precious to be stuck at MYTH."

"Isn't it natural for talent to gather there? They're a major agency. They probably have ten times our number of employees."

"Why are you dissing UtopiA? We have plenty of talent here too!"

"You know what people call us, don't you?"

*They called it the miracle of a small-to-medium enterprise...*

I should have told him not to get arrogant, but I could not refute it. It was true that our combination was practically a cheat code.

spArk was already famous as a group whose members were brought to UtopiA one after another, like fish on a line. The term 'multi-level marketing recruitment' was not flattering, but I could not deny it. It would not be easy to gather members like this even if you tried.

In any case, it seemed unlikely that MYTH could escape the crisis with just a five million won fine. The thought of a large sum of money leaving someone else's pocket felt so refreshing. If their stock price dropped due to business risks and the shareholders came running, they would probably want to log out of reality.

*Who did they think they were, living like that, only to reap what they've sown.*

They must have been so happy on their winning streak, so I hope they now feel the burden of cleaning up the mess. It would be even better if they suffered a bit in the process. Would other companies clean up their own acts unless MYTH received its due punishment?

Just as I inwardly chanted a curse for MYTH to fall, a message came in from Polo.

Senior Hellas Polo [Are you at work?]

Me [We have a comeback next week, is there any chance we wouldn't be in the practice room!]

Senior Hellas Polo [Right]

Senior Hellas Polo [If you weren't, you'd have to start preparing for retirement...]

Me [Haha]

Me [What's up?]

Senior Hellas Polo [If you're on a break, want to meet up for a bit?]

It was a summons for an unknown reason. I asked the members for their understanding and left the basement. When I knocked on the studio door, Polo greeted me warmly.

"Sorry to call you when you're busy. You must be swamped, right?"

"It's okay. You seniors must have a tight schedule too, right?"

For a group like Hellas, a full-group comeback was not easy. Idols with many years in the industry were swamped with individual activities.

Even if their album release was later than ours, they did not have enough time to gather for meetings and practice, so everyone was busy trying to coordinate their schedules.

"Well. The schedule is what it is. Something else is the problem."

The team that had to handle that tight schedule was once again in turmoil thanks to its ill-fated relationship with its former company.

I felt partly responsible for this, so I kept my mouth shut.

Before things blew up, I had given Hellas a heads-up. They were people who wanted to avoid getting entangled with MYTH just as much as spArk did, yet their precious promotional period had been disrupted twice now.

If I anticipated they would not be in a good mood, then sharing the information beforehand was only what a decent human being would do.

"I'm sorry for making things uncomfortable for you."

"Did spArk do anything wrong?"

"I have a stake in it, so I can't say we didn't."

"I never thought of it as wrong, but you gave me some vicarious satisfaction, so I'll forgive you."

"It's just the beginning. What satisfaction is there to speak of?"

"A story this big made it to the press. Do you know how terrifyingly MYTH controls the media? I never imagined I'd live to see the day MYTH gets attacked from all sides. Yuur-hyung was so stoked, he even drank soju, not whiskey."

Just hearing that made it clear how arduous the fight must have been for those people.

Moreover, I was not the only one who had reported to Polo beforehand.

"Gawoon contacted me before he posted. You know, he said he was sorry for getting us mentioned together."

"He must have been very worried."

"Those Parthe guys don't have a single likable trait... but I'll put up with it for Gawoon's sake."

The downward-flowing love was touching.

*I guess you really have to live a good life. Don't you get a senior who saves you for your own sake, even when your colleagues are all doing stupid things?*

"You must have been very fond of him."

"Who? Parthe?"

"Senior Gawoon."

"I like all juniors who are diligent and work hard at their jobs. Isn't that normal?"

I bit back the nasty thought that some seniors prefer juniors who are good at sucking up, even if they do not work.

"That's why I called you, Iwol, to check if you spArk guys are doing okay."

"So that's why you called me?"

"Did you think I called you because I was bored?"

Polo smiled with a good-natured expression.

*It's not like I came here sweating buckets thinking I was going to get scolded, but I did expect a warning like, 'What were you thinking, making such a big mess?'*

I felt needlessly sheepish.

"I told Gawoon that while we won't sue MYTH, we can be witnesses for him. We'll make sure they get beaten to a pulp, so you guys don't have to worry about it anymore. What kind of idol gets a nosebleed during a meeting?"

"It's not like he's an overworked office employee."

His insight was incredible for a veteran of the industry.

*Is he going to find out about my dark history of actually being an overworked office employee?*

Still, hearing that Hellas had decided to cooperate was reassuring. I had been feeling bad, as if I had pushed Han Gawoon into a deadly situation all by himself.

For Han Gawoon, who bore the heavy responsibility of leading a group project with Song Minil and his friends, Hellas's unseen help would be a great source of strength. It was a good thing.

"...After a dispute like this, the chances of the Parthe seniors being able to renew their contract with MYTH become slim, right?"

When idols renewed their contracts, they often changed the terms to be more favorable. spArk had also successfully renewed ours with a much higher profit share.

However, when the rift ran this deep, it was hard to even expect a contract renewal, let alone negotiate terms. Even if the members themselves no longer wanted to stay at MYTH, one of their options was disappearing all the same.

"They must have known when they made the decision. You don't need to worry about that for them."

"It's because I've had the same worries before."

"Were you thinking of not renewing your contract with UtopiA?"

To be precise, we had planned a mass exodus back in the old UA days. I stopped spArk's escape back then because I knew how unstable idols become after leaving their agency. Parthe would have to walk a much harder path than we did.

Polo added with a bitter smile, "You probably expect it to some degree. They're not kids with a strong one-team spirit... Once this is all over, it'll be hard for them to gather as a full group. They'll probably do individual activities, or maybe go into M-tubing, which is popular these days."

Members with a bad image or low recognition like Song Minil might find it hard to appear on broadcasts. Running a personal channel would be something their fans would watch.

On the other hand, there were people whose talent was too good to waste. Like Han Gawoon, who had more than enough potential to go solo, but would have a harder time making a comeback now that he was the face of the accusation.

*As the main vocalist, if he had stayed with MYTH, he could have released at least a few solo albums.*

It was not hard to notice that Polo's bitter smile was also directed at himself.

"It would have been better if they had moved as a group from the start instead of him taking all the heat."

"It must be because of his sense of responsibility."

Had he put the group's name on the line from his very first statement, Han Gawoon would not have drawn all the attention onto himself.

*But is he the type of guy to do that?*

He was clearly the Jeong Seongbin type. A personality as unbending as bamboo, the kind that might get bent but would never break. It was a trait I admired, but one that made life tough. Polo probably thought something similar.

"He won't quit singing altogether, right?"

"Of course not. There are so many places to work if you're good at singing. He could be a freelance vocal trainer or open an academy."

For such a cheerful voice, it left a bitter aftertaste. He was saying it to lessen my burden. After one last moment of consideration, I voiced the decision I had made in my heart.

"If things work out in a way that... a path opens for him to come to UtopiA later. Please tell him that it's okay, that we don't mind."

"Huh?"

Polo's eyes went wide. He had clearly not expected that at all.

*I thought he would have at least considered it, given it was the future of a junior he cherished, but I was mistaken.*

"I'm saying we don't have any bad feelings toward Senior Gawoon. It's not certain he'd even want to come, or if UtopiA would even be interested in a contract."

"Are you serious?"

I was. Some people might ask if I had not learned my lesson after everything that happened.

*But I know my personality isn't suited to hating someone who hadn't taken the lead in making things difficult.*

I cleanly admitted it after experiencing how uncomfortable lying to my own parents made me feel. If I could be like this with my messed-up family, what was stopping me from embracing Han Gawoon, who was less of a problem? If living with a clear conscience was easier, then that was how I was going to live.

"If it were Senior Minil, it would be a different story. He gave Cheonghyeon a hard time when we were rookies. I have a bad personality, so I don't forget things like that."

"You'd faint if you saw someone with a truly bad personality," Polo said, laughing.

Unlike before, it was a relaxed smile.

"You two seem to be in contact. Why don't you tell him directly?"

"If I swing the door wide open and say, 'Let's get along!', what does that make the Sparklers who went through so much trouble defending spArk, thinking they were getting us hated? I don't want them to hear 'The idols never had a problem with each other. It was just the fans grabbing each other by the hair for no reason,' after all the emotional energy they've spent, unless it's explained as a matter of adult circumstances. I can't screw over my own fans."

Accepting Han Gawoon and not forgetting our fans' feelings were issues we could handle at the same time. I just wanted to wrap it up with Polo saying something like, 'spArk said they won't give you the stink eye if you come to UtopiA,' whenever the topic of a transfer eventually came up.

"It'll be a while, right?"

"Don't even talk about it. I'm thinking years."

Polo looked utterly fed up, yet his face brightened as he asked if I wanted something to drink.

As water boiled in the coffee pot, I recalled Han Gawoon from 'Act on'. He was a man who suited the variety show name 'Diligent'. Even now, after all this time.

The office worker who had kept his post even in a disreputable company was now trying to protect his group's final image at MYTH. Han Gawoon had made a big decision, not to reclaim their glory, but to at least keep them from hitting rock bottom. It was probably for the fans who were precious to him.

The long story was beginning to fade into the annals of history. People no longer passed down the tales of their glory. The world the gods had left was about to collapse.

*I wonder what kind of person Han Gawoon will become in the UtopiA built by humans, once he descends from the temple.*

I looked forward to the distant future. The white steam from the pot settled, looking like a curtain falling on a stage.
