I compiled a few amusing posts and shared them in our group chat. Everyone enjoyed them. Thanks to that, we energetically continued our promotions.

During promotion periods, we had places to appear other than music shows and self-produced content. Variety shows served as a prime example.

If we just sang a bit and left simply because we had a few years under our belt, we would face scary suspicions about whether we lost our desire to be idols. Therefore, we put our heart and soul into it.

I popped up here and there, either with the spArk guys or solo. Today, I was about to appear on a new variety show.

A sense of grim determination hung in the air at the salon I visited at dawn. The atmosphere felt solemn even though I was not getting hair and makeup done for a performance.

"Did something happen? Your expression looks dark," I asked.

"It's because I'm steeled with determination," came an unexpected answer.

"What kind of determination?"

"They asked me to turn you into a romantic-comedy male lead. It's the determination to meet the client's needs."

"Huh?"

The schedule I attended this time was an ordinary friendship-building variety show. In the past, many shows like this existed.

Programs that searched for acquaintances separated long ago or arranged meetings with people someone wanted to get close to in the entertainment industry were quite popular for highlighting a star's hidden interpersonal relationships.

Then the trend shifted. Honest, down-to-earth celebrities with a human touch became beloved rather than mysterious figures, leading to frequent instances of mutual socializing.

With the rise of social media, hearing news about a celebrity's family or friends became easy, even if it was not publicly released in magazine interviews or TV shows.

In the case of idols, as company management relaxed compared to previous generations, members of the same age created group chats or got introduced through mutual friends to become close.

As the public no longer considered celebrity friendships particularly interesting, introduction-style variety shows temporarily vanished. However, reaching the present where extracting fresh material became difficult, work began to unearth content that had faded into the background of history.

This was how I came to build a friendship with someone through the broadcasting station's arrangement.

I even filmed a preliminary interview. I answered faithfully when asked about the personalities of those around me and the type of personality I wanted to become close with.

"Do I usually think about wanting to form new relationships? I like meeting people and spending time together, so I'm always open to that."

"Most of the friends I met while working are younger than me. The only one the same age as me is Jeho, my group member. Or seniors? I dare boast that I'm close with seniors too, but since they're people I respect, I can't treat them like peers."

"Shyness around strangers? Not at all! Even if it's the first time meeting someone, I greet them, introduce myself, and ask how they came... before I know it, time flies right by."

"There's no personality type I avoid. Rather, I'm more worried about whether the other person might feel burdened by someone who talks a lot. I'll read the room and open or close my mouth skillfully."

I grew curious about how similar the actual cast member would be to the imaginary daily friend from this preliminary interview.

So while being handsomely transformed into a romantic-comedy male lead, I tried guessing whom I would stand in front of the camera with. Based on reviewing previous episodes, a high possibility existed that a member of a peer idol group would appear.

*They wouldn't do something like putting Parthe and me together, right?*

In the first place, it was questionable whether a member of that team could even appear on TV. Since I was generally close with the Berion members, no interesting scenes would be captured even if we filmed.

The ticklish awkwardness and clumsy conversation of a first meeting were the highlights of this kind of variety show. If Berion came out, the moment we sat across from each other, we would end up filming a debate on 'The Attitude Senior Idols Must Take to Survive in the Market.'

My closeness with senior actors was already quite famous in the entertainment industry. Trio of Villains ate roasted sweet potatoes and even went sledding in the dead of winter, so would it not be strange to think we would be awkward?

Maybe it would be different if Teacher Mo Yunguk appeared. However, the production team would have to abandon the illusion that I could become friends with him, unless it was a legendary actor and his sycophant.

Although they asked me to come looking like a romantic-comedy male lead, the current trend was to match genders for filming, so I did not think a female senior would appear as my counterpart.

My only known female seniors were Ha Seomyeong-ssi and Ms. So Saeyoung. Since both of them were busy, there would not be any reason to greet them anew here.

For my outfit, I received a shirt and a coat. The stylist's determination to push for a mature concept was clearly conveyed. Dong Euion-ssi showered me with compliments during the entire trip, so my face was burning hot by the time I stepped out of the car.

The opening was filmed at a large cafe, presumably sponsored. The wood-toned background and plant interior stimulated sensibility.

In a place like this, we should have turned Jung Seongbin or Lee Cheonghyeon into baristas to shoot a pictorial, yet I ended up coming all by myself, which felt immensely disappointing.

"How were you feeling on the way here?" PD-nim asked.

The camera had been rolling since I walked into the cafe, so it seemed they were filming small talk to include in the beginning of the broadcast.

"I was so nervous that I couldn't sleep. I wonder if the person coming slept well?" I said.

"You look very excited."

"I'm excited. I really love making friends. I imagined so much about who might come."

"Who were your candidates?"

"Maybe a fellow idol, I thought?... No? Ah, I guess not. Is it someone doing a different genre of music...?"

While I let my imagination run wild, the cafe door opened. Morning sunlight poured in, casting a halo effect.

The person who walked over to me with firm steps, wearing an outer coat smelling heavily of autumn, was...

"...Manager-nim?"
