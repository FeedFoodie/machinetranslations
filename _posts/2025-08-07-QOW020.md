A Howler burst from the green forest. It was a giant, wolf-like monster with white fur, one of a large pack. Though not intelligent, they possessed excellent cooperative skills in battle and could easily tear a human to shreds with their powerful jaws, sharp teeth, and large claws.

AWOOOOO! AWOOOOONG!

The moment they arrived, the cries of Howlers echoed from all directions. It was the sound of them calling their comrades, of them arranging their ranks for the hunt.

"They're Grade 3 monsters! Don't let your guard down!" Lucius shouted from the front of the formation. "If even one gets past us, the people of the domain will be hurt!"

At his command, the knights expertly spread out into their positions.

"Stay by my side," Lucius said, his voice low. "Do not, under any circumstances, charge ahead. My priority is to eliminate the monsters. Protecting you, milady, comes after that."

"Don't worry. I can take care of myself," Estella replied, her sword held ready as she stared straight ahead.

Lucius let out a small sigh as he watched her, her entire being burning with the will to fight.

*What on earth was the Marquis thinking?*

He had insisted, argued that he absolutely could not take Estella on a monster hunt, but Marquis Cassian's stance had been firm.

> "Let Estella do as she wishes."

"They're coming," Lucius warned. "Get ready."

Estella nodded. This was, after all, her second monster hunt.

The knights' movements were swift and systematic, a testament to their high level of training. The Howlers moved as a pack under a leader's command, but their coordination was no match for that of the Black Lily Knights, who fought as if they were a single body. When a Howler leaped to attack, a defensive wall of massive shields instantly formed to meet it. Through the gaps, swords flashed out, mercilessly slashing at the creatures' legs and bellies. The knights herded the Howlers, trapping them like fish in a net.

However, not all the Howlers could be contained. The ones the knights missed became the responsibility of the mercenary groups positioned behind them. Ciel and the Valiant Squirrels cleanly dispatched the beasts that came running toward their pre-assigned positions. They then occupied a moderately open area to take care of any others that escaped the knights' cordon.

All the mercenary groups did their part, but the Squirrel Mercenaries' movements were on another level. Their numbers were ridiculously small compared to the other groups, yet they had no openings. The performance of their mage, Airi, was particularly dazzling. It was rare for a mercenary group to even have a mage, whose services were worth a fortune. Even if a group did, it was usually a low-level one, since the ability to create a simple fist-sized fireball was enough to be recruited by the Imperial Palace or the Magic Tower for a high price.

"Airi's all excited again!" Riri grinned, watching Airi run wild like a fish in water.

Airi rarely ever smiled. He did not show his emotions outwardly, and in the three-plus years they had been together, Riri could count the number of times he had seen him smile on one hand, but now, Airi was smiling brightly, as if he was truly happy. Monster hunting was, after all, one of the things he enjoyed most.

VWOOOM!

An unsuspecting wolf leaped into the magic circle Airi had created, intending to swallow him in a single gulp. The Howler, drooling at the sight of the delicious-looking prey, only realized something was wrong when flames began to lick at its paws. As the panicked creature spun in place, Airi's magic ring scanned its body like a scanner, leaving only a pile of blackened charcoal in its wake.

"I told you not to burn the fur, Airi," Kyle said with an indifferent expression, throwing a shuriken at a Howler rushing toward him.

While dealing with the monsters, Ciel found himself extremely preoccupied with Estella, who was fighting both alongside and separately from the knights. Her swordsmanship was a bit clumsy, yet she moved with a surprising mastery. The way she swiftly rolled to dodge or counterattack suggested she was someone who instinctively knew how to use her body.

*Was it just that she hadn't learned swordsmanship? Was she a natural?*

"Captain, where are you looking!"

At Bruno's shout, Ciel tore his gaze from Estella and swung his sword. A Howler's massive head, three to four times larger than a normal wolf's, tumbled to the ground and rolled quite a distance. The head, now separated from its body, slowly closed its wide-open mouth and let out a last, thin breath.

"I told you not to damage the hide," came the disappointed voice of Kyle, the mercenary group's treasurer.

Ciel paid him no mind. A hide with the head attached would sell for several times more, of course, but the fur itself was still traded at a fairly high price.

His gaze naturally found Estella again. She was in the middle of stabbing her sword into the side of a Howler that was lunging at a knight from behind. The knight, having narrowly escaped death, immediately beheaded the beast and offered his thanks. The two of them then naturally teamed up as if they had become a team, taking down one more Howler together. After their success, she grinned at her partner knight, a smile that made her round face look even rounder.

"What, can't take your eyes off her?" Riri asked as he approached, his expression playful. "Aish, I got blood all over my hair!" he freaked out, seeing the red staining his locks.

"You got more on your face."

"My hair is more precious than my face."

Riri grabbed his blood-stained hair and rubbed it on his sleeve in frustration, which of course did no good. As Riri despaired, Kyle was repeatedly nagging Airi not to burn the Howlers to death. Near Airi, however, there were already three piles of ash that had once been monsters.

"So, have you fallen for her?" Riri asked with a sly grin, giving up on his hair.

While one side of the clearing was in chaos fighting Howlers, the two of them were as calm as villagers out for a stroll.

"Aren't her movements strange?" Ciel answered with a question of his own.

Seeing Ciel's serious expression, Riri, who had been intent on teasing him, had no choice but to look toward Estella.

"She's pretty good," he admitted. He had thought she would be slow because she was a bit chubby, but she was surprisingly agile.

"Just like a squirrel," Ciel said, his eyes still on Estella.

At his words, the corners of Riri's eyes, which had been downcast because of his hair, shot up in excitement.

*The captain is comparing a woman to a squirrel? He'd never even used that word for Airi, who had the cutest face in the world?*

Riri's mind raced with surging questions. Feeling a pleasant sense of excitement for the first time in a while, he could not help but whistle.

"A squirrel?"

"Just saying. Behind you," Ciel tossed out casually.

Riri immediately spun around and drew his bow at an approaching Howler.

THWACK!

The heavy, steel-tipped arrow pierced the beast's head in one shot. It was a bow that even grown men would struggle to lift, yet Riri ran over as lightly as a feather and easily pulled the arrow from the beast's head with one hand. Blood and brains clung thickly to the arrowhead, but he shook it off as if dusting a rug, then immediately changed direction and pierced another Howler's head.

"Yes, the earhole!" Riri shouted, pumping his fist when the arrow went straight through its ear.

Kyle, who was nagging Airi for incinerating yet another one, looked up and gave him a thumbs-up. Having minimized the damage to the hide, Riri now shouted "The nostril!" and fired his bow, but failed spectacularly. The arrow hit the middle of the beast's cheek.

"Still better than having its head cut off, right?"

The remark was clearly aimed at Ciel, and Kyle once again gave a thumbs-up.

"They don't see me as their captain," Ciel grumbled. He thrust his sword into the mouth of a Howler that had opened its maw wide to devour him, then pulled it back out. The tip of his sword pierced through the top of its head.

"Still made a hole! What's the squirrel lady up to?" Riri grinned, teasing him.

The total number of Howlers killed that day was fifty-seven. The knights killed thirty-two, and the mercenaries killed the remaining twenty-five. Of those, ten were taken down by the Squirrel Mercenaries. The mercenary group with the fewest members had killed the most monsters. It was a major reversal for the Squirrels, who had been subtly looked down upon for their cute name and small numbers.

Everyone stared in amazement, but Ciel's party paid no mind to the others' gazes, as if this was a common occurrence. Jack and his men, who had been gleefully mocking them before entering the forest, could not even turn their heads in their direction, their faces bright red.
