The sky was overcast, as if rain would begin pouring at any moment. The only signs of life nearby were the weeds growing wild everywhere.

Not a single person, not even the bark of a dog, could be heard anywhere in the crumbling village.

WHOOOOSH!

A gust of wind blowing through a narrow alley swept a scattered piece of paper to one side. It was an old, yellowed flyer with faded print.

She glanced down indifferently at the words 'Warehouse Grand Opening' on the flyer rustling at her feet, then looked forward again and signaled to the subordinates following her with a hand gesture.

Three people in military uniforms moved silently between the tense, abandoned buildings. The way they moved quickly, armed and scanning their surroundings, suggested they were no ordinary soldiers.

Without a single word exchanged, the three arrived in front of a derelict building with shattered windows and dangling hinges. It might have once been a wedding hall that had seen the birth of countless couples, but now its atmosphere was gloomier than a funeral home.

Seoyi, her expression taut with tension, sent the two following her into the building first, then quickly followed behind them.

"Target located."

Hansik knelt on one knee before a small explosive and gave the signal. The red dot on the GPS tracker on his wrist matched their location.

The timer on the explosive had just passed the three-minute mark. They were about a minute behind schedule, but it was still enough time to disarm the device.

"Just do as you were trained. What do you do now?" Seoyi asked in a cool voice, looking at the two men fumbling around. Her tone, a reprimand to move calmly, snapped Hansik and Minwoo to attention.

Swallowing dryly, Hansik, whose lips were blue and chapped from tension even though it wasn't cold, carefully examined the ticking explosive in front of him before answering, "Judging by its irregular shape, it's highly likely an IED."

At Park Hansik's answer, Seoyi's lips curled up in a satisfied smirk. Seeing a smile from his notoriously strict superior, a faint sense of relief spread across Hansik's face.

"Good. First, check the fuse and wiring status."

"Yes, ma'am!"

"Watch and learn. You're next," Seoyi said, looking at Minwoo, who was kneeling beside Hansik. Minwoo, a former special forces soldier, was in training to become a special forces operative. She was fond of this junior who learned ten things when taught one and wanted to teach him everything she could.

"Yes, ma'am!"

"What should be disarmed first?"

"R-Remote detonation."

"Correct. Be careful, cutting the wires in the wrong order could set it off."

Hansik recalled his numerous practice sessions and examined the tangled mess of wires before him. Meanwhile, the timer on the explosive ticked away diligently, soon approaching the two-minute mark. Time, which usually crawled, was flying by today. His heart pounded so violently it felt like it was in his throat.

Hansik steadied his breathing and reached for a bright blue wire. Seoyi watched him with a proud expression.

CLICK.

"It's cut!"

Finally!

Just as the faces of all three lit up with relief.

BEEP, BEEP, BEEP, BEEP.

A warning alarm suddenly blared, and a red light began to flash on the timer's screen. The number, which had been over a minute, abruptly changed to 10.

"T-This is!"

"Wasn't this supposed to be a training dummy?"

The two men asked with frightened faces as they saw Seoyi's expression harden.

8, 7, 6.

"Get out."

"Ma'am?"

"Can't you see? Run, now!"

There was supposed to be a training explosive, but for some reason, the bomb flashing before their eyes was real. Seoyi forcefully shoved her two stunned juniors in the opposite direction and hurled the menacingly blinking explosive out the window with all her might.

BOOM!

The explosive thankfully detonated outside the window, but the blast sent fragments of the shattered building flying right at Seoyi. A massive chunk of concrete struck her hard on the head.

CRACK!

"Sergeant Yoon!"

"Sergeant!"

Even as her consciousness faded, Seoyi was relieved to hear that their voices sounded unharmed, but she also realized this was the end. She could feel something flowing not just from her head, but from her abdomen as well.

*Fuck. This hurts like hell.*

BEEEEEEEP.

Along with a ringing that felt like it would tear her eardrums, the two men's voices grew distant. And then, Seoyi's vision went black.

{sep}

Seoyi felt someone shaking her shoulder and forced her heavy eyelids open, but the light was so bright it was hard to keep them open, and she had to squeeze them shut again. With her eyes closed, an unbearable wave of drowsiness washed over her. She had a strong urge to sleep more, without even knowing where she was.

"Ms. Yoon Seoyi?"

A strange woman's voice accompanied another shake of Seoyi's shoulder.

"Let me sleep. Just...."

Grumbling involuntarily against the overwhelming sleepiness, Seoyi's eyes shot open. The last scene she remembered pierced through her heavy drowsiness.

"What happened to Staff Sergeant Park Hansik and Staff Sergeant Tae Minwoo...."

Seoyi's voice, which had started urgently as she shot up, trailed off. Her surroundings were too unfamiliar. It was so bright she thought it might be a hospital room, but that wasn't it either.

There was no bed, no IV line, no doctors or nurses, just white walls everywhere. Suppressing her fear, Seoyi slowly looked down at the clothes she was wearing. The plain white garments were so light and soft it felt as if she were wearing nothing at all.

"Am I... dead?"

"Yes, you have passed away."

"Gasp!"

The situation was hard to accept and she was scared to death, but the voice from a moment ago suddenly spoke right in front of her.

Startled, Seoyi staggered back as if she would fall and stared at the person standing before her. "An... an angel?"

The person standing before Seoyi was a middle-aged woman with blonde hair and blue eyes. She was looking at Seoyi with such sorrow, tears streaming down her face. Seoyi was a person who got her act together. She couldn't even stand the sound of a child crying, let alone someone who bawled their eyes out at the drop of a hat, but seeing this stranger weep endlessly while looking at her, her heart strangely ached. It was an emotion Seoyi had never felt before.

"Why... are you crying?"

The question slipped out on its own, even though she knew this was no time to worry about someone else's situation.

"Please."

"For... what."

"Live my life in my place."

"Excuse me?"

Before Seoyi could even grasp what she was saying, the middle-aged woman extended her hand as if to shake hands. 

As if possessed, Seoyi took the offered hand.

[The contract has been established. In accordance with Estella Cardein's wish, the soul of Yoon Seoyi will be bound to the body of Estella Cardein.]

WHOOOOSH!

A sound like air escaping came from somewhere. Behind the woman, a bright blue, fist-sized hole had opened up like a black hole. Seoyi's short hair fluttered like grass in the wind, and her white clothes flapped like a flag. The wind grew stronger, but for some reason, not a single strand of the woman's hair was out of place.

It felt like if she stayed here, she would be sucked into that hole. Seeing nothing to grab onto for support, Seoyi urgently reached for the woman's arm, but the hand that had been so easily grasped just moments ago was now intangible. Seoyi's arm flailed in the empty air before she was sucked into the gaping hole like water down a sink drain.

"What is this!"

THWUMP!

With Seoyi's final, desperate scream, the round hole sealed itself smoothly.

[Are you satisfied now?]

Estella smiled and nodded at the benevolent disembodied voice. The tears that had welled in her wrinkled eyes fell, rolling down her sagging cheeks.

"Thank you. Truly... thank you."

[May your choice prove to be the right one.]

"She will do well," Estella replied in a choked voice, looking at the wall that had turned completely white.

{sep}

"Aaaaaah!"

Feeling as if she had just fallen from a thousand-foot cliff, Seoyi screamed and shot her eyes open. She expected her body to be broken after falling from such a dizzying height, but strangely, she felt no pain and had no injuries.

"What? What is this?"

Seoyi placed a hand on her chest to calm her racing heart, but something felt different.

"Soft? Very?"

A sense of dread creeping in, Seoyi slowly lowered her head.

"Is it swollen from my injuries?"

The chest in her line of sight was much higher than usual, and the hand and arm touching it were not just plump, but thick. Moreover...

"What am I wearing right now?"

The frilly lace hanging everywhere made her already jumbled thoughts even more chaotic. As tense as if a bullet would hit her if she moved even slightly, Seoyi darted her eyes around to survey her surroundings.

She was lying not on a firm military mattress, but on a soft, plush bed that felt like her body would sink right into it. The four bedposts were intricately carved with flowers, and the cloud-like comforter had a subtle sheen.

*Have I been kidnapped?*

BADUMP, BADUMP...

The suddenness of the situation pushed Seoyi's tension to its peak. The swelling could be a side effect of medical treatment. Fortunately, there was no one else in the room. The one thing that worried her was the taste in clothes of whoever had kidnapped her.

*Damn pervert. I'll definitely kill you.*

She quickly scanned the room to understand the layout of her confinement. All she could see were ornate, glittering objects. A gentle breeze was even wafting in through a slightly open window.

*Don't tell me there are cameras!*

Seoyi quickly scanned the room, looking for likely places a camera might be hidden, but she couldn't see any spots where a hidden camera might be.

*Micro-cameras, maybe. These guys don't seem like amateurs...*

Feeling disheartened, Seoyi held her breath and quietly slipped out of bed.

Beneath the hem of the fluttering lace skirt, a foot as round and fleshy as the back of her hand was revealed. Unlike her usual nimble movements, even walking felt burdensome and heavy. It was like moving with 5kg sandbags tied to every joint. An indescribable sense of déjà vu slowly enveloped her entire body.

Still, getting out of here was the priority. Dealing with the kidnapper bastard would come next.

As Seoyi carefully surveyed her surroundings, her eyes fell on a full-length mirror standing against one wall. She felt slower than usual, but she was a lot more alert.

The mirror, framed in glittering gold, was as ornate as something a princess in a movie would use.

"Fucking pervert."

She needed to assess her current physical condition first. Seoyi listened for any sounds from outside once more, then cautiously walked toward the mirror. Thanks to the plush carpet, her footsteps made no sound.

"What... is this!?"

Standing in the mirror was a young woman who looked vaguely familiar. Long, flowing blonde hair that reached her waist, and bright blue eyes shining from beneath heavy eyelids. This woman, whose body was round as a drum, was someone she had definitely seen before.

> "Live my life in my place."

The woman she had met in that place of endless white walls! But that woman had been old, hadn't she?

In that instant, the memories of Estella Cardein struck the mind of Yoon Seoyi, Sergeant of the Republic of Korea Army Special Forces, like a bolt of lightning.
