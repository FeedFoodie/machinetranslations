The family physician, Alexis, arrived soon after, but Estella sent him away immediately. 

Catherine, her face a mask of worry, urged her several times to get an examination, but it was no use. Ultimately, she had stared at the unusually stubborn Estella before nodding, her expression so pitiful it could wring a confession from an innocent man. 

Estella, however, felt not a shred of sympathy for the performance, nor would she feel guilty for a crime she had not committed.

"It's not serious enough to see a doctor," Estella said with a smile. "I think I'll be fine if I just get some rest."

Catherine's expression brightened noticeably. It was a smile that started with her lips and ended in the gentle curve of her eyes.

"Really? Oh, I'm so relieved you're not sick, Sister, but I'm still worried. Please have a hearty breakfast and rest well in your room today. I'll go make you some more milk tea. I'll add extra honey, especially to help you recover. Please wait just a moment."

Catherine's kindness was painstaking, so excessive that anyone should have seen through it. 

*Why hadn't I known?* 

Back then, she had simply thought of it as the deep consideration of a kind younger sister who genuinely pitied her. Estella had been too foolish, too naive, to notice the sticky, hidden intentions behind that sickeningly sweet smile. Even at the moment she realized Catherine was deceiving her, Estella had been so terribly foolish that she had allowed herself to be deceived to the very end, just so she would not disappoint her.

Whatever it was, Estella had no intention of being fooled by the detestable act any longer.

"No. There's no need for that, Catherine. I won't be drinking milk tea starting today," she said firmly.

Catherine's eyes widened in disbelief. For a moment, her gaze seemed hurt, but Estella saw the flicker of panic in her trembling pupils and knew she was flustered.

"Oh, w-why? Did I perhaps make some kind of mistake? If that's the case, please don't be upset and forgive me. Making milk tea for you every morning was my greatest joy... I don't know what's wrong, but if you're disappointed in me for some reason, please tell me. I'll fix it."

The sight of Catherine acting so relentlessly kind was suffocating. With the corners of her eyes drooping like a child repenting for a great misdeed, she looked as innocent as a wide-eyed cat from an animated movie. It was such a harmless expression that even knowing she was a wicked bitch, one might wonder if, just for this moment, she was being sincere.

If it affected her this much, the old Estella must have been helplessly swayed.

"It's not like that," Estella said. "I think my tastes have changed a bit, and I don't really feel like eating sweet things. I'm always grateful to you."

"...Really? That's a relief, then. Ah! In that case, I'll bring you an unsweetened tea starting tomorrow!"

Catherine, who had been at a loss after hitting an unexpected wall, suddenly broke into a wide smile. This time, the corners of her eyes curved first.

"No. Don't do that. From now on, Ria will prepare my tea. Right, Ria?"

Ria, who had entered quietly to clean up the broken teacup, widened her eyes at the unexpected command. She immediately stood up and bowed her head silently, her brown hair falling in two neat pigtails below her shoulders.

"Yes, my lady. Starting tomorrow, I will prepare your tea."

"Thank you. I'd like jasmine tea, please."

"......"

Quiet and proper, Ria answered neatly, just as her personality would suggest, and finished cleaning. She was the only maid who had raised her hand when no one else wanted the position of being Estella's maid.

Due to her reserved nature, Estella had never spoken deeply with Ria in her past life. One thing, however, was certain. Ria was the only person who had visited her in prison to check on her until the day of her execution, and the only one who had watched from afar and shed tears the moment she stepped onto the guillotine. She never had the chance to ask why. On that day, there was no opportunity to speak with Ria.

A fresh wave of gratitude and sorrow surged within her.

*What's wrong with me?*

Yoon Seoyi was not the type of person to be swayed by every little emotion. Her nature was to move only after meticulously analyzing every situation and calculating flawlessly until a perfect plan was formed. Perhaps that was why Estella's soft emotions felt unfamiliar and a little overwhelming.

*These kinds of emotions aren't helpful right now.*

Estella calmly composed herself and gazed intently at Ria as she cleaned, as if observing her. Seemingly conscious of the stare, Ria appeared flustered for a moment but, as always, calmly finished her task and left the room.

"Sister? Did you hear me?"

"Hm? What did you say?"

"What were you thinking about that you didn't hear me calling you from right in front of your face?" Catherine asked with a smile so bright it was sickening.

"I wasn't thinking about anything. What did you say?"

"I said that tonight is the day the whole family gathers for dinner. You can't skip it again like last time. Understand? Everyone will be waiting."

"I understand. I'll eat with you all today."

"Really? I'm so happy! Imagine how pleased Father will be when I tell him! I should go tell him right now! Is that okay?"

"Yes. Go ahead."

Like someone truly overjoyed, Catherine hugged Estella, who was several times thicker than her, and gave her a loud peck on the cheek before disappearing out the door as lightly and delicately as a fluttering butterfly.

"Ugh...."

Estella scrubbed the cheek where the damp lips had touched with the palm of her hand. A glance in the mirror revealed a smeared red lipstick stain. Her eyes narrowed in disgust.

As she was vigorously rubbing her cheek, Ria, who had re-entered at some point, asked in a cautious voice, "Shall I prepare some water for you to wash, my lady?"

"Would you?"

"......"

Even though it was just a simple errand, Ria answered with a brightened face, as if she had received a compliment.

"......"

"Yes, my lady."

"Thank you."

"Pardon?"

The unexpected thanks sent a blank ripple across Ria's usually placid face. Lady Estella had never once shown irritation or anger, but she had also never given an order or even called her by name. All commands were relayed through Lady Catherine, and Lady Estella would, for the most part, simply nod in agreement. She had never shown any particular sign of intimacy toward her.

"I said, thank you."

"For, for what... if I may ask."

"For everything you've done for me."

Ria couldn't fully understand what her lady was saying, but she somehow felt that she would come to understand everything in the future. It was as if she were once again meeting the gaze of the brilliant and beautiful young lady who had extended a hand to her long ago when she was starving to death on the streets.

"I... thank you, too."

Her cheeks, dotted with small freckles, flushed red. As color washed over her previously monochrome face, she finally exuded the vitality characteristic of someone her age.

"For what?"

Estella seemed to have forgotten that Ria was the emaciated, dying child from back then. It was understandable. After entering the marquis's household, Ria had spent seven years doing cleaning and laundry before she became Estella's dedicated maid. The scrawny girl was now nineteen and had unhesitatingly volunteered for this position, wanting to stay by the side of the lady who had saved her life.

Although the moment she became Lady Estella's maid she became the target of subtle disregard and contempt from the other maids, it didn't matter at all. She was happy, believing that simply washing the lady's handkerchiefs that had secretly wiped away tears and smoothing her wrinkled bedding was a way to repay her kindness.

"For everything you did for me."

Watching Estella raise an eyebrow as if she didn't understand, Ria was able to smile a genuine, heartfelt smile for the first time since becoming her dedicated maid.

Trouble the waters to catch the fish, kill with a borrowed knife, plunder a burning house, make noise in the east and attack in the west, retreat is the best option.

Ria stared with a dazed expression at Estella, who was walking in the training grounds after eating a breakfast of boiled eggs and salad instead of her usual greasy food. She was still mulling over the conversation she had with Lady Estella that morning.

*From today on, remove foods with a lot of flour and butter from my diet. Take out the greasy sausages and bacon too. I don't need overly sweet desserts either.*

*Then what should I prepare, my lady?*

*For tomorrow morning, let's start with grilled mushrooms, a slice of smoked salmon, and some lemon water?*

Ria thought she must have misheard.

*Will that be enough for you?*

*I have to make it enough.*

To be asked to prepare a meal with items that had previously only been brought in for show, while excluding all her favorite foods... Ria wondered what on earth was happening.

On the other hand, she was also very pleased with her lady's change. She had never said anything, but she had felt so sorry watching her lady stuff her cheeks with food every morning until they were fit to burst. The lady, so focused on eating, had seemed as if her only joy in life was food.

This morning, however, was different. Her eyes, which had always been hazy and clouded, now held a strange light, and the smiles she showed from time to time were full of confidence. Her way of speaking had become somehow more resolute, and her actions as she surveyed her surroundings held a newfound certainty.

*My lady has changed. I know it.*

Whatever the reason, Ria hoped with all her heart that her lady would regain her former self. She was, after all, the only person in the marquisate who had noticed Catherine's true colors.

*Everyone needs to know Lady Catherine's true nature.*

Watching Estella walk the training grounds, panting for breath but refusing to give up, Ria cheered for her deep in her heart.
