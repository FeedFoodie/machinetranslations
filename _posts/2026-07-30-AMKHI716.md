The room fell into chaos all over again. I had been pinching my nose, praying it would not bruise, so the whole situation felt absurd. The moment I moved my hand to change the tissue, blood drops fell as if they had been waiting for the chance.

"Why is there so much blood? Is it just a nosebleed?" Min Jukyung asked in surprise.

*My nose burst before the photos did. What kind of twist is this?*

"It happens sometimes, really. This is strange, I was feeling great..."

I swore to the heavens that my condition had been perfect. My mood had been perfect too. Jeong Seongbin nailing his concept during the behind-the-scenes recording had made me so happy.

The wet tissue got in the way as I tried to explain, sticking stubbornly to my hand. A real pain.

Someone even said to send Iwol down quickly. Concerns about a hospital visit played on like background music, but my thoughts were somewhere else entirely.

*My heart is fine now, so why is my nose still like this?*

Not once had my heart hurt since I parted ways with the SYSTEM. My body had been fine ever since I quit the corporate hellhole the SYSTEM built. The side effects from my regression, after hitting a 100% synchronization rate, had definitely...

Thinking that far, I realized something.

The nosebleeds were an issue I had even before joining Hanpyeong Industry.

I belatedly remembered that they often came when my part-time schedule got tight or when stress piled up. Perhaps that was why nosebleeds hit whenever my fatigue level in the SYSTEM rose. Since it had nothing to do with the SYSTEM's main purpose of death by overwork, it must have simply stayed on as a separate risk.

*Back then, I believed the nosebleeds were also a penalty from the SYSTEM... but proficiency was just a visualization of my job skills improving, and synchronization was a warning device that I could die from overwork at any moment. This must just be a part of my body's natural phenomena.*

There was no way that damn SYSTEM would be flexible enough to look after a pre-existing risk. Once I realized my nose was only asserting its presence as usual, protesting whenever it felt like it, the panic drained out of me.

*I thought I was fine. The MYTH situation must have been more stressful than I realized.*

I felt a little embarrassed. It was like falling on an icy road, where the stares sting more than the impact. That was exactly how I felt.

"I'm sorry, I'll head out first..."

"There's nothing to be sorry about, Mr. Daeyeon! Once his bleeding stops, have him lie down in the break room."

"Should I go?"

"No, if it's anyone else, Kim Iwol will just ignore them and run off again."

*Hey, what do you take me for?*

I had made a bloody mess of the table, however, so I had no right to speak. My hands were covered in blood, so I could not even pat the shoulder of Dong Euion-ssi, who was busy scrubbing the desk and floor with wet wipes. Like a criminal, I was escorted to the break room by Manager Daeyeon's hand.

*They say you should avoid companies with break rooms when you're interviewing. Does that make UtopiA a sweatshop too...? No, wait, was that about overnight duty rooms?*

As my mind grew hazy, only useless thoughts surfaced. I was not sleepy enough to say I was tired, but my eyelids were too heavy to say I was fine. I just felt a little worn down.

"Have you not been sleeping well lately?" the manager asked, sitting me down on an empty mattress.

He even kindly wiped my hands with a wet wipe. I was twenty-six and should have been able to wipe my own hands, but unfortunately, they had to stay pressed against my nose.

"Nah, I've been sleeping well."

"Even so, it's only for a few hours, and you're not eating properly either."

"I ate my whole lunch box. I never leave food behind."

"It's not much, even if you eat all the salad and chicken breast."

I was sleeping much more, much deeper, and eating better than before, yet the manager still pitied me. He worried not just about me, but about all of us. Maybe it was because he used to drive a daycare bus. He is far too warm-hearted to be working in the idol industry.

The bleeding finally stopped after a long while. The manager even lifted my chin to check whether the blood was really gone.

*I guess I must have lied to the managers a lot in the past. Look at how no one believes me right away.*

Dong Euion-ssi brought me a spare t-shirt from his car. After changing and wiping my face clean, I was back to being the perfectly fine Kim Iwol. The perfectly fine Kim Iwol-ssi, however, was not allowed to return to the practice room.

"You came in early and practiced a lot today anyway, so get some sleep before you go down."

"That was to make up for the time I'll miss because of the meeting..."

"Rest now so you don't suffer during promotions. Hurry up."

It was not as if I wanted a nosebleed while performing our new song. Reluctantly, I lay on the mattress and pulled the blanket over me.

"Are you going to take my phone, too?" I asked, trembling with anxiety. The memory of the company switching my phone to a 2G one was still vivid.

The manager calmly asked back, "Well, what should I do?"

"I'll leave it here in case something comes up, but only after you fall asleep, Mr. Iwol."

"You're so meticulous..."

The man left no openings. That sleepiness washes over you after a meal and a meeting is an absolute truth. Unable to resist the soft mattress and the dim room, I fell asleep like a log, despite our promotions being just around the corner.

{sep}

I woke three hours after leaving the conference room. The manager had left my phone by my head, so I could check the time. The area was quiet, every other spot empty.

I had slept so deeply that I did not want to get up.

*Really, I'm a person who loves to rest this much, so why does everyone misunderstand me? It's so unfair.*

As I reluctantly sat up, the break room door opened. I was about to leave, thinking it would be awkward if someone was there, when a familiar voice stopped me.

"You're awake?"

The visitor was Jeong Seongbin. He confirmed no one else was there, then turned on the lights. A black plastic bag hung from his hand.

"Did you know I was here?"

"Euion told me. I stopped by a few times to see when you'd wake up, hyung."

"I made you go through all that trouble."

"It was just a few trips up and down."

Jeong Seongbin rummaged through the bag and pulled something out. It was a cooling patch, the kind for fevers or hot days.

His intention was obvious, so I obediently opened the packaging and stuck the patch on my nose. Jeong Seongbin took the trash.

"I heard you bled a lot."

"Just a little."

"Not like back when we were trainees?"

"You remember that?"

"I also remember you coming back to the company to practice secretly after that."

Jeong Seongbin's quiet words hit a nerve. The smile on his lips made me look away.

*Back then... I couldn't help it. The Job Competency Enhancement Program was in full swing. If it weren't for that, I would have debuted without even raising my dance proficiency.*

"You've really changed a lot, hyung."

"Have I?"

"If it were like before, you would've come straight down to the basement as soon as you opened your eyes, and then you would've turned all our stomachs upside down."

"That's why I listen to you now."

"Hah."

The guy sighed deeply, a helpless smile thrown in as a bonus. He looked a bit fed up with me, too.

*If he really is fed up... well, that's a shame.*

"You don't get angry at me anymore either, Seongbin. You used to yell at me all the time."

"I think I've adapted in a bad way. Hyung, you need to have a little pity on your dongsaengs."

"But I have the biggest soft spot for you guys. That's strange."

*I don't know if my sincere confession reached Jeong Seongbin. Geez, it's not like I can just cut open my heart and show him.*

Jeong Seongbin asked whether I wanted to go back to the dorm or rest more. Instead of answering, I changed the subject.

"I talked with Han Gawoon-sunbaenim on the phone a little while ago."

"With Gawoon-sunbaenim?"

"There was something I wanted to talk about. You know things are noisy on MYTH's side."

Jeong Seongbin, who had been listening quietly, shot me a sharp look.

"Are you involved in that too, hyung?"

"...Just enough to get the tip of my shoe caught?"

"Hah."

His face, which usually looked like a second male lead in a romance manhwa, filled with disbelief in an instant.

"The issues so far have been completely internal, and they faced a backlash recently for unnecessarily mentioning us... I thought they wouldn't make the same mistake again."

"You're right. MYTH didn't throw the first punch. I went to Han Gawoon-sunbaenim and stuck my shoe in his face."

"About what?"

"That MYTH is such a thorn in our side that I want to get rid of them completely, so I asked him to help rally public opinion."

Jeong Seongbin was speechless.

Now that I had said it out loud, the dizziness came back. Stress, definitely.

"I'm dizzy. Can I lean on you for a second?"

"You're like this just from talking about it, and you say you actually did something?"

Despite his sharp tone, Jeong Seongbin helped me lean my head against the wall.

"What can I do if I can't stand the sight of them? I'm tired of letting things slide, and it's not like we're the only victims. So, since I saw an opportunity, I just suggested we take it."

"Did Gawoon-sunbaenim agree to cooperate?"

"I haven't heard back yet. We've been too busy preparing for our comeback to even check. If I get betrayed, I'll just become the scapegoat again, right?"

"Hyung!"

"I'm kidding."

*I'm not worried that Mr. Han Gawoon will make me a scapegoat. That man would rather remain alone in the fiery pit that is MYTH than use someone else as a shield for his own gain. That's why I was the one who said, 'You seem pitiful, so I'll give you a chance to escape.' Though I do wonder if it was nosy of me, coming from a small agency.*

"I'm not at the center of the controversy, and I don't have to do anything proactive, so I thought I wouldn't get stressed, but I guess there was some pressure."

"I've never seen you stir up trouble and not feel responsible for it. How could you not be affected?"

"You know me better than I know myself. Still, I reflected on my actions and slept soundly like a good boy. I've grown, haven't I?"

Jeong Seongbin answered with a sigh. I kindly warned him that he would get old if he kept sighing like that.

"My head feels clearer after resting, so don't worry too much. I'll explain everything to the others later."

"You're going to tell them about Gawoon-sunbaenim's matter too?"

"I have to. If I make more secrets here, Cheonghyeon will be disappointed, and I'll get cut from Choi Jeho's friend list."

At my words about getting cut as a friend, Jeong Seongbin stood up. Just as I reached to fold the blanket, my phone lit up bright.

Han Gawoon-sunbaenim [Keep in mind that the results might not be as good as you expect.]

It was the kind of text only someone who had already made progress could send.

*"Sunbaenim, you don't look it, but you're really decisive."*

It was amazing. This tiny sign that things might work out cleared my head like magic.
