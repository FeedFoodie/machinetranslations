The manager and I spent a cozy time at the arboretum, taking photos of each other.

At least, on the surface, it looked harmonious.

"Mr. Iwol, stand in front of the sign here. I'll take a photo of you."

"No, it's fine! Let me take one of you, Manager!"

"You've been taking photos of me since earlier."

On the other hand, my insides were a complete wreck, barely functioning normally. Still unable to shed the title of 'Manager' yet weighed down by the word 'Hyung', I cried internally while sticking close to the manager's side.

Holding back my tears, I even struck a V-pose under an arborvitae tree.

However, you have to hear my side of the story too. In my long life, I have had Noonas, seniors, teachers, and bosses, but never a Hyung. Since I never had a reason to use the word 'Hyung', saying that word to another person with my own lips felt completely awkward.

Fortunately, the manager did not use the absolute power of the camera to pressure me into calling him Hyung right then and there. The topic of conversation shifted instead.

"Once promotions end, you'll be on break. Do you have any plans?" the manager asked about my schedule, as I was soon to become temporarily semi-unemployed.

Since I had a few scheduled commitments, I would not be completely idle. Still, it was not enough to say I was busy, so I shared my grand ambitions.

"I think I need to start working out again. Also, we haven't opened a new team savings account since the old one matured, so I'm thinking of putting this payout into a savings deposit. How about you, Manager?"

"Just a reunion gathering?"

He seemed to refer to the school reunion with Jukyung-nim. I remembered how Jukyung-nim re-encountered Manager Daeyeon there after a long time and cast him.

Manager Daeyeon, who was looking for a new job, accepted the offer and returned to UA. The story of Manager Daeyeon joining UA was such a moving true story that I could never forget it.

As I got lost in memory, a sudden realization flashed through my mind. I hurriedly stopped the manager in his tracks.

"Are you seriously not able to make plans because of us? Is the rotation... not being adjusted? We can just take a taxi, so why don't you get some rest?"

"No, I usually don't make many plans in the first place."

"Phew..."

A sigh of relief escaped my lips automatically. I was terrified he might say he wanted to quit UtopiA because we did not give him any break or time to make plans.

"And my job as a manager is to pick up Mr. Iwol and spArk, so how could I not do that?"

"That's right. You have to stay with us, Heerang, forever..."

"Forever might be tough."

"Why?"

"Well, if my night vision gets bad, I'll have to put down the steering wheel for spArk's safety."

"No! Don't grow old!"

Without realizing it, I became a young man screaming in a corner of the arboretum. Now was not the time to leisurely freeze-dry title tracks of the tropical house genre. We should have put the managers in the freezer as well.

"You said you'd stay with spArk until you die."

"I made a promise like that?"

"It sounded almost like that to me."

"For my part, I plan to stay with you as long as I can."

"Why do you say 'as long as I can' instead of 'until you die'?"

I felt sorrowful. My determination to have a fun time laughing and chatting with a new friend had completely vanished.

All that remained in my mind was the future image of myself, tears soaking my face as I sang a song wishing for good health and longevity at the manager's 70th birthday party.

"What kind of clothes do you want us to wear for the celebratory performance at your 70th birthday party...?"

"Sigh, really." The manager laughed as if I were hopeless.

I was sincere every single moment though. My heart throbbed as if I had been dumped, just like when we filmed the 『MooNStruck I Ove』 MV.

Next, we went to a restaurant. It was a Western restaurant I had never seen before.

"Have you been here before?"

"No, it's my first time. How about you, Manager?"

"I had it recommended to me as well."

*Is it Jukyung-nim's pick...?*

People working in the entertainment industry know the best places to eat in Seoul inside out. They have to host team dinners, assign restaurants to tab meals for trainees, and treat official contacts to meals...

Since food is an essential part of life, seasoned office workers each had their own food map, which they even shared online.

There was no way the capable Jukyung-nim did not have such a list. She probably had everything, from great places for lunch and dinner to spots ideal for a second round.

The restaurant's signature menu was pasta. If he had deliberately chosen this place for me because I loved noodles, I could have shed tears of emotion right here. However, I had to appear as a cool idol on screen, so I held back.

"What would you like to eat, Manager? The oil pasta is the best seller."

"You pick, Mr. Iwol. You like pasta, don't you?"

"Could it be that you picked this place for me...?"

"Of course."

I was doomed. It was three seconds before my eyes welled up with tears. How did such a precious manager come to our company... If I were a novice in my first life who had not regressed, I would have really cried.

While the manager ordered pasta, risotto, and salad, I diligently poured water. While waiting for the food to come out, I took a trip down memory lane.

"When we were busy, Manager, you used to take us to a meat restaurant and buy us meat and stuff."

"It's been a while since we last went, hasn't it? Everyone's been so busy lately that there's no time for that."

"Is that why you didn't take us? Should I skip the gym for a day after promotions end? This time, I'll treat you. There's a decent place in Gangnam."

"As if I don't know you'll just ask me to drop you off at the gym after eating. Besides, Gangnam is way too expensive."

As expected of the manager. He never fell for a fake easily.

"Mr. Iwol, you'll be twenty-seven next year, right?"

"Yes. It's already been five years since we've known each other, Manager!"

"Has time passed that quickly already?"

"It feels super short to me. We have so many memories together, yet it's only been five years..."

I was overwhelmed with emotion. Back in my trainee days, I was too busy worrying about who the company was going to kick out and how, so I never imagined a peace like this would arrive.

If the blessing that comes to those who endure is a working life this stable, I thought it was worth the patience.

We also engaged in broadcast-friendly conversation. We had a moment of sincerity verification, something unavoidable for anyone appearing on TV.

"From your perspective, Manager, who is the easiest member to take care of?"

"Jeho."

"All the staff like him. Seongbin says Jeho is the member who causes the least trouble too. How on earth did this happen?"

"He's easygoing. He manages himself well, he's reliable, and even though I'm in the position of supporting him, I can rely on him too..."

"That's ridiculous."

He used to have the personality of an unpolished, jagged iron nail! He had no idea that Choi Jeho acting all calm and composed was entirely thanks to me carving him down like an ice sphere for an on-the-rocks drink!

I felt wronged and aggrieved. The days when I whispered the proper conduct of an idol into Choi Jeho's ear flashed before my eyes.

How many times had I given him real-time feedback to fix his way of speaking? After modifying a primitive human into a modern person like that, what? Reliable and dependable?

The human performs the tricks, but the bear gets the money. This is why I never got promoted from assistant manager.

"If I ask who the most difficult member is next, you're going to say me, aren't you?"

"Mr. Iwol is a tricky member, not a difficult one."

"Is it because I don't call you Hyung, Manager?"

"Since you know, why don't you call me that?"

"Sigh...!"

"I'm joking. Thanks to Mr. Iwol, we work so comfortably."

"Even though you said I'm not as good as Jeho."

The answer was not suitable for broadcast, but that was how it ended up. It would probably be entertaining for a variety show. It just meant it was not appropriate for 'my' broadcast image while appearing alongside him.

I ended up being horribly exposed by the manager. In an attempt to request a truce, I conceded both pieces of the appetizer garlic bread to the manager, who ate as much as an ordinary person.

{sep}

The final course after the meal was decided to be 'Taking Friendship Photos', which I had chosen. I thought we would end up going to a four-cut photo booth, so I was a bit flustered to find out we were moving to a proper studio, even if it was self-service.

"This is surprisingly serious."

The manager was surprised as well. Although he smiled often, the manager did not express other emotions much, so if he said this much, it was safe to say it was truly unexpected.

"You saw me taking photos with the kids, right? You can't leave until we take a hundred shots."

"I saw it. That's why I'm so worried."

"Hahaha!"

Before taking photos in earnest, we had time to carefully select props. I picked up a cute star hat and a few adorable sunglasses, placing them on the manager's head and putting them on myself as well.

"Manager, pink suits you really well. You have the face of someone who should wear rabbit ears."

"Rabbit ears?"

"Just close your eyes and trust my recommendation. I'll repay you with guaranteed results."

"Whoa, I think the last time I wore something like this was back in kindergarten."

"And this is the perfect opportunity for you, Manager, to unleash all the rabbit ear energy you've accumulated over the years!"

At my words, the manager raised his hands in surrender. He obediently put on the rabbit ears.

I also wore the puppy headband the manager picked out for me. Although I had never been told I looked like a puppy during my time as an idol, he seemed to have matched the color since I was wearing a brown coat.

"Wear this one later too."

The manager plopped something on top of the towering pile of props. The item picked by the manager sitting on top was a cute headband featuring the head, scales, and tail of a clownfish.

"Are you telling me to wear this headband because I'm like a clown right now...?"

"A clown? Isn't this a clownfish?"

"Cheonghyeon told me that the English name for clownfish literally translates to 'clown fish'."

"I didn't know that. I just picked it because it was orange."

"Manager, stop giving Jeho rides in your car."

"Is it okay for Euion to get influenced by Jeho then?"

"...Can't we just send him off in a taxi?"

Even though Choi Jeho had not been brought along, the curse of orange never ceased. Since it was a request from my friend for a day and my manager, I could not refuse to wear it. Out of options, I snapped photos as an orange head. It was humiliating.

Pressing the shutter button non-stop, I said, "Since you're so harsh to me and only dote on the younger ones, I'm going to be a younger brother too."

"Huh?"

"I'm saying I'll call you Hyung."

If this was the only way to be evaluated as a good and reliable idol, then it could not be helped. I would yield a step so the manager could lead us around for a long time.

"You drop your honorifics too, Hyung!"

At my urging, the manager chuckled warmly.
