When Estella flinched, a shadow instantly fell over Ria's expression. A wave of terror washed over the maid as she wondered if she had learned something she was never meant to know. What if it was a grave sin, one that would get her cast out of the marquis's household? The thought alone made her dizzy. She had nowhere else to go if she left this place, and all she wanted was to serve her lady until the day she died.

"I'm sorry," Ria apologized hastily, her voice timid. "How dare I..."

"What are you talking about?" Estella interrupted, grabbing the maid's politely clasped hands. "You've done something amazing! You're so smart!" All the while, the horn signals continued to blare. "Did you understand what that sound means, too?"

Ria, who had braced for a severe scolding, could only nod with a dazed expression.

"What does it mean?"

"It means a Grade 4 monster has appeared, and there are about ten of them," Ria explained. "The Reusilin Forest is very close, so the knights went there quickly. There are houses not far from the forest."

A Grade 4 monster. Estella didn't know much about them, only that they were divided into five grades by danger level, with the fourth grade being on the less dangerous side.

*In that case, maybe I can go take a look?*

A powerful curiosity stirred within her. Since the knights had already gone, it probably wouldn't be dangerous as long as she kept her distance and just watched. She wanted to know how strong the monsters were and what it would take to defeat them.

The question of whether Estella could ride a horse was answered the moment she swung into the saddle. She could, and she was very good at it.

Leaning low, Estella urged her horse into a gallop toward the Reusilin Forest. It was her first time riding since gaining Yoon Seoyi's memories and becoming overweight, yet her posture felt as natural as remembering how to ride a bicycle. She cut through the wind, racing toward the forest at a breathtaking speed.

The forest itself was shockingly beautiful.

Deep, peaceful shade pooled beneath intertwining trees that soared as if to pierce the sky. Thin rays of sunlight filtered through the branches, casting delicate silver-white beams across the forest floor. Flowers of all sizes bloomed in a riot of color, and soft moss blanketed the tree roots.

"Wow..."

Estella had been momentarily lost in the beautiful scenery, forgetting her purpose, when a sound from not too far away snapped her back to reality.

"Groooaaar!"

"Grrrrrr!"

The chaotic roars of beasts she had never heard before mingled with the shouts of the knights. It was a strange sound, not the howl of a wolf nor the cry of a deer.

"Aren't there more than ten?"

She couldn't gauge their size by sound alone. Estella dismounted, gripping the dagger she had brought in both hands as she moved slowly toward the noise. Compared to the one she used to use, it was dull and heavy, but it was far better than nothing. Besides, the way it fit snugly in her hand was quite thrilling.

*I should still get this dagger looked at. It has too many useless decorations and the blade is longer than necessary.*

She had worried if she could use the martial arts she had learned before in this body, but the moment she gripped the dagger, a sensation coursed through her like a jolt of electricity. She realized her body remembered, just as it had with riding the horse.

Thump, thump.

As the sounds grew closer, her own heartbeat seemed to match the noise of the rampaging monsters and the clash of swords. After a brief survey of her surroundings, Estella began to climb a tree. Her body was heavy, so she couldn't ascend as lightly as before, but since she had lost a lot of weight, it wasn't too difficult.

*I need to lose more. My strength is seriously lacking.*

She desperately missed her old, agile, and toned body. It wounded her pride to be panting after climbing a single tree.

Finally, she reached a spot with a clear, open view.

"What is that?"

Estella was startled by the appearance of a monster she had never seen before. She had assumed a Grade 4 monster wouldn't be very dangerous, but its appearance alone was extremely threatening. It was neither a hippo nor a crocodile, yet it ran around frantically with its enormous mouth wide open, trying to bite people. Its face was the same size as its body, making her wonder how it could even move with such a large head.

Its body was the size of a bull, but thankfully, its movements didn't seem very agile.

The knights were fighting splendidly. At this rate, it seemed they would be able to handle the monsters without much trouble.

Just as Estella felt relieved and was about to turn back, one of the rampaging monsters abruptly changed direction and started running toward the tree she was in. Compared to the other slow and clumsy monsters, this one was swift and agile.

*You must be the leader.*

Estella's eyes narrowed as she watched it. Looking closely, she saw a small, shabby red crest on its head, like a rooster's. Five or six knights were in hot pursuit.

It was a spectacle.

*This is better than a 4D movie, isn't it?*

Estella's eyes sparkled with an intrigued expression. She had wanted a closer look at how they dealt with these monsters anyway. If they took care of it right below her, she thought she could learn how the knights hunted. Estella's grip on her dagger tightened.

The monster soon crashed into the tree she was perched on and stopped.

"......"

"Ah!"

Estella, who had nearly fallen, hastily grabbed a branch to steady herself. The monster, which the pursuing knights had been focused on, must have heard her because it lifted its head.

The moment its yellow eyes met Estella's, the creature's mouth gaped wide open. Then, another mouth shot out from within, extending all the way up to the branch Estella was sitting on.

"Is this an Alien?!"

A startled Estella yelled as she threw her body back. Her subsequent actions flowed as naturally as water.

Dodging the creature's sharp teeth, she readjusted her grip on her dagger with a flick of her wrist and slashed horizontally. The creature's snout, which looked like a peeled snake, was severed by the dull blade with a grating sound.

"......"

"Kuaaaaargh!"

The creature, its snout cut off, thrashed about in agony. Blood from the monster splattered across Estella's face.

Estella wiped the blood from her face with the back of her hand. The knights who had just arrived were momentarily surprised to see her in the tree, then quickly subdued the thrashing monster.

The monster soon took its last breath, and from all around, sounds announced that the other monsters had been dealt with.

The total number of monsters handled was twelve.

Two that had been hiding appeared late, but Lucius and Gai each took one, dispatching them in a single blow. Their skill was far superior to the other knights who had ganged up on the monsters.

Estella couldn't take her eyes off their swordsmanship for a second.

*I have to learn that.*

She was more confident than anyone when it came to using daggers, throwing shurikens, and shooting. She had learned kendo, but swordsmanship used in actual combat was of a different caliber. Having found a martial art she wanted to learn after so long, Estella's heart pounded as if she had met her ideal type.

"Lucius!"

Estella leaped down from the tree and approached the knight commander when she spotted him.

Lucius, who had been watching the knight apprentices dismantle the monsters, turned his head in surprise at the sound of his name. It was a voice that should never be heard in the forest.

"Why are you here!"

The knights around them flinched at Lucius's furious tone.

The moment Lucius saw Estella standing in the monster-infested forest, he was overcome with uncontrollable rage. In such a dangerous place, without a single personal guard! A young woman, all alone, with monster blood splattered on her face, she was an absolute mess. To a stranger, she wouldn't look like a noble lady, but worse than a wandering mercenary.

"Do you have any idea where you are!" Lucius's voice was filled with fury.

Even though he was the knight commander, Estella was the marquis's eldest daughter. The only reason he could get this angry at his lord's daughter was because he was Lucius. As a longtime friend of Marquis Cassian, he was genuinely happy about Estella's change, but he had not wished for such a dramatic one.

He had just been feeling relieved that the marquis's household was finally getting back on track, but seeing her act so recklessly after getting a little better made his blood boil. He scanned the area for any remaining monsters before barely suppressing his anger.

"This place is incredibly dangerous. Return to the castle at once..."

"I want to learn how to hunt monsters."

"......?"

Lucius thought he had misheard Estella's unexpected reply. Not flower arranging, not embroidery, but monster hunting. It was absolutely out of the question.

"What in the world are you talking about! Monster hunting is too...!"

WHOOSH! THWACK!

Before Lucius could finish, the dagger Estella was holding embedded itself in a tree trunk.
