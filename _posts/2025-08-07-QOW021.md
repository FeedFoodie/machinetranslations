In a clearing of the Azure Forest, mercenaries gathered to organize the monsters they had caught, a necessary step before the Knight Commander's inspection would register them as assets.

Vice-Commander Gai, his eyes carefully scanning and his hands meticulously recording, came to a halt before the spoils of the Squirrel Mercenaries.

"Are you telling me six people caught all of this?" he asked, his surprise evident. The haul stood in stark contrast to that of the Golden Scorpion Mercenaries next to them, who had managed only five monsters with thirty members. Noticing the comparison, the leader of the Golden Scorpions let out an awkward, dry cough.

"The Squirrel Mercenaries," Gai muttered, watching Ciel and his subordinates intently for a moment before walking away.

Riri and Bruno slapped a high-five and laughed cheerfully. Airi stared ahead with his usual indifferent expression, while Kyle and Haru were locked in a serious discussion that, upon closer listening, was about the dinner menu.

Leaving his eccentric subordinates to their own devices, Ciel scanned the area again, his gaze searching for Estella. He found it hard to understand why he was trying so hard to find her, but one thing was clear. He was very curious about the person named Estella.

Very curious indeed.

Then, as his eyes found her amidst the blurry, passing scenery, his pupils froze. His busy head automatically followed.

Estella was talking with the Knight Commander, their heads close together. The conversation seemed quite serious, judging by the Knight Commander's expression.

"What on earth are they talking about," Ciel wondered aloud.

"Why? Curious what the squirrel is talking about?" Riri chimed in. She wore a peculiar expression, her eyebrows and the corners of her mouth twitching upward at the same time.

"Haa... Riri."

"Yes, Captain."

"I'd appreciate it if you'd stop paying so much attention to me."

"Yes, I will."

Surprised by her ready obedience, Ciel’s eyes widened as he looked down at her. However.

"Should I find out what the squirrel is saying?" Riri asked, her turquoise eyes twinkling as if she had never been reprimanded.

*That's more like it.*

Ciel stared ahead, feeling resigned. Not a single member of the Squirrels was normal.

Sighing deeply, he habitually glanced toward Estella again, only to flinch. She was walking toward him with big strides, her walk a bouncy, jaunty gait.

{sep}

Estella fought much better than Lucius had thought. Even now that the monster hunt was over, she did not return right away. Instead, she mingled naturally with the knights who had worked so hard, offering them encouragement and sharing jokes.

*Is this really the lady I know...?*

Lucius stared blankly at Estella as she wiped the blood from her sword. He remembered a younger Lady Estella, one who had laughed often, run around freely, and been surprisingly brilliant. Then one day, she had suddenly begun to gain an unusual amount of weight, and her personality and behavior had changed as if she were a different person.

In a way, she had simply returned to her original self. It was a welcome change, but he could not shake an inexplicable sense of unease.

"Hahahahaha! Really? Did Denian actually do that?"

"My lady, no. There's no way I would do that!"

Estella was busy teasing the soft-hearted Denian along with the other knights. Denian protested vehemently, but he knew Estella was not being serious and scratched his head with a broad smile on his lips.

The atmosphere of the always-rigid knight order had changed noticeably since Estella joined them. There was more laughter, morale was boosted, and they were all more motivated than before. At first, Lucius thought it was because of the physical training room Estella had created.

He was wrong. The one who had a greater influence on the knights was not the novel exercise equipment in the training room, but Estella herself.

After chatting with the knights for a while, Estella spotted Lucius and walked over to him.

"Do you know how many monsters I caught today?" she asked, her expression full of confidence. It was the first thing she said to him. Her boastful face held a trace of her younger self, and Lucius could not help but smile.

*That's right. Lady Estella was originally like this.*

"How many did you catch?" Lucius asked reluctantly.

Estella smiled like a child and held up three fingers.

"I caught six by myself."

"Hey, you can't compare the Knight Commander with a prospective member."

"It's also unreasonable to compare a hot-blooded youth with a worn-out old man," Lucius said, patting his back for effect.

"You were still flying all over the place. It was amazing. I'm serious," Estella said, giving him a thumbs-up with both hands.

"Will you continue to come on the monster hunts?"

"Yes, but more importantly, there's something I want to ask."

Lucius stared intently at Estella, wondering what on earth she wanted to ask. *Is she curious about the price of monsters? Or about swordsmanship?*

"Is it possible for me to personally make a contract with a mercenary group?"

Estella's question once again completely defied his expectations.

{sep}

"You're the captain of the Squirrel Mercenaries, right?"

As Estella approached and asked the question, Ciel kept his mouth shut and simply nodded. He could hear Riri shouting, "It's the squirrel, the squirrel!" from the side, but he pretended not to notice. The back of his head prickled under the intrigued gazes of his subordinates.

"Could we talk for a moment?" she asked.

"Right... now?" Ciel replied, glancing around.

*Did she fall for me after seeing me in action? In that short amount of time?*

He forcibly suppressed the smile creeping onto his lips and waited for her to speak. She was not exactly his type, but she was round and cute, so he thought it might be okay to date her.

"The squirrel is mumbling! Our captain's favorite squirrel is mumbling!" Riri started singing in a strange tune.

Unable to watch any longer, Kyle covered her mouth and dragged her away somewhere. Ciel felt genuinely grateful and gave Kyle a nod.

"Then, let's move to..." Ciel began, trying to quickly move them to a different spot. He figured the lady's pride would be hurt if she had to confess first, and he had no intention of refusing.

"Won't you make a contract with me?"

However, what Estella said had nothing to do with his assumptions.

"Pardon?"

"I want to make a contract with the Squirrel Mercenaries."

*A contract? Not that she's interested in me?*

Ciel tried to ignore the wave of disappointment as he continued. From somewhere, he thought he could hear Riri's teasing voice and his subordinates' laughter.

*Damn Riri. Damn subordinates.*

"But we already have a contract with the Marquis for monster subjugation."

"That's fine. He said a dual contract would be okay, as long as the monster subjugation takes priority. So, what do you say? Won't you work with me?" Estella asked, cheerfully extending a hand toward him.

*A dual contract. That's ridiculous.*

Ciel was about to refuse flatly. However.

"Alright."

The moment he saw the small hand Estella offered, he found himself agreeing without thinking.

Later, when his subordinates asked why he had made such an un-captain-like contract, Ciel answered.

Her hand was too small.

How could he ignore a hand like that?

{sep}

Just after Estella and Ciel had briskly finalized their verbal agreement, a Howler they thought was dead suddenly shot to its feet. It began to sprint frantically toward the forest. Desperate to survive despite its severe injuries, the creature moved several times faster than usual.

A commotion erupted among the quiet knights.

"Don't let it escape! We must catch it!"

A Howler that survived a fight and escaped would always return with a larger pack to take revenge. If they let it get away, a much larger group of Howlers would likely attack the populated areas.

The knights, who had let their guard down and lowered their weapons, quickly drew their swords and pursued the fleeing monster.

Estella was about to follow immediately when Ciel grabbed her wrist. "Where are you going?" he asked, his eyes wide.

"I have to catch the Howler that got away!"

"The knights are more than enough to handle it. Why do you have to get involved in everything?"

As he asked the question, Ciel simultaneously realized why he was so interested in Estella. This noble lady was stubbornly inserting herself into things she did not need to, into situations where she could be seriously injured or even killed. That bothered him, and he was curious about her reasons.

"Why not?"

"Pardon?"

"I'm asking why I shouldn't get involved."

"Because if you're in danger, other knights will be put in even greater danger."

"Then you can just protect me. We have a contract, don't we?" Estella said it as if it were the most obvious thing in the world. She then leaped onto her horse and galloped in the direction the knights had gone.

Ciel felt as if he were completely bewitched.

*What is this feeling of hearing an answer, yet not hearing one at all?*

While Ciel stood there for a moment, the Squirrel Mercenaries quickly followed after Estella.

"Protect the squirrel!"

The sight of them running off to Riri's cry was needlessly valiant.

"Haa... This is so tiring." Ciel shook his head and mounted his horse lightly.

However, contrary to his words of fatigue, his expression as he rode toward the forest looked brighter than ever.

When it was ambiguous whether to go or not, you just had to go and see. That was the only way to find out what lay at the end.
