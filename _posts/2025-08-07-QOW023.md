Though neither Catherine nor Marcella knew of the paternity test, the marquis carried a profound guilt over it. This secret was the very reason he treated Catherine with such particular softness.

More than a decade had passed, yet whenever he saw Catherine, the old guilt still reared its head before any flicker of affection could. That guilt had only deepened after Marcella died from the plague. Despite being half-sisters, Catherine took meticulous care of Estella, and it was all the more touching to see one troubled child looking after another.

"Did you hear about my sister's incredible achievements in the Azure Forest?" Catherine rejoiced in Estella's accomplishments as if they were her own.

A smile touched the marquis's face as he slowly nodded.

"I'm so proud of her. You are too, right, Father?"

He still didn't answer, but his smile deepened. Catherine continued to chatter like a lark.

"But people are being so mean." Her excited voice suddenly turned sullen, and the marquis's soft gaze fixed upon her. "Why are they making such a fuss about her bringing back some Nebrion? I'm sure she had her reasons for taking it."

The marquis listened in silence as she went on.

"But is Nebrion really that worthless? Is there no use for it at all? The color is pretty, so maybe it could be made into a necklace or a brooch like the ones she likes. Ah, but it's too soft, so that's probably impossible. I heard she went back into the Azure Forest with the mercenaries she hired and brought back a whole cartload of it. To be honest, I'm a little worried. It bothers me that she's getting so close with those vagrant mercenaries. Sir Dmitri also came by several times but had to leave without seeing her. He did meet her yesterday, but... haah..."

After chattering on, Catherine stopped and let out a deep sigh. Cassian had also heard that Dmitri had left without meeting Estella.

"Why? Did something happen?"

Sensing the gravity in her father's voice, Catherine seemed to hesitate for a moment before speaking. "She was busy with the knights, so she ignored Sir Dmitri and just sent him away. He tried so hard to spend some time with her, but she... Still, please don't worry too much. She's liked Sir Dmitri a lot since she was a child, so I'm sure it'll be fine."

"I see."

He did not say much, but Catherine was certain that the marquis was once again disappointed in Estella.

"Where is Estella now?"

"Ah... well..."

When Catherine hesitated, the marquis looked her straight in the eye and asked again. "Catherine, where is your sister right now?"

"Well, I saw her leaving with the mercenary captain earlier. She probably went out for something important, but I'm really worried about her. I looked into the mercenary group she hired, and no one seems to know anything specific about them. What if she's getting involved with strange people?" Catherine looked genuinely worried, her eyes welling up with tears.

The marquis was always concerned for the kindhearted Catherine. To be so soft-hearted.

"I'll look into it, so don't you worry too much. By the way, your birthday is coming up. If there's a gift you want, tell the butler in advance. I'll have it prepared."

"No, it's alright. I was just planning to have a small tea party with a few friends. The estate's finances aren't great right now, so I can't be spending money too. Birthdays come every year. You can throw me a huge party later, when the estate's situation improves."

The marquis gazed fondly at his admirable daughter, then asked with a smile, "Still, I have to give you a gift. So tell me."

"Then, write me a letter!"

"A letter?"

"Yes. So whenever I'm having a hard time, I can read the letter you wrote me and find strength!"

The marquis could not help but smile at Catherine's words, delivered with a bright, girlish grin. He thought how fortunate it was that Estella had Catherine by her side.

"There's somewhere I need you to go with me."

At Estella's words, Ciel stood up immediately without a word. His subordinates could not take their eyes off his back as he trotted after her like a gentle lamb.

"See, the captain likes squirrels."

The place Estella took Ciel was a blacksmith's forge. Ciel, who had been secretly wondering where they were going, now stood blankly before it, the intense heat palpable even from the outside. He had thought of her as an unpredictable woman, but he never imagined she would come to a place like this. A noble lady, at that. The more he saw of her, the more fascinating and amusing she became.

"Give it here."

When Estella held out her hand, Ciel handed over the bag he was carrying. It was the one full of Nebrion they had brought back from the Azure Forest, and though it was quite heavy, Estella took it from him with ease. The corner of Ciel's mouth twitched into a smirk.

"Oh! My lady, you're here! What do you want to make this time?"

Dolan spotted Estella, put down the hammer he was holding, and rushed outside. In his daily routine of making the same things over and over, she was a welcome presence who had reignited his faintly dying creative passion.

"Open it," Estella said, handing him the bag with a confident expression.

Dolan, who had opened the bag with a beaming smile full of anticipation, saw the lackluster glow of the Nebrion inside. His face immediately fell with disappointment. Estella, however, just smiled faintly, as if she had expected this very reaction.

"Dolan, I'm about to make you a very important proposal."

He blinked his large eyes and listened intently. He had only worked with Estella a few times, but the results of her proposals had always exceeded his expectations. The modified knight's dagger, in particular, was one of the finest pieces he had ever crafted.

"Melt the Nebrion into the molten iron according to the ratio written here and forge a sword."

"What? If you add something that soft, the sword will be useless, won't it?"

"Just make it first. Shall we start with this dagger?" Estella asked, holding out Dolan's own masterpiece.

Dolan took the Nebrion she gave him and tapped it with a hammer, breaking it into small pieces. It was such a soft stone that it crumbled like a cookie the moment the hammer touched it.

"Hmph."

Dolan's displeased sigh could be heard, but Estella paid it no mind. Ciel, who had approached without her noticing, also watched the process with a look of fascination. Standing side by side, their eyes sparkling as they watched, the two of them looked just like a pair of mischievous children.

"What happens if you mix that in there?" Ciel could not contain his curiosity and asked. He wanted a closer look, but he could not because Estella shut the door to the refining room.

"Trade secret, but I promise something amazing will happen. Prepare to be blown away."

"I'm already being surprised every single moment."

"Hm? Why?"

Estella turned her head to look up at him. Naturally, Ciel's head also turned toward her. Standing so close together, their faces were only inches apart. Her face, flushed from the heat of the forge, looked just like a ripe peach. Her soft cheeks looked like cream-filled buns, making him want to take a bite.

*Am I hungry?*

Having never once compared a person to food, Ciel was confused and dizzy from his own wild thoughts.

"What do you think you're doing?"

Only after hearing the anger in Estella's voice did Ciel realize he had just poked her cheek with his index finger.

*Damn it!*

Heat rushed to his face in embarrassment. "Ah, that... you had something on your face."

"Really? I could have gotten it myself. Next time, just tell me. I don't like people touching my face," Estella said, slapping his hand away from her cheek.

"Yes, I'm sorry," Ciel apologized hastily.

It was a huge relief that Riri was not there. If he had seen this, he was the type of person who would have teased him about it for the next thirty years.

"So, what were you so surprised about?"

The squirrel-like young lady was also persistent.
