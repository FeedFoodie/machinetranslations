Experience living alone had taught me simple cooking and how to handle a knife. I had lived independently even before my regression, so fixing a meal was not difficult.

"I see yogurt is used in all sorts of things... It doesn't go in omurice, does it? You'd eat it separately, not together, right?"

"Is this the only cutting board? A wooden one... Ah, I must've used it when I was making something for Lord Seongbin. I should order a new one."

"I don't know why making one dish creates so much to wash. It's so uneconomical. I should buy a one-pan recipe book."

My mouth was simply bored. The sudden quiet after living with six bustling people felt awkward enough to kill me. My professional spirit could not stand dead air, so I started muttering nonstop, even though this was not an ASMR video.

I sang my part from 『Alone』 while chopping and hummed solo songs by Jeong Seongbin and Kang Kiyeon while making the sauce. My hands moved cheerfully, and I finished prepping the ingredients in no time.

*What should I do while the rice is cooking?*

Work... I had shown similar things a few times, so it probably would not be interesting.

I was told to guard the dorm, so I could not go outside. It was a shame, because I would have gone to see a cute puppy or a nice landscape.

"I should order some daily necessities before the rice is done."

I had no choice but to start an online shopping session and reveal the items we use at the dorm.

The shared memo was already about to explode with supply requests.

I filmed the memo with the handycam first. Then, while the laptop booted up, I quickly checked which items I needed to buy.

"Why are there so many things we're out of? They didn't just write these down without checking the stock, did they?"

I took the camera and went to the storage room. I placed Bangjeon on the first shelf, Ponpon on the second, and Manggae on the third to act as guards. All the other friends had names, and the 'off-duty' cat would be sad without one, so I decided to call him Bangjeon for the day.

"There's a whole box of unopened soap right here, so why are they asking me to buy more? Jeho, are you watching? Do you have any idea how much this soap costs, ordering it so recklessly?"

"If you keep this up, I might have to revoke your stake in the memo."

"I stocked up on a lot of dehumidifiers last time they were on sale, but we've already used them all. I'll have to reorder this."

"Manggae, do you know what this is? It's called a foam board. A long time ago, people would write their names on these and wear them on their heads when they appeared on Visible Radio."

I got sidetracked and unearthed things we had not seen in over three years. The need to clear them out for more storage space consumed me. The sound of the rice cooker finishing pulled me back from the world of organizing just before I was left with nothing but the determination to clean.

I finished the omurice quickly. The egg blanket turned out beautifully. I wondered what to write on it, and when I wrote the title 『Alone』, it became a lonely, blood-stained meal. The plate looked like it had a major complaint about being a table for one. It fit the purpose of the self-produced content, so it should be fine, right?

I took a lot of pictures to post on BubblePop, but I did not upload them right away.

"It seems like it'd be a spoiler, so I should reveal it later..."

I also brought a meal for my dining mates, Manggae, Ponpon, and Bangjeon.

To stop them from getting jealous of the human eating alone, I also set down the food-shaped magnets Kang Kiyeon collects as souvenirs from his overseas tours.

"Somehow, your menu looks better than mine."

The figurine friends looked very happy with their brunch set, hamburger set, and taco. The omurice did not taste bad, so I enjoyed my meal too.

"I guess they have magnets like this in Korea too, right? What kind of magnets do they usually sell in Korea?"

Curious, I searched it up and even looked at the road view of a handmade shop with all sorts of magnets. The soy-marinated crab magnet was especially cool. Its quality made me want to buy one for Kang Kiyeon someday. I realized the world was full of strange things.

"Eat up while I'm feeding you. Once you guys go back to that room, you'll be trapped in an acrylic case, just staring at delicious-looking keychains."

After threatening them, I washed the empty plates and frying pan. Even after all that, it was only 1 PM. The day was too long.

{sep}

Time passed relatively well for about two hours. I was busy sorting the things I had messed up before eating. I took a poll in the group chat, asking them to confirm what to throw away and what to keep. I stacked the antiques we decided to part with neatly by the shoe rack to be taken out for recycling all at once.

Then, surprisingly, I ran out of things to do. Every minute and second crawled by like an eternity. I even reached the point of asking, "Manggae, can you turn on the TV?"

"Why isn't anyone coming home from work? Don't they want to rest early?"

Checking the shared calendar was useless. Lee Cheonghyeon would be the first to arrive, and his estimated arrival time was 8 PM. It was despairing.

"...Should I play a game?"

A brilliant idea flashed through my mind as I sat blankly on the sofa. I had told Sparkler I play games, but I had never actually shown myself playing 'My Farm'. There had been requests for it, so it seemed like the perfect time.

I usually used my small laptop screen, but today I connected it to the TV. The farm looked like it had expanded.

"Was my land always this big? Or is it just because it's been a while?"

If all this land belonged to the real Iwol, Dot Iwol would have made a fortune hiring people to farm instead of doing it himself. Or he could run a weekend farm for city folks.

"Bangjeon, are you interested in country life? There's a second career where you don't have to get hit with files at the office. You just have to be prepared for bug bites."

Judging by his expression, Bangjeon seemed willing to move to the countryside even if it meant getting bitten by bugs a hundred times, but nothing in the world is easy. With a grim heart, I pressed the 'Load Data' button to give him an indirect experience of harsh farming life.

[Arthur: Yiwol, it's been so long! I've been managing the farm for you in the meantime!]

Arthur, whom I had not seen in a long time, greeted me warmly, but for someone who supposedly missed me and managed the farm, the fields were a complete mess. His words about becoming life partners and cultivating the town's agriculture together must have been lies.

"See? This is my farming mate, but I'm the only one who has to pull the weeds. I usually focus on farming... but for you, I'll try fishing today."

I felt sorry for setting up the fake taco meal, so I decided to let him see some fish graphics. I took Dot Iwol to the coast for the first time. It was a proper fishing spot where I could use the fishing rod I received when I first came to this town.

I did not catch a fish on my first try. After a few trials and errors, I finally caught something.

['Sea Plastic' has been caught.]

"Isn't sea plastic just trash?"

I wondered if it had any value in this town, so I took it to the supermarket. They told me their supermarket was not a garbage dump.

"Then what do I do with this...?"

Dot Iwol wandered for a while and arrived in front of a trash can. Doubtfully, I opened the trash can, selected the sea plastic, and the item plopped right inside.

"Oh my god."

I had suddenly become the resident who caught trash. There was no greater tragedy.

I did not give up easily. For Bangjeon, and for the Sparklers who would watch this content later, I had to show a cool image of myself (my avatar, Dot Iwol) catching a big fish.

I grimly ran to the shore and cast my fishing rod again. I did not back down even when I pulled up a discarded fishing net and a boot, fighting against the waves.

After thirty fishing attempts, I had caught ten pieces of sea plastic, nine discarded fishing nets, eight single boots, and three anchovies. I went to the supermarket and checked the price of the beginner fishing rod, the next version of the old one. I could only afford it if I sold a hundred anchovies.

Swallowing my tears, I withdrew my saved money from the bank and changed my fishing rod. The beginner fishing rod had no mold, and the bait was a bit plumper.

Dot Iwol went to the sea confidently. After another thirty attempts, he obtained five pieces of sea plastic, five discarded fishing nets, three boots, ten anchovies, and three horse mackerels. The thing that came up on the end of the fishing rod I cast one last time was a new piece of trash.

"Humans, stop throwing trash in the sea!"

A shout burst from deep within me before I realized it. I was heartbroken.

{sep}

The members finally came home after I went out for recycling (I think that much should be allowed, it's a household chore), ate dinner, and even folded the fresh laundry and put it in everyone's closets. Exhausted from playing by myself, I welcomed them with all my heart.

"Just how bored were you to get this worn out?" Lee Cheonghyeon asked.

"Don't even start. You won't know until it's your turn."

"They say modern people need time alone because they have so much interaction with others, but I guess that's not always the case?"

"Either I'm not a modern person, or the standard for 'a lot' of interaction is set improperly. It must be one of the two."

"Wouldn't it be weird if a celebrity wasn't in the category of people who have a lot of interaction with others...?"

I pushed the puzzled Lee Cheonghyeon into his room. He looked back, bewildered, then realized Manggae and Ponpon were back in their proper places.

"Why did you put them here? I took them out so you could play with them if you were bored!"

"It was time for them to go, so I sent them home."

"How kind of you. The kids must've been touched."

"They must be tired from playing with me all day, so you should tell Manggae and Ponpon they did a good job, too."

"Your naming sense is truly desperate."

I ignored Lee Cheonghyeon shaking his head and returned to my room. I put Bangjeon back with the other cats. Then, in my mind, I added today's self-produced content to the Hall of Fame of my most difficult shoots.
