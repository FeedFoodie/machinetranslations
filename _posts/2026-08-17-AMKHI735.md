『Do you hyungs have any hobbies like collecting stuff? I guess Seongbin-hyung collects ballpoint pens a little?』

『It's not really collecting on purpose, just buying one or two if I get them as gifts or find pens that write smoothly?』

『I don't have one. Should I pick up something this time?』

『You hyungs have plenty of space in your rooms, so it'd be fine to collect whatever.』

Just as Kang Kiyeon said, there was hardly any clutter in Kim Iwol and Choi Jeho's rooms.

Pictures of celebrities' homes commonly featured interior props or designer furniture. In contrast, the eldest members' rooms contained nothing more than a wardrobe, a chest of drawers, a desk, and a bed.

Imagining that room filled with miniatures felt totally out of place.

『Our contact names changed too, didn't they?』

Jeong Seongbin pointed out the exact detail that Sparklers had been curious about.

Kim Iwol smiled faintly with nostalgia.

『My contact names always get revealed publicly in unexpected places.』

『What are Seongbin-hyung and I saved as?』

『Seongbin is Leader, and Kiyeon, you're Reliance.』

『That sounds like a magical warrior style.』

『May I ask why you changed them like that?』

『Just because. Since way back, you guys always felt like that to me.』

The birthday celebration stage at the concert was truly touching. Sparklers viewed Kim Iwol's new contact names as an extension of the video letter from the concert and could hardly shake off the lingering emotion.

Won Chaehee felt the same way. For about a week, her mind stayed stuck on that celebration event.

The emotion did not last long, however. Every time Manggae and Bangjeoni came up, Won Chaehee pondered Kim Iwol's naming sense deeply.

She even considered it a relief that Kim Iwol had not named them Dog Turd. It was a harsh reality to endure for a fansite master who loved beautiful things as much as her idol.

『Senior Jahan contacted you back then too. Are you planning to go grab that meal you missed out on anytime soon?』

『Senior Yunjae contacted me later, and we talked about setting a date to meet up. Maybe around the end of the year or early next year? Senior Jahan really has good luck with broadcasts because we don't actually talk on the phone that often. However, he calls miraculously whenever there's a camera around.』

『Didn't he contact you on your birthday specifically to congratulate you, hyung?』

『Our Kiyeon has such a good memory. Except for that time, I mean.』

At the mention of meeting at the end or beginning of the year, Won Chaehee caught the distinct vibe of a seasoned working adult. It sounded just like a year-end or New Year party.

This was why adults across the country grew busy whenever the year neared its end.

『Seongbin, you seemed to be doing almost nothing but work?』

『I came off looking exceptionally busy like a celebrity that day. I'm not usually like that.』

『You're pretty much the same on normal days, hyung.』

Even though the older members teased him like that, Kang Kiyeon acknowledged Jeong Seongbin's leader-like conduct.

『Iwol-hyung's nagging was just like usual too.』

*...Is he lenient only toward Jeong Seongbin and relentless toward Kim Iwol?*

At the utter coldness of that evaluation, Kim Iwol looked dumbfounded.

*However, Kim Iwol, you're the one sending those 'read more' messages every single day. You have no right to scold Kiyeon.*

Won Chaehee took Kang Kiyeon's side for once.

Instead of offering a rebuttal, Kim Iwol simply pulled up his blanket neatly.

『Kiyeon worked hard too. I'm always grateful that you even take care of our shoes.』

The gentle leader Jeong Seongbin brought up his younger teammate's thoughtful action.

『It doesn't even take that long, and I think I just looked like I roamed around more because I was contrasted with Joowoo-hyung, who came right before me.』

『Joowoo moves quietly. Whatever he does, he's the type to stay calmly in one spot and stick with one thing for a long time.』

『On the flip side, I can't keep my body still. Though I'm not as active as Iwol-hyung either.』

『Kiyeon, your movement tendencies seem similar to mine. Iwol-hyung and Cheonghyeon are more active than us, while Jeho-hyung and Joowoo are on the calmer side.』

『Still, grouping Joowoo-hyung together with Jeho-hyung is a bit...』

『Right. Joowoo repotted plants, watered them, made ice, and cleaned up. I've never seen Jeho lift his back off the sofa when there are no cameras around.』

『Was Jeho-hyung really that bad?』

『That's just because you view him through rose-colored glasses, Seongbin. He really is that bad. I bet Bangjeoni on it.』

After chatting about their self-produced content for a while, the shorter members moved on to sharing behind-the-scenes stories from music shows. Topics distinct from the typical MV reaction videos uploaded right after music video releases poured out.

The atmosphere quickly grew lively with stories about their conversations while deciding on ending poses and the specific costume details they paid attention to for each stage.

『When we first received the song, there was even a suggestion about using standing mics and choreographing something moderate with them.』

『That probably would've had its own charm. I'd love to try it at a concert if we get the chance.』

『Focusing primarily on key point choreography? That sounds nice.』

『Kiyeon, a clip of your bracelet getting caught on a belt loop during a recent stage is circulating online.』

『I was wearing it as an accessory over our team bracelet, but the loop attached to the charm happened to get stuck right there... Since it was live, I broke into a cold sweat.』

『You handled it well without making it obvious, though. I didn't even realize you couldn't use one hand until Iwol-hyung performed a different movement. You were pulling off your part so naturally.』

『You were amazing for noticing just from seeing me step out of line for a moment, Seongbin. It was the hand on the side you couldn't see from your angle. Plenty of people didn't realize even after watching the video!』

『You never stray from the fixed choreography, hyung. So when you suddenly stopped mid-transition and reached out toward Kiyeon, I thought it was strange. Did you unhook the caught charm in that brief moment?』

『I had to clean it up quickly, so I snapped it off with force.』

『Pardon?』

『It wasn't picked up by the microphone, but I heard it. There was a loud crunching sound. Suddenly, my hand gained freedom...』

『Iwol-hyung takes pictures of me really often in the waiting room.』

『Your hair, makeup, and styling are the condensed essence of our album this time. Our facial features lack warmth, so we need to bring out delicate differences in temperature. You play a crucial role whenever needed from the most dramatic position, Seongbin. I'm always thankful for that.』

『Sometime, let's gather only the legendary spArk selected by Iwol-hyung and hold a spArk World Cup.』

『Who do you expect to be the winning candidate, Kiyeon?』

『Everyone probably knows. By the way, if we chatter like this, will Sparklers be able to fall asleep listening to us?』

『......』

『......』

At Kang Kiyeon's sharp question, the two older members went silent.

Won Chaehee, watching the flood of passionate comments, typed and sent 'Keep going' to help shape public opinion.

『I heard the taller members praised the three of us who weren't on that live stream. Shouldn't we repay them by talking about our members' good points?』

『Of course. Cheonghyeon said he'd barge in as our representative to protest if we didn't do the same.』

『Did he watch all of that?』

『Iwol-hyung watched everything until he got kicked out halfway through.』

Won Chaehee had also seen the scene where Lee Cheonghyeon told Kim Iwol to turn off the broadcast. Kim Iwol really seemed to monitor it, turning off the TV at a single word from Lee Cheonghyeon.

『Kiyeon, did you hear what compliments the members gave?』

『I watched it through Metube clips. What about Seongbin-hyung?』

『I watched in real-time with Iwol-hyung. I was able to watch my part because Cheonghyeon didn't kick me out.』

『They said a lot of good things. I was too embarrassed to tell them I enjoyed watching it, though. Do you think the hyungs and Lee Cheonghyeon are watching this stream...? It's embarrassing.』

『All three of them have schedules right now, so they won't be able to watch right away. Don't worry.』

『As expected, you know everything inside out.』

『I don't memorize it on purpose. What can I do when looking at the schedule just pops it into my head?』

Kim Iwol cracked a joke. He did not seem to brag. Though he often showed strange sides of himself in the media, he was originally famous for his intelligence.

Judging by how long he held grudges, his memory was certainly exceptional.

『I have something to praise Jeho for.』

With that, Kim Iwol slowly pulled back his blanket. One of Kim Iwol's characteristics was maintaining manners even in minor details.

When he wore a suit in private, he always buttoned only the top button of his jacket and unbuttoned it when sitting down. When listening to others, he looked at the speaker without turning his back too much to the camera.

*Including tidying up his surroundings whenever talking about someone else.*

Simply put, he had good manners. Some said he was overly conscious of the camera, but acting consistently like that for six years made it fair to consider it part of his personality.

Having lain down until now for the live stream concept, he seemed unable to fight his natural inclination and straightened his posture.

『I praise him for being a good hyung to the younger members.』

At Kim Iwol's words, Jeong Seongbin and Kang Kiyeon looked surprised. They looked as if they had heard something unbelievable.

Won Chaehee doubted her ears as well. She could hardly believe such wonderful praise, rather than nagging, came out of Kim Iwol's mouth regarding Choi Jeho.

Whenever they appeared on variety shows, Kim Iwol adopted the persona of a home shopping host and delivered long speeches about Choi Jeho's incredible fancams, yet when left to themselves, they acted as dry friends. Apparently, they had been building a deep friendship behind the scenes all along.

『I nag you guys a lot. I'm the type to forbid many things and constantly step in to interfere, but Jeho seems to take on the role of waiting quietly until you talk about your worries first and trusting you.』

『What you say isn't nagging either, hyung, it's advice.』

『I'm grateful you say so. From the stance that the more people you can rely on or consult with, the better, I think Jeho performing the role of a reliable hyung has a positive impact on the team and the younger members. Don't you guys also feel that the nuance of the topics differs when consulting me compared to discussing things with Choi Jeho?』

『There is... a difference.』

Kang Kiyeon immediately agreed.

『I know you confide productive concerns like choreography feedback or mental growth to him. Those topics never reach my inbox. To me, huh? You just ask stuff like, 'Should I post this photo or that photo on social media?' Am I right, or am I wrong?』

『It's because you checked everything throughout our trainee days, hyung, so it became a habit. I bet we rely on you for a lot more than just photos though!』

『Your tone sounds speculative, doesn't it? Fine, go to Choi Jeho, all of you. Become 'Jeho-hyung and the Kids.'』

As if intending to demonstrate the true purpose of a lying-down stream, Kim Iwol flopped back down onto his mattress.

Jeong Seongbin tried to soothe Kim Iwol by asking what was wrong, but Kang Kiyeon responded nonchalantly.

『Alright. We'll form a big orange and kumquat gathering then.』

The moment Kang Kiyeon finished speaking, Kim Iwol's upper body bounced up like a spring.

For a second, Won Chaehee thought Kim Iwol was going to fly out of the screen.

Kim Iwol vented his fury.『Of all people, you, who are in charge of our team's fashion sense! How could you say something so terrible! How many times must I tell you that if even you get tainted by his evil clutches, spArk has no future! After Cheonghyeon ended up like that, do you know how much I...!』

With a familiar look on his face, Kang Kiyeon chimed in, 『Shall I give the next compliment?』
